# Documentation ownership and navigation

This map routes the maintained documentation for the checked-out source.
Usage guides show how to call an interface; contracts specify admission and
outputs. The [theory reading route](../theory/README.md#a-short-reading-route)
leads to the owners of definitions, hypotheses and proofs. The
[research program](../theory/RESEARCH_PROGRAM.md#research-current-question)
alone selects the current research question.

Start with the [root README](../README.md) for installation and an organized
mathematical object, then use the
[example learning route](../examples/README.md#begin-with-an-organized-mathematical-object)
and [recovery-report guide](guides/relational/RELATIONAL_CAPTURE_AND_MEMORY.md#read-an-organized-objects-recovery-report)
to inspect its state, law, identity and response. Continue with the
[CLI/SDK object workflow](CLI_AND_SDK.md#work-with-an-organized-object) or the
[regional workflows](guides/REGIONAL_AND_RELATIONAL.md) for the supported
actions and observations. The [theory-to-execution map](../theory/THEORY_TO_EXECUTION.md)
connects the corresponding mathematical owners to engine modules and checks.
Numerical evidence and conditional certificates retain the premises of their
source objects; they are not empirical validation.

Choose the execution contract before following an example:

- **Native relational evolution:** the
  [joint execution guide](guides/relational/RELATIONAL_EXECUTION.md#execute-the-conditional-relational-model)
  and [contract](contracts/relational/RELATIONAL_EXECUTION.md#conditional-relational-execution)
  describe the argument-based law, held capacity and phase-domain checks.
- **Normalized-sine constructions and comparisons:** the
  [formed-object workflow](guides/relational/SINE_PATTERNS.md#formed-object-sdk-workflow)
  and [comparison contract](contracts/relational/SINE_COMPARISON_AND_INFERENCE.md#detached-normalized-sine-complete-law-comparison)
  retain their distinct smooth law. These observation, certificate and forecast
  APIs do not change the law executed by `Network.step_relational`. Alternative
  reciprocal mobilities have their own complete laws; shared storage does not
  transfer every theorem between them.
- **Operator words:** [CLI and SDK](CLI_AND_SDK.md) and
  [event contracts](contracts/OPERATOR_EVENTS.md) cover registered operators,
  grammar and live admission. Word counts are not elapsed structural time.
- **Continuous nodal flow:** [API contracts](API_CONTRACTS.md) and the
  [architecture flow](../ARCHITECTURE.md#nodal-execution-flow) identify pressure,
  integrator, clock and source responsibilities. Held pressure, refreshed
  pressure and operator events have different execution semantics.

Use the [regional workflow index](guides/REGIONAL_AND_RELATIONAL.md) to find an
interface and its linked proof owner to assess the scientific claim. A captured
state, supplied invariant family, finite enclosure and physical measurement
have different source obligations.
Common variables or storage do not join their theorems. The
[research program](../theory/RESEARCH_PROGRAM.md#research-current-question)
selects research work; an available guide does not authorize a campaign.

## Update rules

Change the implementation or definition and its owner together. Summaries link
to that owner; they do not copy derivations, configuration tables or delivery
histories. Keep usage examples separate from the full admission contract.
The agent mirror is the only intentionally exact prose duplicate.

`theory/RESEARCH_PROGRAM.md` owns the objective and current question in a small
entry document. Each linked chapter is a canonical Markdown file that can be
opened and edited directly. `theory/research_catalog.json` supplies metadata
for generated navigation. The compiled site reads these same sources; the
optional `scripts/research_book.py` workflow is described in
[Contributing](../CONTRIBUTING.md#documentation). Generated pages preserve
paths, relative links and frozen proof associations. Old plan, strategy and
portfolio routes are compact compatibility views, not active guidance.

Keep the research program within 96 KiB and each research chapter, program and
maintained guide at most 4,000 physical lines. These limits are checked by
`scripts/check_documentation.py`. Split chapters
by model and responsibility. Put shared admission in its contract, proofs in
their chapter, and examples in the matching guide. Preserve published routes
and section aliases. Archived records and frozen evidence retain their own
provenance and are outside this size guard.

The catalog above owns the technical website menu; `theory/research_catalog.json`
owns the theory menu metadata. `python scripts/check_documentation.py --write-generated` refreshes
both menus, the registry-derived operator table and the glossary index. The
ordinary gate checks catalog coverage and generated agreement. Link/fragment
checking and a strict site build also belong to the
[documentation checks](../scripts/README.md). Generated consistency does not
validate a theorem or a physical identification.

The glossary's declaration classes and dependency/evidence checks are specified
by its own template and `scripts/check_glossary.py`. Do not redefine concept
admission in another index or infer scientific proof from a valid link.

## Evidence and generated material

`docs/assets/` holds website support and referenced frozen research evidence,
including source archives and numerical reports. Preserve those paths and bytes;
new runs belong in `artifacts/`, not over the published record. An evidence
record establishes only its declared scope and does not redefine a live API.
Use [retained-evidence checks](../TESTING.md#current-checks-and-retained-evidence)
and the [instrument lifecycle](../benchmarks/README.md#freeze-before-evaluating-a-reserved-response)
to distinguish read-only audits from a separately declared new evaluation.

`artifacts/`, ignored `output/`, `outputs/` and `results/` retain local receipts
and source contexts. They are not guides or another task queue. The
[documentation tools](../scripts/README.md) own source staging and the treatment
of frozen source captures; maintained proofs and contracts remain checked.
`build/docs-source/` and `site/` are generated; edit their maintained inputs.

Retire a document only after moving useful material to its owner and repairing
maintained references. Preserve source-bound observations and mark their scope
and replacement. [Archive records](../theory/research/archive/README.md) explain
supersession; manifests retain their original recovery paths and hashes.
Unchanged committed material with verified Git recovery does not need another
archive copy. Useful conditional results, counterexamples, uncommitted work
and unique frozen evidence require preservation before removal.
