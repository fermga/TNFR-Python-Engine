    Relational dynamics contract index¶

    Choose the complete law and evidence contract before using a relational API. Native execution, detached smooth-sine assessments, shared observations and conditional proof tools keep distinct admission and numerical guarantees.

    Chapters and scope¶

    Chapter Responsibility
    Native relational execution and observations Argument-based field and finite-step execution, native tangents, prepared patterns and hypothetical support/state events.
    Native relational capture, memory and continuous proofs Native capture and exclusions, cycle/contact memory, transit, reflected proofs, regularity and retained robustness evidence.
    Shared observations, information and finite-sample bounds Regional and source-relative observations, exact linear and phase-moment information, finite-sample evidence and shared circular geometry.
    Normalized-sine comparison, inference and forecasts Shared sine-law report admission, captured comparison, retained mediation, sampling, hidden state/capacity inference and prior-only forecasting.
    Sine response, constitutive comparisons and hidden memory Declared input/output resonance, bridge-law and clock discrimination, return-path geometry, causal hidden memory, autonomous pulses and recurrence distinctions.
    Sine pattern geometry, formation and recovery Relative patterns and origins, composition, equilibrium and symmetry, prepared formation, recovery/capture, controlled slow references and budget exclusions.
    Conservative sine saddle preparations and certificates Signed-involution preparation, the C5/private-leaf saddle, directed passage, same-orbit retention, sensitivity, retained-metric forecasts and constitutive controls.
    Conservative sine regional and collective dynamics Regional storage and currents, conservative winding acquisition and retention, compatible collective families, actual contact feedback and forecast-derived organization.
    Retained sine pair state, identification and pulses Sufficient unordered/mixed pair state and cancellation inversion, capacities and support symmetry, pairing observations and windows, emission/transfer and doubled-C5 pulse response.

    Existing section links remain available through this index. The execution plan alone assigns research work.
