    Normalized-sine comparison, inference and forecasts¶

    Shared sine-law report admission, captured comparison, prepared transport, source and interaction budgets, retained mediation, sampling, hidden state/capacity inference and prior-only forecasting.

    Part of Relational dynamics contract index. Section links remain stable; hypotheses and model changes remain local to each result.

    Admission when chaining sine reports¶

    Report-consuming readers that link here re-admit the declared inputs needed by their calculation, reconstruct consumed derived quantities and apply their own theorem hypotheses. Cached bounds, degrees, rates or success flags cannot replace those inputs. Each reader's section identifies its reconstruction inputs and any stronger domain or numerical budget.

    For complete captured or relative sources, _sine_admission.py checks the declared sine law, simple connected unit support, node-coordinate order, degree/neighbor consistency, finite form/phase/capacity vectors and nonnegative residual radii. Neighbor-row order does not change support equivalence. Authoritative stored model coefficients are admitted without a second normalization: exact rationals retain their values; negative loss, Boolean or nonfinite coefficients reject. Admission precedes coefficient equality, including comparisons between a pattern and its full forecast.

    Graph capture and exact detached declarations are different input paths. bound_relational_sine_exchange first captures graph coordinates through the shared binary64 boundary; the resulting fractions represent those captured values, not necessarily an originally supplied Fraction. A downstream reader that admits exact rational primitives can instead consume an explicitly declared detached source in its retained node order. That declaration is a new mathematical preparation, not an exact reconstruction of a rounded graph. Rebuilding rates and bounds is required after such a change. Neither rounded radians nor a small residual can stand for a separately declared exact turn target, branch history or invariant family.

    A consumed SineRelativeForecast must retain the pattern's model, support and held capacities, a complete endpoint layout and a valid actual time within its declared horizon. Visible capacities equal the pattern's exact values; the initial final-capacity coordinate uses the interval owner's outward rounding of the pattern's final exact capacity, and its nonnegative endpoint interval must still contain the held value. A wider endpoint enclosure is permitted. The changed-capacity freeze_hidden control is unsupported by these pattern readers. They assess the full endpoint at validated_end_time, retaining the solver's status and requested horizon; they do not replace it with projected rows or original static bounds.

    The hidden-state/capacity/prior chain instead reconstructs its inverse from retained visible state, unit incidence, first-rate observations and prior metadata in the observation owner. Capacity inference also uses its supplied acceleration channels, with omitted channels unavailable. The forecast owner rebuilds joint witness admission and the complete initial box before propagation. These detached stages preserve exact values without recapturing a graph or accessing the live Gamma registry.

    Calculations use the admitted, normalized inputs. Source association is separate: complete-source readers may retain the supplied report, while inverse/prior adapters preserve an original only when it equals the rebuilt report after validation. Neither association supplies raw inputs to subsequent arithmetic. Other unconsumed snapshot fields may remain attached; their presence or object identity is not evidence that they were recomputed. Only the requested calculation's inputs are checked. This boundary does not authenticate acquisition, source chronology or public dataclasses, and does not replay a forecast or prove that observations share a trajectory. Export preserves the report under its existing schema.

    Shared coefficient and source admission imposes no global geometry or solver work cap. Positive loss/capacity, exact symmetry, target criticality and each finite work budget remain requirements of the individual consumer.

    For bare degree d_i, weighted form gradient q=L_W*x and unweighted phase current S_i=sum_(j in U_i) sin(theta_j-theta_i), its original-clock rows are p_i=w*S_i/(pi*d_i), x_dot_i=nu_i*p_i and theta_dot_i=w*nu_i*q_i/(beta*pi*d_i). Conductance enters the form storage and its reciprocal phase row. Presence enters the phase current, phase storage and normalization. A present zero-conductance edge remains a phase neighbor; it is not an absent edge. Conductances and capacities are held between events, with no inferred reinforcement or occurrence law.

    Finite form response under the complete weighted law¶

    SineSupportExchangeComparison.finite_form_response_bounds(duration=h) returns SineFiniteFormResponse from the same shared support owner. It first re-admits the complete source and a strictly positive, finite, non-Boolean duration in the original structural clock. Exact rational durations remain exact. The connected conservative support law keeps its actual exchange coefficient w, phase-storage coefficient beta, held nonnegative conductances and capacities, including zero-capacity nodes. No forcing, inputs or events are admitted during the window.

    The global bound |x_dot_i|<=w*nu_i/pi gives ||P x(t)||_2<=||P x(0)||_2+h*w*||nu||_2/pi for 0<=t<=h, where P subtracts the instantaneous arithmetic mean. This projection has norm one; the arithmetic mean need not be conserved. The resulting outward radius supplies the previously conditional acceleration envelope throughout the window. Every endpoint is enclosed by its initial form plus h times its initial-rate interval and the symmetric remainder A_i*h^2/2. The radius and acceleration calculation share the same freshly admitted source within this call. Standalone acceleration calls and exports still perform their own complete primitive admission; reuse does not trust a previously cached source or certificate.

    The report retains the fresh source, duration, initial and whole-window centered-form radius upper bounds, global form-speed upper bound, initial_form_rates, acceleration_bounds, form_remainder_upper_bounds and endpoint_form_bounds, with node order inherited from source.nodes. The initial-rate interval uncertainty and analytical nonlinear remainder remain separate; there is no numerical integration step or discretization error. Positive durations have no theorem-imposed upper limit, but a wide enclosure may not resolve a requested observation.

    status="enclosed" certifies the enclosure, not a response sign, identity, operator trigger or acquired temporal history. Subtracting two endpoint intervals gives a valid contrast enclosure; one containing zero cannot establish that the contrast vanishes. Exact null controls need an independent invariance argument. Disconnected alternatives require their admitted component laws, not relaxed connected-support admission.

    Full-state errors under one complete held law¶

    source.initial_state_perturbation_bounds(duration=h, form_error_bounds=ex, phase_error_bounds=et) returns SineInitialStatePerturbation from the shared support owner. It re-admits the primitive source and complete law, a strictly positive finite duration, and two nonnegative error vectors in source.nodes order. Form errors use the source's form units; phase errors are errors in declared real radian lifts. Boolean, nonfinite, negative or wrong-length errors reject before arithmetic. Exact rational inputs remain exact; represented inputs retain their admitted values.

    The report compares every solution starting in this coordinate box with the solution starting at source, under the same held support, conductances, capacities, coefficients and original clock. No node, mean, within-block error or phase lift is projected away. It neither compares different laws nor recomputes an operator's affinity weights.

    With weighted strengths s_i, outward local constants are a_i >= 2*w*nu_i/pi and b_i >= 2*w*nu_i*s_i/(beta*pi*d_i). Set A=max(a_i), B=max(b_i), u=max(ex), v=max(et) and D=1-A*B*h^2. For D>0, whole-window uniform bounds are X=(u+A*h*v)/D and Theta=(v+B*h*u)/D; the retained nodal bounds are ex_i+a_i*h*Theta and et_i+b_i*h*X. These follow from the full nonlinear sine Lipschitz inequality and both evolution rows, not a constant-Jacobian approximation. Zero capacities retain their frozen rows.

    For nonzero errors with D<=0, the time-envelope status is unavailable and its bounds are absent. This is a limitation of this sufficient estimate, not evidence of instability or failure of the global solution. An exactly zero error box has zero deviation at every duration by uniqueness, even when this denominator is nonpositive. Every supported zero-conductance edge retains bare sine incidence. When all conductances are zero, the zero phase gain admits arbitrarily long windows.

    The independent initial-storage upper bound sums W_ij*(abs(x_i-x_j)+ex_i+ex_j)^2/2 and beta*min(2, upper(1-cos(theta_j-theta_i))+et_i+et_j) over actual edges. It remains available when the time envelope is unavailable. Conservation under this complete law retains that bound; a named identity still needs its own support, initial-membership and boundary-barrier premises.

    Direct schema tnfr.sine-initial-state-perturbation.v1 and generic SDK export rebuild from primitive source, duration and error vectors, ignoring cached bounds and status. This is a finite tracking certificate, not decay, source acquisition, event admission or a measured temporal history. See the proof and fixed contact comparison, controls and example.

    Joint state and conductance uncertainty on fixed support¶

    source.state_and_conductance_perturbation_bounds(duration=h, form_error_bounds=ex, phase_error_bounds=et, conductance_error_bounds=rho) returns SineStateConductancePerturbation. Node budgets follow source.nodes; conductance budgets follow source.edges. All budgets are finite, nonnegative admitted real scalars. The candidate law has the same bare support, capacities, exchange coefficient, storage coefficient and clock as the reference; each candidate conductance is nonnegative and within its supplied radius. A radius larger than its nominal conductance is allowed, with the candidate domain still intersected with nonnegative conductances.

    The shared nonlinear comparison retains the preceding form gain a_i and uses strengths from W+rho in its phase gain b_i. Nominal reference form speed obeys v_i=w*nu_i/pi; consequently each reference edge contrast is at most abs(x_i-x_j)+h*(v_i+v_j) throughout the window. Multiplying these bounds by rho_ij*w*nu_i/(beta*pi*d_i) and summing gives an outward phase-forcing bound F_i.
