# Book philosophy

This document owns the educational purpose and evidence policy of the Spanish
manuscript. It defines pedagogy, not TNFR physics. The [manual index](INDEX.md)
owns chapter inventory and publication status; the [documentation map](../docs/README.md)
identifies the technical owners.

## Purpose and reader

Help a scientifically curious secondary-school student describe persistent
organization, ask how interactions change it, and evaluate a mathematical model.
The reader should be able to explain an idea, identify its assumptions, compare
alternatives and state what would challenge it. Agreement with TNFR is not a
success criterion.

Everyday scenes motivate questions. They do not establish that TNFR is a
necessary or unique explanation, that other sciences have failed, or that
physical particles have been derived.

## Narrative arc

1. Observe change and persistence in familiar phenomena.
2. Distinguish what a snapshot, a network and an evolution rule each describe.
3. Introduce TNFR as a candidate framework with explicit variables and contracts.
4. Read results critically, separating proofs, computations and open questions.

Each chapter should develop a motivated question for the next one. Narrative
continuity is an editorial choice; it must never imply scientific inevitability.

## Voice and mathematics

Write in Spanish for the reader; the existing authoring guides remain in English.
Use a scientist-explorer voice, concrete scenes and respectful questions. Define
terms before relying on them. Introduce an equation with its motivating question,
the meaning of its symbols and its scope, then explain the result in ordinary
language. A suggestive analogy cannot replace derivation or measurement.

Use mathematical checks to test intuition, including when they overturn it.
Do not claim that intuitively plausible conclusions are firmer than proofs.

## Evidence policy

- Separate observation, modeling assumption, derived consequence and hypothesis.
- Declare support, state variables, initial conditions and any imposed driving.
- Distinguish synchronization, resonant response, graph nesting and geometric
  self-similarity; one does not automatically establish the others.
- Treat thresholds, telemetry and auxiliary flows according to their actual
  implementation and scope.
- A physical interpretation requires an independently specified measurement
  map and predictions evaluated without later adjustment.
- Retain failures and counterexamples. Do not explain disagreement as a reader's
  failure to understand.
- Label imagined exercises as such; never invent experimental results or
  reader-pilot responses.

Definitions and mathematical boundaries belong to [foundations](../theory/FUNDAMENTAL_THEORY.md)
and the linked specialist references. Research status and priorities belong to
the [execution plan](../theory/research/FIVE_STAGE_EXECUTION_PLAN.md), not this book.
