1. OBJECTIVE, CONSTRAINTS AND MEANING OF COMPLETION
================================================

Macro objective
---------------
Test whether explicit TNFR foundations and derived dynamics can account for
the formation, persistence, interaction and dissolution of coherent patterns,
and produce quantitative predictions for observable laboratory phenomena.
The long-term question is whether a common TNFR description can explain
multiple physical regimes without importing their laws as extra premises.
Whether this is possible remains an open question, not a conclusion to defend.

The next meaningful milestone is narrower: a reproducible differentiated
pattern with a declared formation mechanism, a measured perturbation response
and an explicit account of what maintains it. A later milestone is a justified
measurement map and a laboratory comparison capable of rejecting the model.
Neither a simulation nor a finite set of matches demonstrates that all reality
necessarily emerges from TNFR or uniquely identifies its underlying ontology.

Governing derivation chain
-------------------------
    TNFR foundations -> explicit constitutive/evolution rules -> derivation
    -> structural observable/prediction -> reproducible experimental test.

The nodal equation is dEPI/dt = nu_f * DeltaNFR. Its factorization alone does
not determine the pressure law, phase evolution, capacity updates, topology
or next operator. Choosing DeltaNFR = prescribed_rate/nu_f after observing a
target trajectory encodes that trajectory; it does not derive its dynamics.

Every candidate must distinguish:
- Primitives: the stated TNFR structural state and canonical invariants.
- Constitutive/operator choices: pressure realization, factors, phase rules,
  support changes and grammar policies; identify unresolved derivations.
- Preparation/input: initial graph/state, supplied schedule and boundary data.
- Derived content: equations, coefficients, balances and predictions obtained
  from those premises independently of the desired result.
- Numerical settings: timestep, tolerances, arithmetic and resource limits.
- Measurement: a justified observation map, units and instrument uncertainty.

No independent physical law, noise model, probability rule, Hamiltonian,
interaction field or fitted explanatory constant may repair a failed TNFR
derivation. Existing engine defaults are implementation choices unless their
claimed physical role is derived. Mathematical transformations are allowed
when their equivalence and domain are established. Comparison models remain
controls, not hidden ingredients of the TNFR explanation.

Experimental boundary
---------------------
All calculations and experiments must be feasible on an ordinary workstation
or at most a research laboratory on Earth's surface. Exclude astronomical,
astrophysical and cosmological data, including ground-telescope observations;
exclude satellite/space-mission data and required large accelerators,
observatory-scale facilities or mandatory supercomputer access.
Check origin, apparatus, acquisition, uncertainty, replication and resources
before accepting a dataset. Unknown provenance means pending eligibility.
No apparatus or dataset has yet been selected for the current O6 programme.
Physical Hz, temperature, energy or length require a justified structural
measurement map; Hz_str and engine time are not automatically physical units.

Evidence status has three independent axes
-----------------------------------------
Mathematics: exact scoped result / conditional result / counterexample / open.
Implementation: implemented and checked / finite observation / not implemented.
Physical evidence: untested / supported in a stated test / rejected / inconclusive.
A passing test checks an implementation claim; it is not empirical confirmation.
A finite causal execution is not proof of future admission or infinite-time
stability. Historical test totals must not be added as a completion percentage.

have a closed autonomous multichannel explanation of a self-restoring physical

ontological predictions. Those gaps have different dependencies and must not
be compressed into a single claim that the paradigm is almost complete.

3. ONE OWNERSHIP MAP FOR ALL OPEN QUESTIONS
=========================================

O1 — What state and laws are actually specified?
----------------------------------------------
O1.a owns sufficient state, realizability and induced field evolution.
Reuse S3/E1 observation and history work; B1 closes only the P2 subproblem.
For an observation h and candidate state law F, first ask whether equal h
and equal declared inputs imply equal observed future evolution. A proposed
field flow must also preserve the realizable image of h. P4/P5 and the P2
harmonic obstruction are shared positive/negative controls, not new projects.

O1.b owns endogenous scheduling and policy viability (E2/S5). Admission of a
supplied word is available; selection of that word is not thereby derived.
Distinguish a designed fixed policy used as a control from a rule derived from
TNFR. Test invariant admission/termination on declared full states and histories.

S10 supplies the separate O1 question of operator completeness: define an
admissible transformation space independently of the thirteen-operator catalog
before asking generation or redundancy. Atomic stages are infrastructure,
not a completeness proof.
Exit gate: one specified candidate, an input/derived ledger, and a behavior
excluded by its laws. Broader minimality and endogenous closure remain open.

O2 — Which geometry, clocks and propagation emerge?
-------------------------------------------------
O2.a owns dimension selection; S4/S13/E3 own its directed-transport dependency.
Reuse graph spectra, actual THOL/UM snapshots and established read-outs. Keep
supplied topology, conductance, metric length, embedding and time distinct.
Clique size, simplex dimension, growth dimension and heat-trace dimension
are different measurements. A chosen lattice does not select its own dimension.
Require an actual canonical topology trace before attributing growth to glyphs.
Then seek a controlled scale interval/refinement and a mechanism that selects
it across declared preparations, without target-dimension tuning.
Propagation: separate a threshold-defined response front, exact support bounds,
and probability factorization. None automatically derives physical relativity.
Exit gate: a scoped label-invariant geometry/clock construction, its retained
inputs and controlled limits. Physical time and autonomous geometry are open.

O3 — Can differentiated structures maintain and restore themselves?
------------------------------------------------------------------
O3.a is the active owner of persistence and recovery (E4), with S2/E5 event
accounting and S1/E6 defect budgets as shared prerequisites. Formation,
transport, protected winding, active maintenance, recovery and two-pattern
interaction each need their own evidence status.
Protected winding plus a loss mechanism is delivered. Finite birth/attachment
is delivered. Sustained localized self-restoration under a common autonomous
law remains open. Consensus, vanishing amplitude, exhausted capacity or a
shifted comparison profile do not establish that result.
Exit gate for the next bounded delivery: correct event accounting, admitted
matched continuations and a classified corrective response or obstruction.
Exit gate for a stronger claim: positive-capacity persistence, an invariant
policy/state domain and a declared physical observation map. Two-pattern
interaction and repeated birth follow a justified single-pattern result.

O4 — Which effective laws survive elimination and change of scale?
---------------------------------------------------------------
O4.a owns reduced dynamics and derived memory (S8/S9/E7), sharing state
sufficiency with O1.a. B2.a-c are reusable completed restricted deliveries;
do not reopen exact elimination, P5 truncation or its quotient as future work.
New work needs a named extension: phase branches/resultants, nonlinearity,
time-varying generator, changed support/history or a new justified error bound.

O4.b owns a realizable coupling between derived sectors (S12), with gauge
controls. Derive terms before testing Jacobi, degeneracy, covariance and
balance. A decoupled product and pure gauge do not supply an interaction field.
The fixed-P2 obstruction rules out simply promoting the harmonic substrate.

O4.c owns irreversibility and universality (S6). Graph relaxation is available;
physical entropy, ensembles, fluctuation laws and universal exponents are not.
Signed EPI is not a probability distribution. Derive a statistical/observation
construction before interpreting entropy or adding fluctuations. A finite-size
static-ensemble diagnostic is not an executed critical transition.
Exit gate: one effective law with a TNFR derivation, approximation domain,
error/scale statement and sensitivity controls. Do not add a target law first.

O5 — Is there a derived measurement and quantum-composition bridge?
----------------------------------------------------------------
O5.a owns the operational-state/detector assumptions audit. It consumes the
O1.a observation contract and any O4 derived composition; it must not build a
second competing complete-state project. Current phasors, graph modes,
winding and classical polarization are controls, not quantum probabilities.
Keep exp(i*theta), the geometric field K_phi+i*J_phi and a proposed detector
amplitude distinct. Born/outcome rules, noncommuting measurement structure
and entangled composition have not been derived.
The classical local-response CHSH control retains its bound under its stated
assumptions. Adding local memory does not by itself evade those assumptions.
Exit gate: a TNFR-derived preparation/composition/detector contract, or a precise
obstruction. No quantum-emergence or particle-mass fitting campaign precedes it.

O6 — What can be tested and distinguished in a surface laboratory?
---------------------------------------------------------------
O6.a owns observation/clock identifiability (E8/S15) and empirical protocol
(E9). Reuse signal and manifest interfaces; do not create another calibration
or inverse-model framework before specifying what is observable.
The existing fitted diffusion and signal-comparison routes are diagnostic
baselines. Fitting c=nu_f*dt does not identify both factors or derive a physical
clock; fitting a physical law is not allowed as the TNFR explanation.
Protocol preparation can run beside the mathematical work. A smallest thermal,
mechanical/electrical or acoustic setup is a candidate only; select none until
measurement map, derived prediction, equipment, provenance and uncertainty
are explicit. Laboratory laws may describe comparators, not become TNFR axioms.
Exit gate before acquisition: eligible source, identifiable prediction,
independent unit/instrument calibration, fixed held-out intervention, equally
informed baseline and rejection rule. An equivalent model is a valid outcome;
do not manufacture distinctiveness by weakening the comparator.
