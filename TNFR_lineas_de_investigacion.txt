======================================
Test whether explicitly derived TNFR dynamics can predict formation,
persistence, interaction, recovery and loss of coherent patterns in
independently specified observations. The next structural milestone is a
closed canonical generation mechanism or an exact obstruction in a declared
family; independent physical prediction remains a required subsequent bridge.
Neither milestone would show that all physical reality emerges from TNFR.

would arise as persistent and interacting TNFR coherence patterns rather
than being assigned in advance to fundamental graph nodes. This is the
open research objective, not a demonstrated physical result. Structural
priority is not an assertion of an earlier physical time. The current P1-P5
programme supplies necessary scoped tests; completing it would not by itself
derive physical particles or establish the full hypothesis.

The macro objective exceeds relabeling measurements as EPI or capacity:
derive observable behavior from specified nodal mechanisms and test its
transfer across preparations or scales. Measurement correspondence,
mathematical emergence under projection and physical emergence are separate
claims. Their shared criterion and existing memory witness are centralized
in theory/NODAL_RESEARCH_STRATEGY.md, section "Measurement correspondence
versus generative emergence". P2/P3 establish the bridge; P4/P5 test admitted
reductions and mechanisms. Physical priority or universality remains unproved.

All explanatory mechanisms must follow from the nodal equation and admitted
structural/operator laws. Its factorization alone does not determine pressure,
phase/capacity evolution, support or policy selection. Keep primitives,
constitutive choices, preparation, numerical settings and measurement
calibration distinct. Do not set pressure equal to measured derivative divided
by fitted capacity, or import a physical law to repair a failed derivation.

resources. No astronomy, satellite/space data or mandatory large facilities.

support are separate evidence. A negative result can complete a study without
fulfilling its positive objective. Inconclusive mandatory gates stay open.
