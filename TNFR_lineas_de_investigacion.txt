1. PURPOSE AND DOCUMENT RESPONSIBILITIES
========================================
The primary objective is to determine whether coherent, interacting patterns
and eventually properties of physical constituents can emerge from justified
TNFR structure and dynamics. EPI need not correspond directly to a sensor
reading. A collective measurement bridge may be appropriate, but physical
emergence remains a hypothesis, not an established consequence of the nodal
identity.

An NFR is part of the same structural configuration it affects. Its effective
interactions must be inherited from the admitted fine dynamics, not supplied
again as unexplained forces or extra agents. Support, initial conditions,
capacity laws and constitutive premises must remain explicit.

This file classifies branches and their roles; it contains no separate task
queue, experimental protocol or delivery chronology.
