TNFR - RESEARCH PORTFOLIO
========================

1. PURPOSE AND OWNERSHIP
=======================
Investigate the scope of TNFR: organized structures, their evolution and
interaction, and the properties supported by their composition. Universal
realization, autonomous hierarchy and a uniquely selected law remain open.
Existing organized objects are mathematical instruments for this inquiry:
their sufficient state, complete law, identity, actions and observations make
foundational claims testable under explicit premises. This file classifies
contributions; it is not a task queue, result catalog or history.

  Active question, acceptance and reopening:

  Definitions, proofs, implementation and tests:

2. PRIMARY CONTRIBUTIONS
========================
A. STATE, COMPLETE LAWS AND JUSTIFIED INTERACTION
Determine which state an object and its interactions consume, what predicts
their future, and which independent premises restrict the complete law.
Definitions, constitutive assumptions, derived restrictions and countermodels
are distinct. Native relational, normalized-sine and supplied source models
retain their own laws; a common vocabulary does not make them interchangeable.

  theory/NODAL_PARAMETER_FOUNDATIONS.md#derived-information-ledger
  theory/DIAGNOSTIC_AND_GRAMMAR_SCOPE.md#justified-law-admission
  theory/nodal/RELATION_FOUNDATIONS.md

B. ORGANIZATION, IDENTITY AND DYNAMICAL RESPONSE
Determine what makes a pattern an organized object, which identity forms,
what maintains it and how it responds under the same complete law. Constituent
reorganization and changed trajectories are allowed; overlap and nonlinear
response alone do not establish a common identity. Prepared objects, invariant
families and acquired organizations have different evidence obligations.

  theory/EMERGENT_ONTOLOGY.md
  theory/nodal/SINE_PATTERN_DYNAMICS.md
  theory/nodal/RESONANCE_FOUNDATIONS.md

C. GRAMMAR, OPERATOR ACTION AND OCCURRENCE
Determine which registered actions an actual object admits, what state and
history they consume, and when a collective representation preserves their
ordered composition. An intervention, its grammatical admission and the law
selecting its occurrence are separate. A continuous collective law does not
automatically supply a collective grammar or a complete operator repertoire.

  theory/STRUCTURAL_OPERATORS.md#collective-grammar-descent
  theory/UNIFIED_GRAMMAR_RULES.md
  docs/contracts/OPERATOR_EVENTS.md

D. COMPOSITION, SCALE AND RETAINED MEMORY
Determine which information and dynamics survive grouping or eliminating
constituents. Preserve consumed boundary associations, hidden initialization
and input. Exact charts, invariant families and approximate reductions have
different obligations; nesting alone does not establish emergent scale.

  theory/TNFR_SCALE_GEOMETRY_AND_BRIDGE.md
  theory/nodal/RELATIONAL_PATTERN_COMPOSITION.md
  theory/nodal/RELATIONAL_PATTERN_MEMORY.md
  theory/DERIVED_EPI_MEMORY.md

E. OBSERVATION AND PREDICTIVE INFORMATION
Determine what a readout retains and what it discards from the object's
state and history. Diagnostics, sufficient observations and physical
measurements require different admission. Use matched-state counterexamples
and finite uncertainty bounds to establish which responses can be distinguished;
an observed score or mean is not automatically complete state.

  theory/DIAGNOSTIC_AND_GRAMMAR_SCOPE.md
  theory/MINIMAL_STRUCTURAL_DEGREES.md
  theory/nodal/RELATIONAL_PATTERN_MEMORY.md

3. SUPPORTING METHODS
=====================
Shared numerical admission, execution, diagnostic implementations, proof kernels
and evidence readers serve these mathematical questions. More precision,
repetitions or operator combinations are not independent scientific objectives.
Resource-limited work reopens only with an explicit need and an admitted time,
storage and error budget.

Physical comparison is deferred. It requires independent preparation,
observation, input, clock and uncertainty under P1-P5, with calibration and
reserved evaluation separate. Collective measurement maps are allowed. Use workstation-accessible
terrestrial data; laboratory protocols remain designs until source/apparatus
availability is admitted. No astronomical or large-facility program is active.

  TESTING.md
  docs/guides/RESEARCH_EVIDENCE.md
  theory/research/FIVE_STAGE_EXECUTION_PLAN.md#supporting-measurement-bridge
  theory/PHYSICAL_REGIME_CORRESPONDENCES.md

4. AUXILIARY MATERIAL AND OPEN DEPENDENCIES
==========================================
Arithmetic/Riemann studies, graph waves, field-space Hamiltonians, cotangent
comparisons and gauge constructions retain their own premises. They can supply
methods or counterexamples. Their validity within a supplied model does not
make them necessary foundations for every organized-object study.

Capacity evolution, support birth, clock origin, autonomous preparation and
physical identification become tasks only when the execution plan names a
claim that requires them. Supplied or held premises remain explicit while
deferred. Preserve frozen sources, protocols, responses and negative results;
evaluated observations cannot become reserved evidence again.

  theory/research/FIVE_STAGE_EXECUTION_PLAN.md#deferred-work-and-reopening-conditions
  theory/research/archive/README.md
