1. PURPOSE AND DOCUMENT RESPONSIBILITIES
========================================
The primary objective is to determine whether coherent, interacting patterns
and eventually properties of physical constituents can emerge from justified
TNFR structure and dynamics. EPI need not correspond directly to a sensor
reading. A collective measurement bridge may be appropriate, but physical
emergence remains a hypothesis, not an established consequence of the nodal
identity.

An NFR is part of the same structural configuration it affects. Its effective
interactions must be inherited from the admitted fine dynamics, not supplied
again as unexplained forces or extra agents. Support, initial conditions,
capacity laws and constitutive premises must remain explicit.

This file classifies branches and their roles; it contains no separate task
queue, experimental protocol or delivery chronology.

Role: identify the state and justify the dynamics before claiming emergence.
Status: scoped reference admissions are established; unique foundations are
not selected.

The S reference retains signed scalar form and independent primitive phase;
D observes a restricted transport sector of S. A regular chart change, an
exact symmetry and a lossy observation are different operations. The nodal
identity alone does not determine pressure, phase, capacity, support or events.
Source-relative observations and fixed-law closure criteria now have shared
engine/SDK owners. They retain their supplied-source and domain assumptions.

Connection foundations now distinguish declared support, normalized transport
weight, observation distance and causal dependence. A zero-weight edge is not
necessarily an absent relation, and first-neighbor normalization differs from
contact between already active regions. The relation state and zero-boundary
admissions retain distinct autonomous passive contact completions, without
selecting relation-only storage or kinetics. Fixed-support nodal evolution
cannot determine them. These are reusable foundational constraints, not an
active parallel creation-law search.

The coefficient audit likewise separates conditional model consistency from
constitutive selection. Balance, independent capacity composition, clock
covariance and synchronized replicas permit different chi values. Prepared
response and memory can identify chi within the admitted two-channel family;
they do not derive a universal coefficient or reduce all TNFR freedom to it.
The execution plan alone sets the current calibration/discrimination gate.

C. COMPOSITION, RETAINED INFORMATION AND MEMORY
Role: determine what must be retained when interacting coherent regions are
described at a larger scale.
Status: exact tangent reduction and nonlinear closure obstructions are known;
the bounded derived-memory prediction gate is complete.

For the admitted single-bridge reference, regional mean/port observations
require additional shape coordinates even at first variation. The resulting
coarse state is not a complete nonlinear predictor; adding its instantaneous
rate also fails. The existing full pattern report retains the omitted
internal information.

Exact hidden-state elimination and symmetry give conditional approximation
orders for prepared small perturbations. A frozen finite memory prediction
improved both tangent and direct-cubic controls on matched Euler grids.
That result neither certifies an exact-ODE error at the evaluated amplitude
nor supplies an autonomous compressed node or a fitted memory law.

Retained mechanisms in this branch are classified by what they preserve:
- Attachment and relocation retain port neighborhoods, full state and event
  work. A passive change and subsequent recovery have separate hypotheses;
  neither selects the event or its time.
- Joint resets retain simultaneous nodal/support changes. Actual UM and
  RA-then-UM witnesses are conditional budget results, not autonomous birth.
  The all-operator map owns configured actions and activation boundaries.
- Mediation retains joint form/phase memory and hidden initial information.
  A frozen intermediary can restore geometry without transmitting changes;
  a causal-null control is therefore necessary for an effective relation.
- Effective links combine mutual influence with local restoring geometry on
  supplied fine support. Return paths and damped modes add scoped geometric
  information; a visible or permanent pulse is not required.
- Fast and series reductions retain selected phase lifts, original port
  normalization, inherited storage and the initial transient. Stationary
  composition and finite-capacity evolution have distinct error obligations.
- Three-port reduction retains genuinely collective phase information;
  independent pair interactions cannot generally replace the whole law.
- Pressure-state counterexamples retain the environment's hidden coordinates.
  Zero pressure is not absence of structure, and a physical vacuum remains
  an unestablished interpretation.

These results are linked from their owners below and the theory-to-execution
