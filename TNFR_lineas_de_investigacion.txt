TNFR - RESEARCH PORTFOLIO
========================
Updated: 2026-10-05.

1. PURPOSE AND OWNERSHIP
=======================
The main hypothesis is that coherent, interacting TNFR organizations could
generate effective constituents and eventually explain physical observations.
A larger NFR would retain its smaller constituents and their influence.
Neither this interpretation nor the nodal identity uniquely selects a
complete law, initial substrate, support or physical correspondence.

This file classifies branches and their contributions. It is not a task
queue, proof catalog or delivery log. Open questions are not scheduled work.

2. PRIMARY GENERATIVE LINE - THREE CONNECTED BRANCHES
====================================================

Shared fundamental questions connect these branches: sufficient relational
state, justification of complete exchange, and formation/composition. Their
dependencies and reuse boundaries have one owner, not separate task queues:

  theory/NODAL_RESEARCH_STRATEGY.md#fundamental-research-dependencies

A. STATE, COMPLETE LAWS AND CONSTITUTIVE JUSTIFICATION
Contribution: identify sufficient predictive information and distinguish
derived restrictions from supplied pressure, phase, capacity and support laws.
Status: reference models, consistency restrictions and discriminators exist;
unique microscopic laws, loss selection and substrate origin remain open.

Signed form, circular phase and a derived regional angle have different roles.
Locality, covariance, storage and symmetry constrain models without selecting
all coefficients or events. A chart, a symmetry quotient and a lossy observation
discard different information. A desired outcome does not justify a new law.
Conditional exchange selection connects whole-form balance to odd pairwise
source composition within the graph-uniform incident-gap information class.
Changing phase storage can preserve balance and the consensus tangent, so
the conservation and storage premises still need independent justification.
Compatibility alone does not select a fundamental law.

B. FORMATION, MAINTENANCE AND INTERACTION
Contribution: determine when organization absent from a declared initial
state can form, retain an identity and affect another region.
Status: prepared formation, recovery and scoped exclusions are established
under distinct complete laws. Autonomous preparation and support selection
remain open. A useful finite-lived pattern need not be eternally maintained.

The native Arg-based law has validated prepared formation and separate
consensus/target-exclusion controls. The smooth positive-loss sine law has
prepared winding acquisition, full-state capture, bounded-budget consensus
and an equal-budget symmetry discriminator. They are conditional results,
not interchangeable trajectories. Scalar storage does not specify geometry.
Conservative identity protection and recurrence have different formation
obligations. The strategy's premise ledger owns these cross-model boundaries.

C. COLLECTIVE STATE, COMPOSITION AND MEMORY
Contribution: describe interacting organizations without silently discarding
their internal state, relative origins, environment or event costs.
Status: exact retained-state reductions, conditional composition rules,
hidden-state memory and selected scale inheritance are established. General
hierarchy formation and a closed scalar macro-node law do not follow.

Eliminating an intermediary can generate effective coupling with memory;
hidden initialization and inventory remain part of the prediction. A constant
collective observable need not reveal continuing internal motion. An observed
group or admitted bridge is not a derived support-creation event. Independent
internal descriptions also need relative frame information before they can
be joined. A local nonlinear conserved origin coordinate and primitive linear
form balance have different admission requirements.

3. SUPPORTING BRANCHES
======================

D. MATHEMATICAL AND NUMERICAL ADMISSION
Exact algebra, observability, uncertainty and validated enclosures make the
primary claims checkable. Reuse their shared owners when hypotheses agree.
The theory-to-execution map locates them; TESTING.md owns verification routes.
More trajectories or passing tests alone do not establish a theorem.

E. OBSERVATIONS, OPERATORS AND EVENTS
Diagnostics, grammar admission and continuous/event balances expose what an
execution actually does. The tetrad is not a complete state. Configured
policies and named operators do not derive autonomous occurrence. Definitions
belong to theory/STRUCTURAL_OPERATORS.md, theory/UNIFIED_GRAMMAR_RULES.md and
docs/STRUCTURAL_FIELDS_TETRAD.md; execution belongs to the API contracts.

F. PHYSICAL CONFRONTATION AND COMPARISON ATLAS
P1-P5 requires independent preparation/measurement/clock admission and reserved
predictions. A collective measurement map is allowed; EPI need not be a sensor
reading. No public dataset has passed complete physical admission. TCLab
remains evaluated exploration with bias and identification limits; historical
Volts data do not validate the revised model. Evaluated data cannot be reserved
again. The atlas compares reductions and discriminators, not parallel campaigns.
Use workstation-accessible terrestrial data or ordinary laboratory measurements;
extraterrestrial observations and large-facility programmes are outside scope.

4. AUXILIARY, DEFERRED AND HISTORICAL WORK
========================================
Arithmetic/Riemann studies, graph waves, field-space Hamiltonians, cotangent
comparisons and gauge constructions retain their own premises. They can supply
tools or alternatives, but do not establish particles, quantum theory or an
initial substrate. The exact Poisson/storage representation of the complete
sine law is a same-law identity, distinct from an auxiliary Hamiltonian.

The execution plan alone lists deferred questions and reopening conditions,
including earlier receiver-transfer and runtime-stability campaigns. A retained
benchmark or an open hypothesis does not start another search. Valid conditional
results and negative controls remain useful; their original protocols, source
archives, responses and verdicts must not be rewritten or replayed for cleanup.
