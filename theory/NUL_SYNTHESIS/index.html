    Contraction, retained synthesis and response sufficiency¶

    This chapter owns the actual Contraction (NUL)/Reception (EN) fragment, its concentration law, amplification/contraction composition and the information needed for continued interaction. The operator specification owns the member/port interface and instantaneous scalar-reduction obstruction. The research program selects the next implementation question.

    Retained concentration through actual NUL and EN¶

    Historical NUL synthesis motivates integration toward a core while retaining diversity and memory. Neither a scalar average nor a renamed coordinate chart executes that function. The retained concentration interface defines a bounded, supplied operator fragment: actual simultaneous NUL on an ordered pair (core, member), then actual EN at the core with that member as its sole explicitly selected input. NUL alone is the matched comparison. This uses existing actions and factors, not an unrecorded change to NUL or a new token. The current domains remain 0<f<1 for NUL_scale and 0<m<=1 for EN_mix; an EN event with zero mixing is not an admitted control.

    capture_retained_pair retains the owner-bound member/port observation and adds exact rational form_mean q and form_contrast d from its admitted binary64 forms. The ordered roles need no inferred ancestry. The real-value identities xp=q+d, xc=q-d do not make q,d a complete state: capacities, pressure, phases, kinds, individual contexts and ports remain in their original records. The additional reception_kinds retains the strings actually read by EN: an absent kind gives '', whereas an explicit None gives 'None'. This differs from the generic retained member view, which normalizes both to ''; execution re-admits the extra channel against live aliases without changing EN's existing semantics. Two floats cannot always store the exact chart. With xp the least positive subnormal and xc=0, both halves are nonzero exact rationals but round to zero as floats. Original records also retain signed-zero association that a rational zero cannot encode.

    execute_retained_synthesis_stage re-admits the pair and requires a realized member-to-core arc or their undirected edge. A core-to-member arc alone is insufficient; the fragment neither inserts a reverse edge nor invokes UM. Runtime uses the actual nodes and kernels, never forms reconstructed from a rounded chart. The core's selected EN omits external donors from this one read without removing their ports. All own grammar and strict policies still apply. No callback, source override or replacement glyph is accepted.

    One graph-owned transaction covers both substages and their observations. Support/edge data, phases and nonmember state remain unchanged; core EN also preserves the member's post-NUL state. Each actual event updates only its own targets' context. The result retains before, contracted, after, the real contraction result and the real reception result. These are associated state and execution summaries, not a checkpoint or authenticated invocation proof. No hierarchy, member deletion, autonomous occurrence or elapsed flow is added.

    Concentration and its information boundary¶

    In the ideal same-kind scalar branch with edge-aware form scaling and inactive projection, write xp=q+d, xc=q-d. NUL alone gives (f*q,f*d); the compound fragment instead gives

    xp' = f*((1-m)*xp+m*xc),  xc' = f*xc
q'  = f*(q-m*d),         d'  = f*(1-m)*d.

    Its extra core response versus NUL alone is -2*f*m*d. Subsequent admitted RA at an external peer with only the core as form donor and mixing r changes by -2*r*f*m*d. Thus the added internal EN has a distinct causal effect. The absolute form contrast contracts. For nonzero forms of the same sign, rho=d/q lies in (-1,1) and becomes (1-m)*rho/(1-m*rho), also decreasing its magnitude when nonzero. Opposite signs or zero mean do not inherit that normalized guarantee; neither statement is a universal coherence theorem.

    When edge awareness is disabled, the form factor in these equations is one, while capacities still scale by f and stored pressures by its reciprocal. Active projection, kind changes and represented rounding retain their actual maps. Exact rational endpoint observations do not certify an exact binary64 matrix identity for arbitrary coefficients. Phase is held by both actions; the ideal stored product nu*p is preserved by NUL, without implying its preservation after an independent pressure refresh or across form jumps.

    The ideal form determinant is f^2*(1-m). At m=1 both current forms equal f*xc, erasing the previous core form from that pair. A before-record does not make the live reset invertible or derive an internal memory law. For m<1 the ideal form map is invertible, but that does not invert context, clipping or admission. Common NUL and EN commute in ideal unclipped form coordinates; their different event orders still have different histories and can encounter different intermediate admission. No order is uniquely derived.

    Same mean is insufficient even for this fragment. (xp,xc)=(3/4,1/4) and (1/4,3/4) share q but, at f=1/2,m=1/4, give core forms 5/16 and 3/16. An external peer can distinguish them. Sum of forms changes by 2*(f-1)*q-2*f*m*d; no conserved scalar resource or energy is assumed. The member remains addressable with its own state and ports, but preservation of every past form contrast or historical reabsorption has not been proved.

    The interface controls cover association, exact chart, direction, protected state and rollback. The finite continuation controls use actual derived VAL and UM preparation, NUL-only comparison and subsequent RA. On the supplied dyadic source (3/4,1/4), two compound actions with f=1/2,m=1/4 and explicit DNFR_RECEPTION_MAX=3/4 give (5/16,1/8) then (17/128,1/16). The core pressure rises from 1/8 to 1/4 then 1/2; the unchanged default EN ceiling instead rejects the first post-NUL EN. The selected ceiling is a supplied policy, not a concentration theorem. In general, enabled strict EN admission requires the actual post-NUL signed pressure below that ceiling (ideally p_core/f < DNFR_RECEPTION_MAX), besides its other conditions. This is not an absolute-pressure bound. A third attempt in the admitted control fails the unchanged default NUL member-EPI threshold 1/10 and restores the source. This is policy rejection, not nonexistence of the member. Neither NUL nor EN discharges the core's preceding VAL debt. These finite interventions establish an elementary concentration mechanism, not persistent organization or complete canonical synthesis.

    Concentration and a response-sufficient representation¶

    The historical TNFR.pdf pp109-112 describes reabsorption while retaining the constituents' diversity and active memory. Its unspecified merge_structures does not define a deletion rule, conservation identity or sufficient successor state. Three operations must be distinguished: actual concentration can change a response; encapsulation can retain an interior behind an interface; a lossy reduction discards information. Calling each of these synthesis does not make their contracts interchangeable. The existing retained fragment implements the first and retains state needed to investigate the second.

    For a declared family of partial actions T_a and observation pi, exact effective replacement requires the permitted action domains and observed successors to factor through pi. In particular, pi(s)=pi(t) must imply the same admissibility and, when admitted, pi(T_a(s))=pi(T_a(t)) for each allowed action. Iteration then preserves the corresponding observations along finite allowed words. An external-output version must specify its boundary actions and observations at every prefix. One equal output is insufficient. This is the contextual replacement criterion specialized to the intended synthesis, not an engine certificate or a proof that the present pair observation is a minimal complete state.

    Equal first output can conceal a later response difference¶

    Fix the undirected path peer--core--child, same-kind positive scalar forms, compatible phases, the actual policies and each action's grammar admission. Let x,c,v be the forms after the concentration fragment. In the unclipped restriction, execute Resonance (RA) at the peer with factor r, ordinary Reception (EN) at the core with factor m, and another RA at the peer:

    v1 = (1-r)*v + r*x
x1 = (1-m)*x + (m/2)*(v1+c)
v2 = (1-r)*v1 + r*x1.

    For two such states with common x,v, a retained-child difference dc leaves the first boundary output v1 identical but changes v2 by r*m*dc/2. Therefore the current exported core form and even one exact boundary response cannot replace the child for this future word. This temporal obstruction does not repeat the instantaneous rank-two result, prohibit a restricted quotient, or exclude a representation that retains sufficient internal state or memory.

    The finite response controls use declared initial pairs A=(3/4,1/4) and B=(1/2,1/2), peer 3/8, and actual NUL/selected EN with f=m=1/2. Both initial means are 1/2, but the complete states differ. The fragment gives (core,child)=(1/4,1/8) versus (1/4,1/4). With future r=m=1/2, the independent exact predictions are:

    Boundary word prefix A B
    First peer RA, v1 5/16 5/16
    Subsequent core EN, x1 15/64 17/64
    Second peer RA, v2 35/128 37/128

    The final difference is 1/64. These controls execute the actual owners with strict admission; the forms are not advanced by a replacement matrix. Other RA effects, including capacity, retain the real implementation. The equations are a conditional form-channel identity; exact dyadic control arithmetic does not establish exact equality for arbitrary binary64 policies.

    An actually born child can enter the concentration fragment¶

    The same response fixture also connects existing operators end to end. Actual IL/OZ/AL produces the physical source history for eligible THOL birth; the live association consumer obtains the isolated child for actual UM. The source and its chronology are owned by the birth continuation. The child has its own fresh context, not the parent's measured history.

    Three actual parent IL actions reduce pressure from 9/16 to 9/128. NUL at f=1/2 gives 9/64, below the unchanged default EN ceiling 0.15. The pre-NUL child form 15/128 also exceeds the unchanged NUL form floor 0.1. No lowered threshold or pressure assignment forces admission. Selected core EN uses m=1/2. The actual NUL-only comparison retains the same source, support and preparation:

    Observation NUL only NUL then selected EN
    Core after fragment 25/128 65/512
    Child after fragment 15/256 15/256
    Peer after RA 73/256 257/1024
    Core after ordinary EN 47/256 577/4096
    Peer after second RA 15/64 1605/8192

    The initial peer form is 3/8 and subsequent r=m=1/2. These are distinct admitted responses under the same declared continuation. NUL's actual capacity and reciprocal-pressure changes, retained ports, phase, child identity and own context remain part of the executed state. Instantaneous actions do not add elapsed time or current physical samples; old samples can be stale after form jumps. Source preparation, invocation occasions and coefficients are supplied. This integration establishes neither persistent concentration nor autonomous occurrence, but extends synthesis participation to an actually born child.

    Numerical agreement does not erase own grammatical memory¶

    The independent memory controls start with a declared equal-form pair and acquire different child context through actual Coherence (IL). Both branches first execute IL at the core; this common preparation supplies no handler context to the child. With both forms 1/2, capacities 1, zero pressure and phase, actual NUL(f=1/2) and full selected EN(m=1) leave both forms 1/4 and capacities 1/2. In one matched branch, an actual child IL at zero pressure and zero phase-locking gain retains these primitive coordinates and support while adding its own handler context.

    A subsequent actual Dissonance (OZ) request has the same numerical domain in both branches: form 1/4 and capacity 1/2 satisfy the ordinary floors and pressure zero satisfies its ceiling. The branch without child IL must reject U4a; the branch with its own IL can execute. NUL's separate role as a resolver in OZ's bounded overload policy does not make NUL an IL/THOL grammar handler. The admitted OZ retains its real near-zero pressure rule, not a presumed zero product. Rejection must preserve the pre-request state and must not substitute another glyph. This is acquired grammar memory affecting a later action, not a proof that arbitrary past form information was retained.

    The useful design conclusion is to retain internal state, addressed ports and own action context until a specified replacement preserves the requested continuations. Neither current form agreement nor a first matching response authorizes pooling histories or removing constituents. A complete encapsulated NFR, its autonomous action selection and any justified lossy reduction remain separate implementation and mathematical obligations.

    Addressed external input with retained interior state¶

    The retained Reception consumer adds an executable input boundary to the retained organization. Its input addresses are derived from member order and each member's incoming neighbor order, excluding every internal neighbor. A caller selects an ordinal and an invocation occasion; the consumer resolves the actual member and external source, then executes the existing Reception (EN) stage. It accepts no separate target/source pair. An address is an interface to an actual constituent, not a macro-node coordinate, a new operator or an inferred occurrence law.

    Direction and state ownership matter. A reverse-only directed arc supplies no input, and parallel edges retain their attributes but represent one unique donor. The owner-bound observation must still match all member state, own context and incident support when consumed. A changed member or incident edge requires a new capture. External donor form and kind are deliberately live: the shared EN snapshot reads them at invocation. A passive address table is neither live authorization nor a cached input payload. The returned after observation can support another invocation after fresh admission; a consumed before observation becomes stale when the member's own context changes.

    Only the addressed member receives EN. Its own grammar and the actual EN admission policy remain authoritative; other members supply no grammar credit. The operation preserves every other node, support, phase, target capacity and target pressure. Target form/kind, its own event bookkeeping and ordinary graph telemetry may change. One graph-owned transaction covers the action and its postconditions. Later Resonance (RA) is a separately admitted action, outside that transaction. The API contract owns exact options, rejection and association boundaries.

    Independent interior and live-input response¶

    The four finite controls use a declared directed graph with retained members (core, child), internal arcs in both directions, donor -> child and peer -> core. Address zero is (core, peer) and address one is (child, donor). The label child here is a supplied member role, not a newly claimed THOL birth. Actual IL acquires core, child and peer context. Actual NUL at f=1/2 followed by selected core EN at m=1/2 prepares initial form pairs A=(3/4,1/4) and B=(1/2,1/2) into (x,c)=(1/4,1/8) and (1/4,1/4). Capacities become 1/2; pressure and phase remain zero. The peer form is v=3/8 and the external donor begins at d=1/4.

    After capture, a matched donor-only intervention executes instantaneous Emission (AL) with boost 1/8, producing d=3/8. This changes no member state and leaves the retained input address admissible. It supplies no elapsed time, flow-derived rate or acceleration sample. A finite-duration episode would have a different history boundary and cannot silently replace this jump.

    Addressed EN at the child uses only the live donor, despite the child's additional internal incoming neighbor. Actual RA then acts at the core and finally at the peer, reading their respective outgoing singleton neighbors. With the declared same-kind scalar domain, inactive projection and blends m=r=s=1/2, the ideal affine maps are

    c_new = (1-m)*c + m*d
x_new = (1-r)*x + r*c_new
v_new = (1-s)*v + s*x_new.

    The following dyadic controls are exactly represented; the formulas do not assert exact real arithmetic for arbitrary binary64 inputs.

    Interior Donor AL Live donor Child after EN Core after RA Peer after RA
    A absent 1/4 3/16 7/32 19/64
    B absent 1/4 1/4 1/4 5/16
    A present 3/8 1/4 1/4 5/16
    B present 3/8 5/16 9/32 21/64

    Subtracting these affine rows gives Delta(v_new)=s*r*(1-m)*Delta(c) at fixed x,v,d, and Delta(v_new)=s*r*m*Delta(d) at fixed x,v,c. Each selected difference is 1/64. Thus equal exported core form does not determine this response, while freezing the donor payload would erase a distinct input effect. This is a conditional response discriminator, not a universal minimal-state theorem.

    The tests retain actual RA capacity amplification: final core capacity 9/16, peer capacity 9/8 and child capacity 1/2. Histories remain individual: the core has IL,NUL,EN,RA; the child IL,NUL,EN; the peer IL,RA; the donor has no action or its own AL. No copied history, pooled debt or temporal sample supplies admission. The interface controls separately cover addresses, live kind/form, stale records, policies and rollback. This specifies the bounded input-routing function, while autonomous collective identity, complete synthesis and cross-scale replacement remain open.

    Amplification, contraction and participant-supported maintenance¶

    The reciprocal return exposes capacity growth under repeated Resonance (RA). Contraction (NUL) modifies more than capacity, so a compensating factor must be assessed through the complete action. The existing neighbor, pointwise and Coupling (UM) stages already compose these operators with live grammar; no additional operator or replacement evolution law is needed for the following conditional analysis.

    Complete RA--NUL rows and the neutral-capacity obstruction¶

    Consider a same-kind positive scalar target (x,c,p,theta) with one compatible neighbor of form u and phase phi. There is no inter-event flow or pressure refresh. All stages use their actual strict preconditions and own grammar, with substitution rejected. Assume active RA amplification (u > 1e-9), mixing 0<m<=1, amplification a>0, phase coefficient 0<beta<=1, NUL factor 0<f<1, edge-aware form scaling and inactive hard clipping. On a circular chart with d=theta-phi strictly inside the U3 sector, one actual RA followed by NUL has ideal numerical rows

    x' = f*((1-m)*x + m*u),   c' = q*c,   q = f*(1+a),
p' = p/f,                d' = (1-beta)*d.

    Support and the neighbor's primitive state remain unchanged in this fragment. Only the target records its own RA,NUL events. Each prefix must retain the active trigger, sign/kind, representability, configured phase and strict capacity/form/pressure/density bounds. NUL's minimum capacity is strict; its form threshold is signed, whereas RA's uses magnitude. Passing an entry guard does not establish admission of a later request.

    For a fixed neighbor, the ideal form row contracts toward f*m*u/(1-f*(1-m)). This does not control capacity or pressure. Conditional on remaining in this branch, c_k=q**k*c_0: q below one loses any uniform positive lower bound, q above one loses any finite upper bound, and q equal to one retains every initial capacity without attraction. Independently, p_k=p_0/f**k. A nonzero pressure therefore leaves any fixed pressure bound; the exact plane p=0 is not robust against pressure perturbations. In particular, the neutral choice f=1/(1+a) preserves capacity while amplifying pressure. The stored nodal product across the fragment is (1+a)*c*p, even though NUL alone preserves that product ideally. These are obstructions to the stated maintenance domain, not claims about execution after a guard has rejected.

    The same capacity/pressure obstruction survives disabling edge-aware form scaling; only the form factor f disappears.     The source and closed-population boundaries¶

    If actual NUL first contracts the neighbor, its form and capacity become u=3/8, M=1/2, changing the next receiver return. UM must use that live M, and RA that live u. From x=3/8,c=1/2, the uncontracted source gives x'=9/32,c'=59/128; the contracted source gives x'=3/16,c'=43/128. These are distinct complete preparations, not a later change to a frozen law.

    If instead every node in a finite closed population receives the same active RA gain and NUL factor before UM, all capacities first acquire q. UM capacity proposals are convex combinations of those post-NUL capacities, and IL does not change them. Thus max(c_after)<=q*max(c_before). For q=9/16, repeated complete rounds cannot retain a uniformly positive capacity scale throughout that population. In the symmetric two-node case each capacity is exactly q**k*c_0 ideally; the form also halves each round in this branch. This is a law-specific obstruction, not a theorem excluding all closed TNFR organizations. Different roles, active branches, factors, ordering or state-dependent occurrence require a new complete return and admission argument.

    The reference-supported return therefore identifies useful participation of four actual operators while exposing the missing closed-system dependency. It establishes neither autonomous occurrence nor acquired collective identity, memory-preserving synthesis, a universal balance or physical identification.

    Admission, reference preservation and ordered return¶

    The partial-action criterion already includes consumed participant state, own grammar, policy and execution path. For a declared family F of marked actions, write A_F(s)={a in F: s in D_a} for actual admission and V_K,F(s)={a in A_F(s): T_a(s) in K} for one-step preservation of a reference region K. Incremental grammar admission alone is weaker than membership in A_F(s). A returned proposal alone proves neither predicate.

    An ordered return instead requires every actual prefix to be admitted in its declared execution envelope E and its terminal state to belong to K. Prefixes may leave K. This uses the existing composite domain D_a intersect inverse_image(T_a,D_b), not a second grammar or executor. A required response, such as a future actual Resonance (RA) reading a live participant, is another explicit obligation. Finite reachability, existence of an infinite continuation and success for every choice have different quantifiers. A rejected RA does not imply that no canonical action is available.

    For an observation Q to decide preservation of a marked action, admission and the truth of T_a(s) in K must agree for all compared states with equal Q. Exact response requires the stronger existing action-descent condition. The current Default selector's Si/normalized-pressure/acceleration projection does not generally meet this preservation requirement. Its heuristic is not specified to certify an arbitrary reference domain. Native lag priorities and direct-glyph execution are separate consumers; applying a Default proposal through an atomic neighbor stage is an explicitly supplied composition.

    Noncommuting form transport and live prefix admission¶
