    Theory-to-execution map¶

    This map routes a model or responsibility to shared code and representative checks. The linked mathematical owner retains definitions, hypotheses and result-specific evidence; this page does not repeat its theorem inventory. Architecture owns dispatch, Testing owns test selection and commands, and the SDK guide owns usage.

    Detached reports, exact prepared-family assessments and validated forecasts have different inputs and guarantees. Sine readers do not change native execution. Tangent, nonlinear and finite-response evidence remain distinct; an SDK adapter is not another law or a complete checkpoint. Case-study links provide access to retained evidence without reopening their campaigns.
