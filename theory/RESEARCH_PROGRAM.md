# TNFR research program: organization, dynamics and predictive state

<a id="research-objective"></a>
## Scientific objective

Determine which justified states, laws and interactions allow organized
structures to form, persist, change and compose, and which information predicts
their response. The engine, conditional mathematics and retained experiments
make these questions concrete. Universal realization, autonomous hierarchy,
a uniquely selected fundamental law and physical identification remain open.

An organized object is an instrument for investigating those questions. Its
support, preparation, identity and complete law make a claim decidable under
explicit premises. A successful construction establishes that scoped result;
it does not make its graph, coefficients or event schedule mandatory for TNFR.
The [ontology](EMERGENT_ONTOLOGY.md) distinguishes graph birth, regional
formation and a sufficient effective description. None substitutes for another.

This program and its linked chapters form one maintained research account.
The program owns orientation and the current decision; each technical chapter
owns its definitions, proofs, counterexamples, protocols or implementation
contracts. Published pages are generated from those canonical Markdown files.
Verified corrections govern current claims. Superseded errors, duplicate
overviews and completed task logs belong in Git history; valid negative results
and the evidence needed to assess a correction remain part of the research.
Some protocol chapters retain evaluated prospective prefixes verbatim,
including historical references to retired plans. Those references preserve
evidence context; this program alone owns the current task. Historical study
instructions do not activate work. The
[theory-to-execution map](README.md#theory-to-execution) locates code and tests.

<a id="research-selection"></a>
## How the next question is selected

Begin with the scientific consequence, then compare candidate questions on
three grounds. **Impact:** would the answer establish or exclude a missing
organizational capability, or resolve a premise consumed by several results?
**Discrimination:** can a proof, matched counterexample or admitted observation
separate the live alternatives? **Feasibility:** can that separation be reached
with available sources, mathematical methods and a credible total budget?

These are reasons to compare explicitly, not numerical scores. Prefer a
question that advances the same capability with fewer unsupported premises or
a clearer discriminator. Reject an infeasible evaluation even when its target
is important; name the prerequisite instead. Ease of extending recent code,
a larger repetition count or an attractive visualization is insufficient.

For the selected question:

1. Name the object, intended capability and observation; distinguish formation,
   retention, prediction, action and occurrence.
2. Inventory the sufficient state, complete law, source and relevant existing
   results. Include boundary associations and every discarded coordinate.
3. Try an analytical obstruction or equivalence before adding computation.
   Check conserved quantities, symmetry, accessibility and observational ambiguity.
4. Choose the smallest discriminating claim. Record the competing explanations,
   acceptance conditions, resource limits and what an unavailable result means.
5. Freeze any new evaluation prospectively, preserving inspected evidence as
   prior information. Run only the admitted calculation and affected controls.
6. Close with a proof, counterexample or named missing premise. Reconsider the
   global priorities before selecting another extension of the same example.

## Retained research candidate: collective identity after birth and contact

**Can the existing parent-child composition support a prospectively specified
relational identity and subsequent response that distinguish collective
organization from admitted birth, connection and inherited branch geometry?**

If selected after the documentation gate, the task is analytical admission
of one such identity/response contract, or a precise obstruction or missing premise. It does not activate
a new event, response producer, trajectory, parameter search or repeat of the
retained hierarchy evidence. The starting object is the existing 21-node
parent-child composition; the later isolated descendant is separate evidence.

Re-admit the actual source, support, conductances, positive capacities, phase
lifts, clock and complete law from the retained THOL/UM records. Choose and
declare the continuous segment being discussed: UM changes support and
Silence changes capacities, so their continuations are different parameter
states. The weighted conservative two-row law supplies no positive loss.
The dissipative 45-node source and its child-relaxation theorem cannot supply
missing contraction, constants or formation evidence for this object.

The contract must specify a relational observation with a domain before and
after the support change. An unavailable pre-birth observation is not a
measured absence. Identity must have a justified width and signal floor,
an absence criterion, an entry criterion, a declared lifetime and a continued
response without resetting the acquired state. The
[common-organization contract](nodal/LOCALIZED_ORGANIZATION_GEOMETRY.md#common-pattern-formation-contract)
provides those obligations; its localized source and coefficients do not transfer.

Compare against the corresponding disconnected continuation and any equally
informed alternative needed by the claim. Connectedness, nonlinear response,
transient overlap or a common label alone cannot be the discriminator.
Check compatibility with the actual storage, both means, phase geometry and
complete rows before proposing a finite evaluation. Conservative volume and
recurrence obstructions retain their domains; they neither promise permanent
attraction here nor exclude every finite-lived or internally moving identity.

State separately what predicts the selected response. Test proposed collective
coordinates against fine-state ambiguity; retain hidden initialization,
boundary association or derived memory when necessary. A sufficient full-state
description can establish an organization without a reduced autonomous law.
Failure of a proposed reduction answers that information question, not the
existence question.

Completion is one admitted contract with its analytical feasibility and exact
remaining evidence requirements, or a scoped obstruction that explains which
premise must change. No formation verdict follows merely from this admission.
A future evaluation needs its own prospective freeze; existing inspected
responses cannot become reserved evidence for a newly chosen identity.

The symmetric-core tracking result is closed. A hybrid extension through
a word would require reset comparison, changed-capacity dynamics and a joint
mean/error budget. It is a deferred technical dependency, reopened only if the
selected organizational prediction consumes it.

Constitutive uniqueness requires independent information that distinguishes
retained countermodels. A matched equilibrium, tangent, storage balance or
successful organization does not select the nonlinear law. Conditional
formation research can proceed while that uniqueness question remains open.

capacity arguments need not remain uniform near zero. Stored pressure,
constitutive refresh, predicted derivatives and acquired temporal samples are
different evidence. A child inherits no parent's measured history. Continuous
loss, support-event work and an independently supplied reservoir are separate
balances. Missing observations remain unavailable.

## Frozen evidence and resource constraints

Keep source archives, prospective declarations, responses, failures and verdicts
unchanged. Corrections have separate records. Hashes associate bytes; they do
not authenticate acquisition or chronology. Routine regression and editorial
reorganization do not regenerate research producers. The
[evidence guide](../docs/guides/RESEARCH_EVIDENCE.md) and
[archive index](research/archive/README.md) retain recovery responsibilities.

Two deferred numerical boundaries deserve an explicit reminder:

- The [relative-coupling freeze](research/RELATIVE_COUPLING_PROBE.md#5-frozen-declaration-and-numerical-boundary)
  remains unevaluated: four nominal twenty-coordinate states, sixteen steps
  of `1/4096` per branch, order twelve and outward dyadic128 arithmetic.
  The single bounded attempt allows 120 wall seconds per branch, 512 MiB
  memory and a 32 MiB response; no retries or changed steps/order.
- The [canonical variational study](nodal/LOCALIZED_RETENTION_MECHANISMS.md#canonical-variational-first-panel)
  retains its pi/4 carrier, periodic `m=1/2` profile, phase time and error class.
  Apply gain 512 once to accumulated initial error, defects and joins. The
  target remains `2^-24`, pre-gain allowance at most `2^-34`, with analytical
  tails separate. Preserve signed cancellation, both rows, endpoint carry,
  every residual degree and independent joins; admit total cost before reopening.

Other completed studies keep their own exact budgets in their technical
chapters and immutable protocols. An allowance cannot be borrowed from a
different source or observation. Unavailable within budget is not a negative
physical result; a failed sufficient certificate is not a nonexistence proof.
