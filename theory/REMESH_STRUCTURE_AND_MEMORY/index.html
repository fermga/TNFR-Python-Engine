    Recursivity: structure, memory and post-birth participation¶

    Recursivity (REMESH) historically concerns an organized pattern expressed again across scales. Its current executable fragments are narrower: a grammar-aware advisory, a separately invoked temporal EPI map, and separate topology/identity helpers. This chapter distinguishes those functions, clarifies local versus collective memory, and owns the finite post-birth participation contract. It does not declare a completed canonical REMESH law. The operator owner, fixed-delay derivation and collective sine specialization retain their existing maps and mathematical hypotheses. The program alone selects work.

It matches the source retained by the earlier canonical-operator audit. The locators below are one-based PDF pages; inspected printed footers agree.     This gives REMESH a differentiated historical function: transporting or re-expressing organized behavior, potentially including operator compositions, across levels. It does not make REMESH exempt from grammar, a controller of all other operators, or a proven irreducible fourteenth execution layer. Expansion and Self-organization also have constructive roles; their relation to REMESH requires a realization or an explicit constitutive distinction.

    Memory is not assigned exclusively to REMESH even in this source. Page 111 associates Contraction (NUL) with stable synthesis of experience; p.108 also links Expansion (VAL) with transmission of an operative pattern. Those passages motivate different roles in acquiring, retaining and reusing organization. They do not identify all memory with a delayed list of numbers. The inspected historical passages do not specify a unique local/global two-delay memory law. The present runtime formula must therefore retain its status as a particular implemented constitutive fragment, not a deduction from those passages.

    Historical algebra also needs independent checking. Pages 226-228 sketch operator identities and closure, including idempotence and Silence as an identity. These are not literal identities of the current state/context maps. The contextual-action analysis owns the applicable restrictions. Historical motivation cannot silently replace the live grammar or eliminate consumed context.

    Contraction, Expansion and structural re-expression¶

    VAL and NUL belong to one implemented family of target-local scale actions, but are not inverse complete maps. On the ideal edge-aware branch with inactive boundary adaptation/projection, VAL_g sends (x,nu,p) to (g*x,g*nu,p), whereas NUL_f sends it to (f*x,f*nu,p/f), with g>1 and 0<f<1. Reciprocal factors can restore form and capacity after either order, while pressure remains p/f. With edge awareness disabled, form is simply unchanged. Default factors are not reciprocal. Rounding, clipping and strict prefix admission further restrict reversal; each invocation retains its own context, and NUL does not discharge VAL's U2 debt. These are state-changing events, not passive coordinate transformations. The shared kernel and retained-member contract own these maps; the pressure asymmetry is not silently replaced by a new law.

    Their historical constructive roles complement REMESH's proposed re-expression. Ordinary VAL/NUL preserves membership and ports. The separate derived-member VAL family adds an isolated successor with explicit inheritance and fresh context; the NUL/EN fragment concentrates form toward a core while retaining members and ports. Neither supplies a general memory-preserving merge or inherited executed history. REMESH still requires an explicit correspondence of organization, boundary response and own admission across its re-expression. Actual admitted VAL/NUL and other operators may participate in that realization; rescaling coordinates or restoring one form value does not establish it. This is a dependency of the same program question, not another research queue.

    What local and global memory mean in the current engine¶

    Retained channel Actual role What it does not establish
    Per-node epi_time_history and legacy epi_history Associated form samples consumed under specific time/step contracts, including acceleration or Mutation admission Another node's history, or a population-wide REMESH snapshot; a single birth value gives no elapsed-time secant
    Own glyph history and persistent grammar state Past occurrences, stabilization/debt and depth context used for future admission A global pooled allowance; one member's successful action does not credit its siblings
    Birth associations, lifecycle and retained organization records Explicit membership/access and source-bound observations of primitive state, own context and ports Authenticated ancestry merely from metadata, or a proved persistent collective identity
    Graph _epi_hist Indexed whole-population snapshots, each mapping a node to that node's EPI A spatial average, a single collective EPI, a memory of every possible scale, or proof that supplied entries were acquired
    Structural signatures and identity/topology helpers in remesh.py Separately requested similarity, grouping, propagation or support-change policies Implicit effects of the advisory or the delayed map; universal identity preservation

    In the delayed map, both local and global read the same node from the same _epi_hist at two sample offsets. Defaults are tau_local=4 and tau_global=8; they can be configured. These are not two separate stores, one for a constituent and another for its enclosing organization. Nor does global average the network. The coefficient is resolved through the existing configuration/factor precedence, not inferred from the labels of those delays.

    For fixed admitted coefficient a, delays, clipping and no callback, the raw row for node i is

    x_i_new = (1-a)^2*x_i + a*(1-a)*H[k-tau_local][i]
                         + a*H[k-tau_global][i].

    The shared sampler appends the current pre-map state, and delay tau is read at history[-(tau+1)]. Here k indexes sampling occasions. Equal timestamps can contain different states separated by actual operator jumps; this does not turn an index delay into a positive physical duration. The map changes EPI over all current nodes, preserving capacity, phase, pressure and support before any separately declared refresh. It appends no delayed sample itself.

    At this fixed configuration, equal current/selected historical vectors give the same raw echo even on different supports with the same node identities. Yet EN/RA admission and response can differ because they consume realized relations. The row alone therefore cannot define structural re-expression or guarantee its preservation of interaction. Likewise, a configurable similarity score does not supply the missing identity theorem.

    A precise reading of the local/collective-memory intuition¶

    The useful research hypothesis is that REMESH can re-express an organization while respecting both members' own memory and the retained relational state needed for collective behavior. This is broader than temporal echo and remains unresolved. Local/global are relative to a declared organization and scale, not absolute labels that determine a universal memory hierarchy.

    Start with an organized state containing member identities, interior support, external ports, primitive coordinates, own histories/context and the boundary inputs needed for its actions. A collective memory may be distributed across that state; no extra scalar register or network average is presumed sufficient. The NUL response and memory analysis already demonstrates why equal exported form can hide different later responses and admission.

    For a declared family of admissible words and observations, a sufficient retained representation must determine both whether each word can execute and what the selected response will be. Preservation must hold at every consumed prefix, not only at the final plotted EPI. This reuses the existing partial-action/grammar descent requirement rather than introducing a second theory of grammatical credit.

    A candidate re-expression thus needs an explicit correspondence of members, ports and observations, rules for retaining or generating memory, and actual operator realization. Reusing an ancestor's pattern does not imply that newly created constituents lived through the ancestor's events. Existing members may retain their own valid past; genuinely new members require fresh context. If information is deliberately discarded, its effect on future response and admission must be bounded or shown irrelevant in the selected domain.

    These are prerequisites for a future REMESH law, not a proposed universal map or a completed cross-scale proof. The program selects the first bounded discriminator; this chapter does not activate an additional research queue.

    Post-birth sampling and existing admission¶

    The shared source helper uses actual Coherence (IL), Dissonance (OZ), two finite Emission (AL) episodes, eligible Self-organization (THOL), birth-associated Coupling (UM), and further IL. The child exists before positive REMESH sampling begins. This is an explicit observation initialization, not an automatic memory-reset policy.

    There are two distinct existing read domains:

    • The direct delayed map returns insufficient_history until at least max(tau_local,tau_global)+1 rows exist. It then admits the two selected lag rows against current support and signed scalar form.
    • The event/REMESH cycle admits every retained row against current node support before the schedule. It requires unchanged ordered node identities throughout that schedule, appends one actual pre-map row, then invokes mixing.

    Consequently a genuinely captured pre-birth row can invalidate the cycle even after later samples make the direct map's selected rows complete. This is a stronger contract, not permission to pad the old row with a child's current value. Adding samples does not immediately discard that old row. A new member also cannot be born inside the unchanged fixed-node cycle contract.

    The admission controls retain these distinctions, actual sampling, the no-history advisory, genuine birth rejection inside the cycle, and rollback after a late observer fault. The late-fault control uses a callback registry normalized before cycle entry. The prepared source with a still-dirty registry rejected earlier at callback normalization; the passing late-fault control does not certify that transition. They do not implement a reset, history repair, automatic occurrence or a new interpretation of the old delayed-map APIs.

    Finite continued participation through actual operators¶

    The source begins with an ordered parent-peer edge, form 1/4, capacity 1, pressure 1/2 and phase zero at each node. Actual IL/OZ and two Euler AL episodes of duration 1/8 acquire samples at times 0,1/8,1/4. Between episodes, parent pressure changes from 1/2 to 5/8 through actual IL/OZ; peer pressure stays 1/2. Their observed accelerations are respectively 1 and 0. At the supplied threshold 1/2, actual eligible THOL creates one isolated child, then actual birth-access UM connects it. Further IL leaves pressures (9/128,1/8,0). The selected source, occurrence occasions and inherited coefficients remain supplied premises, not autonomous formation laws.

    Both arms of the response control acquire the same first live post-birth snapshot:

    H0 = (parent, peer, child) = (25/64, 3/8, 15/128).

    Actual instantaneous AL at the child adds 1/8. Both arms execute the same full-population depth-one REMESH advisory schedule with zero-duration intervals and append one actual pre-map snapshot:

    H1 = (25/64, 3/8, 31/128).

    The advisory-only arm uses the existing schedule executor and sampler; the action arm uses the existing event/REMESH cycle. With both delays one, a=1/2, hard clipping on [-1,1] inactive and no pressure refresh, both selected rows are H0. The actual map is x_new=x/4+3*H0/4: parent and peer stay fixed, while child becomes 19/128. The map acts on all nodes; only the child had changed between the selected history and current state in this particular control. Both retained histories stay [H0,H1]; the mapped endpoint is not a third row.

    Selected EN at the parent reads only the child with blend 1/2, under the unchanged pressure ceiling. RA at the peer then reads its sole parent neighbor with form blend 1/2 and capacity gain 1/8:

    Form observation Advisory only Advisory plus actual delayed map
    Child before EN 31/128 19/128
    Parent after EN 81/256 69/256
    Peer after RA 177/512 165/512

    The peer contrast is -3/128, the child's map difference propagated through the two actual blends. These forms are exact dyadics on the declared branch; UM's represented capacity/affinity are retained without an exact-dyadic claim. Clock stays 1/4. Sampling after same-time jumps does not create elapsed time. The delayed map's own temporal right-endpoint recording is distinct from the two-row sampling history and may differ between arms.

    Final lifetime operator counts are parent 13, peer 8 and child 3. With the current seven-entry retained glyph window, their respective suffixes are THOL,UM,IL,IL,IL,REMESH,EN, OZ,AL,AL,IL,IL,REMESH,RA, and IL,AL,REMESH. A map invocation supplies no second REMESH occurrence. This bounded composition realizes temporal memory participation after actual membership growth. It does not complete the historical cross-scale function, derive a local/collective-memory law, establish persistent identity or prove canonical completeness of the thirteen operators.

    Structural re-expression of one addressed port¶

    This bounded construction retains two logical ports, represented by actual members (A,B) or (A,C,B). Port A reads the singleton A or the pair A,C; port B remains independently addressed. The correspondence is x=(A+C)/2, with a retained nonzero contrast d=(A-C)/2. This is a declared interface observation, not a replacement of the actual members by a scalar macro-node. State, capacity, pressure, phase, semantic kind, own history and realized support remain part of the implementation and its admission.

    The retained output consumer complements addressed incoming Reception. It resolves an external receiver shared by every member's outgoing support, then executes actual EN at that receiver with precisely those members as its selected sources. The existing unweighted unique-neighbor reader owns the mean. Directed reverse-only edges do not carry this output, and parallel edges do not multiply its sources. The API contract owns live re-admission, receiver state, source preservation and transaction boundaries. A passive output table is neither an execution authorization nor an identity certificate. Sources acquire no extra glyph from another node reading them.

    Conditional preservation through a marked input/output family¶

    Let coarse A have form x and refined A,C have forms x+d,x-d, with d nonzero. Let B have form y in both realizations. Actual EN with the same factor m reads an independent donor u into A, or into both A,C. EN with factor n reads another donor v into B. On the same-kind scalar branch with inactive projection:

    x1 = (1-m)*x + m*u,  d1 = (1-m)*d,  y1 = (1-n)*y + n*v.

    The mean follows the coarse form while both refined members remain present. For 0<m<1 the contrast remains nonzero. Give an external receiver initial form z. It first executes addressed EN from port A with factor q, then from B with factor s. A further observer with initial form w executes actual RA with sole neighbor that receiver and factor r. Corresponding ideal rows are

    z1 = (1-q)*z + q*x1
z2 = (1-s)*z1 + s*y1
w1 = (1-r)*w + r*z2.

    Each realized incoming edge, own target context and pressure/capacity condition must admit its action. Preserve both receiver-prefix observations z1,z2; the final scalar w1 is not a complete encoding of two independent inputs. This is a finite marked-language statement under the existing partial-action criterion, not equivalence of arbitrary words or full constitutive dynamics. Individual histories and capacities need not be identical across the two representations. Arbitrary binary64 evaluation does not inherit a literal real-matrix identity.

    Acquired unequal source and finite continuation¶

    The actual-source controls start from the same six-node graph (A,B,U,V,R,W): A,B are members; U,V are input donors; R is the receiver and W its sole-neighbor observer. Initial edges are A-B, U-A, V-B, A-R, B-R and R-W. All phases and Si are zero; capacities start at one. Form starts at 1/4 for A,B,R, 1/8 for U,V and 3/8 for W. Pressure starts at 1/2 for A,B and zero externally. Actual IL/OZ and two AL episodes of duration 1/8 acquire A=25/64, B=3/8 and accelerations 1,0 at clock 1/4. The external inputs are still unchanged.

    The refined branch executes eligible THOL at A, obtaining an isolated child C=15/128 with fresh context, then actual birth-access UM supplies A-C. C acquires its own IL. Ordinary UM at C uses its existing A neighbor and the supplied candidate list (U,R) with candidate count zero, creating C-U and C-R through the current affinity law. It neither uses the disjoint-component request outside its domain nor pretends new candidates were existing consensus participants. At zero phase/Si and these positive forms, affinity is at least 3/4, above the supplied 1/2 threshold. No other candidate is exposed to UM; W retains sole neighbor R. These choices are declared access and occurrence, not an autonomous policy.

    Actual EN at C from A with factor 1/2 gives C=65/256 and acquires the parent's actual form kind. Further actual IL lowers pressure. A public instantaneous AL at refined A then adds the prospectively derived boost 35/256, giving

    A_refined = 135/256, C = 65/256
mean = 25/64 = A_coarse, contrast = 35/256.
