    TNFR theory: reading route and chapter catalog¶

    The research program owns the objective, current question and priorities. The chapters below own definitions, conditional results and proofs. This index helps choose a reading route; it does not introduce another research plan.

    A short reading route¶

    Question Primary reading
    What are the state, complete laws and levels of evidence? Conceptual core, with the glossary for definitions and dependencies
    What makes a collection an organized object? Emergence and ontology: formation, identity, maintenance and collective prediction
    What would justify a physical interpretation? Physical correspondence atlas, including the electromagnetic prerequisite audit
    How do these distinctions apply to an interacting pair? Marked-pair identity and its source-associated prediction; follow the linked premises for the complete construction
    Where are a result's implementation, contract and checks? Theory-to-execution map and technical documentation

    Each complete law retains its own source, state and observation contract. The worked pair is a conditional construction, not a replacement for those foundations or a demonstrated physical identification. Use the full catalog for other models and proofs; their presence does not schedule an experiment.

    Open and edit the linked canonical Markdown files directly, or read the compiled site. theory/research_catalog.json owns the catalog metadata; scripts/prepare_docs.py publishes the same sources without duplicating their ownership. The catalog and compatibility routes below are generated.
