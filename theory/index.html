    TNFR theory: reading routes, mathematical owners and implementation¶

    This is the maintained theory catalog. Use it to find definitions, proofs and their execution paths. The glossary classifies concepts; the strategy explains research dependencies and the portfolio classifies contributions. The execution plan alone owns tasks, status and reopening conditions. Engineering guidance belongs to the documentation map.

    Choose the complete model before reusing a result. Registered operators, the native relational law and the normalized-sine comparison have different evolution laws and admission contracts. Shared storage or matching local derivatives do not make them interchangeable. The nodal identity alone selects none of them uniquely; formation, maintenance and physical identification retain their separate hypotheses.

    Choose a question¶

    Question Start here
    What are form, capacity, pressure, phase and time? Foundations and parameter ledger
    Which information and laws does interaction consume? Relation foundations, constitutive information
    What forms a pattern, and what maintains it? Formation and identity, native exchange, sine patterns
    How do internal motion, scale and memory survive composition? Resonance, collective state, composition, memory
    What do execution and observations guarantee? Operators and grammar, diagnostics, theory to execution
    What can be compared with physical observations? Physical atlas, measurement protocols
    What do arithmetic applications establish? Arithmetic and applications, under their supplied mathematical premises

    A definition specifies a model; a conditional theorem needs its hypotheses; finite evidence records particular checks; an auxiliary construction adds premises. These can coexist in one document. Research priority is separate from validity, and a filename is not evidence.
