    TNFR research program: organization, dynamics and predictive state¶

    Scientific objective¶

    Determine which justified states, laws and interactions allow organized structures to form, persist, change and compose, and which information predicts their response. The engine, conditional mathematics and retained experiments make these questions concrete. Universal realization, autonomous hierarchy, a uniquely selected fundamental law and physical identification remain open.

    The matter-emergence hypothesis orients this work toward a generative account of matter and its observed fundamental properties. The ontology owns that open hypothesis; each research gate tests a named mathematical or observational prerequisite without assuming its physical conclusion.

    Use known experimental responses to identify what a successful explanation would have to produce, then select a missing structural mechanism that TNFR can discriminate under explicit premises. Keep observed behavior, its current physical description and a proposed TNFR mechanism distinct. The collective-property atlas owns those targets and their unresolved bridges. Published responses are known constraints, not reserved validation data. Favor a coherent account of formation, identity and interaction under one complete law; direct access to each primitive coordinate is a route-specific requirement, not the global objective. A generic organizational result remains a prerequisite, not a derivation of a particular physical property.

    The physical target includes electromagnetic, weak, strong and gravitational phenomena, quantum matter and their experimentally constrained relations. Seek compatible effective regimes and reproducible organizations under one justified complete dynamics. Electromagnetism is a highlighted route within that objective; a possible double-slit comparison retains its own observation obligations. The ontology owns the hypothesis, and the shared physical prerequisites connect the different phenomena without opening parallel research queues.

    An organized object is an instrument for investigating those questions. Its support, preparation, identity and complete law make a claim decidable under explicit premises. A successful construction establishes that scoped result; it does not make its graph, coefficients or event schedule mandatory for TNFR. The ontology distinguishes graph birth, regional formation and a sufficient effective description. None substitutes for another.

    This program and its linked chapters form one maintained research account. The program owns orientation and the current decision; each technical chapter owns its definitions, proofs, counterexamples, protocols or implementation contracts. Published pages are generated from those canonical Markdown files. Verified corrections govern current claims. Superseded errors, duplicate overviews and completed task logs belong in Git history; valid negative results and the evidence needed to assess a correction remain part of the research. Some protocol chapters retain evaluated prospective prefixes verbatim, including historical references to retired plans. Those references preserve evidence context; this program alone owns the current task. Historical study instructions do not activate work. The theory-to-execution map locates code and tests.

    Reading the research as one program¶

    Start with the conceptual core for structure, sufficient state, identity and complete dynamics. Its claim-strength table, dependency map and progressive examples explain how to read the technical proofs below. Use the glossary for concept classification and the relevant chapter for the actual definition, law or proof. The route is core -> glossary as needed -> model owner -> implementation/tests -> current task.

    Part Scientific question and technical entry
    State and complete laws Which coordinates and independent premises determine evolution? Parameter foundations, relation foundations
    Organization and identity What is acquired, how long does it persist, and what counts as its transformation? Ontology, pattern dynamics
    Interaction and composition Which constituent and boundary information survives contact? Relational composition, resonance foundations
    Structural actions and grammar Which actions and ordered words descend to an organization? Operator contracts, grammar
    Scale and predictive state When can constituents be grouped, eliminated or approximated? Scale and bridge, relational memory

    Mathematical and computational methods Which proof, representation and numerical allowance establish the claim? The corresponding technical derivations, shared kernels and testing guide

    Read each study with its complete model. Native relational dynamics, normalized-sine comparisons and supplied source-port laws have distinct pressure, phase, capacity, support and clock contracts. Their shared language supports comparison; a transfer of a theorem requires matching its premises. Arithmetic, graph-wave, Hamiltonian and gauge constructions retain their own assumptions and can contribute methods or counterexamples without becoming prerequisites for every organized-object investigation.

    The thirteen actions as capabilities, not a prescribed itinerary¶

    The mechanism audit owns the execution paths, available mechanisms, consumed state and open occurrence questions for all thirteen actions. Consult that owner when a scientific question requires a transformation.

    An object can realize a useful subset. A registered catalog does not establish that every organization admits every action, that all words preserve one identity, or that the continuous law selects their occurrence. Collective words require descent at every prefix, including actual targets, history, context and successor state. Supplied selectors remain executable policies until their selection law is justified.

    How the next question is selected¶

    Begin with the scientific consequence, then compare candidate questions on three grounds. Impact: would the answer establish or exclude a missing organizational capability, or resolve a premise consumed by several results? Discrimination: can a proof, matched counterexample or admitted observation separate the live alternatives? Feasibility: can that separation be reached with available sources, mathematical methods and a credible total budget?

    These are reasons to compare explicitly, not numerical scores. Prefer a question that advances the same capability with fewer unsupported premises or a clearer discriminator. Reject an infeasible evaluation even when its target is important; name the prerequisite instead. Ease of extending recent code, a larger repetition count or an attractive visualization is insufficient. Prefer a prerequisite shared by several physically justified targets when it has a concrete discriminator. Preserve cross-phenomenon consistency of units, state and parameter matching; separate fits do not establish unification.

    Number theory and geometric constructions, including those described as "sacred geometry", are additional sources of precise mathematical hypotheses under the ontology's evidence distinctions. Reuse the arithmetic owner, structural observability and scale/geometry owner. For such a candidate, identify the exact relation or invariant, distinguish encoded premises from derived selection, and state which missing organizational capability it tests. Do not select a preferred number, shape or ratio because it resembles the desired outcome. Compare alternatives under the same declared constraints; symbolic interpretation and physical identification retain separate obligations.

    An immediate methodological use is to check the symmetries of the complete law, prepared state, intervention and observation together: a mode reached by the probe need not be visible to the readout, and an imposed asymmetry is not spontaneous selection. Re-establish that statement for the actual model before using a result for a different linear, arithmetic or geometric construction. This criterion informs the existing single candidate declaration; it opens neither a polygon/prime scan nor an additional active research queue.

    For the selected question:

    1. Name the object, intended capability and observation; distinguish formation, retention, prediction, action and occurrence.
    2. Inventory the sufficient state, complete law, source and relevant existing results. Include boundary associations and every discarded coordinate.
    3. Try an analytical obstruction or equivalence before adding computation. Check conserved quantities, symmetry, accessibility and observational ambiguity.
    4. Choose the smallest discriminating claim. Record the competing explanations, acceptance conditions, resource limits and what an unavailable result means.
    5. Freeze any new evaluation prospectively, preserving inspected evidence as prior information. Run only the admitted calculation and affected controls.
    6. Close with a proof, counterexample or named missing premise. Reconsider the global priorities before selecting another extension of the same example.

    Four mandatory pillars and their closure criteria¶

    A resolution establishes a justified decision on an explicit domain, with proof or appropriately bounded evidence, alternatives and an independent review. A definition, a model card, passing implementation tests or a proposed experiment alone is insufficient. A negative result can close a specific claim, but leaves the pillar open if a capability required by the intended foundation still lacks a viable account. Primitive versus derived status is an obligation within the first two pillars, not a fifth competing research line. The four pillars are:

    Pillar / readiness ID Question and evidence already available Required resolution before physical mapping
    P1. Operator causality / operator_causality What makes transformations occur, and which are primitive mechanisms, realized effective actions or supplied interventions? The role and source audit, native selector evidence and pulse closure constraints separate these claims. Specify an independently defined complete causal law, actual state/history, occurrence or continuous realization, admission, reaction and balances for every mechanism consumed by the foundational model. Demonstrate its claimed capability or resolve the obstruction by an explicitly justified revision. Explain which registered actions are included, derived or outside scope; neither the registry nor its thirteen names establishes necessity or completeness.
    P2. Structure and observation / state_observation What exists in the model, what represents it, and what can a selected observation predict? The state/chart contract, closure and derived memory retain independent state and hidden initialization. Declare primitives, coordinates, observations, domains, boundary associations and units. Establish sufficient predictive state or an explicit memory description for the claimed response, including events and admitted continuation. A positive result needs a closure proof or a stated approximate prediction bound with domain and horizon. Equal-observation/different-response controls can refute a proposed reduction; absence of a counterexample is not proof of sufficiency. Do not define pressure from the observed rate, replace a rich state by an unsupported scalar, or equate EPI with every possible measurement.
    P3. Persistent organization and activity / organizational_identity What makes an NFR the same organization while it changes? The formation contract distinguishes identity, formation and retention; pulse observations distinguish capacity, frequency and clocks. Define identity independently of a diagnostic maximum or desired waveform; establish retention and continued response under declared perturbations and a stated interval/domain. If formation is claimed or needed, establish initial absence and acquisition. Give activity, rhythm, timing and synchronization separate observations; exact periodicity is optional unless explicitly claimed.
    P4. Justified model and research selection / research_selection What distinguishes the selected explanation from alternatives? Constitutive counterfamilies and the selection procedure prevent treating a well-studied example as a uniquely derived law. Compare retained alternatives under matched state, clock, observation and intervention. Justify the working premises independently of the desired response; retain exclusions and unresolved freedom. Select a discriminating prediction, equivalence or obstruction and review compatibility of P1-P3 in the same realization. Conclude why this foundation is sufficient for the proposed next claim; another success of one waveform or a bibliography alone does not close this pillar.

    The purpose is an explicit, defensible foundation, not a claim that all possible TNFR laws have been classified. Conversely, a narrow illustrative closure cannot be used to waive an unresolved premise consumed by a later physical claim. Remaining freedom must be explicit and immaterial to that claim, or the physical prerequisite stays open.

    Repository prerequisite for physical research¶

    Starting or extending a fundamental-physics analogy, particle identification, field/force mapping or experimental fit is prohibited while a pillar is open or its evidence/review is invalid. A historical physical chapter, an old plan, a successful animation or an agent's conversational approval does not bypass this rule. Existing material can be read to understand targets and missing premises. Mathematical foundation work remains permitted even when its implementation lives in src/tnfr/physics/.

    The readiness record contains only pillar status, evidence associations and review references. This program remains the sole task queue. The active activity marker above must stay foundations until a physical task is actually admitted. The prerequisite checker validates records, local evidence hashes and review associations; documentation integrity runs it in CI. Before a physical study, also run:

    python scripts/check_research_prerequisites.py --require-physical-mapping

    It must fail while any requirement or final readiness review is missing. A resolved entry requires evidence at its maintained proof owner and an independent review with a declared author and different reviewer. The final readiness review must bind all four current resolutions and assess their joint scope, dependencies and intended physical task. It also binds the exact program bytes, so changing the active question or activity invalidates that readiness review. Changed evidence invalidates the associated closure until reviewed again. A new evaluation must still meet F1-F4 and the independent physical preparation/observation requirements; passing the prerequisite is necessary, not empirical validation. The checker verifies the integrity of declared evidence and review, not their scientific truth or the identity of a reviewer. Agents must perform the review rather than manufacture approval metadata.

    Dependency order and a bounded first decision¶

    Apply P4's selection discipline from the start. Establish the joint P2/P1 state-and-causality contract before deciding whether its organization meets P3. Then review all three together under P4. These are dependencies within one investigation, not four independent solvers or parallel campaigns.

    The native foundation account and acquired-response owner retain acquisition, local response separation and recovery under AR1. The source-entry audit preserves the exact DA/W endpoint boxes. No useful complete tail certificate has yet connected those boxes to AR15 under its fixed work policy. This is a missing domain, norm and decay-rate certificate, not a failed trajectory, an irreducible uncertainty floor or a proof of impossible entry.
