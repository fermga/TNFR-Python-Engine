# Theory-to-execution map

This map routes a model or responsibility to shared code and representative
checks. The linked mathematical owner retains definitions, hypotheses and
result-specific evidence; this page does not repeat its theorem inventory.
[Architecture](../ARCHITECTURE.md) owns dispatch, [Testing](../TESTING.md)
owns test selection and commands, and the [SDK guide](../docs/CLI_AND_SDK.md)
owns usage.

Detached reports, exact prepared-family assessments and validated forecasts
have different inputs and guarantees. Sine readers do not change native
execution. Tangent, nonlinear and finite-response evidence remain distinct;
an SDK adapter is not another law or a complete checkpoint. Case-study links
provide access to retained evidence without reopening their campaigns.
