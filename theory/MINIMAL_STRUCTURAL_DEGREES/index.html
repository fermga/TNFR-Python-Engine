    Structural Observation Families and Tetrad Reconstruction Scope¶

    Status: Task-dependent observation design, with the historical tetrad retained as a compatible selected diagnostic view. No fixed number of channel names determines a minimal or sufficient structural state. This chapter owns the reconstruction and dependency criteria; the field guide owns field definitions and public contracts, and the mathematical scope note retains the related conditional dynamical results.

    1. Statement¶

    The structural tetrad (Φ_s, |∇φ|, K_φ, ξ_C) is the engine's selected readout of pressure accumulation, local phase stress, local circular curvature, and static pressure-derived spatial structure. Its four established field definitions and APIs remain available. The broader structural observation family may also retain primitive state, resolved profiles, oriented phase relations and independently measured history when the selected task requires them. These additions are observations of the model, not new constitutive parameters merely because they receive separate output names.

    Here "four" counts diagnostic channels, not four scalar degrees of freedom. On an N-node graph, the first three channels each retain up to N nodal values; ξ_C is one graph-level estimate, with its availability and provenance. Global summaries discard further information. The fields read stored pressure, primitive phase, support and the declared geometry. They do not directly read EPI, capacity or history; dependence on those quantities requires the supplied pressure law or other model relations, including their freshness conditions.

    The selection is a diagnostic design. It does not establish that every scalar observable is a function of the four reported channels, or that the tetrad determines a full graph state and its evolution under

    ∂EPI/∂t = ν_f · ΔNFR.

    A completeness claim must specify a state space, admissible observables, gauge identifications, and what information is retained by the readout. A minimality claim must additionally prove that no selected channel is redundant for that specified task. These stronger claims are not established by naming four structural questions or by computing the fields on a collection of graphs.

    1.1 Specify the task before minimizing its observation¶

    Let z denote the admitted state, including every state/history variable consumed by the selected model. Fix the support, metric, law parameters, inputs, clock and allowed equivalences as part of the comparison, or include them among the observed variables when they can change. Let O(z) be an observation and R(z) the particular state property or response of interest. For that task, sufficiency requires

    O(z1) = O(z2)  implies  R(z1) = R(z2)

    on the declared domain. Equivalently, R must factor through O there. State reconstruction requires injectivity up to exactly the declared equivalences. Future prediction concerns the selected response over a specified horizon under a complete well-posed law. It need not require reconstructing state directions that never affect that response.

    Minimality also requires an admissible class of observations and a cost: for example, the number of individually measured edge relations, the rank of a linear observer, or memory/computational cost at stated resolution. One channel can contain an N-vector; counting its name as one scalar gives no dimension theorem. Different sufficient families can be incomparable, and an algebraically minimal one can be poorly conditioned.

    Availability and node/edge association belong to the observation contract. Equal available components of two partial observations do not establish equality of the missing information. A selected diagnostic view can remain useful even when its unknown components prevent a sufficiency verdict.

    2. Four diagnostic questions¶

    Question Field Construction
    How much pressure accumulates from other nodes? Φ_s Distance-weighted aggregation of ΔNFR
    How misaligned is a node with its neighbors? |∇φ| Mean magnitude of wrapped phase differences
    How does its phase differ from the neighborhood mean? K_φ Wrapped deviation from the circular mean
    What distance scale summarizes the pressure-derived product profile? ξ_C Static exponential fit; separate spectral reference when the fit is unavailable

    This organizes local phase information and non-local aggregation/correlation. Φ_s is a per-node value with global dependence; it is not a purely local quantity. ξ_C compresses a spatial correlation profile into a length. The table describes this selected view; it does not exhaust graph observations or require all four channels for every task. The coherence fit and spectral fallback retain their separate units, meanings and availability.

    3. The operator-derivative tower¶

    3.1 Construction¶

    ΔNFR → distance-kernel aggregation → Φ_s
phase → wrapped neighbor differences → |∇φ|
phase → circular-neighborhood deviation → K_φ
local coherence → spatial correlation → ξ_C

    The first two phase operations are related to first and second derivatives. For an undirected graph, an oriented incidence matrix gives an edge gradient, and composing it with its weighted adjoint gives the graph Laplacian. For small phase spread within a consistent branch and matching neighbor weights, circular curvature approaches L_rw φ. The equality is a linearized description; the exact implemented field uses circular means and wrapping.

    3.2 Algebraic generation does not terminate information at second order¶

    Higher Laplacian powers are compositions of the same operator. This fact does not mean their actions are linearly dependent or recoverable from lossy pointwise summaries. If L has at least four distinct eigenvalues, then I, L, L², and L³ are linearly independent. Otherwise a polynomial of degree at most three would vanish at four distinct eigenvalues.

    The finite-dimensional cutoff is controlled by L's minimal polynomial. It depends on the graph and is not universally second order. In addition, |∇φ| discards signs and ξ_C discards most of the correlation profile. A generating set of operators and a sufficient set of state statistics are different mathematical objects.

    3.3 Dynamical non-reconstruction¶

    At fixed graph, phase, EPI, and pure-EPI pressure, a uniform rescaling of ν_f leaves the tetrad unchanged while scaling ∂EPI/∂t = ν_f ΔNFR. Consequently the tetrad alone does not determine the full dynamical state. The structural triad, frequency, pressure law, and graph remain part of the model.

    The same-law P2 witness is stronger: it fixes capacity, clock and a complete conditional cotangent law. Two states with identical full tetrads, pressure and initial form rates have different potential derivatives and relative-form accelerations because their omitted EPI means differ. Thus a snapshot tetrad need not close even its own evolution under a known law. This does not exclude reconstruction from a time series or the restricted positive result below.

    3.4 Conditional reconstruction in the pure EPI channel¶

    The capacity-rescaling result identifies capacity as a necessary predictive model parameter. A graph-specific positive result is also available when the complete nodal structural-potential field is retained.

    For fixed connected reciprocal nonnegative conductance with positive nodal strengths and fixed positive capacities, let K be the canonical inverse-square shortest-path kernel on its declared distance domain. With unit pure-EPI channel coefficient, pressure gives

    Phi_s = -K L_rw x,    x = EPI.

    Write O=-K L_rw. Since L_rw 1=0, uniform EPI shifts are always invisible to Phi_s. If rank(O)=N-1, this is the only invisible EPI direction. Appending the fixed-capacity conserved mean

    m(x) = sum_i (d_i/nu_i)x_i / sum_i(d_i/nu_i)

    makes [O; m] injective and hence reconstructs absolute EPI in exact arithmetic. Together with the capacity vector and known graph, this determines the future pure-diffusion trajectory. Thus, for this restricted algebraic task, the full potential field plus one zero-mode scalar is sufficient for form reconstruction, while capacity remains necessary to determine the clock/generator. Numerical promotion additionally requires scale-aware rank and residual checks.

    epi_diffusion_reconstruction_certificate computes the potential and augmented ranks with separate scale-aware SVD thresholds, then requires a small relative reconstruction residual before it reports numerical success. The exhaustive NetworkX graph atlas through six nodes gives rank(O)=N-1 on all 142 connected simple graphs in that finite domain. This is a measured finite-domain result, not a proof for every graph, weight or structural-potential kernel.

    Algebraic rank is also weaker than robust reconstruction. On a four-node weighted star with edge weights [10^-3, 10^3, 10^3], the rank remains N-1 but the nonzero singular-value condition number exceeds 10^10. Thus an exact observer can be practically unstable under extreme geometry. Uniformly scaling all conductances to 10^-8 or 10^8 also preserves the structural nullity but can make the unscaled augmented observer numerically singular. The certificate reports its thresholds, condition number and relative residual so downstream inverse methods can distinguish algebraic identifiability from numerical resolution.

    Global tetrad summaries remain insufficient because they discard the nodal potential field. Changing capacity ratios also removes the fixed conserved mean, as shown by the time-varying result in TNFR_DIFFUSION_STABILITY_THEOREM.md.

    3.5 Pressure reconstruction depends on the actual potential kernel¶

    For a known fixed distance kernel, write Phi_s = B_G p in exact arithmetic. The full potential reconstructs an independently variable pressure vector if and only if B_G is injective on its admitted pressure domain. On a restricted domain the required condition is

    ker(B_G) intersect {p1 - p2 : p1,p2 admitted} = {0}.

    Invertibility of the ambient matrix suffices, but is not a universal property of every allowed support. In particular, directed distance kernels can be singular. Pressure-derived coherence profiles are already determined by pressure and geometry; adding their output names cannot create independent information when pressure is reconstructed.

    For an unrestricted real pressure space, suppose dim ker(B_G)=d and the columns of N form an orthonormal basis of that kernel. Then

    p -> (B_G p, N^T p)

    is injective: a difference in its kernel has the form N a and satisfies N^T N a=a=0. At least d additional independent linear scalar observations are necessary because fewer cannot have an injective restriction to the d-dimensional kernel. This is minimality within linear augmentations of this fixed observer, not within arbitrary nonlinear encodings or a different pressure domain. Numerical inversion additionally needs condition and residual bounds. Direct stored pressure is often the clearer observation when the engine already exposes it.

    Same full tetrad, different signed pressure and form rate¶

    Take the outward directed star with three leaves at each explicit edge length r=1,2,3,4 and unit conductance. Set zero phases, unit capacities and zero EPI. Prepare leaf coherence c_i=2^(-r) and hence leaf pressure p_i=2^r-1. Compare center pressure zero and one, corresponding to center coherence one and one half. All other inputs agree.

    Only the center reaches other nodes, so

    \[\Phi_s(0)=3\sum_{r=1}^4\frac{2^r-1}{r^2} =\frac{499}{48},\qquad \Phi_s(i)=0\quad(i\ne0).\]

    Center pressure is in the kernel of this potential map. Gradient and curvature are zero, with the documented isolate convention at leaves. The twelve positive-distance pairs give q_r=c_0*2^(-r), three per bin. Both fits have slope -log(2) and xi_C=1/log(2), while their intercepts are zero and -log(2). Thus the full tetrad is equal in exact arithmetic, whereas the full coherence profiles distinguish the preparations.

    Under the same supplied held-pressure law x'=p, p'=0, with unit capacity, phase and support fixed and no events, the center form rate is respectively zero and one. This is missing signed driver information under a fixed law, not just uncertainty about which law applies. The fixed observation controls compare the public readouts with these analytic targets. Equality of exact formulas is distinct from tolerance-based agreement of floating regressions.

    There is a stronger algebraic corollary with the same kernel and leaves: center pressures +1 and -1 also leave the potential unchanged, and now their equal magnitudes preserve the entire coherence profile. Their center form rates under the held-pressure law are +1 and -1. Thus the rich profile does not universally repair this missing signed driver. This corollary is an exact consequence of the pressure kernel and absolute-value definition, not another evaluated fixture.

    3.6 Constructive circular phase reconstruction on fixed support¶

    Let z_i=exp(i*theta_i) be circular phase on a finite known graph, and fix an orientation for each observed edge. The edge phasor is

    \[u_{ij}=z_j\overline{z_i},\qquad u_{ji}=\overline{u_{ij}}.\]

    The graph of available observations is considered with its underlying undirected connectivity. Reversing the orientation uses the inverse unit phasor; it does not require a reverse dynamical arc. Let the original observation support have n nodes and k weak components, including isolates.

    Reconstruction theorem. Edge phasors on any spanning forest of that support determine every nodal circular phase up to one common unit factor per component. Choose one root r per component and set its reconstructed phasor to one. For each node, multiply the edge phasors, or their inverses when traversed backward, along its unique forest path from the root. The products telescope:

    \[\widehat z_i= \prod_{(a,b)\text{ along }r\leadsto i}u_{ab} =z_i\overline{z_r}.\]

    Supplying the root phasor recovers z_i=z_r*hat_z_i. More generally, the selected individual edge observations suffice modulo one component factor if and only if they connect each original component. Necessity follows by rotating one connected piece of a disconnected observed subgraph while leaving the rest of its original component fixed. Every observed edge phasor stays unchanged, but the original component's relative phase changes.

    A spanning forest uses n-k edges and is therefore minimal among such individual edge measurements: fewer cannot connect all original components. Each phasor is one circle-valued coordinate, represented by two real components with a unit-norm constraint. This count does not provide a global uncut real-angle chart or minimum cardinality among arbitrary encodings.

    If all edge phasors are retained, cycle products must equal one. Conversely, unit edge data satisfying the fundamental-cycle products reconstruct nodal phasors by the same forest construction: each non-tree edge agrees with its reconstructed endpoint ratio. The extra edges provide consistency checks, not extra independent phase coordinates for already integrable data. Noisy or rounded edge data require a separate consistency/error contract; they may not be silently renormalized into an exact certificate.

    Derived phase fields, winding and absolute references¶

    In exact circular arithmetic, with S_i=sum_(j in N(i)) u_ij, the phase observation formulas satisfy

    |grad phi|(i) = mean_j |Arg(u_ij)|,
K_phi(i) = wrap(-Arg(S_i)) when S_i is nonzero.

    The isolate convention is separate from a zero resultant on nonempty support. The mean sine current is the mean of Im(u_ij). Thus the full directed edge phasors generate these local phase readouts with their stated support and branch conventions; the converse generally loses information.

    The implemented phasor/resultant readers separately retain represented-component and rounding semantics. These ideal identities do not turn rounded relative phasors into an exact reconstruction certificate for their numerical output.

    For a declared oriented cycle away from the wrap cut,

    \[W=\frac{1}{2\pi}\sum_{(i,j)\text{ on cycle}}\operatorname{Arg}(u_{ij})\]

    is an integer because the edge product is one. It is derived from the edge phasors, including the non-tree edges determined by reconstruction; it is not an additional independent continuous coordinate. It can change when an edge crosses the selected cut. The existing winding owner reports branch margin and availability separately from U3 admission. The marked eight-cycle witness already proves that winding can distinguish identical full tetrads. That proof retains orientation and label equivalences rather than treating every reflection as a different physical state.

    For circular-state reconstruction, one root phase per component restores absolute phase. For prediction those anchors can be omitted only if the complete law and specified inputs admit the corresponding component rotations as symmetries, and the target response respects them. An externally referenced phase law or an interaction joining components can consume the missing relative reference. Circular reconstruction also does not determine arbitrary continuous phase lifts or their histories; a law using those lifts needs its own retained branch information.

    Algebraic minimality does not ensure uniform numerical robustness. For unit phasor data with chord errors bounded by epsilon_e, telescoping the product difference gives a reconstruction chord error no larger than the sum of epsilon_e along the selected forest path, plus any root-anchor error. Long paths can accumulate error. Redundant edges may support a separately specified consistency or estimation method; they do not automatically produce an uncertainty reduction without a noise model.

    For the broader estimation problem with noisy relative angles, see Amit Singer, Angular Synchronization by Eigenvectors and Semidefinite Programming. That work studies recovery up to a common phase. The finite exact forest argument above requires no noise model; statistical recovery claims retain their separately stated assumptions.

    3.7 Snapshot reconstruction, temporal observability and closure differ¶

    For a complete smooth law z'=F(z) and observation y=O(z), the initial observed rate is D O(z) F(z). A necessary condition for an autonomous first-order law in y is that this rate agree on every admitted fiber O(z)=y. Existence, uniqueness and domain/event behavior are additional requirements. The same-law coherence tangent violates that fiber condition even after retaining the expanded static coherence profile. Its omitted EPI is an existing state coordinate, not an additional fundamental field derived by naming the obstruction.

    For the special linear finite-dimensional system z'=A z, y=C z, three different algebraic questions have exact answers:

    Question Condition under this fixed known model
    Does one snapshot reconstruct all z? rank(C)=n
    Does an ideal output history reconstruct the initial z? rank([C; C A; ...; C A^(n-1)])=n
    Does y obey a closed autonomous linear first-order law? ker(C) is invariant under A, equivalently C A=M C for some M

    The history condition assumes ideal derivatives or equivalently a complete continuous output record on an interval, known clock and dynamics. It is not a guarantee for a finite noisy sample. The existing linear_observability_certificate checks the specified finite-dimensional rank with a numerical tolerance; it is not a universal tetrad or nonlinear observability theorem.

    For a state response R(z)=Q z, equality of its whole linear future on every C-fiber is equivalent to

    ker(C) subset of intersection_(j=0,...,n-1) ker(Q A^j).

    The necessity follows by differentiating Q exp(t A) z at zero; sufficiency follows from Cayley-Hamilton and the exponential series. Consequently a response can be predictable even when the whole state is not observable. For pure EPI diffusion a uniform form shift is invisible to pressure at all times, but is needed if the requested response includes absolute EPI.

    The oscillator block x'=p, p'=-x makes the distinction concrete. Observing p alone does not reconstruct x at one instant and does not close a first-order p law. The ideal derivative p'=-x resolves the missing form; the two-row observability matrix has full rank. Describing the same pressure response by p''=-p retains two initial conditions, rather than eliminating the missing state by renaming the output.

    3.8 Consumed form, capacity, sources and event history¶

    For the unforced instantaneous form rate, signed pressure and nonnegative capacity give x'=nu_f*p. A known held product can suffice for that rate, while separate pressure and capacity may remain necessary for their future laws. With a live source, the complete row is x'=nu_f*p+Gamma and the source must also be retained. Inferring pressure by dividing an evaluated rate by capacity is circular and fails at zero capacity.

    Holding pressure fixed while scaling capacity from one to two leaves the full tetrad unchanged and doubles a nonzero form rate in the same declared clock. Adding the admitted capacity resolves this particular ambiguity; it does not recover omitted form or pressure evolution. Under a supplied oscillator, two EPI preparations with identical pressure and capacity have the same initial form rate but different pressure derivatives, as the linked exact witness shows. Under fixed pure diffusion, the narrower mean and potential reconstruction in Section 3.4 can instead be sufficient.

    Event decisions can require information beyond all instantaneous primitive values. Mutation consumes a live two-sample EPI secant and independent grammar context. An operator-name history is categorical context, not the timestamped or declared-step EPI evidence. The current snapshot and the nodal-product rate cannot substitute for that observed secant. The event contracts and mutation admission controls own its actual validity requirements. Hidden reservoir, boundary, selector or memory state remains part of a model whenever its law consumes it.

    Thus primitive values, derived views and history are selected against the actual response and execution path. Neither appending a few diagnostics nor exposing a categorical history automatically supplies a complete checkpoint or sufficient state for every operator and continuous law.

    4. Field scales and selected thresholds¶

    The exact phase-wrap maximum π must be distinguished from policy values expressed as fractions of π. The numerical values below remain unchanged in canonical.py.

    4.1 Structural potential¶

    The canonical exponent is α = 2 in B_ij = d(i,j)⁻². The resulting field is linear in ΔNFR and unbounded under pressure rescaling. Even at zero phase, unit pressure on K₄ gives Φ_s = 3. No phase-wrap argument can enforce π/4 or π/2 for this linear field without an additional pressure/geometry constraint.

    The chain sums Σ d⁻² = ζ(2) and Σ d⁻⁴ = ζ(4) are correct particular values. They do not uniquely select exponent 2: absolute chain sums converge for all α > 1 and independent unit-variance pressure sums for all α > 1/2. Graph families with growing distance shells need their own summability assumptions. The inverse-square kernel is the canonical modeling choice, not a uniqueness theorem from those series.

    4.2 Phase gradient¶

    The mean of absolute wrapped neighbor differences is bounded by π. The warning value π/16 is selected policy; a measured synchronization onset depends on the experiment's frequency spread, topology, and coupling protocol.

    Canonical aggregate coherence is

    C(t) = 1 / (1 + mean|ΔNFR| + mean|dEPI|).

    It does not identify the spatial location of phase stress. It also is not scale invariant: with dEPI = 0 and uniform |ΔNFR| = 1, C(t) = 1/2; doubling pressure gives 1/3. Only the separate normalized dispersion statistic C_disp = 1 − σ_ΔNFR/ΔNFR_max is invariant under proportional positive pressure scaling when its denominator is nonzero.

    4.3 Circular curvature¶

    Where the circular mean is available, K_φ = wrap(φ_i − circular_mean(neighbors)) gives |K_φ| ≤ π by definition. The 0.9π threshold is an operational margin inside that exact bound. The shared reader accepts every nonzero represented phasor resultant without an arbitrary small-magnitude cutoff; exact represented cancellation on nonempty support makes curvature unavailable. Isolates retain the documented zero convention. These arithmetic availability conditions do not prove a trigonometric singularity or a singularity of a continuous manifold.

    4.4 Correlation length¶

    The current estimator first fits pressure-derived coherence products against graph distance. If that fit is unavailable under its admission policy, it uses a spectral reference from the graph: the inverse square root of its smallest normalized-Laplacian eigenvalue above 1e-9. On an admitted connected undirected graph this is 1/√λ₂ only when that gap clears the numerical cutoff. A fitted correlation length need not equal that reference for every state; exponential spatial decay is itself a fitting assumption.

    The fitted statistic is an uncentered product of static pressure-only coherences, not a connected covariance. Its acceptance rules require a finite negative fitted slope, but do not test goodness of fit. The fitted length scales with an independent uniform path-length rescaling; the dimensionless normalized-Laplacian fallback does not. Keep these distinct observations and their provenance when comparing states, scales or thresholds.

    On disconnected graphs, a second-smallest eigenvalue and a smallest positive eigenvalue need not coincide. Reports must identify the estimator/fallback and graph regime. A failed fit or large estimate alone does not prove a phase transition.

    The coherence observation foundations derive exact finite-profile identities and counterexamples: a perfect prepared exponential need not measure dynamic correlation, an accepted slope can hide an oscillatory profile, and exact-distance binning can lose availability after a small metric change. The optional profile observer retains discarded evidence. Its moments have explicit algebraic dependencies. A marked-cycle winding witness distinguishes states with the same full tetrad; a same-law oscillator witness shows why even the expanded static profile need not close dynamics.

    5. Diagnostic removal studies¶

    The four established fields address accumulated pressure, local mismatch, local curvature and a compressed spatial profile. They remain useful selected views, without requiring all of them in every task. A non-redundancy study should construct explicit pairs of states with identical retained readouts and a differing omitted readout, or specify a detection problem and prove the omitted channel changes the decision.

    Descriptions such as “a blind spot appears” require the actual fixture, readout resolution, and detection threshold. Passing many implementation tests does not prove universal irreducibility. Nor does usefulness of each channel prove that no additional observable can carry independent information.

    A strong minimality claim is meaningful only after fixing its target state space, equivalences, admissible measurements and cost. The unrestricted four-field completeness claim is ruled out by the explicit missing-pressure, capacity, form and orientation witnesses above. Restricted representations can still have positive injectivity, closure and minimality results.

    A positive example with those choices fixed is the P5 reflection quotient. Its three even coordinates and constrained quadratic hidden-mode matrix reconstruct form up to reflection under the declared pure-EPI law. This is not the four-field tetrad, a universal state basis, or a reduction to three dimensions near the reflection-fixed stratum. The linked derivation owns the injectivity, rate, decoder and field-covariance results.

    The coherence observation owner derives the exact dependencies among profile moments and their distance endpoint populations. A circular phase alignment and its circular variogram are also related by variogram=1-alignment; counting both as independent channels would duplicate information. The phase-resultant magnitude already retained by the curvature observer can help describe directional conditioning, but is not a new dynamical law or a substitute for an error certificate.

    The post-Silence parent-child case makes this distinction concrete on a retained composition. A marked pair's form and phase differences need the parent's boundary inputs even for their first derivative. Equal pair state and equal instantaneous inputs can still have different second derivatives. Adding those two input values therefore does not itself close the pair dynamics; retain the relevant environmental state or its derived history. The conditional relational identity and its matched control belong to that model's owner.

    Optimization can therefore mean retaining a lossless generating description for the chosen task, requesting only useful derived views, and preserving availability and conditioning. It need not mean increasing or reducing a universal channel count. A costly or redundant view may still be convenient for interpretation, while a smaller output is acceptable only when its lost information does not affect the admitted task.

    6. Energy, currents, and conservation¶

    The tetrad can participate in energy-like diagnostics with phase and pressure currents:

    V = ½ Σ_i (Φ_s(i)² + |∇φ|(i)² + K_φ(i)²),
T = ½ Σ_i (J_φ(i)² + J_ΔNFR(i)²).

    These are nonnegative functionals of measured fields. Their existence does not establish state reconstruction, does not make ΔNFR equal to −∂V/∂EPI, and does not turn all implemented operators into Hamiltonian transformations. Conjugate coordinates belong to a separately specified substrate model.

    Likewise a continuity residual or measured energy descent is evidence about the tested trajectory. Universal Noether conservation and asymptotic stability require the stated symmetry, flow, and Lyapunov hypotheses. The mathematical scope is developed in the variational note and the conservation note; the tetrad's canonical diagnostic status does not supply those proofs.

    7. Symmetry-restricted research¶

    Symmetry can restrict which sectors a graph readout detects. A statement about the tetrad on a particular prime-ladder graph with uniform parameters must name that graph, its action, and the parameter restrictions. Invariance under that action constrains reach; it does not imply that the tetrad spans every vector in the invariant sector.

    The REMESH-∞ and Riemann programs use separate projection and observability constructions. Their particular graph certificates do not establish a universal complete “smooth structural sector” on arbitrary networks. See REMESH_INFINITY_DERIVATION.md and TNFR_RIEMANN_RESEARCH_NOTES.md for the precise program assumptions and open conjectures.
