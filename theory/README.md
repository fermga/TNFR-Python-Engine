# TNFR theory: reading routes, mathematical owners and implementation

This is the maintained theory catalog. Use it to find definitions, proofs and
their execution paths. The [glossary](GLOSSARY.md) classifies concepts; the
[strategy](NODAL_RESEARCH_STRATEGY.md) explains research dependencies and the
[portfolio](../TNFR_lineas_de_investigacion.txt) classifies contributions. The
[execution plan](research/FIVE_STAGE_EXECUTION_PLAN.md#current-g3-gate) alone
owns tasks, status and reopening conditions. Engineering guidance belongs to
the [documentation map](../docs/README.md).

Choose the complete model before reusing a result. Registered
[operators](STRUCTURAL_OPERATORS.md), the [native relational law](nodal/RELATIONAL_EXCHANGE_ADMISSION.md)
and the [normalized-sine comparison](nodal/SINE_CONSTITUTIVE_INFORMATION.md)
have different evolution laws and admission contracts. Shared storage or
matching local derivatives do not make them interchangeable. The nodal
identity alone selects none of them uniquely; formation, maintenance and
physical identification retain their separate hypotheses.

A definition specifies a model; a conditional theorem needs its hypotheses;
finite evidence records particular checks; an auxiliary construction adds
premises. These can coexist in one document. Research priority is separate
from validity, and a filename is not evidence.
