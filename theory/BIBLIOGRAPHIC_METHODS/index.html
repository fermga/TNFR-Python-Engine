    Bibliographic methods for TNFR foundations¶

    The supplied books help choose and combine methods for a precise TNFR question. Their strongest common lesson is to retain the relationship between the complete dynamics, actual preparation, selected observation and the claim being made. A compatible shape, an acquired organization, its persistence and a sufficient description of its response require different evidence.

    This chapter owns the literature-to-method synthesis. Definitions and proofs remain at their linked owners; the research program alone selects work. The applications below are methodological judgments, not new TNFR theorems or physical identifications. The final synthesis consolidates the three selective reviews and identifies which apparent gaps already have results.

    Form becomes useful through a declared response¶

    Wagensberg's distinction between internal organization, boundary shape and relations to the surroundings suggests asking what a proposed form does under specified conditions. His boundary-based meaning of form must not replace TNFR's EPI definition, and a natural example does not select a TNFR pressure law, operator occurrence or preferred polygon.

    The formation and composition contract already distinguishes membership, initial absence, acquisition, maintenance, recovery and response. Its practical use is to choose the same observation on the source, interacting system and control, then establish the claimed change and retained predictive consequence. An observation undefined before contact is not measured absence. Retention of the constituents is not by itself retention of their joint relation.

    The two-port target and prepared-source capture are existing, different results. Connecting an actual formed source to that organization remains a source-dependent question. Likewise, the single-bridge cycle obstruction excludes one proposed carrier of identity, not interaction, response or all collective organization. These examples make a new general ontology chapter or another generic geometry scan unnecessary.

    Symmetry reduces work when the law, state and question match¶

    Golubitsky and Stewart supply a systematic language for symmetry and invariant families. Newman shows why graph modes become dynamically informative only after specifying the node and coupling laws. For a TNFR use, distinguish:

    Object What has to be checked
    Complete law and support The proposed action preserves all consumed rows, capacities, weights, degree normalization, boundaries and event rules. A graph drawing alone establishes none of these.
    Reference state The actual reference is fixed by the claimed symmetry, or is carried to a separately specified equivalent state. A symmetric law also permits nonsymmetric states.
    Source/error family Initial errors and their correlations respect the restriction, or off-family deviations are explicitly retained and bounded. Independent node errors do not generally lie in a fixed subspace.
    Observation The claimed equivalence really leaves the readout unchanged; otherwise the transformation changes the question.

    Invariance of a family permits an exact restriction of trajectories within that family. It does not prove attraction from surrounding states or a closed observation for every state. The latter needs the projectability criterion at joint form-phase closure. An approximate restriction additionally needs transverse and nonlinear error control for the actual preparation and horizon.

    Under a smooth equivariant flow with uniqueness and a local inverse, the state's exact isotropy is preserved at finite times within that domain (Golubitsky/Stewart, section 1.2, proposition 1.18, printed page 12). A limiting equilibrium can have more symmetry. Thus exact attainment of that symmetry is a poor generic finite-time formation criterion; a justified finite membership domain is a different claim. A support event must be treated separately because it changes the field. This does not exclude formation.

    Before choosing a relational-response test, check whether its observation is forced to hide the proposed change by symmetry. Passing this filter establishes only that a witness is not excluded that way, not a nonzero response or gap.

    The two-port capture owner already separates its symmetric reference from the surrounding error family. The reduced-port owner retains a controlled linear internal surrogate and nonlinear bridge; it does not claim that its reflection restriction is an exact quotient of the full nonlinear sine law. Reuse these distinctions rather than install another symmetry wrapper. A cyclic relabeling can align port names without making a formed source equal to a midpoint-aligned preparation.

    Laplacian eigenvalues alone do not certify the stability of a coupled form/phase/capacity system. Use its complete Jacobian, relevant conserved leaf and nonlinear domain. A master-stability calculation requires the appropriate identical-unit synchronous solution and coupling structure; it cannot be imported for an internally twisted organization by matching a graph spectrum. Also retain the conductance/length distinction: visual geometry, path metric and dynamical coupling need not coincide.

    Reduction retains the cost of discarded information¶

    Zwanzig's block elimination explains why an exact observed equation can have both a history term and a term carrying hidden initialization. TNFR already has this reusable deterministic mechanism in coordinate memory and the derived-memory chapter. A new general projection library would duplicate it without resolving the current scientific gap.

    Distinguish three operations: a deterministic coordinate projection, a symmetry quotient, and a conditional expectation under a probability measure. An interval of allowed initial states is not a distribution. The hidden initialization term is deterministic once the fine initial state is fixed; calling it noise does not make it white, Gaussian, independent or negligible. An equation for an ensemble mean is not a closed equation for every member.

    The reached-state closure obstruction already shows why equal visible data can hide different later responses. The reduced-port model retains initialization and nonlinear discrepancy. Both are useful methods with model-specific coefficients, not transferable source verdicts.

    For a new reduction, first declare the prediction and source family. Either retain sufficient state, carry explicit memory, or justify an approximation with a bound on initialization, kernel/tail, retained evolution, domain and output error over the intended horizon. Small coupling or a small kernel alone does not show that the retained variable is slow. Equilibrium-weighted projections and fluctuation-dissipation relations need their own measure, generator and preparation hypotheses; a dissipative node-space metric supplies none of those identifications by itself.

    Stability, pulse and phase have different conclusions¶

    Strogatz and Pikovsky/Rosenblum/Kurths make it useful to keep the following result types visible rather than compress them into a statement of stability:

    Result What it establishes and what remains
    Compatible invariant or stationary organization It can exist under the selected law. This does not establish source-to-target entry.
    Local attraction Nearby admitted preparations approach it. A finite entry bound from the actual source needs more.
    Finite passage and capture The particular source reaches an admitted trapping domain by a bounded time; preparation, error and domain remain part of the result.
    Retained observable response The declared readout persists or discriminates a control on its specified interval; another readout may require more state.

    The native source-entry audit already identifies a missing useful domain/metric/rate certificate. A generic Lyapunov argument does not fill it. For semidefinite loss, zero instantaneous loss need not mean equilibrium; compactness, forward continuation and the invariant zero-loss set matter. The existing permanent-pulse account owns the actual law-specific argument.

    A decaying complex mode, conservative recurrent motion, externally maintained response and an attracting self-sustained cycle are different mechanisms. TNFR has damped collective response, conservative organization and an exact phase-advance branch with finite timing retention. Keep those positive results and their limits. The bibliography neither derives a universal sustaining pulse nor licenses adding a favorable damping term or operator schedule. A complex eigenpair is not by itself a Hopf bifurcation or a new organized identity.

    Primitive nodal phase, a geometric observation, a constant asymptotic origin and an advancing oscillator phase are different coordinates. The book's isochrone reduction starts from an attracting periodic orbit, then controls weak forcing and transverse deviations; averaging adds further assumptions. An exact invariant or conservative reduction may have a different valid contract. In every case retain the phase map, domain, actual initial state, complete field, error and horizon. Synchronization also needs a specified readout: frequency agreement, bounded relative phase and finite tick agreement are distinct. A monotone clock requires additional state and timing premises.

    Scaling joins geometry, memory and time without conflating them¶

    Barenblatt's analysis starts with the governing parameters, including source and boundary data. Dimensionless groups constrain dependence but do not select the complete law. Their size alone does not justify deleting a term. The scale distinction separates change of units, exact reduction, geometric repetition and an asymptotic similarity claim. Complete, incomplete and absent similarity in the declared limits and power-type variables are all possible outcomes; the last does not exclude every other form of reduction, and a fitted power does not choose among them.

    This connects three already useful TNFR tools: symmetry can identify a candidate invariant family; observation can require additional state or memory; scale/clock transformation must retain their initialization and error. The registered-pattern inheritance and nested reduction give scoped examples. They do not imply an indefinitely iterable hierarchy or a universal fractal exponent.

    The energy-informed slow comparison already offers a more relevant method than an unqualified slow-manifold approximation: it carries the fast transient, reconstruction and finite-time error. Reuse it only after matching the actual source, degree normalization, phase origin and comparison domain. A small coefficient can accumulate over a long interval; changing the clock cannot erase the inherited preparation.

    Integration decisions¶

    These are method choices for the program, not separately activated studies.

    Decision Concrete consequence
    Integrate now Use one source/event/identity declaration carrying symmetry, retained state or memory, prediction type, finite domain and error. Link the existing proof owners rather than restating their results.
    Clarify now Distinguish complete-law symmetry from source symmetry; deterministic uncertainty from statistical ensembles; finite entry from qualitative attraction; geometric repetition from asymptotic or exact dynamical scale transfer.
    Reuse conditionally Existing Jacobian/energy, coordinate-memory, linear-observation, reduced-port and slow-comparison methods when their hypotheses match the actual consumer. Retain full state when reduction offers no proved benefit.
    Defer Statistical closure, Markovianization, new phase-only models, renormalization exponents and iterative fractal hierarchies until a selected question needs them and their missing premises can be supplied.
    Reject as unsupported shortcuts Imported midpoint preparations, silent removal of source errors, privileged polygons, graph-spectrum-only stability, visual fractality as identity, and physical identification from bibliographic resemblance.

    No verified implementation error or obsolete shared kernel was found in this review. The warranted optimization is method routing and sharper transfer conditions, not speculative code replacement. The physical-readiness record and all source-specific mathematical verdicts retain their existing scope.

    Beyond one contact: reusable organization mechanisms¶

    The second reading asks how the bibliography could improve TNFR's general account of emergent organizations, beyond the currently selected experiment. The most useful common target is an already admitted organization acting as a predictive component: a retained internal state, an interface, a specified response, and a valid continuation when another organization or an action interacts with it. Several scoped instances already exist. The opportunities concern additional consumers and missing bridges, not a new NFR definition, primitive substance or universal law. The final audit below refines their priority against the existing interface and event results.

    Several seemingly new ideas are already substantial TNFR results: non-orbital collective families, finite memory forecasts, exact phase-advance pulses and their distinct labeled/exchanged periods, acquired native alternatives, and interface-dependent effective laws. The opportunities below identify bridges between those results rather than reclassifying their existence as unfinished. Each proposed gain remains unproved and requires its own selected study.

    1. Internal state that survives a change of interaction¶

    Zwanzig's additional elimination, finite-bath and response-chain examples (printed pages 18-29, 160-164, 193-197 and 205-207) suggest a constructive use of memory: derive a few internal coordinates from the response they preserve, and initialize them from the actual constituents. Memory can then be a compact part of the effective object's state rather than a long recorded history. The book's ensemble assumptions and bath interpretations do not transfer automatically to TNFR.

    The linear observation implementation already constructs an exact sufficient linear state for a fixed generator and output, as well as coordinate memory with hidden initialization. The organized reservoir already shows locally attracting geometries on fixed-J leaves under its supplied zero-leakage auxiliary law. These results do not yet derive that reservoir from the interior of an arbitrary organization or make a state sufficient for every new connection.

    Existing results also cover more than a fixed isolated flow: finite diffusion/REMESH words have a sufficient retained history state; the nonlinear sine interface carries hidden initialization across a finite impulse family; and causal component composition bounds substitution through both form and phase input channels after feedback. The bounded contact-continuation record already carries the original preparation and a contact-history bit into its four allowed queries. This is a source-specific finite prediction, not an indefinitely reusable autonomous state or proof of a minimal encoding. Their domains and work/identity premises remain essential. Reusable interfaces and finite event words are therefore not missing general achievements.

    Consumer-specific question: does a proposed smaller state preserve the additional boundary response or continuation that an existing result does not cover, with a useful cost/error advantage? A mode invisible in isolation can become visible through a new port. Choose the permitted inputs, outputs and continuations before eliminating it. Full state remains a valid reference; compression is not a prerequisite for source or identity admission. Reuse the collective action criterion: if two fine states have the same retained state but the event exposes different successor observations, the old state cannot determine that successor.

    For such a new consumer, compare composition followed by elimination with elimination followed by composition, for the same joint law and nonzero hidden preparation. Preserve any input feedthrough actually present, source correlations, registration, clock, continuous storage and separate attachment work. The existing causal sine map has no instantaneous feedthrough of its continuous inputs; its declared form jumps are handled separately. Existing mediator composition and fixed history packets are controls, not missing achievements. The new result would be sufficiency across the selected additional continuation, or a witness identifying the extra information it requires. If exact compression returns the full state, that is an informative answer.

    A further optimization is available only when nonlinear hidden feedback is actually resolved by the observation. Constituent memory already derives quadratic interface corrections and higher-order obstructions; compressed memory already has a useful static-variance forecast. For a justified tangent, deterministic products of hidden coordinates evolve linearly in product space. One could apply the existing observation method to retain only combinations needed for the chosen quadratic output, with the known nonlinear remainder propagated separately. These products are not statistical covariances unless an ensemble is supplied. A moving carrier needs its actual time-dependent coefficients or a proved common invariant space. The test is a strict state/error advantage over the successful static forecast and a same-law full-state reference; no advantage means no reason to replace the existing method. The exact deterministic Gram closure already retains its own realizability and continuation premises. The proposed gain here is a useful dynamic subset for the specified nonlinear response, not the first occurrence of product-state closure in TNFR.

    2. Useful imperfect collective organizations¶

    The imperfect-interface result already supplies both an information-loss lower bound and observation-only upper error bounds for its directed pure-EPI law. Useful prediction after loss of exact closure is established there. The star below is a different possible consumer; its nonlinear coefficients and event bounds need their own argument, not a transfer of that result's constants.

    Golubitsky/Stewart's coupled-cell and spatiotemporal viewpoint suggests looking at the inputs received by a group, rather than relying only on global graph automorphisms. TNFR already goes beyond that suggestion: the star construction in collective phase dynamics includes a non-orbital grouping with an exact input criterion, retained collective state and transverse control. Different degrees and capacities need not prevent a collective description when the complete rows balance.

    New question: how much departure from that balance can a useful reduced prediction tolerate after an actual structural action? Start with the local capacity-reset example already known to break star tangency, under the same weighted-form/bare-sine law, fixed support and original clock. This is initially a mathematical primary-capacity reset; it does not establish execution of Silence. A canonical action additionally requires actual represented outputs, history, grammar and context admission. Retain the resulting transverse source and compare the reduced response against the full post-event law. The desired result is an output-error bound over a fixed domain and horizon, and a decision about which additional mode must be kept. An exact all-state quotient and a finite approximate description are different successful outcomes; the latter need not require perfect geometry.

    The smallest informative control is the exactly balanced family with its known zero defect, followed by one declared balance-breaking reset. A merely small instantaneous defect is insufficient if transverse dynamics amplifies it. Use the actual complete propagator or a justified metric bound and retain event initialization. If the error budget fails, the outcome localizes the missing transverse coordinate or usable time window. The benefit would be predictive organizations robust to imperfections, rather than another example of exact synchrony. It does not show spontaneous acquisition of the group.

    3. An active organization as a responsive component¶

    Pikovsky/Rosenblum/Kurths, sections 8.1-8.2, make reciprocal amplitude and timing effects a useful source of questions. TNFR already derives collective-wave timing effects and reciprocal response, in addition to its exact phase-advance branch and finite retention. Neither phase advances, distinct symmetry-related periods nor the existence of that feedback is a new proposal here.

    New question: does one actual, admitted local Emission action on the exact phase-advance branch produce a predictable finite change in relative tick times while the organization's activity and identity remain observable? This joins a structural action to a functional property of the organization. A marked port can distinguish members previously identified by a permutation; the collective state may have to retain that association and the internal phase. It cannot silently use a smaller symmetry quotient after the port has broken its observational equivalence.

    The first decision is whether the real event's tangent has a nonzero effect on a preselected relative crossing-time observation. Then, if justified, bound one finite event image and its full-state remainder using the existing branch retention. Retain amplitude, neutral shear and collective perturbations. Conservative pulses do not automatically admit the attracting-cycle phase-reset model in the book. The branch theorem also supplies no arbitrary practical amplitude radius. Compare with no action and a common timing shift; admit any uniform form control under the actual law. Refresh the selected complete-law pressure after the event and preserve public operator/context requirements.

    A positive result would add a structural-action-to-timing response that can be used in composition. A zero first-order projection closes only that linear discriminator; it does not exclude a higher-order finite response. Such a response would need a separately justified question, not an automatic extension. An unavailable crossing rejects that selected observation on its declared domain. Neither result excludes all active organization.

    Higher-harmonic examples in Synchronization section 12.3.1 also motivate a bounded alternative: test one second internal envelope pattern against the already derived quartic pulse interaction. First establish inequivalence and stationarity, then its constrained and full transverse behavior. An existing sideband in a stability calculation is not automatically another carrier organization. Such a result is lower priority unless it enables a new response or acquisition; a prepared-pattern catalogue is not the objective.

    4. Composable scale descriptions and response classes¶

    Barenblatt section 5.3 (printed pages 102-108) links a specified generalized homogeneity to transformations of parameters and the response. Sections 7.1-7.2 (123-132) use agreement of direct and staged geometric subdivisions to motivate a scaling relation under their regularity and similarity assumptions. This suggests a concrete TNFR test of composition, not a claim that lossy graph aggregation is the book's invertible transformation group.

    New question: is there a family of organized descriptions whose first output is a valid input for a second grouping, with compatible response, initialization, boundary, storage and clock? Permit inherited registration and internal state instead of demanding the same scalar formula at every level. Compare the final output of direct and staged reduction, exactly or with a controlled error. First check that both operations are admitted; multiplying parameter factors while ignoring their domains is not this comparison.

    The registered inheritance map already changes form, clock and constitutive coefficients. Its output is not automatically another C9 organization. Network compatibility already shows why valid pair charts can fail to coexist around a cycle while a richer description closes. The nested P5 example already separates exact first reduction from memory at the second. Preserve those controls. The new capability would be a composable family with an admissible second step, or a precise description of the additional information needed for it.

    A related question concerns dynamical response classes: can different admitted internal laws or geometries have the same bounded response after justified state/clock transformations? The constitutive counterfamilies match some targets and local derivatives while retaining different nonlinear profiles. Select one existing alternative and a fixed response domain to distinguish coordinate equivalence, finite approximate similarity and a nonlinear difference. Only afterward ask whether another grouping reduces that difference. Use either a proved exact observational equivalence or a declared family compared with one reference and a uniform error budget. Pairwise agreement within a tolerance is not generally transitive and does not define an equivalence class. Propagate inherited errors through the next grouping. Neither construction alone establishes asymptotic universality; a geometric exponent cannot replace its response contract.

    Reuse structural_morphism.py, the existing organized inheritance and memory owners. The supplied graphs in multiscale/hierarchical.py and the descriptive fits in fields.py are different instruments. No new hierarchy engine or renormalization API is justified before a specific composition test succeeds and a consumer benefits. Formation of the hierarchy remains separate from representing one that has been supplied.

    5. Persistence, adaptation and a durable change of organization¶

    Wagensberg's passive/active/new-independence discussion (printed pages 112-118) suggests comparing retention by boundary protection, internal change and a change of identity. These are questions for TNFR, not a biological selection law or a reason to require increasing complexity. A retained object should also retain the declared interactive response; isolation alone can make a retention score misleading.

    The native foundation already compares different capacity laws and establishes different attracting destinations. Acquired response already separates organizations under a single dynamic-capacity law and different preparations. Capacity diffusion smooths capacity contrasts; it is not already feedback responding to an identity error or environmental signal. A stronger adaptive-compensation claim would need an independently specified feedback law and complete balance, distinct from the engine's gated adaptation policy.

    New question with a stronger generative payoff: can one finite admitted action switch an already acquired native twist into retained consensus under the same resumed DA1 law, while the unacted continuation keeps the twist? This would connect acquisition, structural transformation and durable response, rather than merely demonstrate two coexisting prepared equilibria.

    The first gate is an event-image question: does a fixed work-bounded action family on the actual acquisition image intersect a certified source-to-consensus domain, or does an invariant/retention bound exclude that range? Begin there, before any transit. Existing capacity-selection preparations are not that intersection proof. Preserve evolving capacity, native resultant-domain admission and event work. The held-capacity native executor does not implement DA1 merely by a flag, so a later evaluation needs its actual execution path.

    Success would be a retained organizational state change under one resumed law. It would not establish autonomous occurrence or a hysteresis cycle. Failure can quantify robustness or locate a missing capture certificate. Existing selector hysteresis is policy memory and does not establish this dynamical mechanism. A comparison of boundary protection with redistribution is useful only when the selected response and surrounding perturbation require it, with resource and timing effects separated from a new identity claim.

    Final synthesis: select a missing capability, then reuse its methods¶

    The final review consolidates the earlier opportunities instead of opening five method-development projects. State sufficiency, imperfect-interface error and composable descriptions are obligations of one selected response problem. Their theorems and model premises remain distinct. Pulse response is a possible functional application; durable acquired reorganization has the additional source-to-destination obligation. Neither is established by a compact representation alone.

    The closing audit found that causal component substitution, finite impulse families, source-aware memory and useful imperfect observations already have scoped results in this repository. The priority is therefore not to invent a general interface framework. Use those results for an actual missing consumer; derive a smaller state only if it reduces a declared cost or improves useful accuracy while preserving the requested prediction. Exact compression can return the full state, and an existing finite/static approximation can already be sufficient.

    Three distinctions that make the combined methods useful¶

    Prediction and generation. A sufficient interface predicts what an organization does under its admitted preparation and interaction. Acquisition establishes how it enters an identity, and an occurrence law establishes why an admitted event happens. Wagensberg's function/selection discussion helps ask about retained consequences; it does not make a supplied TNFR contact autonomous or select an optimal score. The ontology already owns these distinctions. A source family has no probability distribution unless one is independently specified and justified.

    Exact equivalence and useful approximation. A quotient must respect the chosen source domain, laws, port marks, events and outputs. A frame in which a pattern repeats need not preserve its externally marked interaction. An averaged symmetry need not be instantaneous symmetry. For approximate prediction, use a common reference and a uniform error budget; pairwise closeness at a tolerance is not generally transitive. Propagate error through the actual next operation, including feedback, instead of assigning a fresh tolerance at each grouping. Finite output robustness is not a theorem of structural stability of the full flow.

    A useful finite window and an asymptotic claim. Before proposing longer integration or another scale, compare in the same clock the domain/entry and response-resolution requirements with the source, reduction, event and numerical error bounds. If a discarded contribution must decay, establish that decay; if phase shear or cumulative forcing limits the window, retain that limit. Barenblatt's intermediate-asymptotic viewpoint motivates checking their common interval. It does not impose scale separation on exact closures. A finite response needs no general attracting cycle or all-time stability theorem. Conversely, frequency locking, a new peak or a timing shift alone does not prove a new identity or a durable switch.

    The P5 memory bound already retains the hidden source even at zero convolution window. The imperfect-interface bounds already bracket what can be predicted from incomplete information in their own model. They exemplify useful finite descriptions; neither their constants nor the claim of useful accuracy transfers to another law automatically.
