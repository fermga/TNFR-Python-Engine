    Bibliographic methods for TNFR foundations¶

    The supplied books help choose and combine methods for a precise TNFR question. Their strongest common lesson is to retain the relationship between the complete dynamics, actual preparation, selected observation and the claim being made. A compatible shape, an acquired organization, its persistence and a sufficient description of its response require different evidence.

    This chapter owns the literature-to-method synthesis. Definitions and proofs remain at their linked owners; the research program alone selects work. The applications below are methodological judgments, not new TNFR theorems or physical identifications. The final synthesis consolidates the three earlier selective reviews. The source-transport reading adds two books for source-to-regime obligations. The general reuse assessment connects their reduction and numerical methods to shared engine responsibilities. The nine-source integration revisits the complete selected methodological bibliography and derives a source-aware accuracy budget for structural simplification. The held-input extension then preserves the current model's sustained relative form while bounding transient error.

    What transfers, and what must first be represented¶

    The common distinction is between a well-formed request, its admission in the current state, its complete effect, and its occurrence policy. TNFR already has owners for all four, with unresolved constitutive choices. The useful comparison is therefore against actual actions and consumed state, not a translation of glyphs into another alphabet.

    The comparison also needs the right types: an L-system module or bigraph node can represent a constituent, while a TNFR glyph names an action. The natural candidate correspondence is NFR state to the rewritten structure and operator instances to guarded transformations. Equating component labels with operator names would conflate the object with the mechanism that changes it.

    Method Concrete TNFR use Remaining obligation
    Guarded attributed rewriting Describe the numerical and structural domain of an operator, its actual target binding, resets and retained history. Pull successor guards back through an ordered word. An embedding must preserve and reflect admission and effects, or explicitly offer only one-way simulation. Numerical thresholds and Python callbacks are not automatically finite structural application conditions.
    Dependency and conflict analysis Include absent-support conditions, neighbor reads, lifecycle and refresh effects when deciding which actions interfere. Reuse existing conservative stage resources. A fixed list of operator-name pairs cannot decide all state-dependent conflicts. Preserve the declared sequential or snapshot schedule.
    Separation of nesting and connectivity Keep THOL ancestry, collective membership, potential access and realized UM transport distinct. Genealogy need not be containment. An ordinary forest cannot directly represent overlapping memberships; sharing variants exist but require their own semantics and theorem hypotheses. Direction, multiedges, weights, attributes and history need a specified encoding.
    Contextual equivalence Require a collective NFR description to preserve future permitted boundary actions and responses. Declare the interface and context class; prove admission, outputs and successor closure. Importing a congruence theorem requires its actual structural hypotheses.
    Parallel developmental rules Make the generation boundary and all-prestate reads explicit, including when a newborn becomes an eligible target. Existing TNFR stages have operator-specific merge laws. A symbolic generation is neither a physical interval nor permission to activate a newborn immediately.
    Explicit environment exchange Associate returned observations with the actual target, source state, boundary and clock before consumption. A reply is an input requiring admission, not authenticated structural evidence merely because a protocol returned it. Existing shared readers remain authoritative.

    There are two complementary uses. Formal composition can reduce redundant work by deriving a successor's required preparation instead of searching parameters blindly. Structural interfaces can show when an apparently local rule actually consumes wider context. Neither benefit requires adding another rule registry or changing the scientific meaning of an operator.

    Conditional consequences integrated into the operator owner¶

    The guarded-action analysis contains the original TNFR-facing deductions and their proofs:

    1. The domain of first a then b is D_a intersect inverse_image(T_a,D_b). It includes the actual history and support updates. The existing birth/contact preparation illustrates why two IL steps admit EN's pressure gate in one branch while three are needed in the matched no-contact branch.
    2. Deterministic actions with stable complete read/write sets commute under the stated disjointness and frame hypotheses. Two half-mixing EN actions provide an exact counterexample to replacing sequential updates by a common snapshot merely because target writes differ. Shared-partner UM requests also conflict through the isolated-partner guard.
    3. The existing partial-action descent theorem extends to interleaved admitted boundary actions when their admission, successors and compared outputs also descend. This is a conditional contextual-replacement criterion, not a proof that any currently displayed pattern is a collective NFR.
    4. A transport-only observation cannot uniquely select an unseen isolated child without additional distinguishing information. Actual parent/child metadata can supply that information. Consuming it as candidate access is a policy choice distinct from creating transport or requiring an event.
    5. Identical untimed rules admit distinct clocks. Under a separately declared finite-state Markov jump model, common positive rate rescaling changes holding times without changing enabled actions. Match multiplicity affects rates; projecting it away needs a timed-state argument, not just event descent.

    These are analytical consequences and uses of existing finite controls. They were independently reviewed; no new engine trajectory was evaluated. The categorical concurrency and bigraph congruence theorems remain external tools with unproved representation bridges to the full TNFR runtime.

    Research decision and limits of the analogy¶

    State-derived candidate access has since been integrated in the birth-association consumer. It uses actual NFR-held birth information, abstains on empty or ambiguous access, and retains live UM admission. Parent selection, invocation and the access policy remain supplied; ancestry is neither contact nor an obligation to couple. That completed dependency must not be mistaken for another active task. The program alone selects the next operator function.

    Next in usefulness are guarded compositions and context-preserving collective interfaces, followed by proved execution optimizations. For the unresolved roles of Expansion, Contraction, Mutation and Recursivity, these methods help specify what is created, retained, transformed or observed. They do not decide that synthesis means deleting nodes, that copying state creates memory, or that a scalar form is sufficient at the next scale.

    Repeated rules can produce repeated structure without establishing geometric fractality. A fractal claim needs a geometric interpretation, declared finite-scale or limiting object, scale range and stated similarity or dimension property. A biological growth image also supplies no TNFR stability, autonomous occurrence or physical identification. Conversely, a finite alphabet can have rich generative power; its size alone establishes neither insufficiency nor completeness. The open TNFR question is the faithful generative reach of its specified actions under their grammar, state domains, access and occurrence laws.

    Existing methodological coverage¶

    The selected integrations cover local stability, signed boundaries, symmetry, projection, memory, scaling, time-scale separation and viability. The two-port exclusion now closes one source-dependent selection question. Its unresolved outgoing destination does not automatically make another trajectory the best priority. A bounded literature assessment may also question a premise, connect existing results or formulate a testable hypothesis before a method has failed.

    Judge a reading by its concrete contribution and limits: a discriminating hypothesis, a controlled simplification, a missing proof tool or a counterexample. Keep the scientific question and its preserved evidence explicit. Neither a bibliographic addition nor an appealing example automatically selects a new law, controller, numerical campaign or physical interpretation. The research program owns the live priority; the joint acquisition/maintenance analysis below retains its historical scope.

    The recent reversible EPI integrations remain useful quantitative results: a reduced prediction can preserve a held profile and retain transient error. They do not transfer to the nonlinear phase/form law. Match source, boundary, clock, initialization and output before combining any of these methods.

    Complementary contributions¶

    The hybrid model distinguishes flow data (C,F) from jump data (D,G) and continuous time from the jump index. A solution must satisfy each consumed domain, while existence, uniqueness and selection of an occurrence remain separate questions. Memory examples show why a logical mode may belong to the state: equal continuous coordinates need not determine the next action. The earlier viability reading already included hybrid resets; the new book adds a systematic solution and well-posedness framework rather than introducing hybrid reasoning from zero.

    The graph-transformation book adds systematic backward construction of application conditions and typed attributed examples to the already reviewed 2008/2009 papers. It distinguishes guaranteeing a constraint from preserving one already true. Changing the permitted attribute morphisms can change the meaning of a constraint. The later paper's nested-condition concurrency premises cannot be replaced by an unconditional overview theorem.

    For TNFR, the immediate common use is the existing ordinary partial-map calculation: for action T with full domain D and desired postcondition Q, require z in D and Q(T(z)); apply it recursively at every prefix. A preservation implication I(z) => I(T(z)) does not admit invalid entry states. Recovering AL eligibility is a desired postcondition, not an invariant that SHA should preserve. Own grammar, lifecycle, history, configured factors, actual targets and ports remain part of the consumed state.

    Obligation Consequence for the existing engine
    State and guards Retain member-level state and own context; a mean form or latency flag cannot stand for all successor conditions.
    Full event effect Derive NAV pressure and phase changes alongside capacity recovery, then check the actual AL/EN/RA domains.
    Preservation versus continuation Verify form/support at the SHA jump separately from later permitted motion and the existence of a recovery prefix.
    Time and occurrence Keep runtime time, own operator-step count and UTC lifecycle metadata distinct. A supplied finite schedule is not an endogenous clock.
    Failure alternatives Retain capacity, pressure, phase or grammar obstruction and atomic rejection; an admitted event need not enable the requested successor.

    Symmetry reduces work when the law, state and question match¶

    Golubitsky and Stewart supply a systematic language for symmetry and invariant families. Newman shows why graph modes become dynamically informative only after specifying the node and coupling laws. For a TNFR use, distinguish:

    Object What has to be checked
