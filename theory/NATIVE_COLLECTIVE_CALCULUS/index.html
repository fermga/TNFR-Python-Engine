    Native collective calculus¶

    This owner combines conditional moment identities, finite admission margins and bounded collective observations for the general native operator runtime. The source and branch studies own the underlying preparations and counterexamples. These are detached mathematical descriptions of the declared execution path, not a new runtime or a derived occurrence law. The aggregate transition owner separately proves a restricted lossy reduction under the actual uniform-capacity native policy, retaining its admission margins and discarded-output boundary. The research program remains the sole research queue.

    Conditional collective calculus for five native operators¶

    Emission (AL), Reception (EN), Coherence (IL), Resonance (RA) and Transition (NAV) share an exact conditional event-plus-held-flow description on doubled C5. Unequal constituent forms are allowed. The event mean and internal contrast have distinct roles; the complete native state also retains constituent-associated memory. This synthesis reuses the block descent, four-operator extension, Transition map and correlated Reception results. It does not assert that the default selector realizes every assignment of these operators.

    Domain and request origin. Keep the block-descent execution premises: unit undirected doubled C5, consecutive nonadjacent twins, whole-pair order, default mixed pressure/Si and direct glyph execution, strict live grammar, hard clipping, live built-in zero Gamma and one Euler substep delta=1/16. No external nodes, custom callbacks, optional mathematics engine or optional Resonance tracking are included. Automatic REMESH must independently abstain; insufficient retained stable_frac history is one sufficient reason. It is not removed from the law. Each node receives exactly one effective request.

    Forms are finite admitted signed scalars (including admitted uniform-real BEPI), with common nonempty semantic kind. Capacities are positive and paired; primitive phases are paired and admit an inherited common lift of width less than pi. Every proposed event and flow form lies strictly inside its rails. Post-Resonance capacities also lie inside their rails: integration uses the amplified value before the later clamp. Consumed histories, counters, rates, clocks, normalized diagnostics and produced arithmetic retain their admission. The equations below are ideal-real identities over represented coefficients, not universal binary64 enclosures.

    Both effective requests in each pair must agree and pass their own live grammar, identity and operator gates. Retain their actual origin, including selection preparation and the increment-before-comparison AL/EN lag priority. A supplied paired transcript can test the algebra conditionally; it is neither a default-selector witness nor a derived occurrence law. Prepared requests can differ within a pair because its pressures and Si differ. Rejection is sequential: a successful first twin or earlier pair is not rolled back by a later rejection. The theorem concerns successful complete calls.
