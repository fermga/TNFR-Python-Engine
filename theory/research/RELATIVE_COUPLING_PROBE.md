## 2. Finite observation and cancellation

`nu_4/d_4=13/32`. Read its phase in calibrated radian units, with initial
readings at each post-word boundary and final readings at `h`. Branch-constant

\[
q_j=[Y_{j,\mathrm{high}}(h)-Y_{j,\mathrm{high}}(0)]
    -[Y_{j,\mathrm{low}}(h)-Y_{j,\mathrm{low}}(0)].
\]

`r=g_5/g_0=w_A/2`, with `9/20<r<1/2`.

The shared [initial-state envelope](../../src/tnfr/physics/relational_sine_support.py)

\[

|q_j^{\mathrm{nominal}}-hg_j|\le R
 =\frac{\Delta a AB^2h^3}{6D}=\frac1{209551360}.
\]

This is a full nonlinear finite-time statement. It uses neither a frozen phase
nor a tangent trajectory. It identifies a relative interaction coefficient
under the supplied law, not a spatial distance or a fundamental physical force.
