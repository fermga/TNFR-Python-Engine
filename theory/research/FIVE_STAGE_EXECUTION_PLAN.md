# Nodal research execution plan: four foundational stages

**Revised 2026-09-27. Sole active research queue.** The primary question is
whether a justified TNFR structure and dynamics can generate interacting
coherence patterns and, eventually, explain measured collective properties.
Physical constituents emerging from such patterns remain an open hypothesis.
Primitive EPI need not be directly identified with a laboratory magnitude.

The method is **F1 state -> F2 complete laws -> F3 joint consistency ->
F4 discriminating prediction**. These are repeatable admission stages, not a
claim that all foundations have been settled. Completed results stay with
their proof owners; historical stage names do not reopen their campaigns.

The filename is retained for compatibility. P1-P5 below remain the supporting
measurement/evaluation protocol, not five competing primary research stages.
The [portfolio](../../TNFR_lineas_de_investigacion.txt) classifies branches;
[the strategy](../NODAL_RESEARCH_STRATEGY.md) explains priorities and reuse;
the [theory catalog](../README.md#theory-to-execution) connects proofs to code,
tests and public interfaces. None maintains another task queue.

## 1. Objective and scientific acceptance

Establish a minimal, explicit family of nodal models whose predictions follow
from stated premises. Separate definitions, independent hypotheses, derived
consequences, configured policies, numerical observations and physical evidence.
The nodal equation itself is a revisable model premise; its written product
does not uniquely select the state space, pressure, phase/capacity laws,
support or event schedule.

Some independent postulates are unavoidable in a proposed theory. Calling
one emergent requires an actual derivation from previously stated structure
and dynamics, not a new name or a successful simulation. External mathematical
tools may test the model; imported physical equations cannot become unstated
TNFR laws. A useful conditional family need not be uniquely selected by
current premises. Unresolved alternatives must remain identifiable.

Completion of F1-F4 means a scoped mathematical foundation and a testable
prediction, not proof that TNFR is a fundamental physical theory. Physical
identification needs the independent P1-P5 bridge. Infinite pattern lifetime,
substrate creation from nothing, a Hamiltonian representation and universal
operator selection are not prerequisites for every restricted result.

**Present decision.** Support-event accounting and nonselection are established
for state-preserving unit-edge events. Requiring zero-supply passivity is an
additional premise: pure addition then needs equal endpoint form and phase,
and a continuous addition intensity on an open full-state domain must vanish.
An atomic bridge relocation now has an explicit open domain that lowers
storage and retains the two-ring recovery theorem, without adding a reservoir
or changing nodal coordinates. The [next work package](#current-g3-gate)
isolates support-law closure: candidate choice and event timing. Identity
and the budget permit the event; neither has supplied its cause.

## 3. Next work package: support-law closure, choice and clock

**Question.** Once a passive identity-preserving relocation is admitted, what
do the complete nodal state, its symmetries and declared clock determine about
which candidate occurs and when? What independent constitutive premise, if any,
would justify a nontrivial occurrence law?

The [relocation theorem](../nodal/RELATIONAL_PATTERN_COMPOSITION.md#identity-preserving-bridge-relocation)
has removed the immediate budget/recovery obstruction on a stated open set.
Its explicit preparation and two controls need no replay. The problem is now
causal closure, not another topology, amplitude, memory or basin search. Fixed
edge count, preserved internal cycles and atomic exchange remain premises.

### Fixed scope and work order

1. **Specify the missing row.** Retain the existing full state, held capacities,
   fixed internal cycles and finite candidate bridge exchanges. Distinguish
   event admissibility, candidate selection, occurrence, waiting time and any
   consumed event history. A graph label or insertion order cannot be a hidden
   physical coordinate.
2. **Derive restrictions before proposing a rule.** Test relabeling, common
   form/phase-frame covariance, structural clock changes, passivity and recovery
   admission. Reuse the exact finite-action selector owner where its declared
   action applies. A symmetry obstruction is not a stochastic law; a storage
   difference has storage units and is not itself an occurrence rate.
3. **Test remaining freedom explicitly.** Exhibit compatible different choices
   or clock laws if they exist, or prove a forced restriction. Retain abstention.
   Compare complete candidate models, not only scores. Any added deterministic
   guard, intensity, randomness or reservoir must be named as an independent
   premise and accompanied by a discriminating consequence before evaluation.
4. **Make a bounded decision.** Admit at most one independently motivated
   extension for a later prospective test, or retain support changes as supplied
   interventions with a proved closure boundary. Do not manufacture an autonomous
   selector merely to continue this branch. Integrate only derived reusable
   restrictions; no automatic default change follows.

### Reuse and validation

- [Composition, event accounting and relocation](../nodal/RELATIONAL_PATTERN_COMPOSITION.md#identity-preserving-bridge-relocation)
  own the admitted event; the shared
  [relational observer](../../src/tnfr/physics/relational_observations.py) and
  [transport reset](../../src/tnfr/physics/support_transport.py) own computations.
- [Local recovery](../nodal/RELATIONAL_EXCHANGE_ADMISSION.md#relational-local-recovery)
  and [single-bridge interaction](../nodal/RELATIONAL_EXCHANGE_ADMISSION.md#relational-region-interaction)
  provide scoped identity and support results. The two-bridge capture API is
  not a certificate for this support.
- Reuse static [event controls](../../tests/test_relational_attachment.py),
  [selector symmetry](../../src/tnfr/physics/selector_symmetry.py) and exact SDK
  report export. Preserve frozen responses and source archives.

**Stop condition.** An explicit premise/restriction/freedom ledger and a concrete
admission or nonselection result. If no independently justified event premise
emerges, close autonomous support selection as unresolved and retain the valid
intervention results; do not launch another parameter search. Repeated-switching
stability, continuous conductance, new capacity laws and physical intake are
outside this package.

## 4. Reusable F1-F4 admission method

Apply these gates to a changed model or a new claim. They do not reopen
completed reference studies automatically.

| Stage | Required deliverable | Acceptance boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| F1: state and equivalence | Domains, units, primitive/derived/held roles, zero and branch limits, target observation and discarded information | A regular chart change, fixed-law symmetry and lossy observation require different proofs. The tetrad is not a complete state in general. |
| F2: complete conditional law | Every consumed evolution/held/input/event row and a premise -> restriction -> derived term -> remaining freedom record | No response-derived pressure, hidden forcing or telemetry relabeled as a derived selector. State a well-posed domain and any unselected constitutive freedom. |
| F3: joint consistency | Candidate-wide covariance, boundary, balance, observation and event verdict | Check the actual complete law and shared implementation; combine no theorems with incompatible graph, capacity, phase or clock hypotheses. |
| F4: prospective discrimination | Analytic prediction and bounded control, or a proved observational equivalence/obstruction | Freeze preparation, law, clock, observation, horizon, source and numerical budget before response evaluation. Do not retune an evaluated prediction. |

<a id="prospective-constitutive-admission"></a>
### Constitutive and integration discipline

Hold support, capacity or input only as explicit laws. Pressure refreshed from
state differs from held-pressure intervals and named operator jumps. Reuse
the existing [pressure premises](../nodal/PRESSURE_CONSTITUTIVE_SCOPE.md),
[joint parameter response](../nodal/JOINT_PARAMETER_RESPONSE.md),
[capacity admission](../CAPACITY_LOCALIZATION_BALANCE.md#capacity-law-admission)
and [clock covariance](../NODAL_PARAMETER_FOUNDATIONS.md#pressure-clock-full-state-closure).
A synchronizing phase alone need not define a monotone clock.

Admitted derived mechanisms belong in their shared engine owners and consuming
interfaces. A fitted gain, comparison model, theorem requiring extra premises
or failed hypothesis does not become a default dynamics change. Existing
cotangent and relational models keep their different storage/capacity premises.
Useful restrictions need not wait for a proof of unique ontology.

## 5. Shared variable and evidence contract

For EPI, capacity, pressure, phase, support, clock and every input/history
coordinate actually consumed, record three distinct entries:

| Entry | Required content |
| --- | --- |
| Mathematical definition | Domain, chart, units, dependencies; primitive, derived, held or diagnostic role; valid zero/branch limits |
| Operational identification | Observation/preparation, frame, uncertainty and identifiable combinations; hidden-state treatment where needed |
| Evolution | Independently specified pressure and auxiliary/event laws; derived versus assumed terms; execution path and temporal convention |

A latent microscopic coordinate need not have its own sensor. Physical claims
still need a justified collective observation map and uncertainty propagation.
Fitting on separate calibration data is permissible under a declared rule;
it does not make a fitted factor independently measured or a law uniquely true.
`P=x_dot/nu` from the evaluated response is retrospective reconstruction, not
prediction; it is undefined at zero capacity and incomplete with forcing.

For each result retain source/configuration, support/node order, inputs,
seed when relevant, precision/backend, refresh policy, actual events,
observation and residuals. Use exact arithmetic for finite algebra when
appropriate; it does not certify transcendental floating-point execution.
A history-sensitive runtime needs retained execution state, not a recipe-only
checkpoint. Reuse the shared integrator, pressure, field and evidence owners.

## 7. Source audit, stopping rules and resumption

Read current definitions together with actual source, tests, experiments and
relevant historical bytes. A historical name such as canonical, emergent or
verified supplies no proof. Record what was inspected, its model, result class,
reusable calculation and excluded interpretation. The
[audit ledger](../NODAL_RESEARCH_STRATEGY.md#four-foundation-source-audit)
is the evidence map; proofs stay in their existing topical owners.

Keep historical files recoverable by commit/blob/hash; preserve uncommitted
history before rewriting it. Do not restore an obsolete instrument simply
because its original question was relevant. Inspect retained artifacts without
claiming their dirty-source producer was reproduced on today's tree.

Stop a branch when its proposed conclusion is already refuted in scope,
when the calculation cannot distinguish alternatives, or when success must
be supplied as an input. A named independent revision can reopen it. Do not
continue tightening a secondary bound solely because its machinery exists.

Resume at the [support-law closure work package](#current-g3-gate), retaining
the completed passive relocation and recovery result. Earlier C4/C8 continuations, C5
searches, C6 global-runtime certification, derivative ladders, further memory
sweeps and broad physical intake are parked. Historical O/S/G/B milestone
labels are not additional task queues. Valid results stay in topical owners;
superseded planning and retirement provenance belong to the [archive](archive/README.md).

Update this board when an acceptance condition is met or priority changes,
linking the result owner rather than copying its proof or run log. Formation,
identity, physical identification and substrate origin remain the long-term
questions; the stages make their prerequisites reviewable without promising
the outcome. Completion means a scoped result and its required integration,
not proof that TNFR is a fundamental physical theory.
