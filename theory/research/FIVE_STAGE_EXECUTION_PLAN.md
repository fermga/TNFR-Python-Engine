## 1. Target, boundaries and completion

Test whether coherent patterns, their persistence and their interactions can
be derived from explicitly specified TNFR structure and dynamics. The nodal
identity `dEPI/dt=nu_f*DeltaNFR` determines a form rate after its inputs are
specified; it does not by itself select pressure, capacity, phase, support,
initial conditions or a physical interpretation.

Every proposed mechanism must distinguish derived relations, constitutive
assumptions, preparation, boundary input, numerical implementation and observed
outputs. Pressure must not be reconstructed retrospectively from a desired
response. A declared graph, oscillator or operator policy is not spontaneous
formation. No current result establishes emergence of physical particles or
all observed reality from TNFR.

Empirical work must use publicly available data or observations feasible on
Earth's surface with a workstation or ordinary research laboratory. Astronomical,
satellite and large-facility data are excluded by the user's constraint.
Physical correspondence requires an independently specified measurement map
and reserved predictions; passing software tests is not that correspondence.

A completed study can support, reject or leave its hypothesis inconclusive.
Keep implementation completion separate from scientific support. Preserve
negative results and source-bound evidence; do not rewrite old outcomes.

## 6. P3 — Make and evaluate a reserved prediction

Save content-hashed predictions before revealing the reserved response.
Separate open-loop forecasting from measurement-fed one-step scores. Evaluate
all reserved runs without changing coefficients, thresholds or normalization.
Report incompatibility, support in scope or inconclusiveness under the admitted
measurement model. Agreement with a shared classical diffusion limit is not
proof of a uniquely TNFR physical mechanism.

## 7. P4 — Partial observation and derived memory

Declare the observation map and legitimately available initial information.
Reuse the exact eliminated-state kernel and its hidden-initial-state source;
a configured REMESH echo is not automatically that kernel. A reduced predictor
may not consume the unobserved evaluation state. Require a sufficient-state
contract, an error enclosure or a same-observation/different-future obstruction.

## 8. P5 — Maintenance, recovery and transitions

Pure EPI diffusion on fixed connected reciprocal support with positive fixed
capacity relaxes toward uniform form. Any claimed differentiated maintenance
must retain its declared source, boundary, history or admitted additional law.

### P5.1. Fixed-target maintenance and recovery

Specify original identity, amplitude, phase, boundary exchange and observation
metric before perturbation. Distinguish passive retention, driven persistence,
recovery and autonomous maintenance. Post-event recentering must not redefine
a failed target as success.

### P5.2. A causal regime study

Fix the model, control parameter, observable and finite-size protocol before
sampling. A classifier threshold, branch switch or static ensemble alone does
not demonstrate a dynamical phase transition or a universality class.
