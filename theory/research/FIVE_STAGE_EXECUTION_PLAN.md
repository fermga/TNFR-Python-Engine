# Nodal research execution plan

This is the sole research task queue. The objective is to establish when
coherent, interacting patterns can form and retain an identity under justified
TNFR dynamics, then confront independently measurable consequences.
Constituent emergence and a uniquely selected physical law remain hypotheses.

The [portfolio](../../TNFR_lineas_de_investigacion.txt) classifies branches;
the [strategy](../NODAL_RESEARCH_STRATEGY.md) explains their dependencies.
Definitions, completed results, implementations and tests belong to the
[theory-to-execution map](../README.md#theory-to-execution).
The method is **F1 state -> F2 complete law -> F3 consistency -> F4 discrimination**.
P1-P5 below governs physical evaluation; it is not another active queue.

The governing priority is to identify and justify the laws and hypotheses
of TNFR one at a time, using the constructed mathematical organizations
to reassess and strengthen the foundations. Definitions and foundational
premises, including the nodal row, remain revisable with explicit scope;
completion of a model does not by itself require replacing that row.
Further response calculations serve a named foundational or generative
question; increasing precision or adding an observable alone does not
determine the next task. The intended route remains formation and interaction
of organizations, then independently testable physical properties, including
the eventual constituent hypothesis.

Newly admitted organizations can motivate new collective properties or
effective laws; the present inventory is not assumed complete. Apply the
[ontology's property/control criterion](../EMERGENT_ONTOLOGY.md#research-target-physical-properties-of-collective-patterns)
within the active gate before promoting such a possibility to a result.
