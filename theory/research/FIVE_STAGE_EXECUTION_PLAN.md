# Nodal research execution plan

**Current status: 2026-09-20.** This is the sole active research queue.
The objective is a predictive account of coherent patterns from justified nodal
structure and dynamics. Existing foundational choices are revisable hypotheses,
not outcomes the research must preserve. The current priority is model and
premise discrimination, not completion of a predetermined C5 maintenance proof.

The [portfolio](../../TNFR_lineas_de_investigacion.txt) classifies branches;
[the strategy](../NODAL_RESEARCH_STRATEGY.md) owns interpretation and the
[theory index](../README.md) identifies proof owners. The
[pre-reassessment snapshots](archive/README.md#foundation-reassessment-2026-09-20)
preserve the previous plan and portfolio, including uncommitted research history.
Old next-step instructions are superseded; their mathematical results are not.

## 1. Target, boundaries and completion

Determine which observable structures and responses follow from an explicitly
specified TNFR model, which survive justified changes of its premises, and
which interpretations fail. A useful result may be a conditional model family,
a sufficient reduced state, a discriminating prediction or a counterexample.
Uniqueness of all physical laws is not a prerequisite for useful mathematics.

The nodal identity `dEPI/dt=nu_f*DeltaNFR` relates a rate to supplied factors.
It does not select the pressure realization, state space, phase/capacity laws,
support, clock, initial conditions or operator schedule. Calling a quantity
canonical or emergent does not supply those missing dependencies. The identity
and its interpretation are themselves subject to explicit mathematical and
empirical review; changing one defines a new model rather than retroactively
changing an old theorem.

A revised mechanism must specify its independent premises before seeing the
response. It may use justified nodal relations or an explicitly identified
candidate law. It cannot reconstruct pressure retrospectively, add a fitted
sustaining force, or introduce a controller solely because it creates the
wanted pattern. External models may serve as comparisons, not hidden TNFR laws.

Observable identity can be geometric, orbital, transient or stationary, but
must be declared before evaluation. A failed stationary scalar target does not
rule out every pattern. Conversely, changing identity after failure does not
turn that experiment into a success. Physical identification remains open.

Empirical work is restricted to publicly available terrestrial surface data or
workstation/ordinary laboratory observations. Astronomical, satellite and
large-facility data remain excluded. Physical claims require an independent
measurement/clock map and reserved responses, beyond passing software tests.
