# Nodal research execution plan

This is the sole research task queue. The objective is to establish when
coherent, interacting patterns can form and retain an identity under justified
TNFR dynamics, then confront independently measurable consequences.
Constituent emergence and a uniquely selected physical law remain hypotheses.

The [portfolio](../../TNFR_lineas_de_investigacion.txt) classifies branches;
the [strategy](../NODAL_RESEARCH_STRATEGY.md) explains their dependencies.
Definitions, completed results, implementations and tests belong to the
[theory-to-execution map](../README.md#theory-to-execution).
The method is **F1 state -> F2 complete law -> F3 consistency -> F4 discrimination**.
P1-P5 below governs physical evaluation; it is not another active queue.

## Active admission: finite interaction near an invisible pair family

**Question:** can internal orientation change a composite's actual form
exchange when its neighborhood is prepared near a family that hides that
orientation? Distinguish instantaneous current, sufficient predictive state
and the complete law governing that state.

**Immediate next task:** on the conservative doubled C5, keep the law,
support and selected pair fixed. For one declared invisible family, identify
symmetry-preserving perturbations that retain invisibility and a realizable
symmetry-breaking preparation that distinguishes two internal orientations.
Declare the neighboring preparations before evaluating their response. Derive
a nonzero finite-time difference in actual pair-to-pair form exchange, or a
precise obstruction. Different phase derivatives alone do not establish that
exchange difference.

Resume from the whole-network invisible families in the
[cancellation classification](../nodal/SINE_PAIR_STATE.md#sine-pair-cancellation-observability),
retaining the [global pair state](../nodal/SINE_PAIR_STATE.md#sine-global-pair-state)
and [interaction interface](../nodal/SINE_PAIR_INTERACTION.md#sine-moving-pattern-interface).
Representation and exact observability are closed in those stated scopes.
Use the shared `derive_sine_global_pair_state` or
`evaluate_sine_global_pair_state` for realizability and instantaneous rows;
the [API contract](../../docs/contracts/relational/SINE_PAIR_DYNAMICS.md#sine-global-pair-state)
owns node/edge order, rate numerators and mean-form versus weighted-cut
normalization. These evaluators are not finite-time propagators.

The [two-derivative observation contract](../../docs/contracts/relational/SINE_PAIR_DYNAMICS.md#sine-pair-cancellation-observability)
consumes exact evidence in its declared clock; it abstains when higher-order or
invariant-history evidence is required. A universal derivative-order bound or
noisy inverse problem is not needed here. Existing
[finite-response tools](../nodal/SINE_REPLICA_PULSE.md#sine-moving-pulse-finite-work-response)
may supply methods only after their preparation and complete-law premises match.

1. **F1 — state and observable.** Specify the full realizable pair/environment
   states, which internal orientations the interface initially identifies,
   actual directed form current or its time integral, clock and held quantities.
   Retain internal associations, origins and hidden initialization.
2. **F2 — complete law.** Use the declared conservative sine rows and fixed
   support throughout. State the perturbation class and remaining constitutive
   assumptions. Native resultant-argument pressure is a different model;
   additivity and first-moment sufficiency are conditional premises, not
   consequences of the nodal identity.
3. **F3 — compatible dynamics.** Check the symmetry-preserving control and
   the symmetry-breaking source against realizability, storage and internal-state
   balances. These are distinct autonomous preparations: exact interval silence
   cannot restart later along an unchanged analytic trajectory. An imposed time
   source, new edge rule or fitted pressure would answer a different question.
4. **F4 — one finite consequence.** Freeze preparation, law, observation,
   horizon and error budget before evaluating. Establish actual nonzero exchange
   with a justified remainder, or report the precise obstruction/unavailable
   bound. Do not substitute an instantaneous derivative for the finite claim
   or continue with an unbounded parameter sweep.

**Deliverable and stopping rule:** one scoped result linking internal
organization to interaction, with symmetry control, complete-law provenance
and retained balances. Keep the proof with the state/exchange owner and any
reusable calculation in its shared engine owner. Replace this gate's status
with a link when it closes; do not append a delivery log or repeat the existing
law classifications. A missing premise remains explicit.

This task examines how an organized pattern acts as a constituent. It does
not identify an electron/nucleus, impose a physical orbit, prove formation or
select a unique pressure law. The [generative target](../EMERGENT_ONTOLOGY.md#generative-bound-organization)
retains those separate obligations. No physical-source search is active.

evidence. Reuse the [source audit](archive/README.md#four-foundation-source-audit)

evidence stays unavailable; orientation or cleanup does not require rerunning
its producer.

identified missing premise. Do not continue optimizing a secondary bound that
cannot decide the claim. After closure, replace this gate's status and link
its result owner rather than append run history or duplicate the result
catalog. Superseded planning and retirement provenance remain in the
