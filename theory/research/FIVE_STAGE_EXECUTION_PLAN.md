## 1. Target, boundaries and completion

Test whether coherent patterns, their persistence and their interactions can
be derived from explicitly specified TNFR structure and dynamics. The nodal
identity `dEPI/dt=nu_f*DeltaNFR` determines a form rate after its inputs are
specified; it does not by itself select pressure, capacity, phase, support,
initial conditions or a physical interpretation.

Every proposed mechanism must distinguish derived relations, constitutive
assumptions, preparation, boundary input, numerical implementation and observed
outputs. Pressure must not be reconstructed retrospectively from a desired
response. A declared graph, oscillator or operator policy is not spontaneous
formation. No current result establishes emergence of physical particles or
all observed reality from TNFR.

Empirical work must use publicly available data or observations feasible on
Earth's surface with a workstation or ordinary research laboratory. Astronomical,
satellite and large-facility data are excluded by the user's constraint.
Physical correspondence requires an independently specified measurement map
and reserved predictions; passing software tests is not that correspondence.

A completed study can support, reject or leave its hypothesis inconclusive.
Keep implementation completion separate from scientific support. Preserve
negative results and source-bound evidence; do not rewrite old outcomes.
