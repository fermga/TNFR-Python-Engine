# Nodal research execution plan

This is the sole research task queue. The objective is to establish when
coherent, interacting patterns can form and retain an identity under justified
TNFR dynamics, then confront independently measurable consequences.
Constituent emergence and a uniquely selected physical law remain hypotheses.

The [portfolio](../../TNFR_lineas_de_investigacion.txt) classifies branches;
the [strategy](../NODAL_RESEARCH_STRATEGY.md) explains their dependencies.
Definitions, completed results, implementations and tests belong to the
[theory-to-execution map](../README.md#theory-to-execution).
The method is **F1 state -> F2 complete law -> F3 consistency -> F4 discrimination**.
P1-P5 below governs physical evaluation; it is not another active queue.

<a id="current-checkpoint"></a>
## Current checkpoint and resumption

**Primary question:** how does retained internal organization determine a
composite's interactions under justified complete nodal laws? The route is
**information -> interaction -> complete law -> collective organization
-> independent observation**. A sufficient representation does not independently
justify the law that evolves it.

**No research campaign is active.** The latest
[formation-and-response admission](#current-g3-gate) is closed in its stated
scope. Resume only after naming a new discriminating premise or downstream
need and declaring its F1-F4 obligations. Completed controls are reusable
evidence, not work to repeat. Physical constituent emergence and independent
law selection remain open; no empirical response has passed complete physical
admission.

## Stopping, provenance and resumption

Read the active gate together with its actual proof, implementation and
evidence. Reuse the [source audit](archive/README.md#four-foundation-source-audit)
when an older hypothesis matters; its label is not proof. Retain frozen
declarations, protocols, source archives, responses and verdicts unchanged.
Corrections or current-source regressions need separate provenance. Missing
evidence stays unavailable; orientation or cleanup does not require rerunning
its producer.

Stop when the admitted claim is proved, excluded or limited by a precisely
identified missing premise. Do not continue optimizing a secondary bound that
cannot decide the claim. After closure, replace this gate's status and link
its result owner rather than append run history or duplicate the result
catalog. Superseded planning and retirement provenance remain in the
[archive](archive/README.md).
