# Nodal research execution plan

**Revised 2026-10-05. Sole research task queue.** The objective is to determine
which interacting, persistent collective patterns follow from justified TNFR
state and laws, and eventually whether their properties explain independent
physical observations. Physical constituents and a uniquely selected
fundamental law remain open hypotheses.

The method is **F1 state -> F2 complete laws -> F3 joint consistency ->
F4 discriminating prediction or obstruction**. The filename is retained for
compatibility; P1-P5 is the supporting measurement protocol, not a second queue.
The [portfolio](../../TNFR_lineas_de_investigacion.txt) classifies branches, the
[strategy](../NODAL_RESEARCH_STRATEGY.md) explains their dependencies, and the
[theory-to-execution map](../README.md#theory-to-execution) locates proofs,
implementations and tests. Completed calculations stay with those owners.

## Active gate: justify form balance against the common-origin symmetry

**Question.** When does conserving `Q=sum_i(d_i/nu_i)*x_i` express a restriction
on primitive TNFR exchange, rather than a choice of common form origin? The
[selection theorem](../nodal/RELATIONAL_EXCHANGE_ADMISSION.md#closed-form-balance-and-source-selection)
has closed the algebraic implication under explicit premises. This gate tests
the justification of its conservation premise before adopting it as fundamental.

This is the shared state/equivalence and constitutive-justification gate in the
[strategy's fundamental dependency map](../NODAL_RESEARCH_STRATEGY.md#fundamental-research-dependencies).
Reuse the existing relative-field, clock and weighted-mean owners. Record which
absolute or inter-component offsets must survive for the retained-frame gate;
this dependency does not activate its contact adapter or a capacity/support-law
campaign. Physical interpretation retains its independent P1-P5 admission.

The existing results constrain this comparison without selecting a law. The
[sine symmetry generator](../nodal/RELATIONAL_PATTERN_MEMORY.md#sine-relative-pattern-state)
explains conservation after choosing its bracket; it cannot independently
justify that choice. [Alternative phase storage](../nodal/RELATIONAL_EXCHANGE_ADMISSION.md#phase-storage-selection-boundary)
can preserve balance, the same bracket and the consensus tangent while
changing nonlinear pressure; the cosine-storage premise remains explicit.
[Capacity and event reweighting](../FORCED_SUPPORT_BALANCE.md#prescribed-capacity-and-event-mean-balance)
distinguishes raw Q from its normalized mean when the held-weight premise is
changed. Keep the active comparison on fixed support and held capacities;
retain [hidden inventory and its memory source](../nodal/RELATIONAL_PATTERN_MEMORY.md#conserved-hidden-inventory)
and component offsets before transferring its result to a reduced or joined
pattern. A stationary hidden substitution can conserve a visible weighting
while losing the original total; it cannot justify the primitive balance.
The existing [asymptotic-origin chart](../nodal/RELATIONAL_PATTERN_MEMORY.md#asymptotic-origin-coordinate-scope)
also distinguishes bare-Q drift from the absence of any conserved origin
coordinate. Its nonlinear correction is conditional on a selected recovering
law and retained state; it does not establish a primitive-local or global
chart. Keep these alternatives separate when interpreting the quotient.

### Deliverables and stopping rule

1. **F1:** retain a fixed connected simple unit graph, held positive capacities,
   signed form, circular phase and the original clock. Distinguish the complete
   state from the quotient by a common form shift; retain the absolute form
   observation whenever an actual consumer uses it. Reuse the existing relative
   field and weighted-mean reports rather than introduce a second state catalog.
2. **F2:** compare a declared exchange field with the candidate common-direction
   correction `xdot -> xdot - (Qdot/W)*1`, where `W=sum_i d_i/nu_i`; keep the
   phase row unchanged. Establish what this operation preserves and changes.
   Determine whether the resulting pressure is primitive-local,
   capacity-independent and well defined as capacities approach zero; do not
   assume those properties or identify a changed field with a clock change.
3. **F3:** prove equivalence or obstruction for relative form/phase evolution,
   storage and whole-state observations. Trace every information dependency.
   Distinguish a coordinate description of an existing trajectory from an
   autonomous full-state law, and from eliminating a physically measured
   coordinate. Existing common-family and Jacobi results are inputs, not tasks
   to repeat.
4. **F4:** before any new evaluation, fix one static graph/preparation and the
   independent quantities that discriminate the complete fields. Reuse the
   closed gate's acute star and shared readers where sufficient. Include a
   remote-neighborhood or capacity-boundary countercontrol if the proposed
   correction needs information outside the primitive contract. No trajectory
   survey, coefficient fit or frozen-producer replay is required.

**Acceptance:** a proved equivalence or obstruction with an explicit retained
observation/information contract, independent static checks, and a decision on
which selection premise still needs justification. Change engine behavior only
if the result establishes a reusable contract absent from its current owners.

**Stop boundary:** no physical charge identification, automatic law selection,
primitive edge birth or new default runtime. Preserve the native and sine laws
as distinct complete models until a justified condition selects between them.

## Source audit, stopping rules and resumption

Read definitions together with actual code, tests, experiments and relevant
historical bytes. The [retained source audit](archive/README.md#four-foundation-source-audit)
locates useful sources; a filename or old success label supplies no proof.
Preserve uncommitted material before major consolidation, and retain frozen
records and original source identity. Missing local evidence stays unavailable;
do not regenerate its producer as an orientation step.

Stop a proposed branch when its claim is refuted in scope, when the calculation
cannot distinguish alternatives or when success must be supplied as an input.
A justified independent revision can reopen it. Do not keep tightening a
secondary bound solely because its tools exist.

Resume only at the [active gate](#current-g3-gate). When a gate closes, update
its status and link the result owner instead of appending proof or run history
here. Superseded planning and retirement provenance belong to the
[archive](archive/README.md). Completion means a scoped result and required
integration, not a promise that TNFR is a fundamental physical theory.
