    Nodal research execution plan: four foundational stages¶

    Revised 2026-10-02. Sole research task queue. The primary question is whether a justified TNFR structure and dynamics can generate interacting coherence patterns and, eventually, explain measured collective properties. Physical constituents emerging from such patterns remain an open hypothesis. Primitive EPI need not be directly identified with a laboratory magnitude.

    The method is F1 state -> F2 complete laws -> F3 joint consistency -> F4 discriminating prediction. These are repeatable admission stages, not a claim that all foundations have been settled. Completed results stay with their proof owners; historical stage names do not reopen their campaigns.

    The filename is retained for compatibility. P1-P5 below remain the supporting measurement/evaluation protocol, not five competing primary research stages. The portfolio classifies branches; the strategy explains priorities and reuse; the theory catalog connects proofs to code, tests and public interfaces. None maintains another task queue.

    1. Objective and scientific acceptance¶

    Establish a minimal, explicit family of nodal models whose predictions follow from stated premises. Separate definitions, independent hypotheses, derived consequences, configured policies, numerical observations and physical evidence. The nodal equation itself is a revisable model premise; its written product does not uniquely select the state space, pressure, phase/capacity laws, support or event schedule.

    Some independent postulates are unavoidable in a proposed theory. Calling one emergent requires an actual derivation from previously stated structure and dynamics, not a new name or a successful simulation. External mathematical tools may test the model; imported physical equations cannot become unstated TNFR laws. A useful conditional family need not be uniquely selected by current premises. Unresolved alternatives must remain identifiable. Physical mechanisms mentioned in discussion are prompts for mathematical questions, not required outcomes of the active work. Its immediate objective is to develop TNFR's own state, laws, composition and emergence criteria.

    Completion of F1-F4 means a scoped mathematical foundation and a testable prediction, not proof that TNFR is a fundamental physical theory. Physical identification needs the independent P1-P5 bridge. Infinite pattern lifetime, substrate creation from nothing, a Hamiltonian representation and universal operator selection are not prerequisites for every restricted result.

    Present decision. Conditional mechanisms are available, but agreement with a storage balance or one response does not select a constitutive law. The coefficient audit establishes nonselection and an identification route; its bounded P2 acquisition verifies that route within a known software model. The next obligation is a calibrated, uncertainty-aware discriminator among the named same-jet alternatives, specified before any new trajectory. The completed contact, recovery, mediation and reduction results are retained in the status board and their owners, not repeated as competing priorities. Primitive formation, law selection and the physical bridge remain distinct.

    Constitutive and integration discipline¶

    Hold support, capacity or input only as explicit laws. Pressure refreshed from state differs from held-pressure intervals and named operator jumps. Reuse the existing pressure premises, joint parameter response, capacity admission and clock covariance. A synchronizing phase alone need not define a monotone clock.

    Admitted derived mechanisms belong in their shared engine owners and consuming interfaces. A fitted gain, comparison model, theorem requiring extra premises or failed hypothesis does not become a default dynamics change. Existing cotangent and relational models keep their different storage/capacity premises. Useful restrictions need not wait for a proof of unique ontology.

Use exact arithmetic for finite algebra when appropriate; it does not certify transcendental floating-point execution. A history-sensitive runtime needs retained execution state, not a recipe-only checkpoint. Reuse the shared integrator, pressure, field and evidence owners.

    7. Source audit, stopping rules and resumption¶

    Read current definitions together with actual source, tests, experiments and relevant historical bytes. A historical name such as canonical, emergent or verified supplies no proof. Record what was inspected, its model, result class, reusable calculation and excluded interpretation. The audit ledger is the evidence map; proofs stay in their existing topical owners.

    Keep historical files recoverable by commit/blob/hash; preserve uncommitted history before rewriting it. Do not restore an obsolete instrument simply because its original question was relevant. Inspect retained artifacts without claiming their dirty-source producer was reproduced on today's tree.

    Stop a branch when its proposed conclusion is already refuted in scope, when the calculation cannot distinguish alternatives, or when success must be supplied as an input. A named independent revision can reopen it. Do not continue tightening a secondary bound solely because its machinery exists.

    Resume at coefficient admission and observable response when requested, retaining the contact, recovery and nonselection results with their original hypotheses. Earlier C4/C8 continuations, C5 searches, C6 global-runtime certification, derivative ladders, further memory sweeps and broad physical intake are parked. Historical O/S/G/B milestone labels are not additional task queues. Valid results stay in topical owners; superseded planning and retirement provenance belong to the archive.

    Update this board when an acceptance condition is met or priority changes, linking the result owner rather than copying its proof or run log. Formation, identity, physical identification and substrate origin remain the long-term questions; the stages make their prerequisites reviewable without promising the outcome. Completion means a scoped result and its required integration, not proof that TNFR is a fundamental physical theory.
