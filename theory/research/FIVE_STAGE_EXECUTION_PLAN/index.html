    Nodal research execution plan¶

    Current status: 2026-09-19. This is the sole active research queue. Documentation is consolidated under the owners below. Scientific execution resumes at the G3 gate; historical next-step paragraphs are not additional active tasks. This cleanup did not run a new experiment.

    The portfolio classifies priorities; the strategy explains the scientific choices; the theory index identifies the current owner of each result. The full preceding delivery chronology is preserved in the planning archive. This file retains decisions and gates, not duplicate proofs or session logs.

    1. Target, boundaries and completion¶

    Test whether coherent patterns, their persistence and their interactions can be derived from explicitly specified TNFR structure and dynamics. The nodal identity dEPI/dt=nu_f*DeltaNFR determines a form rate after its inputs are specified; it does not by itself select pressure, capacity, phase, support, initial conditions or a physical interpretation.

    Every proposed mechanism must distinguish derived relations, constitutive assumptions, preparation, boundary input, numerical implementation and observed outputs. Pressure must not be reconstructed retrospectively from a desired response. A declared graph, oscillator or operator policy is not spontaneous formation. No current result establishes emergence of physical particles or all observed reality from TNFR.

    Empirical work must use publicly available data or observations feasible on Earth's surface with a workstation or ordinary research laboratory. Astronomical, satellite and large-facility data are excluded by the user's constraint. Physical correspondence requires an independently specified measurement map and reserved predictions; passing software tests is not that correspondence.

    A completed study can support, reject or leave its hypothesis inconclusive. Keep implementation completion separate from scientific support. Preserve negative results and source-bound evidence; do not rewrite old outcomes.

    Current G3 gate: controlled effective descriptions¶

    Next scientific step: test controlled loss of decaying hidden form. Use the existing P5 generator, reversible metric and retention/memory owners. Derive pressure and specified field errors when hidden form is omitted. Keep symmetry equivalence distinct from dynamical decay. A reflection quotient removes a discrete label choice; its intrinsic continuous dimension is still five.

    The current result retains (a,p,c,r^2,r*s,s^2) with induced rates and a constrained decoder. The xi continuity boundary already shows why small pressure error does not imply uniformly small error in an unqualified fit/fallback value. Preserve estimator method and input provenance.

    Acceptance requires an explicit domain and output-error bound, or a precise obstruction. Do not introduce a second solver, fitted sustaining force, topology sweep or arbitrary telemetry control to make the result pass. A static family is not a trajectory. Exact arithmetic, represented coefficients and runtime rounding remain separate. The latest scientific checkpoint is tetrad and symmetry validation (artifacts/research/tetrad_symmetry_validation_2026_09_19.json, local evidence).

    After this bounded decision, return to the effective-substrate question: which state and laws are inherited, which inputs remain supplied, and what additional evidence would support formation or interaction? Do not let an approximation theorem silently discharge the missing autonomous-law obligations.

    3. Shared evidence contract and resource discipline¶

    Record the model/domain, primitive inputs, graph/node order, active channels, seed when relevant, operator word, time and pressure-refresh policy, observation map, numerical defects and source version. State whether a result is exact, conditional, a finite numerical observation, an auxiliary model or an open hypothesis.

    Freeze a prospective decision rule before examining reserved responses. Use the same information budget for comparison models. Do not reuse evaluated outcomes as blind data after changing a model. Stop a bounded test when its admission fails; a failure does not authorize an unbounded search.

    4. P1 — Correct diagnostics and isolate evaluation¶

    The implemented instrument separates descriptive fit from evaluation and retains missing/degenerate-input outcomes. A positive diagnostic is not itself a dynamical law or physical evidence.

    Implemented contract and migration¶

    Use the existing calibration/evaluation APIs and their validation owners. Preserve run/subject boundaries and causal feature access: full-record normalization, centered derivatives or transforms can leak future information. The current physical admission and source inventory live in the passive-transport protocol.

    5. P2 — Admit one measurement and dynamical model¶

    The user has only a computer and needs public data. No physical dataset has passed the complete measurement contract. Continuous P2 reference bounds and the completed fixed-design Volts exploration do not fill missing instrument, clock or causal-preparation evidence. The protocol annex owns those details. Do not run physical P3 with assumed uncertainties or reopen public-data searches automatically. A new model must declare all input and observation dependencies before calibration and must reserve independent evaluation evidence.

    6. P3 — Make and evaluate a reserved prediction¶

    Save content-hashed predictions before revealing the reserved response. Separate open-loop forecasting from measurement-fed one-step scores. Evaluate all reserved runs without changing coefficients, thresholds or normalization. Report incompatibility, support in scope or inconclusiveness under the admitted measurement model. Agreement with a shared classical diffusion limit is not proof of a uniquely TNFR physical mechanism.

    7. P4 — Partial observation and derived memory¶

    Declare the observation map and legitimately available initial information. Reuse the exact eliminated-state kernel and its hidden-initial-state source; a configured REMESH echo is not automatically that kernel. A reduced predictor may not consume the unobserved evaluation state. Require a sufficient-state contract, an error enclosure or a same-observation/different-future obstruction.

    8. P5 — Maintenance, recovery and transitions¶

    Pure EPI diffusion on fixed connected reciprocal support with positive fixed capacity relaxes toward uniform form. Any claimed differentiated maintenance must retain its declared source, boundary, history or admitted additional law.

    P5.1. Fixed-target maintenance and recovery¶

    Specify original identity, amplitude, phase, boundary exchange and observation metric before perturbation. Distinguish passive retention, driven persistence, recovery and autonomous maintenance. Post-event recentering must not redefine a failed target as success.

    P5.2. A causal regime study¶

    Fix the model, control parameter, observable and finite-size protocol before sampling. A classifier threshold, branch switch or static ensemble alone does not demonstrate a dynamical phase transition or a universality class.

    9. Resumption, archival work and final delivery¶

    Documentation consolidation is complete; resume only the G3 step above. C6 remains parked at B75 (41/56 excluded first-exit labels, 15 open); no global stability closure has been obtained. The prepared ring-response experiment, polyhedral geometry, Millennium programmes and public-data campaigns are also parked.

    Keep one next scientific task here, evidence in its result owner and historical chronology in the archive. Update this status when evidence changes; do not append another full delivery ledger. Preserve the current implementation and research artifacts when performing further maintenance.
