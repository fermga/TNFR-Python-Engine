    Scope of this consolidation¶

    The live references correct overstated conservation, complete-state, autonomous birth, freezing and particle-generation claims; arithmetic notes distinguish synthetic contract probes, supplied targets and same-target calibration from runtime execution and reserved evaluation. Body corrections retain valid conditional results and counterexamples. README/AGENTS are syntheses again; the portfolio classifies branches and the execution plan is the only queue.

    Source explanatory text was aligned without changing numerical calculations, flags or result schemas. Documentation tooling now stages the portfolio and math assets, renders formulas, and excludes frozen run captures from live-link scans. The source/AST/link/archive checks are recorded locally in artifacts/research/theoretical_documentation_cleanup_validation_2026_09_19.json. They audit documentation and implementation correspondence; they are not an independent proof of every retained theorem or a new physical experiment.
