    Research archive and recovery index¶

    The research program owns the current question and priorities. Old plan, strategy and portfolio paths are generated compatibility routes, not active instructions. This archive retains scoped evidence, inactive narratives and byte-level recovery records. Use the theory catalog for current definitions and mathematical capabilities. Historical instructions, milestone labels and reported successes are not current authority.

    What to retain and where to read it¶

    Material Role
    Ontology result survey Inactive accumulated synopsis of conditional objects and case studies; current ontology owns formation, identity and collective-description criteria
    Campaign and measurement evidence Source-bound results and limitations; each maintained mathematical owner supplies its model and present interpretation
    Recovery records Exact identities for retired or consolidated material; these receipts do not validate the former claims

    An inactive investigation can contain reusable mathematics. Archiving its narrative changes neither its conditional result nor its frozen evidence. Reuse a result through its maintained owner; selecting further work belongs only to the research program.

    Retained boundaries and replacements¶

    • The theory catalog owns definitions/proof routes; the documentation map owns usage and contract routes. Former navigation catalogs and per-turn delivery logs are recovered from the manifests, not maintained as additional authorities.
    • Retired particle/chemistry, Yang-Mills and Navier-Stokes mappings had supplied external assumptions, not a derived nodal bridge. Their useful limits remain in retired-programme boundaries. Shared graph, winding, symmetry and evidence kernels retain their explicit premises.
    • Removed APIs have no alias silently substituting a different model. Exact former names and implementation bytes remain in the foundation manifest. Current public interfaces belong to the API contracts.
    • Duplicate conservation comparisons do not replace the trajectory-balance owner. Arithmetic/spectral claims retain the Riemann scope.
    • Reported interface observations preserve their data/model limitations. Moving or retiring a narrative does not rerun the experiment or establish physical validation.
    • Benchmark and example catalogs identify maintained instruments. Frozen protocols, source archives, responses and verdicts remain unchanged at their recorded paths.
