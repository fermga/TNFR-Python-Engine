    Archived ontology result survey¶

    Status: inactive editorial record. This is the superseded, cumulative presentation of organized-pattern studies. Its research targets, ordering and priority language do not define the current programme. It is retained because the working-tree survey contains source associations and distinctions that must remain recoverable; it is not a second theory catalog or task queue.

    Use the current ontology for definitions and admission criteria, the theory catalog for the maintained proof owners, and the execution plan for current work. Linked conditional results and counterexamples retain their own premises; archiving this presentation does not invalidate those results.

    1. Substrate, formation and identity¶

    Research target: physical properties of collective patterns¶

    The primary hypothesis is that material constituents and their observable properties could arise from interacting TNFR patterns. It does not require one microscopic EPI to be a voltage, temperature, concentration or particle attribute. A prospective generative chain is fine nodal state/law -> collective identity and effective dynamics -> physical observations. The fine state includes form, phase, capacity, support and any history actually consumed by the law. The effective object may carry properties of their joint configuration and trajectory which no individual node has.

    The existing repertoire of collective properties is not assumed exhaustive. When a more complex organization becomes admissible, reassess its identity, sufficient state and effective dynamics: a lower-level description may omit new relational information. A proposed property needs a defined observation and a control that distinguishes organization-dependent behavior from what the prepared parts already possess. An effective law derived on that domain is compatible with an unchanged fine law; it does not by itself select a new universal microscopic law.

    A common substrate could admit distinct, reproducible families of collective organization at different scales. This is stronger than a mere change of scale: the proposed families must have distinguishable dynamics, composition rules and observable responses. Sharing a substrate would not erase observed differences between material systems. Names such as hydrogen remain physical identifications to establish through those responses, not names for an assumed TNFR primitive. Neither a common substrate nor scale nesting alone derives the chemical elements.

    Candidate particle species could be classes of stable or dynamically maintained organization under one complete law; treating number symbols as reductions of such organization is a further hypothesis. Each symbolic map must declare its retained and discarded information and prove the arithmetic relations it preserves. A claim of emergent physical constituents additionally needs a source/formation account, identity over the claimed lifetime and independently measured responses. The arithmetic-spectral scope retains supplied modular carriers and graph constructions; its conditional consequences do not derive those carriers or identify particle species.

    The intended physical question is whether nodal organization can generate the effects attributed to material constituents, including bound composite structure and reproducible changes under interaction. A nucleus/electron picture is a possible explanatory target, not a partition or force law to insert into the engine. Atomic comparison would eventually require spatial measurement distributions, bound-state energies and transition responses; see the hydrogen-state calculation. A classical orbit, a graph cycle or a discrete winding label alone does not establish those observations.

    The present persistent collective organization and restoring effective relation are reusable mathematical ingredients with different complete-law premises. Both retain supplied fine support. The collective mean-closure restriction explains why composing such organizations generally needs their internal information, even when their collective means coincide. This supports studying sufficient interaction state and inherited dynamics before physical naming; it does not derive an atom or a unique constitutive law.

    The weighted-star state study makes this information obligation explicit for one marked organization and its retained dynamical boundary. Equal collective means and storage do not determine the collective response; adding a complex phase moment still leaves its derivative sensitive to internal form/phase association. An invertible chart retaining all internal deviations supplies a complete continuous state for that model. It proves neither a minimal collective state nor an autonomous organization without its boundary. Formation, finite-error maintenance and the histories/context consumed by canonical operators remain separate requirements. Linear transverse stability does not erase internal information or establish those requirements. A separate finite-amplitude identity certificate uses the same model's full storage to retain explicit internal form/phase margins for all time, including a nonzero neighborhood of the supplied coherent family. This proves maintenance under that law, not acquisition, tracking of one orbit or a universal definition of an NFR.

    The two-star composition theorem then retains both marked identities under a supplied boundary connection, with nonzero exchange on an explicit open preparation set. Its guarantee uses the actual joined law, including changed boundary normalization and accounted support work. Separate isolated certificates do not supply that joint budget. This is conditional coexistence of interacting organizations, not derivation of the connection or acquisition of a new collective identity.

    The canonical weighted-star contact separately admits a supplied grammatical word and its actual affinity-weighted support. Its finite-response comparison also separates a center-leaf form contrast from the matched disconnected control at a fixed elapsed structural time. A whole-window nonlinear bound supports that conclusion; an initial tangent alone would not. Identity retention and an interaction-induced internal response therefore coexist under the same declared law, without discarding the internal coordinates. The exact contact state retains that internal contrast in five form/phase blocks. Its inherited law keeps cross-edge multiplicities, conductance sums and inverse-capacity mean weights separately. A collective block therefore cannot generally be substituted by a simple node with only a changed scalar capacity. This is an exact description of the admitted family, not a projection of arbitrary fine states, a physical mass identification or a newly formed NFR. The off-family robustness theorem also bounds every fine coordinate in a positive-width preparation box around the actual post-word sources. At the unchanged horizon, the internal contrast still separates attached and disconnected laws while both identities persist. This gives a finite domain of predictive usefulness beyond exact equality; it neither makes the perturbed state a member of the five-block family nor derives decay, uncertain event outputs or autonomous assembly. The preparation-to-flow admission separately retains live word/selector margins and the variation of generated conductances. Its exact-map theorem and conditional arithmetic allowance keep distinct scopes; represented controls admit their own raw outputs and exact integer phase lifts.
