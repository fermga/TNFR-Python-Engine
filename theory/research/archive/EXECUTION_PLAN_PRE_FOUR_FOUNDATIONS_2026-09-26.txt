# Nodal research execution plan

**Current status: 2026-09-26.** This is the sole active research queue.
The primary objective is to determine whether material constituents and their
observable properties can emerge as interacting coherent patterns from justified
TNFR nodal structure and dynamics. Primitive EPI need not equal a directly
measured physical magnitude. Existing foundational choices are revisable hypotheses,
not outcomes the research must preserve. The current priority is testing an
explicit geometric phase/form closure, with capacity held and its added premise
kept separate from consequences, before further formation searches.
Its target remains conditional pattern appearance, evolution and loss, with
explicit premise discrimination. Neither indefinite persistence nor a
unique completion of the nodal identity is a prerequisite for that work.

The [portfolio](../../TNFR_lineas_de_investigacion.txt) classifies branches;
[the strategy](../NODAL_RESEARCH_STRATEGY.md) owns interpretation and the
[theory index](../README.md) identifies proof owners. The
[pre-reassessment snapshots](archive/README.md#foundation-reassessment-2026-09-20)
preserve the previous plan and portfolio, including uncommitted research history.
Old next-step instructions are superseded; their mathematical results are not.

## 1. Target, boundaries and completion

Determine which observable structures and responses follow from an explicitly
specified TNFR model, which survive justified changes of its premises, and
which interpretations fail. A useful result may be a conditional model family,
a sufficient reduced state, a discriminating prediction or a counterexample.
Uniqueness of all physical laws is not a prerequisite for useful mathematics.

The nodal identity `dEPI/dt=nu_f*DeltaNFR` relates a rate to supplied factors.
It does not select the pressure realization, state space, phase/capacity laws,
support, clock, initial conditions or operator schedule. Calling a quantity
canonical or emergent does not supply those missing dependencies. The identity
and its interpretation are themselves subject to explicit mathematical and
empirical review; changing one defines a new model rather than retroactively
changing an old theorem.

A revised mechanism must specify its independent premises before seeing the
response. It may use justified nodal relations or an explicitly identified
candidate law. It cannot reconstruct pressure retrospectively, add a fitted
sustaining force, or introduce a controller solely because it creates the
wanted pattern. External models may serve as comparisons, not hidden TNFR laws.

Observable identity can be geometric, orbital, transient or stationary, but
must be declared before evaluation. A failed stationary scalar target does not
rule out every pattern. Conversely, changing identity after failure does not
turn that experiment into a success. Physical identification remains open.

One fixed law can yield different responses under independently specified
preparations, connectivity and surroundings. Formation, finite persistence,
transformation and loss are legitimate targets; every pattern need not live
forever. Distinguish appearance of an observable, birth of a graph region,
restoration of identity and physical formation. Observation thresholds may
define a reserved visibility window but cannot become undeclared dynamic
switches or evidence of a bifurcation.

Empirical work is restricted to publicly available terrestrial surface data or
workstation/ordinary laboratory observations. Astronomical, satellite and
large-facility data remain excluded. Physical claims require an independent
measurement/clock map and reserved responses, beyond passing software tests.

**Dependency order.** First admit one complete conditional candidate and its
predeclared identity/reachability prediction at the current gate below. Then
admit a physical observation and clock map in P2 before evaluating physical
predictions in P3. P4–P5 reuse that evidence contract for hidden state, lifetime
and transitions. An already specified transport model can support a narrower
P2–P3 test without waiting for autonomous NFR formation; it cannot by itself
validate the stronger generative interpretation. Scope checks for auxiliaries,
policies and proof apply to these same deliverables, not separate campaigns.

**Empirical interpretation.** Predeclared agreement within a
justified error budget supports the tested model in scope. A comparison must
report whether competing models are distinguishable under the available
measurements. Identical observable predictions leave the interpretation
underdetermined. A claim of additional predictive value needs a reserved
discriminator and a fair comparison, rather than merely renaming a known
equation. Universal superiority or an absolutely exclusive prediction is not
required for a valid limited test. Stronger physical claims require evidence
across independently acquired preparations/conditions and an explicit record
of replication; one successful trace would not establish a fundamental theory.
