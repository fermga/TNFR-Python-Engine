1. OBJECTIVE, CONSTRAINTS AND MEANING OF COMPLETION
================================================

Macro objective
---------------
Test whether explicit TNFR foundations and derived dynamics can account for
the formation, persistence, interaction and dissolution of coherent patterns,
and produce quantitative predictions for observable laboratory phenomena.
The long-term question is whether a common TNFR description can explain
multiple physical regimes without importing their laws as extra premises.
Whether this is possible remains an open question, not a conclusion to defend.

The next meaningful milestone is narrower: a reproducible differentiated
pattern with a declared formation mechanism, a measured perturbation response
and an explicit account of what maintains it. A later milestone is a justified
measurement map and a laboratory comparison capable of rejecting the model.
Neither a simulation nor a finite set of matches demonstrates that all reality
necessarily emerges from TNFR or uniquely identifies its underlying ontology.

Governing derivation chain
-------------------------
    TNFR foundations -> explicit constitutive/evolution rules -> derivation
    -> structural observable/prediction -> reproducible experimental test.

The nodal equation is dEPI/dt = nu_f * DeltaNFR. Its factorization alone does
not determine the pressure law, phase evolution, capacity updates, topology
or next operator. Choosing DeltaNFR = prescribed_rate/nu_f after observing a
target trajectory encodes that trajectory; it does not derive its dynamics.

Every candidate must distinguish:
- Primitives: the stated TNFR structural state and canonical invariants.
- Constitutive/operator choices: pressure realization, factors, phase rules,
  support changes and grammar policies; identify unresolved derivations.
- Preparation/input: initial graph/state, supplied schedule and boundary data.
- Derived content: equations, coefficients, balances and predictions obtained
  from those premises independently of the desired result.
- Numerical settings: timestep, tolerances, arithmetic and resource limits.
- Measurement: a justified observation map, units and instrument uncertainty.

No independent physical law, noise model, probability rule, Hamiltonian,
interaction field or fitted explanatory constant may repair a failed TNFR
derivation. Existing engine defaults are implementation choices unless their
claimed physical role is derived. Mathematical transformations are allowed
when their equivalence and domain are established. Comparison models remain
controls, not hidden ingredients of the TNFR explanation.

Experimental boundary
---------------------
All calculations and experiments must be feasible on an ordinary workstation
or at most a research laboratory on Earth's surface. Exclude astronomical,
astrophysical and cosmological data, including ground-telescope observations;
exclude satellite/space-mission data and required large accelerators,
observatory-scale facilities or mandatory supercomputer access.
Check origin, apparatus, acquisition, uncertainty, replication and resources
before accepting a dataset. Unknown provenance means pending eligibility.
No apparatus or dynamic dataset has yet been admitted for the current O6
programme. Zenodo 3521009 is now a candidate: only metadata and its small
topology archive have been inspected; no time series has been downloaded or
validated as TNFR evidence. See theory/NODAL_RESEARCH_STRATEGY.md.
Physical Hz, temperature, energy or length require a justified structural
measurement map; Hz_str and engine time are not automatically physical units.

Evidence status has three independent axes
-----------------------------------------
Mathematics: exact scoped result / conditional result / counterexample / open.
Implementation: implemented and checked / finite observation / not implemented.
Physical evidence: untested / supported in a stated test / rejected / inconclusive.
A passing test checks an implementation claim; it is not empirical confirmation.
A finite causal execution is not proof of future admission or infinite-time
stability. Historical test totals must not be added as a completion percentage.

Meaning of a C6 region
----------------------
A region is a set of numerical states of the six-node ring, rather than a
location in physical space. Each state includes the six visible EPI values
and the exact carried numerical remainder. A first-exit region collects
states whose next nodal step would cross one declared candidate boundary.
The 56 original exit regions are proof obligations, not 56 experiments,
equally likely outcomes or a percentage of stability. Excluding a region
means proving it cannot be reached from the stated origin before the first
candidate exit. An unresolved region is neither a proven exit nor a proven
safe case. Leaving this candidate alone would not prove global unboundedness.

Why the current count is 56
--------------------------
Each of the six scalar EPI coordinates has two adjacent binary64 values
bracketing its candidate forced-profile coordinate. Their 2^6 = 64 rounded
rows label rounding cells, each also containing the carried remainder.
For every row, the benchmark tests six coordinates and two exit directions:
64 * 6 * 2 = 768 labelled conditions. It intersects each condition with
the reverified B49 source envelope, using the original complete rounding-cell
cube bounds and the exact canonical nodal increment. Before that source-cover
restriction, 72 conditions are nonempty. The retained B49 envelope eliminates
all eight node-3 lower and all eight node-5 lower conditions, leaving exactly
56 nonempty intersections; the other 712 are empty in that cover.

Using zero-based engine node indices, the nonempty counts are:
    node             0   1   2   3   4   5
    lower exit       8   0   8   0   0   0
    upper exit       0  16   0   8   8   8
    total            8  16   8   8   8   8  = 56
This is an enumerated outcome for the selected candidate and source cover,
not a TNFR constant or the number of physical states. Conditions with
different labels may overlap. Nonempty candidate regions still require
origin-reachability exclusion; their nonemptiness is not an observed exit.
The owner is _rebuild_c6_outgoing_regions in
benchmarks/c6_winding_invariant_region.py. A changed candidate, source cover
or representation can change the count without changing the nodal equation.
The independent reconstruction is c6_b61_region_count.py/.json under
artifacts/research. Its 56 hypothetical nodal-step witnesses establish local
nonemptiness only; none is a trajectory from the actual origin. Later
origin-cover refinements preserve these original labels while narrowing
their represented targets. Whole-label exclusion requires the corresponding
coverage premise, not merely emptiness of an arbitrarily smaller set.
