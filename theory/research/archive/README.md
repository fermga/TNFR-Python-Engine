## Recovery records

On 2026-11-06 the [retention policy](../../../AGENTS.md) retired material that
no active question consumes: frozen evidence of secondary campaigns under
`docs/assets/`, the consumed formed two-port attempt, the archived
factorization lab and primality adapters, historical captures and cleanup
manifests formerly kept here, secondary chapters without a maintained
implementation, and the tests, producers and research modules that served only
them. Everything is recoverable from commit `279054b8694a9bf5fd3a7e67da65847a3e36d749`
(tag `pre-retirement-2026-11-06`):

```sh
git show 279054b8694a:<path> > <file>
git checkout pre-retirement-2026-11-06 -- <path>
```

Use a binary-safe writer when recovering frozen bytes. Maintained documents link
retired sources by permalink to that commit. Earlier retirements, with their
revisions, blobs and hashes, are listed in the
[previous version of this index](https://github.com/fermga/TNFR-Python-Engine/blob/279054b8694a9bf5fd3a7e67da65847a3e36d749/theory/research/archive/README.md#recovery-records).
