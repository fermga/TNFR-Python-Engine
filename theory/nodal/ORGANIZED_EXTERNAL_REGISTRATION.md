Use the complete 54-node support, both boundary channels and the storage

 \tag{1}
\]

Prime denotes elapsed contact time in the declared tau clock. Unit capacity,

not normalized by its degree. Both rows transform under the original clock.
All 54 signed forms and 54 phase coordinates remain state variables; their

Fix one actual host source and one actual independently recovered external

\[
 \begin{split}
 \theta_{H,c,j}(0)&=\psi_j+H_{c,j},&
 \theta_{D,j}(0)&=\psi_j+\beta+z_{D,j},\\

\]

Here H includes the actual carrier endpoint, all transported fine errors

no ideal source or observation-based reconstruction replaces either state.

The parameter beta is supplied at the donor's primitive preparation. Common

Preparation start times have the same synchronization as the earlier contact.
Once joined, rotating the donor alone changes the contact law's evaluated

The joined total form is zero. With the stated lift convention its phase

For the initial contact sine moment, same-slot carrier differences cancel:

\[

Consequently \(|Q|\le5\) and its value is unchanged when only this supplied

## 2. Uniform finite response under the complete law

\(\mathcal A_\beta(t)\) be the contact sine moment along its actual joined

\[

\]

This identity holds for the actual fine state, not only on the invariant
twelve-coordinate carrier. The donor reaction, host backreaction and all

\[
 B_o=X+gT,\qquad m=2gB_oT,\qquad B=B_o+\frac74gh.

\]

Each contact gap changes by at most \(6gBt\), so

\[

 \tag{6}

\[

The first remainder term bounds \((3/2)\int_0^h|R_\beta(t)|dt\), and the

Their derivation uses the actual joined law, rather than transferring a
verdict from beta=pi/2. The finite response need not be an exact sinusoid:

## 4. Capability, attainability and the order of quantifiers

Let Y_beta be the difference of the two noisy donor readings. Their errors
may be correlated, including across origins and source families. Their
difference n_beta still satisfies \(|n_\beta|\le2\eta\), where
\(\eta=10^{-10}\). Put \(\epsilon=E+2\eta\). Equation (7) becomes

\[
 Y_\beta=-\frac{\gamma h}{4}\operatorname{Im}(e^{i\beta}Q)
                  +r_\beta+n_\beta,\qquad
 |r_\beta+n_\beta|\le\epsilon.
 \tag{11}
\]

For each fixed actual source member and any admissible assignment of the
reading errors, define its finite capability
\(M_Y=\sup_{\beta\bmod2\pi}|Y_\beta|\). Then

\[
 \max\left\{0,\frac{\gamma h}{4}|Q|-\epsilon\right\}
 \le M_Y\le\frac{\gamma h}{4}|Q|+\epsilon.
 \tag{12}
\]

The upper inequality holds for every origin. For Q nonzero, choose
\(\beta_* =\pi/2-\arg Q\) modulo a full turn. The harmonic in (11) has
magnitude \(\gamma h|Q|/4\) there. The reverse triangle inequality gives
the lower bound at this particular origin for **all** permitted reading
errors. The existence of this witness precedes any choice of noise; no
continuity of the reading errors or attainment of their supremum is needed.
If Q is zero, (12) gives only the trivial nonnegative lower bound.

Let \([\gamma]=[\gamma_-,\gamma_+]\) be its outward enclosure. Combining
(10)--(12), with outward evaluation of products, gives the uniform bounds

\[
 L_w=\max\{0,\gamma_-hA_{w,-}/4-\epsilon\},\qquad
 U_w=\gamma_+hA_{w,+}/4+\epsilon,
 \qquad L_w\le M_{Y,w}\le U_w.
 \tag{13}
\]

The frozen decisions are \(U_1<10^{-8}\) and \(L_0>3\cdot10^{-7}\).
The first makes every outer-one response small at every relative origin.
The second supplies, for each actual outer-zero source, at least one
origin with a large response for all reading errors. The witness may depend
on unknown fine coordinates. It is not an origin inferable from the
single-history winding record or an autonomous alignment mechanism.

At the outer-zero witness, retain its paired outer-one source and the same
donor residual. If both decisions hold, the absolute response difference

\[

       >3\cdot10^{-7}-10^{-8}=2.9\cdot10^{-7}.
 \tag{14}
\]

This assertion includes all four reading errors through the two separately

Even the true finite response can vanish at some origins. Whenever

beta_*+pi. Smooth dependence of the complete solution on its prepared
