## 2. One reference from attachment through observation

Let tau=0 denote outer attachment, after the isolated recoveries. Each child

\[
 U_c(0)=M_c,\quad \Phi_c(0)=0,\qquad
 M_c=4092(c-2)\quad\hbox{or}\quad M_c=0.
 \tag{4}
\]

The branch is part of the supplied preparation. No hidden residual is set
to zero. Define a mathematical carrier reference by these same means,

\[
 \overline U'=(-L_5\overline U+\gamma S_5(\overline\Phi))/4+f,

 \tag{5}
\]

Here f is zero during formation and is exactly the original prescribed
waveform during observation. The reference continues across the boundary;

For full differences from this carrier, five independent recovered children
give initial Euclidean radii `r0<sqrt(5)*q_x<3e-9` and

\[
 Z=2(z_0+g r_0)=1.4\cdot10^{-11},\qquad
 R=r_0+24gZ,
 \quad \|\theta-\overline\theta\|\le Z,\quad
 \|x-\overline x\|\le R.
 \tag{6}

The radii on the right use the conservative bounds `r0=3e-9`, `z0=6e-12`.
The form convolution uses the same global zero-mean difference and spectral

Orthogonal removal of child means is contractive. Consequently the actual

## 3. Mean prediction with carried initialization

The full form radius R is unnecessarily loose for predicting means. The
initial collective difference in (4) is exactly zero. The actual projected
row differs from (5) by the quadratic sine remainder, with
`||R_mean||2<=rho=gZ^2/9` throughout the combined history. Heat contraction
and the live phase row therefore give, with `k=gH=9/125<1`,

\[
 E_U\le H\rho+k E_\Phi,\qquad E_\Phi\le k E_U,
 \qquad
 E_U\le\frac{H\rho}{1-k^2}<1.6\cdot10^{-24},\qquad
 E_\Phi\le\frac{kH\rho}{1-k^2}<1.2\cdot10^{-25}.
 \tag{8}
\]

The bound is uniform on `0<=tau<=200+15.00015<216`, not merely a fitted
endpoint estimate. It includes uncertainty in all admitted fine preparation
directions. It meets the declaration's `1.5e-20` intrinsic target without
five measured statistics or separately known means at tau=200. No extra gain
is asserted from comparing the carrier with itself using the same priors;
the tighter prediction follows from retaining stronger source information.

The actual formation and complete input word retain their original identity
proofs; formation is allowed to acquire outer winding. The small observation
phase difference also transfers the existing strict mean-gap margin to the
reference. This does not reconstruct fine-node identities from five phases.
The actual external power remains `9(L5 U)^T f`; the predicted work differs
by at most `36 E_U integral ||f||1`. Absolute and positive parts refer to the
total external power, not separate fine-node channels. Preparation and
attachment work, hidden loss and fine storage retain their original owners.

Equation (8) is an exact-solution comparison. A numerical approximation
adds its own propagated residual; an empirical forecast also needs an
independently justified preparation, clock, input and observation bridge.
Neither budget is included in the small intrinsic error above.

## 4. Why the noisy records alone do not supply the variances

For the observation-only route retain the common coarse bounds
`a0=31/10^9`, `b0=601/10^10` and the original larger inference class.
The two declared states have equal initial collective coordinates and
norm certificates. The first lies on the exact carrier. The second has
the [proved variance difference](ORGANIZED_COMPRESSED_MEMORY.md#5-initialization-debt-and-verification)

\[
 \|m^{(1)}-m^{(0)}\|_1=4a^2/9>2\cdot10^{-17},
 \qquad a=2^{-26}.
 \tag{9}
\]

Both solutions exist under the same complete finite input word and retain
the original class's identities. The shared carrier bound, freshly rebuilt
from these coarse primitives on T=16, gives

\[
 \sup_\tau\|U^{(1)}(\tau)-U^{(0)}(\tau)\|_2
 \le E_0<2.3\cdot10^{-18}<\eta_{raw}=10^{-14}.
 \tag{10}
\]

For every one of the 150 samples, define a symbolic record equal to the
first solution's true mean. Its noise is zero for that solution; for the
second solution its noise is their coordinate difference, uniformly smaller
than eta_raw. These two error vectors are admitted simultaneously with
their respective complete histories. Thus the *joint* sets of possible
records overlap. This constructs existence without evaluating or inventing
an acquired response and does not rely on marginal interval compatibility.

Any single-valued estimator of m receives the same record and even the same
gifted exact initial means in both cases. The triangle inequality and (9)
exclude error at most `1e-17` for both. The original noise permits arbitrary
correlations, so treating samples as independent noise or averaging them
does not repair this worst-case obstruction. Additional sample times in
the same bounded history retain (10). Noiseless data, different observations,
different inputs or a more restrictive preparation require their own proof.

This excludes a uniform observation-only inverse on the containing class,
not initialization of the actual prepared image. It also leaves the
existing winding inverse intact: both states here have the same winding.

## 5. Consolidation and remaining execution boundary

each within E0 of one common carrier history. Their mean-output diameter is
therefore at most `2E0`, much smaller than the raw reading allowance, even
when their variance statistics cannot be identified at the requested
precision. This is a finite output cover, not an exact state quotient or
a transitive equivalence relation inferred from noisy-record overlap.

The prepared route is stronger: it supplies accurate initialization and
prediction from the original history. The observation-only route identifies
a genuine information limit for a larger class. Retaining both avoids
treating a discarded source bound as a fundamental dynamical obstruction.
The conditional compression theorem and its general counterexamples remain
valid; the five-statistic representation is useful only when a selected
