admission with declared current-engine static controls, not an acquired-source
response, autonomous event selection or continuous trajectory campaign.

executing the word does not derive that continuation uniquely. Its full

The [law-admission owner](../DIAGNOSTIC_AND_GRAMMAR_SCOPE.md#canonical-execution-and-law-selection)

## 4. Complete event work and a uniform joint storage margin

For the actual weighted support use

\[
 H=\tfrac12\sum_{\{i,j\}}W_{ij}(x_i-x_j)^2
       +\sum_{\{i,j\}}[1-\cos(\theta_j-\theta_i)].
\]

so `H^-=8a^2=1/32`. AL changes the four old left boundary-edge form
differences from `a` to `c`. Its complete old-edge storage jump is

\[
 \Delta H_{\mathrm{AL}}
    =4(c^2-a^2)=-8ab+4b^2=-\frac{127}{262144}.
\]

A positive form increment need not increase this storage: here it
reduces the target's contrast with its four old neighbors. This is an
accounted hybrid jump, not continuous dissipation.

\[

   =2W_Ac^2+\tfrac12W_Bb^2+5(1-\cos\alpha)>0.

\[
 \boxed{\qquad
 H^+=4c^2+4a^2+2W_Ac^2+\tfrac12W_Bb^2
                        +5(1-\cos\alpha).
 \qquad}
\]

The actual represented endpoint must use its actual forms, phases and
weights in this sum, rather than an ideal weight substituted after
execution. The following generous ideal bound uses only positivity,

Thus `9/20` is a convenient prospective upper target, not an estimated

## 9. A full-law finite form enclosure from a derived radius

The following shared estimate applies beyond the particular stars.
Retain a finite connected simple undirected bare support, fixed finite
nonnegative conductances, held nonnegative capacities, positive exchange
coefficient `w` and phase-storage scale `beta`, zero loss, no inputs
and no events. Both original-clock rows are

\[
 \dot x_i=\frac{w\nu_i}{\pi d_i}
          \sum_{j\sim i}\sin(\theta_j-\theta_i),\qquad
 \dot\theta_i=\frac{w\nu_i}{\beta\pi d_i}(L_Wx)_i .
\]

The phase lifts evolve under the second row; they are not frozen for
the estimate. The field is globally Lipschitz, hence supplies a unique
solution on every finite time interval. A zero capacity freezes the
corresponding rows of this complete law. A supported zero-conductance
edge still contributes to bare degree and sine incidence.

Let \(P=I-\mathbf1\mathbf1^T/n\) be the fixed orthogonal
arithmetic-centering projector, and let
\(\mathcal R_0=\|Px(0)\|_2\). Bare incidence bounds each form
speed globally by `w nu_i/pi`, giving

\[
 \|\dot x(t)\|_2\leq V:=\frac{w}{\pi}\|\nu\|_2,\qquad
 \|Px(t)\|_2
 \leq\mathcal R_0+\int_0^t\|P\dot x(s)\|_2\,ds
 \leq\mathcal R_0+Vh=:\mathcal R_h
 \quad(0\leq t\leq h).
\]

This derives confinement on the whole requested window. It does not
assume that the arithmetic mean is conserved: the projection has norm
one even when the mean moves. Positive `h` is elapsed original
structural time. The estimate has no imposed small-time domain, although
its resulting interval can become uninformative for a large duration.

Write the weighted strength, bare-normalized mobility and their product
as

\[
 s_i=\sum_{j\sim i}W_{ij},\qquad
 \mu_i=\frac{\nu_i}{d_i},\qquad
 g_i=\mu_i s_i .
\]

Since every form contrast is at most `sqrt(2) ||Px||_2` in magnitude,
the actual weighted phase row satisfies

\[
 |(L_Wx)_i|\leq\sqrt2\,\mathcal R_h s_i,\qquad
 |\dot\theta_i|\leq
           \frac{\sqrt2\,w\mathcal R_h}{\beta\pi}g_i .
\]

Differentiate the complete form row, retaining the phase evolution:

\[
 \ddot x_i=\frac{w\mu_i}{\pi}
       \sum_{j\sim i}\cos(\theta_j-\theta_i)
                              (\dot\theta_j-\dot\theta_i).
\]

Consequently the shared conditional acceleration estimate has its
whole-window confinement premise established by the preceding proof:

\[
 |\ddot x_i(t)|\leq A_i:=
 \frac{\sqrt2\,w^2\mathcal R_h}{\beta\pi^2}
       \mu_i\left(\sum_{j\sim i}g_j+d_i g_i\right),
 \qquad 0\leq t\leq h .
\]

Actual conductances enter `g`, while bare neighbors and degree enter
the outer sum. They cannot be interchanged. Integrating the second
derivative gives an exact nonlinear remainder bound,

\[
 \left|x_i(t)-x_i(0)-t\dot x_i(0)\right|
       \leq \frac12 A_i t^2,\qquad 0\leq t\leq h.
\]

For a contrast, the sum of its two acceleration bounds is sufficient.
No constant-Jacobian model, sampled trajectory or Euler update is used.
The numerical implementation can take outward upper enclosures of
\(\mathcal R_0,V,A_i\) and outward intervals of the independent initial
constitutive rates. Its endpoint interval is
`x_i(0)+h*initial_rate_i+[-A_i h^2/2,A_i h^2/2]`.
Interval width and the analytic nonlinear remainder remain separate;
there is no numerical-integration discretization error to estimate.

## 14. Exact zero-offset state in declared radian lifts

Consider a finite connected simple undirected support, positive held

\[
 \mu_i=\frac{\nu_i}{d_i},\qquad
 \dot x_i=\frac{\mu_i}{\pi}\sum_{j\sim i}\sin(\theta_j-\theta_i),

                 \sum_{j\sim i}W_{ij}(x_i-x_j).
\]

For an ordered partition \(\mathcal B=(B_0,\ldots,B_{k-1})\), define the
linear lift \(\iota\) by

\[
 x_i=X_a,\qquad \theta_i=\Theta_a\quad(i\in B_a),\qquad
 (X,\Theta)\in\mathbb R^{2k}.
\]

phase averages or a projection of an arbitrary fine state onto the family.
For \(b\ne a\), write

\[
 n_{ib}=\#\{j\in B_b:j\sim i\},\qquad
 c_{ib}=\sum_{\substack{j\in B_b\\j\sim i}}W_{ij}.
\]

The family is invariant for every collective state if and only if both
\(\mu_i n_{ib}\) and \(\mu_i c_{ib}\) are independent of \(i\in B_a\),
for every distinct pair of blocks. Internal edges contribute zero to both
rows on this zero-offset family. Sufficiency follows by substitution.
For necessity, vary the independent external phases, and separately the
independent external forms, while comparing the rows of two nodes in the
same block. Each external sine coefficient and each external linear
coefficient must agree. Equality at a single source is insufficient.

When these conditions hold, define

\[
 M_a=\sum_{i\in B_a}\frac{d_i}{\nu_i},\qquad
 N_{ab}=\sum_{i\in B_a}n_{ib},\qquad
 C_{ab}=\sum_{i\in B_a}c_{ib}\quad(a\ne b),
\]

with zero diagonal. The undirected fine support gives symmetric \(N,C\).

its block proves that its value is \(N_{ab}/M_a\) or \(C_{ab}/M_a\).

\[
 \boxed{\displaystyle
 \dot X_a=\frac1{\pi M_a}\sum_{b\ne a}N_{ab}
                  \sin(\Theta_b-\Theta_a),\qquad
 \dot\Theta_a=\frac1{\pi M_a}\sum_{b\ne a}C_{ab}(X_a-X_b).}
 \tag{14.1}
\]

family and every real elapsed time. No Taylor truncation or numerical

\]

Constituent pressures can differ within a block even though their form rates

## 19. Whole-window error from two solutions of the same held law

This comparison extends the complete-state envelope of Section 9 to
initial uncertainty. In each application, both solutions use the same
support, conductances, capacities, coefficients and original clock.
The attached and disconnected laws require separate applications.

For the shared conservative comparison, let \(w,\beta>0\), let held
capacities and conductances be nonnegative, and retain a finite
connected simple bare support. Write \(s_i=\sum_{j\sim i}W_{ij}\),
\(d_i=\#\{j:j\sim i\}\), and \(\mu_i=\nu_i/d_i\). The complete rows are

\[
 \dot x_i=\frac{w\mu_i}{\pi}
                 \sum_{j\sim i}\sin(\theta_j-\theta_i),\qquad
 \dot\theta_i=\frac{w\mu_i}{\beta\pi}
                 \sum_{j\sim i}W_{ij}(x_i-x_j).
 \tag{19.1}
\]

Keep real phase lifts for both solutions. Suppose their initial
coordinate differences obey supplied component bounds

\[
 |\widetilde x_i(0)-x_i(0)|\le e_i,\qquad
 |\widetilde\theta_i(0)-\theta_i(0)|\le f_i,\qquad e_i,f_i\ge0.
\]

These are independent coordinate bounds; no zero-mean constraint,
synchronization or equality within a block is assumed.
The global inequality \(|\sin u-\sin v|\le|u-v|\) gives

\[
 |\dot{\widetilde x}_i-\dot x_i|
 \le a_i\|\widetilde\theta-\theta\|_\infty,\qquad
 a_i=\frac{2w\nu_i}{\pi},
\]
\[
 |\dot{\widetilde\theta}_i-\dot\theta_i|
 \le b_i\|\widetilde x-x\|_\infty,\qquad
 b_i=\frac{2w\mu_i s_i}{\beta\pi}.
 \tag{19.2}
\]

Bare degree cancels in \(a_i\), whereas the weighted strength remains
in \(b_i\). A zero-conductance edge therefore still contributes to
the sine law; it does not supply the same contribution to the
phase-gradient law. Zero capacity sets both coefficients to zero
in this particular complete model.

Set \(e=\max_i e_i\), \(f=\max_i f_i\), and choose finite upper bounds
\(A\ge\max_i a_i\), \(B\ge\max_i b_i\). For a positive duration \(h\),
define the actual whole-window suprema

\[
 E_x=\sup_{0\le t\le h}\|\widetilde x(t)-x(t)\|_\infty,\qquad
 E_\theta=\sup_{0\le t\le h}
                 \|\widetilde\theta(t)-\theta(t)\|_\infty.
\]

The globally Lipschitz fields ensure that both solutions exist and
these suprema are finite. Integrating (19.2), then taking the
suprema, gives \(E_x\le e+AhE_\theta\) and
\(E_\theta\le f+BhE_x\). Consequently, if

\[
 D=1-ABh^2>0,
\]

the entirely rational comparison in \(A,B,e,f,h\) is

\[
 \boxed{\displaystyle
 E_x\le X=\frac{e+Ahf}{D},\qquad
 E_\theta\le T=\frac{f+Bhe}{D}.}
 \tag{19.3}
\]

Using those global bounds once more in each component gives

\[
 |\widetilde x_i(t)-x_i(t)|\le e_i+a_i hT,\qquad
 |\widetilde\theta_i(t)-\theta_i(t)|
                         \le f_i+b_i hX
 \quad(0\le t\le h).
 \tag{19.4}
\]

Replacing \(a_i,b_i,A,B\) throughout by rigorously outward upper
bounds preserves all conclusions. This proves the numerical
enclosure without replacing either solution by an Euler step,
linearization or independently chosen trajectory.

The condition \(D>0\) is sufficient for this particular comparison,
not a dynamical instability threshold. When it fails, a nonzero
initial box receives an unavailable error envelope rather than a
claim of divergence or a division by a nonpositive denominator.
If every \(e_i=f_i=0\), uniqueness gives exact zero differences
for any finite \(h\), independently of that comparison condition.
An unavailable temporal envelope can still retain a separately
valid initial storage bound.

### 19.1 A complete initial storage box

For the same law the conserved full storage is

\[
 H_\beta(x,\theta)=\frac12\sum_{\{i,j\}}W_{ij}(x_i-x_j)^2+
                  \beta\sum_{\{i,j\}}[1-\cos(\theta_j-\theta_i)].
\]

At the supplied reference source define
\(q_{ij}=x_i(0)-x_j(0)\), \(\gamma_{ij}=\theta_j(0)-\theta_i(0)\),
\(r_{ij}=e_i+e_j\), and \(z_{ij}=f_i+f_j\).
The cosine is globally one-Lipschitz, and its potential lies in
\([0,2]\). Therefore every initial state in the coordinate box satisfies

\[
 H_\beta(\widetilde x(0),\widetilde\theta(0))
 \le
 \frac12\sum_{\{i,j\}}W_{ij}(|q_{ij}|+r_{ij})^2+
 \beta\sum_{\{i,j\}}
       \min\{2,\;1-\cos\gamma_{ij}+z_{ij}\}.
 \tag{19.5}
\]

An outward upper bound for the reference cosine potential can replace
its exact value. Equation (19.5) includes every held edge and the
actual \(\beta\); it is not an average of component budgets. It needs
no temporal small-gain condition. Under a matching positive-storage
identity theorem, conservation can transport this source budget for
all time even though the solution-difference envelope is only finite.

## 34. A full-state nonlinear error bound about consensus

\((x_*,\theta_*)\), with real continuous phase lifts.

\[
 |\xi_i(0)|\le e_i,\qquad |\eta_i(0)|\le f_i.

The following reusable estimate allows the conservative coefficients
\(w,\beta>0\); the frozen star comparison has \(w=\beta=1\).

\[
 a_i=\frac{2w\nu_i}{\pi},\qquad
 b_i=\frac{2w\nu_i}{\beta\pi d_i}\sum_{j\sim i}W_{ij},
 \qquad A=\max_i a_i,\quad B=\max_i b_i.                  \tag{34.1}
\]

The bare neighbor count belongs in the first channel; the
conductances belong in the second. Since \(|\sin z|\le|z|\),
the component inequalities are

\[
 |\dot\xi_i|\le a_i\|\eta\|_\infty,\qquad
 |\dot\eta_i|\le b_i\|\xi\|_\infty.                        \tag{34.2}
\]

Put \(e=\max_i e_i\), \(f=\max_i f_i\).
For a positive duration \(h\) with \(D_h=1-ABh^2>0\),
the coupled supremum argument of Section 19 gives, throughout
\(0\le t\le h\),

\[
 \|\xi(t)\|_\infty\le X=\frac{e+Ahf}{D_h},\qquad
 \|\eta(t)\|_\infty\le T=\frac{f+Bhe}{D_h},                 \tag{34.3}
\]
\[
 |\xi_i(t)|\le X_i=e_i+a_i hT,\qquad
 |\eta_i(t)|\le T_i=f_i+b_i hX.                           \tag{34.4}
\]

These are derived amplitude bounds for the full nonlinear solution.
They do not presume either the nonlinear five-block family or
closeness to a chosen tangent trajectory. A nonpositive comparison
denominator is failure of this sufficient finite-window estimate,
not failure of the dynamics. The exactly zero initial box is
separately settled by uniqueness at equilibrium.

### 34.1 The missing sine terms are cubic and enter only the form row

For every real \(z\), Taylor's integral remainder gives

\[
 |\sin z-z|\le |z|^3/6.
\]

Subtract the full tangent initialized at the same point.
With \(\delta\xi=\xi-\xi_{\rm lin}\) and
\(\delta\eta=\eta-\eta_{\rm lin}\), the exact error equations are

\[
 \delta\dot\xi=-\frac w\pi D_\nu L_N\delta\eta+r(t),
 \qquad
 \delta\dot\eta=\frac w{\beta\pi}D_\nu L_W\delta\xi,
 \qquad
 \delta\xi(0)=\delta\eta(0)=0,                            \tag{34.5}
\]

where \(D_\nu=\operatorname{diag}(\nu_i/d_i)\), and

\[
 r_i(t)=\frac{w\nu_i}{\pi d_i}
 \sum_{j\sim i}
 \left[\sin(\eta_j-\eta_i)-(\eta_j-\eta_i)\right].
\]

Thus (34.4) supplies the uniform residual bounds

\[
 |r_i(t)|\le R_i=
 \frac{w\nu_i}{6\pi d_i}\sum_{j\sim i}(T_i+T_j)^3,
 \qquad R=\max_i R_i.                                   \tag{34.6}
\]

No conductance factor is inserted into this bare sine remainder.
The phase row is already linear in form, so it has no separate
nonlinear residual. Applying the same two-channel supremum
inequalities to (34.5) yields

\[
 E_x:=\sup_{[0,h]}\|\delta\xi\|_\infty
 \le\frac{hR}{D_h},\qquad
 E_\theta:=\sup_{[0,h]}\|\delta\eta\|_\infty
 \le\frac{Bh^2R}{D_h},                                   \tag{34.7}
\]
\[
 |\delta\xi_i(t)|\le hR_i+a_i hE_\theta,\qquad
 |\delta\eta_i(t)|\le b_i hE_x.                           \tag{34.8}
\]

Every fine coordinate is included. In particular, a fine direction
in the kernel of the tangent observation can contribute to \(r\)
and then to the observed nonlinear error.

For a fixed linear observation row \(c\), its error is bounded by
the sum of the absolute row coefficients times the corresponding
component bounds in (34.8). The two regional rows have
\(\|\ell\|_1=2\), so

\[
 |y_x-y_{x,\rm lin}|\le2E_x,\qquad
 |y_\theta-y_{\theta,\rm lin}|\le2E_\theta.                \tag{34.9}
\]

The initialization of the minimal realization remains the full
\(C(\xi(0),\eta(0))\). An arbitrary selected Krylov chart uses its
own \(|C|\) error projection; its coordinate scaling is not
silently replaced by the normalization of these regional rows.
