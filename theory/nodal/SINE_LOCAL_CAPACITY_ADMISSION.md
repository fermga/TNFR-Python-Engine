## 6. Observation and reader error remain separate from access

The true-history statement (25) uses exact values of the same continuous

As a sufficient, explicitly hypothetical budget, require each materialized

No timestamp jitter is admitted; the absolute dyadic times and their
differences are exactly representable in binary64.

With spacing `h_t=2^-20`, the three-sample functional has coefficient absolute
sum `4/h_t^2`. Its endpoint-error contribution is therefore at most

\[
 E_{\mathrm{samples}}\leq\frac{4\epsilon_{\mathrm{obs}}}{h_t^2}
 =2^{-10}.
 \tag{27}
\]

The strict form range (8), including this tiny endpoint allowance, lies inside

the exact slope factor `2^20` gives at most `2^-35`. Each slope magnitude is
below `3*2^17`. Their subtraction has magnitude below `3*2^18` and adds at
most `2^-34` rounding error. The inherited two slope errors and this rounding
sum to at most `2^-33`. The final exact factor `2^20` gives

\[
 E_{\mathrm{reader}}\leq2^{-13}.
 \tag{28}
\]

All nonzero intermediates remain finite and normal in this estimate. It

\[
 \widehat A_{2,\mathrm{binary64}}
 >\frac18-2^{-10}-2^{-13}

The earlier illustrative endpoint allowance `2^-40` would instead allow

The bound (23) already controls the actual finite-window average. No additional
