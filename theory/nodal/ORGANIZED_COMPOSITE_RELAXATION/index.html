    Finite observable return under a persistent composite contact¶

    The persistent-contact theorem retains both original composite identities for every continued age of the unit A port and proves full form/phase relaxation. It gives no finite deadline for an observer to resolve the return of transferred form. This chapter supplies a conservative sufficient deadline for the original aggregate, under the same actual source and complete law.

    The result is a conditional bound on every admitted history. It neither integrates a new trajectory nor supplies a measured return time. Its very large structural deadline is not calibrated to laboratory time.

    1. Retain the reached source and the original observation¶

    Keep the actual first-section donor and independently mature recipient, their original error pairs, associated held weights, unit capacities, internal supports, continuous phase branches and independent phase gauges. The full-contact prefix lasts \(2000000\), the cut lasts \(10000\), and the unit nine-slot A port then continues uninterrupted. The new contact age \(s=0\) is that original activation, at structural time \(2010000\). There is no reset at the already certified \(s_h=1/2\) endpoint. The structural clock remains \(\tau=(1023/1024)t\).

    On the conserved whole-zero-form leaf and fixed per-copy phase gauges, the complete actual rows are

    \[ x'=-Dx-\frac{\gamma}{9}K(y)y,\qquad y'=\frac{\gamma}{9}Hx,\qquad D=H/9+G_A,\qquad \gamma=\frac1{1023\pi}. \tag{1} \]

    Here \(K(y)y=\nabla U(\Theta+y)\) is the integrated bare phase Hessian from the stationary winding reference. It is the exact sine force, not a tangent approximation. The common-form mode remains part of \(x\). The supplied port changes neither the internal degree normalization nor the phase rows. Actual unequal \(H\) and \(G_A\) need not commute.

    The earlier aggregate contract uses

    \[ Q_A(s)=-\sum_{j\in D}x_j(s)=-9a(s),\qquad \theta_Q=10^{-8},\qquad \nu_h,\nu_\ell\le10^{-9}. \tag{2} \]

    The zero donor entry total is derived from the prefix, not newly measured. Early and late records must refer to the same actual member and history. Their additive reading errors are independent and paid once each. Neither record is supplied here, and this precision does not grant current or phase precision.

    The fixed return target is an absolute true-aggregate band \(\rho_Q=10^{-9}\), rather than a negative value merely below the threshold. We seek a deadline after which \(|Q_A|\le\rho_Q\), keeping the original positive half-unit certificate and threshold unchanged.

    2. What the existing storage supplies¶

    Write \(N^2=\|x\|^2+\|y\|^2\), and retain the common-form storage

    \[ E_+=\tfrac12x^THx+c\,x^TPx+U(\Theta+y)-U(\Theta). \]

    Its original source and actual-weight bounds give

    \[ \frac15N^2\le E_+\le9N^2,\qquad E_+'\le-\alpha\|x\|^2,\qquad \alpha=\frac14-84\delta_w>\frac15,\quad \delta_w=2\,10^{-60}. \tag{3} \]

    At A activation \(a=0\), so

    \[ E_+(0)\le E_0= \frac{27}{2}\Delta^2+486\eta+18\eta^2+9r_*^2 <\frac1{50000}, \quad \Delta=1/1024,\ \eta=2\,10^{-69},\ r_*=2^{-28}. \tag{4} \]

    The already proved invariant domain retains \(N<1/100<1/32\) for all contact ages. Its strict marked-registration theorem remains available independently; the present calculation does not restart that proof.

    On this domain,

    \[ \|H\|\le18,\qquad \|D\|\le18/9+2=4,\qquad K(y)\succeq\frac25I,\qquad \gamma_{\rm lo}=\frac1{3300}<\gamma< \gamma_{\rm hi}=\frac1{3000}. \tag{5} \]

    The phase bound uses the actual retained acute chart, with cosine at least \(1/10\), and the bare graph gap at least four on each mean-fixed phase leaf. It is not inferred merely from a total-storage flag. Phase gauges must be fixed: this strictly positive Hessian bound does not hold on unremoved constant-phase directions.

    Form loss in (3) alone does not yield a full-state decay rate. At \(x=0\) and noncritical \(y\), its instantaneous bound vanishes even though the complete form row immediately moves the state. A mixed form/phase term can retain that restoring mechanism in a strict comparison storage.

    3. A strict-storage lemma without a commutation premise¶

    Consider a complete system on a declared invariant leaf with

    \[ x'=-Dx-\gamma K(y)y/d,\qquad y'=\gamma Hx/d,\qquad d>0. \]

    Suppose a differentiable storage \(E\) and fixed scalar bounds satisfy

    \[ L N^2\le E\le U N^2,\quad E'\le-A\|x\|^2,\quad \|H\|\le H_*,\quad\|D\|\le D_*,\quad K(y)\succeq k_*I, \quad0\le\gamma_{\rm lo}\le\gamma\le\gamma_{\rm hi}. \tag{6} \]

    Assume \(0<L\le U\), \(A>0\), and nonnegative norm/stiffness bounds. The coupling and chosen coefficient \(\varepsilon\ge0\) are held. Define

    \[ V=E+\varepsilon\gamma x^Ty,\quad L_s=L-\varepsilon\gamma_{\rm hi}/2,\quad U_s=U+\varepsilon\gamma_{\rm hi}/2. \tag{7} \]

    Then \(L_sN^2\le V\le U_sN^2\). Differentiating both full rows gives

    \[ V'\le-\left(A-\frac{\varepsilon\gamma_{\rm hi}^2H_*}{d}\right) \|x\|^2 +\varepsilon\gamma D_*\|x\|\|y\| -\frac{\varepsilon\gamma^2k_*}{d}\|y\|^2. \tag{8} \]

    Young's inequality with the fixed form-loss budget \(A/2\) gives

    \[ \varepsilon\gamma D_*\|x\|\|y\| \le\frac A2\|x\|^2+ \frac{\varepsilon^2\gamma^2D_*^2}{2A}\|y\|^2. \]

    If the candidate coefficients

    \[ q_x=\frac A2-\frac{\varepsilon\gamma_{\rm hi}^2H_*}{d}, \qquad q_y=\varepsilon\gamma_{\rm lo}^2 \left(\frac{k_*}{d}-\frac{\varepsilon D_*^2}{2A}\right) \tag{9} \]

    and \(L_s\) are strictly positive, the bracket in \(q_y\) is positive, so replacing \(\gamma^2\) there by its lower bound is legitimate. Hence

    \[ V'\le-q_x\|x\|^2-q_y\|y\|^2 \le-\lambda V,\qquad \lambda=\min(q_x,q_y)/U_s>0. \tag{10} \]

    This uses neither \(HG_A=G_AH\), a positive inverse of \(H\) on the live common-form direction, nor a derivative of \(K(y)\). It does require the complete rows, fixed coupling and storage/domain premises in (6). Nonpositive candidate coefficients supply no positive recovery rate; they do not establish instability or failure of the underlying dynamics.

    The appended strict-energy helper returns these exact scalar candidates and a rate only under the strict positivity conditions. All ten rational inputs normalize before algebra. It certifies no matrix, source, invariant chart, observation or physical energy. Existing isolated and commuting-exchange kernels retain their own premises and remain unchanged.

    4. A fixed conservative deadline for the actual aggregate¶

    Apply (6)--(10) with

    \[ L=1/5,\ U=9,\ A=1/5,\ H_*=18,\ D_*=4,\ k_*=2/5,\ d=9, \qquad \varepsilon=1/2000. \tag{11} \]

    The actual loss \(\alpha>A\) justifies the conservative form budget. The exact candidates obey

    \[ L_s>1/6,\quad U_s<10,\quad q_x>1/12,\qquad q_y=\varepsilon\gamma_{\rm lo}^2(2/45-1/50) >\varepsilon\gamma_{\rm lo}^2/45. \tag{12} \]

    Thus a simpler, deliberately rounded rate is

    \[ \lambda_0=\frac{\varepsilon\gamma_{\rm lo}^2}{450} =\frac1{9801000000000},\qquad V(s)\le V(0)e^{-\lambda_0s}. \tag{13} \]

    The phase/form cross term changes the comparison quantity, not the field or a physical energy assignment. At original A activation, (3)--(4) imply

    \[ V(0)\le(1+5\varepsilon\gamma_{\rm hi}/2)E_0<2E_0, \qquad N(s)^2\le12E_0e^{-\lambda_0s}. \tag{14} \]

    The aggregate has covector norm three on the whole-zero-form leaf: its representative \((-1_D,0_R)\) projects to \((-1_D/2,1_R/2)\), whose squared norm is nine. Therefore

    \[ |Q_A(s)|^2\le9\|x(s)\|^2 \le108E_0e^{-\lambda_0s}. \tag{15} \]

    The instantaneous-current covector is a different observation; it is not used in (15). A nonzero unaccounted whole mean would invalidate this decay target for the declared aggregate.

    Freeze the integer exponent \(n=64\) and the sufficient contact age

    \[ T_{\rm ret}=\frac{64}{\lambda_0}=627264000000000. \tag{16} \]

    Since \(e>2\), exact integer arithmetic gives \(e^{-64}<2^{-64}\). For every \(s\ge T_{\rm ret}\),

    \[ |Q_A(s)|^2\le\frac{108E_0}{2^{64}} <\frac{108}{50000\,2^{64}}<10^{-18}=\rho_Q^2. \tag{17} \]

    The return band is retained for the entire later tail. This argument uses no sampled endpoint, square-root approximation, exponential solver or trajectory with an astronomical number of integration steps.
