    Retained organized identity under sufficiently weak external contact¶

    1. A controlled change of contact strength¶

    The unit-contact problem leaves stationary stability in the original complete marked class unresolved. This result changes one supplied model parameter: all 45 external spokes have the same positive weight eta. It proves stationary existence and all-future identity retention for a declared weak-contact range, including the original acquired and observed source family. It is not a solution of the unit-weight stationary problem. An exact stationary continuation also identifies a strictly stable full-state branch and its collective fold. The larger branch interval gives local stability of that branch; the acquired-source inclusion retains its own smaller interval. Within that source interval, the full fine residuals decay exponentially and the actual collective rows approach the six-node wheel law with an explicitly bounded remainder. The original all-origin source family cannot, however, consist entirely of preparations converging to locally asymptotically stable equilibria: its retained relative-phase circle forces at least one exceptional origin for every fixed source member. At the fixed strength eta=1/100000, a separate transient estimate certifies five explicit donor-origin intervals selecting the five attractive relative registrations, while retaining every original source error and the complete observation history.

    Eta is supplied contact strength, not a selected law, inferred physical constant or control inferred from dissipation. Eta=0 is a disconnected reference used in the proof; every dynamical conclusion below requires strictly positive eta.

    2. The old marked cell retains its complete boundary barrier¶

    Use the original lifted branches: internal closing edges and host rim closing edges receive their fixed 2*pi addition. Every old edge gap lies in (-pi/2,pi/2). The resulting cell preserves the complete marked class, including the zero mixed host periods, not just fifteen winding labels. Spokes are unrestricted.

    Put

    \[ \begin{split} V_n&=n[1-\cos(2\pi/n)],\\ b_n&=1+(n-1)[1-\cos(3\pi/(2(n-1)))],\\ V_*&=6V_9+9V_5,\qquad B=\min\{b_9-V_9,\ b_5-V_5\}>\frac7{500}. \end{split} \tag{3} \]

    The strict rational lower bound is the existing cycle-face inequality. Its method applies here because the 99 old unit edges are partitioned into six C9 and nine C5 cycles. Convexity of 1-cos on the closed acute interval bounds their total phase cost below by \(V_*\).

    On a positive face of an n-cycle, one angle equals pi/2 and the remaining angles sum to 3pi/2, giving \(b_n\) by Jensen. A negative internal face instead has remaining mean 5pi/16 and a larger cost. A negative rim face is empty: one angle -pi/2 and four at most pi/2 cannot sum to 2*pi. These cases cover all 198 signed old-edge faces: 54 positive and 54 negative internal faces, 45 positive rim faces and 45 empty negative rim faces. No joint attainability of separate cycle minima is assumed.

    The weighted spoke potential and the full form storage are nonnegative. Consequently every actual boundary state satisfies

    \[ H_\eta\ge V_*+B. \tag{4} \]

    The old graph has two components. After fixing an origin in each, its closed acute cell is a bounded polytope of relative lifts. Restoring the relative host-donor origin on the circle makes its closure compact modulo common rotation. A fixed lifted mean does not require pretending that this relative origin is fixed.

    Entry and retention theorem. If the initial old phases belong to this cell and \(H_\eta(0)<V_*+B\), no future old edge reaches an acute face under (1). A first exit would contradict (2)--(4). The strict energy margin separates the retained phase sublevel from all old faces. All six internal and nine rim windings and all original mixed host periods therefore persist for every future time.

    For every fixed eta>0 the complete graph is connected. Thus the form part of \(H_\eta\) bounds all forms on the zero-mean leaf. Together with the compact circular phase description this makes the retained sublevel precompact. This is an all-future trapping argument for the full state; no carrier restriction, acute-spoke premise or phase reset is used.

    3. Positive contact admits stationary minima and a stable retained set¶

    Consider the reference phase circle

    \[ x=0,\qquad \theta_{c,j}=\psi_j+\frac{2\pi(c-2)}5\quad(c<5),\qquad \theta_{5,j}=\psi_j+\beta,\qquad \psi_j=\frac{2\pi(j-4)}9. \tag{5} \]

    It lies strictly inside the old cell for every circular beta. The fifth roots of unity sum to zero, so its unweighted spoke potential is exactly 45, independently of beta. Therefore

    \[ H_\eta\big|_{\text{reference circle}}=V_*+45\eta. \tag{6} \]

    For every

    \[ 0<\eta<B/45, \tag{7} \]

    the reference circle lies below the boundary barrier. Minimizing \(V_\eta\) on the compact closed old cell, including its relative-origin circle, then gives an interior minimum: its value is at most (6), whereas every boundary value is at least \(V_*+B\). At that minimum all full phase derivatives vanish and the full relative phase Hessian is positive semidefinite. With x=0 it is a stationary point of both rows in (1).

    This proves existence in the original complete marked class. It does not make the reference circle itself stationary when eta>0, select an origin, or prove a strictly positive Hessian. The minimum may be degenerate.

    Let \(\mathcal M_\eta\) be the compact set of phase minima just obtained, modulo common rotation. The equilibrium set with x=0 and phase in \(\mathcal M_\eta\) is Lyapunov stable in the relative circular phase space with mean-zero forms. Equivalently one may use its full periodic preimage on a fixed-mean lift leaf; a single compact choice of lifted representatives is not asserted to be invariant. Subtract its minimum storage value from \(H_\eta\). Within the trapping cell this difference is nonnegative and vanishes exactly on that set: connectedness makes its form contribution zero only at x=0. Compactness gives a positive storage separation from the complement of any neighborhood of the set inside a bounded retained sublevel. Continuity provides a smaller initial neighborhood below this separation, and (2) prevents exit. This is set stability, without an assumed isolated or strictly positive stationary point.

    There is a nonempty open set of initial full states below the barrier, since (6) is strict. Every such state admitted inside the cell retains the original identity. The actual acquired preparation requires its own inclusion, supplied in section 5.

    4. The weighted complete law still converges to one equilibrium¶

    The complete-contact convergence method must be re-admitted for (1). This section holds throughout \(0<\eta\le1\), independently of the narrower trapping condition (7). The latter is included because B<5 already follows from (3). Edgewise comparison gives

    \[ 0<L_\eta|_{\mathbf1^\perp},\qquad L_\eta\le L_1,\qquad \mu_\eta=\|L_\eta\|\le10,\qquad \ell_\eta=\lambda_{\min}(L_\eta|_{\mathbf1^\perp})>0. \tag{8} \]

    The positive lower bound is for each fixed eta. No uniform positive gap is asserted as eta approaches zero.

    Set \(k=\gamma^2/16\), \(U_\eta=kV_\eta\), \(g_\eta=\nabla U_\eta\), \(P_\eta=L_\eta^{-1}\) on the relative space, and \(v=\theta'=\gamma L_\eta x/4\). Differentiating the actual second row gives

    \[ P_\eta v'+\tfrac14v+g_\eta=0,\qquad E_\eta=\tfrac12v^TP_\eta v+U_\eta=kH_\eta,\qquad E_\eta'=-\tfrac14\|v\|^2. \tag{9} \]

    The Hessian of \(V_\eta\) is the actual weighted cosine Laplacian, between \(-L_\eta\) and \(L_\eta\) in Loewner order. The same proof function \(F_\eta=E_\eta+g_\eta^Tv/8\) therefore obeys, with all coefficients now consuming eta,

    \[ F_\eta' \le-\frac{17}{128}\|v\|^2-\frac1{16}g_\eta^TL_\eta g_\eta \le-\min\{17/128,\ell_\eta/16\} (\|v\|^2+\|g_\eta\|^2). \tag{10} \]

    These estimates use k<1/16 and (8). \(F_\eta\) is an analytic proof function, not a substituted storage or law.

    Bounded form energy, compact circular phase space and the unchanged autonomous loss identity put every omega-limit point at \(v=g_\eta=0\). The potential and \(F_\eta\) are real analytic. The finite-chart analytic gradient inequality and finite-length argument in the linked owner therefore apply with \(P_\eta\) and \(\ell_\eta\): every finite zero-form-mean initial state has one limiting equilibrium, x tends to zero, and the original continuous phase lifts have finite total variation and converge. This derives lift boundedness rather than assuming it. The graph metric \(P_\eta\) carries no identification with physical mass.

    In particular every state trapped by section 2 converges to an equilibrium strictly inside the same old acute cell, retaining all its marked information. The limit is not selected and need not belong to \(\mathcal M_\eta\); convergence by itself does not imply that the limiting Hessian is positive semidefinite. No convergence rate uniform in eta, finite settling deadline or statement at eta=0 follows.

    5. The original acquired and observed source enters the weak-contact trap¶

    Keep the complete outer acquisition, sequential observation word and independent donor preparation. The five host sources and donor retain all twelve original allowances in [0,1e-10], their separate zero-sum errors and all 96 relative directions. Every beta is supplied at donor primitive preparation. No reached coordinate, residual, clock, command or waiting interval is changed. Only the subsequently supplied spoke weight is eta.

    The retained acquisition inequalities give, before the observation word,

    \[ H_H(0)<5V_9+9V_5+\frac{11}{1000},\qquad \|x_H(0)\|_\infty\le X=\frac1{100}. \tag{11} \]

    Use the new analytic upper bound \(\bar\gamma=1/3000>\gamma\), not a re-evaluated archived interval endpoint. Over the original word of duration T=1/5000, put

    \[ \begin{split} B_o&=X+\bar\gamma T<\frac{101}{10000},\\ d_x&=(2B_o+\bar\gamma)T<\frac1{200000},\\ m&=2\bar\gamma B_oT<\frac1{500000000}. \end{split} \tag{12} \]

    The degree-four host heat contraction bounds every form by \(B_o\), including all fifteen supplied phase events. Forms never jump. The full form row then bounds each net form change by \(d_x\). The phase row bounds each continuous phase drift by m. The final commanded phase offset is exactly zero, while that drift and the form change remain.

    Each of the 90 host edges consequently has net form-gap change at most \(2d_x\) and phase-gap change at most 2m. Its initial and final form gaps have magnitude at most \(2B_o\). The square difference identity and the global Lipschitz constant one of 1-cos give the endpoint bound

    \[ H_H(T)-H_H(0) \le360B_od_x+180m<\frac1{50000}. \tag{13} \]

    This bound compares actual endpoints. It neither cancels the paid work of intermediate events nor treats a final inverse command as restoration of state. All earlier acquisition work, event work and continuous losses retain their original accounting.

    The recovered donor bounds give form and relative-phase norms at most q=1e-70. Its isolated twist has zero phase gradient and internal Laplacian norm below four, hence

    \[ H_D\le V_9+4q^2. \tag{14} \]

    The phases at contact also lie in the required old acute cell. This follows separately from the original source and observation-history inclusions. With zeta=1e-6, A=7/5 and \(\alpha=2\pi/9\), the final host rim gaps have magnitude at most \(A+2\zeta+2m<\pi/2\), and the host internal gaps lie in \([\alpha-2\zeta-2m,\alpha+2\zeta+2m]\subset(0,\pi/2)\). The donor internal gaps lie in \([\alpha-2q,\alpha+2q]\subset(0,\pi/2)\). The observation word already preserves the original branches and mixed periods. Energy alone is not used to infer this initial phase admission.
