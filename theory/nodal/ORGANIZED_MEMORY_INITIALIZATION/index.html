    Preparation information and collective prediction¶

    Fixed analytical comparison¶

    This comparison asks whether independent preparation or existing observations supply the information consumed by the compressed forecast. Distinguish the actual prepared image from its larger norm enclosure. The original recovery retained separate form and phase bounds before replacing both by q=1e-8 in later sufficient comparisons.

    Result: preparation admits what the loose observation class cannot¶

    Under declaration 6c16052d, the existing preparation admits five zero variance estimates with joint error below 8.72e-23, well within 1e-17. The same preparation fixes the collective means at attachment. Carrying a ten-coordinate reference from that origin through formation and observation gives a uniform mean-form error below 1.6e-24 and lifted-phase error below 1.2e-25. No observed fine phase or post-formation reset is needed.

    This recovers information discarded by the later common q=1e-8 enclosure. It changes no preparation tolerance, recovery dwell, dynamics or numerical precision. The five zero estimates make the compressed law the existing carrier law; this result does not resolve a nonzero hidden correction or require five measured internal statistics for the actual prepared family.

    In contrast, the existing noisy form records cannot uniformly identify the variances at the requested precision on the larger norm-containing class. That obstruction survives even when exact initial means are granted to the inverse. The two statements have different domains and are consistent.

    1. Retaining separate recovery information¶

    The joint-resource proof retains own-law entry into the full relative ball and the independent energy trap before recovery. At that entry, the actual class-one state has ||x||^2+||theta-Theta1||^2<r^2, r=1/12. This is the complete entry condition, not a consequence of phase trapping alone. All original primitive allowances, zero-sum conditions, formation, energy and storage hypotheses remain required. A source with only a claimed winding or a cached passing flag cannot use this premise.

    For eta=gamma^2, the shared pi bounds imply 9e-8<eta<1e-7. With g0=1/5, Lambda=2 and epsilon_L=1/20, the unchanged class-one curvature satisfies

    \[ c_1=\cos(2\pi/9+\sqrt2/12)>2/3. \tag{1} \]

    For example, pi<22/7, sqrt(2)<99/70 put its argument below 2057/2520, and cos(y)>=1-y^2/2 gives the strict lower bound. The modified-energy coefficients from the existing proof then obey

    \[ a_->1/400,\quad a_+<3/50,\quad \kappa>2\cdot10^{-9}, \qquad V_0<3/1000. \tag{2} \]

    The last inequality follows directly from the pointwise modified-energy upper bound and the admitted entry ball. Even bounding each of its two norms separately by r gives V0<[(3/2)*1e-7+3/10]/144<3/1000. No archived returned norm or energy estimate enters this calculation.

    The quarter-factor dwell is still D=8e10; both rows inherit the original half-time comparison. Thus kappa*D/2>80, and the unchanged upper bound 2^-80 on decay gives

    \[ \begin{aligned} \|x_{handoff}\|^2 &<\frac{2\cdot10^6}{3}\,2^{-80}<10^{-18},\\ \|\theta_{handoff}-\Theta_1\|^2 &<\frac{12}{5}\,2^{-80}<4\cdot10^{-24}. \end{aligned} \tag{3} \]

    Use the conservative radii q_x=1e-9, q_theta=2e-12. They describe the same uninterrupted reached state, not a smaller supplied preparation box. The sixth constituent in the historical resource comparison is not attached in the present five-child word; all five hosts must individually satisfy the original preparation hypotheses.

    2. One reference from attachment through observation¶

    Let tau=0 denote outer attachment, after the isolated recoveries. Each child retains its separate zero form and target-phase residual means up to this event. The supplied translations therefore give exactly

    \[ U_c(0)=M_c,\quad \Phi_c(0)=0,\qquad M_c=4092(c-2)\quad\hbox{or}\quad M_c=0. \tag{4} \]

    The branch is part of the supplied preparation. No hidden residual is set to zero. Define a mathematical carrier reference by these same means, unforced evolution for 200 units, and then the unchanged finite input word:

    \[ \overline U'=(-L_5\overline U+\gamma S_5(\overline\Phi))/4+f, \qquad \overline\Phi'=\gamma L_5\overline U/4. \tag{5} \]

    Here f is zero during formation and is exactly the original prescribed waveform during observation. The reference continues across the boundary; its post-formation means are not reset to the actual means or reconstructed from later readings.

    For full differences from this carrier, five independent recovered children give initial Euclidean radii r0<sqrt(5)*q_x<3e-9 and z0<sqrt(5)*q_theta<6e-12. The global sine Lipschitz bound and the outer heat identity hold under the identical input: its contribution cancels from the difference rows. They allow the original nonacute outer transit. For H=216, 2g^2 H<1/2 and exp(1/2)<2, so the shared heat-error kernel gives

    \[ Z=2(z_0+g r_0)=1.4\cdot10^{-11},\qquad R=r_0+24gZ, \quad \|\theta-\overline\theta\|\le Z,\quad \|x-\overline x\|\le R. \tag{6} \]

    The radii on the right use the conservative bounds r0=3e-9, z0=6e-12. The form convolution uses the same global zero-mean difference and spectral gap lambda2(L_f)>1/3. This is a conditional nonlinear transport theorem; the kernel neither assesses a source nor evaluates an exponential trajectory.

    Orthogonal removal of child means is contractive. Consequently the actual hidden phase z also has norm at most Z, without a factor-two Euclidean inflation. In particular, at the observation start

    \[ \sum_e m_e^0=\frac19\|D_o z\|^2 \le\frac49 Z^2<8.72\cdot10^{-23}<10^{-17}. \tag{7} \]

    Choosing all five estimates zero therefore satisfies the compressed initialization target on every source member covered by the preparation. Their actual values need not be zero. The old collision at a=2^-26 lies outside this sharpened prepared enclosure, although it remains within the old larger inference class.

    3. Mean prediction with carried initialization¶

    The full form radius R is unnecessarily loose for predicting means. The initial collective difference in (4) is exactly zero. The actual projected row differs from (5) by the quadratic sine remainder, with ||R_mean||2<=rho=gZ^2/9 throughout the combined history. Heat contraction and the live phase row therefore give, with k=gH=9/125<1,

    \[ E_U\le H\rho+k E_\Phi,\qquad E_\Phi\le k E_U, \qquad E_U\le\frac{H\rho}{1-k^2}<1.6\cdot10^{-24},\qquad E_\Phi\le\frac{kH\rho}{1-k^2}<1.2\cdot10^{-25}. \tag{8} \]

    The bound is uniform on 0<=tau<=200+15.00015<216, not merely a fitted endpoint estimate. It includes uncertainty in all admitted fine preparation directions. It meets the declaration's 1.5e-20 intrinsic target without five measured statistics or separately known means at tau=200. No extra gain is asserted from comparing the carrier with itself using the same priors; the tighter prediction follows from retaining stronger source information.

    The actual formation and complete input word retain their original identity proofs; formation is allowed to acquire outer winding. The small observation phase difference also transfers the existing strict mean-gap margin to the reference. This does not reconstruct fine-node identities from five phases. The actual external power remains 9(L5 U)^T f; the predicted work differs by at most 36 E_U integral ||f||1. Absolute and positive parts refer to the total external power, not separate fine-node channels. Preparation and attachment work, hidden loss and fine storage retain their original owners.

    Equation (8) is an exact-solution comparison. A numerical approximation adds its own propagated residual; an empirical forecast also needs an independently justified preparation, clock, input and observation bridge. Neither budget is included in the small intrinsic error above.

    4. Why the noisy records alone do not supply the variances¶

    For the observation-only route retain the common coarse bounds a0=31/10^9, b0=601/10^10 and the original larger inference class. The two declared states have equal initial collective coordinates and norm certificates. The first lies on the exact carrier. The second has the proved variance difference

    \[ \|m^{(1)}-m^{(0)}\|_1=4a^2/9>2\cdot10^{-17}, \qquad a=2^{-26}. \tag{9} \]

    Both solutions exist under the same complete finite input word and retain the original class's identities. The shared carrier bound, freshly rebuilt from these coarse primitives on T=16, gives

    \[ \sup_\tau\|U^{(1)}(\tau)-U^{(0)}(\tau)\|_2 \le E_0<2.3\cdot10^{-18}<\eta_{raw}=10^{-14}. \tag{10} \]

    For every one of the 150 samples, define a symbolic record equal to the first solution's true mean. Its noise is zero for that solution; for the second solution its noise is their coordinate difference, uniformly smaller than eta_raw. These two error vectors are admitted simultaneously with their respective complete histories. Thus the joint sets of possible records overlap. This constructs existence without evaluating or inventing an acquired response and does not rely on marginal interval compatibility.

    Any single-valued estimator of m receives the same record and even the same gifted exact initial means in both cases. The triangle inequality and (9) exclude error at most 1e-17 for both. The original noise permits arbitrary correlations, so treating samples as independent noise or averaging them does not repair this worst-case obstruction. Additional sample times in the same bounded history retain (10). Noiseless data, different observations, different inputs or a more restrictive preparation require their own proof.

    This excludes a uniform observation-only inverse on the containing class, not initialization of the actual prepared image. It also leaves the existing winding inverse intact: both states here have the same winding.

    5. Consolidation and remaining execution boundary¶

    Fine states with the same initial means and the coarse hidden bounds are each within E0 of one common carrier history. Their mean-output diameter is therefore at most 2E0, much smaller than the raw reading allowance, even when their variance statistics cannot be identified at the requested precision. This is a finite output cover, not an exact state quotient or a transitive equivalence relation inferred from noisy-record overlap.

    The prepared route is stronger: it supplies accurate initialization and prediction from the original history. The observation-only route identifies a genuine information limit for a larger class. Retaining both avoids treating a discarded source bound as a fundamental dynamical obstruction. The conditional compression theorem and its general counterexamples remain valid; the five-statistic representation is useful only when a selected source and prediction actually require that information.

    The static controls reuse the modified-energy, heat-error and exact-arithmetic owners. They check the recovery constants, anisotropic transport, mean initialization, joint error inequalities and record-overlap obstruction without source replay, trajectories or frozen-response evaluation. The separate computable forecast uses this initialization with shared validated integration and preserves source, numerical and observation errors separately. The sole queue owns the next observation-to-prediction question.
