    1. Fine law, normalization and attachment storage¶

    Let \(H\) denote the original 36-node host, \(D\) the donor C9, and \(B\) the nine new edges. Each internal cycle has nine unit edges; the four original matching child interfaces contribute 36 more, and the donor interface contributes nine. Thus the enlarged support has 45 nodes and 90 edges. Its degrees are those in the declaration, rather than a common degree four. With \(e=1023/1024\), its complete rows are

    \[ \begin{split} S_i(\theta)&=\sum_{k\sim i}\sin(\theta_k-\theta_i),\\ x_\tau&=\tfrac14[-Lx+\gamma S(\theta)],\qquad \theta_\tau=\tfrac\gamma4Lx,\qquad \tau=e t . \end{split}\tag{1} \]

    The uniform mobility \(1/4\) is a supplied coefficient. It is not the reciprocal of each new degree, a changed capacity, or an inference from the nodal identity. Both original-time rows are \(e\) times (1). For unit capacity the independently declared pressure is the form row in the selected clock, with the corresponding structural units. There are no continuous external inputs after the supplied attachment. These equations are the detached comparison law, not the native resultant-direction pressure or an operator word.

    Use continuous phase lifts, retaining the original closing-edge branch on each child when reading its winding. Sine and cosine are periodic, so the vector field itself is independent of those branch representatives. It is globally Lipschitz on the finite-dimensional lifted state: the linear form rows and globally bounded sine derivatives give a unique complete solution for every finite initial state. This is existence of the specified law, not an assertion of future identity.

    The actual storage and its derivative are

    \[ \begin{split} H_{45}(x,\theta)&=\tfrac12x^TLx+ \sum_{\{i,k\}}[1-\cos(\theta_k-\theta_i)],\\ (H_{45})_\tau&=-\tfrac14\|Lx\|_2^2,\qquad (\mathbf1^Tx)_\tau=(\mathbf1^T\theta)_\tau=0 . \end{split}\tag{2} \]

    Indeed \(\nabla_xH_{45}=Lx\) and \(\nabla_\theta H_{45}=-S\); substitution of both rows cancels the reciprocal products. Symmetry and oddness cancel every edge in the two sums. The phase sum refers to the declared continuous lifts, not a circular mean or phase-resultant angle.

    For each new edge put

    \[ u_j=x_{36+j}-x_j,\qquad \delta_j=\theta_{36+j}-\theta_j,\qquad H_B=\sum_{j=0}^8[\tfrac12u_j^2+1-\cos\delta_j]. \tag{3} \]

    The attachment changes no state coordinate. Consequently \(H_{45}^+-H^- = H_B\), where \(H^-\) includes both the actual host and donor internal storage. Each edge is counted once. In particular, the contact form storage depends on a difference of two existing forms; no independent \(r^2/2\) storage has been added. Positive \(H_B\) requires a supplied structural-work source. Past dissipation is not a declared reserve, and (3) supplies neither funding nor event occurrence.

    2. Actual boundary channels, reaction and regional balance¶

    Let \(L_H,S_H\) contain only the original host edges and \(L_D,S_D\) only the donor cycle. A selector \(E\) inserts a nine-vector at the host slots \(j=0,\ldots,8\). Define the two vector channels

    \[ f_j=\frac{u_j+\gamma\sin\delta_j}{4},\qquad g_j=-\frac{\gamma u_j}{4}. \tag{4} \]

    The full law splits exactly as

    \[ \begin{array}{ll} (x_H)_\tau=(-L_Hx_H+\gamma S_H)/4+Ef, &(\theta_H)_\tau=\gamma L_Hx_H/4+Eg,\\ (x_D)_\tau=(-L_Dx_D+\gamma S_D)/4-f, &(\theta_D)_\tau=\gamma L_Dx_D/4-g . \end{array}\tag{5} \]

    The source reacts in both channels, with the opposite currents under this common mobility. Its boundary values are evolving fine state; they are not a prescribed waveform. The host's two charge rates are \(\sum_j f_j\) and \(\sum_j g_j\), while the donor rates are their negatives. A zero form gap removes the instantaneous phase channel, but it does not remove the dependence of the form channel on phase.

    To make the regional work explicit, write \(a_H=L_Hx_H\), \(a_D=L_Dx_D\), \(b_H=-S_H\), \(b_D=-S_D\), and let \(H_H,H_D\) denote the respective internal storages. Direct differentiation gives

    \[ \begin{split} (H_H)_\tau&=-\tfrac14\|a_H\|_2^2+ (E^Ta_H)^Tf+(E^Tb_H)^Tg,\\ (H_D)_\tau&=-\tfrac14\|a_D\|_2^2-a_D^Tf-b_D^Tg,\\ (H_B)_\tau&=\sum_j[u_j(u_j)_\tau+ \sin\delta_j(\delta_j)_\tau],\\ (H_H+H_D+H_B)_\tau&=-\tfrac14\|Lx\|_2^2 . \end{split}\tag{6} \]

    These supplies depend on internal form pressures and phase gradients. They are not form times form input alone. The sum of the two isolated losses is generally not the complete loss; differentiating the contact storage restores precisely (2). This is the same component-balance method as the earlier causal composition, with the present support, mobility and nine actual channels re-derived.

    3. Exact ten-coordinate restriction¶

    Let \(c=0,1,2,3\) label the original children and \(c=4\) the donor. The interchild edge set and its unweighted Laplacian are

    \[ \begin{split} \mathcal E_C&=\{\{0,1\},\{2,3\},\{0,2\},\{1,3\},\{0,4\}\},\\ K&=\begin{pmatrix} 3&-1&-1&0&-1\\ -1&2&0&-1&0\\ -1&0&2&-1&0\\ 0&-1&-1&2&0\\ -1&0&0&0&1 \end{pmatrix}. \end{split}\tag{7} \]

    On the declared family \(x_{c,j}=X_c\), \(\theta_{c,j}=\psi_j+\Phi_c\), the two internal cycle sine currents cancel and internal form currents vanish. Every matching edge has the same form and phase gaps at every slot. Both fine rows are therefore constant within each child. Smooth uniqueness proves invariance, and substitution gives all ten rows:

    \[ q=KX,\qquad s_c(\Phi)=\sum_{b:\{c,b\}\in\mathcal E_C}\sin(\Phi_b-\Phi_c), \qquad (X_c)_\tau=(-q_c+\gamma s_c)/4,\quad (\Phi_c)_\tau=\gamma q_c/4 . \tag{8} \]

    Writing \(R=X_4\), \(\Psi=\Phi_4\), \(u=R-X_0\), \(\delta=\Psi-\Phi_0\), the donor and its increments to the original host child-zero rows are in particular

    \[ \begin{array}{ll} F=(u+\gamma\sin\delta)/4,&G=-\gamma u/4,\\ R_\tau=-F,&\Psi_\tau=-G=\gamma u/4. \end{array}\tag{9} \]

    Thus the constituent supplies a closed two-coordinate reaction on this family. The ten coordinates include both the donor and the host; the port has not counted the source again as an independent force.
