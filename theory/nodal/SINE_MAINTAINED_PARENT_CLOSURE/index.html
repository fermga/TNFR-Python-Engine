    Maintained-parent SHA: grammatical closure and the changed complete law¶

    The committed UM successor provides a concrete VAL,THOL,UM prefix with genuine parent history and a connected child. This study admits one parent-target SHA closure, accounting for its capacity reset, latency metadata and complete subsequent law. The research program owns task status.

    SHA is a deliberately simple control for isolating capacity effects. The canonical grammar admits other closure roles, including OZ, NAV and REMESH. A shared role does not make their effects equivalent or allow automatic substitution at this prefix: the whole word, live preconditions, reset, history and successor law require their own admission. No unique, preferred or universal closing operator is derived.

    1. Source, identity and execution boundary¶

    The source reader verifies the retained UM transaction, its original 25/27 assessment and the separate 27/27 review. It re-admits the actual THOL source and rebuilds the UM prediction and endpoint checks from primitive records. Archive receipts associate bytes; they do not independently authenticate acquisition history. A mutable live endpoint must still match freshly re-admitted primitives. The reader executes no event, pressure refresh or trajectory.

    Retain all 21 nodes, the 53 undirected positive-conductance edges, node and neighbor order, signed form x, primitive circular phase with its declared real lifts, positive capacities nu and the structural clock. Parent 2 has actual prefix VAL,THOL,UM, zero U2 debt and its acquired two-sample history. The child 2_sub_0 retains its identity, parent association and missing temporal baseline. SHA targets only parent 2. Membership in the new support does not make the child an additional word target or give it a copied history.

    The word continues existing form under the core initial_epi_nonzero=True context; this is not an initialization-from-empty claim. All strict live gates remain enabled, with grammar rejection by raising. No IL intervention or alternative closure is inserted into the reserved composition.

    The instantaneous storage is

    \[ H(x,\theta;W)=\frac12\sum_{\{i,j\}}W_{ij}(x_i-x_j)^2 +\sum_{\{i,j\}}[1-\cos(\theta_j-\theta_i)]. \]

    Before and after the event, the chosen complete segment law is

    \[ p_i=\frac{S_i(\theta)}{\pi d_i},\quad S_i=\sum_{j\sim i}\sin(\theta_j-\theta_i),\quad \dot x=MS(\theta),\quad \dot\theta=ML_Wx,\quad M=\operatorname{diag}\frac{\nu_i}{\pi d_i}. \]

    Each segment holds its own capacities, support and conductances fixed, with zero forcing and loss. This detached weighted two-row model differs from native relational execution and the general EPI-only event runtime. The shared atomic stage executes SHA; detached field admission supplies the complete continuation contract, without advancing either continuous row.

    2. Default reset and exact arithmetic¶

    The prediction owner resolves the actual runtime factor through the shared factor registry. The source has no SHA override. Its represented default is

    \[ f=\frac{518151819521135}{562949953421312} \approx0.9204225284540524. \]

    This is the implementation's represented default associated with 1-1/(4*pi), not an assertion of exact equality to that transcendental expression. The factor 1/2 used in an earlier contact study does not transfer. For parent capacity nu_2^-, distinguish the ideal exact product and the binary64 product admitted by the shared scalar kernel:

    \[ \widehat\nu_2^+=f\nu_2^-,\qquad \nu_2^+=\operatorname{fl}(f\nu_2^-),\qquad r=\frac{\nu_2^+}{\nu_2^-}. \]

    On this source the represented product changes approximately 117.87376674577816 to 108.49367042655233. Its absolute error relative to the exact product is approximately 1.2901876873056387e-15. All other capacities are unchanged. The represented ratio r, not an unrounded f, governs identities for the represented successor state.

    SHA leaves form, phase, support and conductances untouched. Consequently H^+-H^-=0 exactly from identical storage arguments. Subtracting separately rounded storage enclosures would not establish this identity. Stored pressure also remains unchanged during SHA. In this particular sine law, the independently specified fresh pressure depends on phase and bare support, so its constitutive value is unchanged as well. General pressure mixtures that consume capacity do not inherit that last conclusion.

    The public SHA lifecycle writes its declared latency snapshot, including preserved form and a wall-clock timestamp. This timestamp does not advance structural time; latent=True installs no continuous stopping rule.

    3. Changed mobility, means and continuation¶

    With positive represented capacity, only the parent coefficient changes:

    \[ M_2^+=rM_2^-,\qquad \rho_2^+=\rho_2^-/r, \qquad \rho_i=\frac{d_i}{\nu_i}. \]

    At the event's common x and theta, both parent evolution rows multiply by r. All other instantaneous rows remain unchanged. On this source the predicted parent form rate changes approximately 0.01647756447 to 0.01516632156, and phase rate 0.3963025696 to 0.3647658132. Both remain nonzero. The same instantaneous neighbor rates do not imply unchanged future motion, because later phase and form evolve through the altered coupled field.

    For either retained coordinate vector y, let m^-=sum(rho_i^- y_i)/R, R=sum(rho_i^-) and a=rho_2^-(1/r-1). Then the reset changes the corresponding mean by

    \[ m^+=m^-+\frac{a}{R+a}(y_2-m^-). \]

    This is reweighting with unchanged coordinates, not an unrecorded form or phase jump. The phase mean uses the declared real lifts, including the unchanged negative lifts at nodes 10 and 11. Each mean is conserved on its own positive-capacity held-law segment; a common value across the event is not generally conserved. H remains the same storage functional, with the usual cancellation between both complete rows on each segment.

    The general scalar SHA contract allows intentional zero attenuation or zero source capacity, while rejecting a positive product lost to represented zero. That boundary is outside this selected positive-capacity continuation. If the stated reciprocal two-row law is explicitly extended to nu_2=0, both parent rows vanish because M_2=0, and the H cancellation still holds. However, rho_2=d_2/nu_2 and the positive-weight mean theorem are unavailable. The nodal identity alone would freeze only the unforced form row, not every coordinate or boundary flux of the rest of the network.

    The new parent capacity lies strictly outside the narrow parameter family of the UM half-unit certificate. Parent-only attenuation is not a common clock rescaling. Equal instantaneous geometry, unchanged H and grammatical closure therefore do not transfer that certificate or its turning time to the post-SHA law. Fresh law-specific retention needs its own proof; an additional trajectory is not automatically required by admission alone.
