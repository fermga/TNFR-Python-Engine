    Canonical contact between maintained weighted stars¶

    This study admits an actual registered action on the state-complete weighted stars. The earlier unit-bridge comparison is a control, not an operator output specification. This is an analytical admission with declared current-engine static controls, not an acquired-source response, autonomous event selection or continuous trajectory campaign.

    Canonical here identifies the registered preparation and its admitted grammar. The held weighted sine continuation is a separately declared complete model; executing the word does not derive that continuation uniquely. Its full real-lift tangent is a comparison instrument with its own domain and storage. The law-admission owner separates execution conformance, constitutive justification and linearization.

    1. Prospective source, word and discriminators¶

    • For each block, forms are (m+a,m+a,m+a,m+a,m) with a=1/16, and capacities are (4,2,2,2,8).     2. Word admission and complete primary reset¶

    Write b=1/1024 and c=a-b=63/1024. The following algebra first describes the ideal-real maps on the source family. The frozen represented probes retain their separate arithmetic checks in Section 7.

    AL admits the selected source form m<1 and capacity 8>1/8. Its only primary coordinate change is

    \[ x_4:m\longmapsto m+b. \]

    The form rail is inactive. UM then reads the four existing neighbors 0,1,2,3, whose phases and the target phase are all zero. Their consensus and all bidirectional phase proposals are zero. No right-node phase contributes to this reset: those nodes are new-link candidates, not existing alignment neighbors. Thus every phase is unchanged by the complete word.

    The target form after AL exceeds 1/8, its capacity exceeds 1/8 and its four existing neighbors satisfy U3 exactly. Their capacity mean is (4+2+2+2)/4=5/2. The configured synchronization and silence maps therefore give

    \[ \nu_4^{\mathrm{UM}} =8+\tfrac18(\tfrac52-8)=\tfrac{117}{16},\qquad \nu_4^+=\tfrac12\nu_4^{\mathrm{UM}}=\tfrac{117}{32}. \]

    All other capacities are unchanged. Stored pressure starts at zero; UM's multiplier is 1-1/8=7/8, so its target stored-pressure write is also zero. AL and SHA do not supply a nonzero stored-pressure change. SHA sees capacity 117/16>1/8 and retains the post-AL form.

    The ordered core word is AL,UM,SHA: AL supplies the opening, UM retains its independent live U3 obligation, and SHA is this word's admitted closure. Only node 4 is a target. Its resulting glyph history is (AL,UM,SHA) and its operator step count is three; old neighbors receiving phase writes do not thereby execute those glyphs. The complete stage implementation also owns activation, latency, preserved-form and context metadata. Those records are checked through the shared lifecycle, not inferred from the primary reset equations. Structural time remains 7; the three events are not three elapsed units of the subsequent continuous law.

    The execution boundary is stage-wise. A rejected UM must leave its own post-AL input snapshot unchanged, but it does not undo the already committed AL. Neither a valid word nor its successful primary reset implies whole-word rollback or autonomous occurrence.

    3. Actual affinity, candidate inventory and five-edge contact¶

    The live phase-affinity owner uses 1-|wrap(theta_j-theta_i)|/pi, not cosine. Its score is a configured operator policy, distinct from the sine dynamics and cosine storage. With equal Si=1/2 and positive forms, the ideal-real functional-link formula reduces to

    \[ W_{4j} =1-\frac{\alpha}{2\pi} -\frac{|x_4-x_j|}{4(x_4+x_j+\epsilon_E)}, \qquad \epsilon_E=\operatorname{float}(10^{-9})>0 . \]

    The regularizer is the represented implementation constant. Runtime evaluation normalizes the two forms before the subtractions and divisions; this real formula does not prescribe a different binary64 operation order.

    The target's only nonneighbors are the five right nodes. All have phase gap alpha<=2/5<pi/2, so both the candidate and final phase gates admit them. With the full candidate inventory, exactly five new edges are proposed. Their two ideal weight classes are

    \[ \begin{aligned} W_A&=1-\frac{\alpha}{2\pi} -\frac{c}{4(2m+a+b+\epsilon_E)} &&\text{on }(4,5),(4,6),(4,7),(4,8),\\ W_B&=1-\frac{\alpha}{2\pi} -\frac{b}{4(2m+b+\epsilon_E)} &&\text{on }(4,9). \end{aligned} \]

    Uniformly on the declared ideal family, every new form difference is strictly less than a=1/16, and its positive endpoint sum exceeds 2m>=15/32. Using pi>3 gives

    \[ \frac{\alpha}{2\pi}<\frac1{15},\qquad \frac{|x_4-x_j|}{4(x_4+x_j+\epsilon_E)}<\frac1{30}, \qquad \frac9{10}<W_A,W_B<1 . \]

    Both weight classes therefore exceed the declared 3/4 threshold with a strict margin. No edge is replaced by a unit weight; all original edge attributes remain associated with their old support.

    The actual successor has nineteen edges and ordered bare degrees and capacities

    \[ \begin{gathered} d=(4,2,2,2,9,\ 5,3,3,3,5),\\ \nu=(4,2,2,2,117/32,\ 4,2,2,2,8). \end{gathered} \]

    Its mobilities nu_i/d_i are

    \[ (1,1,1,1,13/32,\ 4/5,2/3,2/3,2/3,8/5). \]

    This is neither the unit-bridge model nor a copy of either isolated star law. The positive held capacities are the actual post-SHA values. The right center and leaves, in particular, no longer have equal mobility.

    4. Complete event work and a uniform joint storage margin¶

    For the actual weighted support use

    \[ H=\tfrac12\sum_{\{i,j\}}W_{ij}(x_i-x_j)^2 +\sum_{\{i,j\}}[1-\cos(\theta_j-\theta_i)]. \]

    Before the word the two isolated star storages are 4a^2 each, so H^-=8a^2=1/32. AL changes the four old left boundary-edge form differences from a to c. Its complete old-edge storage jump is

    \[ \Delta H_{\mathrm{AL}} =4(c^2-a^2)=-8ab+4b^2=-\frac{127}{262144}. \]

    A positive form increment need not increase this storage: here it reduces the target's contrast with its four old neighbors. This is an accounted hybrid jump, not continuous dissipation.

    UM leaves every phase and form unchanged on its old support, so its old-edge coordinate work is zero. Its capacity reset adds no storage term. The five actual new edges supply the entire remaining jump:

    \[ \Delta H_{\mathrm{UM}} =2W_Ac^2+\tfrac12W_Bb^2+5(1-\cos\alpha)>0. \]

    SHA changes only capacity and associated lifecycle state, hence Delta H_SHA=0. The exact ideal endpoint storage is consequently

    \[ \boxed{\qquad H^+=4c^2+4a^2+2W_Ac^2+\tfrac12W_Bb^2 +5(1-\cos\alpha). \qquad} \]

    The actual represented endpoint must use its actual forms, phases and weights in this sum, rather than an ideal weight substituted after execution. The following generous ideal bound uses only positivity, W_A,W_B<=1, b,c<a and 1-cos alpha<=alpha^2/2:

    \[ \begin{aligned} H^+ &<8a^2+\tfrac52a^2+\tfrac52(2/5)^2\\ &=\frac1{32}+\frac5{512}+\frac25 =\frac{1129}{2560} <\frac9{20}<\frac{35}{64}. \end{aligned} \]

    Thus 9/20 is a convenient prospective upper target, not an estimated response. It leaves margin 31/320 to the identity barrier. The admitted ideal domain has a nonempty open interior in the two source parameters m,alpha. It does not assert robustness to independent perturbations of all twenty coordinates through the runtime word.
