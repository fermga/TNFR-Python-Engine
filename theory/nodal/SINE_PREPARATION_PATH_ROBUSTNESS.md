## What this robustness establishes and leaves free

The captured identity agrees throughout this already declared law family
on the entire original uncertain source corridor. This particular
formation observation consequently does not discriminate \(\lambda\).
The laws still have different currents, transient histories and higher
nonlinear fields. Their
[nominal distinct-neighbor interaction onset](ORGANIZED_PATTERN_CONSTITUTIVE_FREEDOM.md#organized-pattern-mixed-onset)

its own joined support, nominal preparation, supplied inputs and local
time scope; acquisition here does not install its contact or replace
reached states with nominal targets.

The result is conditional robustness over a bounded class of complete laws.
It establishes neither one attractor shared by all TNFR dynamics, absence
of fixed natural laws, unique constitutive selection nor physical identity.
The earlier 57-state sine critical census is neither transferred to
\(\lambda>0\) nor needed here: convergence follows from this law's own
acute Hessian, loss and means after full-storage trapping.
Other source-path intervals remain unresolved. Capture at (26) means a
protected acute identity cell; it does not assert the older \(1/12\)
radius, a specified recovery precision, contact readiness or a closed
collective response at that deadline.
