    State-guard sensitivity of the organized word¶

    Conditional result. A mandatory first downward crossing of a supplied energy level gives a locally unique occurrence of Reception (EN), Coherence (IL) and Silence (SHA) near the existing organized-source witness. Its response derivative contains the change in event time as well as the reset derivative. The correction is nonzero in a fixed internal observation, even after both compared words have completed at a common structural time.

    Under one fixed guard, equal child means, energy and instantaneous loss can still give different next occurrence times. The predictive-state obstruction identifies fine information omitted by that instantaneous summary.

    This owner extends the complete flow/word comparison. The new statement is a local hybrid sensitivity law, not another generic argument that policies are supplied. Existing support-law countermodels and transverse admission crossings retain their separate models. Neither a guard's mathematical regularity nor its implementation would derive the choice of threshold from TNFR.

    1. Complete model and mandatory occurrence¶

    Retain the organized source's 45 labeled nodes, ninety unit edges, marked targets (0,1), raw form, primitive phase lifts and structural clock tau. The support and targets are supplied. Initially all capacities are one; after the word they are kappa at the targets and one elsewhere. Let L be the actual fine Laplacian, q=Lx, gamma=(1023*pi)^-1 and

    \[ S_i(\Theta)=\sum_{j\sim i}\sin(\Theta_j-\Theta_i),\qquad F_D(x,\Theta)=\frac14 \begin{pmatrix}D[-q+\gamma S(\Theta)]\\ \gamma Dq\end{pmatrix}. \]

    Both rows use the same positive held capacity matrix D. Pressure is the independent formula p=(-q+gamma*S)/4, refreshed before admission and continuation. There is no external forcing. The actual excess is

    \[ E(x,\Theta)=\tfrac12 x^T Lx+ \sum_{\{i,j\}}[1-\cos(\Theta_j-\Theta_i)]-H_*, \qquad H_*=45[1-\cos(2\pi/9)]. \]

    Use the section 23 ideal domain, including its acute lift branch, E<1/100, initial abs(m(1))<1/30, shared represented factors and all execution-context premises. These include the common organized-source kind, initial AL, IL history, ordinal two, zero debt, prior Coherence, initialized core grammar, raise, hard unit rails, explicit IL event_domain policy, actual Reception ceilings and Silence floor. Retain the actual context updates and weighted means, with carrier subtraction for phase origins. No custom monitor or additional callback mutation is present.

    For a fixed constant ell, supply the following occurrence policy:

    1. With both target ordinals two, flow while E>ell. At the first downward crossing E=ell, Reception is mandatory; continued pre-word flow through that crossing is not another permitted choice.
    2. After successful Reception both target ordinals are three. Immediately apply Coherence, then Silence at ordinal four. Each two-target stage reads its own snapshot and is separately atomic. There is no intervening flow and no claim of a single rollback transaction.
    3. At ordinal five disable further word requests and continue with the changed held capacities. No second word or renewal mechanism is present.

    The monotonic ordinal is existing retained context, owned by read_grammar_state and current_operator_step; it is not the length of a bounded trace. This policy specifies how that context is consumed; recording an ordinal alone would neither execute nor authenticate an event. Urgency and the ordered requests are additional model premises. Starting on or below the guard, mismatched ordinals and failed live admission lie outside the source domain proved below.

    The rule reads the complete network state. No local sensor, unmarked-target selection or default native-selector realization is established. It is autonomous on the mathematical and grammar state because it does not read absolute time. Silence's wall-clock timestamp remains execution telemetry. The threshold, target marks and request policy are supplied law parameters, not an unrecorded clock or an additional hidden latch. In particular ell is not recomputed from each perturbed initial state's energy.

    2. Local positive waiting and unique crossings¶

    Write z=(x,Theta) on the fixed-capacity source slice. The energy differential and its pre-word rate are

    \[ n=DE=(q^T,-S^T),\qquad nF_1=-\tfrac14 q^Tq=-\rho. \]

    The phase terms cancel because both complete rows are retained. Nonincrease alone does not imply rho>0; transversality is a separate local hypothesis.

    Use the exact prior witness x_*=h(e_0-e_1), h=1/64, and Theta_*=psi, where psi_(9c+j)=2*pi*(j-4)/9. Its values are

    \[ E_*=6h^2=3/2048,\qquad \|Lx_*\|^2=80h^2, \qquad\rho_*=20h^2=5/1024>0. \]

    The previous radius-10^-5 box is already inside the ideal domain. In that box the degree-five target rows give abs(q_i-q_*i)<10*epsilon for i=0,1, hence

    \[ \rho\ge\tfrac12(6h-10\varepsilon)^2>0, \qquad\varepsilon=10^{-5}. \]

    This estimate applies to independent phase perturbations as well. Smoothness gives a short pre-word flow segment remaining inside the box. Its energy decreases strictly, so every sufficiently close level below E_* is reached once on that segment, at a positive time. The first crossing is unique; the prescribed urgent stages and subsequent smooth flow then give a unique local hybrid solution for each specified level and admitted initial state. There are only three zero-duration stages and no recurrent event sequence.

    For a crossing state y=Phi^-_tau(s), define G(s,ell,tau)=E(Phi^-_tau(s))-ell. Since G_tau=-rho(y)<0, the implicit-function theorem gives a smooth crossing time in a neighborhood of each such source and level. For each fixed sufficiently close level the initial neighborhood can be chosen open in all ninety continuous coordinates, with E(initial)>ell, the same context and a positive waiting time. This does not claim all section 23 levels or preparations cross transversely.

    3. The derivative at a common structural time¶

    Let W be the complete primary word and let F_+=F_D use its actual Silence successor capacities. On the fixed discrete-context domain define

    \[ K(y)=DW(y)F_1(y)-F_+(W(y)). \]

    The earlier complete formula retains Coherence's nonidentity phase derivative. At a fixed common time T strictly after the crossing, set

    \[ Z(T;s,\ell)=\Phi^+_{T-\tau(s,\ell)} W\Phi^-_{\tau(s,\ell)}(s), \quad M_-=D\Phi^-_\tau(s),\quad M_+=D\Phi^+_{T-\tau}(W(y)). \]

    All quantities below are evaluated at this crossing. Differentiating G gives

    \[ \delta\tau=\frac{nM_-\delta s-\delta\ell}{\rho}. \]

    Differentiating the full endpoint, including the changed duration of the post-word flow, then gives

    \[ \begin{split} \delta Z&=M_+\{DW M_-\delta s+K\delta\tau\}\\ &=M_+\left\{\left(DW+\frac{K\otimes n}{\rho}\right) M_-\delta s-\frac K\rho\delta\ell\right\}. \end{split} \]

    Thus the event-local derivative at matched clocks, often called the saltation matrix, and the threshold sensitivity are

    \[ \mathcal S=DW+K\otimes n/\rho, \qquad\partial_\ell Z=-M_+K/\rho. \]

    Using only DW would omit the rank-one timing correction. Its sign has an independent consistency check:

    \[ \mathcal S F_1=DW F_1+K(nF_1)/\rho=F_+\circ W. \]

    A perturbation along the incoming flow must follow the outgoing flow after matching clocks. Both phase and form participate in this identity. Under a common constant clock rescaling, K and rho scale by the same factor, so K/rho and the timing correction retain their coordinate meaning; the crossing-time covector itself transforms with the clock unit.

    These derivatives concern the full ninety-coordinate primary state on the declared fixed initial-capacity and fixed-factor domain. Successor capacity is the same D for every compared level and contributes no threshold derivative. The observation clock T is held fixed. Derived fresh pressure has variation delta p=(-L*delta x+gamma*DS*delta Theta)/4. Record/lifecycle updates remain their separate maps. Neither weighted means nor primary coordinates are projected away to obtain this derivative.

    4. An exact internal correction and two fixed levels¶

    At the carrier, n=(q^T,0) although the incoming phase row is nonzero. For the fixed observation

    \[ \mathcal O(z)=\bigl(x_8-x_2, (\Theta_8-\psi_8)-(\Theta_2-\psi_2)\bigr), \]

    the previous exact result gives D O K_*=(3*beta*h/5)*(1,-gamma). Consequently the event-local threshold coefficient is

    \[ -D\mathcal O K_*/\rho_* =\frac{48\beta}{25}(-1,\gamma)\ne0. \]

    This is a local crossing-state coefficient, not an untransported coefficient at an arbitrary later T. A separate initial-state control takes the direction v=(e_0,0). Here n v/rho=96/5 and

    \[ D\mathcal O(\mathcal S-DW)v =\frac{9\beta}{50}(1,-\gamma)\ne0. \]

    In that direction DW alone gives zero for this contrast. The timing correction therefore changes an actual prediction of the local derivative, not just a bookkeeping label. Common form translations and common phase rotations give n v=0; the correction vanishes on those offset directions. As before, only offsets that stay inside the admitted absolute rails are compared.

    To relate this derivative to two positive waiting times, fix the two law parameters ell_1=E_*-eta and ell_2=E_*-2*eta for sufficiently small eta>0. Their common initial state is s_*, and

    \[ \tau_1=\eta/\rho_*+O(\eta^2),\qquad \tau_2=2\eta/\rho_*+O(\eta^2). \]

    There is a T_0>0 such that, for every fixed 0<T<T_0, a sufficiently small positive eta makes both crossings occur before T and gives

    \[ \mathcal O(Z(T;s_*,\ell_2))-\mathcal O(Z(T;s_*,\ell_1)) =\eta\,D\mathcal O\,D\Phi^+_T(Ws_*)K_*/\rho_*+O(\eta^2). \]

    The displayed coefficient tends to (48*beta/25)*(1,-gamma) as T tends to zero, so both components remain nonzero for sufficiently small fixed positive T. This continuity statement includes the required post-word transport; the bare event-local coefficient cannot replace it at finite T.

    For each chosen such eta and T, continuous dependence gives a smaller open initial neighborhood where both positive crossings and the observed distinction persist, with the two levels held fixed. It need not be uniform as eta tends to zero. Both histories have completed AL, IL, EN, IL, SHA, both capacities equal D and both structural clocks equal T. The source's geometric identity, form rails and fresh-pressure bounds follow from the existing one-word domain.

    This is a distinction between two complete models with different supplied thresholds, not two solutions of one fully specified law. The internal contrast removes common coordinate offsets. No numerical value for T_0, eta's admissible radius or a finite separation margin has been evaluated. There is no claim of asymptotic separation, optimized activation or physical identification.

    5. Detached implementation and controls¶

    The private _assess_organized_source_carrier_guard_sensitivity reuses the complete carrier timing reader. It re-admits the primitive form and represented factors, derives rho from the shared gradient, and rejects a zero-loss carrier state before division. Its guard level is only the energy at the supplied crossing point; the reader does not choose a threshold for another preparation or predict a first-hit time.

    The report retains K, the exact event-time covectors, the factorization of the timing correction, and -K/rho in both rows. Phase expressions retain their rational coefficients of cos(2*pi/9) and their gamma factor, with outward interval enclosures. Noncarrier crossings and propagation to T are covered by the conditional theorem, not evaluated by this carrier reader.

    Independent tests check the incidence loss, the implicit crossing condition, complete incoming and outgoing flow consistency, the failure of a reset-only derivative, threshold signs, common-offset controls and nontransverse/invalid rejection. Runtime events, integration and frozen producers are prohibited in these algebraic checks. No live guard, scheduler or new occurrence policy is installed.

    The result identifies what a supplied state guard contributes to a complete prediction: its level and urgency, a transverse crossing, and the event-time term in the derivative. Justifying that guard from an independent premise, or using it in a finite numerical prediction, remains a separate question.

    6. Collective summaries do not predict the same guard time¶

    Conditional obstruction. Even with the threshold and occurrence policy fixed, the five child form/phase means, capacities and grammar context, excess energy E and instantaneous loss rho do not form sufficient predictive state for the next guard occurrence. Two admitted fine preparations have the same such snapshot but different first-hit times. They also have identical initial primary state, fresh pressure and continuous rates at the marked targets. The distinction concerns hidden fine preparation under one law, rather than different choices of event threshold.

    This specializes the collective descent criterion to the first occurrence time of this word. Generic failures of scalar storage and loss as predictive state already appear in constituent memory and the native formation controls. Those results keep their own supports, complete laws and predictions; the present calculation derives the same-guard time distinction on this support.

    Observation and complete incoming law¶

    Let m_c=(1/9)*sum_j x_(9c+j) and a_c=(1/9)*sum_j(Theta_(9c+j)-psi_(9c+j)), using the admitted primitive lifts. These are phase-offset means, not arguments of circular resultants; the resultant of the exact winding-one carrier is zero. Retain the five pairs (m_c,a_c), the initial capacity vector and common supplied grammar context, E and rho. The support, marks, factors, clock origin and fixed level are the same. The strengthened snapshot may additionally retain the primary coordinates, fresh pressure and both continuous rates at targets zero and one. It does not retain the full fine form, fine pressure or their association.

    For unit incoming capacities, the complete law gives

    \[ \dot E=-\rho=-\tfrac14q^Tq,\qquad \dot q=\tfrac14L[-q+\gamma S(\Theta)],\qquad \ddot E=\tfrac18\{q^TLq-\gamma q^TLS(\Theta)\}. \]

    At the exact initial carrier S(psi)=0, so ddot E(0)=x^T L^3 x/8. This is an instantaneous identity of the coupled model: dot Theta=gamma*q/4 is generally nonzero, and the subsequent phase is not held at the carrier. Away from that initial point the displayed phase-dependent term remains part of the law.

    An exact matched pair in the admitted domain¶

    Let h=1/128 and repeat one profile in all five children:

    \[ x^A_{9c+j}=h(\delta_{j3}-\delta_{j6}),\qquad x^B_{9c+j}=h(\delta_{j3}-\delta_{j7}),\qquad \Theta^A=\Theta^B=\psi. \]

    All child form and phase-offset means are zero. Matching edges have zero form and phase difference because corresponding child profiles agree. The repeated-child subspace is invariant before the event: matching currents vanish and every child obeys the same two coupled cycle rows, regardless of its degree in the child graph. Antisymmetry of the cycle currents therefore preserves each of those means during pre-word continuation.

    For the nine-cycle Laplacian L_9, write A_k for its distance-k adjacency matrix. Exact multiplication gives

    \[ L_9^2=6I-4A_1+A_2,\qquad L_9^3=20I-15A_1+6A_2-A_3. \]

    The two signed pairs have cycle distances three and four. Both have first quadratic moment four and second moment twelve; their third moments are forty-two and forty. The matching-edge terms vanish also on their successive Laplacian images. Thus the full fine quantities are

    Quantity Preparation A Preparation B
    Initial excess E_0 10*h^2 = 5/8192 10*h^2 = 5/8192
    Initial loss rho_0 15*h^2 = 15/16384 15*h^2 = 15/16384
    Initial energy acceleration sigma 105*h^2/4 = 105/65536 25*h^2 = 25/16384

    Both have E_0<1/100, unit capacities and zero initial global form mean; the exact carrier lies in the required acute lift branch. They therefore belong to the section 23 ideal one-word domain with the shared initialized context and explicit IL event-domain policy. Zero target forms are permitted with the retained established history. No claim of acquiring these fine preparations by that history is made.

    The source is different from section 2's particular witness and radius box. Here both target form values and q values are zero initially, so fresh target pressure and both target rates are zero in both preparations. The full q is nonzero and rho_0>0. Smoothness gives a neighborhood of each preparation with strictly positive loss; the general transverse-crossing argument applies without using the earlier degree-five target lower bound.

    No support-preserving relabeling identifies the pair: x^T L^3 x is invariant under every such permutation but differs here. A common form shift cannot remove that difference, and both states already have the same zero means. The observation has discarded dynamically relevant fine arrangement.

    One fixed level, different positive occurrence times¶

    Choose one constant level ell=E_0-eta with sufficiently small eta>0, and hold it fixed for both preparations. Apply the same mandatory first crossing and EN–IL–SHA policy from section 1. Strict positive initial loss gives a unique positive first crossing for each preparation in a short pre-word interval. Both words remain within the shared ideal admission domain.

    For a fixed preparation with initial loss rho_0 and acceleration sigma, smoothness and the implicit-function theorem give

    \[ E(t)=E_0-\rho_0t+\tfrac12\sigma t^2+O(t^3),\qquad \tau(\eta)=\frac{\eta}{\rho_0} +\frac{\sigma}{2\rho_0^3}\eta^2+O(\eta^3). \]

    This is an expansion in a family of fixed levels for one initial point, not the fixed-level derivative with respect to a varying source in section 3. At every chosen eta the two compared experiments use exactly the same law parameter ell. Substitution of the exact pair yields

    \[ \tau_A(\eta)-\tau_B(\eta) =\frac{\eta^2}{5400h^4}+O(\eta^3)>0 \]

    for every sufficiently small positive eta. Preparation A reaches the level later despite equal initial energy and loss. The nonzero quadratic term proves unequal exact times by the local remainder statement; it is not a computed finite waiting time or an evaluated error bar. No numerical eta radius, crossing forecast or later endpoint separation is claimed.

    Predictive-state consequence and implementation boundary¶

    A deterministic next-time function of this instantaneous summary, with the same fixed level and parameters, would give the same output for both inputs. The proved distinction rules it out on any source class containing this pair. The summary also fails the continuous descent criterion directly: dot rho=-sigma differs at the matched snapshot. It cannot have a single autonomous first-order evolution law reproducing the full projected flow on that source class.
