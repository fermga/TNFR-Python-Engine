    Regional contact phases and retained profile information¶

    1. One unchanged pair and a new observation question¶

    The conditional pair identity retains four nine-cycle regions, their marked relations and the original source association under a supplied dissipative law. This chapter asks whether the phases of their actual contact coefficients factor through one phase per region, and what an instantaneous regional response loses when only coefficient magnitudes are retained. The prospective scope is artifacts/mixed-regional-loop-20261026/scope.json.

    No new preparation, topology, contact, source replay or trajectory is supplied. The two acquired preparations, all held unequal conductances, unit capacities, phase lifts, source-correlated initialization and previously paid attachment work remain those of the linked source. Time is the retained structural clock \(\tau=(1023/1024)t\). The phase-sensitive interface owns the full seventy-two rows and the attachment accounting.

    Write the regions as \(D_A,D_B,R_A,R_B\), each of size nine. Let \(H\) be the two-copy held form Laplacian and \(G\) the eighteen matching-pair Laplacian. The complete law, with \(L_+=H+9G\) and \(\gamma=1/(1023\pi)\), is

    \[ x'=-\frac{L_+x}{9}+\frac{\gamma}{9}S_+(\theta),\qquad \theta'=\frac{\gamma L_+x}{9},\qquad (S_+)_i=\sum_j\sigma_{ij}\sin(\theta_j-\theta_i). \tag{1} \]

    The symmetric sine coefficients are one on the held internal support and nine on each inter-copy matching edge. They are the coefficients of the bare sine potential, not the unequal conductances in \(H\). The divisor nine and both evolution rows are unchanged.

    Use the carried stationary template \(\Theta\) and continuous phase lifts. The canonical mean frame in this chapter is template-relative:

    \[ \varphi_a=\frac19\sum_{i\in a}(\theta_i-\Theta_i),\qquad h_i=\theta_i-\Theta_i-\varphi_a,\qquad \eta_i=\Theta_i+h_i,\qquad \sum_{i\in a}h_i=0. \tag{2} \]

    Thus \(\theta_i=\varphi_a+\eta_i\). No assumption that each template's arithmetic regional mean vanishes is necessary. Changing individual circular representatives without transporting the admitted lifts is not a new admissible source gauge.

    2. Coefficients inherited from the actual sine support¶

    For distinct adjacent regions define

    \[ C_{ab}=\sum_{\substack{i\in a\\j\in b}} \sigma_{ij}e^{i(\eta_j-\eta_i)},\qquad F_{ab}=\operatorname{Im}\!\left[ e^{i(\varphi_b-\varphi_a)}C_{ab}\right]. \tag{3} \]

    Here \(F_{ab}\) is exactly the outgoing-neighbor sine sum in the form row of region \(a\); the word contact does not add an independent transport law. Symmetry of \(\sigma\) gives \(C_{ba}=\overline{C_{ab}}\) and \(F_{ba}=-F_{ab}\). Internal edges within one region cancel on summation. For regional mean form \(m_a=\sum_{i\in a}x_i/9\), the exact projected rows are

    \[ m_a'=-\frac1{81}\sum_{i\in a}(L_+x)_i +\frac{\gamma}{81}\sum_{b}F_{ab},\qquad \varphi_a'=\frac{\gamma}{81}\sum_{i\in a}(L_+x)_i . \tag{4} \]

    The retained weighted form term and profiles still depend on the fine state. These identities therefore do not assert closure on four means or install a reduced dynamical model.

    Reindex each \(B\) region through its carried marked partner in \(A\). The template phasors then agree at matched phase-bin index \(j\in\mathbb Z_9\). Set

    \[ \omega=2\pi/9,\quad \zeta=e^{i\omega},\quad {\cal M}=\{0,1,2,-1,-2\},\quad c_0=\sum_{m\in{\cal M}}\zeta^m =1+2\cos(2\pi/9)+2\cos(4\pi/9) =1+2\cos(\pi/9),\qquad 2<c_0<3 . \tag{5} \]

    The original support supplies exactly those five cross-region phase-bin offsets. Branch turns can differ by integers; their phasors agree. Define

    \[ \begin{split} C_D&=\sum_{j=0}^8\sum_{m\in{\cal M}}\zeta^m e^{i(h_{D_B,j+m}-h_{D_A,j})},\\ C_R&=\sum_{j=0}^8\sum_{m\in{\cal M}}\zeta^m e^{i(h_{R_B,j+m}-h_{R_A,j})},\\ P_A&=9\sum_{j=0}^8 e^{i(h_{R_A,j}-h_{D_A,j})},\qquad P_B=9\sum_{j=0}^8 e^{i(h_{R_B,j}-h_{D_B,j})}. \end{split} \tag{6} \]

    Consequently the ordered rectangle \(D_A\to D_B\to R_B\to R_A\to D_A\) uses \(C_D,P_B,\overline{C_R},\overline{P_A}\). On the equitable regional reference \(h=0\), these are the positive real numbers \(9c_0,81,9c_0,81\), for every retained regional mean offset. This nominal statement does not discard actual source profile errors.

    3. Description covariance and exact vertex factorization¶

    A change of the regional frame split, \(\varphi_a\mapsto\varphi_a+\chi_a\) and \(\eta_i\mapsto\eta_i-\chi_a\) for \(i\in a\), leaves every actual \(\theta_i\) unchanged. It induces

    \[ C_{ab}\mapsto e^{i(\chi_a-\chi_b)}C_{ab}. \tag{7} \]

    Equation (3) is invariant. This is a redundancy of the description, not a physical phase intervention or a newly established dynamical gauge symmetry.

    Only when each required coefficient is nonzero define

    \[ u_{ab}=\frac{C_{ab}}{|C_{ab}|},\qquad {\cal H}=u_{D_A D_B}u_{D_B R_B}u_{R_B R_A}u_{R_A D_A}. \tag{8} \]

    Reversal conjugates a unit link, and regional frame factors cancel in the loop product. Missing or zero coefficients give an unavailable chart, not zero curvature. Magnitudes remain separate observations; (8) does not encode coupling strengths.

    On a connected coarse graph with these reversal rules, the unit links admit vertex phases \(u_{ab}=e^{i(\alpha_b-\alpha_a)}\) if and only if all independent cycle products equal one. Necessity follows by telescoping. For sufficiency, choose one root phase and propagate phases along a spanning tree. Each remaining edge agrees with its assigned endpoint phases exactly when its fundamental cycle product is one. The present rectangle has one independent cycle, so its condition is \({\cal H}=1\). The nominal coefficients in (6) satisfy this condition exactly.

    No independently propagating edge degree of freedom follows from a failure of vertex factorization: the coefficient map (3) is already a function of retained fine profiles. A claim about autonomous transport, response memory or an electromagnetic field needs additional dynamics.

    4. Centered-profile cancellation and original-source bounds¶

    Write \(s_a=\|h_a\|_2^2\) and \(S=\sum_a s_a\). The elementary remainder \(|e^{iv}-1-iv|\le v^2/2\) applies to every real lifted difference. For an internal coefficient, its linear term vanishes:

    \[ \sum_{j,m}\zeta^m(h_{B,j+m}-h_{A,j}) =c_0\left(\sum_jh_{B,j}-\sum_jh_{A,j}\right)=0 . \tag{9} \]

    Both complex cross-block row and column sums are the same real \(c_0\). Each vertex has five cross neighbors. Bounding each squared difference by twice the sum of its squared endpoints therefore gives

    \[ \begin{split} |C_D-9c_0|&\le5(s_{D_A}+s_{D_B}),& |C_R-9c_0|&\le5(s_{R_A}+s_{R_B}),\\ |P_A-81|&\le9(s_{D_A}+s_{R_A}),& |P_B-81|&\le9(s_{D_B}+s_{R_B}). \end{split} \tag{10} \]

    The matching linear term also vanishes by regional centering. No claim that the internal imaginary remainder is cubic is needed: its quadratic cross-profile term can be nonzero, as section 6 demonstrates.

    The marked-identity comparison retains an analytical regional reference driven by the same actual \(\psi(\tau)\). Its original-source estimate is \(\sqrt{\|e_x\|^2+4\|e_y\|^2}\le B_{\rm diff}\) for all postattachment ages. Let \(P_{\rm reg}\) average separately within each of the four regions. The reference centered phases are regional constants. Hence

    \[ h=(I-P_{\rm reg})e_y,\qquad S\le\|e_y\|^2\le S_*:=B_{\rm diff}^2/4 . \tag{11} \]

    This uses a contraction of the same admitted error, not a newly chosen initial profile or an observation of the reference. Its actual phase driver does not become an independently computable predictor.

    Equations (10)--(11) yield retained magnitude intervals

    \[ \begin{split} 9c_0-5S_*&\le |C_D|,|C_R|\le9c_0+5S_*,\\ 81-9S_*&\le |P_A|,|P_B|\le81+9S_* . \end{split} \tag{12} \]

    The two lower margins must be admitted before normalization. The original strict marked certificate \(q_{\rm hi}+3B_{\rm diff}/4<\Delta/4\), with \(q_{\rm hi}\ge0\) and \(\Delta=1/1024\), already implies \(B_{\rm diff}<\Delta/3\), which is sufficient for their positivity. Implementation nevertheless rebuilds the consumed source conditions and these margins rather than trusting an old global verdict.

    For \(z\ne0\) and real \(c>0\),

    \[ \left|\frac z{|z|}-1\right|\le\frac{2|z-c|}{c}. \tag{13} \]

    For example, add \(z/c\) and use \(\big||z|-c\big|\le|z-c|\). Telescoping the product of four unit factors then bounds its difference from one by the sum of their differences. Each pair of internal regions and each matching partition counts \(S\) once. Thus the source-conditioned loop allowance is

    \[ |{\cal H}-1| \le\left(\frac{10}{9c_0}+\frac29\right)S \le\frac79 S_* =\frac{7B_{\rm diff}^2}{36}. \tag{14} \]

    This is dimensionless and uniform on the original admitted source. It is an upper bound, not proof that the actual loop equals one or differs from one. It cannot be assigned the earlier form-output tolerance merely because both are numbers.

    5. A same-state description with coefficient phases removed¶

    In the fixed frame (2), retain the actual \(\varphi_a\), \(m_a\), coefficient magnitudes and weighted form contribution in (4). Define a comparison of instantaneous regional form rows by replacing \(C_{ab}\) with \(|C_{ab}|\) on the four contacts. This supplies no independently evolved four-state model and changes no complete field.

    For a coefficient with positive nominal value \(c\), \(|C-|C||\le2|C-c|\). Every region has one internal and one inter-copy contact. Its comparison error in mean-form rate therefore satisfies

    \[ |m_a'-\widetilde m_a'| \le\frac{2\gamma}{81} \left[5(s_a+s_{\text{internal partner}}) +9(s_a+s_{\text{matching partner}})\right] \le\frac{28g}{81}S_* =\frac{7gB_{\rm diff}^2}{81}, \qquad g=1/3000 . \tag{15} \]

    The allowance has mean-form-per-structural-time units under the retained normalization. Multiplying by nine instead gives a region-total rate allowance. It is not a finite-horizon trajectory error or an observed sensor resolution, and the earlier \(Q\) error contract remains unchanged.

    Under a regional frame rewrite, the comparison coefficients must transform by (7), along with the mean phases. Replacing them again by positive real magnitudes in the new frame would define a different comparison. The actual loop observation is frame invariant; this explicitly registered comparison has its own transported description.

    6. Equal means and magnitudes can hide different regional forces¶

    This exact static witness belongs to a larger identity-shaped class. It is not asserted to be an endpoint of either original acquired preparation. Keep the same node labels, all unequal form weights, support, matching, template and clock. Put all forms and all template-relative regional means equal to zero, and take

    \[ a=\Delta/10000,\quad h_{D_A}=a(e_0-e_1),\quad h_{D_B}=a(e_1-e_2),\quad h_{R_A}=h_{R_B}=0. \tag{16} \]

    State II swaps only the two donor profile arrays in phase-bin order. It does not swap nodes or their held conductances.

    Let \(T_{ij}=\zeta^{j-i}{\bf1}_{j-i\in{\cal M}}\). Symmetric offset support gives \(T=T^*\). With \(p_j=e^{ih_{D_A,j}}\), \(q_j=e^{ih_{D_B,j}}\), the internal coefficient is \(C_D=p^*Tq\), so the profile swap conjugates it. Both states have

    \[ C_R=9c_0,\qquad P_A=P_B=9(7+2\cos a)>0 . \tag{17} \]

    In particular all four coefficient magnitudes coincide, as do the regional means and three of the four unit links.

    Put \(d=2(1-\cos a)>0\). Expanding the four nonconstant products in \(p^*Tq\) gives the exact expression for state I:

    \[ \begin{split} C_D&=9c_0-2c_0d+ d\left[2e^{i\omega}-e^{i(2\omega-a)}-e^{ia}\right],\\ \operatorname{Im}C_D &=2d\sin\omega\,[1-\cos(\omega-a)]>0 . \end{split} \tag{18} \]

    The sign follows from \(0<a<\omega<\pi\), not a numerical phase estimate. The coefficient is nonzero, the state-I loop is \(C_D/|C_D|\), and the state-II loop is its distinct conjugate. The nominal and matching coefficients remain positive.

    Here \(S=4a^2\) and the identity radius of the previous pair chapter is \(Z^2=\|x_c\|^2+4\|y_c\|^2=16a^2\). Each original marked-gap increment and cycle-edge perturbation has magnitude at most \(2a\). Thus \(Z<1/500\), marked gaps remain strictly below \(\Delta/4\), and \(2a<\pi-4\pi/9\) preserves all four carried windings. Both states have zero total form and \(\psi=0\). They meet these membership-shaped conditions without acquiring the original source's history or forecast admission.

    All weighted form terms vanish because \(x=0\). The matching regional sine sums vanish by (17), and the recipient internal sine sum vanishes as well. The two exact regional mean-form rate vectors differ by

    \[ (m_{D_A}',m_{D_B}',m_{R_A}',m_{R_B}')_{\rm I} -(m_{D_A}',m_{D_B}',m_{R_A}',m_{R_B}')_{\rm II} =\frac{2\gamma\,\operatorname{Im}C_D}{81}(1,-1,0,0)\ne0 . \tag{19} \]

    Their whole-copy exchange derivative \(Q'=-9(m_{D_A}'+m_{D_B}')\) is zero in both states at that instant. Consequently the witness establishes information loss for this regional response, not an immediate difference in \(Q\), a failure of its existing predictor or an acquired-source response. Coefficient magnitude alone cannot repair the missing contact phase.

    7. Implementation, evaluated result and scientific boundary¶

    The private regional-loop reader provides a detached coefficient observation and source-bound assessment. It retains all four complex coefficients and their magnitudes, admits nonzero normalization, rebuilds the original source premises and distinguishes the constructed witness from the admitted source family.

    The first frozen evaluation passed all fifteen controls: the twelve regional-loop controls and three shared controls, with no skips. Black and full flake8 checks also passed. The unchanged original form and phase error bounds were each \(10^{-10}\) for both sources. The result is conditional regional contact information; the frozen proof, source archive and exact observation record remain in artifacts/mixed-regional-loop-20261026/.

    The stored rational certificates give the following conservative strict decimal ceilings, uniformly over the admitted postattachment history:

    \[ \begin{split} B_{\rm diff}&<5.506\,10^{-7},& S&<7.579\,10^{-14},\\ |{\cal H}-1|&<5.895\,10^{-14},& \max_a|m_a'-\widetilde m_a'|&<8.733\,10^{-18}. \end{split} \]

    The loop allowance is dimensionless. The last allowance is regional mean form per structural \(\tau\), not a trajectory or sensor-error bound. Neither exact factorization nor a nontrivial actual-source loop was certified.

    For the fixed constructed witness, the positive imaginary coefficient is approximately \(2.8683413091893663\,10^{-15}\), and the regional mean-rate separation is approximately \(2.2036889701418784\,10^{-20}\) form per \(\tau\). These printed values summarize the retained interval bounds and establish no experimental detectability. The exact matched-magnitude information-loss statement remains the larger-class result (16)--(19).
