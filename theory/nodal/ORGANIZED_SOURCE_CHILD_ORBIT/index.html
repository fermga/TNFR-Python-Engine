    Exact organized-source state modulo a global child exchange¶

    Conditional construction. The two exchangeable children need not have equal form or phase. Their averaged coordinates together with one joint rank-at-most-one Gram matrix retain the complete primary state up to the single exchange of those children. On the specified organized-source law, these coordinates close both nonlinear continuous rows, energy and loss, the fixed energy guard and the admitted marked Reception–Coherence–Silence maps. Separate pair summaries can lose the relations required for this prediction.

    This extends the represented domain of the fixed-sector chart, whose 72 primary coordinates require equal children. It does not replace its separate finite-error results. The present generic primary dimension is still 90. Removing a redundant global label is different from reducing the number of continuous degrees of freedom. Section 7 separately proves a quantitative approach to that sector in the admitted acute energy domain, with finite tolerance deadlines and decaying instantaneous prediction defects. Section 8 bounds the accumulated error of a separately evolved symmetric core, for unit capacities without events.

    The construction applies the existing support-symmetry criterion and joint Gram method to the actual 45-node source, reciprocal phase row and marked ideal word. Its new obligation is to identify which relations across the nine paired slots this support preserves. It introduces no new constitutive law.

    1. Actual symmetry and retained state¶

    Use the source support and law: nodes 9*c+j, five nine-cycles and the matching child edges (0,1),(2,3),(0,2),(1,3),(0,4). All ninety edges have unit conductance. The global permutation J exchanges whole children one and two and fixes children zero, three and four. It fixes the marked targets zero and one. The cycles couple adjacent slots, so swapping just one corresponding pair of nodes is generally not a support automorphism.

    Keep the ordered child orbits A={0}, B={1,2}, C={3}, D={4}, with nine slots each. Their masses are 1,2,1,1; write M_mass for the diagonal 36-coordinate mass matrix. P copies projected coordinates to their fine nodes and R=M_mass^-1 P^T averages the fibers. Thus Pi=PR=(I+J)/2. The inherited weighted Laplacian is L_w, as derived in the fixed-sector owner; its 63 edges have total weight ninety.

    Declare the raw real carrier lift psi_(9c+j)=alpha*(j-4), alpha=2*pi/9. Retain 36 projected forms u and 36 projected real phase offsets d. Write vartheta=bar_psi+d. The unequal children have

    \[ x_{1j}=u_{Bj}+a_j,\quad x_{2j}=u_{Bj}-a_j,\qquad \Theta_{1j}=\vartheta_{Bj}+b_j,\quad \Theta_{2j}=\vartheta_{Bj}-b_j. \]

    All other children are copied from their singleton orbit. Positive held capacities must satisfy nu_(1j)=nu_(2j). Their 36 retained coordinates are separate law parameters, not part of the 90-primary-coordinate count. They may vary between slots and between the four child orbits.

    Set v=(a_0,...,a_8,b_0,...,b_8) and retain

    \[ G=vv^T= \begin{pmatrix}G_{aa}&G_{ab}\\G_{ab}^T&G_{bb}\end{pmatrix}. \]

    The domain requires one jointly symmetric positive-semidefinite matrix of rank at most one. Its 171 distinct entries are redundant coordinates. They are products of one deterministic hidden vector, not an ensemble covariance, and cannot be replaced by unrelated positive local blocks. The joint-realizability owner explains the general distinction between pairwise admissibility and a common underlying state.

    If G is nonzero, choose its first positive diagonal entry G_kk and set v_k=sqrt(G_kk)>0, v_i=G_ik/v_k. Rank-one realizability proves that these values reconstruct G, and its only real lifts are v and -v. At G=0 the only lift is zero. Hence (u,d,G,nu) reconstructs the fine primary state up to exactly J. Separate signs for different slots or for the form and phase blocks do not describe the same equivalence relation.

    The generic dimension is 72+18=90, with a singular zero-hidden stratum. The canonical sign rule is an algebraic representative, not a globally smooth choice of labeled state. At v=0 the differential of v -> vv^T vanishes; this coordinate singularity does not make the fine flow singular. Labeled nonsymmetric observations need a choice between the two lifts. Invariant observations and fixed-target values do not.

    These coordinates retain declared real phase lifts. They do not quotient independent turns, define a global chart on circular phases, or infer a preferred lift from wrapped observations.

    2. Closed projected nonlinear law¶

    The complete unforced law in structural time tau is

    \[ \dot x=\tfrac14D_f[-L_f x+\gamma S_f(\Theta)],\qquad \dot\Theta=\tfrac\gamma4D_fL_f x,\qquad \gamma=(1023\pi)^{-1}, \]

    where S_f(Theta)_i=sum_(k~i) sin(Theta_k-Theta_i) and Gamma is absent. Every consumed capacity is held between events; the same capacity multiplies both rows. Define q=M_mass^-1 L_w u and let D be the diagonal retained capacity matrix. Hidden form cancels in the projected Laplacian, so R L_f x=q even away from the fixed sector.

    For nonnegative r, put

    \[ C(r)=\cos\sqrt r,\qquad F(r)=\frac{\sin\sqrt r}{\sqrt r},\quad F(0)=1. \]

    Their power series in r remove any division at zero. For a B-cycle edge jk define

    \[ r_{jk}=G_{bb,jj}+G_{bb,kk}-2G_{bb,jk}=(b_k-b_j)^2, \qquad r_j=G_{bb,jj}=b_j^2. \]

    The projected sine current s_G=R S_f(Theta) is obtained by these exact contributions; each displayed term is added to the indicated row:

    Row and neighbor Contribution
    B slot j, adjacent B slot k sin(vartheta_Bk-vartheta_Bj)*C(r_jk)
    B slot j, U slot j, U=A or C sin(vartheta_Uj-vartheta_Bj)*C(r_j)
    U slot j, B slot j, U=A or C 2*sin(vartheta_Bj-vartheta_Uj)*C(r_j)
    Every unaffected cycle or A–D edge Its original fine sine term

    These follow from sin(t+b)+sin(t-b)=2*sin(t)*cos(b) and the inherited neighbor counts. Consequently both projected rows close:

    \[ \dot u=\tfrac14D(-q+\gamma s_G),\qquad \dot d=\tfrac\gamma4Dq. \]

    The phase row does not depend directly on hidden coordinates, but the form row consumes cross-slot entries of G_bb. Their evolution will also consume the mixed block G_ab. Keeping just the phase correction at one instant does not establish future closure.

    3. Joint Gram evolution and exact balance¶

    Let H=L_C9+2I, with diagonal four and adjacent entries minus one, and V=diag(nu_Bj). Define the symmetric matrix K as a weighted cycle Laplacian with edge weight

    \[ \cos(\vartheta_{Bk}-\vartheta_{Bj})F(r_{jk}), \]

    plus the diagonal entry

    \[ [\cos(\vartheta_{Aj}-\vartheta_{Bj})+ \cos(\vartheta_{Cj}-\vartheta_{Bj})]F(r_j). \]

    The half-difference of the two fine sine currents is exactly -K b. Subtracting the complete fine rows therefore gives

    \[ \dot a=-\tfrac14VHa-\tfrac\gamma4VKb,\qquad \dot b=\tfrac\gamma4VHa,\qquad \dot v=\mathcal A v,\quad \mathcal A=\tfrac14 \begin{pmatrix}-VH&-\gamma VK\\\gamma VH&0\end{pmatrix}. \]

    The entries of K depend on (d,G_bb), not on the lift's overall sign. The exact joint equation is thus

    \[ \boxed{\dot G=\mathcal A G+G\mathcal A^T.} \]

    These equations are smooth on the realized domain, including G=0; the power series also define a smooth extension to ambient coordinates. Starting from a realized G, the fine lifted solution proves preservation of rank-one realizability and supplies the unique projected solution. The two signs give the same retained trajectory. This is a constructive closure proof, not a prescription to integrate redundant unconstrained entries or repair a matrix that has drifted out of its domain.

    Let bar E be the fixed-sector excess at (u,d), and H_*=45*(1-cos(alpha)). Exact edge grouping yields

    \[ \begin{split} E={}&\bar E+\operatorname{tr}(H G_{aa})\\ &+2\sum_{jk\in C_9}\cos(\vartheta_{Bk}-\vartheta_{Bj})[1-C(r_{jk})]\\ &+2\sum_j[\cos(\vartheta_{Aj}-\vartheta_{Bj})+ \cos(\vartheta_{Cj}-\vartheta_{Bj})][1-C(r_j)]. \end{split} \]

    Each unoriented cycle edge appears once. This identity does not require acute angles; the correction need not be nonnegative outside an acute projected domain. For arbitrary admitted symmetric positive capacities,

    \[ \rho=\tfrac14q^T M_{\rm mass}Dq+ \tfrac12\operatorname{tr}(H V H G_{aa}),\qquad \dot E=-\rho. \]

    Indeed the two hidden fine gradients are q_B+Ha and q_B-Ha, whose weighted squares cancel the cross terms. Slot-dependent V must remain between the two H factors. The fine inverse-capacity-weighted form and phase-offset totals reduce to sum(mass/nu*u) and sum(mass/nu*d); hidden terms cancel because the paired capacities agree.

    These are exact identities for the supplied law. They neither select its constitutive coefficients nor identify these quantities with laboratory energy, time or measured information.

    4. Guard and marked hybrid maps¶

    Use the same supplied fixed energy level and mandatory first downward crossing from the guard owner. Since the full energy is reconstructed exactly, its crossing set is the same for both fine lifts and the retained trajectory. Existence, positive first occurrence and transversality remain the guard owner's separate premises; this representation does not establish them anew.

    For the complete ideal word, intersect with the section 23 word domain: initial unit capacities, its acute lift cell, E<1/100, abs(initial form mean)<1/30, and complete supplied admission context. The continuous identity above allows more general held capacities; it does not widen this particular first-word admission theorem.

    Let beta, lambda and kappa be the already admitted Reception, Coherence and Silence factors. Each stage uses one common fine snapshot at the fixed targets i=0,1. Reception retains the same inherited mean:

    \[ u_i^+=(1-\beta)u_i+\frac\beta5\sum_bN_{ib}u_b. \]

    The hidden forms cancel in the two exchanged neighbors. Only these two singleton projected forms change; d, G and capacities are held.

    For Coherence, write j=i for the target's slot. Its relative resultant is

    \[ z_i=\sum_{k\in\{\text{two A-cycle neighbors, D slot }j\}} e^{i(\vartheta_k-\vartheta_i)} +2e^{i(\vartheta_{Bj}-\vartheta_i)}C(G_{bb,jj}). \]

    This is the exact five-neighbor fine sum. On the existing regular argument branch, d_i^+=d_i+lambda*Arg(z_i); every other projected phase, all forms, G and capacities are held. Silence then sends the two target capacities to kappa*nu_i, holding u, d and G. It preserves equality between the capacities of children one and two, so the subsequent held-capacity law remains in this domain.

    Each primary stage commutes with J. The represented state therefore reconstructs the marked ideal word and its changed-capacity continuation up to that same global exchange. The hidden vector itself is unchanged by these marked maps; a capacity jump does not reset G.

    Histories, ordinals, debt, prior Coherence, policies and any labeled selector inputs remain separately retained and admitted. Arbitrary runtime contexts are not functions of G. Full labeled execution needs the matching fine context and lift; no quotient of arbitrary context or new occurrence law follows. Stored-pressure jumps and fresh-law association retain their existing contracts. See the operator descent and grammar descent conditions. The proof concerns the primary ideal maps under those premises, not a new runtime executor.

    5. Frozen information-loss controls¶

    Both controls were fixed at h=1/128, unit capacity and carrier projected phase before evaluation. They are static state and derivative identities, not samples from a trajectory or unknown crossing states. They disprove the named omissions; they do not prove that all 171 entries are a minimal collection of stored numbers.

    Cross-slot phase association is visible immediately¶

    Set projected form h*(e0-e1) on child A, every other projected form zero, and a=0. In the first hidden phase vector all b_j are +h except b_2=-h; in the second, only b_4=-h. Every local pair Gram is (a_j^2,a_j*b_j,b_j^2)=(0,0,h^2). Their hidden means agree, so the means of each labeled child also agree. Both states have

    \[ E=6h^2+4\cos\alpha(1-\cos2h)+36(1-\cos h) \le32h^2=1/512<1/100,\qquad \rho=20h^2. \]

    Their marked targets and every incident initial form, phase and capacity agree. Thus initial fresh target pressure and direct ideal word outputs at the targets agree as well, conditional on equal admitted context. The largest cycle gap is at most alpha+2h<pi/2, matching gaps have magnitude h and the global form mean is zero.

    Nevertheless their projected B-slot-1 form rates differ, first minus second, by

    \[ \dot u^{(1)}_{B1}-\dot u^{(2)}_{B1} =\frac\gamma4\sin\alpha(\cos2h-1)<0. \]

    The missing entry G_bb,1,2 is respectively -h^2 and +h^2. Adjacent phase associations matter even when local pair Grams, child means, energy, instantaneous loss and initial target data all agree.

    Separate complete form and phase Grams still lose mixed information¶

    Now set every projected form to zero, a=h*e0, and compare b=+h*e1 with b=-h*e1. All local pair Grams agree because the two supports are disjoint. Even the full separate matrices G_aa and G_bb agree, as do both projected first derivative rows. Both preparations are acute, have zero global form mean, and satisfy

    \[ E=4h^2+4(1+\cos\alpha)(1-\cos h)\le8h^2<1/100, \qquad \rho=9h^2. \]

    But G_ab,0,1 is respectively +h^2 and -h^2. Since (Ha)_1=-h, the squared hidden phase has

    \[ \frac{d}{d\tau}b_1^2=\mp\frac\gamma2h^2. \]

    This distinction reaches a projected observable at the next derivative:

    \[ \ddot u^{(+)}_{B0}-\ddot u^{(-)}_{B0} =\frac58\gamma^2h\sin\alpha\sin h>0. \]

    To verify the coefficient, the initial projected phase rate is zero and the two projected form rates coincide, so their q derivatives coincide. Only the derivative of the cycle phase correction differs. Here dot b_1-dot b_0=-5*gamma*h/4; differentiating the two cosines gives a projected sine-rate contrast 5*gamma*h*sin(alpha)*sin(h)/2. The additional form-row factor gamma/4 yields the stated curvature. The mixed block records a relative association between hidden form and phase that neither separate full Gram retains.

    6. Detached implementation and boundary¶

    The private orbit reader encodes fine primitives, decodes admitted invariant coordinates to a canonical fine lift and observes the same complete law through the shared fine capacity reader. Projected rows are actual fiber averages; selecting one representative off the fixed sector would be incorrect. The joint Gram derivative is built from the fine hidden derivatives. No second sine kernel, numerical integrator or event executor is introduced.

    The shared exact rank-one lift normalizes every matrix entry before checking symmetry and realizability. It also serves the existing P5 invariant decoder. The implementation retains exact rational or exactly represented real input values and rational canonical lifts. A realizable rational Gram whose canonical square root is irrational is explicitly unsupported by this decoder, though it is a valid point of the real mathematical domain. It is not rounded to a nearby lift or declared physically impossible.

    The encoded fine state and every consumed primitive are readmitted; cached reports, Boolean scalars, nonfinite values, unequal paired capacities or arbitrary positive-semidefinite higher-rank matrices do not replace these premises. The fine state in a decoded report is the canonical representative, not a claim to recover the original labeled preparation without its missing overall sign.

    Independent controls compare exact reconstruction, the shared-law observation and the prospectively fixed state/derivative distinctions. Algebraic closure and the conditional word proof establish sufficiency under this selected law.
