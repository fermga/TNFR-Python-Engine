## One joined state, event work and both retained identities

Write \(a,b\) for the donor and receiver central ports. Put
\(A=K^+L^+\) after joining, with \(K^+_{aa}=K^+_{bb}=1/3\)
and \(K^+_{ii}=1/2\) elsewhere. All later rows are exactly
\[
x'=-Ax+\gamma K^+S(\theta),\qquad \theta'=\gamma Ax. \tag{4}
\]
The actual contact states satisfy
\[
\|x_{\mathrm D}\|,\|x_{\mathrm R}\|,
\|u_{\mathrm D}\|,\|u_{\mathrm R}\|\le\epsilon,
\]
where \(u\) is the phase error from each component's limiting twist
and its original common origin. Each of these four error rows has
zero unweighted mean before contact.

Choose the aligned joined critical phase geometry
\(\Theta_k\): donor twist \(k\alpha(j-4)\), receiver twist
\(\alpha(j-4)\), with \(\alpha=2\pi/9\) and both port phases
zero. Internal sine sums cancel and the bridge sine is zero, so
\(S(\Theta_k)=0\) on the full joined support. It is a reference for
the retention proof, not the actual postevent state. With
\(P=I-\mathbf1\mathbf1^{\mathsf T}/18\), the actual squared
quotient distance to this reference satisfies
\[
Z^2=\|Px\|^2+\|P(\theta-\Theta_k)\|^2
\le4\epsilon^2+\frac92\varphi^2. \tag{5}
\]
Indeed, centering the two nominal origins gives offsets
\(-\varphi/2\) and \(+\varphi/2\) on nine nodes each.
Their cross terms with the phase errors vanish by the retained
component zero-sum constraints. No independent favorable error
alignment has been imposed.

Let
\[
H=\frac12x^{\mathsf T}L^+x+
U^+(\theta),\qquad
U^+(\theta)=\sum_{\{i,j\}\in E^+}[1-\cos(\theta_j-\theta_i)].
\]
The exact supplied support-event work is
\[
W_{\mathrm{join}}=\frac12(x_b-x_a)^2+
1-\cos(\varphi+u_b-u_a),
\]
and obeys
\[
0<W_{\mathrm{join}}\le
2\epsilon^2+\frac12(\varphi+2\epsilon)^2<10^{-6}. \tag{6}
\]
Strict positivity follows from
\(0<\varphi-2\epsilon\le|\varphi+u_b-u_a|
\le\varphi+2\epsilon<\pi\). There is no assumption of a passive
contact or an undeclared reservoir paying this work.

On each original cycle, \(\lambda_{\max}(L_{\mathrm{C9}})\le4\).
Its target is phase-critical, so Taylor's integral bound gives
internal phase excess at most \(2\|u\|^2\); internal form storage
is at most \(2\|x\|^2\). Adding the bridge exactly yields
\[
H(\tau_c^+)-U^+(\Theta_k)
\le10\epsilon^2+\frac12(\varphi+2\epsilon)^2<10^{-6}. \tag{7}
\]
This is a complete joined-state bound, not subtraction of unrelated

The joined graph has eighteen nodes and diameter nine. If \(z\)
has zero mean and range \(R_z\), then
\[
\|z\|^2\le\frac{18}{4}R_z^2,\qquad
R_z^2\le9\,z^{\mathsf T}L^+z.
\]
The second inequality follows along a shortest path between a
maximum and a minimum. Hence \(\lambda_2(L^+)\ge2/81\).
Throughout the relative radius-\(r\) chart, every edge has positive
cosine at least \(\cos(4\pi/9+\sqrt2r)>1/20\).
For closing cycle edges, this statement uses their fixed turn offsets
in the retained local lift chart. It does not replace a real closing
gap by an unrelated representative during evolution.

Interpolation to \(\Theta_k\) stays in the convex chart.
The form quadratic and the integrated phase Hessian therefore give
the strict boundary barrier
\[
H-U^+(\Theta_k)\ge
\frac{\lambda_2(L^+)}2
\cos(4\pi/9+\sqrt2r)\,Z^2
\ge\frac{Z^2}{1620}. \tag{8}
\]
The coefficient is strictly greater than \(1/1620\), so the last
inequality is strict on the boundary \(Z=r>0\).
The frozen bounds satisfy
\[
Z^2<5\varphi^2<r^2,\qquad
H(\tau_c^+)-U^+(\Theta_k)<10^{-6}
<\frac{r^2}{1620}=\frac1{233280}.
\]
Along the entire joined continuous flow, (4) gives exactly
\[
H'=-(L^+x)^{\mathsf T}K^+(L^+x)\le0. \tag{9}
\]
The mixed terms cancel using the symmetric diagonal mobility.
A first exit through \(Z=r\) would contradict (7)--(9).
Thus every actual joined member remains in the acute chart, retaining
donor winding \(k\) and receiver winding one.

The postevent conserved means use the new degrees, whose total is

## The first donor-dependent receiver derivative

For the response comparison only, start (4) at zero form and at
\(\theta_k^0\), obtained by adding the receiver origin \(\varphi\)
to the receiver block of \(\Theta_k\). This defines an ideal
comparison flow; the actual state remains the one bounded in (3).
Put
\[
q=e_a-e_b,\quad \sigma=\sin\varphi,\quad s=|\sigma|,\quad
f=K^+S(\theta_k^0)=\frac{\sigma}{3}q.
\]
The common vector \(f\) is independent of donor class.
Let \(B_k=K^+L_{\cos(\theta_k^0)}\) be minus the derivative of
the normalized sine field at the comparison state, and define
\[
D=K^+L_{\mathrm D},\qquad
d_c=\cos\alpha-\cos2\alpha>0.
\]
Here \(L_{\mathrm D}\) contains only the donor's nine internal
edges, embedded in the full eighteen-node matrix. Thus
\(B_2-B_1=-d_cD\), including the correct degree-three donor port.

All derivatives in this section are in the scaled clock. Direct
differentiation of the complete rows at time zero gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
x_k'&=\gamma f,& \theta_k'&=0,\\
x_k''&=-\gamma Af,& \theta_k''&=\gamma^2Af,\\
x_k'''&=\gamma A^2f-\gamma^3B_kAf,&
\theta_k'''&=-\gamma^2A^2f,\\
x_k''''&=-\gamma A^3f+
\gamma^3(AB_kA+B_kA^2)f.
\end{aligned} \tag{11}
\]
The terms containing a first phase derivative vanish here; nonlinear
phase derivatives have not otherwise been discarded.

For \(Y_k=e_b^{\mathsf T}x_k\), the first three derivatives are
class-independent. More explicitly,
\[
Y_k'=-\frac{\gamma\sigma}{3},\qquad
Y_k''=\frac{4\gamma\sigma}{9},\qquad
Y_k'''=-\frac{19\gamma\sigma}{27}
+\frac{\gamma^3\sigma}{27}(11\cos\alpha+8\cos\varphi).
\]
The receiver row of \(D\) vanishes. The relevant fine-row values are
\[
(Aq)_a=4/3,\qquad (Aq)_{a-1}=(Aq)_{a+1}=-1/2,\qquad
(DAq)_a=11/9,\qquad
e_b^{\mathsf T}ADAq=-11/27.
\]
Only the bridge entry \(A_{ba}=-1/3\) connects the receiver row to
the donor block. Equation (11) consequently proves
\[
Y_2^{(j)}(0)-Y_1^{(j)}(0)=0\quad(0\le j\le3),\qquad
Y_2''''(0)-Y_1''''(0)=\frac{11}{81}\gamma^3\sigma d_c. \tag{12}
\]
The class changes the donor's phase feedback before it changes the
receiver. Equal initial port currents therefore do not imply equal
later responses. For \(\sigma=0\), the ideal state is instead
stationary and the [central contact null](SINE_PATTERN_DYNAMICS.md#sine-formed-class-contact-admission)
applies.

## A correlated finite-horizon remainder

Rescale the comparison form as \(x=\gamma v\), set
\(\eta=\gamma^2\), and write \(y=\theta-\theta_k^0\). Then
\[
v'=-Av+F_k(y),\qquad y'=\eta Av,\qquad
F_k(y)=K^+S(\theta_k^0+y).
\]
The matrix \(A=I-P_{\mathrm{rw}}\) has a row-stochastic
neighbor matrix \(P_{\mathrm{rw}}\). Hence
\(H(t)=e^{-At}\) is nonnegative and row-stochastic, with
\(\|H(t)\|_\infty=1\). Globally,
\[
\|A\|_\infty,\|D\|_\infty,\|B_k\|_\infty\le2,\quad
\|F_k(y)-F_k(z)\|_\infty\le2\|y-z\|_\infty,
\]
and scalar sine Taylor remainder gives
\[
\|F_k(y)-f+B_ky\|_\infty\le2\|y\|_\infty^2. \tag{13}
\]
No acute-phase premise is needed for these response bounds.

Define the same first comparison for both classes:
\[
u(t)=\int_0^t H(t-s)f\,ds,\qquad
z(t)=\int_0^t Au(s)\,ds=tf-u(t).
\]
Variation of constants gives
\[
v_k(h)=u(h)-\eta\int_0^h H(h-t)B_kz(t)\,dt+R_k(h). \tag{14}
\]
For \(0<h\le1\), the fixed \(\eta\) satisfies
\(\eta h^2\le3/4\). A Volterra bootstrap using (13) yields, for
\(0\le t\le h\),
\[
\|v_k(t)\|_\infty\le\frac{2s}{3}t,\qquad
\|y_k(t)\|_\infty\le\frac{2\eta s}{3}t^2.
\]
Indeed, with \(V_h=\sup_{0<t\le h}\|v_k(t)\|_\infty/t\),
the integral equations imply
\(V_h\le s/3+(2\eta h^2/3)V_h\).
The same equations give
\[
\|v_k-u\|_\infty\le\frac{4\eta s}{9}t^3,\qquad
\|y_k-\eta z\|_\infty\le\frac{2\eta^2s}{9}t^4.
\]
Substituting these into (14), using (13), proves
\[
\|R_k(h)\|_\infty
\le\frac{4s+8s^2}{45}\eta^2h^5
\le\frac{4s}{15}\eta^2h^5. \tag{15}
\]
This controls the full nonlinear flow, including receiver backreaction.

Subtract (14) before bounding it. Its first-\(\eta\) contrast is
\(\eta d_c\int_0^hH(h-t)Dz(t)\,dt\). The absolutely convergent
matrix series for that integral is
\[
\sum_{p,q\ge0}
\frac{(-1)^{p+q}A^pDA^{q+1}f}{(p+q+3)!}\,
h^{p+q+3}. \tag{16}
\]
Every \(p=0\) term vanishes at the receiver. The \(p=1,q=0\)
term is exactly (12) divided by \(4!\) after restoring
\(\gamma\eta d_c\). For \(n=p+q\ge2\), there are \(n\)
remaining terms. Their receiver tail is at most
\[
\frac{s}{3}\sum_{n=2}^\infty
\frac{n\,2^{n+2}h^{n+3}}{(n+3)!}
\le\frac{4s\,h^5}{45(1-h/2)}. \tag{17}
\]
The successive positive-term ratio is
\(2h(n+1)/(n(n+4))\le h/2\).
The common support and common forcing have been retained in this
subtraction; two unrelated marginal remainder estimates would lose
the cancellation that identifies the class response.

Combining (15)--(17) gives the complete ideal-flow bound
\[
\boxed{
\left|Y_2(h)-Y_1(h)-
\frac{11}{1944}\gamma^3\sigma d_c h^4\right|
\le
\frac{4s\,d_c\gamma^3h^5}{45(1-h/2)}
+\frac{8s\gamma^5h^5}{15}.} \tag{18}
\]
The extra power of \(h\) in the tail distinguishes an actual
fourth-order class response from an unbounded formal derivative.

## Actual prepared states, observation errors and the disconnected control

Use the maximum norm on the full original \((x,\theta)\) state,
without rescaling the form error by \(\gamma\). The field in (4)
is globally Lipschitz with constant at most \(2+2\gamma<3\).
At contact, each ideal comparison differs from its actual joined
state by at most \(\epsilon\) in this norm, by (3).
For \(0<h<1/3\), the integral comparison therefore gives
\[
\sup_{0\le t\le h}
\|(x,\theta)_{\mathrm{actual}}-(x,\theta)_{\mathrm{ideal}}\|_\infty
\le\frac{\epsilon}{1-3h}. \tag{19}
\]
For example, taking the supremum in the integral inequality gives
\(E_h\le\epsilon+3hE_h\). This bound retains every initial
form and phase error in both components and all later feedback.
There is no ideal-target reset and no new state-error injection.

The two donor comparisons may have independent admitted preparation
errors. Their contrast error from (19) is at most
\(2\epsilon/(1-3h)\), and their readout errors contribute at most
\(2d\). In the frozen case \(\sigma=s>0\), a sufficient recorded
contrast lower bound is consequently
\[
\begin{aligned}
{\cal L}={}&\gamma^3s d_c h^4
\left[\frac{11}{1944}
-\frac{4h}{45(1-h/2)}
-\frac{8\gamma^2h}{15d_c}\right]\\
&-\frac{2\epsilon}{1-3h}-2d. \tag{20}
\end{aligned}
\]
No cancellation of preparation or measurement errors is assumed.

Removing the bridge leaves the receiver's complete field independent
of every donor coordinate. Thus a fixed actual receiver preparation
has exactly the same disconnected trajectory under either donor
label. For independently varying receiver preparations within the
admitted endpoint families, the disconnected recorded contrast
instead lies in the explicit control band
\[
\left|Y_{2,\mathrm{disc}}-Y_{1,\mathrm{disc}}\right|
\le\frac{2\epsilon}{1-3h}+2d. \tag{21}
\]
An exact null for matched states is not an assertion that all
independently noisy records coincide.
