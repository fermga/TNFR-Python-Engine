    The original receiver source across the admitted constitutive interval¶

    This owner admits the unchanged two-ramp source under every law in the fixed interval \(|\lambda|\le1/10000\), then compares its actual slow phase with the preserved sine reference enclosures. Source preparation, support, capacities, clocks and error allowances stay fixed. The comparison does not integrate a new trajectory or transfer the sine outcome through matching target derivatives. It rebuilds the changed-law source transport, velocity defect, residual and observation margins.

    \[ \Lambda=1/10000,\quad P(d)=\cos d(1-\cos9d)^2,\quad V_\lambda(d)=1-\cos d+\lambda P(d),\quad j_\lambda=V_\lambda',\quad |\lambda|\le\Lambda. \]

    \[ F_{\lambda,i}(\theta)=\tfrac14\sum_jw_{ij} j_\lambda(\theta_j-\theta_i), \qquad x_\tau=-Ax+\gamma F_\lambda(\theta),\quad \theta_\tau=\gamma Ax. \tag{1} \]

    Here \(\tau=(1023/1024)t\), capacities are held at one, and support and conductances are fixed. Both rows acquire the factor \(1023/1024\) in the original clock. Phase angles are measured in radians; form, time and storage retain this model's structural units, with no laboratory-unit bridge supplied. There is no forcing, event, reset or installed operator word. All form and phase coordinates remain, on the conserved global mean-zero leaf. The complete own-law storage obeys

    \[ H_\lambda=\tfrac12x^TLx+\sum_{\{i,j\}}w_{ij} V_\lambda(\theta_j-\theta_i),\qquad (H_\lambda)_\tau=-\tfrac14\|Lx\|^2. \tag{2} \]

    Keep the original primitive source

    Observations and decision scope¶

    Rebuild winding margins from the actual reference boxes and new full-state errors. Any newly certified finite nonacquisition concerns only the fixed source, law interval and horizon; it does not decide later formation or regeneration.     Result: uniform finite receiver nonacquisition¶

    The fixed eight-cone comparison closes all 76 prospective domains. Its terminal full-source phase radius satisfies

    \[ e_{76}<287/1000, \qquad e_{76}\ \text{is approximately }0.286189004. \tag{23} \]

    \[ w_2(\tau)=0\quad\text{for every original admitted source, every }|\lambda|\le1/10000, \quad 0\le\tau\le400+\frac{19}{2\gamma^2}. \tag{24} \]

    Thus the receiver has not acquired winding one anywhere in this entire finite interval. In particular, receiver acquisition with continuously retained donors and joined winding-one capture does not occur within this horizon for the admitted source and constitutive family.

    The donor endpoint decision is separate. The new ball is wider than the endpoint radius permitting a robust winding-zero reading for either donor. Both corresponding sufficient branch margins are below \(-349/1000\). Their negativity makes this changed-law donor observation unavailable; it proves neither retention nor loss. The original whole-source donor-loss conclusion at \(\lambda=0\) remains valid from its narrower, separately preserved enclosure. It is not extended to all \(\lambda\) by the present comparison.
