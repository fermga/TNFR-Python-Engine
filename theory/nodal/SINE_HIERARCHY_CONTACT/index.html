    Hierarchy-to-contact composition under an explicit intermediate law¶

    This owner studies actual THOL birth followed by parent-target UM. The isolated prefix uses an explicitly supplied frozen-isolate completion; the connected sine reader is not silently widened. The result concerns a conditional mechanism on an open connected-state family, not the formation or maintenance of the previously studied winding organizations.

    The research program owns status. The hierarchy owner, actual UM owner and native birth/transport comparison retain their contracts. Their laws and earlier finite responses are not substituted for the model below.

    1. Protocol fixed before event evaluation¶

    \[ \frac{31}{64}<A=x_0<\frac{33}{64},\quad \frac{47}{64}<B=x_1<\frac{49}{64},\quad \frac{31}{16}<\nu_0,\nu_1<\frac{33}{16},\quad \theta_0=\gamma,\quad\theta_1=\gamma+\phi,\quad \frac7{64}<\phi<\frac9{64}. \tag{1} \]

    The complete word is OZ -> THOL -> UM -> SHA, always targeting only the original parent. Use initialized nonzero form, supplied AL,IL history and zero initial debt; validate the whole word and every live prefix with the core grammar and GRAMMAR_REJECTION_MODE="raise".     2. Prefix law and the connected restriction¶

    On each connected component with at least two nodes, retain the selected weighted-form/bare-phase completion

    \[ q=L_Wx,\quad S_i=\sum_{j\sim i}\sin(\theta_j-\theta_i),\quad m_i=\frac{\nu_i}{\pi d_i},\qquad p_i=\frac{S_i}{\pi d_i},\qquad \dot x_i=m_iS_i,\quad\dot\theta_i=m_iq_i. \tag{2} \]

    Support is finite, simple, loop-free and undirected. Conductances and held capacities are nonnegative; the constructive family has positive ones. There is no forcing or continuous loss. Bare degree counts edges, including zero-conductance support; it is not weighted strength. At every isolate explicitly declare p=xdot=thetadot=0, held form, phase and capacity. This is the supplied frozen-isolate completion, not a value assigned to the undefined quotient 0/0 in (2).

    The shared detached owner keeps bound_relational_sine_support_exchange restricted to connected support and exposes the distinct opt-in bound_relational_sine_frozen_isolates comparison. Such a report supplies instantaneous fields for the declared law; it installs no integrator, event selector or autonomous hierarchy mechanism.

    Use edge-only storage

    \[ H=\frac12\sum_{\{i,j\}}W_{ij}(x_i-x_j)^2 +\sum_{\{i,j\}}[1-\cos(\theta_j-\theta_i)]. \tag{3} \]

    Each nontrivial component conserves H under (2); isolates contribute no term. An intrinsic child storage would change the birth budget and is outside this completion. With positive capacities, inverse-capacity means are defined on each nontrivial component using rho_i=d_i/nu_i. Frozen child coordinates are independent constants; assigning them zero mean weight merely ignores them and is not a complete observation of the enlarged state.

    This declaration admits the original graph, the actual isolated birth prefix and the connected UM successor under one explicit piecewise state-domain contract. On the final connected support its fields are exactly those of (2). No elapsed evolution is inserted between events by this admission theorem.

    5. Complete event work, degree changes and mean leaves¶

    For a general admitted UM stage that preserves form, let F be its actual set of newly committed edges, E the old support, and theta^+ its complete phase endpoint, including old-neighbor writes. Old conductances are unchanged. The exact storage jump is

    \[ \begin{split} H^+-H^-={}&\frac12\sum_{\{i,j\}\in F}\omega_{ij}(x_i-x_j)^2\\ &+\sum_{\{i,j\}\in E} [\cos(\theta_j-\theta_i)-\cos(\theta_j^+-\theta_i^+)]\\ &+\sum_{\{i,j\}\in F}[1-\cos(\theta_j^+-\theta_i^+)]. \end{split} \tag{11} \]

    Ignoring the middle line would omit bidirectional UM's work on old edges. Capacity is absent from this storage, so its reset has no additional term. THOL birth has zero edge-storage jump under the explicitly selected isolate convention; this is not a claim of zero physical creation cost.

    For the source family, (11) becomes

    \[ \Delta H_{\rm UM}=\frac12\omega D^2 +\cos\phi-\cos(7\phi/8)+1-\cos(\phi/16). \tag{12} \]

    The old-edge phase term is negative. Nevertheless (6), (9), phi<3/20, and 1-cos(phi)<=phi^2/2 give

    \[ \Delta H_{\rm UM}> \frac25\left(\frac7{25}\right)^2- \frac12\left(\frac3{20}\right)^2 =\frac{2011}{100000}>\frac1{50}. \tag{13} \]

    The final SHA leaves H unchanged. OZ and refreshed THOL also leave the old edge storage unchanged. Thus the complete word's positive storage jump is accounted for by (12), not claimed as energy conservation or an unbudgeted autonomous gain. An event reservoir or selection law has not been derived.

    Degree normalization changes both rows. For a general old node, define a_i=sum_{new j}omega_ij*(x_i-x_j), and let Delta S_i include the phase change on old edges and all new sine terms. With m_i^+=nu_i^+/(pi*d_i^+),

    \[ \dot x_i^+-\dot x_i=(m_i^+-m_i)S_i+m_i^+\Delta S_i, \qquad \dot\theta_i^+-\dot\theta_i=(m_i^+-m_i)q_i+m_i^+a_i. \tag{14} \]

    These are fresh-law comparisons, not the raw pressure-relief map. For a former isolate use its explicitly zero old rows and the full new rows; there is no old nu/(pi*0) to substitute into (14).

    The old P2 means use weights (1/nu_0,1/nu_1) and total R. Let mu_x and mu_theta denote those means before birth. Immediately after UM set r_0=2/n, r_1=1/nu_1, r_c=1/nu_c, and R^+=r_0+r_1+r_c. Then

    \[ \mu_x^+-\mu_x= \frac{(r_0-1/\nu_0)(A-\mu_x)+r_c(z-\mu_x)}{R^+}, \tag{15} \]
    \[ \mu_\theta^+-\mu_\theta= \frac{(r_0-1/\nu_0)(\gamma-\mu_\theta) +(r_0-r_1)\delta+r_c(\gamma-\mu_\theta)}{R^+}. \tag{16} \]

    The new child's weight and the changed parent degree/capacity are all retained. The isolated prefix did not already possess this connected mean. For the final SHA endpoint replace r_0 by 4/n; all other terms are unchanged. Each ensuing fixed-support segment conserves its own new means, not the old metric's values. Phase means require the declared continuous lifts.

    6. A dynamically active child and its disconnected null¶

    Let k=1 immediately after UM and k=1/2 after parent-only SHA, so the parent capacity is k*n. Equations (7), (10) give the child's full rows

    \[ \dot x_c=\frac{\nu_c}{\pi}\sin\delta>0,\qquad \dot\theta_c=-\frac{\nu_c\omega D}{\pi}<0. \tag{17} \]

    The instantaneous form acceleration follows by differentiating the declared two-row law with held support/capacity, not by inferring it from telemetry:

    \[ \ddot x_c= \frac{\nu_c\cos\delta}{\pi^2} \left[\frac{k n}{2}(-u+\omega D)+\nu_c\omega D\right]. \tag{18} \]

    For both k values, dropping a nonnegative contribution gives

    \[ \frac{k n}{2}(-u+\omega D)+\nu_c\omega D >\frac95\frac45\frac7{25}-\frac{21}{20}\frac3{10} =\frac{441}{5000}. \tag{19} \]

    Here nu_c>9/5, n<21/10, u<3/10 and D>7/25. With cos(delta)>99/100 and pi^2<10,

    \[ \ddot x_c> \frac{(9/5)(99/100)(441/5000)}{10} =\frac{392931}{25000000}>\frac3{200}. \tag{20} \]

    The parent phase gradient need not be positive throughout the source box; (19) does not assume it. The child's negative phase rate and the resulting relative phase velocity suffice. This accounts for the actual UM parent capacity blend and for its later SHA reduction.

    In the disconnected null, the child construction, phase and capacity are the same, and the UM phase/capacity/pressure proposals on old nodes are identical: functional candidates are not members of that snapshot's old neighborhood. Disabling only functional links therefore leaves the child isolated under the declared extension. Its form rate, phase rate and form acceleration are identically zero. A stale prebirth candidate inventory is a second nonattachment control, not a proof that the live-inventory protocol fails.

    This discriminates a connected dynamical degree of freedom from an added hierarchy record. It does not establish that the enlarged system is a persistent composite, that the old organization is retained, or that nature selects either event. Rates and local derivatives provide no finite-time response amplitude or lifetime without further analysis.
