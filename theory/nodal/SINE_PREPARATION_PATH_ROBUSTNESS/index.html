    Constitutive robustness of the source-specific winding selector¶

    Every member of the declared matched-potential family acquires the same winding-one identity from the original uncertain Cayley corridor. This is a uniform statement for all preparation parameters \(s\in[3/4,4/5]\), all admitted latent errors and all fixed constitutive parameters \(\lambda\in[0,1]\). It is proved from each law's own source transport, current ordering, curvature and full storage. Matching the targets or their tangents alone would not imply this result.

    The sine selector supplies the two-stage argument. The constitutive family supplies the alternative complete laws. This owner joins those mechanisms only after re-admitting their hypotheses. No trajectory, source sweep, coefficient producer or frozen response is evaluated.

    Complete laws and the retained identity¶

    Use one isolated unit C9, signed form \(x\), primitive phase lifts \(\theta\), degree matrix \(D=2I\), unnormalized Laplacian \(L\) and \(A=L/2\). Hold capacities at one and retain the same structural clocks and coefficients:

    \[ e=1023/1024,\qquad \tau=et,\qquad \gamma=1/(1023\pi),\qquad \eta=\gamma^2. \tag{1} \]

    For each fixed \(\lambda\in[0,1]\), the potential and current are

    \[ \begin{aligned} V_\lambda(\delta)&=1-\cos\delta+\lambda W(\delta),& W(\delta)&=\frac{(1-\cos9\delta)^2}{6\,9^4},\\ j_\lambda(\delta)&=\sin\delta+ \frac{\lambda}{2187} \left(\sin9\delta-\tfrac12\sin18\delta\right),& f_{\lambda,i}(\theta)&=\tfrac12\sum_{j\sim i} j_\lambda(\theta_j-\theta_i). \end{aligned}\tag{2} \]

    The actual complete rows and storage balance are

    \[ \begin{aligned} x'&=-Ax+\gamma f_\lambda(\theta),& \theta'&=\gamma Ax,\\ H_\lambda&=\tfrac12x^TLx+ \sum_{\{i,j\}}V_\lambda(\theta_j-\theta_i),& H_\lambda'&=-\tfrac12\|Lx\|_2^2. \end{aligned}\tag{3} \]

    Primes here denote \(\tau\). Both original-time rows are multiplied by \(e\), as is the loss. The first row independently supplies the pressure for the unit-capacity nodal equation. Both ordinary means remain zero. Support, capacity, loss, clock and source are supplied premises; there are no events, controllers, coefficient changes or state resets.

    The organization is the same winding identity as in the sine proof: full constituent form and phase remain available, and a phase cell with an admitted full-storage margin is forward trapped. The conclusion concerns acquisition on the supplied substrate. It does not derive constituent birth, a support selector, an autonomous preparation, a collective event or realization of the thirteen operator contracts. The execution path is a detached continuous-law proof, not an operator word or the native relational runtime.

    Sufficient constitutive conditions for the mechanism¶

    For an even periodic potential \(V\) and odd current \(j=V'\), the same reciprocal rows admit the mixed coordinate

    \[ z=\gamma x,\qquad y=\theta+z,\qquad \sigma=\eta(\tau-200),\qquad y_\sigma=f(y-z)=f(y)+r. \tag{4} \]

    This is an identity on the actual full solution; \(y\) is not a replacement primitive phase. The following are sufficient conditions, not claimed necessary properties of every selector or every TNFR law:

    1. Complete source transport. A globally admitted field with \(\|f\|_D\le F\), \(D\)-Lipschitz constant \(L_f\), the declared spectral gap and the original correlated source gives a uniform future form bound \(Z\), hence \(\|r\|_D\le\varepsilon=L_fZ\). Its own mixed initialization must satisfy the source cone below.
    2. Passage ordering. The current increases on the ordinary gap interval. With \(k(q)=j(\pi-q)\), its actual closing current, require \(j(m)-k(q)>3/10+\varepsilon\) whenever the proof has \(-7/4\le q\le63/100\), \(m\ge0.899+q/2>0\), and the admitted ordinary gaps. This closes the cone and bounds the passage time.
    3. Finite contraction. On the subsequent chart \(|\delta|\le7/5\), require \(V''\ge\mu>0\), an admitted contraction rate \(\alpha\le\mu/5\), and enough finite time to lower the full state storage. The bounded residual must keep both \(y\) and the actual \(\theta=y-z\) in this chart during that stage.
    4. Permanent storage protection. The actual reciprocal loss must hold, with an upper bound on complete reached storage below every face of the enclosing winding-one cell. Uniqueness of its critical relative phase geometry must be proved for this current. Positive curvature over the entire open acute cell is one sufficient condition for that uniqueness.

    The retained source correlations, both means and the full form energy are part of these conditions. The following sections verify them uniformly for (2). They do not infer them from equilibrium agreement.

    The numerical budgets coincide with those of the sine proof because these new bounds suffice, not because the old deadline transfers automatically. They remain conservative structural times without a laboratory unit bridge.

    What this robustness establishes and leaves free¶

    The captured identity agrees throughout this already declared law family on the entire original uncertain source corridor. This particular formation observation consequently does not discriminate \(\lambda\). The laws still have different currents, transient histories and higher nonlinear fields. Their nominal distinct-neighbor interaction onset can even have opposite signs. That separate interaction theorem retains its own joined support, nominal preparation, supplied inputs and local time scope; acquisition here does not install its contact or replace reached states with nominal targets.

    The result is conditional robustness over a bounded class of complete laws. It establishes neither one attractor shared by all TNFR dynamics, absence of fixed natural laws, unique constitutive selection nor physical identity. The earlier 57-state sine critical census is neither transferred to \(\lambda>0\) nor needed here: convergence follows from this law's own acute Hessian, loss and means after full-storage trapping. Other source-path intervals remain unresolved. Capture at (26) means a protected acute identity cell; it does not assert the older \(1/12\) radius, a specified recovery precision, contact readiness or a closed collective response at that deadline.

    The private own-law calculator re-admits primitive deformation/source intervals and error budgets, reuses the shared exact source geometry, and evaluates the bounds above. Its independent controls check current reflection, source errors, uniform margins, full storage, representation boundaries and explicit abstention when sufficient conditions fail. An incoming report, cached verdict or sine transit result cannot replace an actual-law premise. The execution plan alone owns subsequent research admission.
