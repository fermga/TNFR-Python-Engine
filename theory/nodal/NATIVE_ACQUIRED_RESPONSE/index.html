    Acquired native organization and a regional response¶

    Status: conditional analytical separation for one prescribed intervention and observation under the complete two-bridge native law. The reference jet has one exact symbolic cross-check. The acquired-state theorem in sections 4--5 is qualitative. The local finite certificate adds explicit amplitude, readout, full-state and reading-error budgets, with an exact scalar audit. Finite entry of the actual acquisition sources into those target domains remains unquantified. These results establish neither physical identification nor an executed nonlinear dynamic-capacity simulator. The program owns the current task.

    1. One law, two acquired organizations¶

    Use DA1 with kappa=1, e=w=1/2, beta=1, unit conductances, cycles (0,1,2,3,4) and (5,6,7,8,9), and both bridges (0,5),(1,6). Write B=D-A_G for the ordinary Laplacian, N=diag(nu) and retain all ten signed forms, circular phases and positive capacities on sum(nu)=10. The complete structural-time law is

    \[ \dot x=N(-D^{-1}Bx/2+g(\theta)/2),\qquad \dot\theta=H(\theta)^{-1}NBx/2,\qquad \dot\nu=-B\nu/2. \tag{AR1} \]

    The native source owner defines g,H on the regular domain: each relative neighbor resultant avoids the nonpositive real axis. Pressure and metric are refreshed from the full state. No held-capacity, held-pressure, sine or reduced-witness law replaces (AR1). There are no inputs or events during its continuous segments.

    The reference organizations have uniform form and capacity one. Consensus has constant phase; the aligned twist has theta_i=2*pi*(i mod 5)/5 up to one common origin. This is the same phase orbit as DA3. Their full-state invariant attracting neighborhoods are established by DA2 and CS6. Only common form and common phase origins are quotient symmetries; the joined rings do not have independent origins.

    DA supplies initially winding-zero states acquiring the twist. The dynamic branch of W/RB supplies initially winding-one states reaching consensus. Both use (AR1). Their preparations differ; the held branch of the selection comparison is not used here. The proof below consumes the retained convergence results, without replaying either transit producer or substituting an equilibrium at its finite capture checkpoint.

    2. Probe, observation and supplied work¶

    Fix one idealized intervention and one receiver reading:

    \[ P_\epsilon(x,\theta,\nu)=(x+\epsilon e_0,\theta,\nu),\qquad O(x,\theta,\nu)=x_5-x_7. \tag{AR2} \]

    The same positive amplitude is applied at the same labeled donor port in both comparisons. The readout contrasts its directly connected receiver port with a receiver nonport. With Phi_t the full flow, the probe effect is

    \[ R(s,\epsilon,t)=O(\Phi_t(P_\epsilon s))-O(\Phi_t(s)). \tag{AR3} \]

    Each control starts from exactly the same reached state as its own probed branch. Thus ongoing relaxation is subtracted. This operation preserves phase, capacity and support at the instant of intervention, then all rows resume. No regional alignment or capacity reset is performed.

    The endpoint storage identity for S=x^TBx/2+V_phi gives

    \[ \Delta S=\epsilon(Bx)_0+\frac32\epsilon^2. \tag{AR4} \]

    The degree of node zero is three. At either exact reference the first term vanishes; at an acquired state it must be retained. In a relative form neighborhood of radius delta_x, its magnitude is at most 2*sqrt(3)*epsilon*delta_x, since ||B e_0||=2*sqrt(3) and B*1=0. Equal amplitude therefore does not assert equal work at arbitrary reached states. The intervention is supplied, with its storage exchange exposed; it is not an autonomous occurrence law or automatically a runtime operator. Storage has the model's structural normalization, not an assigned SI energy.

    3. Exact finite-kick jets at the critical references¶

    Let gamma be the ring-edge cosine, equal to one at consensus and (sqrt(5)-1)/4 at the twist, and put h=1+2*gamma. On this actual graph,

    \[ K=\gamma B_{\rm cyc}+L_{05}+L_{16},\quad H_i=\begin{cases}\pi h&i\in\{0,1,5,6\},\\ 2\pi\gamma&\text{otherwise}.\end{cases} \tag{AR5} \]

    Here L_ij=(e_i-e_j)(e_i-e_j)^T. These are the DA4 geometry, or the CS6 consensus specialization K=B,H=pi*D. The critical-point identity is Dg=-H^-1*K.

    Define b=e_0, c=e_5^T-e_7^T and

    \[ A=-D^{-1}B/2,\qquad C=-H^{-1}K/2,\qquad Q=H^{-1}B/2. \tag{AR6} \]

    At a reference state after the finite kick, phase remains critical and capacity remains uniformly one. Consequently nudot=0, xdot=epsilon*A*b and thetadot=epsilon*Q*b. The form row is affine in form and depends on phase through g; its derivative in phase is C at this phase. Its capacity derivative is multiplied by nudot=0. Therefore, exactly at t=0,

    \[ R(0)=0,\qquad R'(0)=\epsilon cAb,\qquad R''(0)=\epsilon c(A^2+CQ)b. \tag{AR7} \]

    This establishes the finite-kick identity separately from the tangent matrix product. It makes no assertion that higher derivatives or finite-time nonlinear flow equal a tangent solution. At reached states, capacity and D(H^-1) contributions remain in the full flow used below.

    For a direct check, Bb has entries 3,-1,-1,-1 at nodes 0,1,4,5 and zero elsewhere. Hence cAb=1/6 and cA^2b=-1/6. Put v=H^-1*Bb. Only v_0=3/(pi*h) and v_5=-1/(pi*h) contribute to row five of Kv; rows six and nine of v vanish. Thus (Kv)_5=-(h+3)/(pi*h), while (Kv)_7=0. It follows that

    \[ cCQb=\frac{h+3}{4\pi^2h^2}. \tag{AR8} \]

    The complete reference results are

    Reference R'(0)/epsilon R''(0)/epsilon
    Consensus 1/6 -1/6+1/(6*pi^2)
    Aligned twist 1/6 -1/6+(4-sqrt(5))/(4*pi^2)

    In particular,

    \[ R''_{\rm tw}(0)-R''_{\rm con}(0)=\epsilon a,\qquad a=\frac{10-3\sqrt5}{12\pi^2}>0. \tag{AR9} \]

    The exact symbolic evaluator independently returns (sqrt(5)+15)/(12*pi^2*(sqrt(5)+3)) for a, the same algebraic number. Positivity follows from 3*sqrt(5)<10. The common transport contribution cancels in the contrast; the difference comes from the phase geometry's metric and stiffness in the same law. This is not a conserved scalar label or a claim that winding alone predicts every response.

    4. A finite response while identity is retained¶

    Choose invariant attracting neighborhoods W_tw,W_con from DA2/CS6, in local quotient coordinates (u,v,z) on the mean-one leaf, using an orthonormal basis U of 1^perp for each relative channel. Choose a fixed sufficiently small epsilon>0 so both reference points and their shifted points lie in their respective open neighborhoods. The probe shifts the form quotient by epsilon*U^T*e_0; it need not conserve arithmetic form mean. Both trajectories then remain regular and recover their original phase organization for all future time.

    For this fixed amplitude, smoothness and (AR7)-(AR9) give

    \[ R_{\rm tw}(\epsilon,t)-R_{\rm con}(\epsilon,t) =\frac{\epsilon a}{2}t^2+o(t^2). \tag{AR10} \]

    There is therefore a positive h_0(epsilon) such that, for any fixed 0<h<h_0(epsilon), the actual nonlinear reference gap d satisfies

    \[ d=R_{\rm tw}(\epsilon,h)-R_{\rm con}(\epsilon,h) \ge\epsilon a h^2/4>0. \tag{AR11} \]

    These are finite positive choices, not a measurement of an exact derivative. The argument supplies existence, not a numerical value of h_0 or an explicit Taylor remainder bound.

    5. Transfer to actual acquisition with evolving capacity¶

    Keep those same fixed epsilon,h. The observation, probe and full flow are continuous on a sufficiently small neighborhood of each reference orbit. They respect the two common-origin symmetries, so the following argument can be made in local quotient coordinates without changing any reached state.

    Choose open target neighborhoods V_j, for j=tw,con, small enough that both V_j and P_epsilon(V_j) lie inside W_j, and

    \[ |R(s,\epsilon,h)-R(s_j^*,\epsilon,h)|<d/4 \quad\text{for all }s\in V_j. \tag{AR12} \]

    This continuity bound covers form, phase and capacity together. It is not an equilibrium-jet substitution at s. For arbitrary independent reached states s_tw in V_tw and s_con in V_con,

    \[ R(s_{\rm tw},\epsilon,h)-R(s_{\rm con},\epsilon,h) >d/2\ge\epsilon a h^2/8>0. \tag{AR13} \]

    Different residual capacities or preparation details within these sets cannot erase the separation. They are not asserted equal, irrelevant in general, or inferable from one scalar reading. Both probed and unprobed branches retain their identity and converge to their respective phase orbits.

    Now use convergence of the actual DA reference and the dynamic W reference. There is a common finite waiting time T, later than both retained capture checkpoints, at which each lies strictly inside its corresponding V_j. Their prior segments are compact and regular. Finite-transit continuous dependence pulls V_j back to open initial neighborhoods around each source. Shrink them to retain positive mean-one capacities and the original source windings: zero for DA, one for W. Every member of these smaller source sets is regular up to T, reaches its own V_j and obeys (AR13) after the same intervention at T. This does not establish a uniform time for the entirety of the previously admitted acquisition basins.

    The twist source neighborhood contains an open nonempty subset with nonuniform capacity; the W reference is already nonuniform. Remove the uniform slice in the first set and shrink the second if needed. Since

    \[ \nu(t)-\mathbf1=e^{-Bt/2}(\nu(0)-\mathbf1), \tag{AR14} \]

    invertibility at finite time and positivity of B on 1^perp imply that both the capacity contrast and its full derivative remain nonzero for such sources at every finite time. The probe leaves capacity unchanged. The separation thus holds on nonempty open families with genuinely evolving capacities, without freezing or resetting them. This does not prove that capacity transport is necessary for the response distinction.

    7. An explicit local response-and-retention certificate¶

    This is a conditional postacquisition certificate for (AR1)--(AR3), not an executed source-to-response trajectory. Fix

    \[ \epsilon=2^{-12},\qquad h=2^{-18},\qquad \delta=\eta=2^{-64}. \tag{AR15} \]

    For each organization, choose its common form/phase origins and retain all thirty coordinates with initial sup-distance less than delta from its reference, on the positive mean-one capacity leaf. Each actual state has its own unprobed control. Allow an independent absolute error at most eta in each of the four final readings. The readout duration is the exact dyadic structural time h; this certificate includes no clock uncertainty or laboratory-time identification. These fixed conditions imply a recorded twist-minus-consensus response contrast strictly greater than 2^-56. Both organizations retain their identity and recover after the probe.

    The state allowance is about 64 binary fractional digits in local coordinates; it is stringent but representable within a 128-bit local data budget. Common origins must be handled exactly or with their effect included in the reading budget. This is a finite mathematical prediction contract, not a demonstrated preparation accuracy or a validated full-state integrator implementation.

    A regular full-state tube and derivative bounds¶

    Put r=1/64. Consider the convex lifted sup-tube of radius r about either reference, with N_max=1+r. Write C_i+i S_i=sum_j exp(i(theta_j-theta_i)). At a twist nonport its real part is (sqrt(5)-1)/2>3/5, and at a port it is (sqrt(5)+1)/2>8/5; consensus resultants are larger. Phase perturbations change the real part by at most 2*d_i*r. Thus throughout both tubes,

    \[ C_i>\min(3/5-4r,8/5-6r)>1/2. \tag{AR16} \]

    Reuse the smooth positive-real representation in RB7:

    \[ g_i=\pi^{-1}\operatorname{Im}\log(C_i+iS_i),\qquad k_i=H_i^{-1}=\pi^{-1}\int_0^1\operatorname{Re}(C_i+i tS_i)^{-1}\,dt. \tag{AR17} \]

    All derivative norms below are induced by the coordinate sup norm, with independent unit directions for a bilinear derivative. For the resultant, the first and second phase derivatives have magnitude at most 2*d_i<=6 and 4*d_i<=12. The real-linear map z -> Re(z)+i*t*Im(z) contracts complex magnitude for 0<=t<=1; its real part remains greater than 1/2. Differentiating the logarithm and inverse, using pi>3, gives

    \[ \|Dg_i\|<4,\quad \|D^2g_i\|<56,\quad 0<k_i<2/3,\quad\|Dk_i\|<8,\quad\|D^2k_i\|<208. \tag{AR18} \]

    For example the second inverse bound is [2*6^2/(1/2)^3+12/(1/2)^2]/3=208; the logarithm uses [12/(1/2)+6^2/(1/2)^2]/3=56. These smooth formulas also cover S_i=0. Because g_i vanishes at the reference, |g_i|<4r, while |q_i|<=6r for q=Bx. The complete rows are F_x=nu*(-q/(2d)+g/2), F_theta=nu*k*q/2, F_nu=-B*nu/2. Their Jacobian bounds are respectively

    \[ J_x\le3N_{\max}+3r<4,\qquad J_\theta\le2N_{\max}+24N_{\max}r+2r<4,\qquad J_\nu\le3. \tag{AR19} \]

    The full second derivative bounds, including mixed capacity directions, are

    \[ M_x\le28N_{\max}+6<64,\qquad M_\theta\le624N_{\max}r+48N_{\max}+4+48r<64, \qquad M_\nu=0. \tag{AR20} \]

    For M_theta, these four terms are respectively the phase/phase, form/phase, form/capacity and phase/capacity contributions. The factor two in each mixed term is retained. These are bounds on the actual full field, not on a frozen-capacity tangent or a copied/reflected invariant slice.

    Finite readout, all residuals and four reading errors¶

    Let G=1/(1-4h). The positive exponential series gives exp(4h)<=G<2. Up to any first exit from a tube, Gronwall bounds distance from its reference by its initial distance times G. Since (epsilon+delta)*G<r, no exit occurs before h. This applies to every probed and unprobed actual branch, as well as the two reference probe branches. Capacity evolves throughout.

    On either reference probe branch, ||F||_infinity<8*epsilon=1/512. For an autonomous complete field the third solution derivative is

    \[ s'''=D^2F[F,F]+DF\,DF\,F. \tag{AR21} \]

    Consequently each coordinate has third derivative magnitude less than 64*(1/512)^2+16/512=129/4096<1/16. Each receiver contrast has norm two. The unprobed reference branches are stationary, so for the difference D of the two reference responses, |D'''|<1/4. Equation (AR9), with sqrt(5)<9/4 and pi<4, gives a>13/768>1/64. Taylor's integral remainder therefore yields

    \[ D(h)>\epsilon h^2/128-h^3/24. \tag{AR22} \]

    For any actual initial state in its admitted target domain, compare both of its flows with the corresponding reference flows. The same convex tube and full Jacobian bound give a distance at most G*delta for each comparison. There are four flows and each observation has norm two. Thus residual form, phase and capacity together cost at most 8*G*delta. Four independent reading errors add at most 4*eta. The recorded response contrast obeys

    \[ \widehat R_{\rm tw}-\widehat R_{\rm con} >\epsilon h^2/128-h^3/24-8G\delta-4\eta >2^{-56}>0. \tag{AR23} \]

    No equality of initial transmitted drifts is assumed at the actual states. Their capacity-dependent drift differences are covered by the full-flow error bound. Nor are shared offsets or independently prepared residuals silently cancelled.
