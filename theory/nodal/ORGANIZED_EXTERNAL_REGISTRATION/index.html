This admission separates the finite response envelope from that particular alignment, retaining the same actual acquired and observed sources. It does not create an alignment selector or replace the complete state with an instantaneous boundary moment.

    1. Prepared family, sufficient state and symmetry¶

    Use the complete 54-node support, both boundary channels and the storage balance from the external-contact owner. With the unnormalized joined Laplacian L, the complete equations are

    \[ x'=(-Lx+\gamma S(\theta))/4,\qquad \theta'=\gamma Lx/4,\qquad \gamma=\frac1{1023\pi}. \tag{1} \]

    Prime denotes elapsed contact time in the declared tau clock. Unit capacity, the uniform quarter coefficient, full-circle sine law and contact support remain supplied. In particular the seven-neighbor external constituent is not normalized by its degree. Both rows transform under the original clock. All 54 signed forms and 54 phase coordinates remain state variables; their relative preparations contain the same 96 independent error directions.

    Fix one actual host source and one actual independently recovered external source. At contact write

    \[ \begin{split} \theta_{H,c,j}(0)&=\psi_j+H_{c,j},& \theta_{D,j}(0)&=\psi_j+\beta+z_{D,j},\\ \psi_j&=2\pi(j-4)/9,& Q&=\frac19\sum_{c=0}^4\sum_{j=0}^8 e^{i(z_{D,j}-H_{c,j})}. \end{split}\tag{2} \]

    Here H includes the actual carrier endpoint, all transported fine errors and continuous phase motion during the complete observation word. The final commanded observation offset is zero, but that motion and form backaction are retained. The donor residual satisfies its original recovered bounds; no ideal source or observation-based reconstruction replaces either state.

    The parameter beta is supplied at the donor's primitive preparation. Common phase translation is an exact symmetry of its isolated complete law, so every member of this family has the same donor forms and relative phase trajectory at contact. No extra waiting, freezing or phase reset occurs. Preparation start times have the same synchronization as the earlier contact. Once joined, rotating the donor alone changes the contact law's evaluated state and is not a symmetry of the complete field.

    The joined total form is zero. With the stated lift convention its phase total is 9*beta, since both unshifted component phase totals vanish. These correlations are conserved by (1). Replacing beta by a full turn changes this lift total without changing circular state or response. Retaining the lift convention is necessary when reporting means.

    For the initial contact sine moment, same-slot carrier differences cancel:

    \[ \mathcal A_\beta(0)=\frac19\sum_{c,j} \sin(\theta_{D,j}(0)-\theta_{H,c,j}(0)) =\operatorname{Im}(e^{i\beta}Q). \tag{3} \]

    Consequently \(|Q|\le5\) and its value is unchanged when only this supplied rigid origin changes. A different reference origin merely rotates the two quadratures or reparametrizes beta. This is invariance within the declared prepared family, not invariance under changing the interface, internal configuration, donor source or complete law. Off the carrier, Q consumes actual same-slot phase correlations, not just child-origin means.

    2. Uniform finite response under the complete law¶

    Let \(R_\beta(t)=\frac19\sum_jx_{D,j}(t)\) and let \(\mathcal A_\beta(t)\) be the contact sine moment along its actual joined solution. The two reciprocal contact rows and the exact zero total form give

    \[ R_\beta'=-\frac32R_\beta-\frac\gamma4\mathcal A_\beta, \qquad R_\beta(0)=0. \tag{4} \]

    This identity holds for the actual fine state, not only on the invariant twelve-coordinate carrier. The donor reaction, host backreaction and all internal motion remain in the solution that defines the moment.

    Write \(q=10^{-70}\), \(g\ge\gamma\) for the shared outward upper bound, \(X=1/100\), \(T=1/5000\) and \(h=1/1000\). Rebuilding the preceding observation proof gives

    \[ B_o=X+gT,\qquad m=2gB_oT,\qquad B=B_o+\frac74gh. \tag{5} \]

    At contact each host form has magnitude at most B_o and each donor form at most q. The joined heat semigroup contracts the maximum norm and each sine sum has magnitude at most seven. Thus every beta has \(\|x(t)\|_\infty\le B\) for \(0\le t\le h\). The complete phase row bounds a host phase rate by \(5gB/2\) and a donor phase rate by \(7gB/2\). Each contact gap changes by at most \(6gBt\), so

    \[ |\mathcal A_\beta(t)-\mathcal A_\beta(0)|\le30gBt. \tag{6} \]

    Neither estimate consumes the initial origin. From (4), its nonnegative damping kernel and \(|\mathcal A_\beta|\le5\), one obtains \(|R_\beta(t)|\le5gt/4\). Integrating (4) and retaining both damping and phase motion therefore gives, uniformly over the whole prepared family,

    \[ \begin{split} R_\beta(h)-R_\beta(0) &=-\frac{\gamma h}{4}\operatorname{Im}(e^{i\beta}Q)+r_\beta,\\ |r_\beta|&\le E:=\frac{15}{16}gh^2+\frac{15}{4}g^2Bh^2. \end{split}\tag{7} \]

    The first remainder term bounds \((3/2)\int_0^h|R_\beta(t)|dt\), and the second bounds \((\gamma/4)\int_0^h|\mathcal A_\beta(t)-\mathcal A_\beta(0)|dt\). Their derivation uses the actual joined law, rather than transferring a verdict from beta=pi/2. The finite response need not be an exact sinusoid: the remainder can depend on beta through the nonlinear trajectory.

    3. Whole-source quadratures and amplitude bounds¶

    Re-admit the twelve original nonnegative source allowances, each at most \(10^{-10}\), and rebuild acquisition, recovery and sequential observation. Let P_c be the carrier phase interval at host time 200 and let Z be its rebuilt fine phase bound. The contacted actual coordinates satisfy

    \[ H_{c,j}\in P_c+[-Z-m,Z+m],\qquad |z_{D,j}|\le q. \tag{8} \]

    For outer zero use P_c={0}, with that source's own transported residual. Equal original error allowances do not identify the two reached states. Define \(e=Z+m+q\). Evenness of cosine and oddness of sine give

    \[ \operatorname{Re}Q\in C:=\sum_c\cos(P_c+[-e,e]),\qquad \operatorname{Im}Q\in S:=-\sum_c\sin(P_c+[-e,e]). \tag{9} \]

    Every one of the nine same-slot contributions for a given c belongs to its corresponding interval, so taking their mean preserves that enclosure. The rectangle C+iS may contain unrealizable corners. Its use only enlarges the actual correlated source; it assumes neither independent quadratures nor independent errors at different nodes or preparation stages.

    For a real interval J define \(d(J)=\inf_{u\in J}|u|\) and \(b(J)=\sup_{u\in J}|u|\). Outward square-root arithmetic then bounds the actual source amplitude by

    \[ A_-\le |Q|\le A_+,\qquad A_-=\sqrt{d(C)^2+d(S)^2},\qquad A_+=\sqrt{b(C)^2+b(S)^2}. \tag{10} \]

    The endpoints in use are rounded outward; an additional universal upper bound is 5 from (2). Distances to zero, not squaring interval midpoints or assuming a sign, determine the lower bound. These source-family enclosures do not reconstruct Q from an observed record.

    4. Capability, attainability and the order of quantifiers¶

    Let Y_beta be the difference of the two noisy donor readings. Their errors may be correlated, including across origins and source families. Their difference n_beta still satisfies \(|n_\beta|\le2\eta\), where \(\eta=10^{-10}\). Put \(\epsilon=E+2\eta\). Equation (7) becomes

    \[ Y_\beta=-\frac{\gamma h}{4}\operatorname{Im}(e^{i\beta}Q) +r_\beta+n_\beta,\qquad |r_\beta+n_\beta|\le\epsilon. \tag{11} \]

    For each fixed actual source member and any admissible assignment of the reading errors, define its finite capability \(M_Y=\sup_{\beta\bmod2\pi}|Y_\beta|\). Then

    \[ \max\left\{0,\frac{\gamma h}{4}|Q|-\epsilon\right\} \le M_Y\le\frac{\gamma h}{4}|Q|+\epsilon. \tag{12} \]

    The upper inequality holds for every origin. For Q nonzero, choose \(\beta_* =\pi/2-\arg Q\) modulo a full turn. The harmonic in (11) has magnitude \(\gamma h|Q|/4\) there. The reverse triangle inequality gives the lower bound at this particular origin for all permitted reading errors. The existence of this witness precedes any choice of noise; no continuity of the reading errors or attainment of their supremum is needed. If Q is zero, (12) gives only the trivial nonnegative lower bound.

    Let \([\gamma]=[\gamma_-,\gamma_+]\) be its outward enclosure. Combining (10)--(12), with outward evaluation of products, gives the uniform bounds

    \[ L_w=\max\{0,\gamma_-hA_{w,-}/4-\epsilon\},\qquad U_w=\gamma_+hA_{w,+}/4+\epsilon, \qquad L_w\le M_{Y,w}\le U_w. \tag{13} \]

    The frozen decisions are \(U_1<10^{-8}\) and \(L_0>3\cdot10^{-7}\). The first makes every outer-one response small at every relative origin. The second supplies, for each actual outer-zero source, at least one origin with a large response for all reading errors. The witness may depend on unknown fine coordinates. It is not an origin inferable from the single-history winding record or an autonomous alignment mechanism.

    At the outer-zero witness, retain its paired outer-one source and the same donor residual. If both decisions hold, the absolute response difference at that common origin obeys

    \[ |Y_0(\beta_*)|-|Y_1(\beta_*)| >3\cdot10^{-7}-10^{-8}=2.9\cdot10^{-7}. \tag{14} \]

    This assertion includes all four reading errors through the two separately admitted bounds. It neither claims a signed contrast at every origin nor identifies a single operationally chosen origin common to all source members.

    Even the true finite response can vanish at some origins. Whenever \(\gamma h|Q|/4>E\), (7) gives opposite strict signs at \(\beta_*\) and \(\beta_*+\pi\). Smooth dependence of the complete solution on its prepared initial origin makes the true response continuous in beta; the intermediate value theorem supplies a zero between them. No analogous continuity of arbitrary measurement errors is assumed. Nonzero interaction capability therefore does not imply a nonzero response at every registration.

    5. Uniform contact work, storage and marked identity¶

    The instantaneous contact preserves coordinates and adds all 45 new edges. Its full supplied work is

    \[ W_B(\beta)=\sum_{c,j}\left[ \frac12(x_{D,j}-x_{H,c,j})^2+ 1-\cos(\beta+z_{D,j}-H_{c,j})\right],\qquad 0\le W_B(\beta)\le W_+:=90+\frac{45}{2}(B_o+q)^2. \tag{15} \]

    It can vary with beta, although its global upper bound does not. Let \(V_9=9[1-\cos(2\pi/9)]\), \(V_5=5[1-\cos(2\pi/5)]\), and let U be the rebuilt outer-one excess at its carrier endpoint. The same continuous dissipation and prior absolute observation work at most 36 give

    \[ \begin{split} H_{{\rm all},1}(0)&\le6V_9+9V_5+U+36+4q^2+W_+,\\ H_{{\rm all},0}(0)&\le6V_9+36+44q^2+W_+. \end{split}\tag{16} \]

    These are storage present at contact, not total historical preparation cost. The six constituent preparations, host acquisition and earlier observation events remain separately accounted. No reservoir or event-occurrence law follows from dissipativity, and dissipated storage is not used as payment.

    The new support retains \(H_{\rm all}'=-\|Lx\|_2^2/4\) for every origin. This supplies the continuous balance; it does not replace the finite identity check. Put \(D_o=2m\), \(D_j=7gBh\), \(\alpha=2\pi/9\), \(\zeta=10^{-6}\) and \(A=7/5\). Sufficient strict margins for all beta are

    \[ \begin{split} &\alpha-2\zeta-D_o-D_j>0,& &\pi/2-\alpha-2\zeta-D_o-D_j>0,\\ &\alpha-2q-D_j>0,& &\pi/2-\alpha-2q-D_j>0,\\ &\pi/2-A-2\zeta-D_o-D_j>0. \end{split}\tag{17} \]

    The initial donor rigid shift changes none of its internal gaps. On the joined law all endpoint phase rates have magnitude at most \(7gB/2\), so D_j bounds every edge's subsequent gap change. Equations (8) and (17) keep the six internal cycles positive and acute, and the nine old outer cycles strictly in their inherited acute charts through h. Their windings cannot change without crossing those chart boundaries. This establishes all fifteen marked identities for the finite interval, independently of registration. No identity is assigned to a new contact loop; new edges need not be acute. The earlier host-product capture result does not imply later capture or indefinite maintenance on the enlarged support.

    6. Fixed assessment and what its contrast establishes¶

    Rebuilding the actual sources with all twelve original allowances at their declared maximum, using the same outward dyadic128 arithmetic throughout, gives the following conservative strict enclosures:

    \[ \begin{split} -0.069910&<\operatorname{Re}Q_1<-0.069711,& |\operatorname{Im}Q_1|&<0.000110,\\ 0.069711&<|Q_1|<0.069910,& 4.999999993&<|Q_0|\le5,\\ E+2\eta&<4.918\cdot10^{-10}.&& \end{split}\tag{18} \]

    The last upper bound on \(|Q_0|\) is the exact triangle inequality in (2); the computed interval square root may include additional rounding width above 5. Applying (13) without discarding that outward width gives

    \[ \begin{split} 4.931\cdot10^{-9}&<M_{Y,1}<5.93\cdot10^{-9}<10^{-8},\\ 3\cdot10^{-7}<3.884\cdot10^{-7}&<M_{Y,0}<3.895\cdot10^{-7},\\ |Y_0(\beta_*)|-|Y_1(\beta_*)|&>3.825\cdot10^{-7}. \end{split}\tag{19} \]

    These statements hold for every member of the respective original source families and every admissible assignment of the reading errors.
