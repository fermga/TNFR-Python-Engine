    Sufficient hybrid state on the organized child-swap sector¶

    Conditional construction. Equality between children one and two at every slot defines a 72-primary-coordinate representation of the organized source. It exactly reconstructs both continuous rows, the energy guard, the admitted Reception–Coherence–Silence word and its changed-capacity continuation. Inherited neighbor counts are essential to that word.

    Section 6 separately bounds a nearly symmetric source up to its first fixed energy-level crossing. That approximation uses an explicit projection and retains a finite error allowance; it does not extend the exact restriction to unequal inputs. Section 7 carries that allowance through the first ideal word, comparing each successor at its own event time. The separate global child-exchange state retains a joint hidden Gram to reconstruct unequal children up to their single swap. Its generic primary dimension remains 90; it does not turn this 72-coordinate restriction into an exact averaging of arbitrary states.

    The exact chart supplies a sufficient representation for both preparations in the same-guard predictive-state obstruction. It does not give a minimum dimension or compress arbitrary fine states by averaging. The rigid carrier and signed slot-reflection representations remain distinct: the former is not preserved by this marked word, and the latter does not contain both of those preparations.

    1. Retained sector and exact reconstruction¶

    Keep the 45 labeled nodes 9*c+j, the ninety unit edges and the exact carrier psi_(9c+j)=alpha*(j-4), alpha=2*pi/9. The child edges are (0,1),(2,3),(0,2),(1,3),(0,4), with matching slots on each edge. The permutation exchanging children one and two and fixing the others is a support automorphism. It fixes each marked target, nodes zero and one.

    The sector requires equal form, equal declared real phase lifts and equal held positive capacities at corresponding slots of children one and two. Use four child orbits, ordered

    \[ A=\{0\},\quad B=\{1,2\},\quad C=\{3\},\quad D=\{4\}, \qquad (m_A,m_B,m_C,m_D)=(1,2,1,1). \]

    Each orbit retains its nine separate slots. Let P copy the resulting 36 coordinates to their fine nodes, R select the representatives, and M=P^T P=diag(m_a), with each child multiplicity repeated for nine slots. Then RP=I_36. Write

    \[ x=Pu,\qquad \Theta=P(\bar\psi+\delta),\qquad \nu=P v, \qquad \bar\psi_{9a+j}=\alpha(j-4),\quad v_a>0. \]

    The represented primary state has 36 form and 36 phase-offset coordinates. Capacity remains a separately retained law parameter between events. The lift is injective, and restriction is defined only on its image. Equal circular phases with unequal raw lift turns are not equal coordinates in this chart. No wrapping, mean subtraction or projection is part of restriction.

    Retain the target marks and original labeled context consumed by the word: histories, monotonic ordinals, debt, prior Coherence, kind and admission configuration. Non-target context can remain in its original fine record; the 72-coordinate count concerns primary continuous state, not total runtime storage. Any context claimed to share the child symmetry must itself agree on exchanged nodes. Neither the chart nor copied values authenticate history.

    This transfers the existing weighted partition method and capacity descent condition after deriving this actual law. It does not transfer their different clock or constitutive coefficients.

    2. Inherited support, both rows and energy¶

    Here a and b index the 36 node fibers, each comprising one fixed slot in one child orbit. Let N_ab count neighbors in fiber b of one fine member of fiber a. The partition is equitable and m_a N_ab=m_b N_ba. There are no edges inside a node fiber. The aggregated conductance is therefore w_ab=m_a N_ab, symmetric in a and b.

    There are 63 distinct reduced edges: four nine-cycles and three matching interfaces. Cycle weights are respectively 1,2,1,1; the interfaces A-B, B-C, A-D have weights 2,2,1 at every slot. Their total weight is ninety. The two target rows have four distinct reduced neighbors but five inherited fine neighbors.

    Let L_w be the aggregated weighted Laplacian, and set

    \[ q=M^{-1}L_wu,\qquad s_a=M_{aa}^{-1}\sum_b w_{ab}\sin(\vartheta_b-\vartheta_a), \qquad \vartheta=\bar\psi+\delta. \]

    Direct edge counting gives L_f P=P M^-1 L_w and S_f(P*vartheta)=P*s. With D=diag(v) and gamma=(1023*pi)^-1, the complete reduced law in the same structural time tau is

    \[ p=\tfrac14(-q+\gamma s),\qquad \dot u=D p,\qquad \dot\delta=\tfrac\gamma4Dq. \]

    The pressure p is per fine member. Equivalently both rows use D M^-1 with the unnormalized aggregate fields. Orbit multiplicity is not capacity: v remains the fine capacity consumed by Silence and its floor. The effective capacity of a strength-normalized pure-EPI quotient belongs to another model and cannot supply this aggregate-sum mobility.

    The fine vector field intertwines with this lift. Smooth uniqueness thus preserves the sector on every held-capacity segment. This is an exact nonlinear statement for all admitted sector coordinates, not only the carrier tangent. All clock changes multiply both rows by the same existing conversion factor.

    The pulled-back excess and its complete balance are

    \[ \bar E=\tfrac12u^TL_wu+ \sum_{a<b}w_{ab}[1-\cos(\vartheta_b-\vartheta_a)]-H_*, \quad H_*=45(1-\cos\alpha), \qquad \dot{\bar E}=-\tfrac14 q^T M Dq. \]

    They equal the fine energy and loss exactly. The five original child cycles and their winding observations are reconstructed; treating the merged child as one unweighted cycle would change storage. On a fixed positive-capacity segment the conserved form and phase-offset totals are sum_a(m_a/v_a)*u_a and sum_a(m_a/v_a)*delta_a. Their weighted means divide by sum_a m_a/v_a. These are the original fine inverse-capacity totals; the word may change them according to its existing event maps.

    3. The marked word consumes inherited counts¶

    Intersect the sector with the section 23 ideal word domain: initial unit capacities, its acute lift cell, E<1/100, abs(initial form mean)<1/30 and the complete supplied context and policies.     Let beta, lambda and kappa be the shared admitted Reception, phase-locking and Silence factors. Targets i=0,1 are singleton orbits. Each stage uses its own common input snapshot, so the two target proposals are simultaneous.

    Reception has the exact inherited form map

    \[ u_i^+=(1-\beta)u_i+\frac\beta5\sum_bN_{ib}u_b, \qquad i\in\{0,1\}. \]

    All other forms and every phase/capacity are held. Coherence then holds form and capacity and applies, on the existing regular argument branch,

    \[ z_i=\sum_bN_{ib}\exp(i(\vartheta_b-\vartheta_i)),\qquad \vartheta_i^+=\vartheta_i+\lambda\operatorname{Arg}z_i. \]

    These are the fine neighbor mean and phasor sum grouped by equal values. The multiplicities are inherited integers, not new fitted weights.     The grammar request and live admission are evaluated with the same retained target context, fine capacities, fresh pressure and configuration. Stored-pressure event updates remain their own maps, followed by the stipulated fresh-law association before continuation. Lifecycle timestamps do not become structural time. Each stage is separately atomic; there is no new whole-word rollback guarantee.

    4. The same guard, occurrence time and continuation¶

    Use the same fixed level ell and mandatory first downward crossing from the guard owner. Until that first event, the lifted reduced solution is the fine solution and bar E(t)=E_f(t).     At the crossing, lift and all three admitted stage maps commute. Target ordinals and urgency select the same complete word. The full primary hybrid response and its admitted fine observations are reconstructed at every common structural time for which the stated one-word model is defined. This is a conditional solution identity, not an evaluated numerical trajectory.

    Both previous matched-summary preparations repeat their profile in all five children and therefore belong to this sector. They give different u vectors in this chart.     The chart admits perturbations only within its fixed sector. It does not bound arbitrary departures of children one and two, establish attraction back to equality, or derive the preparation's symmetry. Other words or targets require their own preservation and context checks. This is neither an all-operator quotient nor a minimum-state or performance theorem.

    5. Detached implementation and independent controls¶

    The private child quotient reader derives support from the existing organized-source geometry. It reuses observe_quotient_structure only for exact neighbor counts and aggregated conductance; that metadata observer's pressure/rate normalization is not this complete law.

    Lift and restriction admit primitive real values before comparisons. They retain all offsets and positive capacities and reject off-sector inputs.     Independent controls rebuild the fine incidence and count the inherited support, compare both nonlinear rows, energy, loss and weighted totals, check strict reconstruction and both earlier preparations, and compare complete ideal word algebra. The bare-neighbor controls require both a form discrepancy and an off-carrier phase discrepancy. Runtime events, trajectories and frozen response producers remain excluded from these checks.

    6. A finite first-hit certificate near the sector¶

    The exact chart leaves an operational question: if the two children are only approximately equal, how far apart can their predicted first guard times be? The following certificate derives both crossing existence and a timing allowance from initial primitives, without a numerical trajectory. Its useful conclusion is the fixed-level bracket, beyond the quadratic projected-error mechanism already available in repeated composition and compressed memory.

    Two incoming-law extensions and an explicit projection¶

    This section fixes all 45 capacities to one, the ninety-edge support, both complete rows and structural time from section 2. No additional forcing, capacity update or support change is allowed on the comparison interval. Let J exchange children one and two, and let Pi=(I+J)/2 act on both form and continuous raw phase offsets. Initialize a full solution z by arbitrary finite real primitives and a symmetric reference w by w(0)=Pi*z(0). The latter is the fine lift of the exact 36-node chart.

    Both are mathematical extensions of the incoming law through the declared interval [0,T], even if their actual hybrid executions would already have reset. The smooth vector field is globally Lipschitz, so these extensions exist uniquely. Real lifts need not remain in an acute cell for the estimates below. Crossing this energy level does not itself admit an organized identity, the subsequent word or its grammar context; those retain their separate source-domain requirements.

    At each slot write the hidden deviations as

    \[ a_j=(x_{1,j}-x_{2,j})/2,\qquad b_j=(\delta_{1,j}-\delta_{2,j})/2,\qquad h_0=\max(\|a(0)\|_\infty,\|b(0)\|_\infty). \]

    These are declared normalized coordinates, with the same source units and clock; the joint norm does not identify form and phase as one physical unit. The averaging operation is separate from strict restriction, which still rejects unequal primitives without a tolerance or phase wrapping.

    Quadratic defect and uniform state allowance¶

    The fine Laplacian commutes with Pi. Therefore the projected phase row has zero direct defect, and only the sine term of the form row contributes. Let vartheta=Pi*Theta, with child orbit labels A, B, C, D as in section 1. For example the defect at A and at B is

    \[ \begin{aligned} d_{A,j}&=2\sin(\vartheta_{B,j}-\vartheta_{A,j})(\cos b_j-1),\\ d_{B,j}&=\sum_{k=j\pm1}\sin(\vartheta_{B,k}-\vartheta_{B,j}) [\cos(b_k-b_j)-1]\\ &\quad+\sum_{U=A,C}\sin(\vartheta_{U,j}-\vartheta_{B,j})(\cos b_j-1). \end{aligned} \]

    The C row has the same paired-neighbor expression as A and the D row is zero. Since 1-cos(s)<=s^2/2, the two cycle terms in a B row each contribute at most 2*||b||_infty^2 and its two matching terms at most ||b||_infty^2/2 each. Thus

    \[ \|\Pi S(\Theta)-S(\Pi\Theta)\|_\infty\le5\|b\|_\infty^2. \]

    Let g be a certified upper bound for gamma and set L=5*(1+g)/2, c=5*g/4. The fine degree is at most five, so ||L_f||_infty<=10, the sine map is 10-Lipschitz in the infinity norm, and the complete joint vector field is L-Lipschitz. Comparison of z with Jz gives ||(I-Pi)z(t)||_infty<=h0*exp(L*t).

    Choose outward upper bounds G>=exp(L*T) and Phi>=(exp(L*T)-1)/L, and define the allowances

    \[ H=h_0G,\qquad e=cH^2\Phi. \]

    On the entire interval the hidden state is bounded by H, while ||Pi*z(t)-w(t)||_infty<=e. Indeed the latter error starts at zero and satisfies the upper-Dini comparison e'(t)<=L*e(t)+c*H^2. Consequently fine reconstructed error is bounded by H+e, not by e alone. Hidden form cannot be omitted: it generates hidden phase through the second row even when b(0)=0. No attraction toward the symmetric sector is asserted.

    Energy and whole-bracket loss from primitives¶

    Put K=L_C9+2I, so ||K||_2<=6. Pairing exchanged edges cancels the linear energy term. With d_j=vartheta_(B,j+1)-vartheta_(B,j) and g_(U,j)=vartheta_(B,j)-vartheta_(U,j), the exact hidden energy difference is

    \[ \begin{aligned} E(z)-E(\Pi z) &=a^TKa+2\sum_j\cos d_j[1-\cos(b_{j+1}-b_j)]\\ &\quad+2\sum_j[\cos g_{A,j}+\cos g_{C,j}](1-\cos b_j). \end{aligned} \]

    For global real lifts its absolute value is at most a^T K a+b^T K b<=54*(||a||_infty^2+||b||_infty^2)<=108*H^2. It need not be positive outside an acute cell. The same splitting gives the exact instantaneous loss identity rho(z)-rho(Pi*z)=||K*a||_2^2/2; this does not equate the projected state with the separately evolved reference.

    Set X0=||x(0)||_infty. The diffusion maximum principle and |S_i|<=5 bound both incoming forms by X(t)=X0+5*g*t/4. Comparing ninety fine edges bounds the form-energy discrepancy between Pi*z and w by 360*X(T)*e, and their phase-energy discrepancy by 180*e. Hence the uniform energy allowance is

    \[ B_E=108H^2+[360X(T)+180]e,\qquad |E(z(t))-E(w(t))|\le B_E\quad(0\le t\le T). \]

    This is quadratic in the initial hidden norm at fixed horizon and bounded form amplitude. To establish transversality without sampling a response, use q=L_f*x and

    \[ \|\dot q(t)\|_\infty \le25X_0+\frac{25g}{2}+\frac{125g}{4}t,\qquad D_q=\left(25X_0+\frac{25g}{2}\right)T+\frac{125g}{8}T^2. \]

    For each of the full and reference initial gradients, computed separately, define

    \[ r_f=\tfrac14\sum_i\max(|q_{f,i}(0)|-D_q,0)^2,\qquad r_c=\tfrac14\sum_i\max(|q_{c,i}(0)|-D_q,0)^2. \]

    These bound the respective losses below on the whole interval. They are source-derived sufficient floors; zero does not imply actual zero loss. The use of raw X0 is conservative: adding a common form offset can change these numerical allowances and their availability, although it leaves the underlying form differences and dynamics unchanged.
