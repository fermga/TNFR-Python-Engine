    Collective Coupling and the post-event support law¶

    This bounded admission uses the existing word source, the pair-support classification and the actual registered UM snapshot stage. A valid collective event, an admitted continuous law and subsequent geometric identity are separate claims.

    1. Frozen source, action and comparison¶

    Before evaluating a selected word, retain the following protocol:

    • Use the ordered ten-node doubled C5, pairs (2*k,2*k+1), and all four unit cross edges between neighboring pairs, with no internal edges. Keep the complete conservative normalized-sine law e=0,w=beta=1 and original structural clock. The target winding has angle alpha=2*pi/5.
    • Retain the word source's 9/32<m<11/32, centered full-state radius Z_f<z=1/512 and equal-member capacities 1/2<v_k<2. Capacities are held between events. Preserve the full constituent state and its associated AL-only history/lifecycle stratum. Supply Si=1/2 at every node as an auxiliary operator input; it is not reconstructed from the continuous law. Stored pressure is finite with abs(p_i)<=1/2, separately from evaluated sine pressure. No source or occurrence law is inferred from these inputs.
    • Select pair 0 in fixed order (0,1) and the canonical classes with tokens emission,coupling,silence. Validate the whole word with the core compatibility profile and pass its contexts to the shared snapshot-stage dispatcher. Retain strict rejection and live precondition checks, the original EPI rails, capacity thresholds and minimum coupling degree. Explicitly set UM_MIN_EPI=UM_MIN_VF=1/8.
    • Set AL_boost=1/1024, SHA_vf_factor=1/2, UM_theta_push=1/1024, UM_vf_sync=1/8 and UM_dnfr_reduction=1/8. All four UM flags (BIDIRECTIONAL, SYNC_VF, STABILIZE_DNFR, FUNCTIONAL_LINKS) are true. Use UM_COMPAT_THRESHOLD=3/4, UM_CANDIDATE_COUNT=0 (all candidates), UM_CANDIDATE_MODE="proximity", and no restricted node sample. Set DELTA_PHI_MAX=UM_MAX_PHASE_DIFF=pi/2; candidate and final checks both apply.
    • Retain fixed execution seed 17, structural time 7, history window 8 and supplied per-stage timestamps. There are no optional diagnostics, monitors, callbacks or user extensions. Neighbor phase writes and actual edge attributes are part of the result; no weight or length is substituted. Authoritative theta agrees with its retained phase alias, if present. Graph phase/edge version counters are absent or valid nonnegative integers, and retained caches obey their shared-setter mutation scopes. These extra premises admit the phase and support writes absent from the AL/SHA source.
    • First classify the admitted missing edges, the complete reset and its associated context under pair interchange. Then check the actual weight against the unit-conductance hypothesis of the current sine reader. A rejection must retain the completed event and its precise model boundary. A unit-edge subdomain needs its changed degrees, storage work and fresh continuation proof; the old no-internal-edge certificate cannot be copied.
    • Use exact-real algebra and independent static represented controls. The two nominal probes have m=5/16, capacities one, common phase origin zero, and exact target midpoint phases. One has selected internal coordinates u_0=1/8192, delta_0=1/32768; the other has u_0=delta_0=0. Other internal coordinates vanish. Controls retain the represented phase materialization alpha_hat=674651885/536870912, also used by the prior word controls, and arithmetic defects separately from exact alpha. Unequal AL-only member histories are allowed with their physical association, and fixed timestamps use 2030-01-02T03:04:05+00:00 in the static controls.
    • Stop with an admitted continuation subdomain or a precise obstruction. No trajectory, parameter search, changed source geometry, forced unit edge, new pressure law or event selector belongs to this protocol. This is a prospective analytical admission, not a numerical formation campaign.

    The threshold and all-candidate policy are supplied operator parameters. The finite probes do not prove the open-domain theorem. Exact-real descent does not by itself establish arbitrary bitwise relabeling invariance of transcendental calls or order-dependent diagnostics.

    2. U3 admits the internal edge and excludes new interpair contacts¶

    Write k=0 for the marked pair, b=h=1/1024, and use the source's signed representatives x_(j,+/-)=X_j+/-u_j and theta_(j,+/-)=Theta_j+/-delta_j. The AL stage changes only the two selected forms and their associated execution records. All four existing neighbors of each selected member have phase gaps bounded by

    \[ \alpha+\sqrt2z<\pi/2. \]

    Every missing edge from a selected member is either its partner or a member of a distance-two base pair. The partner gap is at most sqrt(2)*z, whereas the wrapped gap to every distance-two pair is at least

    \[ 2\alpha-\sqrt2z>\pi/2. \]

    The UM candidate reader applies U3 to the snapshot phases before its phase proposals. Thus each target has exactly one possible new neighbor, its partner. Moving other phases later in the same stage cannot introduce a candidate that failed this first gate. The two proposals concern the same undirected internal edge; the merge creates one edge, not two.

    For each selected member s in {+,-}, the bidirectional consensus is

    \[ C_s=\arg\left(e^{i(\Theta_k+s\delta_k)} +2R_{k-1}e^{i\Theta_{k-1}}+2R_{k+1}e^{i\Theta_{k+1}}\right), \qquad R_j=\cos\delta_j. \]

    Choose its lift near theta_(*,k)+gamma. At the reference twist, the resultant is (1+4*cos(alpha))*exp(i*(theta_(*,k)+gamma)), of magnitude sqrt(5). The five constituent phase deviations give a chord error at most sqrt(5)*z. Consequently the resultant is nonzero and

    \[ |C_s-\theta_{*,k}-\gamma| \le\arctan\frac{z}{1-z}<2z. \]

    Target-to-consensus displacements are below 3*z. For each of the four neighbors, the displacement to either consensus on the nearest lift is below alpha+3*z<4/3. These statements fix the shortest-arc branches consumed by the actual stage, including the edge crossing the displayed 0,2*pi cut. A simultaneous phase-origin rotation changes none of the admission bounds.

    After AL, all selected forms remain in (1/4,1/2). Capacities remain in (1/2,2) until their convex UM blend, and SHA then puts selected capacities in (1/4,1). The declared 1/8 form/capacity thresholds therefore have strict margins. All original fine degrees are four. This supplies the live preconditions independently of the whole-word grammar validation.

    3. Complete primary map and associated execution state¶

    Let C_bar=(C_++C_-)/2 on the preceding local lifts. The two target phase proposals read the same immutable post-AL snapshot. The common neighbors receive both proposals; the actual merge averages their shortest-arc displacements. Hence the exact-real map on the selected pair is

    \[ \begin{aligned} X_k'&=X_k+b,&u_k'&=u_k,\\ \Theta_k'&=(1-h)\Theta_k+h\bar C,& \delta_k'&=(1-h)\delta_k+\frac h2(C_+-C_-),\\ v_k'&=\frac12\left[\frac78v_k+ \frac18\frac{v_{k-1}+v_{k+1}}2\right]. \end{aligned} \]

    For each adjacent pair j=k-1,k+1, retain the lift of C_bar nearest its midpoint. Its map is

    \[ X_j'=X_j,\quad u_j'=u_j,\quad \Theta_j'=(1-h)\Theta_j+h\bar C,\quad \delta_j'=(1-h)\delta_j,\quad v_j'=v_j. \]

    Every other primary form, phase and capacity is unchanged. In all cases the unordered coordinates are obtained from the actual resulting members: R_j'=cos(delta_j'), U_j'=(u_j')^2, and Q_j'=u_j'*sin(delta_j'). In particular, bidirectional UM changes the neighboring pairs as well as the selected pair. It does not change their forms or capacities.

    The pressure-relief branch writes, on each selected member only,

    \[ p_{k,s}'=p_{k,s} \left[1-\frac18 A_s\right],\qquad A_s=\frac14\sum_{n\in N_{k,s}} \left(1-\frac{|\operatorname{wrap}(\theta_n'-\theta_{k,s}')|}{\pi}\right). \]

    Here p denotes the supplied stored pressure consumed by UM. The branch uses the four original compatible neighbors and the merged phases, not the newly added partner. Neither these stored values nor their attenuation replace pressure evaluated from the post-event continuous law. Neighbor phase writes leave their stored pressures unchanged. AL and SHA preserve the stored pressure channel on this path.

    For the final existing-neighbor U3 check, each target differs from its neighbor by (1-h) times their initial gap plus at most h*|C_s-C_bar|<2*h*z. Therefore its magnitude is below alpha+sqrt(2)*z+2*h*z<pi/2. The partner also remains admitted. To see the latter without assuming exact initial synchrony, fix the shared external resultant T and differentiate the target map in its input angle:

    \[ \frac{d}{dt}\big[(1-h)t+h\arg(e^{it}+T)\big] =1-h+h\frac{1+\Re(Te^{-it})}{|1+Te^{-it}|^2}. \]

    Every external phase stays within alpha+sqrt(2)*z of every intermediate target angle, so Re(T*exp(-i*t))>0. The displayed derivative lies strictly between zero and one. The final selected phase separation is therefore smaller than its initial value unless both were zero, and delta_k'=0 holds exactly when delta_k=0.

    The full collective event retains the pair-swap orbit of physical state and its associated constituent records, together with the action mark, support, all operator parameters and timestamps. Swapping a selected pair exchanges C_+,C_-, changes the signs of u_k,delta_k,delta_k', and leaves the common neighbor update and internal edge unchanged. Swapping a neighbor pair leaves its two-member resultant and mean capacity unchanged. Thus all displayed primary rows descend to that retained orbit. The homogeneous Si input and all-candidate policy introduce no hidden member selection.

    Only the marked targets execute AL, UM and SHA lifecycle/history updates; a neighbor's phase write does not execute its own UM word. Existing AL origins and lineage stay attached to their constituent. UM appends its glyph with the fixed history window, advances the selected operator-step indices by one and writes their UM source glyph. Their U2 debt remains zero and their prior-IL flag false. The AL/UM and UM/SHA prefixes have no IL-pattern recognizer match, so this stratum appends no recognized pattern and emits no related warning. UM adds no emission or latency fields. SHA then writes the existing latency contract with the boosted form as preserved form and final selected step indices n_i+3.

    Shared phase writes update both theta and phase aliases on the selected pair and its four neighbors and invalidate their declared trigonometric caches. Neighbor histories and source glyphs stay unchanged. Selected capacity and stored-pressure writes use the shared cache owners. The added edge has exactly {"weight": W} with no supplied length; support versions and affected caches are updated. The fixed execution seed is read, but the all-candidate configuration draws no random values. Its node-offset map is prepared on the detached proposal snapshot.

    Graph stage status, actual edge attributes and these associated writes belong to the result. Every stage validates its complete proposal before committing graph-owned state. That stage contract does not assert rollback of the entire three-stage word or external resources. The primary equations alone do not establish lifecycle equivalence. An order-sensitive diagnostic or separately exposed cache witness requires its own transported mark, as in the source owner. Static controls exercise the represented implementation; the pair-interchange proof concerns exact real arithmetic.

    4. Actual edge weight and the unit-support boundary¶

    Let epsilon_E=1e-9 denote the implementation's positive form-similarity regularizer. With the homogeneous Si=1/2, the shared compatibility formula reduces in exact real arithmetic to

    \[ \boxed{W=1- \frac{|\operatorname{wrap}(\theta_{k,+}'-\theta_{k,-}')|}{2\pi} -\frac{|x_{k,+}+b-x_{k,-}-b|} {4(|x_{k,+}+b|+|x_{k,-}+b|+\epsilon_E)}.} \]

    The cancelling boosts in the numerator emphasize that UM leaves the post-AL form difference unchanged. Both directed candidate proposals have this same symmetric score. Phase contraction, x+b>1/4 and the source norm imply 1-W<sqrt(2)*z<1/4. Thus the internal edge is indeed added, with W>3/4; the finite candidate score does not merely leave its existence undecided. No interpair edge is added.

    Every deficit in this expression is nonnegative. Therefore

    \[ \boxed{W=1\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad u_k=0,\ \delta_k=0.} \]

    Without the frozen homogeneous Si premise, equality of the two supplied Si values would be a third necessary condition. The exact unit-edge family is a synchronized-member stratum. It retains positive relative width in the other pairs' internal states, all mean states and paired capacities, but it is not open in the full source family. Every source with nonzero selected form or phase split produces a strictly subunit edge under this exact-real protocol.

    The current complete normalized-sine reader requires unit conductances. The generic weighted result is consequently an admitted UM event followed by an unsupported continuation request under that reader. Replacing its weight by one, dropping the edge or importing unmodified degree-four coefficients would change the evaluated model. Pair-swap symmetry of the new internal edge does not remedy this missing constitutive premise. This unit-model assessment introduces no weighted law; Sections 7-10 separately admit a completion retaining the actual conductance.

    These exact-real equalities are not authentication rules for rounded data. The production score uses its prescribed normalized form arithmetic and binary64 phase affinity; a sufficiently small positive deficit can round to a stored weight of one. The represented continuation reader must admit the actual stored graph and coordinates. Conversely, the exact theorem requires its exact hypotheses; rounded weight equality alone does not prove exact member equality. Static controls below keep the nominal mathematics and represented evaluation distinct.

    5. Unit-edge continuation: new rows and an identity barrier¶

    Restrict now to u_k=delta_k=0. The two consensuses coincide, the new edge has unit weight, and the selected members retain identical forms, phases and capacities. Write E'=E union {{k,+},{k,-}}, let L' be its unit Laplacian and D_i its actual fine degrees. They are five on the selected pair and four elsewhere. The post-word capacities are the positive held values from Section 3. The admitted complete continuation is

    \[ \dot x_i=\frac{\nu_i}{\pi D_i} \sum_{j\sim_{E'}i}\sin(\theta_j-\theta_i),\qquad \dot\theta_i=\frac{\nu_i}{\pi D_i}(L'x)_i. \]

    Pressure is the independently specified sine sum in the first row. These are original-clock rows on the new support; neither stored UM pressure nor the old degree-four normalization is substituted.

    The existing internal-edge derivation extends directly from common capacity to the held paired capacities, which multiply each pair's two fine rows equally. With e_j=1 for j=k, zero otherwise, d_j=2, D_j=4+e_j, and

    \[ J_j=\sum_{\ell\sim j}R_\ell\sin(\Theta_\ell-\Theta_j),\quad C_j=\sum_{\ell\sim j}R_\ell\cos(\Theta_\ell-\Theta_j),\quad q_j=\sum_{\ell\sim j}(X_j-X_\ell), \]

    the exact pair rows are

    \[ \begin{aligned} \dot X_j&=\frac{2v_j}{\pi D_j}R_jJ_j,& \dot\Theta_j&=\frac{2v_j}{\pi D_j}q_j,\\ \dot u_j&=-\frac{2v_j}{\pi D_j}\sin\delta_j(C_j+e_jR_j),& \dot\delta_j&=\frac{2v_j}{\pi D_j}(2+e_j)u_j. \end{aligned} \]

    Every independent pair interchange is still a support automorphism. The exact unordered description therefore persists. At the selected tip, u_k=delta_k=0 makes both internal rates zero, so that tip remains invariant. As a useful exact reuse, throughout this invariant stratum the internal edge contributes zero to both fine sums. The entire new-support flow then equals the old doubled-C5 flow with auxiliary effective target capacity (4/5)*v_k, and all other capacities unchanged. This is a restricted equivalence of complete rows, not a modification of the actual stored capacity or an admission of the new graph by the old strict replica reader. At the same fine state, the actual selected pressure itself is 4/5 of the old-support pressure; the auxiliary-capacity representation instead keeps that old pressure and puts the same factor into its capacity. The two representations agree on both continuous rates, not on every primitive label. Representing the auxiliary rational capacity in binary64 introduces its own rate defect and must not be asserted exactly equal by floating-point coincidence.

    For completeness, the identity barrier can be checked directly on the actual new graph. Its storage is

    \[ H_{E'}=\frac12 x^TL'x+ \sum_{\{i,j\}\in E'}[1-\cos(\theta_j-\theta_i)]. \]

    Differentiating cancels the two rows term by term, so dH_(E')/dt=0. The old winding target remains a critical point: the new internal edge has zero target form and phase differences. It adds zero target storage, so the same target value H_* applies. With l=e_(k,+)-e_(k,-),

    \[ L'=L+ll^T,\qquad \lambda_2(L')\ge5-\sqrt5,\qquad \lambda_{\max}(L')\le2\max_iD_i=10. \]

    The phase changes of Section 2 have Euclidean norm below 3*h: there are two selected displacements below 3*h*z and four neighboring displacements below 4*h/3. The AL centered form increment has norm sqrt(8/5)*b. Centering can only reduce these norms. Hence the post-word full centered norm satisfies

    \[ Z_f' < z+\sqrt{8/5}\,b+3h <\frac{13}4z<\frac1{128}. \]

    Take r=1/16. All old edge target gaps are alpha, and the new target gap is zero. On the radius-r chart the storage Hessian therefore gives
