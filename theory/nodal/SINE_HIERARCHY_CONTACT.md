## 5. Complete event work, degree changes and mean leaves

For a general admitted UM stage that preserves form, let F be its actual set
of newly committed edges, E the old support, and `theta^+` its complete phase
endpoint, including old-neighbor writes. Old conductances are unchanged.
The exact storage jump is

\[
 \begin{split}
 H^+-H^-={}&\frac12\sum_{\{i,j\}\in F}\omega_{ij}(x_i-x_j)^2\\
 &+\sum_{\{i,j\}\in E}
       [\cos(\theta_j-\theta_i)-\cos(\theta_j^+-\theta_i^+)]\\
 &+\sum_{\{i,j\}\in F}[1-\cos(\theta_j^+-\theta_i^+)].
 \end{split}
 \tag{11}
\]

Ignoring the middle line would omit bidirectional UM's work on old edges.
Capacity is absent from this storage, so its reset has no additional term.
THOL birth has zero edge-storage jump under the explicitly selected isolate
convention; this is not a claim of zero physical creation cost.

For the source family, (11) becomes

\[
 \Delta H_{\rm UM}=\frac12\omega D^2
       +\cos\phi-\cos(7\phi/8)+1-\cos(\phi/16).
 \tag{12}
\]

The old-edge phase term is negative. Nevertheless (6), (9),
`phi<3/20`, and `1-cos(phi)<=phi^2/2` give

\[
 \Delta H_{\rm UM}>
 \frac25\left(\frac7{25}\right)^2-
 \frac12\left(\frac3{20}\right)^2
 =\frac{2011}{100000}>\frac1{50}.
 \tag{13}
\]

The final SHA leaves H unchanged. OZ and refreshed THOL also leave the old
edge storage unchanged. Thus the complete word's positive storage jump is
accounted for by (12), not claimed as energy conservation or an unbudgeted
autonomous gain. An event reservoir or selection law has not been derived.

Degree normalization changes both rows. For a general old node, define
`a_i=sum_{new j}omega_ij*(x_i-x_j)`, and let `Delta S_i` include the phase
change on old edges and all new sine terms. With

These are fresh-law comparisons, not the raw pressure-relief map. For a
former isolate use its explicitly zero old rows and the full new rows;

\[

\]

\[

The new child's weight and the changed parent degree/capacity are all retained.
