# Acquired mediator class and a transmitted form response

<a id="sine-class-mediated-response"></a>

**Status:** conditional finite-response theorem and prospective protocol
under the supplied complete sine law. The finite estimate below supplies a
single preparation, probe and error budget for separate reserved assessment.
Any retained assessment is recorded separately below; previous frozen
reports remain unchanged. Task status belongs to the
[execution plan](../research/FIVE_STAGE_EXECUTION_PLAN.md#current-g3-gate).

The question is whether an acquired mediator's internal organization
changes a donor-induced response in a different receiver while all three
patterns retain their identities. This is a generative interaction claim:
the class-dependent response must follow from the full nodal dynamics and
its prepared organizations. A physical force, particle property or material
identification is not inserted into the observation. The
[ontology](../EMERGENT_ONTOLOGY.md#generative-bound-organization) retains
those later physical obligations.

indexed \(i=0,1,2\), with local node order \(j=0,\ldots,8\).
Their class tuple is \((1,k,1)\), with \(k\in\{1,2\}\).

\((0,4)\) to \((1,4)\), and \((1,4)\) to \((2,4)\).
Thus the joined central degrees are \((3,4,3)\), and every other degree
is two. Both class comparisons use precisely this same support.

Let \(\alpha=2\pi/9\) and
\(\Theta_{i,j}=k_i\alpha(j-4)\), with \(k_i=(1,k,1)_i\).
The common source origins are aligned at zero. This is a declared
choice of the initial preparation, permitted by the isolated components'
common-phase symmetry; it is not a rotation or reset of an already reached
state. The earlier
[composition protocol](SINE_REDUCED_PORT_COMPOSITION.md#sine-reduced-port-composition)
used origins \((0,1/1000,0)\) and remains unchanged.

Retain the actual formation preparations
\[
x_{i,j}(0)=k_i m(j-4)+\xi^x_{i,j},\qquad
\theta_{i,j}(0)=\xi^\theta_{i,j},\qquad
m=\frac{2046}{9}\left(\frac{355}{113}\right)^2.
\tag{1}
\]
The original per-coordinate error bounds are \(10^{-10}\), with zero
component sum separately in each channel. All sixteen relative source
coordinates per component remain admitted; no symmetry constraint is
added to these errors. Each complete family starts with zero winding and
must reach its own class target by the existing
[two-class formation theorem](SINE_PATTERN_DYNAMICS.md#sine-formed-class-pair),
with its primitive premises freshly admitted.

For each admitted source and each class, compare two continuations of the
same reached state: one receives the declared donor form jump and one
receives no jump. The observation is the receiver central form increment
between these paired continuations,
\[
R_k(h)=x^{\mathrm{probe}}_{2,4}(\tau_c+h)
       -x^{\mathrm{base}}_{2,4}(\tau_c+h).
\tag{2}
\]
The class contrast is \(R_1(h)-R_2(h)\), with its orientation fixed
before evaluation. Pairing is exact within each probe/baseline pair; a
uniform claim across classes must cover the independently admitted source
residuals or explicitly retain any stronger cross-class pairing premise.
An unmeasured baseline cannot be replaced by zero merely because the
aligned nominal target is stationary.

All coordinates, event amplitudes and readouts use the same normalized
structural units as their owners. Angles are radians, and the response
horizon is in the structural clock declared below. A recorded version
would need an independently supplied observation law and error allowance;
the mathematical form readout supplies no laboratory-unit calibration.

## F3: source cost, work, identity and cancellations

The source information is supplied, and its class-specific cost must be
retained. Equation (1) has nominal component storage \(36k_i^2m^2\);
the whole-family ceiling includes both residual channels as in the
formation owner. Changing only mediator class therefore does not mean
equal preparation cost. The prospective comparison changes acquired
internal organization under a common declared preparation budget.

For each support, use its actual storage
\[
H(x,\theta)=\tfrac12x^{\mathsf T}Lx+
 \sum_{\{a,b\}\in E}\bigl[1-\cos(\theta_b-\theta_a)\bigr].
\tag{5}
\]
Continuous joined flow obeys
\(H'=-(Lx)^{\mathsf T}K(Lx)\le0\).
The contact work is the sum of the two newly installed edge storages;
aligned nominal origins make its nominal value zero, without making the
actual full-family work vanish. On the joined support the exact form-jump
work is
\[
W_{\mathrm{probe}}=a(Lx^-)_{0,4}+\tfrac32a^2.
\tag{6}
\]
Its linear term retains the actual pre-jump state. Contact and probe work
need separate prospective allowances; previous dissipation is not an
undeclared reserve that pays either event.

The degree-weighted form and lifted-phase charges are conserved between
events. The probe changes the joined form charge by \(3a\), or its
weighted mean by \(3a/58\), while leaving the phase charge unchanged.
Any recovery statement must use this new conserved-mean leaf rather than
silently imposing the unprobed common form. Contact changes the degree
weights without resetting the states.

The [joined acute-storage argument](SINE_REDUCED_PORT_COMPOSITION.md#actual-source-handoff-support-work-and-joined-identity)
applies to both classes and both continuations as follows. Write
\(u_i=\theta_i-\Theta_i\) at the handoff. The inherited component
sums are zero, and \(\|x_i\|_2,\|u_i\|_2\le\epsilon\).
The aligned contact has work in \([0,8\epsilon^2]\), and its joined
storage excess is at most \(22\epsilon^2\). These bounds follow from
the two added edge storages and the per-cycle Hessian bound four.

After the probe, the squared Euclidean distance on the quotient by common
form and phase, the storage excess, and the probe work obey
\[
\begin{aligned}
Z_+^2&\le6\epsilon^2+2a\epsilon+\frac{26}{27}a^2,\\
H_+-H_*&\le22\epsilon^2+6a\epsilon+\frac32a^2,\\
W_{\mathrm{probe}}&\in
 [\tfrac32a^2-6a\epsilon,\tfrac32a^2+6a\epsilon].

\]
For the first bound, arithmetic centering replaces the kick by

For the last two, \(|(Lx)_{0,4}|\le6\epsilon\).

The joined fine diameter is at most ten, so
\(\lambda_2(L)\ge2/135\). In the radius \(r=1/12\) quotient ball,
every edge cosine exceeds \(1/20\); hence the acute-storage coercivity
coefficient exceeds \(1/2700\). If
\[
Z_+^2<r^2,\qquad
22\epsilon^2+6a\epsilon+\tfrac32a^2<r^2/2700,
\tag{8}
\]
the first-exit and zero-loss arguments retain all three windings for all
subsequent unforced joined time and give recovery to the unique local

satisfies the smaller bounds obtained by setting \(a=0\). The preprobe
degree-weighted form and target-phase means lie within
\(4\epsilon/58\) of zero, since only the additional central degree
weights remain after the component sums cancel. The probe adds the
already stated \(3a/58\) to the form mean; no mean is reset.

Retaining these identities is a separate calculation from separating the

## Finite response from internal mediator feedback

Set \(A=KL\), \(\eta=\gamma^2\), and let \(C_k\) be the
degree-normalized phase Hessian at the aligned target. Its cycle edges
have coefficients \(\cos\alpha\), \(\cos(k\alpha)\),
\(\cos\alpha\), while the two contact coefficients are one. Let
\(D_M\) be the mediator's internal cycle Laplacian normalized by the
actual joined inverse degrees, extended by zero outside that component.
Then
\[
C_1-C_2=d_cD_M,\qquad
d_c=\cos\alpha-\cos(2\alpha)>0.
\tag{9}
\]
These coefficients are derived from the fixed nonlinear law and target
geometry, not inserted as fitted interaction parameters.

For the ideal comparison only, start at the aligned target and apply the
same donor form jump. This reference is used to bound the reached family;
it does not replace the original full-state coordinates of that family. Put
\(y=\theta-\Theta\). The tangent evolution in proof coordinates
\((x,y/\gamma)\) has matrix
\[
M_k=\begin{pmatrix}-A&-\eta C_k\\A&0\end{pmatrix},
\qquad (x(0),y(0)/\gamma)=(a e_D,0).
\tag{10}
\]
Here \(D=(0,4)\) and \(R=(2,4)\) in subscripts of coordinate
vectors; the scalar denominator introduced below is written \(D_h\).
The first four tangent form derivatives are
\[
\begin{aligned}
X_0&=ae_D,&X_1&=-aAe_D,\\
X_2&=a(A^2-\eta C_kA)e_D,&
X_3&=a[-A^3+\eta(AC_kA+C_kA^2)]e_D.
\end{aligned}
\tag{11}
\]
Their receiver differences vanish through order two. Since
\(e_R^{\mathsf T}D_M=0\), the order-three difference is
\(a\eta d_c e_R^{\mathsf T}AD_MAe_D\). The mediator central entry
of \(Ae_D\) is \(-1/4\), its internal-neighbor entries are zero,
and the central diagonal of \(D_M\) is \(2/4\). The receiver
contacts that support only through its central neighbor, with coefficient
\(-1/3\). Therefore
\[
e_R^{\mathsf T}AD_MAe_D=\frac1{24},\qquad
P(h):=\frac{a\gamma^2d_c h^3}{144}>0.
\tag{12}
\]
The order-zero through order-two cancellations isolate later mediator
feedback from the common initial diffusion response.
The same third derivative holds for the ideal nonlinear receiver. Its
additional term is
\(\gamma D^2(KS)_R(\Theta)[\theta'(0),\theta'(0)]\).
Every receiver internal edge has zero initial phase velocity at both
ends, while its contact edge has zero base gap and hence zero sine second
derivative. That additional receiver term vanishes exactly.

For \(0<h<1/3\), \(\|M_k\|_\infty\le3\) and
\(\|M_1-M_2\|_\infty\le2\eta d_c\). Telescoping the matrix
powers gives
\(\|M_1^n-M_2^n\|_\infty\le2\eta d_c n3^{n-1}\).
The absolute tangent contrast tail starting at order four is bounded by
\[
B_T=\frac{9a\gamma^2d_c h^4}{1-3h/4}.
\tag{13}
\]
Indeed its first bound is the numerator, and each successive term has
ratio at most \(3h/4\). Independent marginal tangent remainders are
not substituted for this correlated class-difference estimate.

The full nonlinear remainder retains every fine coordinate. The diffusion
semigroup is a maximum-norm contraction; normalized sine is globally
Lipschitz with constant two. For the ideal nonlinear probe trajectory,
\[
D_h=1-2\eta h^2>0,\qquad
\sup_{t\le h}\|x(t)\|_\infty\le a/D_h,\qquad
\|y(t)\|_\infty\le2\gamma at/D_h.
\tag{14}
\]
The first inequality follows by substituting the phase integral into the
form Duhamel formula. Every normalized sine Taylor defect is at most
\(2\|y\|_\infty^2\), so its form forcing is at most
\(8\gamma^3a^2t^2/D_h^2\). Comparing the nonlinear and tangent
equations in (10), their whole-window maximum error is bounded by its
forcing integral plus \(3h\) times that error, using the proof coordinates
\((x,y/\gamma)\). This also bounds the needed form readout. Each class contributes
at most \(8\gamma^3a^2h^3/[3D_h^2(1-3h)]\). Both ideal baselines
are exactly stationary, giving the pair bound
\[
B_N=\frac{16\gamma^3a^2h^3}{3D_h^2(1-3h)}.
\tag{15}
\]
No invariant nonlinear reflection-even subspace is assumed.

Return to the actual full source family in the original coordinates
\((x,\theta)\). Its complete field has maximum-norm Lipschitz
constant less than three. Each actual probe or baseline differs from its
ideal reference by at most \(\epsilon/(1-3h)\). This comparison does
not divide phase uncertainty by \(\gamma\). Four histories and four
scalar recording errors of magnitude at most \(\delta\) therefore give
\[
\left|\widehat R_1(h)-\widehat R_2(h)-P(h)\right|
\le B_T+B_N+\frac{4\epsilon}{1-3h}+4\delta.
\tag{16}
\]
Here the two readings defining each \(\widehat R_k\) retain the same
actual preprobe source. Cross-class source errors may be independent.

The conditional sufficient separation criterion is that the right-hand
error in (16) be strictly smaller than \(P(h)-c_{\min}\), for a
prospectively declared positive threshold \(c_{\min}\). Failure of
that sufficient inequality alone does not prove that the responses agree.

\[

\tag{18}
\]
