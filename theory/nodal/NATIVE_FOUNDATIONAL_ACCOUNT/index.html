    Native C5 foundation account and dynamic-capacity admission¶

    This account applies the four foundation prerequisites to one provisional realization. Its structural capability is retention of a nonuniform organization and a reproducible response to small perturbations. It does not require a permanent pulse. The existing native reciprocal law is the reference; releasing held capacity is an explicitly supplied comparison. Definitions, previous proofs and new joint-model claims are distinguished below. No foundational pillar or physical identification is closed by this single example.

    1. Why this reference, and what the alternatives retain¶

    Candidate Useful capability and independent premises Decision for this account
    Native relational C5 Existing native pressure, a conditional reciprocal phase row, positive capacities and a strict winding-one geometry with proved local recovery Selected because it provides a complete, implemented reference and an identity distinct from a periodic scalar signal.
    Normalized-sine law Global smooth pressure and mobility supplied by a different constitutive law; extensive pulse and collective results Retained alternative. Another property of a periodic branch would not resolve the present causal premise.
    Ordinary native hybrid runtime Registered actions, stored pressure, histories, diagnostics, gains, target order and an outer decision schedule Retained for operator-occurrence questions. Disabling glyphs does not turn this execution path into the relational law.

    This selects an instrument for the stated capability, not a uniquely correct TNFR law. The native admission, sine distinction, hybrid occurrence audit and threshold result retain their separate scopes. The native recovery theorem admits a simple-cycle twist only when |ell|<n/4; C5 is the smallest simple cycle with a nonzero strictly acute twist in this class, not a universally minimal NFR.

    2. Complete reference and sufficient state¶

    Use the fixed labeled unit C5, no loops or inputs, signed dimensionless form x, circular primitive phase theta, and positive capacity nu. Let B=2I-A, L=B/2 and N=diag(nu). Effective coefficients e,w>0, e+w=1, and beta>0 are fixed independently of a response. Time is a declared structural parameter. In this normalized form chart, capacity and the coefficient kappa have inverse-clock units; nu_dot has inverse-clock-squared units. The comparison below fixes e=w=1/2, beta=1.

    On the native regular chart, write the relative resultant z_i=sum_(j~i) exp(i*(theta_j-theta_i)), g_i=Arg(z_i)/pi and H_i=pi*abs(z_i)*sinc(Arg(z_i))>0, with sinc(0)=1. The selected two-channel pressure and reference rows are

    p = P(x,theta) = -e*L*x + w*g(theta),
x_dot = N*p,
theta_dot = (w/beta)*H(theta)^(-1)*N*B*x,
nu_dot = 0.

    The native phase metric and source use the same relative resultant. The reciprocal phase row follows conditionally from the independently specified joint storage and exchange premise, or the alternative locality and capacity hypotheses in the admission owner. It does not follow from x_dot=nu*p alone.

    The existing field evaluator and executor provide the held-capacity implementation.     3. Identity, observations and operator roles¶

    The reference equilibrium has x=c*1, theta_i=2*pi*i/5+alpha modulo 2*pi, and positive held capacities. Its nonuniform phase geometry has winding one. Small joint perturbations, rather than a prescribed periodic signal, test retention and response. Common form and phase origins are symmetries; their actual means need not be conserved during a response. Retention of the winding geometry is weaker than recovery to its equilibrium shape, and neither establishes acquisition from a state lacking that identity.

    The continuous form/phase exchange is a supplied constitutive mechanism in this account. Registered jumps are available interventions under their own contracts; they are not hidden commands executing these differential rows. No autonomous invocation of all thirteen operators, support birth or capacity replenishment is inferred. The causal-role owner retains the stronger proposal that an organization could realize an effective named action.

    Several tempting first discriminators are already settled and are reused as constraints, not new results:

    • The native decision schedule and selector memory affect occurrence.
    • A pressure-only Coherence write can be erased by constitutive refresh.
    • Public/staged Coherence also changes phase and fresh neighboring pressures; its full handoff and state boundary already distinguish those effects.
    • The declared Emission/Coherence/Silence cycle lacks the required restoration.
    • Native C5 local recovery and insufficiency of arbitrary aggregate observations have existing proof owners.

    The remaining scoped question is whether positive capacity must stay fixed for this particular joint identity and response, or whether a declared autonomous evolution of that property is compatible with them.

    4. Admission of the capacity comparison¶

    The earlier capacity-law comparison already supplies nu_dot=-kappa*L*nu under a different form/capacity law with held phase. Its preparation, asymptotics and differentiated EPI result do not transfer to the present coupled native phase law. Likewise, time-varying pure-EPI stability does not establish the joint phase result below.

    The comparison is motivated by explicit additional premises: capacity transfer uses only incident capacity contrasts, has one reciprocal edge rule, is linear/additive and regular in that contrast, and has no source. On the regular C5 these motivate the support-averaging direction. Locality or covariance alone does not force linearity, a constant rate or absence of other feedback. The nonnegative rate kappa remains free; zero retains the reference and every positive-rate theorem is for a fixed positive value. No rate is fitted to a desired waveform.

    This is distinct from the native adaptation policy: that writer consumes stored pressure and Si, qualifying-call counts, bounds and a selected subset. A continuous limit, endogenous occurrence rule or equivalence to that complete policy is not asserted. Redistribution also does not supply capacity lost in a Silence event. The new mathematical question is the admissibility, response and local identity of the combined three-row system, not another proof that averaging can reduce contrast.

    Joint capacity admission and local recovery¶

    This section admits one autonomous capacity completion of the native relational law and proves its local consequence on the prepared winding-one unit C5. The capacity completion is supplied under explicit premises. It is not selected by the nodal identity, installed in the runtime, or identified with the default gated adaptation policy.

    The native exchange owner supplies the form/phase law, storage and regular phase metric. Its local recovery theorem already establishes recovery with positive held capacities. The separate capacity-law comparison already uses continuous capacity diffusion with fixed phase and a capacity-pressure channel. The result here couples that admitted capacity law to the native evolving form/phase rows, with the capacity-pressure channel absent. The new question is whether recovery survives this enlargement of the retained state.

    Declared complete law and its premises¶

    Let the supplied support be the undirected unit C5, indexed modulo five. Write its adjacency matrix as \(A\), and set

    \[ B=2I-A,\qquad L=B/2,\qquad N=\operatorname{diag}(\nu_i). \]

    The retained state consists of five signed form values \(x_i\), five circular phases \(\theta_i\), and five strictly positive capacities \(\nu_i\). Work in one fixed nondimensional structural clock. No graph changes, event requests, controller state, clipping, history reads or external input are included.

    On a regular phase chart define the native source and metric by

    \[ \begin{aligned} g_i(\theta) &=\frac1\pi\operatorname{wrap} \left(\arg\sum_{j\sim i}e^{i\theta_j}-\theta_i\right),\\ H_i(\theta) &=\pi\left|\sum_{j\sim i}e^{i\theta_j}\right| \operatorname{sinc}(\pi g_i)>0,\\ V_\phi(\theta) &=\sum_{\{i,j\}\in E}[1-\cos(\theta_j-\theta_i)],\\ H g&=-\nabla V_\phi,\qquad q=Bx . \end{aligned} \]

    Here \(\operatorname{sinc}(z)=\sin(z)/z\), continuously extended at zero. Strictly acute principal edge gaps provide the local domain used below; the prepared winding does not require all node phases to occupy one common semicircle.

    The comparison fixes effective normalized coefficients

    \[ e=w=\tfrac12,\qquad \beta=1,\qquad \kappa>0 . \]

    The inherited symbols \(e,w,\beta\) are retained in the algebra below to show which coefficient enters each term. No additional coefficient is inferred from their appearance. The complete autonomous law is

    \[ \boxed{ \dot x=Np,\quad p=-eLx+wg,\qquad \dot\theta=\frac w\beta H^{-1}Nq,\qquad \dot\nu=-\kappa L\nu . } \tag{JC1} \]

    Pressure is refreshed from the current form and phase. It is not an independent held coordinate, nor reconstructed from a measured form rate. The phase and capacity-contrast pressure coefficients are different objects: in (JC1), the capacity-pressure and topology-pressure coefficients are zero. Both native rows consume the full instantaneous \(N\).

    The underlying form/phase source is the one admitted by dynamics/relational.py. That implementation's capacity is held during its selected step; (JC1) adds a mathematical comparison row rather than changing that execution contract. Ordinary native pressure normalization is retained. There is no default capacity-gradient source hidden in \(p\), and no replacement of the native Arg source by a sine evolution law.

    The additional constitutive premise is reciprocal continuous capacity transfer on the supplied edges:

    \[ J_{i\leftarrow j}=\frac\kappa2(\nu_j-\nu_i) =-J_{j\leftarrow i}. \]

    One way to specify this restricted class is to require the same continuous, additive function of signed capacity contrast at every edge. Additivity and continuity force \(J(s)=a s\); preservation of the nonnegative capacity cone requires \(a\ge0\). On C5 write \(a=\kappa/2\). The choice \(\kappa>0\) excludes the still-admissible held law. Locality, covariance or homogeneity alone do not establish this entire class or select a numerical \(\kappa\).

    This rule has no supplied oscillation period and no event-count clock. In a dimensional structural clock, \(\kappa\) has inverse-clock units; a clock conversion must transform it together with the two native rows. The rule is not a continuous-limit proof for the snapshot-based, gated capacity adaptation in dynamics/adaptation.py.

    Near-identity agreement, time calibration and its remaining error¶

    Fix a capacity vector while \(s\downarrow0\). Write \(a=\kappa T\) and compare the full capacity outputs using their Euclidean norm.
