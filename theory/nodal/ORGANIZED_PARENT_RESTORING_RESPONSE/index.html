    Restoring response of an acquired organized parent¶

    Fixed intervention and analytic question¶

    This declaration continues the actual source family of parent acquisition at its slow time s=4. It fixes one supplied intervention and its paired comparison before evaluating the analytic response bounds. It does not evaluate a numerical trajectory.

    Item Fixed choice
    Source The four original independently uncertain recovered C9 children, each with form and phase allowances 1e-10, carried through the unchanged acquisition law to s=4; retain all original fine errors and reached forms and phases
    Complete continuous law The matching 36-node law, held unit capacities, all 72 unit edges and the original structural clocks tau=(1023/1024)*t, s=gamma^2*tau/2, gamma=1/(1023*pi)
    Supplied event At s=4, simultaneously set theta+[0,a,j]=theta-[0,a,j]+(2*a-1)/4 for every slot of parent zero; leave all other phases, all forms, support and capacities unchanged
    Event interpretation A specified zero-duration hybrid phase intervention with marked targets and no autonomous occurrence law; no registered operator name is claimed
    Preserved observations Each child's winding and both parents' form and lifted-phase means at the event; retain the same continuous phase lifts
    Work Account for the exact complete storage jump H+ - H-, supplied externally with a fixed budget of three structural storage units; previous dissipation is not a reservoir
    Paired control The same actual acquired state continued without the event, under the same complete continuous law and clocks
    Finite response readout At s=5, the intervened outer parent lifted-phase gap minus the paired control gap must exceed 1e-6 for the entire original source family
    Predictive state Initialize the exact eight-child-mean law from the actual post-event child means; its outer parent phase-gap error at s=5 must be below 1e-7. Compare with four parent means, which are identical across the paired preparations at the event
    Recovery deadline By s=8, restore both marked parent identities: every intraparent same-slot gap has magnitude strictly below 1/4
    Child retention Every marked C9 keeps winding one throughout 0<=s<=8, including the event
    Decision Admit source-inclusive disruption, finite recovery, accounted work and a resolved predictive distinction, or an explicit scoped obstruction; a failed sufficient estimate remains unavailable

    The half-radian relative displacement deliberately exceeds the registration threshold while preserving each child internally. One and four subsequent slow units are fixed readout and recovery horizons, without a scan or source refinement. The eight-coordinate prediction retains its supplied law and hidden initialization. The four-coordinate comparison concerns sufficiency of that state alone; adding an intervention history supplies extra information. Neither the fixed grouping, event occurrence, work supply nor continuous law is selected autonomously. This question does not identify physical binding, particles, a laboratory clock or a unique hierarchy.

    The declaration above was committed as 37add507 before its analytic certificate was assessed. The proof below retains those choices and the complete original uncertainty. No fine or reduced trajectory is evaluated.

    1. The actual event and the retained continuous law¶

    Write \(\sigma_g=2g-1\), \(\sigma_a=2a-1\), \(\alpha=2\pi/9\) and \(\psi_j=\alpha(j-4)\). The matching graph is \(C_9\square K_2\square K_2\), with its original marked nodes and unnormalized Laplacian \(L\). Between events the complete rows remain

    \[ x_\tau=(-Lx+\gamma S(\theta))/4,\qquad \theta_\tau=\gamma Lx/4,\qquad S_i(\theta)=\sum_{k\sim i}\sin(\theta_k-\theta_i). \tag{1} \]

    The supplied jump is the simultaneous map \(T\) declared in the table: add \(-1/4\) to every phase of child \((0,0)\), add \(1/4\) to every phase of child \((0,1)\), and preserve the other state. It acts on the actual pre-event state, not on its nominal target. Uniform translation within a child preserves every internal phase difference and its winding. The opposite translations preserve each parent's lifted phase mean; all form means are unchanged. Retain the existing lifts through the jump.

    This defines a detached hybrid intervention with explicit fine realization, target marks and externally supplied work. It does not claim registered Mutation, Self-organization or another runtime operator: their live evidence, grammar, descent and occurrence obligations are separate. No finite-duration pressure input is invented for the jump. The subsequent sine current is evaluated from the post-event phases under (1).

    2. The exact four-child reference after intervention¶

    The original moving reference reaches its own values \(B_4,\Delta_4,v_4,u_4\) at \(s=4\). These are the acquired values from the previous theorem; none is reset. After applying the same jump to that reference, the eight-coordinate child-constant family remains exactly invariant under (1). Its zero-global-mean relative chart is

    \[ \begin{split} x^*_{g,a,j}&=\tfrac12(\sigma_g U+\sigma_a V+ \sigma_g\sigma_a W),\\ \theta^*_{g,a,j}&=\psi_j+\tfrac12(\sigma_g D+\sigma_a B+ \sigma_g\sigma_a C). \end{split}\tag{2} \]

    The conserved common form and phase origins can be added independently. Here \(D\) is the outer parent phase gap, the two intraparent child gaps are \(B-C\) and \(B+C\), and \(C\) records their difference and the associated outer-port mismatch. Direct substitution into both fine rows gives

    \[ \begin{array}{ll} U_\tau=-U/2-\gamma\sin D\cos C/2,&D_\tau=\gamma U/2,\\ V_\tau=-V/2-\gamma\sin B\cos C/2,&B_\tau=\gamma V/2,\\ W_\tau=-W-\gamma(\cos B+\cos D)\sin C/2,&C_\tau=\gamma W. \end{array}\tag{3} \]

    The internal cycle currents cancel. The three graph modes have Laplacian eigenvalues two, two and four, respectively; omitting the last mode would change both the form and phase dynamics after this intervention.

    Use elapsed slow time \(h=s-4\), \(\varepsilon=\gamma^2\), and

    \[ K=-C,\qquad y_D=-U/\gamma,\qquad y_B=-V/\gamma, \qquad z=2W/\gamma. \]

    Both rows transform, yielding

    \[ \begin{array}{ll} D_h=-y_D,&\varepsilon(y_D)_h=\sin D\cos K-y_D,\\ B_h=-y_B,&\varepsilon(y_B)_h=\sin B\cos K-y_B,\\ K_h=-z,&\varepsilon z_h=2[(\cos B+\cos D)\sin K-z]. \end{array}\tag{4} \]

    The post-event reference initialization is exactly

    \[ D(0)=\Delta_4,\quad B(0)=B_4+1/4,\quad K(0)=1/4, \quad y_D(0)=-u_4/\gamma,\quad y_B(0)=-v_4/\gamma, \quad z(0)=0. \tag{5} \]

    The first two velocities are the nonzero acquired form coordinates. Setting them to zero or to an assumed equilibrium current would be a different initial-value problem. The no-event reference instead continues the original separated family, with \(C=W=0\).

    3. Full source transport and retained children¶

    Rebuild the four original isolated sources and their acquired-state bounds as in parent acquisition. Let \(q=10^{-70}\) be the unchanged recovered norm allowance per child and per channel. The global initial deviations from the moving reference have norms at most \(2q\). All 64 independent fine source directions remain admitted; no smaller preparation, later dwell or symmetry is imposed.

    Apply the same jump to the actual and reference phases. Their differences \(r=x-x^*\), \(\zeta=\theta-\theta^*\) have no jump. The support and diffusion operator remain fixed. Consequently the exact integrated heat identity from the acquisition proof applies piecewise across the event, with the same global constants

    \[ \lambda_2(L)>1/3,\qquad \|L\|_2\le8,\qquad \|S(\theta)-S(\theta^*)\|_2\le8\|\zeta\|_2. \]

    It gives, on either paired branch throughout \(0\le s\le8\),

    \[ Z_8=2q(1+\bar\gamma)3^{32},\qquad R_8=2q+24\bar\gamma Z_8,\qquad \|\zeta\|_2\le Z_8,\quad\|r\|_2\le R_8. \tag{6} \]

    Here \(\bar\gamma\) is an outward upper bound, and \(2\gamma^2\tau=4s\le32\). The form deviation stays globally mean zero. This is the original-source comparison with a moving exact reference, not a new source at the event and not a local energy trap.

    For every marked C9 the reference's internal edge branches remain \(\alpha\). Actual branches therefore lie in \([\alpha-2Z_8,\alpha+2Z_8]\subset(0,\pi/2)\); their residual differences telescope around the cycle. The branch integer on the closing edge is retained, giving winding one throughout the declared interval. The uniform event translations preserve that winding exactly at the jump.

    4. Disruption and a finite restoring response¶

    Put \(a=1+2\varepsilon\). The earlier source theorem supplies

    \[ 1/1200<\Delta_4<1/500,\qquad 0<B_4<1/64, \quad 0\le-u_4/\gamma\le a\Delta_4, \quad 0\le-v_4/\gamma\le aB_4. \]

    In the notation of (5), this says directly \(0\le y_D(0)\le a\Delta_4\) and \(0\le y_B(0)\le aB_4<aB(0)\). Also \(B(0)<4/15\), \(K(0)=1/4\), and \(z(0)=0\).

    For \(0<\varepsilon<10^{-7}\), the domain

    \[ \begin{split} &0<D\le D(0),\quad 0<B\le B(0),\quad 0<K\le1/4,\\ &0\le y_D\le aD,\qquad 0\le y_B\le aB, \qquad 0\le z\le2aK \end{split}\tag{7} \]

    is invariant. At either \(y=aD\) boundary, the scaled outward derivative is at most \((1-a+\varepsilon a^2)D<0\); replacing the forcing by \(\sin D\cos K\le D\) only strengthens that bound. At \(z=2aK\), it is at most \(4(1-a+\varepsilon a^2)K<0\). Lower velocity boundaries have nonnegative derivatives. The cones imply positive exponential lower bounds for all three gaps, precluding a finite-time exit through zero.

    Thus the three positive gaps decrease. Elementary sine/cosine bounds on the above rectangular domain give

    \[ \sin B\cos K\ge(19/20)B,\qquad (\cos B+\cos D)\sin K\ge(19/10)K. \tag{8} \]

    For example, use \(\sin B/B\ge1-B^2/6\), \(\cos K\ge1-K^2/2\), and the corresponding bounds for \(B<4/15\), \(D<1/500\), \(K\le1/4\). The variation-of-constants formulas for the velocities, their nonnegative initial values and monotonicity of \(B,K\) then imply

    \[ B(h)\le(B_4+1/4)e^{-(19/20)(h-\varepsilon)},\qquad K(h)\le\tfrac14e^{-(19/10)(h-\varepsilon/2)}. \tag{9} \]

    Define \(B_+,K_+\) by these right sides at \(h=4\), with outward source/coefficient bounds. The fixed inequalities give \(B_++K_+<1/50\). At the event, every parent-zero same-slot gap is at least \(B_4+1/2-2Z_{\rm pre}>1/4\), so the entire actual family leaves that parent's registration domain. Here \(Z_{\rm pre}\) is the previous acquisition phase-error bound, valid at \(s=4\).

    At the fixed recovery deadline, let \(X_g\) be the actual parent form mean and \(\Phi_g\) the mean of \(\theta_{g,a,j}-\psi_j\). The latter equals the raw lifted phase mean because the winding carrier has zero mean. Define the phase residual using those offsets, not by subtracting only the parent mean from the primitive winding phase:

    \[ r_{\theta,g}=\max_{a,j}|\theta_{g,a,j}-\psi_j-\Phi_g|, \qquad r_{x,g}=\max_{a,j}|x_{g,a,j}-X_g|. \]

    Both actual parents then satisfy

    \[ \begin{split} \max_j|\theta_{g,1,j}-\theta_{g,0,j}| &\le B_++K_++2Z_8<1/4,\\ r_{\theta,g}&\le(B_++K_+)/2+2Z_8<1/100,\\ r_{x,g}&\le\bar\gamma(1+2\bar\varepsilon)(B_++K_+)/2 +2R_8<1/100. \end{split}\tag{10} \]

    The last two residuals are taken about each actual parent mean. They use \(|B\pm K|\le B+K\) and \(|V\pm W|\le\gamma a(B+K)\), together with the full-source deviations. Thus the parents return even to the stronger centiradian domain, with all children retained. This proves a finite return by the supplied deadline, not indefinite post-return stability or physical binding.

    5. A finite interaction that four parent coordinates cannot determine¶

    Let \(d=\Delta_4\). In the intervened reference, the hidden mode \(K\) reduces the external restoring current to \(\sin D\cos K\). The invariant cone gives \(K(h)\ge(1/4)e^{-2ah}\). Therefore the missing unit-time restoring factor obeys

    \[ I_K:=\int_0^1[1-\cos K(h)]dh \ge\frac{1-e^{-4a}}{192a}>1/200. \tag{11} \]

    This uses \(1-\cos K\ge K^2/3\) on \([0,1/4]\). One simple rational lower bound for implementation replaces the numerator by \(1-e^{-4}\) and the denominator by \(192\bar a\).

    Write \(G(t,d)=2\arctan(e^{-t}\tan(d/2))\) for the solution of \(G_t=-\sin G\). The gradient approximation with the actual reference history \(K(h)\) ends at \(G(1-I_K,d)\); the no-event gradient approximation ends at \(G(1,d)\). Since \(G\) decreases and \(G(1,d)\ge d/e\),

    \[ \begin{split} G(1-I_K,d)-G(1,d) &\ge I_K\sin G(1,d)\\ &>\frac1{200}\frac3{10000} =\frac3{2\cdot10^6}. \end{split}\tag{12} \]

    Indeed \(\sin G(1,d)>(99/100)d/e>3/10000\), using \(d>1/1200\), \(d<1/500\) and \(e<11/4\). This is a finite response bound, not the already known instantaneous port-current difference.

    The gradient approximations are controlled without replacing either form row. For the intervened reference, let \(f=\sin D\cos K\) and \(\ell=y_D-f\). Then

    \[ \ell_h=-\ell/\varepsilon-f_h,\qquad |\ell(0)|<1/400,\qquad |f_h|\le a d(1+2K(0)^2)<1/400. \tag{13} \]

    The bounds follow directly from (7), with the actual acquired initial form coordinate in (5). Exponential convolution gives \(\int_0^1|\ell|dh<\varepsilon/200\). The time-dependent gradient row \(-\sin D\cos K(h)\) is nonexpansive on the positive small-angle domain, so this integral bounds its endpoint error. The no-event reference retains the previous lag error at most \(\varepsilon/10\). A common safe bound for their sum is \(\varepsilon/5\).

    Each actual branch differs from its corresponding reference parent phase gap by at most \(2Z_8\). Combining this with (12)-(13) proves the prospectively selected contrast for every paired original source:

    \[ D_{\rm actual,kick}(5)-D_{\rm actual,control}(5) >\frac3{2\cdot10^6}-\frac{\bar\varepsilon}{5}-4Z_8 >10^{-6}. \tag{14} \]

    Both actual preparations have exactly the same four parent means immediately after the event. The induced event map on those four observations is the identity. Their later responses nevertheless differ by (14), so those four coordinates alone do not determine this finite response. An intervention label, retained child association or memory would supply additional state; the result does not exclude such an augmented predictor. In particular, one forecast based only on the common four-coordinate state must miss at least one paired endpoint by more than \(5\cdot10^{-7}\), which exceeds the fixed \(10^{-7}\) prediction budget.

    6. Eight-coordinate prediction from actual post-event observations¶

    Initialize the exact four-child law from all eight actual child means just after the event, including their reached forms. In full child coordinates that law is

    \[ X_h=\frac{-\bar L X+\gamma\bar S(\phi)}{2\gamma^2}, \qquad \phi_h=\frac{\bar L X}{2\gamma}, \tag{15} \]

    where \(\bar L\) is the unit square Laplacian and \(\bar S\) sums sine currents over its two neighbors. This transforms both rows of the existing eight-coordinate restriction; it installs no additional law and makes no exact-quotient claim for arbitrary fine states.

    The averaging projection onto child means is orthogonal after constant lifting. For a 36-vector of fine errors, its four mean errors therefore have Euclidean norm at most one third of the fine norm. Also \(\|\bar L\|_2=4\), so the same integrated-heat argument amplifies the predictor's initial phase/form discrepancy by at most \(e^{2h}\). The parent gap is the child-mean linear observation with coefficients \((-1/2,-1/2,1/2,1/2)\), of norm one. Consequently, at the readout,

    \[ |D_{\rm actual}-D_{\rm eight}| \le\frac{Z_8+e^2(Z_{\rm pre}+\bar\gamma R_{\rm pre})}{3} <2Z_8+18(Z_8+\bar\gamma R_8)<10^{-7}. \tag{16} \]

    The first term is the actual fine-source error about the moving reference; the second carries the eight-coordinate predictor's true initialization. Using the looser shared bound in the last expression keeps every original fine direction and the fixed resolution. These correlations come from the carried source and event history. A generic parent residual ball or a tangent restriction alone would not justify this slow-time prediction.

    7. Event work and its actual-state correction¶

    With the complete storage from the acquisition theorem, the exact jump is

    \[ W_T=H(T(x,\theta))-H(x,\theta) =\sum_{\text{affected }\{i,k\}} [\cos(\theta_k-\theta_i)-\cos(\theta_k-\theta_i+\delta_{ik})]. \tag{17} \]

    All forms, internal C9 phase differences and support remain unchanged. There is no additional form-storage term. The nine intraparent contacts of parent zero have relative increment \(1/2\); the eighteen outer contacts have increments of magnitude \(1/4\). On the moving pre-event reference,

    \[ W_T^*(B_4,\Delta_4) =9[\cos B_4-\cos(B_4+1/2)] +18\cos\Delta_4[1-\cos(1/4)]. \tag{18} \]

    The acquired residual child means need not vanish. There is no first-order cancellation analogous to the initial attachment, and no quadratic error claim is made. For \(g_k(v)=\cos v-\cos(v+k)\), \(|g_k'(v)|\le|k|\); each actual edge-gap error is at most \(2Z_{\rm pre}\). Thus
