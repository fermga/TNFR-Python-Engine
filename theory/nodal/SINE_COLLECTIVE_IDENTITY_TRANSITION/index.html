    Identity through an admitted form-capacity transformation¶

    The directional result rejected retention of the original joint radius after a complete NUL/IL/SHA word. This owner identifies a weaker, independently defined organizational class that the word can preserve, and the larger state needed to predict its actual action. It instantiates the existing operator and grammar descent criteria for this contact; it introduces neither a replacement retention radius nor a new constitutive law. The research program owns subsequent work.

    1. A marked phase class is not a sufficient state¶

    Keep the existing twenty-node, 45-edge contact: two doubled C5 constituents, the internal edge {0,1}, and four cross edges between {0,1} and {10,11}. Nodes 0 and 1 remain individually marked ports. Let sigma fix those ports and exchange (2,3),...,(18,19). Require sigma to preserve the complete bare support, each actual edge conductance and each capacity. Equality of aggregate conductance sums alone is not the reused involution theorem. No exchange of the two marked ports is assumed.

    Use the existing reference phase, increasing by alpha=2*pi/5 around each oriented constituent cycle. Choose an orientation of every fine edge and its reference integer k_ij so that

    \[ g_{ij}=\theta_j-\theta_i-2\pi k_{ij}\in(-\pi/2,\pi/2). \tag{1} \]

    The organizational class considered here is the marked contact in this acute phase sector, with positive capacities and the nine exact physical twin equalities. The reference sector fixes every cycle winding; in particular, both original oriented C5 windings are +1. On a fixed phase lift this sector is an open convex intersection of edge inequalities, with a free common phase origin. Its continuous paths cannot change the associated integers without leaving the sector. This is a topological/geometric classification, not a scalar macro-node or a claim that all its members have identical responses. The supplied support and constituent organization are not newly formed by this classification.

    There are two distinct equivalences:

    • Same organizational class: membership in this marked acute sector, with its prescribed twin structure. Different internal shapes, form origins, positive capacities and admitted actual weights can belong to it.
    • Same predictive execution description: equality of the reduced physical coordinates and every retained associated input and context specified below. Equality of the winding labels alone does not imply this equality.

    The old observation Z<1/16 remains a different, finer claim. Leaving it can coexist with membership in (1). Neither membership in (1) nor a passing U6 snapshot proves that the class will persist indefinitely.

    2. A contextual chart for the exact twin stratum¶

    The existing reduction retains eleven form values X and eleven lifted phase values Theta, with multiplicities (1,1,2,...,2). Retain their full common origins. Also retain:

    • actual support, conductances, geometric lengths, per-member bare counts, capacities, fine-node/cell association and marked targets (0,1);
    • the independently specified pressure law and the full consumed stored pressure, including raw-versus-refreshed provenance;
    • target-associated ordered histories, persistent grammar debt and prior coherence, lifecycle data and all other persistent records consumed or exposed by the chosen stage path;
    • fixed configuration, node and neighbor iteration order, target order, sequence context, supplied timestamp and structural clock;
    • each consumed U6 reference, its geometry and observation convention.

    For a represented comparison, retain the admitted primitive representations and attribute associations as well. Do not identify circularly equivalent large floating-point angles, reordered sums or a scalar with a different runtime representation without a separate equivalence proof. No stochastic operation is selected by this word; a later randomized action would need its own retained random input/state.

    Let Q record these data while suppressing the duplicate physical form/phase rows within the nine equal pairs. Its declared lift L expands those rows back to the same marked twenty-node preparation. Nonphysical per-member records remain associated with their member, even when they differ within a physical twin pair. This is a lossless chart of the selected physical stratum with retained context, not an eleven-node simple graph and not a quotient that identifies arbitrary runtime permutations. Recomputing degrees on an eleven-node simple graph would change the law.

    For the deterministic, fixed configured stage path, equal Q records give the same lifted primitive inputs. Consequently admission, proposals, context updates and declared outputs agree. This is a concrete application of the existing partial-transition criterion, not a claim of a minimal context. Opaque callbacks or unrecorded process state are outside the comparison; rebuildable caches cannot serve as authoritative physical inputs.

    The physical stratum is preserved by the chosen events: NUL and SHA change only singleton ports; IL changes only their phases and stored pressures. No paired form, phase or capacity is changed asymmetrically. A pressure-only refresh cannot break these physical equalities. Its represented per-member pressure rows need not be compressed or presumed bit-identical. After the word, the held-support conservative flow preserves the physical stratum by the already proved equivariance and smooth uniqueness. This statement retains the actual weights, capacities and law; it is not a robustness result for nonsymmetric fine preparations.

    3. A positive-width, one-word admission domain¶

    The following analytical subdomain makes the physical and phase claims explicit. It is not an inference from a successful nominal execution. Admit the support and symmetry above, old conductances one, and new conductances in (3/4,1]. Geometric lengths equal those conductances for the U6 comparison. On the exact twin stratum require

    \[ 1/4<x_i<3/8,\qquad 3/8<\nu_0,\nu_1<5/8, \qquad 0<\nu_i\leq1\quad(i\ne0,1), \tag{2} \]

    and, for one retained common phase origin tau,

    \[ \theta_i=\tau+\theta_{*,i}+\eta_i,\qquad \max_i|\eta_i|<1/8. \tag{3} \]

    Condition (3) is a componentwise phase chart, not the old joint Z-ball. Its constant comes from the existing acute validation margin, and no trajectory response is used to choose it. It is only a source condition; the output need not satisfy the same box.

    Use the selected conservative law, so initial stored pressure is the fresh value p_i=S_i/(pi*d_i). In particular |p_i|<=1/pi. NUL uses its default factor c, with 7/8<c<1, edge awareness enabled and hard EPI bounds [-1,1]. IL uses the selected phase coefficient 0<=lambda<=3/10 and pressure retention 0<=kappa<1; the configured binary64 0.3 satisfies the phase bound. SHA uses factor 1/2. Retain the selected thresholds: NUL requires capacity above 1/10, signed form at least 1/10, density at most 10; IL requires positive form and capacity; SHA requires capacity at least 1/8. These are configured admission policies, not deductions of universal constants from nodal flow.

    The initial discrete context keeps history AL,UM,SHA,IL,OZ at port 0 and AL,AL,AL,UM,SHA,IL,OZ at port 1, each in a deque of maximum length eight, with persistent debt one and prior coherence present. Keep strict rejection, live precondition validation, atomic target order (0,1) and the actual ValidatedSequence context {"initial_epi_nonzero": true, "compatibility_profile": "core"}. For these three tokens, the discrete checks do not consume phase compatibility or temporal secants: U3 acts on coupling/resonance, and this word contains no mutation, bifurcation or recursive action. Its validated prefix context is retained rather than erased by starting a new word.

    The physical margins follow directly. Initially NUL's density satisfies

    \[ \frac{|p_i|}{x_i}<\frac4\pi<10. \]

    NUL has no clipping on (2), and its ideal primary changes are x_i^+=c*x_i, nu_i^+=c*nu_i, p_store,i^+=p_i/c at each port. Hence x_i^+>7/32 and nu_i^+>21/64. IL leaves those two channels fixed, so its positive-state checks and the later SHA capacity check both hold strictly. SHA leaves capacity c*nu_i/2>21/128>0. At every stage the pressure refresh uses the declared independent law; the reciprocal stored-pressure write cannot substitute for that refresh. Untargeted form, phase and capacity stay fixed at the events; their fresh pressure can change after the IL phase reset.

    NUL adds no U2 debt, IL discharges one unit, and SHA adds none. Thus the retained debt progresses 1 -> 1 -> 0 -> 0. Zero terminal debt is the selected whole-word postcondition. SHA alone is not a universal zero-debt gate: a different initial debt can leave different obligations even when the same physical word executes.

    The successor domain consists of the three actual prefix images with their updated context, followed by the post-word continuous state with held support/capacity and the same law. It does not require the images to belong to the source box (2)--(3). At completion the port form lies in (c/4,3*c/8) and capacity in (3*c/16,5*c/16), and the phase bounds below replace (3). This proves one ordered transformation on a nonzero-width relative stratum. It does not admit repetition from the new history, every possible next operator, or generic perturbations breaking the twin equalities.

    4. The ideal word stays in the original acute phase sector¶

    At either marked port the reference neighborhood contains three zero-phase neighbors and four at reference angles +alpha or -alpha. Its complex resultant, after removal of tau, is therefore

    \[ R_*=3+4\cos(2\pi/5)>4. \]

    Under (3), the phasor Lipschitz bound gives a resultant perturbation smaller than 7/8. Its real part exceeds 25/8 and its imaginary part has magnitude less than 7/8. Its selected mean angle mu consequently satisfies

    \[ |\mu-\tau|<\arctan(7/25)<7/25. \tag{4} \]

    The target and mean differ by less than 1/8+7/25<pi, so IL's shortest circular update uses this same local lift. Simultaneous IL reads both means from one immutable source. Each port's new phase error satisfies

    \[ |\eta_i^+|<(1-\lambda)/8+7\lambda/25 \leq343/2000. \tag{5} \]

    All untargeted phase errors remain below 1/8. An old edge incident to a port therefore has error below 343/2000+1/8=593/2000<3/10<pi/10. An untouched old edge has error below 1/4, also below pi/10. Adding the reference principal magnitude 2*pi/5 leaves every old edge strictly below pi/2. The new edge {0,1} has error below 343/1000; each cross edge has error below 593/2000. Both are also acute. The straight phase interpolation between source and output preserves these inequalities, so no edge changes its reference integer in (1). NUL and SHA do not change phase.

    Thus the ideal complete word deforms the organization within the same marked acute sector, including its two +1 constituent windings. It does not preserve the finer joint Z-ball, predict the subsequent sector lifetime, or select an autonomous occurrence of the word. The fine-to-collective continuous law and its sufficient state remain the existing twenty-two-coordinate model.

    5. Ideal finite U6 bounds on the same domain¶

    This result additionally uses the declared fixed geometric lengths and initially fresh stored pressure. It does not treat the original raw OZ snapshot as a fresh constitutive evaluation.

    Write the canonical potential as Phi_s=B*p, where B_ij=1/dist(i,j)^2 for distinct nodes and the diagonal is zero. Every positive shortest distance is greater than 3/4. For any pressure difference,

    \[ \frac1{20}\sum_i |(B\Delta p)_i| \leq\frac{76}{45}\|\Delta p\|_1. \tag{6} \]

    The target phase displacement in IL is at most a=(3/10)*(7/25+1/8)=243/2000. Only the two ports, their four old neighbors 2,3,8,9, and cross neighbors 10,11 change fresh pressure. The sine Lipschitz bound and actual degrees give

    \[ \|p_{\rm fresh}^+-p_{\rm fresh}\|_1 \leq \frac a\pi \left(2\frac87+4\frac12+2\frac13\right) =\frac{1053}{1750\pi}. \tag{7} \]

    Equation (7) bounds both the completed IL stage and the completed whole word: NUL and SHA change no phase, and fresh pressure is independent of capacity and form in this selected law. NUL's raw reciprocal write instead changes only two stored pressures, with total change below 2/(7*pi). The IL raw pressure retention changes at most 2/pi; its subsequent refresh differs from that raw value by at most this amount plus (7). Therefore the largest of these conservative raw/refresh bounds is

    \[ \frac{76}{45}\left(\frac2\pi+ \frac{1053}{1750\pi}\right) =\frac{346028}{78750\pi}<\frac32<\frac\pi2. \tag{8} \]

    SHA does not change stored pressure before its refresh. Thus every selected ideal before/completed and raw/refreshed U6 comparison, including the fresh-source/complete-word comparison, passes the fixed pi/2 policy on this domain. These are finite snapshot bounds; they prove neither U6 confinement between later flow times nor the geometric Z-retention hypothesis. Changing the length convention, baseline, pressure law or threshold requires renewed admission. Represented fields and refresh residuals retain their own checks.

    6. Represented controls and the frozen negative result¶

    The analytical maps above use exact real arithmetic. Full runtime descent in the contextual chart instead concerns the actual configured operations on their admitted represented inputs. It preserves their exact decisions and results by the retained reconstruction; it does not make their rounded phase updates equal to the ideal formulas. A broad floating-point error bound is not inferred from the strict ideal margins.

    The synthetic transition controls exercise the existing shared stages on independent preparations, retaining all live preconditions, context and U6 observations. Their controls distinguish raw pressure from refresh, and their counterexamples expose missing neighbor multiplicity, absolute form origin and persistent grammar debt. These controls establish implementation behavior, not a proof by sampling of the analytical domain. Neither the retained producer nor a new trajectory is executed by this analytical comparison.

    The original represented preparation has a separate complete-word and validated-flow record. Its unchanged result certifies exit from Z<1/16 by elapsed 57/4, while every certified flow tube retains acute edges and both original windings through that time. That finite conclusion remains exactly as reported. The present contract explains how grammatical transformation and retained organizational class can coexist with that geometric departure; it does not relabel the failed prediction as successful maintenance.

    The information boundary is substantive: dropping the common form origin fails through local NUL scaling, as already proved in the form-origin obstruction. On a broader raw-pressure execution domain, dropping member-associated stored pressure can change NUL density admission. In the initially fresh subdomain of section 3, that pressure is already determined by the retained phase, support and law; its explicit record is sufficient, not a minimal-state claim. Raw stages still need their declared refresh provenance and pressure map. Dropping persistent debt changes the outstanding grammatical obligations. Keeping only winding, means or unordered context inventories consequently cannot supply this collective execution map. Retaining the marked internal coordinates and their consumed context does supply the stated one-word map; physical identification, autonomous selection and unrestricted collective realization of all registered operators remain separate questions.
