    Parent registration acquired from organized children¶

    Fixed analytic question and source¶

    This admission tests whether the matching-contact 36-node law in repeated composition can acquire its declared parent identity from children whose relative registration initially lacks that identity. The source, clock, deadlines and readout below are fixed before assessing the analytic bounds. No numerical trajectory or reserved response is evaluated by this declaration.

    Item Fixed choice
    Complete law and support The matching control of the repeated-composition law: four marked C9s, unit cycle and intraparent edges, matching outer edges, weighted degree four, held unit capacities
    Clock tau=(1023/1024)*t, gamma=1/(1023*pi); slow clock s=gamma^2*tau/2 transforms both evolution rows
    Original children Four independently admitted isolated class-one C9 source families, each with the original form and phase allowances 1e-10, including all their fine directions and the unchanged fixed recovery dwell
    Recovered uncertainty The original recovery certificate must bound each child's actual form and phase residual Euclidean norms by 1e-70; no endpoint is reset to its nominal pattern
    Supplied child origins phi[g,a]=(2*g-1)/40+(2*a-1)/4; the nominal intraparent gap is 1/2, twice the identity threshold, and the nominal outer gap is 1/20
    Source means Each child's form mean is zero; its phase residual about its supplied origin and winding carrier has mean zero
    Attachment Both contact layers are supplied at release; their actual storage work is retained separately from the preceding isolated recovery
    Acquisition deadline s=4, four slow relaxation units after attachment
    Entry domain Both form and lifted-phase residuals about each actual parent mean are strictly below 1/100 in coordinate sup norm
    Identity Every child retains winding one from release through s=5; every intraparent same-slot gap is strictly below 1/4 throughout 4<=s<=5
    Predictive handoff Continue from the actual parent means and residuals at s=4; do not reset forms or restore the original outer phase gap
    Readout and resolution Predict the parent lifted-phase-gap change from s=4 to s=5 with absolute error below 1e-5; require a decrease greater than 1e-4 for the entire original source family
    Decision Source-inclusive entry, retained identity and resolved predictive handoff, or an explicit scoped obstruction; a failed sufficient inequality is unavailable

    The half-radian intraparent source expresses missing registration, independently of its eventual response. Four relaxation units and the subsequent single-unit readout are fixed analytic horizons, not values chosen by a trajectory search. Relative origins, contact occurrence and their work remain supplied premises. These clocks and observations are structural quantities without a laboratory measurement bridge.

    An exact antipodal child-registration control may test nonentry under the same complete law. Such an invariant control concerns its explicitly restricted source; it cannot replace the four independently uncertain acquired families or establish nonentry for a neighborhood without a separate proof.

    1. Complete law, state and the two moving modes¶

    Retain all 36 signed forms and primitive phases, with continuous phase lifts on the marked nodes 18*g+9*a+j. Let alpha=2*pi/9 and psi[j]=alpha*(j-4). The graph is the Cartesian product \(C_9\mathbin\square K_2\mathbin\square K_2\), with the fixed unit conductances specified above. Its unnormalized Laplacian is \(L\). The complete rows and storage are exactly those of the matching control:

    \[ x_\tau=\frac{-Lx+\gamma S(\theta)}4,\qquad \theta_\tau=\frac{\gamma Lx}4,\qquad S_i=\sum_{k\sim i}\sin(\theta_k-\theta_i), \tag{1} \]
    \[ H=\tfrac12x^TLx+\sum_{\{i,k\}}[1-\cos(\theta_k-\theta_i)],\qquad H_\tau=-\tfrac14\|Lx\|_2^2. \tag{2} \]

    The first row independently supplies the pressure for the held unit capacity. Support, the two child/parent markings, capacity and structural clock remain supplied premises. There is no additional input after attachment. All phases and both global means remain part of the law; a circular resultant is not substituted for the lifted phase observations.

    Define a moving reference, not a replacement of the recovered source:

    \[ \begin{split} x^*_{g,a,j}&=\tfrac12[(2g-1)u+(2a-1)v],\\ \theta^*_{g,a,j}&=\psi_j+ \tfrac12[(2g-1)\Delta+(2a-1)B]. \end{split}\tag{3} \]

    The two coordinate directions separate under the actual matching contacts. Internal cycle currents cancel, intraparent currents depend on \(B\), and outer currents depend on \(\Delta\). Consequently, both pairs \((w,D)=(v,B)\) and \((u,\Delta)\) obey

    \[ w_\tau=-\tfrac12w-\tfrac\gamma2\sin D,\qquad D_\tau=\tfrac\gamma2w. \tag{4} \]

    This follows by substituting into both fine rows, so smooth uniqueness preserves the separated family. In the slow clock, put \(\varepsilon=\gamma^2\) and \(Y=-w/\gamma\). Both rows transform to

    \[ D_s=-Y,\qquad \varepsilon Y_s=\sin D-Y, \qquad (D(0),Y(0))=(d,0), \quad d\in\{1/2,1/20\}. \tag{5} \]

    The form coordinate is retained as \(Y\); replacing the second row by \(Y=\sin D\) would be a different, approximate law.

    2. Source admission and full fine-state error¶

    Rebuild four independent copies of the isolated own-law recovery at deformation zero from the original 1e-10 allowances. Each carries its actual state after the unchanged formation and recovery times, with \(\|r_c(0)\|_2,\|z_c(0)\|_2\le q=10^{-70}\), winding one and both residual means zero. Translating the four disconnected phase origins does not change their internal storage or dynamics. These translations supply new preparation information and do not assert autonomous registration.

    For the joined state set \(r=x-x^*\) and \(z=\theta-\theta^*\). Then \(\|r(0)\|_2,\|z(0)\|_2\le2q\). The source retains 64 independent fine residual directions: eight zero-mean coordinates in each channel of each child. It is not the earlier 68-direction parentwise residual ball. After attachment only the global means are conserved; no child mean is reset.

    The fixed support obeys

    \[ \lambda_2(L)>1/3,\qquad \|L\|_2\le8. \tag{6} \]

    Indeed its eigenvalues are sums of those of \(C_9,K_2,K_2\). The C9 gap is \(2-2\cos(2\pi/9)>104/243>1/3\), using \(2\pi/9>2/3\) and the cosine Taylor bound. Degree four gives the upper bound eight. For any two lifted phase states, the mean-value edge Hessian gives \(\|S(\theta)-S(\theta^*)\|_2\le8\|z\|_2\), even without acute gaps. Its possibly signed conductances have magnitude at most one.

    Integrating the form row first and substituting it into the phase row yields the exact identity

    \[ \begin{split} z(\tau)={}&z(0)+\gamma(I-e^{-L\tau/4})r(0)\\ &+\frac{\gamma^2}{4}\int_0^\tau (I-e^{-L(\tau-t)/4})[S(\theta(t))-S(\theta^*(t))]dt. \end{split}\tag{7} \]

    Both matrix norms \(\|e^{-Lt/4}\|_2\) and \(\|I-e^{-Lt/4}\|_2\) are at most one. Gronwall therefore gives \(\|z(\tau)\|_2\le2q(1+\gamma)e^{2\gamma^2\tau}\). The form error is globally mean zero; (6) bounds its heat convolution. For the complete declared interval \(0\le s\le5\), retain the exact rational bounds, with \(\bar\gamma\) the outward upper bound of \(\gamma\):

    \[ Z=2q(1+\bar\gamma)3^{20},\qquad R=2q+24\bar\gamma Z,\qquad \|z\|_2\le Z<10^{-60},\quad \|r\|_2\le R<10^{-60}. \tag{8} \]

    Here \(2\gamma^2\tau=4s\le20\) and \(e<3\). The factor 24 is \((8/4)/(\lambda_2(L)/4)\le24\). These are bounds on all original fine perturbations about a moving reference, not a symmetry assumption on the actual source. Their smallness comes from the already required recovery; no original allowance or dwell is changed. Tiny exact quantities remain rational rather than being rounded to zero on the interval grid.

    3. Whole-source entry and child retention¶

    Let \(a=1+2\varepsilon\), with \(0<\varepsilon<10^{-7}\). For each equation (5), the region

    \[ 0<D\le d,\qquad 0\le Y\le aD \tag{9} \]

    is invariant. At its upper sloped boundary, \(\varepsilon(Y-aD)_s\le(1-a+\varepsilon a^2)D<0\); at \(Y=0\), the derivative is nonnegative. In particular \(D\) decreases and stays positive, with \(D(s)\ge d e^{-as}\). Since \(D\le1/2\), \(\sin D\ge(23/24)D\). The exponential convolution for \(Y\) and monotonicity of \(\sin D\) along the trajectory give

    \[ Y(s)\ge\sin D(s)(1-e^{-s/\varepsilon}),\qquad d e^{-as}\le D(s)\le d\exp[-(23/24)(s-\varepsilon)]. \tag{10} \]

    The right inequality integrates \(D_s/D\); dropping the positive \(\varepsilon e^{-s/\varepsilon}\) term only enlarges the bound. At the fixed deadline it implies

    \[ B(4)<1/64,\qquad \Delta(4)>1/1200. \tag{11} \]

    These strict elementary inequalities are checked with outward exponential bounds. For example \(e^{4+8\varepsilon}<60\), using \(e<11/4\) and \(e^{8\varepsilon}\le(1-8\varepsilon)^{-1}\), gives the second inequality. Both gaps keep decreasing in the continuation.

    At release every actual intraparent same-slot gap is at least \(1/2-2q>1/4\). Thus the entire source initially lacks parent identity. Throughout passage, every oriented internal edge has its continuous branch in \([\alpha-2Z,\alpha+2Z]\subset(0,\pi/2)\). The closing edge retains its original branch integer, and the residual differences telescope to a total \(2\pi\). Every child keeps winding one on \(0\le s\le5\).

    Use the residual convention of repeated composition: subtract the actual parent form mean from form, and subtract the winding carrier \(\psi_j\) and actual parent phase mean \(\Phi_g\) from phase. Thus \(r_\theta=\max_{g,a,j}|\theta_{g,a,j}-\psi_j-\Phi_g|\), not the distance of all primitive phases from a common phase. The carrier has zero mean, so the mean of phase offsets equals the mean of these declared phase lifts. For every \(4\le s\le5\), the fine residual sup norms satisfy

    \[ r_\theta\le B(4)/2+2Z<1/128+2Z<1/100, \quad r_x\le\bar\gamma(1+2\bar\varepsilon)B(4)/2+2R<1/100, \tag{12} \]

    where \(\bar\varepsilon\) is an outward upper bound of \(\gamma^2\). Every actual intraparent same-slot gap is smaller than \(1/64+2Z<1/4\). This proves entry by the fixed time and subsequent maintenance on the declared interval, for the entire source, while all four child identities remain intact.

    4. Predictive use from the reached state¶

    The outer current on the moving separated reference is exactly \(\sin\Delta\), although its children still have different origins. Both parents carry the same internal mode \(B\), so the actual port association matters. Bound (8) retains its perturbation during the whole slow interval. Membership in the parent residual ball alone does not prove this long prediction: the older generic theorem has horizon one in \(\tau\), not one in \(s\).

    First initialize the nonlinear four-coordinate parent law from all four actual observations at \(s=4\): \((X_0,X_1,\Phi_0,\Phi_1)\). There is no replacement by zero forms or the original Delta=1/20. The two-node version of (7), with Laplacian norm two, implies over the following slow unit

    \[ |\Delta_{\rm actual}(s)-\Delta_{\rm parent}(s)| \le 2Z+e^{s-4}(2Z+2\bar\gamma R) <8Z+6\bar\gamma R. \tag{13} \]

    The first term compares the actual fine state with the original moving reference; the second carries the reduced predictor's true initial discrepancy. Thus the controlled parent dynamics keeps actual forms and means, with a quantitative source-specific error rather than an exact quotient.

    An explicit forecast for the selected observation uses

    \[ F(A)=2\arctan(e^{-1}\tan(A/2)),\qquad A=\Delta_{\rm actual}(4). \tag{14} \]

    This is the time-one map of \(D_s=-\sin D\), a declared approximation. Its error is justified from the full retained history. For the nominal outer mode, write \(h=Y-\sin D\). Variation of constants in (5) gives

    \[ h(s)=-\sin(d)e^{-s/\varepsilon} +\int_0^s e^{-(s-t)/\varepsilon}\cos D(t)Y(t)dt. \tag{15} \]

    For \(s\ge4\), \(e^{-s/\varepsilon}\le\varepsilon/4\), while \(Y\le ad\) and \(d=1/20\). Therefore \(|h|\le d\varepsilon(1/4+a)<\varepsilon/10\). The positive small-angle gradient flow is nonexpansive; its comparison with the nominal pendulum for one unit costs at most \(\varepsilon/10\). The initial and final actual-gap errors each cost at most \(2Z\). Hence

    \[ |\Delta_{\rm actual}(5)-F(\Delta_{\rm actual}(4))| \le\bar\varepsilon/10+4Z<10^{-5}. \tag{16} \]

    The observed initial gap is not a nominal reset. Forms and source history enter the lag bound (15); (14) by itself certifies neither that history nor a universal phase-only law.

    The readout is resolved, not merely enclosed near zero. On \([4,5]\), (10) implies \(Y>(3/4)D\), so \(D(5)<e^{-3/4}D(4)<D(4)/2\). Using (11) and retaining both fine errors proves

    \[ \Delta_{\rm actual}(4)-\Delta_{\rm actual}(5) >1/2400-4Z>1/2500>10^{-4}. \tag{17} \]

    The forecast error bound is independent of evaluating a reduced or full trajectory. This is a finite analytic prediction about the declared source family, without a sensor or laboratory-time interpretation.

    5. Preparation and attachment work¶

    The four isolated phase translations preserve internal storage. At attachment, both sets of new contacts contribute their actual instantaneous storage jump:

    \[ W=\sum_{\text{new }\{i,k\}} [\tfrac12(x_k-x_i)^2+1-\cos(\theta_k-\theta_i)]. \tag{18} \]

    At the nominal source, \(W^*=18[1-\cos(1/2)]+18[1-\cos(1/20)]>2\). All first-order potential corrections cancel by the original childwise zero sums, despite nonzero nominal contact currents. The contact Laplacian has norm four, and \(\|r(0)\|_2,\|z(0)\|_2\le2q\). Taylor's second-order bound gives

    \[ 0\le W-W^*\le16q^2. \tag{19} \]

    The lower bound uses convexity along the acute initial contact gaps; the upper bound includes both form and phase terms. Likewise, \(0\le H(0)-[36(1-\cos\alpha)+W^*]\le32q^2\), using \(\|L\|_2\le8\). These exact tiny corrections are retained separately from nominal irrational intervals. The support event requires external work; prior continuous loss is not reused as an undeclared reservoir. Source storage, acquired registration and positive dissipation do not establish a physical binding energy.

    6. Acquired-source antipodal nonentry control¶

    Keep the same matching support and complete law. Within each parent choose two identical copies of an actual recovered child state, and translate one copy's phase by exactly \(\pi\). Fix their supplied origins as \(\phi^\pi_{g,a}=(2g-1)/40+(2a-1)\pi/2\), retaining the outer nominal gap \(1/20\). Thus

    \[ x_{g,1,j}=x_{g,0,j},\qquad \theta_{g,1,j}=\theta_{g,0,j}+\pi. \tag{20} \]

    This is a correlated source subset with genuine nonzero fine residuals allowed. Intraparent form and sine currents vanish; internal cycle currents and same-child outer currents are identical in the two copies. Both evolution rows preserve (20) by uniqueness. Consequently every intraparent gap remains antipodal for all future time, and parent registration never enters its quarter-radian identity domain. This uses the exact circular identity \(\sin\pi=0\), not the sine of a floating-point approximation to pi.

    The same global perturbation bound around the antipodal moving reference preserves each child's winding through \(s=5\). No all-future winding claim or independently perturbed nonentry neighborhood follows. Its nominal work is \(W^*_{\pi}=36+18[1-\cos(1/20)]\), with correction in \([0,8q^2]\). This is a different preparation and work budget, not an equal-work comparison. It proves that the admitted law and acquired children alone do not force every registration to organize; relative preparation remains consequential.

    7. Evidence and scope¶

    The detached assessor re-admits all primitive allowances before rebuilding the four isolated recoveries. It reuses the existing matching geometry and shared outward arithmetic, retaining source admission separately from conditional bounds. The independent controls check fine and separated rows, both clocks, spectral bounds, every strict entry/readout margin, work corrections, primitive rejection and the antipodal symmetry. The forecast arithmetic helper supplies only (14), not an acquisition certificate for an arbitrary supplied observation.

    The result supplies acquired parent registration and a resolved subsequent interaction from the original uncertain children under one supplied law and support. The inherited long recovery dwell and the slow structural horizon remain mathematical preparation premises. Autonomous preparation, contact selection, structural separation resistance, indefinite maintenance, other constitutive laws and physical identification require their own arguments. The matching support is symmetric under exchange of its two binary factors; their nominal modes obey the same relaxation law. The different initial amplitudes and the marked grouping do not select a unique hierarchy or a separation of internal and external timescales.

    The separate restoring-response admission carries this reached source through one supplied internal phase intervention, accounts for its work and tests finite recovery and sufficient predictive state. It does not extend this theorem's unchanged-source prediction to an intervened state without that additional argument.
