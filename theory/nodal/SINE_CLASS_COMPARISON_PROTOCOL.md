# A scaled complete-law protocol for comparing mediator organizations

<a id="sine-class-comparison-protocol"></a>

The [amplitude feasibility theorem](SINE_CLASS_AMPLITUDE_FEASIBILITY.md#sine-amplitude-feasible-interval)
admits a whole interval of interventions with a central
organization-dependent contrast separated from the declared noisy null
alternative. This protocol fixes one intervention and an independent
forward calculation. It preserves the actual acquired source families
while using nominal full-state trajectories as numerical references and
transporting them with a separate, proved source-error bound.

This owner admits and freezes a prospective numerical policy. It does
not evaluate a response or promise that the chosen interval algorithm
will complete within its width target. The
[execution plan](../research/FIVE_STAGE_EXECUTION_PLAN.md#current-g3-gate)
owns any subsequent execution and retention of its first outcome.

Retain all 54 original form and continuous phase coordinates on three
C9 rings, contacts \((4,13)\), \((13,22)\), unit capacities and
the complete law
\[
 x'=-Ax+\gamma KS(\theta),\qquad \theta'=\gamma Ax,\qquad
 A=KL,\quad K=D^{-1},\quad
 \gamma=\frac1{1023\pi},\quad \tau=et,\quad e=\frac{1023}{1024}.
 \tag{1}
\]
The degree matrix \(D\) is fixed by the actual joined support. Compare
the two component classes \((1,1,1)\) and \((1,2,1)\).
The [original preparation and source costs](SINE_CLASS_NONLINEAR_ORGANIZATION.md#sine-nonlinear-organization-source)
remain unchanged: after the specified acquisition and relaxation,
each component has separate zero-sum form and target-phase deviations
with Euclidean norms at most \(\epsilon=10^{-32}\). The two class
families have independent residuals. Within each class, all four
continuations share one actual complete source.

Select
\[
 \lambda=\frac75,\qquad a=b=\frac7{10000},\qquad
 s=1,\quad T=2.
 \tag{2}
\]
This choice follows a declared criterion, not a response search.
On the already admitted interval \([4/3,7/5]\), use its uniform
upper bound \(E(7/5)\) for the complete higher-amplitude and source
allowance. The certified oriented lower bound
\(-j_+\lambda^3-E(7/5)\), with the retained \(j_+<0\), is strictly
increasing. The upper endpoint maximizes that certified bound while
retaining every proved work and identity guard.

The two donor-only form jumps retain node \(4\), their exact times
and all complete preevent states. Neither event changes phase.
The missing-event controls are \((00,10,01,11)\); the delayed-only
history carries its unprobed prefix. No source, mediator, mean,
phase lift or hidden mode is reset at the second event.

For class \(k\), let
\[
 M_k=x_{k,00,22}(T)-x_{k,10,22}(T)
                  -x_{k,01,22}(T)+x_{k,11,22}(T),
 \qquad D_{\mathrm c}=M_1-M_2.
 \tag{3}
\]
There are eight final scalar form readings. Each retains its own
error bound \(\delta=10^{-30}\), with no distributional assumption.
Hence \(|\widehat D_{\mathrm c}-D_{\mathrm c}|\le8\delta\).
The separately noisy zero-class-contrast alternative has statistic
in \([-8\delta,8\delta]\). Its exclusion through (3) requires the
strict sufficient true-response condition beyond \(\pm16\delta\),
not merely a signed numerical midpoint.

Keep radius \(r=1/12\), contact-work allowance \(10^{-12}\) and
each probe-work allowance \(2\,10^{-6}\). The
[carried-work theorem](SINE_CLASS_AMPLITUDE_FEASIBILITY.md#sine-amplitude-carried-work)

## Actual source covers and numerical references

Let \(\Pi=[\pi_-,\pi_+]\) be the independently certified
256-bit rational enclosure of \(\pi\) supplied by the shared
Machin construction. For component \(c\), local node \(j=0,\ldots,8\)
and class tuple \(k_c\), retain the canonical actual-source covers
\[
 I^{\mathrm{actual}}_{x,cj}=[-\epsilon,\epsilon],\qquad
 I^{\mathrm{actual}}_{\theta,cj}
   =\frac{2k_c(j-4)}9\Pi+[-\epsilon,\epsilon].
 \tag{4}
\]
Use the correct endpoint order for negative phase coefficients and
retain the exact rational pairs before dyadic materialization.
The original componentwise Euclidean caps and zero sums describe a
correlated acquired subset of these covers. Arbitrary independent
corners need not be acquired or satisfy those correlations.

The independent full-law numerical references instead use
\[
 I^{\mathrm{ref}}_{x,cj}=[0,0],\qquad
 I^{\mathrm{ref}}_{\theta,cj}=\frac{2k_c(j-4)}9\Pi.
 \tag{5}
\]
These boxes enclose the exact nominal target
\(x=0,\ \theta_{c,j}=2\pi k_c(j-4)/9\).
They are proof references for a numerical comparison, not instructions
to replace or sharpen the physical preparation. The actual source
families and their covers (4) are retained separately in the protocol
association. Every reference history obeys the complete nonlinear
54-coordinate law and the same carried events as its actual counterpart.
It is not a tangent trajectory or an amplitude-coefficient solution.

This separation addresses a numerical dependence issue without
discarding physical source uncertainty. Direct propagation of Cartesian
source uncertainty by interval arithmetic can lose the correlation
between a source coordinate and its increment. Even the scalar
contractive equation \(z'=-z\) illustrates the problem: direct interval
Taylor terms from \(z(0)\in[-\epsilon,\epsilon]\), combined without
their common-source dependence, enlarge that radius despite contraction
of the exact flow. Finer steps do not in general remove this source
wrapping. This example is a method warning, not a proof that the
complete sine comparison or every box method must fail.

No assumed factorization of actual sources is introduced by (5).
The following bound transports numerical reference observations to the
unchanged acquired families after the forward calculation.

## Independent full-law transport of actual-source errors

Set \(g=1/3000>\gamma\). For an actual history and its matching
exact nominal reference, let \(X(t)\) and \(Y(t)\) be maximum-norm
differences in the original form and phase coordinates. Initially
both are at most \(\epsilon\). Heat contraction, the real normalized
sine Lipschitz bound two and the complete phase row give
\[
 X(t)\le\epsilon+2g\int_0^tY(v)\,dv,\qquad
 Y(t)\le\epsilon+2g\int_0^tX(v)\,dv.
 \tag{6}
\]
The same supplied jump is applied to both trajectories at each event,
so their differences do not jump. Consequently
\[
 \max(X(t),Y(t))\le\epsilon\exp(2gt)
                 \le\frac{\epsilon}{1-2gT},\qquad 0\le t\le T.
 \tag{7}
\]
This bound is global in the real phase lifts. It does not divide
phase uncertainty by \(\gamma\), assume nominal reflection symmetry
of actual residuals, or replace the source family by a zero-error state.

If the two complete forward reference calls supply mixed intervals
\(I_1,I_2\), form their independent cross-class enclosure
\[
 I_{\mathrm{ref}}=I_1-I_2,\qquad
 B_{\mathrm{source}}=\frac{8\epsilon}{1-2gT},\qquad
 I_{\mathrm{actual}}=I_{\mathrm{ref}}
                      +[-B_{\mathrm{source}},B_{\mathrm{source}}].
 \tag{8}
\]
The factor eight counts the eight scalar endpoints. Independence of
cross-class source residuals is retained; no actual-source cancellation
is assumed. The same actual source within each four-history family
remains essential to the definition of \(M_k\).

The transport claim applies to the original correlated family relative
to its exact nominal target, not to every independent corner of the
\(\Pi\)-expanded boxes (4). Target rounding and outward materialization
in (5) already belong to \(I_{\mathrm{ref}}\); they are not ignored
or represented as measurement noise. At the fixed design,
\[
 B_{\mathrm{source}}<8.011\,10^{-32}.
 \tag{9}
\]
The coefficient prediction supplies neither \(I_1\), \(I_2\) nor
the transport mechanism (6)-(8).

## F3: complete forward execution and numerical budget

Use the existing
[four-history full-state producer](../../src/tnfr/physics/relational_sine_class_readout.py)
once per class with (5). Admit both complete primitive sources and
the common execution parameters before the first call. Fix class-one
then class-two order. Retain each class's two prefixes and four
suffixes, full-state endpoint handoffs and exact donor jumps.
No cross-class prefix is shared.

For each class, retain all four raw endpoint bands and the primary
mixed interval formed from the four suffix receiver increments.
Shared-prefix receiver coordinates cancel symbolically within that
class. This avoids subtracting an endpoint box from itself, but does
not restore all remaining dependencies between steps or branches.
Keep the raw endpoint cross-class difference as a separate diagnostic;
neither construction is intersected with the analytic prediction.

Fix the following numerical policy before the reserved response:

| Quantity | Fixed value or rule |
| --- | --- |
| Common scale | \(7/5\), selected by the certified monotone lower bound |
| Form impulses | \(a=b=7/10000\) at structural times 0 and 1 |
| Final time | 2 |
| Step | \(1/128\), clipped only at declared event/final boundaries |
| Taylor order | 16 |
| Unique attempts | At most 768 per class; 1536 in total |
| Complete branch work | Two prefixes and four suffixes, each of 128 steps per class |
| Arithmetic | Shared exact rational and outward dyadic128 intervals |
| Target enclosure | Independent 256-bit rational \(\pi\) bounds, then outward source materialization |
| Tube and remainder | Unchanged strict Picard inclusion and source-box Taylor kernel |
| Width postcondition | \(\operatorname{width}(I_{\mathrm{actual}})\le10^{-30}\) |
| Adaptation | None: no step, precision, source, amplitude or coefficient search |
| First failure | Stop all later steps, branches and class calls; retain completed and failed evidence |

For a bounded order-17 remainder coefficient, halving the step doubles
the number of steps and reduces the accumulated local truncation scale
by \(2^{16}\). This motivates the selected step relative to the earlier
one-class calculation, but it is not a proof of the actual interval
width. Natural interval jets, Picard admission, arithmetic rounding and
wrapping still require their own retained postconditions. No bound on
exact derivatives alone is presented as a bound on the computed
interval width.

The width target applies after the source transport (8). It leaves
the reference calculation the exact allowance
\[
 W_{\mathrm{ref}}=10^{-30}-2B_{\mathrm{source}}
 >8.397\,10^{-31},\qquad
 \operatorname{width}(I_{\mathrm{ref}})\le W_{\mathrm{ref}}.
 \tag{10}
\]
Both widths and \(B_{\mathrm{source}}\) are retained separately.
This is a fixed sufficient acceptance budget, not a guarantee that
the selected algorithm meets it. Removing repeated propagation of
\(\epsilon\) through independent boxes does not remove that
obligation.

There is one shared cap of 1536 attempts. Each child receives the
remaining allowance, subject to the producer's existing per-call cap;
its fixed grid has at most 768 planned attempts and admits no retry.
Thus the first child's retained cap need not equal the second's.
The cap counts kernel attempts, including a failed attempt. A failure
in class one leaves class two unattempted. A failure in class two
retains the completed class-one result and available class-two prefixes.
No missing history, final time or cross-class contrast is represented
by zero. The reference and transported contrasts remain unavailable
until both complete four-history calls succeed.

## F4: prediction, sufficient resolution and first-outcome classification

Exact homogeneity of the retained complete cubic enclosure, with the
proved fifth-and-higher amplitude remainder, supplies the nominal
reference prediction
\[
 J_{\mathrm{ref}}=(-19.254,-19.237)\,10^{-30}.
 \tag{11}
\]
Adding the independent source allowance in (8) to the underlying
exact rational nominal bound, before display rounding, gives the
separately rounded actual-family prediction
\[
 J_{\mathrm{actual}}=(-19.334,-19.157)\,10^{-30}.
 \tag{12}
\]
These are open intervals with exact terminating rational endpoints.
Equation (12) is not obtained by expanding the already rounded
display endpoints in (11).
They are fixed predictions; they never enter the forward field,
Taylor coefficients, tube selection or endpoint arithmetic. The
full comparison uses the forward \(I_{\mathrm{actual}}\), without
intersecting it with (12) or selecting a favorable point from either
interval.

The required width is tied to the predicted margin. For valid
forward enclosures, a common true value belongs to
\(I_{\mathrm{actual}}\cap J_{\mathrm{actual}}\).
If (10)'s transported width postcondition holds, then
\[
 \sup I_{\mathrm{actual}}
  < -19.157\,10^{-30}+10^{-30}
  = -18.157\,10^{-30}<-16\delta.
 \tag{13}
\]
Thus that postcondition is sufficient for the desired separation,
with more than \(2.157\,10^{-30}\) reserve. This conditional
implication does not promise completion or width. The actual
forward sign and separation inequalities are checked directly.

Retain distinct exact decisions:

1. **Forward availability:** both class calls complete their full
   states and four observations under the declared attempt caps,
   law, source, clock, event and numerical evidence.
2. **Width:** both reference and transported widths are reported;
   the latter meets its fixed \(10^{-30}\) budget.
3. **Consistency:** the closed numerical reference interval overlaps
   (11), and the closed transported interval overlaps (12).
   For \(I=[l,u]\), \(J=(L,U)\), overlap means \(u>L\) and
   \(l<U\). Numerical-band containment in a prediction is a
   separate diagnostic, not required agreement or a reason to shrink it.
4. **True and recorded signs:** respectively
   \(\sup I_{\mathrm{actual}}<0\) and
   \(\sup I_{\mathrm{actual}}<-8\delta\).
   The recorded band is \(I_{\mathrm{actual}}+[-8\delta,8\delta]\).
5. **Named-alternative exclusion:** \(\sup I_{\mathrm{actual}}<-16\delta\)
   separates its recorded statistic from the independently noisy
   zero-contrast statistic. The raw eight-record sets are then
   disjoint through their fixed linear statistic.

Missing forward observations make dependent bounds and decisions
unavailable. An available but wide interval can be inconclusive;
failure of a sufficient test is not an overlap theorem. Disjoint
forward and prediction intervals are conflicting evidence requiring
an audit of numerical, mathematical and source premises, not an
automatic physical counterexample. If discrimination inequalities
pass but the width policy fails, retain both facts without claiming
that every protocol postcondition passed.

Freeze source recipes, all realized reference and actual-cover bounds,
the original acquired-family association, the law/support/clock,
event order, observation, prediction, numerical policy, source code
and stopping predicates before executing a reserved response.
Preserve the first completed or failed outcome without changing
parameters after seeing it. Subsequent correction or refinement
would be separate evidence, not a rewrite of this protocol.

The result sought is a complete-law software comparison under
declared source and observation budgets. It neither supplies a
laboratory clock/noise calibration nor identifies a unique physical
law, fundamental particle property or autonomous intervention.
