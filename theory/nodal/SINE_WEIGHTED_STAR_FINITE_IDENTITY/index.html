    Finite registration and collective identity after weighted-star contact¶

    This owner tests necessary conditions for finite common registration under the actual canonical contact. Registration is a supplied geometric observation. Calling an episode a new collective NFR additionally requires the identity, retained state and response obligations in the ontology. The research program alone queues work.

    The nonlinear continuation is the declared held weighted sine model. Its full real-lift tangent at consensus is a separate mathematical control, not a globally equivalent circular field or a second proposed canonical law. Canonical describes the registered AL/UM/SHA preparation; the law-admission owner retains the additional premises needed to justify a complete continuation.

    1. Analytical question and fixed static-control contract¶

    Retain the complete ten-node source, word and held laws of the contact owner. There are no new operator invocations, changed weights, sources, forcing, capacity laws, physical clocks or event selectors. Each nominal post-word law keeps its own support, degrees, capacities and conductances. The existing independent form/radian boxes have radius zeta=2^-20; they do not rerun the word or replace perturbed state by an exact collective family.

    Use the previously fixed observation Q=||P*x||^2/X0^2 + min_phi sum_i d_circle(theta_i,phi)^2/Theta0^2, with P=I-11^T/10, one common circle center, X0 one form unit, Theta0 one radian and target Q<=1/4. Its absence at every nominal preparation and throughout the full-dimensional boxes is already proved. The two original marked constituent identities remain part of the question. A change of the observation or its scales would be a different study.

    The permanent-registration obstruction does not exclude finite entry. Before committing to an episode calculation, compare the following actual laws and distinguish their conserved quantities:

    Alternative State and dynamics Purpose

    Matched disconnected law The existing functional-links-disabled word output, with its own degrees, capacities and two components Test whether separate component invariants exclude the same circular target for all time
    Full consensus tangent The same joined initial fine state and declared real lifts, with sine differences replaced by those real differences in the full twenty-coordinate law Test geometric compatibility of its own quadratic storage and means; later compare whether nonlinear terms are needed for a proposed episode

    The tangent is a separately declared real-lift model. Its linear force does not define a periodic vector field on the phase torus. Neither the two regional outputs nor their eight-dimensional tangent realization determine the full nonlinear registration observation.

    Analytical obligations.     The admissible result is a scoped obstruction or compatibility result, with remaining dynamical and identity premises explicit.     2.1 A protected continuous path with the same displayed invariants¶

    The compatibility is not limited to a disconnected point of the constant-storage set. Use the source's retained lifts and, for \(0\le s\le1\), set

    \[ \theta(s)=\bar\theta{\bf1} +(1-s)(\theta^0-\bar\theta{\bf1}),\qquad v(s)=(1-s)(x^0-\bar x{\bf1})+sX_0q. \tag{2.9} \]

    Here the scalar \(v=W_{49}>9/10\) is the retained edge conductance; the vector \(v(s)\) is the interpolated form direction. This bound includes both endpoints and all independent source errors without sampling the path.

    \[ V(\theta(s))\le V(\theta^0),\qquad H_0-V(\theta(s))\ge F(x^0)>0. \tag{2.11} \]

    2.2 The full circular observation has an exact admitted lift formula¶

    A useful geometric identity avoids a generic circle minimization on this admitted domain. Suppose real representatives \(\vartheta_i\) have \(\max_i\vartheta_i-\min_i\vartheta_i<\pi\). For any candidate common center, let \(\delta_i\) be its closest real phase differences. The variance identity and the definition of shortest circle distance give

    \[ \begin{split} \sum_i\delta_i^2 &\ge \frac1n\sum_{i<j}(\delta_i-\delta_j)^2\\ &\ge \frac1n\sum_{i<j}d_{\mathbb S^1}(\vartheta_i,\vartheta_j)^2\\ &=\frac1n\sum_{i<j}(\vartheta_i-\vartheta_j)^2 =\sum_i(\vartheta_i-\bar\vartheta)^2. \end{split} \tag{2.13} \]

    The strict span assumption justifies the middle equality. Taking the common center to be the arithmetic mean \(\bar\vartheta\) modulo \(2\pi\) attains the last expression, since every \(|\vartheta_i-\bar\vartheta|<\pi\). Thus this is the global circle minimum.

    In the joined support, node 4 is adjacent to all nine other nodes. Every admitted initial hub gap is less than \(2/5+2\zeta<\pi/3\) in magnitude. Conservation of \(H<1/2\) prevents any continued hub edge gap from first reaching \(\pi/3\) or \(-\pi/3\), whose single bare phase cost would already be \(1/2\). Consequently, for all time,

    \[ |\theta_i-\theta_4|<\frac\pi3,\qquad \max_i\theta_i-\min_i\theta_i<\frac{2\pi}{3}<\pi. \tag{2.14} \]

    The same gap condition holds along the constructed path, where every source gap shrinks. Therefore (2.13) gives

    \[ \boxed{\displaystyle Q=\frac{\|Px\|_2^2}{X_0^2} +\frac{\|P\theta\|_2^2}{\Theta_0^2}} \tag{2.15} \]

    on the actual joined flow and on that path, using their retained common lifts. This is an exact formula for the full-state observation, not a reduction to regional means or to the eight tangent coordinates. Individually wrapped stored representatives cannot simply be inserted into the raw variance. Their association with the common hub chart, or with the continued lifts, remains necessary. No assertion about arbitrary phase configurations outside this domain has changed.

    3.1 Associate the circular observation with the retained means¶

    The existing full-coordinate storage bound, Section 20.2, gives each disconnected component the independent all-time storage budget

    \[ H_L,\ H_R\le(1/8+7\zeta)^2<\frac1{32}. \tag{3.3} \]

    Its original internal edge lift gaps have magnitude at most \(2\zeta\). A first crossing of \(\pi/2\) or \(-\pi/2\) would put at least one bare edge phase cost at one, contrary to (3.3). Every such continued edge gap is therefore acute at every time.

    Let

    \[ J(\theta)=\min_{\varphi\in\mathbb S^1} \sum_i d_{\mathbb S^1}(\theta_i,\varphi)^2 \]

    be the full phase part of \(Q\) in the fixed radian unit. Suppose first that \(J\le1\), and choose a minimizing common center. Its nearest phase lifts are \(\varphi+\delta_i\), with \(|\delta_i|\le1\). Write each continued phase as \(\theta_i=\varphi+\delta_i+2\pi n_i\) for integers \(n_i\). Along an old edge the difference between its continued acute gap and \(\delta_j-\delta_i\) has magnitude less than

    \[ \frac\pi2+2<2\pi. \]

    It is also an integer multiple of \(2\pi\), so it is zero. Connectivity of each old star makes \(n_i\) one constant integer on the left and one on the right. Consequently, for

    \[ \ell=(-a_L,a_R),\qquad c=\|\ell\|_2^2 =\frac{17785}{88804}+\frac{17}{81}, \tag{3.4} \]

    the difference of the retained component means gives

    \[ |\ell^\mathsf T\delta| =|\Delta-2\pi k|\ge\Delta \quad\text{for some }k\in\mathbb Z. \tag{3.5} \]

    The last inequality uses (3.2), not an assumed globally single-valued phase mean. Cauchy--Schwarz now yields \(J=\sum_i\delta_i^2\ge\Delta^2/c\).

    The same bound holds when \(J>1\). Indeed each normalized five-node weight vector has squared norm at least \(1/5\), so \(c\ge2/5\); by (3.2), \(\Delta^2/c<5/8<1\). Thus for every time and without a raw-variance substitution,

    \[ \boxed{\displaystyle Q\ge J\ge\frac{\Delta^2}{c} >\frac94\left(\frac38-2\zeta\right)^2 >\frac14+\frac{17407}{262144}.} \tag{3.6} \]

    Here \(c<4/9\), since both summands in (3.4) are less than \(2/9\). The final strict bound follows from \(17/256-27\zeta/8+9\zeta^2>17/256-4\zeta\) at the frozen \(\zeta=2^{-20}\). For the exact nominal source, the stronger \(\zeta=0\) lower bound is available.

    Therefore the full same-observation disconnected alternative never enters \(Q\le1/4\), for any nominal source or any member of its admitted box. This is a full-state, all-time control obstruction. It does not follow merely from target absence initially, from a component being disconnected, or from independently recentering the two stars.
