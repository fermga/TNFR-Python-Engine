    Prospective question and complete-state scope¶

    The finite directional transit proves actual short-arc deformation through slow time \(1/4\). Its endpoint is not an admission to the compatible equilibrium's basin. This separate gate asks whether the same prepared full-state family can reach a certified basin after a longer, explicitly declared transit.

    Retain exactly that owner's two unit C9 cycles, contacts (0,9) and (1,10), held unit capacities, beta one, \(e=1023/1024\), \(w=1/1024\), no forcing and no events. Let \(L\) be the actual unit Laplacian, \(M\) the actual degree matrix, \(K=M^{-1}\), and

    \[ \gamma=\frac1{1023\pi},\quad\eta=\gamma^2,\quad \tau=et,\quad\sigma=\eta\tau. \]

    The complete actual rows are unchanged:

    \[ x_\tau=-KLx+\gamma KS(\theta),\qquad \theta_\tau=\gamma KLx, \quad S_i(\theta)=\sum_{j\sim i}\sin(\theta_j-\theta_i). \tag{1} \]

    \[ \widetilde\Theta_{{\rm D},j}=(j-\tfrac12)\frac29,\qquad \widetilde\Theta_{{\rm R},j}=(j-\tfrac12)\frac19. \]

    Form and phase errors are independent nodewise budgets in form units and radians respectively. No zero error sum or reflection constraint is imposed on the thirty-six actual coordinates. Each member retains its own conserved weighted form and continuous phase means.

    \[ g'_{5\to6}>-25/1536+31/432=767/13824>0. \tag{D6} \]

    Thus M2 points out of (D1) at a feasible boundary state. Its smooth flow from that state immediately leaves the buffered domain, although energy decreases. This refutes forward invariance of the proposed whole domain.

    The witness also lies in the same component of the sharper sublevel U<=U(g0) as the nominal source. First vary the geometric relative origin v from phi to delta with uniform rings. All gaps remain buffered and U_nom(v)=U_floor+2-2*cos(delta)*cos(v-delta) decreases, since its derivative is 2*cos(delta)*sin(v-delta)<0 before the endpoint. Then follow (D3) from zero to u_rho, decreasing energy again. Retain the joined mean along both segments. This proves connectedness within that sublevel, not reachability by the specific source trajectory.

    In particular, this proof does not identify edge 5->6 as the cause of the failed Picard candidate. It neither proves that the nominal reference leaves the buffered cell nor excludes a narrower source-specific enclosure. It closes one proposed invariant-domain argument without altering M1--M9.

    The same potential floor extends beyond the acute cell¶

    For any 0<A<pi/2, define R_A(s)=h(s)-h(A)-sin(A)*(s-A). Its derivative is nonpositive on [-pi,A] and nonnegative on [A,pi-A]. Since R_A(A)=0,

    \[ h(s)\ge h(A)+\sin(A)(s-A),\qquad -\pi\le s\le\pi-A. \tag{D7} \]

    This is a supporting-affine bound, not convexity of each edge potential throughout that larger interval. For nine consistently lifted cycle gaps with sum 9*A, summing (D7) cancels the affine terms. Apply it with A=q for the donor and A=r for the receiver. The unchanged floor obeys

    \[ U\ge9h(q)+9h(r)=U_{\rm floor}\quad\hbox{if}\quad -\pi\le g_{D,j}\le5\pi/9,\quad -\pi\le g_{R,j}\le7\pi/9. \tag{D8} \]

    The two contact contributions are nonnegative for every real gap, so this floor statement requires no further contact bound. The old source upper bound, contact accounting and E0=1/4 remain valid on any actual-phase domain contained in (D8). No numerical extremum or new constant is needed.

    The bounds concern fixed affine lifts. They establish neither principal winding preservation, forward invariance nor positive curvature. Affine cycle sums alone cannot give a global floor: one ring gap equal to 4*pi and eight zero gaps have the donor sum but zero donor potential. Domain information remains indispensable.

    A domain contract for the unchanged comparison¶

    The dependencies of the existing energy-informed comparison can now be stated separately:

    More precisely, let Omega be an open convex phase domain invariant under common shifts. Suppose U(theta)>=U_floor for every theta in Omega, and

    \[ \mathcal H(g)=B^T\operatorname{diag}(\cos g)B\succeq0 \quad\hbox{throughout }\Omega. \tag{D9} \]

    If the reference has dist_D(psi(sigma),Omega^c)>Q throughout the complete horizon Sigma=1025, then the same M3--M7 bounds follow. Indeed, (D9) on segments gives nonexpansion, (D9) along the reference gives nonincreasing speed, and the phase floor supplies the same dissipated-storage integral.     This is a conditional transfer of the existing proof, not a constructed Omega or a new numerical admission. Clearance must cover all reference states and their uncertainty, not selected centers or times. If it is proved only through T=1024, the final unit needs an explicit compatible local collar or a justified transition to the existing acute target proof. Its target-arrival and complete-state obligations remain separate.

    The repository already proves that other graph paths can compensate some negative edge cosines in different admitted models: see relative storage and full-network curvature. Those methods require the actual paths, all relative variations and the combined loads on shared edges. Their graphs, laws, numerical bounds and trajectory conclusions do not transfer to this two-port source automatically.     A positive growth bound in place of (D9) changes both the error comparison and the speed estimate. M6--M9's old numerical constants then require a new derivation; changing only an integrator option cannot supply it.

    Closed decision and remaining foundation¶

    The scalar energy-sublevel invariance route is closed by (D6). The floor extension (D8) removes one unnecessary restriction from a possible wider comparison. The remaining question is whether a source-compatible corridor can supply full-domain curvature and whole-time clearance, or whether a specific proposed corridor has an obstruction. A correlated enclosure within the old acute domain remains an alternative if that reference actually stays there; neither result decides its trajectory.

    The first methodological task is to specify such a corridor using the actual cycle constraints and graph paths, and check its curvature and boundary obligations before selecting a numerical policy. No new transit, implicit restart or repair/replay of the failed auxiliary diagnostic is authorized by this analytical result. Formation of the joint identity from the retained actual source, autonomous contact occurrence and physical mapping remain open.
