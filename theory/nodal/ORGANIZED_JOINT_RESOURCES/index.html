    Joint preparation resources for finite collective interaction¶

    Prospective resource declaration¶

    This analytical admission tests a different preparation of the same complete sine law. It does not modify the frozen sources, responses or verdicts in the outer acquisition, sequential observation, external interaction or relative-origin comparison. The original preparation remains valid under its original premises.

    1. A coupled sufficient domain¶

    Write x=EPI, and let \(\alpha=2\pi/9\), \(\gamma=1/(1023\pi)\), \(V_n=n[1-\cos(2\pi/n)]\). On every fixed support in this history,

    \[ x'=(-Lx+\gamma S(\theta))/4,\qquad \theta'=\gamma Lx/4,\qquad H=\tfrac12x^TLx+\sum_e[1-\cos\delta_e],\qquad H'=-\|Lx\|_2^2/4. \tag{1} \]

    The support changes and phase commands have their actual complete storage jumps. They are supplied events, not continuous pressure flows or inferred occurrence laws. Common phase and form translations are isolated symmetries; relative child origins and common forms become consumed state after contact. The NFR ontology retains membership, constituent and boundary state separately from the observed winding label.

    The following conditions define the resource domain. Each must be admitted on the actual history: isolated acquisition and recovery, full-state outer acquisition and capture, inclusion in the observation class, sequential inference and work, then external response, work and finite identity. A successful last inequality cannot replace an unavailable earlier premise.

    Isolated acquisition and class-one recovery¶

    Use the fresh source estimates (20)--(23) of the own-law acquisition proof at deformation zero, half-factor time 100 and the admitted primitive errors \(\rho_x,\rho_\theta\). Their form and target-phase radii are denoted \(b_x,b_\theta\). The initial class-one cost bound is

    \[ B_{s}=36m^2+32m\rho_x+18(\rho_x^2+\rho_\theta^2). \tag{2} \]

    Require the original source budget, initially zero winding, actual entry into the radius-\(r=1/12\) ball and its strict storage trap. No source error is multiplied by a forcing constant or silently replaced by a target. Inside this class-one ball every principal edge gap differs from \(\alpha\) by at most \(\sqrt2r\). Consequently

    \[ c_1L\preceq\nabla^2U\preceq L,\qquad c_1=\cos(\alpha+\sqrt2r)>0. \tag{3} \]

    This is the actual nonlinear Hessian on the retained trap. The earlier two-class proof used a smaller common curvature to include class two; that frozen result is unchanged. Its existence does not require using its smaller curvature for this class-one-only preparation.

    In the half-factor clock put \(A=L/2\), \(g_0=1/5\), \(\Lambda=2\), \(\eta=\gamma^2\), \(\epsilon_L=g_0/4\), \(\xi=A^{-1/2}(\theta-\Theta_1)\), \(v=\gamma A^{1/2}x\). On the relative leaf the modified energy is

    \[ \mathcal V=\tfrac12\|v\|^2+\tfrac\eta2(U-U_*) +\epsilon_L\xi^Tv+\tfrac{\epsilon_L}2\xi^TA\xi. \]

    With outward lower and upper bounds indicated by subscripts, define

    \[ \begin{split} \mu&=\eta_-c_{1,-}g_0^2,&M&=\eta_+\Lambda^2,\\ a_-&=\mu/2+g_0^2/16,& a_+&=M/2+\epsilon_L\Lambda/2+\epsilon_L^2,\\ \kappa&=\min\{4(g_0-\epsilon_L)/3,\epsilon_L\mu/a_+\},& V_0&=3\eta_+\Lambda b_x^2/4+a_+b_\theta^2/g_0. \end{split} \tag{4} \]

    The shared modified-energy proof gives \(\mathcal V'\le-\kappa\mathcal V\) and \(\mathcal V\ge\|v\|^2/4+a_-\|\xi\|^2\). A quarter-factor dwell D therefore admits the handoff radius q whenever

    \[ \kappa D/2\ge80,\qquad \frac{4V_0\,2^{-80}}{\eta_-g_0}<q^2,\qquad \frac{\Lambda V_0\,2^{-80}}{a_-}<q^2. \tag{5} \]

    Indeed \(e^{-\kappa D/2}\le e^{-80}<2^{-80}\). Every bound concerns the uninterrupted reached state; q is a derived recovery enclosure, not a primitive accuracy requirement. All six separate sources must pass (2)--(5) before any joined-source conclusion is admitted.

    The two return bounds in (5) retain more information than the common q enclosure. The preparation-aware initialization proof uses their separate form and phase allowances to bound constituent variances and carry a collective forecast from attachment. It preserves this source, dwell and decay budget; it neither infers a microstate nor replays the assessor.

    Actual outer acquisition and observation-class inclusion¶

    The exact same carrier, support and clock as the outer-acquisition owner retain their fresh nominal endpoint intervals and radii \(P_c,f_x,f_\theta\). For the actual five-child deviations its global heat argument gives

    \[ Z=6q(1+\gamma_+),\qquad R=3q+24\gamma_+Z. \tag{6} \]

    Require \(\alpha-2Z>0\), \(\alpha+2Z<\pi/2\) throughout the transit, and the nominal endpoint outer gaps enlarged by \(2Z\) strictly inside \((0,\pi/2)\). Rebuild the nominal storage excess \(U_{\rm nom}\) from the same carrier estimate. The actual excess obeys

    \[ U=U_{\rm nom}+8\|\bar x\|_2R+4R^2+14Z+4Z^2. \tag{7} \]

    Require \(U<\min\{b_9-V_9,b_5-V_5\}\), where \(b_n=1+(n-1)[1-\cos(3\pi/(2(n-1)))]\), and require the control bound \(40q^2<\min\{b_9-V_9,1\}\). These are the actual full-product barriers, not the primitive C5 capture verdict.

    To enter the unchanged observation class set \(X=1/100\), \(\zeta=10^{-6}\), \(A_o=7/5\). Require

    \[ f_x+R<X,\qquad 2Z<\zeta, \qquad\max|\hbox{nominal outer gap}|+2Z<A_o. \tag{8} \]

    The factor two in (8) is essential: subtracting each child's actual mean phase error produces the observation class's zero-child-mean residual. Its coordinate magnitude is bounded by \(2Z\), while the mean corrections enlarge adjacent origin gaps by at most \(2Z\). Initial childwise means are not asserted conserved after the outer edges are added.

    \[ L_w=\max\{0,\gamma_-hA_{w,-}/4-E-2\eta_c\},\qquad U_w=\gamma_+hA_{w,+}/4+E+2\eta_c. \tag{11} \]

    Require \(U_1<10^{-8}\), \(L_0>3\cdot10^{-7}\) and the fixed-origin signed contrast lower bound above \(10^{-7}\), retaining its separate \(2E+4\eta_c\) error.     Work, present storage and finite identity¶

    The outer attachment has exact nominal cost \(W_0=1507001760\) and \(W_0\le W_{\rm outer}\le W_0+20q^2\). Require both this upper bound and \(5V_9+W_0+40q^2\) below \(2\cdot10^9\). At the new contact put

    \[ W_+=90+\tfrac{45}2(B_o+q)^2,\qquad D_j=7\gamma_+Bh. \tag{12} \]

    Require \(W_+<100\) and both present-storage bounds \(6V_9+9V_5+U+36+4q^2+W_+<200\) and \(6V_9+36+44q^2+W_+<200\). Check every strict margin in (21) of the external-interaction owner using this q and \(D_o,D_j\). The six internal and nine old outer windings then persist through the whole finite contact, independently of the supplied donor origin. The historical account remains \(\sum_{s=1}^6B_s\), outer attachment, the fifteen absolute observation payments and the new contact payment. Present storage and dissipated storage cannot replace that account.

    All these conditions are simultaneous. Acquisition radii, recovery V0, initial cost and transported errors increase with primitive allowances; phase and moment enclosures are nested as errors increase. The source-class and identity margins consequently decrease, observation widths increase with \(\eta_o\), and contact error increases with \(\eta_c\). Curvature, decay rate, dwell and q are fixed. Thus the declared maximal corner suffices for the full box without a parameter scan. This is an inclusion argument, not a monotonicity assertion about individual trajectories. It does not extend the single-corner verdict to different dwells, handoff radii or decay powers. Those require their own simultaneous checks.

    2. Necessary restrictions that sufficient bounds cannot remove¶

    Finite-time compression of an original phase family¶

    Choose two admitted original sources with identical forms and opposite phase errors \(\pm r_0v\), where v is a mean-zero unit vector and \(0<r_0\le\rho_\theta\). Their initial phase separation is \(2r_0\). Eliminate their form difference in the quarter law, writing \(z=\Delta\theta\):

    \[ z(t)=z(0)+\frac{\gamma^2}4\int_0^t (I-e^{-L(t-s)/4})\Delta S(s)\,ds. \tag{13} \]

    On C9, \(\|I-e^{-Lt/4}\|\le1\) and \(\|\Delta S\|\le4\|z\|\). Gronwall gives \(\|z(t)-z(0)\|\le2r_0(e^{\gamma^2t}-1)\), hence \(\|z(t)\|\ge2r_0(2-e^{\gamma^2t})\). If both reached phases lie within q of the same lifted target, then, when \(q<r_0\),

    \[ T_{\rm prep}\ge\gamma^{-2}\log(2-q/r_0),\qquad D\ge\gamma^{-2}\log(2-q/r_0)-200. \tag{14} \]

    This global argument covers the nonacute formation transit. It does not prove that the earlier supplied dwell was necessary, and gives no positive restriction from this pair when \(q\ge r_0\). For the whole declared box one may take \(r_0=\rho_{\theta,\max}\). An exact rational lower witness can use \(\log(1+u)\ge2u/(2+u)\), \(u=1-q/r_0>0\), without introducing a new logarithm evaluator.

    Reading error and the minimum contact horizon¶

    The two contact readings permit a difference error of either sign and magnitude \(2\eta_c\). An upper capability threshold \(U_{\rm th}\) that must hold for every such error therefore necessarily requires \(2\eta_c<U_{\rm th}\). A witness response above a positive lower threshold \(L_{\rm th}\) for every error requires its true magnitude to exceed \(L_{\rm th}+2\eta_c\). Since the exact donor mean starts at zero, its damping equation and \(|\mathcal A|\le5\) give

    \[ |R_D(h)|\le\frac{5\gamma}{6}(1-e^{-3h/2}) \le\frac{5\gamma h}{4},\qquad \frac{5\gamma h}{4}>L_{\rm th}+2\eta_c. \tag{15} \]

    For the declared capability these thresholds are \(10^{-8}\) and \(3\cdot10^{-7}\). These are necessary observation restrictions; they do not replace the sufficient nonlinear, work or identity checks. Replacing \(\gamma\) by \(\gamma_+\) in the signal margin gives an upper bound: a nonpositive upper margin proves an obstruction, whereas a positive one alone supplies no capability certificate.

    Winding acquisition, duration and supplied contact work¶

    For a fixed-support unforced interval of duration \(T_a\), Cauchy--Schwarz and (1) give, with \(\mathcal L=\int_0^{T_a}\|Lx\|^2/4\,dt\),

    \[ T_a\mathcal L\ge4\|\theta(T_a)-\theta(0)\|^2/\gamma^2. \tag{16} \]

    Initially each raw same-slot outer gap has magnitude at most \(2q\). At an outer-winding-one endpoint, principal gaps sum to \(2\pi\) but raw lifted gaps telescope to zero. Some raw gap in each slot is therefore below \(-\pi\), with the endpoint away from winding cuts. The corresponding two coordinates have squared total displacement at least \((\pi-2q)^2/2\). The nine slots are disjoint coordinate sets, so \(\|\Delta\theta\|^2\ge9(\pi-2q)^2/2\), for \(2q<\pi\).

    If the endpoint retains the declared acute internal and outer cycles, Jensen's bounds give \(H_{\rm final}\ge5V_9+9V_5\); the initial disconnected source has storage at most \(5V_9+20q^2\). Thus

    \[ W_{\rm outer}\ge9V_5+ \frac{18(\pi-2q)^2}{\gamma^2T_a}-20q^2. \tag{17} \]

    This necessary duration--work relation is weaker than the exact fixed-ramp cost \(W_0\), but demonstrates that the preparation cost is not entirely a pessimistic recovery estimate. It applies to the stated support, law, clock and endpoint identity, not arbitrary TNFR acquisition protocols.
