# Native C5 foundation account and dynamic-capacity admission

<a id="native-foundational-account"></a>

This account applies the [four foundation prerequisites](../RESEARCH_PROGRAM.md#foundational-pillars)
to one provisional realization. Its structural capability is retention of a
nonuniform organization and a reproducible response to small perturbations.
It does not require a permanent pulse. The existing native reciprocal law is
the reference; releasing held capacity is an explicitly supplied comparison.
Definitions, previous proofs and new joint-model claims are distinguished
below. No foundational pillar or physical identification is closed by this
single example.

`g_i=Arg(z_i)/pi` and

p = P(x,theta) = -e*L*x + w*g(theta),
x_dot = N*p,

The native phase metric and source use the same relative resultant.
The reciprocal phase row follows conditionally from the independently

### Declared complete law and its premises

Let the supplied support be the undirected unit C5, indexed modulo five.
Write its adjacency matrix as \(A\), and set
\[
B=2I-A,\qquad L=B/2,\qquad N=\operatorname{diag}(\nu_i).
\]
The retained state consists of five signed form values \(x_i\), five circular
phases \(\theta_i\), and five strictly positive capacities \(\nu_i\). Work in
one fixed nondimensional structural clock. No graph changes, event requests,
controller state, clipping, history reads or external input are included.

On a regular phase chart define the native source and metric by
\[
\begin{aligned}
g_i(\theta)
 &=\frac1\pi\operatorname{wrap}
   \left(\arg\sum_{j\sim i}e^{i\theta_j}-\theta_i\right),\\
H_i(\theta)
 &=\pi\left|\sum_{j\sim i}e^{i\theta_j}\right|
                  \operatorname{sinc}(\pi g_i)>0,\\
V_\phi(\theta)
 &=\sum_{\{i,j\}\in E}[1-\cos(\theta_j-\theta_i)],\\
H g&=-\nabla V_\phi,\qquad q=Bx .
\end{aligned}
\]
Here \(\operatorname{sinc}(z)=\sin(z)/z\), continuously extended at zero.
Strictly acute principal edge gaps provide the local domain used below;
the prepared winding does not require all node phases to occupy one common
semicircle.

The comparison fixes effective normalized coefficients
\[
e=w=\tfrac12,\qquad \beta=1,\qquad \kappa>0 .
\]
The inherited symbols \(e,w,\beta\) are retained in the algebra below to show
which coefficient enters each term. No additional coefficient is inferred
from their appearance. The complete autonomous law is
\[
\boxed{
\dot x=Np,\quad p=-eLx+wg,\qquad
\dot\theta=\frac w\beta H^{-1}Nq,\qquad
\dot\nu=-\kappa L\nu .
}
\tag{JC1}
\]
Pressure is refreshed from the current form and phase. It is not an
independent held coordinate, nor reconstructed from a measured form rate.
The phase and capacity-contrast pressure coefficients are different objects:
in (JC1), the capacity-pressure and topology-pressure coefficients are zero.
Both native rows consume the full instantaneous \(N\).

The underlying form/phase source is the one admitted by
[dynamics/relational.py](../../src/tnfr/dynamics/relational.py).
That implementation's capacity is held during its selected step; (JC1)
adds a mathematical comparison row rather than changing that execution
contract. Ordinary native pressure normalization is retained. There is no
default capacity-gradient source hidden in \(p\), and no replacement of the
native Arg source by a sine evolution law.

The additional constitutive premise is reciprocal continuous capacity
transfer on the supplied edges:
\[
J_{i\leftarrow j}=\frac\kappa2(\nu_j-\nu_i)
                 =-J_{j\leftarrow i}.
\]
One way to specify this restricted class is to require the same continuous,
additive function of signed capacity contrast at every edge. Additivity and
continuity force \(J(s)=a s\); preservation of the nonnegative capacity cone
requires \(a\ge0\). On C5 write \(a=\kappa/2\). The choice \(\kappa>0\)
excludes the still-admissible held law. Locality, covariance or homogeneity
alone do not establish this entire class or select a numerical \(\kappa\).

This rule has no supplied oscillation period and no event-count clock.
In a dimensional structural clock, \(\kappa\) has inverse-clock units; a
clock conversion must transform it together with the two native rows.
The rule is not a continuous-limit proof for the snapshot-based, gated
capacity adaptation in
[dynamics/adaptation.py](../../src/tnfr/dynamics/adaptation.py).

### Capacity balance, positivity and storage

The symmetric connected C5 Laplacian has \(L\mathbf1=0\). Therefore
\[
\sum_i\nu_i(t)=\sum_i\nu_i(0),\qquad
\bar\nu=\frac15\sum_i\nu_i(0)>0 .
\tag{JC2}
\]
At a capacity minimum, \(-\kappa(L\nu)_i\ge0\), and at a maximum it is
nonpositive. Equivalently, \(e^{-\kappa Lt}\) is a nonnegative stochastic
matrix: for example its exponential series follows from
\(-\kappa L=-\kappa I+(\kappa/2)A\). Consequently
\[
0<\nu_{\min}(0)\le\nu_i(t)\le\nu_{\max}(0)
\quad(t\ge0).
\tag{JC3}
\]
Since \(L\) is symmetric, its spectral decomposition also gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
\|\nu(t)-\bar\nu\mathbf1\|_2
 &\le e^{-\kappa\lambda_1t}
       \|\nu(0)-\bar\nu\mathbf1\|_2,\\
\frac d{dt}\frac12\|\nu-\bar\nu\mathbf1\|_2^2
 &=-\kappa\nu^TL\nu
  =-\frac\kappa2\sum_{\{i,j\}\in E}(\nu_i-\nu_j)^2 ,
\end{aligned}
\tag{JC4}
\]
where \(\lambda_1=1-\cos(2\pi/5)>0\) is the smallest nonzero eigenvalue of
\(L\). This dispersion identity is not a physical energy identification.

Retain the native storage
\[
\mathcal S(x,\theta)=\frac12x^TBx+\beta V_\phi(\theta).
\]
It has no explicit capacity coordinate. Thus capacity evolution creates no
extra \(\dot\nu\) term in its chain rule. With the actual instantaneous
capacity in both native rows,
\[
\begin{aligned}
\dot{\mathcal S}
 &=q^TN(-eLx+wg)
   -\beta(Hg)^T\frac w\beta H^{-1}Nq\\
 &=-\frac e2q^TNq
  =-\frac14\sum_i\nu_i q_i^2\le0 .
\end{aligned}
\tag{JC5}
\]
The final equality uses the fixed \(e=1/2\). Neither a held \(N\) nor a
capacity-dependent storage is needed for this cancellation. Nonincrease
alone does not establish recovery of the phase shape; the full quotient
linearization below supplies that missing implication.

The arithmetic form and phase means need not be conserved:
\[
\dot{\bar x}=\frac15\mathbf1^TNp,\qquad
\dot{\bar\theta}=\frac w{5\beta}\mathbf1^TH^{-1}Nq .
\tag{JC6}
\]
The phase mean here is a local lifted offset, not a global circular
observable. Subtracting either right-hand side from the original dynamics
would change the selected representative; quotient coordinates do not make
those rows vanish.

### The prepared equilibrium family and all retained modes

Put \(\alpha=2\pi/5\), \(c_\alpha=\cos\alpha>0\), and
\(\theta_{*,i}=i\alpha\) modulo \(2\pi\). Oriented principal edge gaps are
\(\alpha\), including the edge crossing the chosen phase lift.
At this reference,
\[
g_*=0,\qquad H_*=2\pi c_\alpha I,\qquad
\nabla^2V_\phi(\theta_*)=c_\alpha B,\qquad
Dg_*=-\frac{B}{2\pi}=-\frac L\pi .
\tag{JC7}
\]
The resultant is nonzero, and the local phase field is smooth.

An equilibrium of (JC1) has \(L\nu=0\), so \(\nu=\bar\nu\mathbf1\).
Strict positivity and invertibility of \(H\) then make
\(\dot\theta=0\) imply \(Bx=0\), hence \(x=c\mathbf1\).
The form row requires \(g=0\). On the acute C5 winding-one sector, vanishing
phase gradient means that consecutive edge sine currents agree.
The sine function is injective on \((-\pi/2,\pi/2)\), so all principal gaps
agree; their winding sum fixes them to \(\alpha\).
Thus the equilibrium family in this sector is exactly
\[
\mathcal M=
\{(c\mathbf1,\theta_*+a\mathbf1,\bar\nu\mathbf1):
       c\in\mathbb R,\ a\in S^1,\ \bar\nu>0\}.
\tag{JC8}
\]
Uniform scalar form coexists with nonconsensus phase geometry. The
organization is prepared on a supplied cycle; its existence is not a
formation theorem.

Choose a real \(5\times4\) matrix \(R\) with
\(R^TR=I\), \(R^T\mathbf1=0\), and let
\(\Pi=RR^T=I-\mathbf1\mathbf1^T/5\). On the conserved mean-capacity leaf,
write
\[
x=c\mathbf1+Ru,\qquad
\theta=\theta_*+a\mathbf1+Rv,\qquad
\nu=\bar\nu\mathbf1+Rh .
\tag{JC9}
\]
The full state has 15 real local coordinates. Fixing only the conserved
capacity mean removes one; quotienting the two common origins removes two
more. The resulting \(z=(u,v,h)\) has 12 coordinates. In particular, the
four capacity contrasts remain free to perturb; equal capacity is not
imposed on the initial state.

Both common-origin shifts are exact symmetries of (JC1), so the quotient
field is autonomous. At (JC8), derivatives of the native rows with respect
to capacity vanish because \(p=q=0\). The capacity row is independent of
form and phase. The quotient Jacobian therefore has an eight-dimensional
native block and a four-dimensional capacity block, with no linear
off-diagonal coupling between these two blocks.

Diagonalize \(R^TLR\). For each of its four modes of eigenvalue \(\lambda>0\),
the corresponding native block and capacity eigenvalue are
\[
J_\lambda=
\begin{pmatrix}
-e\bar\nu\lambda &-w\bar\nu\lambda/\pi\\
w\bar\nu\lambda/(\beta\pi c_\alpha)&0
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad -\kappa\lambda .
\tag{JC10}
\]
The native characteristic polynomial is
\[
s^2+e\bar\nu\lambda s+
  \frac{w^2\bar\nu^2\lambda^2}{\beta\pi^2c_\alpha}.
\tag{JC11}
\]
Its two coefficients after the leading term are strictly positive, so both
roots have negative real part. The two distinct nonzero C5 eigenvalues are
\(1-\cos(2\pi/5)\) and \(1-\cos(4\pi/5)\), each occurring twice.
All 12 quotient modes are therefore strictly stable for each fixed
\(\kappa>0\), \(\bar\nu>0\).

In the full 15-dimensional linearization the remaining three modes are the
two common origins and the conserved capacity mean. They are semisimple
zero modes: the linearized mean rows vanish at the uniform-capacity
reference, while the 12-dimensional transverse block is invertible.
No common offset or empty contrast direction has been discarded by
declaring it stable.

### Local nonlinear recovery and continuation

For every fixed \(\kappa>0\) and \(\bar\nu>0\), sufficiently small joint
form, phase and capacity perturbations of (JC8), on the same mean-capacity
leaf, remain in the acute winding-one chart for all forward time and recover
exponentially to a member of \(\mathcal M\), modulo the two common origins.
The limiting capacity is \(\bar\nu\mathbf1\). This is a conditional local
theorem, not a claim about arbitrary initial states.

Here is a direct nonlinear argument. The positive-capacity regular-chart
field is smooth. In quotient coordinates it has
\[
\dot z=Jz+\mathcal R(z),\qquad
\|\mathcal R(z)\|\le C\|z\|^2
\]
on a sufficiently small neighborhood, where \(J\) is the Hurwitz matrix
just identified. Its positive-definite matrix
\[
P_J=\int_0^\infty e^{J^Tt}e^{Jt}\,dt
\]
is finite and satisfies \(J^TP_J+P_JJ=-I\). Hence
\[
\frac d{dt}(z^TP_Jz)
\le-\|z\|^2+2\|P_J\|C\|z\|^3
\le-\tfrac12\|z\|^2
\]
in a smaller neighborhood. Choose a sufficiently small sublevel ellipsoid
inside the positive-capacity acute chart. It is forward invariant and
gives constants \(M,\sigma>0\) with
\[
\|z(t)\|\le M e^{-\sigma t}\|z(0)\| .
\tag{JC12}
\]
All field denominators remain away from zero there. The capacity hull is
preserved, and the quotient remains in a compact interior set, giving
forward continuation of the local solution.

The two original common-offset velocities are smooth functions of the
quotient and vanish at its equilibrium. Thus
\(|\dot c|+|\dot a|\le C_1\|z\|\) locally. Equation (JC12) makes these
velocities integrable, so \(c(t)\) and the continuously lifted \(a(t)\)
converge to finite \(c_\infty,a_\infty\), with exponential tails.
This also completes continuation of the unquotiented solution.
Their limits need not equal the initial offsets. The winding stays one
because no edge leaves the chosen acute sector.

The proof permits constants and the admission neighborhood to depend on
\(\kappa,\bar\nu\) and the fixed law coefficients. It asserts no uniform
rate or neighborhood as \(\kappa\downarrow0\) or \(\bar\nu\downarrow0\).
It supplies no numerical radius or validated integrator error.

The \(\kappa=0\) boundary is a genuine different target family. At exact
native lock \(x=c\mathbf1,\theta=\theta_*+a\mathbf1\), all positive
capacity profiles leave both native rows zero. For \(\kappa=0\) a
nonuniform profile stays unchanged; for every \(\kappa>0\) it relaxes
according to (JC4). The four capacity contrast eigenvalues tend to zero
as \(\kappa\downarrow0\). Consequently recovery to uniform-capacity
\(\mathcal M\) cannot have a strictly positive rate uniform in this limit.
This does not contradict held-capacity recovery of the same phase shape.

### A causal distinction before any long-time observation

Compare (JC1) with its held-capacity counterpart \(\kappa=0\), starting at
the same complete \((x,\theta,\nu)\) in the regular domain.
Their initial \(p,\dot x,\dot\theta\) agree. Since \(p=-eLx+wg\) has no
capacity-pressure channel, their initial \(\dot p\) also agrees.
Their capacity rates differ by \(-\kappa L\nu\); differentiating the
actual native rows therefore gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
\ddot x^{(\kappa)}(0)-\ddot x^{(0)}(0)
 &=-\kappa\operatorname{diag}(L\nu)\,p,\\
\ddot\theta^{(\kappa)}(0)-\ddot\theta^{(0)}(0)
 &=-\frac{\kappa w}{\beta}
       H^{-1}\operatorname{diag}(L\nu)\,q .
\end{aligned}
\tag{JC13}
\]
The changes in \(H\), \(q\) and \(p\) arising from the initially equal
form/phase velocities cancel in this subtraction. This is a prospective
law-dependent response, not pressure inferred after measuring acceleration.

For an explicit nonzero preparation, let \(e_0=(1,0,0,0,0)^T\), and set
\[
x=c\mathbf1+\varepsilon\Pi e_0,\qquad
\nu=\bar\nu\mathbf1+\eta\Pi e_0,\qquad \theta=\theta_* .
\]
Take small nonzero \(\varepsilon,\eta\), preserving positive capacity and,
when recovery is invoked, its local admission neighborhood. Then
\[
Le_0=(1,-\tfrac12,0,0,-\tfrac12)^T,
\]
and (JC13) specializes to
\[
\begin{aligned}
\Delta\ddot x
 &=\kappa e\varepsilon\eta\,(Le_0)^{\odot2},\\
\Delta\ddot\theta
 &=-\frac{\kappa w\varepsilon\eta}{\beta\pi c_\alpha}
                               (Le_0)^{\odot2}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{JC14}
\]
The symbol \({}^{\odot2}\) denotes componentwise squaring. Both responses
are nonzero, and their means need not vanish. At exact native lock,
in contrast, \(p=q=0\) makes (JC13) zero even while a nonuniform capacity
profile redistributes. Capacity evolution and its observable native response
therefore have distinct state-dependent conditions.

### What this admission establishes and what remains open

The admitted capacity row can coexist with the native exchange storage and
with recovery of a prepared nonconsensus phase organization, retaining all
capacity perturbations. Its autonomous redistribution supplies an explicit
causal law for a previously held coordinate. This advances the complete
model account without selecting that law as necessary or canonical.

The resulting organization is a locally recovered equilibrium geometry,
not sustained nonzero form activity or an internally generated periodic
pulse. No formation mechanism, event occurrence law, thirteen-operator
closure or source ordering is derived. No capacity is created: (JC2)
conserves the C5 total. In particular, redistribution alone cannot replace
capacity removed by a strict Silence (SHA) event. Such events are outside
(JC1), and adding them changes both the law and its return conditions.

The exact local theorem is separate from finite represented arithmetic.
Finite symbolic source controls can check identities, coefficients and
admission at their declared inputs; they do not prove an exact irrational
equilibrium was materialized, global chart invariance for an integrator,
or the nonlinear basin's size. The four foundation prerequisites remain
open until their own scoped closure criteria are met.

### The retained native state and the precise operator comparison

Retain (JC1), including \(\kappa>0\), the fixed unit C5, and the same
two-channel pressure. Its native-lock manifold is
$$
\mathcal L=
\{x=c\mathbf1,\quad\theta=\theta_*+a\mathbf1,\quad\nu_i>0\}.
$$
This is not the equilibrium family \(\mathcal M\) when capacities differ.
On \(\mathcal L\), \(p=q=g=0\), so both native form/phase rows vanish for
every current capacity. Capacity alone continues to evolve:
$$
\nu(T)=e^{-\kappa TL}\nu(0).
\tag{UR1}
$$
The capacity hull preserves positivity for every finite \(T\ge0\); the
phase chart and winding remain unchanged. Thus using (UR1) retains the
reaction of both native rows, rather than suppressing nonzero reactions.

For the comparison action select the actual
[atomic Coupling stage](../../src/tnfr/operators/network_stage.py), with all
five nodes as targets, target-only phase proposals, capacity synchronization
enabled, functional links disabled, and no grammar replacement. In graph
configuration these choices are UM_BIDIRECTIONAL false, UM_SYNC_VF true and
UM_FUNCTIONAL_LINKS false. Use the live grammar and selected strict activity
policy; these hypotheses do not grant admission by themselves.

The [shared proposal kernel](../../src/tnfr/operators/_coupling_stage_kernel.py)
first selects U3-compatible neighbors from one immutable snapshot. At the
ideal winding-one lock both cycle neighbors are compatible under the default
limit: their principal gaps are \(2\pi/5<\pi/2\). Any configured tightening
must still admit both. The target-only circular phase proposal remains at
the target phase because the two neighbor phasors have resultant
\(2\cos(2\pi/5)e^{i\theta_i}\), with positive real coefficient.
The final merged U3 check remains mandatory.

The kernel's capacity channel reads those two snapshot neighbors and
proposes
$$
\nu_i^+=\nu_i+s\left(\frac{\nu_{i-1}+\nu_{i+1}}2-\nu_i\right),
\qquad U_s\nu=(I-sL)\nu .
\tag{UR2}
$$
Here \(s\) is UM_vf_sync. The
[public factor contract](../../src/tnfr/operators/factor_contracts.py)
admits \(0<s\le1\), and also requires a positive phase-push factor.
The endpoint \(s=0\) below is an explicit analytical extension of (UR2),
or its capacity-disabled comparison, not an admitted zero-gain public UM
invocation. A stationary phase proposal at this preparation is compatible

EPI is not written. Disabling functional links keeps support fixed.
Optional pressure relief still has its own proposal: preparing stored

pressure refresh is supplied, it must retain the account's two-channel

### Complete finite-time classification

The normalized C5 Laplacian \(L=B/2\) has eigenvalues
$$
0,\qquad
\lambda_-=\frac{5-\sqrt5}{4},\qquad
\lambda_+=\frac{5+\sqrt5}{4},
\tag{UR3}
$$
where each nonzero eigenvalue has multiplicity two. Write the orthogonal
decomposition of the initial vector as
$$
\nu=\bar\nu\mathbf1+\nu_-+\nu_+,\qquad
L\nu_\pm=\lambda_\pm\nu_\pm .
$$
An occupied band means \(\nu_j\ne0\); it may contain any combination of
the two real modes in that eigenspace. Put
$$
s_*=\frac1{\lambda_+}=1-\frac1{\sqrt5}\in(0,1).
$$
For fixed \(\kappa>0\), \(s\in[0,1]\) and finite \(T\ge0\), equality
\(e^{-\kappa TL}\nu=U_s\nu\) has exactly the following cases.

| Initial capacity bands | Gain and allowed duration |
| --- | --- |
| Uniform, \(\nu_-=\nu_+=0\) | Every \(s\in[0,1]\) and every finite \(T\ge0\). |
| Any nonuniform vector, \(s=0\) | Only \(T=0\). This is the analytical endpoint, not zero-gain public execution. |
| Low band only | For every \(0<s\le1\), exactly \(T=-\log(1-s\lambda_-)/(\kappa\lambda_-)>0\). |
| High band only | For \(0<s<s_*\), exactly \(T=-\log(1-s\lambda_+)/(\kappa\lambda_+)>0\). No finite match for \(s\ge s_*\). |
| Both nonzero bands occupied | No finite match for any \(s>0\), even with a state-dependent duration. |

To prove completeness, project the equality onto each occupied eigenspace.
For every such \(j\),
$$
e^{-\kappa T\lambda_j}=1-s\lambda_j .
\tag{UR4}
$$
A finite exponential is strictly positive. A zero right-hand side cannot
match at finite time, and a negative one cannot match at any nonnegative
time. When the right-hand side is positive, (UR4) has the unique displayed
duration. Since \(\lambda_-<1\), its factor stays positive throughout
\(s\in[0,1]\). The high factor is positive below \(s_*\), zero at \(s_*\)
and negative above it.

For two occupied bands with \(0<s<s_*\), consider
$$
f_s(\lambda)=\frac{-\log(1-s\lambda)}{\lambda}
            =\int_0^s\frac{du}{1-u\lambda}.
\tag{UR5}
$$
Its derivative in \(\lambda\) is
\(\int_0^s u(1-u\lambda)^{-2}du>0\).
Thus \(f_s(\lambda_+)>f_s(\lambda_-)\), whereas (UR4) would require both
to equal the same \(\kappa T\). The remaining gains already fail positivity
of the high factor. This is a pointwise contradiction at each two-band
initial vector, so allowing the duration to depend on that vector removes
none of it. No regularity of the chosen stopping rule is needed.

At \(s=s_*\), a high-only vector is mapped to its uniform mean by (UR2).
The continuous capacity flow approaches that mean only as \(T\to\infty\).
This limiting equality is not a finite-duration action. For gains above
\(s_*\), the negative high-band factor is not a negative-capacity claim:
for every \(s\in[0,1]\),
$$
U_s=(1-s)I+\frac s2A
$$
is nonnegative and stochastic, so it preserves the positive capacity hull.
Reversal of a spectral contrast and violation of coordinate positivity are
different statements.

For each nonzero fixed gain there is consequently no exact realization of
(UR2) on a nonempty open subset of the full positive capacity cone. Every
such open subset contains two-band vectors. The one-band exceptions have
empty interior in that cone, even though they include positive vectors and
allow arbitrary orientation within their occupied two-dimensional band.

### Failure persists arbitrarily near uniform capacity

The obstruction does not require a large contrast or departure from the
native lock. Let \(e_0=(1,0,0,0,0)^T\), let
\(\Pi=I-\mathbf1\mathbf1^T/5\), and choose
$$
\nu=\bar\nu\mathbf1+\eta\Pi e_0,\qquad
\bar\nu>0,\quad 0<\eta<\bar\nu/2 .
\tag{UR6}
$$
Its entries are \(\bar\nu+4\eta/5\) at node zero and
\(\bar\nu-\eta/5\) at the other nodes. All are positive, and \(\eta\)
can be arbitrarily small. When the strict activity policy is included,
take \(\bar\nu\) above its capacity threshold and reduce \(\eta\) further
to retain that margin; choose the common form independently above its
selected magnitude threshold.

Let \(c_\pm\) denote the real cosine vectors with components
\(\cos(2\pi ki/5)\), with \(k=1\) for the low band and \(k=2\) for the
high band. The standard five-cycle Fourier identities give
$$
\Pi e_0=\frac25(c_-+c_+),\qquad
\nu_\pm=\frac{2\eta}{5}c_\pm,\qquad
\|\nu_\pm\|_2^2=\frac{2\eta^2}{5}>0 .
\tag{UR7}
$$
Thus every positive amplitude in (UR6) occupies both bands. The preceding
classification excludes a finite match for every \(s>0\) despite proximity
to the uniform-capacity equilibrium family. Both maps preserve the same
mean \(\bar\nu\); observing only that mean would conceal this distinction.

### Near-identity agreement, time calibration and its remaining error

Fix a capacity vector while \(s\downarrow0\). Write \(a=\kappa T\) and
compare the full capacity outputs using their Euclidean norm. At the
natural first-order calibration \(a=s\),
$$
E(s,s):=e^{-sL}\nu-(I-sL)\nu
       =\frac{s^2}{2}L^2\nu+O(s^3).
\tag{UR8}
$$
For a direct finite bound, the scalar remainder satisfies
\(0\le e^{-y}-1+y\le y^2/2\) for \(y\ge0\), by its integral remainder.
Orthogonal spectral decomposition then gives
$$
\|E(s,s)\|_2
 \le\frac{s^2\lambda_+^2}{2}
       \|\nu-\bar\nu\mathbf1\|_2,\qquad s\ge0 .
\tag{UR9}
$$
This is an ideal comparison bound, not a time-step or floating-point
certificate for the runtime.

A second-order time correction \(a=s+b s^2+o(s^2)\) gives
$$
\frac{E(s,a)}{s^2}
 \longrightarrow
 \left(\frac12L^2-bL\right)\nu
 =\sum_{j\in\{-,+\}}\lambda_j(\lambda_j/2-b)\nu_j .
\tag{UR10}
$$
For one occupied band, \(b=\lambda_j/2\) cancels this term, consistently
with the exact single-band duration. For two occupied bands the same
coefficient \(b\) cannot cancel both.

More quantitatively, set
$$
W_j=\lambda_j^2\|\nu_j\|_2^2>0 .
$$
The square of the leading defect in (UR10) is
\(\sum_j W_j(\lambda_j/2-b)^2\).
Completing the square gives the unique minimizing time coefficient and
its strictly positive remaining norm:
$$
b_*=\frac{W_-\lambda_-+W_+\lambda_+}{2(W_-+W_+)},
\qquad
C(\nu)=\frac{\lambda_+-\lambda_-}{2}
       \sqrt{\frac{W_-W_+}{W_-+W_+}}>0 .
\tag{UR11}
$$

In fact this constant is optimal over all nonnegative durations, without
assuming a differentiable stopping rule:
$$
\inf_{T\ge0}\|e^{-\kappa TL}\nu-(I-sL)\nu\|_2
 =C(\nu)s^2+o(s^2)
 \quad(s\downarrow0)
\tag{UR12}
$$
for each fixed two-band vector \(\nu\). To justify the lower bound as well
as the formal expansion, first use the trial
\(a=s+b_*s^2\), positive for small \(s\), to obtain the upper bound.
Choose any sequence of approximate minimizing durations whose errors are
within \(o(s^2)\) of the infimum. Their errors are therefore \(O(s^2)\).
Projection onto either fixed occupied band yields
$$
e^{-a\lambda_j}=1-s\lambda_j+O(s^2).
$$
The right-hand side tends to one, so \(a\to0\). Taking its logarithm
then gives \(a=s+O(s^2)\). Thus
\(b_s=(a-s)/s^2\) is bounded. Along any convergent subsequence of these
bounded coefficients, the expansion (UR10) is uniform and its limiting
norm is at least \(C(\nu)\) by (UR11). If a smaller lower limit existed,
one could choose such a subsequence realizing it, a contradiction.
This proves (UR12), together with the trial upper bound.

This argument includes durations selected from the whole initial state.
For a fixed two-band vector no such timing choice achieves \(o(s^2)\)
capacity error. The coefficient is not uniform as an occupied band
vanishes or as the whole contrast amplitude tends to zero. In the uniform
case the error is exactly zero; in a single band the calibrated finite
match already classifies the exception.

For the arbitrarily small preparation (UR6), (UR7) reduces (UR11) to
$$
b_*=\frac56,\qquad C(\nu)=\frac{|\eta|\sqrt{30}}{24}.
\tag{UR13}
$$
These are conditional coefficients of an ideal local gain expansion,
not observed precision thresholds or a new choice of UM gain.

### Causal conclusion and boundaries

The capacity comparison passes a first-order direction test and fails
finite realization on generic positive C5 capacity states. Two independent
spatial relaxation rates are enough to distinguish the supplied continuous
constitutive law from the registered finite capacity channel. Adjusting a
single elapsed time cannot remove that distinction. This is more specific
than saying that jumps and flows have different names.

The conclusion does not forbid Coupling as a primitive discrete mechanism,
exclude another constitutive generator, or derive an autonomous event
selector. It does not assert that every operator must arise from this
particular diffusion. Single-band equality and the approximation bound
are admissible mathematical exceptions; neither settles the remaining
registered writes, history, grammar, occurrence or donor reaction in a
different proposed realization. All four foundational readiness conditions
remain open, and the research program must reassess the next dependency
rather than automatically extending this example.

### Why the conditional adoption is substantive

The [joint proof](#native-capacity-foundation) establishes a smooth,
autonomous full-state field on its admitted domain, preservation of positive
capacity, a conserved capacity mean and a compatible storage loss. Its
15-coordinate linearization retains every capacity perturbation. After fixing

strictly stable. The nonlinear continuation argument supplies a sufficiently

Consequently the state and complete model data determine a unique admitted

uniform positive rate as `kappa` or mean capacity approaches zero, or new

Sufficiency is relative to the law and the requested output. The 15 local

### Three different meanings of capacity composition

Three statements must remain separate when this account is extended.

1. Additivity of the **phase response** in independent capacity vectors,
   at fixed form, phase and support, is a sufficient premise in the phase-row
   selection theorem. It does not specify a capacity-transport law.
2. Conservation of **a sum of capacities** is a consequence of a chosen
   transport generator and support measure. JC1 preserves the ordinary sum
   on C5. This is not a declaration that capacity is a universal additive
   substance, energy, or a conserved quantity under every registered event.
3. An **effective regional capacity** belongs to a proved reduction and its
   retained boundary data. The [scale owner](../TNFR_SCALE_GEOMETRY_AND_BRIDGE.md#1-pure-epi-coarse-graining)
   already obtains coefficients depending on boundary conductance and
   degree/capacity sums in its reversible pure-EPI scope. They are not
   generally an ordinary sum, nor a closure of the full JC1 dynamics.

These are different mathematical obligations despite sharing the word
capacity. Confusing them would make a phase-selection premise appear to
justify conservation or coarse closure that it does not supply.
