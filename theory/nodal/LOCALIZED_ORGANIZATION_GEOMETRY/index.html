    Geometry and the formation boundary of the prepared mobile pair¶

    Scope: stationary, finite-capture and instantaneous-rate restrictions for the prepared mobile pair under the unchanged complete cycle law. The all-time part reuses the sector-capture theorem; the finite parts reuse the full-state transport bounds. A separate static C12 counterexample tests observation sufficiency, outside the original source family. Actual-state observations distinguish coincidence from retention. No trajectory, new reserved response, parameter search, input or support change is evaluated. The earlier nominal interaction result and its unresolved original-family interaction bound remain unchanged. A separately declared preparation and mechanism would be needed to acquire a common, finite-lived organization under this law while retaining the original pair's exclusions. The research program alone admits further investigation of that deferred question.

    1. Which geometry enters this model?¶

    Three distinct geometries must be retained:

    Geometry Role in the present model Boundary
    Support and coefficients The supplied unit cycle determines incidence D, Laplacian L and the actual exchange paths; held capacities enter the complete law. A drawn embedding or changed edge length alone does not change these rate rows. Edge length defines the localization observation, separately from conductance.
    Relative form and primitive phase Neighbor form differences drive phase motion; sine phase differences drive form. Phase differences determine the storage curvature and its critical configurations. The Fourier carrier of z=x+i*theta is not primitive circular-phase winding.
    Organization and boundary A spatial window, profile, carrier band or partition specifies an observation of the retained full state. Its boundary remains coupled to the complement. Overlap, compatible grouping and concentration do not establish actual entry into a new identity or its maintenance.

    These distinctions reuse the relation foundation, collective phase-offset conditions and regional work accounting. They do not introduce an extra geometric force or select a new law.

    The moving centers in the prepared-transport proof are comparison references, not imposed trajectories in (1). Actual form and phase evolve under both complete rows and can alter the observed profiles and their paths within the proved error bounds. A common organization could involve deflection, changed relative motion or internal rearrangement of the incoming patterns. It need not be a third separate object, and its definition must not require that the original patterns first separate. Any inferred position or velocity remains an observation of the field on fixed sites, with its own availability and error; the elementary support vertices do not move in this model.

    A proper contiguous window on a cycle induces a path. It has no internal graph cycle on which to transfer the earlier C9 constituent-winding identity. Closing that path by ignoring its environmental endpoints would change the support. A localized identity can instead retain relative form/phase structure and boundary exchange, but it needs its own prospective formation criterion.

    2. An all-time basin for the original preparation family¶

    Let D be the oriented incidence matrix of a simple unit cycle C_N, N>=3, and L=D^T D, A=L/2. Retain signed real form, real continuous phase lifts, held unit capacities, beta one, positive e,w, and no forcing or events. In the declared clock tau=w*t/pi, with sigma=pi*e/w>0, the complete rows are

    \[ x'=-\sigma Ax-\tfrac12D^T\sin(D\theta),\qquad \theta'=Ax. \tag{1} \]

    Both ordinary means are conserved. Each source member retains its own means; they need not equal the nominal zero means. A constant form or phase reference can be subtracted without changing (1). The following source premise concerns one consistent real lift relative to such a constant reference:

    \[ \|(x(0),\theta(0))\|_2\le M,\qquad H_0=2M^2<1. \tag{2} \]

    This is a sufficient source condition, not a phase reconstruction from form, a physical preparation certificate or an admission from a cached verdict.

    Storage and geometric trapping¶

    The existing structural storage and its actual clock-transformed balance are

    \[ H(x,\theta)=\tfrac12x^TLx+\sum_e[1-\cos(D\theta)_e], \qquad H'=-\tfrac\sigma2\|Lx\|_2^2\le0. \tag{3} \]

    Here H is the original storage, not the packet localization diagnostic. For a cycle, ||L||<=4, and 1-cos u<=u^2/2, so

    \[ H(0)\le2\|x(0)\|^2+2\|\theta(0)\|^2\le H_0. \tag{4} \]

    Initially every lifted edge difference has absolute value at most 2M<sqrt(2)<pi/2. A first exit from this zero-winding acute component would have an edge at +/-pi/2, contributing one to H. All other storage terms are nonnegative, contradicting H<=H_0<1. Therefore every future edge remains in the same lifted acute component, and in fact

    \[ \cos(D\theta)_e\ge1-H_0>0,\qquad (1-H_0)L\preceq\nabla^2V(\theta)\preceq L,\quad V=\sum_e[1-\cos(D\theta)_e]. \tag{5} \]

    This bound concerns phase-storage curvature, not the tetrad's diagnostic curvature. It is valid for all nonnegative structural time, without extending the finite moving-template error tube. The unwrapped gaps still telescope to zero around the cycle, so the circular winding remains zero. Changing that winding would require an edge to cross a circular cut, which is also excluded by the stronger acute trap.

    The only limiting state on each mean leaf¶

    The bounded gaps and fixed phase mean bound every lifted phase coordinate. The form term in H bounds centered form because L is positive on its mean-zero space. The invariant sublevel is thus compact on each mean leaf. The full vector field is globally smooth with bounded sine currents and linear form dependence, so forward continuation is available.

    In the zero-dissipation set, Lx=0, hence form is uniform and theta'=0. An invariant trajectory there must also have D^T sin(Dtheta)=0: otherwise the form row leaves that set, since its ordinary mean derivative is zero. Taking its inner product with theta gives

    \[ 0=\sum_e (D\theta)_e\sin(D\theta)_e. \]

    Every summand is nonnegative and vanishes only at zero in the retained acute chart. Thus every gap vanishes. The sector-capture/LaSalle argument therefore yields

    \[ x(\tau)\longrightarrow\overline x(0)\mathbf1,\qquad \theta(\tau)\longrightarrow\overline\theta(0)\mathbf1. \tag{6} \]

    This is convergence to one state on each actual source member's conserved mean leaf. It supplies neither a convergence rate nor a finite settling time. The original finite packet identities can remain valid during transport even though they eventually relax. Equation (6) excludes a nonuniform stationary asymptote in this source family; it does not exclude finite-lived additional structure before that limit.

    Application without a new response calculation¶

    The two nominal Fejer preparations have orthogonal complex carrier bands and joint norm sqrt(2)*a. Each primitive full-state error is at most epsilon, and the joint source is their exact sum. Hence the entire original family satisfies (2) with

    \[ M=(\sqrt2+1/500)a,\qquad a=2^{-12},\qquad H_0=2(\sqrt2+1/500)^2a^2<\frac3{12500000}=2.4\,10^{-7}. \tag{7} \]

    For example sqrt(2)<70711/50000 proves the last strict rational bound. Neither the computed endpoint defect nor the unresolved uniform interaction verdict is a premise of (7). The conclusion therefore applies to all original source errors, despite the distinct endpoint discrimination limitation. The same estimate gives an all-time phase-Hessian lower factor greater than 1-2.4e-7. It does not give an all-time ||z||<2a bound, preserve the two carrier projections forever, or quantify the lifetime of another pattern.

    3. Why the source's geometry cannot be omitted¶

    Small storage alone does not imply consensus for arbitrary circular sources on this same support. A separately supplied twist with constant principal edge gap 2*pi/N, uniform form and N>4 is a critical configuration: the incoming sine currents cancel and its Hessian is cos(2*pi/N)*L. It is a strict local minimum on the relative-phase leaf and has the existing positive-loss local recovery property. On C_1024 its storage satisfies

    \[ H_{\rm twist}=N[1-\cos(2\pi/N)]\le\frac{2\pi^2}{N}<1. \tag{8} \]

    It has winding one and no all-small zero-component lift around the cycle. It therefore does not satisfy the source condition (2). This counterexample shows why support, storage and the phase component must be matched together. It is an extended prepared pattern, not a localized object formed by the current encounter. It supplies no route across the inaccessible phase boundary.

    4. An exact carrier geometry, without a localized envelope closure¶

    For N divisible by four, the repeated period-four family

    \[ \theta=(\varphi,\psi,-\varphi,-\psi,\ldots),\qquad x=(r,s,-r,-s,\ldots) \]

    is invariant under both complete rows. On this subspace A acts as identity; direct neighbor addition gives

    \[ \varphi'=r,\quad \psi'=s,\qquad r'=-\sigma r-\sin\varphi\cos\psi,\quad s'=-\sigma s-\sin\psi\cos\varphi. \]

    The invertible relative coordinates u=varphi+psi, v=varphi-psi consequently satisfy

    \[ u''+\sigma u'+\sin u=0,\qquad v''+\sigma v'+\sin v=0. \tag{9} \]

    Their initial velocities retain r,s; this exact change of coordinates does not discard form or install a pendulum as a new law. It exhibits nonlinear internal dynamics selected by a compatible carrier geometry under the actual positive loss. The family repeats throughout the support and supplies no localized envelope, acquisition from outside that family or self-binding. The finite-width prepared packets are not confined to this four-dimensional family. Their other modes and boundary exchange cannot be dropped by using (9).

    At the consensus tangent, the two modal exponents are lambda(k)*[-sigma/2 +/- sqrt(sigma^2/4-1)], with lambda(k)=1-cos k. Thus their second k derivatives vanish at the current +/-pi/2 carriers. For the frozen underdamped coefficients their third derivatives do not vanish. Equation (9) retains exact carrier nonlinearity, but neither it nor this dispersion fact supplies a controlled modulation or localization theorem.

    5. Formation of a common relational pattern¶

    The geometric circumstances already matter in (1)--(8): the support supplies paths, the prepared phase component determines accessible critical geometry, and the storage/loss law restricts which regions of state space can be reached. The unchanged small pair cannot acquire another stationary trapping geometry. Its certified nominal nonadditivity can coexist with this obstruction.

    The generative target is a common organization of the incoming patterns: their relative arrangement and joint evolution acquire an identity absent initially. It may move, deform or have bounded internal relative motion; neither unchanged outgoing trajectories nor an extra separate object is required. Retain constituent and environmental boundary information consumed by that identity. If the original constituent identities cease to apply, record that reorganization rather than retaining their labels as evidence of persistence.

    An admissible finite-lived claim specifies:

    • Relational identity. A positive-width class on the actual joint state, with a nonvanishing signal floor and specified relative geometry, primitive phase information and internal response. Any collective center, trajectory or phase registration needs an observation rule, ambiguity handling and error bounds; a band observation is not an independent dynamical constituent.
    • Formation. The original source class lacks that common identity with a margin. Both complete rows must carry it into the declared class. Initial support connectivity, the presence of two packets or transient overlap does not establish this acquisition.
    • Joint retention. The relation remains in a declared nontrivial domain for a specified interval beyond ordinary passage, with environmental work, dispersion and loss accounted for. Bounded distance alone is vacuous on a finite cycle; a common moving center alone can also follow two separating packets. The incoming patterns need not separate before this test begins.
    • Continued response. The reached organization has a discriminating later response without resetting its acquired state. Compare the same-source linear passage and separate histories so that overlap, interference and independently continuing packets cannot alone pass the formation test.

    A third Fourier band or the difference of three histories alone does not satisfy those conditions. The parent-acquisition architecture illustrates this proof structure; its supports, windings and coefficients do not transfer.

    Specify whether the claim concerns the exact nominal preparation or every member of the original source family. A family claim must carry its correlated preparation errors through initial absence, entry, retention and response; the joint source is the sum of the same two uncertain constituent sources. Retain their actual conserved means. Nominal feasibility does not establish family-wide formation, and the unresolved earlier interaction bound neither proves nor forbids a direct formation argument.

    Declare the observation's domain and availability through overlap and deformation. Treat an identification as an exact symmetry only when it preserves the complete law; a lossy observation retains its stated information and error limits. Independent registration of each incoming packet must not discard their relative phase or origin. If an observed region changes membership, include transport across its moving boundary rather than reusing a fixed-region balance unchanged. Loss of the original packet labels is distinct from loss of the retained fine state. A sufficient full-state description can support an organization claim without an autonomous reduced law; that stronger claim needs its own closure or memory argument.

    The signal floor, absence margin, entry and retention windows, and later response discriminator need independent reasons and an error budget before evaluating a formation response. Declare the same-source passage control's law and comparison interval. The actual damped tangent and conservative transport reference are distinct controls; using the latter retains its damping-error allowance. Finite overlap or an arbitrarily small deformation alone cannot establish a sustained identity. If linear passage or independently evolving packets also pass the proposed formation test, it does not yet discriminate the target. Prior inspected responses remain prior information; they cannot be relabelled as reserved evidence for a newly chosen criterion.

    The existing endpoint bounds still restrict the unchanged source family. At tau=256, each nonzero band observation has less than 1/25 of its squared norm outside its radius-96 reference window; the two reference centers have cycle distance 256. If a common cycle ball of radius r contains more than 1/25 of each band's squared norm, it must intersect both reference windows. The cycle-distance triangle inequality then gives 256<=2(96+r), hence r>=32. This necessary geometric restriction neither excludes wider or two-lobed composites nor establishes one above that radius. It concerns the retained band observations, not an additive decomposition of local total density. Radius 32 is not a newly selected formation threshold.

    No such composite identity or binding mechanism is certified here. Comparing nonlinear coupling with dispersion and loss requires the actual carrier geometry: at the present pi/2 carrier the tangent's quadratic dispersion vanishes. A quadratic-dispersion envelope law cannot be imported as a formation theorem. Changing preparation, support, loss or events would define a separately admitted case, preserving the present evidence.

    6. Actual-state coincidence and a finite capture obstruction¶

    This section tests specified geometric requirements for candidate compact-capture identities. It defines a capture class, not a complete NFR identity or a physical binding criterion. The full state, primitive phase lift and environmental influence remain retained. Every conclusion below covers the whole original source family on [0,256], without consuming the evaluated interaction defect.

    6.1 One preparation bound for the actual state and passage controls¶

    Write v=v_A+v_B for the existing opposite-carrier reference, and let U(tau) be the actual damped tangent propagator. Compare three histories:

    • the actual joint flow z_AB=Phi_tau(u_A+u_B);
    • the same-source linear control y=U(tau)(u_A+u_B);
    • the sum of separate nonlinear flows z_A+z_B.

    The primitive errors remain shared as originally specified; the joint source error is at most 2*epsilon, not epsilon. In the notation of the encounter bound, the first two template errors are at most E and 2*epsilon+sqrt(2)*a*d, respectively. For the separate-history sum, contractivity applied to the summed tangent and the two individual Duhamel remainders gives

    \[ E_s=2\epsilon+\sqrt2\,a d+\frac{64}{3}Ta^3 =E+\frac{32}{3}Ta^3. \tag{10} \]

    This uses the individual bootstraps as well as the joint bootstrap; it does not assume that the damped generator is complex-linear or that the bands are closed nonlinear subsystems. The unchanged inputs give, using the same outward bounds as the encounter proof,

    \[ E_s/a<\bar h=\frac{13}{250}. \tag{11} \]

    For each of these histories Z, put f=P_+Z, g=P_-Z. Orthogonal projection therefore gives ||f-v_A||,||g-v_B||<a*hbar and ||f||,||g||>237*a/250. These are observations of two real channels expressed as z=x+i*theta, not independently evolving constituents or complex EPI.

    6.2 A coincidence class on the actual state¶

    Define normalized diagnostic weights and a relative-distance observation by

    \[ \mu_+(j)=\frac{|f_j|^2}{\|f\|^2},\qquad \mu_-(l)=\frac{|g_l|^2}{\|g\|^2},\qquad K_\ell(Z)=\sum_{d_N(j,l)\le\ell}\mu_+(j)\mu_-(l). \tag{12} \]

    The product defines a deterministic diagnostic, not an assumption of physical or probabilistic independence. K lies in [0,1] and measures relative bulk proximity without assigning sites to constituents, selecting peaks or prescribing motion. It is invariant under common cycle translations and reflection with band exchange. It discards internal phase and shape information; it supplies neither a closed future law nor a decomposition of the actual local squared density into two independent energies.

    For a declared distance ell, define the majority-coincidence class by the signal floors ||f||,||g||>=237*a/250 and K_ell>=1/2. The threshold means a majority of the diagnostic pair weight. It is a stated observation policy, not a universal TNFR constant. The definition allows deformation and displacement and has an interior on the full state domain. Initial absence, entry into this class and persistence there can now be tested independently. For any candidate identity that includes this geometric requirement, additional phase, response or memory conditions cannot rescue a trajectory that fails it.

    To bound K, use an auxiliary reference-window radius r. If each band has outside fraction at most eta, then two elementary geometric implications are

    \[ \begin{array}{ll} D=0,\quad\ell\ge2r &: K_\ell\ge(1-\eta)^2,\\ D>2r+\ell &: K_\ell\le1-(1-\eta)^2, \end{array} \tag{13} \]

    where D is the cycle distance between reference centers. The first line includes every pair from the two cores; the second excludes every such pair by the triangle inequality. All remaining pair weight is at most 1-(1-eta)^2. Equality in the second line's separation condition is not admitted by this argument.

    Choose the auxiliary proof radius r=48, half the existing localization radius. It changes neither the source nor the law. The transport tail bound and (11) yield

    \[ H=\frac{16384}{47^3}<\frac4{25},\qquad \eta<\left(\frac{2/5+13/250}{1-13/250}\right)^2 =\left(\frac{113}{237}\right)^2<\frac14. \tag{14} \]

    For every 96<=ell<160, the actual joint state and both passage controls consequently satisfy

    \[ K_\ell(0)<\frac7{16},\qquad K_\ell(128)>\frac9{16},\qquad K_\ell(256)<\frac7{16}. \tag{15} \]

    The norm floors keep K continuous. Thus the majority-coincidence class is absent initially with a margin greater than 1/16, entered during encounter with that margin, and exited before the original endpoint. This is an analytical entry/exit result, not a newly sampled response. Both controls exhibit it, so coincidence acquisition alone fails to discriminate formation. More generally, K_ell<7/16 whenever |256-2*tau|>96+ell. For example, at ell=96 it is excluded at every outgoing checkpoint 224<tau<=256. Choosing a checkpoint strictly below 256 would leave time for a later readout, but coincidence already fails there and stays excluded through 256. No crossing time or behavior at equality is asserted.

    6.3 Circular registration and a relative-retention bound¶

    A second observation restricts relative motion without identifying a peak. For any nonzero band y, let

    \[ M(y)=\sum_j e^{2\pi i j/N}|y_j|^2. \tag{16} \]

    A nonzero norm does not ensure a nonzero M. Here the Fejer reference at center c gives an independent availability argument. Character orthogonality and the adjacent-coefficient autocorrelation imply

    \[ M(v_c)=a^2\kappa_Q e^{2\pi i c/N},\qquad \kappa_Q=\frac{\sum_m b_m b_{m+1}}{\sum_m b_m^2} =\frac{2Q^2-2}{2Q^2+1}. \tag{17} \]

    The admitted bands avoid wraparound aliases in this adjacent sum. For ||y-v_c||<=a*h, the norm-one diagonal character matrix gives

    \[ |M(y)-M(v_c)|\le a^2 h(2+h). \tag{18} \]

    When beta=h(2+h)<kappa_Q, the moment stays nonzero and the disk in (18) bounds the angular error by asin(beta/kappa_Q). Define the positional bound delta=N*asin(beta/kappa_Q)/(2*pi). This is a circular first-moment center, not a center of mass or an autonomous collective coordinate. For (11), kappa_16=170/171, beta=6669/62500, and

    \[ \frac\beta{\kappa_{16}}<\frac19-\frac1{6\,9^3} \le\sin(1/9),\qquad \delta<\frac{1024}{18\pi}<19. \tag{19} \]

    Each actual center can be lifted continuously within delta of its own reference 128-tau or -128+tau. Since 256+2*delta<512, their signed relative center s has a single continuous principal branch and

    \[ |s(\tau)-(256-2\tau)|\le2\delta. \tag{20} \]

    If s stays in any fixed real interval of width W throughout [t_0,t_1], subtracting its endpoint bounds proves

    \[ t_1-t_0\le\frac W2+2\delta<\frac W2+38. \tag{21} \]

    This allows common translation, deformation and relative oscillation inside the interval. It excludes only longer confinement of this observed relation; it does not bound instantaneous velocity or every possible relative identity. The reference passage traverses an interval of width W in time W/2. Equation (21) limits extra residence without assuming a relative force law.

    6.4 Exclusion at the preexisting localization scale¶

    The same result also tests a capture class using the existing radius 96 and fraction 24/25: both bands would have to place at least 24/25 of their squared norms inside one common radius-96 ball. This allows arbitrary common centers and deformed profiles.

    of the ball center. For r=96,f=1/25, alpha=3*pi/16, cos(alpha)>4/5 and 5/96<sin(1/18), so the second term is less than 10 sites. At tau=128 the original joint band-tail certificate puts both bands in the same radius-96 ball with fraction greater than 24/25; the actual damped linear control also satisfies that certificate, since its template error is smaller.     7. The actual relative rate and its missing internal information¶

    7.1 Differentiate the observation under both complete rows¶

    The position bound (20) alone cannot bound a velocity: a small positional error may oscillate rapidly. Instead differentiate the actual observation under (1). With z=x+i*theta, the exact two-real-channel field is

    \[ z'=iAz-\frac\sigma2 A(z+\bar z)+R(\theta),\qquad R(\theta)=-\frac12D^T[\sin(D\theta)-D\theta]. \tag{23} \]

    The conjugate term retains the form-only loss. Dropping it would replace the actual positive-loss law by a complex-linear model. Since the opposite projectors satisfy P_+bar(z)=bar(P_-z) and commute with A, their exact rows are

    \[ f'=iAf-\frac\sigma2 A(f+\bar g)+P_+R,\qquad g'=iAg-\frac\sigma2 A(g+\bar f)+P_-R. \tag{24} \]

    These are observed rates with the full state retained, not autonomous band equations: R consumes the actual primitive phase, including omitted modes. Define the real-linear directional derivative

    \[ \mathcal D_y(v)=\sum_j\chi_j(\bar y_jv_j+y_j\bar v_j), \qquad \chi_j=e^{2\pi i j/N}. \tag{25} \]

    For M(y)!=0 and a continuous center lift,

    \[ M(y)'=\mathcal D_y(y'),\qquad c_y'=\frac N{2\pi}\operatorname{Im}\frac{\mathcal D_y(y')}{M(y)}, \qquad s'=c_f'-c_g'. \tag{26} \]

    The character chi is complex. Replacing (25) by twice a real part outside the sum would incorrectly force the moment derivative to be real. Its real linearity permits a conservative, dissipative and nonlinear contribution to (26), each evaluated at the same actual state. The nonlinear contribution compares the full field with its actual damped tangent at that state. It is not the difference between observed velocities on the nonlinear trajectory and U(tau)u, whose states and moment denominators generally differ. Similarly, the curve z_A+z_B has remainder R(theta_A)+R(theta_B); evaluating the joint field on that sum would instead use R(theta_A+theta_B). The following rate certificate concerns the actual joint flow only, even though its geometric majorants also cover the passage controls.

    7.2 Adjacent frequencies control transport without a large carrier error¶

    Use the unitary discrete Fourier transform and lambda_k=1-cos(2*pi*k/N). For either projected band y, the conservative part of its moment derivative is exactly

    \[ \mathcal D_y(iAy)=i\sum_k(\lambda_k-\lambda_{k+1}) \widehat y_k\overline{\widehat y_{k+1}}. \tag{27} \]

    Only adjacent modes with both coefficients present contribute. In B_+, write k=N/4+m, with -Q+1<=m<=Q-2, and put Delta=pi/N. Then

    \[ \lambda_k-\lambda_{k+1} =-2\sin\Delta\cos((2m+1)\Delta),\qquad v_m=\frac{\sin\Delta}{\Delta}\cos((2m+1)\Delta). \tag{28} \]

    The sign reverses on B_-. Thus the carrier frequency cancels before the estimate. Since |2m+1|<=2Q-3, elementary sine/cosine inequalities give

    \[ |1-v_m|\le\left[\frac16+\frac{(2Q-3)^2}{2}\right]\Delta^2. \tag{29} \]

    Also sum_k |yhat_k||yhat_{k+1}|<=||y||^2, by Cauchy--Schwarz. Equations (26)--(29) compare the two conservative center rates with -1 and +1 without treating a reference trajectory as the actual motion.

    Let h be an admitted common band-template error, U=1+h, and m_0=kappa_Q-h(2+h)>0. The previous moment argument gives ||f||,||g||<=a*U and |M(f)|,|M(g)|>=a^2*m_0. Bounds on the three rate contributions, per center, are

    \[ C=\left[\frac16+\frac{(2Q-3)^2}{2}\right] \left(\frac\pi N\right)^2\frac{U^2}{m_0},\qquad D=2N\frac e w\frac{U^2}{m_0},\qquad V=\frac{32N}{3\pi}a^2\frac U{m_0}. \tag{30} \]

    Here C bounds the conservative deviation from the indicated unit speed. For D, use both band norms in ||A(f+bar(g))||<=2(||f||+||g||) and |D_y(v)|<=2||y||||v||. The clock coefficient sigma=pi*e/w cancels the pi in (26); the conjugate-band loss term is not discarded. For V, the joint bootstrap ||z||<2a and ||R||<=(4/3)||theta||^3 give ||R||<(32/3)*a^3. Neither the sharper cubic-response radii nor an evaluated response are needed. V bounds the magnitude of a nonlinear contribution; it does not determine its sign.

    7.4 Equal observed geometry can have a different nonlinear rate¶

    The geometry still does not supply a generally autonomous rate law. The following exact static counterexample uses the same complete positive-loss law on C_12, with Q=2, B_+={2,3,4} and B_-={8,9,10}. It is not a member of the original C1024 source family and makes no claim about closure restricted to that family.

    Choose 0<q<pi/4, for example q=1/16, and set

    \[ u_j=q[\cos(\pi j/3)+\cos(\pi j/2)],\qquad Z_F=u\ (x=u,\theta=0),\quad Z_P=iu\ (x=0,\theta=u). \tag{33} \]

    Both have zero means and squared norm 12*q^2; the phase-only state's largest edge gap is 2q<pi/2. They can be arbitrarily small. Multiplication by i preserves every band intensity pointwise, hence both band norms, every K_ell, both moments and both observed centers agree. Direct character sums give M_+=M_-=3*q^2>0, so both states have the same regular local relative center s=0. Multiplication by i here changes the allocation between form and primitive phase; it is not a symmetry of the complete law.

    At the form-only state, the actual field is (-sigma+i)Au, and

    \[ M_\pm'=\frac{3q^2}{2}(-3\sigma\mp i),\qquad s_F'=-\frac6\pi. \tag{34} \]

    At the phase-only state, the field is the real vector F=S(u)/2. For this finite sum write tilde for 1/N Fourier coefficients, so Fhat_k=sqrt(12)*Ftilde_k; the profile has utilde_k=q/2 at modes 2, 3, 9, 10. Its two relevant field coefficients and the adjacent zero coefficient are

    \[ \widetilde F_2=\frac{\sin(q/2)-\sin(2q)-\sin(3q/2)}{12},\qquad \widetilde F_3=-\frac{2\sin(q/2)+\sin(2q)+2\sin(3q/2)}{12},\qquad \widetilde F_4=0. \tag{35} \]

    Substitution in (25), or direct sums in site coordinates, gives

    \[ M_\pm'=\mp\frac{iq}{2}[3\sin(q/2)+\sin(3q/2)],\qquad s_P'=-\frac{6\sin(q/2)+2\sin(3q/2)}{\pi q}. \tag{36} \]

    Since sin(t)<t for positive t,

    \[ s_P'-s_F'=\frac2{\pi q}[3q-3\sin(q/2)-\sin(3q/2)]>0. \tag{37} \]

    The actual damped tangent evaluated at either state gives s'=-6/pi. Thus the form-only state has zero nonlinear correction, whereas the phase-only state has the strictly positive correction (37). Equal observed geometry does not determine even this nonlinear contribution: the lost form/phase allocation is consumed by the sine pressure. Both displayed rates remain negative in the chosen small domain. The positive correction slows passage; it establishes neither a restoring force nor retention at s=0.
