## 1. A coupled sufficient domain

Write `x=EPI`, and let \(\alpha=2\pi/9\), \(\gamma=1/(1023\pi)\),
\(V_n=n[1-\cos(2\pi/n)]\). On every fixed support in this history,

\[
 x'=(-Lx+\gamma S(\theta))/4,\qquad
 \theta'=\gamma Lx/4,\qquad
 H=\tfrac12x^TLx+\sum_e[1-\cos\delta_e],\qquad

\]

The support changes and phase commands have their actual complete storage

\[

\[

 \begin{split}

## 2. Necessary restrictions that sufficient bounds cannot remove

### Finite-time compression of an original phase family

Choose two admitted original sources with identical forms and opposite
phase errors \(\pm r_0v\), where v is a mean-zero unit vector and
\(0<r_0\le\rho_\theta\). Their initial phase separation is \(2r_0\).
Eliminate their form difference in the quarter law, writing \(z=\Delta\theta\):

\[
 z(t)=z(0)+\frac{\gamma^2}4\int_0^t
       (I-e^{-L(t-s)/4})\Delta S(s)\,ds.                 \tag{13}
\]

On C9, \(\|I-e^{-Lt/4}\|\le1\) and
\(\|\Delta S\|\le4\|z\|\). Gronwall gives
\(\|z(t)-z(0)\|\le2r_0(e^{\gamma^2t}-1)\), hence
\(\|z(t)\|\ge2r_0(2-e^{\gamma^2t})\). If both reached
phases lie within q of the same lifted target, then, when \(q<r_0\),

\[
 T_{\rm prep}\ge\gamma^{-2}\log(2-q/r_0),\qquad
 D\ge\gamma^{-2}\log(2-q/r_0)-200.                       \tag{14}
\]

This global argument covers the nonacute formation transit. It does not
prove that the earlier supplied dwell was necessary, and gives no positive
restriction from this pair when \(q\ge r_0\).
For the whole declared box one may take \(r_0=\rho_{\theta,\max}\).
An exact rational lower witness can use
\(\log(1+u)\ge2u/(2+u)\), \(u=1-q/r_0>0\), without
introducing a new logarithm evaluator.

### Reading error and the minimum contact horizon

The two contact readings permit a difference error of either sign and
magnitude \(2\eta_c\). An upper capability threshold \(U_{\rm th}\)
that must hold for every such error therefore necessarily requires
\(2\eta_c<U_{\rm th}\). A witness response above a positive lower
threshold \(L_{\rm th}\) for every error requires its true magnitude
to exceed \(L_{\rm th}+2\eta_c\). Since the exact donor mean starts
at zero, its damping equation and \(|\mathcal A|\le5\) give

\[
 |R_D(h)|\le\frac{5\gamma}{6}(1-e^{-3h/2})
          \le\frac{5\gamma h}{4},\qquad
 \frac{5\gamma h}{4}>L_{\rm th}+2\eta_c.                 \tag{15}
\]

For the declared capability these thresholds are \(10^{-8}\) and
\(3\cdot10^{-7}\). These are necessary observation restrictions;
they do not replace the sufficient nonlinear, work or identity checks.
Replacing \(\gamma\) by \(\gamma_+\) in the signal margin gives
an upper bound: a nonpositive upper margin proves an obstruction, whereas
a positive one alone supplies no capability certificate.

### Winding acquisition, duration and supplied contact work

For a fixed-support unforced interval of duration \(T_a\), Cauchy--Schwarz
and (1) give, with \(\mathcal L=\int_0^{T_a}\|Lx\|^2/4\,dt\),

\[
 T_a\mathcal L\ge4\|\theta(T_a)-\theta(0)\|^2/\gamma^2.
 \tag{16}
\]

Initially each raw same-slot outer gap has magnitude at most \(2q\).
At an outer-winding-one endpoint, principal gaps sum to \(2\pi\)
but raw lifted gaps telescope to zero. Some raw gap in each slot is
therefore below \(-\pi\), with the endpoint away from winding cuts.
The corresponding two coordinates have squared total displacement at
least \((\pi-2q)^2/2\). The nine slots are disjoint coordinate sets,
so \(\|\Delta\theta\|^2\ge9(\pi-2q)^2/2\), for \(2q<\pi\).

If the endpoint retains the declared acute internal and outer cycles,
Jensen's bounds give \(H_{\rm final}\ge5V_9+9V_5\); the initial
disconnected source has storage at most \(5V_9+20q^2\). Thus

\[
 W_{\rm outer}\ge9V_5+
       \frac{18(\pi-2q)^2}{\gamma^2T_a}-20q^2.            \tag{17}
\]

This necessary duration--work relation is weaker than the exact fixed-ramp
cost \(W_0\), but demonstrates that the preparation cost is not entirely
a pessimistic recovery estimate. It applies to the stated support, law,
clock and endpoint identity, not arbitrary TNFR acquisition protocols.
