    Collective IL/OZ actions and constitutive-pressure handoff¶

    This owner gives exact-real conditional identities, a simultaneous-IL maintenance theorem and a frozen represented handoff on the actual maintained count-three contact. The executed word retains the joint identity and exhibits the predicted OZ refresh equivalence. Its frozen per-target IL phase-error certificate was unresolved in the original report; a separate retained-input review now certifies the original error allowance. Direct endpoint and work checks have independent support. The research program alone selects further work; the result and its accuracy distinction remain here. No additional pressure law, event selector or research queue is introduced.

    1. Consumed state and the selected complete law¶

    Retain the actual twenty-node, 45-edge contact of SINE_PROXIMITY_CONTACT: forty old unit edges, the internal A0 edge and four A0--B0 edges. All actual weights remain attached to their endpoints and held fixed. The capacities are nu=1/2 on the two A0 targets and one elsewhere; bare degrees are seven on A0, six on B0 and four elsewhere. Use signed real form, primitive circular phase with its declared lifts, no forcing or continuous loss, and the original structural clock.

    Write the consumed continuous state as y=(x,theta,nu,U,W), and put

    \[ S_i(\theta)=\sum_{j\sim i}\sin(\theta_j-\theta_i),\qquad \Phi_i(y)=\frac{S_i(\theta)}{\pi d_i},\qquad M=\operatorname{diag}\frac{\nu_i}{\pi d_i}. \]

    The law is independently specified by

    \[ \dot x=MS(\theta),\qquad \dot\theta=ML_Wx. \]

    The stored pressure p_store consumed by operators is a distinct channel. It need not equal the freshly evaluated constitutive pressure Phi. Keep execution state k as well: associated histories, grammar and lifecycle state, timestamps, policy inputs, random progress and required telemetry. Thus an operator acts on more than y, even when the subsequent continuous law consumes only y. The parameter foundation and form foundation own this distinction; no pressure is reconstructed from a measured form rate.

    The continuous storage and mean weights are

    \[ H(y)=\frac12x^TL_Wx+\sum_{\{i,j\}\in U}(1-\cos(\theta_j-\theta_i)), \qquad \rho_i=\frac{d_i}{\nu_i}. \]

    Here rho=14 on A0, six on B0 and four elsewhere, with totals rho_A=60,rho_B=44,rho_all=104. H and both global rho-weighted form and lifted-phase means are conserved between events. Stored pressure and execution state do not occur in this particular H or these flow rows.

    Use the same common-aligned twist reference theta_* and joint observation

    \[ Z^2=\|P_{20}x\|^2+\|P_{20}(\theta-\theta_*)\|^2,\qquad r=1/16. \]

    The proved storage barrier is H-H_*>=Z^2/25 on Z<=r, with boundary value 1/6400. The maintained source and its independent-registration extension have Z<r and strict storage excess below that value. Those hypotheses, not a diagnostic coherence label, are used below.

    2. Pressure refresh and the exact OZ projection equivalence¶

    Let pi_c(y,p_store,k)=y denote projection onto the consumed continuous state. An admitted canonical OZ action, including its ordinary propagation or noise branch when enabled and finite, changes stored pressure and execution context while preserving x, phase, capacity, support and weights. Consequently

    \[ \pi_c\circ J_{\rm OZ}=\pi_c,\qquad \Phi(\pi_c J_{\rm OZ}z)=\Phi(\pi_c z). \]

    An immediate refresh that evaluates this specified Phi therefore assigns the same constitutive pressure with or without that OZ action. The complete fixed-law continuation from the projected state is identical in both cases for all time, provided no further events, controllers or inputs consume the changed execution state. OZ also has Delta H=0 in this model because H does not contain stored pressure.

    These are projection and continuation identities, not equality of the full operator states. OZ can leave different grammar debt, lineage, history, propagation records, random progress or diagnostic flags. Those differences can affect later eligibility or execution. Removing OZ from a word requires a separate argument about those later consumers; it does not follow from its continuous-state neutrality.

    The OZ mechanism includes multiplicative pressure amplification, a nonzero near-zero seed, optional jitter and signed accumulation of neighbor contributions. The default gain (pi+1)/pi is a supplied factor. The local amplification and final merged pressure are different observations; incoming positive contributions can cancel negative pressure. None of these branches supplies a pressure lifetime or a retained forcing term in the selected continuous model.

    The mathematical projection above is not a new runtime operator. A detached weighted-field report evaluates Phi without mutating stored pressure. An executed refresh must use its declared callback boundary and selected-law writer; the default mixed-pressure callback is not this law. A held-pressure interval or an additional pressure-memory/input model would have different premises and does not inherit this equivalence automatically.

    3. The full simultaneous IL action¶

    The shared IL kernel contracts each selected stored pressure by its admitted retention factor. The ordinary default is pi/(pi+1). Unlike the pressure-only glyph, the public class and immutable snapshot stage also propose a phase change. For target i, define its unweighted old-neighbor resultant

    \[ \mathcal R_i=\sum_{j\sim i}e^{i\theta_j}=R_i e^{i\mu_i},\qquad a_i=\operatorname{wrap}(\mu_i-\theta_i),\qquad d_i^{\rm IL}=\lambda a_i. \]

    The event writes theta_i'=theta_i+d_i^IL modulo 2*pi and preserves x, nu, U and W. The admitted phase coefficient lies in [0,1]; its ordinary implementation default is 0.3. The theorem below covers this entire interval and selects no new coefficient. Both A0 targets read the same immutable snapshot. Neither proposal observes the other's new phase. The kernel also has declared isolated/zero-resultant fallbacks, although the maintained contact domain below excludes those cases locally.

    Storage, identity and mean calculations retain the continuous endpoint lift theta_i+d_i^IL. A wrapped storage representative near 2*pi must not be mistaken for a large physical displacement from a nearby zero lift.

    The default IL retention and OZ amplification are reciprocals only in their ideal scalar multiplicative branches. They are not inverse hybrid actions: IL can change phase, OZ has near-zero/propagation/noise branches, both change execution context, and a declared refresh can intervene. Even cancellation of two stored-pressure factors would not reverse IL's phase reset or erase its history.

    The actual staged word still needs live input and grammar admission. This section defines the primary map conditional on admission; it does not claim that every point in a mathematical identity neighborhood has the history, stored pressure or active runtime settings required to execute that word. Represented trigonometric rounding is also distinct from the exact-real event considered in the proof.

    4. Uniform local resultant and phase-displacement bounds¶

    Let u and v be the adjacent A0 targets. Each has its partner, four old A1/A4 neighbors and two B0 neighbors. At the common-aligned reference, its neighbor resultant is

    \[ R_*=3+4\cos(2\pi/5)=2+\sqrt5. \]

    This is a local resultant. The whole doubled-twist reference has zero first circular resultant, so it does not define a global resultant angle. The proof instead uses the already declared continuous lifts.

    For a state in Z<r, write theta=theta_*+c*1+eta, where eta=P_20(theta-theta_*) and ||eta||<r. On either seven-neighbor set, the elementary bound |exp(i*t)-1|<=|t| gives

    \[ |e^{-ic}\mathcal R_i-R_*| \le\sum_{j\sim i}|\eta_j| \le\sqrt7\,\|\eta\|<\frac{\sqrt7}{16}<\frac16. \]

    Since sqrt(5)>11/5, the real part of exp(-i*c)*Rcal_i exceeds 2+11/5-1/6>4, and its imaginary part has magnitude below 1/6. Hence

    \[ R_i>4,\qquad |\mu_i-c|<\arctan(1/24)<1/24 \]

    on the unique local direction near c. Each target's reference phase is zero, so |theta_i-c|<1/16. The shortest target displacement is unique and satisfies

    \[ \boxed{|a_i|<\frac1{16}+\frac1{24}=\frac5{48}<\frac18.} \]

    These bounds require neither equal target members nor equal boundary conductances. The phase mechanism uses bare neighbors; its local resultant is not weighted by the actual conductances.

    5. Exact shared-edge correction for two simultaneous targets¶

    For clarity put d_u=d_u^IL,d_v=d_v^IL in this section, and write delta=theta_v-theta_u. If only target i moved with its old neighbors fixed, its incident phase-storage change would be

    \[ G_i=R_i\left[\cos a_i-\cos((1-\lambda)a_i)\right]. \]

    Adding the two such expressions counts the shared edge as two independent single-target changes. The true simultaneous storage jump is instead

    \[ \Delta H=G_u+G_v+C_{uv}, \]
    \[ \begin{aligned} C_{uv}={}&\cos(\delta-d_u)+\cos(\delta+d_v) -\cos\delta-\cos(\delta+d_v-d_u)\\ ={}&-4\cos\left(\delta+\frac{d_v-d_u}{2}\right) \sin\frac{d_u}{2}\sin\frac{d_v}{2}. \end{aligned} \]

    All other edges are counted correctly in G_u+G_v; edges not touching u or v do not change. The form part of H is unchanged. The correction has coefficient one because the phase potential uses bare support, even though the internal edge's form conductance is the actual omega.

    The correction has no general sign, but always obeys

    \[ |C_{uv}|\le|d_ud_v|\le\frac12(d_u^2+d_v^2). \]

    For example, on a graph consisting of one edge, full snapshot alignment with lambda=1 exchanges its two distinct phases. The true phase storage is unchanged although each separately evaluated single-target cost is negative. The correction supplies exactly the missing positive amount. Thus the existing single-target IL inequality cannot simply be summed for adjacent targets.

    For the maintained contact, the local bounds in Section 4 give a uniform negative allowance that controls this correction. On 0<=s<=1/8, sin(s)>=3*s/4, since cos(s)>=1-s^2/2>3/4. For 0<lambda<=1,

    \[ \begin{aligned} G_i &=-R_i\int_{(1-\lambda)|a_i|}^{|a_i|}\sin s\,ds\\ &\le-\frac{3R_i}{8}(2\lambda-\lambda^2)a_i^2\\ &\le-\frac32\left(\frac2\lambda-1\right)d_i^2. \end{aligned} \]

    For nonzero d_i the last bound is strict because R_i>4. Combining both targets with the shared-edge correction yields

    \[ \boxed{\Delta H\le-(d_u^2+d_v^2)\le0.} \]

    The first inequality is strict whenever at least one displacement is nonzero. If both vanish, the phase-storage change is zero. At lambda=0 the primary phase map is the identity and the storage jump is exactly zero, while the stored-pressure and execution-context changes may remain. This proves conditional simultaneous passivity on this contact domain; it is not a universal theorem for arbitrary support or simultaneous IL stages, nor a strict-response lower bound for every admitted source.

    6. The reset path preserves the existing joint identity¶

    A nonpositive endpoint storage jump alone would not exclude jumping to a different geometric neighborhood. The actual finite phase displacement also has a continuous comparison path

    \[ \theta(s)=\theta+s d^{\rm IL},\qquad 0\le s\le1, \]

    with x, nu, U and W fixed. Every prefix uses the same snapshot resultants and equals the ideal simultaneous formula with coefficient s*lambda. The preceding work identity and estimate therefore give

    \[ H(x,\theta(s))-H(x,\theta)\le -s^2(d_u^2+d_v^2)\le0. \]

    This inequality is computed from the initial snapshot and does not assume that the whole path already lies inside the identity radius. Suppose the prestate has Z<r and H-H_*<1/6400, as every state on the proved maintained continuation does. If the reset path first reached Z=r, the existing boundary barrier would require H-H_*>=1/6400 there, contradicting its nonincreasing storage. Thus the entire path and the actual endpoint retain Z<r and the strict storage margin.

    Restarting the same held weighted law from that endpoint preserves the identity for all subsequent structural time by the existing conservative barrier proof. An admitted OZ pressure-only action and the selected-law refresh do not alter this conclusion because they leave y fixed. This argument supplies no occurrence schedule or phase-flow law for IL: the path is a geometric certificate for a supplied jump, not an asserted finite-duration dynamics or an interpolation executed by the engine.

    7. Mean leaves, fresh rates and sufficient collective information¶

    IL leaves the global weighted form mean unchanged. Its global lifted-phase mean changes by

    \[ \Delta\mu_\theta=\frac{14(d_u+d_v)}{104} =\frac7{52}(d_u+d_v). \]

    Using the joined regional phase observations, the jumps are Delta Theta_A=7*(d_u+d_v)/30 and Delta Theta_B=0. The post-event continuous flow conserves these new means; it need not retain the pre-event phase-mean leaf. Translating the common reference phase to match the new global mean changes neither H nor Z.

    The fresh continuous rows have distinct instantaneous behavior:

    \[ \Delta(\dot\theta)=0,\qquad \Delta(\dot x)=M\left[S(\theta+d^{\rm IL})-S(\theta)\right]. \]

    The first identity follows from unchanged x, W and nu. The second is the specified constitutive recomputation, not multiplication of the old form rate by IL's stored-pressure retention. Fresh pressure can change at the targets and their neighbors; OZ has zero changes in both fresh rows when y is unchanged. A nodal product built from stale stored pressure is a different observation and cannot replace these evaluated rows.

    For the two IL phase proposals, the common external phasor from the six neighbors other than u and v, together with both target phases, determines both local resultants. That small description suffices for these primary phase proposals and their incident phase work. It does not replace the full fine state needed for subsequent motion, the separate A/B boundary associations needed for regional exchange, or the retained stored pressure and execution context needed for admission.

    Any relabeling symmetry must transport target selection, node state, conductance endpoints, histories and rank-dependent policies together. A snapshot-equivariant primary formula does not create a pair-symmetric continuous quotient for generic actual weights. Neither a common phase mean nor an unordered multiset of independent histories proves descent of the complete word.

    8. Reuse and memory boundaries¶

    The complete reset accounting and identity-retention method provide the useful pattern: retain actual event work, sufficient endpoint state and an applicable barrier. The present proof re-admits their method on this support and corrects its overlapping IL edge explicitly. It does not transfer coefficients from an unweighted or dissipative comparison.

    The memory owner, nonlinear constituent memory and ontology distinction separate eliminated dynamical state, supplied delayed history and operator execution context. Hidden fine initialization can affect a reduced future, and execution history can affect a later operator. Neither fact supplies an independent lifetime for an overwritten stored-pressure intervention. A history buffer or reciprocal scalar factor is not a realization of a derived memory kernel.

    Even differentiating the selected constitutive pressure produces only a consequence on its own state constraint. With D_b=diag(d_i) and the bare phase Hessian K(theta)=L_(cos(theta_j-theta_i)),

    \[ \dot\Phi=-\frac1\pi D_b^{-1}K(\theta)ML_Wx. \]

    This follows by differentiating Phi=D_b^{-1}S/pi under the already specified complete law. It does not prescribe an independent pressure state away from p=Phi(y), authorize an arbitrary pressure reset there, or add a memory/source law. Any such extension needs its own hypotheses, initial-state admission and discrimination.

    If a later study explicitly declares an interval that consumes stored pressure instead, put r_p=p_store-Phi(y) and retain the same phase row. The hypothetical form row and its exact storage balance would be

    \[ \dot x=\operatorname{diag}(\nu)(\Phi+r_p),\qquad \dot H=(L_Wx)^T\operatorname{diag}(\nu)r_p. \]

    Indeed, the q^TMS and -S^TMq terms still cancel, leaving precisely the displayed additional work. This identity specifies no law, duration or reservoir for r_p; even held stored pressure need not give a constant deviation from the evolving Phi. The THOL refresh analysis and held-pressure IL accounting explain why retention order matters. Their models and coefficients do not transfer here. The present gate introduces no such pressure-retention interval.

    9. Scope of the preparatory theorem¶

    The exact-real preparatory result establishes a model-specific OZ projection equivalence and a uniform simultaneous-IL work/identity theorem for the two existing A0 targets. It retains the complete IL phase action, the adjacent-edge correction, actual conductance associations and changed mean leaf. The whole allowed phase-coefficient interval is covered without choosing a coefficient to fit a response.

    No IL/OZ word or trajectory was executed for this derivation. A concrete study separately needs its frozen preparation, actual live operator and grammar admission, declared selected-law refresh writer and callback schedule, retained context and represented output checks. These are separate obligations of the event contract, not consequences of the storage inequality. The retained execution supplies one bounded case with an explicitly unresolved phase-accuracy certificate. A rejected operation remains a rejection, and a detached field evaluation does not perform a refresh.

    The maintained organization may realize these conditional actions while other operators, autonomous event selection, physical calibration and a uniquely selected fundamental law remain open. No additional pressure mechanism is inserted merely to make OZ change this particular continuation.

    10. Execution and evidence preflight¶
