    Complete-state electrical comparison of the organized sine law¶

    Question, reference and scope¶

    The charge observation owner excludes a specified charge-density-only closure. Here phase remains part of the state. The question is whether the same organized quarter law, its retained-reference charge inventory and its full storage can be realized by a conventional passive network of capacitors, resistors and ideal sinusoidal Josephson junctions.

    This is a conditional constitutive comparison, not a physical identification or a derivation of the Josephson relations. The reference uses those relations as independently supplied physical laws. A formal positive quadratic storage is not sufficient to establish electrostatic realizability in physical island charge and voltage coordinates.

    The result below excludes a declared direct island-charge and phase map on C9 and the same-slot 54-node contact, even allowing a dense passive capacitance network, a ground reference, unobserved common phase and statically eliminated passive capacitor nodes. A separate constructive result realizes the exact organized six-child carrier by passive components after retaining one collective charge and phase per child. The direct obstruction does not classify arbitrary collective charge maps, ideal-transformer coordinate mixing, dynamic hidden circuitry, active synthesis, or a map defined only on a lower-dimensional prepared trajectory. Those alternatives need their own independent observation, law and work admissions.

    This detached proof changes no execution law, consumes no newly evaluated trajectory and installs no operator or event selector. Its organized states remain conditional test cases of the supplied complete law, as required by the NFR ontology. The research program alone selects subsequent work.

    Independent passive electrical reference¶

    Orient each junction edge once, and let \(B\) be its node-edge incidence matrix, so \(\delta=B^T\vartheta\). There is no supplied magnetic flux, phase frustration, external current source or time-dependent component. Electrical island phases are circular; a local continuous lift defines their derivatives without making the lift an additional observable charge. Geometric inductance, electromagnetic retardation, thermal noise and quantum fluctuations are omitted in this classical lumped reference. Component laws and that approximation require separate physical admission for an apparatus.

    The ideal single-junction reference is

    \[ I=I_c\sin\delta+\frac VR+C\frac{dV}{dt}, \qquad \frac{d\delta}{dt}=\frac V{a_0}, \qquad a_0=\frac{\hbar}{2e}>0. \tag{1} \]

    Equations (1) and (2) of Babenko et al., Characterization of a Josephson Junction Comb Generator (2020) give the current balance and voltage-phase relation. The exact sine law is an ideal reference premise; admitting it does not establish that every physical junction obeys it on every regime. The quantum constant in (1) comes from the reference and is not obtained from TNFR.

    Kirchhoff balance and capacitor storage assemble the reference into

    \[ q=C V,\qquad \dot q=-G V-B\mathop{\rm diag}(I_{c,e})\sin(B^T\vartheta), \qquad \dot\vartheta=V/a_0, \tag{2} \]

    with the appropriate common-potential gauge for a floating network. Here \(q\) is physical island charge relative to any independently fixed background offset, \(V\) is voltage, \(C\) is a symmetric capacitance matrix and \(G\) a symmetric passive conductance matrix. Dots in this owner mean laboratory time \(t\), in contrast to the structural prime below. All junction critical currents satisfy \(I_{c,e}>0\). The storage and loss are

    \[ E=\frac12 V^T C V+ a_0\sum_e I_{c,e}[1-\cos\delta_e], \qquad \dot E=-V^T G V\le0. \tag{3} \]

    Indeed \(\dot E=V^T\dot q+a_0\sum_e I_{c,e}\sin\delta_e\, \dot\delta_e\); substituting (2) cancels the junction terms. This derivation fixes the orientation and restoring sign rather than relying on a visually similar sine term.

    Ordinary passive two-terminal capacitors give

    \[ C_{ij}=-c_{ij}\le0\quad(i\ne j),\qquad C\mathbf1=c_g\ge0, \tag{4} \]

    where \(c_{ij}\ge0\) is a mutual capacitor and \(c_g\) contains capacitors to the reference. The same sign, symmetry and row-sum conditions hold for the grounded Maxwell capacitance matrix of conductors in the reference's fixed linear electrostatic regime; see Díaz and Herrera, The positivity and other properties of the matrix of capacitance: physical and mathematical implications, equations (3)-(8). A floating complete matrix has zero row sums and the common-potential null mode. A grounded restricted matrix is positive definite under the reference's usual connectedness assumptions. These are conditions on physical conductor charges and potentials, not on arbitrary linear coordinates of a positive energy.

    The two primary sources were reviewed on 2026-10-10; the capacitance reference is pinned to arXiv version 4. Only their constitutive relations and matrix conditions are used here, not their measured responses as TNFR evidence.

    The proof below only needs symmetry, positive energy, (4), and the exact laws (2). No numerical material value, apparatus or experimental dataset is admitted.

    Complete TNFR state and the declared direct map¶

    The separate operational identification derives the remaining component relation after independent calibration and the finite-work boundary for the source's phase interventions. It changes neither this reference nor the exact construction below.

    On a fixed connected simple unit graph with \(n\ge2\), write

    \[ L=BB^T,\quad S(\theta)=-B\sin(B^T\theta),\quad x'=\frac{-Lx+\gamma S(\theta)}4,\quad \theta'=\frac{\gamma Lx}4,\quad \gamma=\frac1{1023\pi}. \tag{5} \]

    Prime denotes the structural clock \(\tau\). Forms are signed real, phases are circular, capacities are held at one, and there is no forcing or event. The organized interaction owner proves

    \[ H=\frac12 x^T Lx+\sum_e[1-\cos(B^T\theta)_e],\qquad H'=-\frac14\|Lx\|^2,\qquad (\mathbf1^Tx)'=0. \tag{6} \]

    Retain a fixed reference \(r\), and specify before comparing responses

    \[ q=\kappa(x-r\mathbf1),\qquad \vartheta=\theta,\qquad \tau=\tau_0+b t,\qquad \kappa>0,\quad b>0. \tag{7} \]

    These are supplied observation and clock scales, not fitted consequences. With the normalized coordinates of (5), \(\kappa\) has units coulombs, \(b\) inverse seconds and \(a_0\) volt-seconds. An independently declared form unit must be retained in \(\kappa\); the numerical normalization is not a laboratory-unit bridge.

    Start on the invariant neutral leaf \(\mathbf1^Tq=0\), equivalently \(r=\bar x\) for that preparation, and let \(P=I-\mathbf1\mathbf1^T/n\). This is a stated restriction, not universal neutrality. Keeping the reference \(r\) is necessary when comparing inventory between regions or supports. The map on this leaf is invertible after the same common-phase convention is adopted on both sides. Simultaneously reversing charge and phase, or consistently permuting nodes and support, does not change the conclusions. An independent sign flip of only one coordinate changes the required capacitance sign or junction restoring sign.

    The map is tested on an open set of form variations in the neutral leaf and independent circular phase variations. It is not merely a match at one equilibrium or along one response trace. The linear phase-row restriction therefore applies in an arbitrarily small full-state neighborhood of an organized twist as well as on the full leaf.

    Necessary voltage, capacitance, conductance and energy scales¶

    The phase row and the reference's voltage-phase relation force

    \[ V=d Lq,\qquad d=\frac{a_0 b\gamma}{4\kappa}>0. \tag{8} \]

    Let \(L^+\) denote the Moore-Penrose inverse, with \(LL^+=L^+L=P\) and \(L^+\mathbf1=0\). A floating capacitance satisfying \(q=CV\) for every neutral \(q\) must be exactly

    \[ C_* = c_*L^+,\qquad c_* = \frac1d =\frac{4\kappa}{a_0 b\gamma}>0. \tag{9} \]

    This is necessary, not a convenient choice: multiplying \(q=dCLq\) on the neutral subspace by its inverse gives (9). Symmetry and the zero common mode fix its full floating extension. Positive definiteness of \(L^+\) on \(\mathbf1^\perp\) establishes positive quadratic energy, but does not yet establish (4).

    The form row becomes

    \[ \dot q=-\frac b4 Lq-\frac{b\kappa\gamma}4 B\sin(B^T\vartheta). \tag{10} \]

    Consequently each original junction needs \(I_c=b\kappa\gamma/4\), and the conductance must act on every relative voltage as

    \[ G|_{\mathbf1^\perp}=g_*I,\qquad g_* = \frac{\kappa}{a_0\gamma}>0. \tag{11} \]

    For a floating resistor network this gives \(G_*=g_*P\), implemented formally by an all-pairs resistor graph with conductance \(g_*/n\) on each pair. Unless the original support is complete, this dissipative support differs from the TNFR adjacency. Equality of nodal divergence or regional total current does not identify its individual physical wires with the original structural edges. Ground shunts \(G=g_*I\) give the same neutral voltage action but also require explicit ground-current accounting. A same-sparse-support resistor realization is already excluded by (11), independently of the capacitance issue.

    If one temporarily permits the formal positive matrix (9), the complete field and storage match exactly:

    \[ E=K H,\qquad K=\frac{a_0 b\kappa\gamma}4>0,\qquad \dot E=-\frac{Kb}4\|Lx\|^2=-V^TG_*V. \tag{12} \]

    The capacitor term follows from \(q^TC_*^+q/2=dq^TLq/2\); the junction term has the same factor \(K\). The scales retain two unselected positive parameters, \(\kappa\) and \(b\), even with \(a_0\) fixed by the physical reference. Neither complete field matching nor energy matching determines a measurement calibration.

    Thus (9)-(12) are an exact formal charge-phase representation of (5). The remaining question is whether its capacitance is a physical passive Maxwell capacitance in these particular charge coordinates.

    C9 gives an exact passive-capacitance obstruction¶

    For the unit cycle \(C_n\), the entry of \(L^+\) at graph distance \(d_{ij}\) is

    \[ (L^+)_{ij}=\frac{n^2-1}{12n} -\frac{d_{ij}(n-d_{ij})}{2n}. \tag{13} \]

    This identity is checked without a spectral approximation: the displayed circulant has zero row sums, and applying the cycle's second difference gives \(LL^+=P\). For C9 its diagonal and distances one through four are

    \[ \frac{20}{27},\quad \frac8{27},\quad -\frac1{27},\quad -\frac7{27},\quad -\frac{10}{27}. \tag{14} \]

    In particular, every adjacent off-diagonal entry of \(C_*\) is \(8c_*/27>0\), whereas every passive Maxwell off-diagonal entry is nonpositive. No positive charge or clock calibration can remove that sign. Node relabeling only relocates it; simultaneous charge and phase reversal leaves the capacitance relation unchanged.

    Conditional obstruction. The direct map (7), with the complete quarter law on C9 and independent neutral form variations, has no realization of (2) by ordinary passive capacitors or electrostatic conductor capacitance. The contradiction already occurs in the phase/voltage/charge relations; fitting a current response, changing resistor values, or adding more response samples cannot repair it. It also holds near an acute organized C9 twist, because the required linear relation (8) does not depend on its phase.

    This is not an obstruction on every graph. On \(K_n\), \(L=nP\) and \(L^+=P/n\); all off-diagonal entries are \(-1/n^2\). Then \(C_*=c_*P/n\) is a passive all-pairs capacitor network, \(G_*=g_*P\) is a passive all-pairs resistor network and the positive junction values above satisfy the complete equations and storage on the neutral leaf. This mathematical control shows why positivity, graph support and coordinate interpretation must be checked rather than globally rejecting the whole reference family. It is a supplied circuit construction, not a derivation of matter or a transfer of C9 acquisition certificates to \(K_n\).

    The same direct fine-node obstruction applies to the actual same-slot support \(C_9\square W_6\), without transferring a C9 subgraph conclusion by assumption. Let \(J\) copy a nine-vector into all six children. Then \(L_{54}J=JL_9\), and uniqueness of the centered inverse gives \(L_{54}^+J=JL_9^+\). In node labels \((c,j)\), for every child \(c\),

    \[ \sum_{d=0}^{5}(L_{54}^+)_{(c,j),(d,j+1)}=\frac8{27}>0. \tag{15} \]

    Every summand is off-diagonal, so at least one is positive. Thus the necessary fine-node capacitance again violates (4). This product argument applies to the same-slot support; it is not asserted for every alternative registration.

    Grounding, an unobserved common phase and eliminated capacitor nodes¶

    Suppose a grounded passive capacitance \(C\) obeys (4), and retain only relative electrical phase. Its voltage may then contain an unobserved common component: \(V=v+v_0\mathbf1\), with \(v=PV=dLq\). Write \(c=C\mathbf1\ge0\), \(s=\mathbf1^Tc\). On the neutral charge leaf,

    \[ 0=\mathbf1^Tq=c^Tv+s v_0. \tag{16} \]

    If \(s>0\), eliminating \(v_0\) gives

    \[ q=C_0v,\qquad C_0=C-\frac{cc^T}s,\qquad C_0\mathbf1=0,\qquad (C_0)_{ij}\le0\quad(i\ne j). \tag{17} \]

    The last inequality follows directly from \(c_i,c_j\ge0\) and (4). Equation (8) again forces \(C_0=c_*L^+\), contradicting (14). If \(s=0\), nonnegative \(c\) implies \(c=0\), so \(C\) is already floating and (9) applies. Thus an unobserved common electrical rotation and a passive ground capacitance do not evade the neutral-sector result. This argument is stronger than fixing zero common voltage in advance.

    Under strict absolute phase matching, symmetry separately permits only \(C=c_*L^++c_0\mathbf1\mathbf1^T/n\); positive energy requires \(c_0\ge0\), which makes the positive C9 off-diagonal entries no smaller. The relative-phase proof above does not rely on that stricter convention.

    Static removal of hidden passive capacitor nodes also preserves the obstruction. Partition a passive matrix into retained and hidden nodes. When its hidden block \(C_{hh}\) is invertible, eliminating held hidden charges gives the incremental retained matrix

    \[ C_{\rm eff}=C_{rr}-C_{rh}C_{hh}^{-1}C_{hr}. \tag{18} \]

    A grounded principal block of a connected passive capacitor network has an entrywise nonnegative inverse: the discrete maximum principle rules out a negative minimum of the solution to \(C_{hh}u=f\ge0\), and invertibility removes a constant isolated null component. Since \(C_{rh}\) and \(C_{hr}\) have nonpositive entries, the subtracted matrix in (18) is entrywise nonnegative, so the reduced off-diagonal entries remain nonpositive. The reduced row sums remain nonnegative; this also follows by treating the ground as a retained vertex of the augmented zero-row-sum capacitor graph. Then (17) applies if its common potential is removed. Held nonzero hidden charges change only an affine offset, not this incremental matrix.

    An isolated singular hidden block must first have its gauge or disconnected component declared; an inverse cannot be silently substituted for it. Dynamic hidden charges are different: eliminating them can introduce state or memory and is outside the static same-state realization established here.

    Exact collective carrier: a passive realization exists¶

    The direct fine-node obstruction is not the complete collective decision. On the same-slot support, retain the already admitted exact carrier

    \[ x_{c,j}=X_c,\qquad \theta_{c,j}=\alpha j+\Phi_c\pmod{2\pi},\qquad \alpha=2\pi/9,\qquad c=0,\ldots,5,\quad j=0,\ldots,8. \tag{19} \]

    The first five children form the host ring and child five is the donor. Their contact graph is the wheel \(W_6\). Each child retains its supplied nine-node identity, marking and internal twist. This is a restricted invariant manifold, not a claim that every recovered fine state lies on it.

    Internal form differences vanish and the two internal sine contributions cancel at every vertex. Each same-slot external neighbor contributes the same term at every slot. Therefore the full nonlinear fine law restricts exactly to

    \[ X'=\frac{-L_WX+\gamma S_W(\Phi)}4,\qquad \Phi'=\frac{\gamma L_WX}4. \tag{20} \]

    Here \(S_W=-B_W\sin(B_W^T\Phi)\). There is no tangent approximation, discarded memory or degree renormalization in (20). Common form and phase translations, circular covariance and wheel relabelings retain the declared mean/reference and marked-child conventions. Internal C9 winding stays one exactly on (19). Maintenance of an outer winding under a particular response is a separate condition; closure does not prove that every outer winding is preserved.

    For each child, preserve the already declared aggregate inventory,

    \[ q_c=\kappa\sum_{j=0}^{8}(x_{c,j}-r) =9\kappa(X_c-r),\qquad \vartheta_c=\Phi_c. \tag{21} \]

    The electrical island now represents a whole organized child, not one of its fine nodes. Its phase is the retained common phase relative to the supplied slot carrier \(\alpha j\), defined circularly on (19). It is not an arbitrary arithmetic average of nine independently chosen phase representatives. The physical observation of such a collective phase still needs independent justification. On the neutral leaf of (21), the same derivation as (8)-(12) gives

    \[ \begin{split} C_W&=\frac{36\kappa}{a_0 b\gamma}L_W^+, &G_W&=\frac{9\kappa}{a_0\gamma}P_6,\\ I_{c,W}&=\frac{9b\kappa\gamma}4, &V_W&=\frac{a_0b\gamma}{36\kappa}L_Wq, \qquad P_6=I_6-\frac{\mathbf1\mathbf1^T}{6}. \end{split} \tag{22} \]

    Unlike the direct C9 inverse, the centered wheel inverse has strictly negative off-diagonal entries. Its exact entries are

    Wheel entry Value of \(L_W^+\)
    Host diagonal \(103/396\)
    Donor diagonal \(55/396\)
    Adjacent host pair \(-5/396\)
    Nonadjacent host pair \(-41/396\)
    Host-donor pair \(-11/396\)

    The symmetric matrix with these entries has zero row sums and satisfies \(L_WL_W^+=P_6\), which verifies the inverse by exact arithmetic. It is positive definite on the neutral subspace. Every pair of distinct islands can therefore be joined by an ordinary positive mutual capacitor:

    \[ c_{cd}=\frac{36\kappa}{a_0b\gamma} \begin{cases} 5/396,&c,d\text{ adjacent host children},\\ 41/396,&c,d\text{ nonadjacent host children},\\ 11/396,&\text{one child is the donor}. \end{cases} \tag{23} \]

    Each of the fifteen island pairs also has resistor conductance \(9\kappa/(6a_0\gamma)\). The ten original wheel edges carry positive sinusoidal junctions with critical current (22). Thus this is an explicit finite passive circuit construction in the ideal reference class, not just a positive matrix lacking a passive component realization. Extra capacitor and resistor connections between nonadjacent host children are part of the construction; the mapping does not equate those wires with original TNFR support edges.
