# Carried regional prediction through a reciprocal finite contact

<a id="mixed-reciprocal-partner"></a>

The [regional-boundary result](ORGANIZED_PATTERN_CONSTITUTIVE_INHERITANCE.md#mixed-regional-boundary)
initializes a useful finite predictor from one associated regional reading.
Its two reference means then evolve under a supplied leak. This chapter asks
whether those means and their accumulated errors can be carried into contact
with another responding composite, without resetting either actual state or
making a new observation at contact entry.

The result is conditional on the same actual-source associations and complete
degree-loss law. A four-mean reference predicts outgoing form transfer within
a fixed error. Two actual partner histories show why omitting the partner's
regional information loses this prediction. This is approximate task-specific
prediction, not an exact reduced NFR, autonomous contact selection or
acquisition of a new joint identity.

[External interaction](ORGANIZED_EXTERNAL_INTERACTION.md#organized-external-interaction),
[interface factorization](ORGANIZED_INTERFACE_FACTORIZATION.md#organized-interface-factorization)
and [finite auxiliary exchange](ORGANIZED_PARENT_RESERVOIR_EXCHANGE.md#organized-parent-reservoir-exchange)
already treat reacting partners on other complete models.
[Preparation-informed initialization](ORGANIZED_MEMORY_INITIALIZATION.md#organized-memory-initialization-result)
and [computable prediction](ORGANIZED_COMPUTABLE_PREDICTION.md)
already distinguish carried references from replacement of an actual state.
The present result supplies this regional predictor's continuation; their
coefficients and source certificates are not transferred.

## 1. Actual sources and the complete supplied contact

For \(p=1,2\), retain an independently associated connected eighteen-node
[conditional UM successor](ORGANIZED_PATTERN_CONSTITUTIVE_INHERITANCE.md#mixed-coupling-successor).

eighteen old unit conductances and sixty-three actual proposed conductances.
Its regions \(A_p,B_p\) have nine nodes each. The winding-one and winding-two
constituents, their registration and actual conductance associations remain
part of the preparation.

Write \(H_p=L_{W_p}\), and let \(S_p\) be the unweighted pairwise sine
current on that same support. Both copies use the same structural clock:
\[
 x_p'=\frac{-H_px_p+\gamma S_p(\theta_p)}9+f_p,\qquad
 \theta_p'=\frac{\gamma H_px_p}9,\qquad
 \gamma=\frac1{1023\pi}<g:=\frac1{3000},\qquad
 \tau=\frac{1023}{1024}t.                                  \tag{1}
\]
Every time below is a duration in \(\tau\). The pressure is the independently
specified first row with capacity one. No laboratory clock, energy, mass or
charge identification is supplied.

Retain the earlier constants
\[
 \Delta=\frac1{1024},\quad \eta=20\,10^{-70},\quad R=\frac1{32},
 \quad L=h=T=\frac1{32},\quad a_{\max}=\frac1{16}.            \tag{2}
\]
Original form and phase preparation allowances are at most \(10^{-10}\).
Each actual source starts at a finite age with its nine marked registration
gaps independently bounded by a supplied \(\rho_p\le\Delta/8\).
Earlier convergence proves existence of finite ages meeting the fixed
positive threshold \(\Delta/8\); it supplies neither an observed numerical
age nor finite attainment of a stronger bound such as \(\rho_p=0\).
The latter remains an independent readiness premise if supplied.

Each source first receives balanced charging \(a_p\) on \(A_p\) and
\(-a_p\) on \(B_p\), with \(|a_p|\le a_{\max}\), for duration \(L\).
Each then receives the earlier leak \(f_p=-P_{A_p}x_p\) for duration \(h\).
An associated first-region mean reading is made before this leak, not after
it. These two actual histories reach the new contact without a state reset.
Their total absolute charging amount is at most
\(2\cdot18a_{\max}L=9/128\), and each balanced charge has zero net form.

At contact, remove both leaks and supply nine reciprocal form channels
between matching \(A\)-slots:
\[
 f_{1,j}=x_{2,j}-x_{1,j},\qquad
 f_{2,j}=x_{1,j}-x_{2,j}\quad(j\in A),\qquad
 f_{p,j}=0\quad(j\in B).                                  \tag{3}
\]
Their strength is one and they last for duration \(T\).
This is a supplied continuous joint input law, not a graph-edge insertion.
Neither internal degree, support, conductance, capacity nor phase row changes.
The full field consumes thirty-six forms followed by thirty-six phases.
Each copy retains its own phase gauge; no cross-copy phase law or common
phase-origin identification is used.

Let \(m_{pA}=\sum_{A_p}x_{p,j}/9\) and similarly for \(m_{pB}\).
The observable is signed **outgoing** structural form from the first copy:
\[
 Q(T)=9\int_0^T(m_{1A}-m_{2A})\,ds
 =9\left[(m_{1A}+m_{1B})(0)-(m_{1A}+m_{1B})(T)\right].
                                                               \tag{4}
\]
The second copy's total form change is \(Q\). Joint total form is conserved;
neither individual total is generally constant. Recorded output has
independent error \(\epsilon_Q\le10^{-6}\). The finite partner reacts
through its complete state, rather than supplying a prescribed waveform.
This fact alone gives no error bound for an approximation which freezes it.

## 2. Complete-state retention and supplied work

Let \(y_p=\theta_p-\Theta_p-b_p\mathbf1\), where \(b_p\) is that copy's
conserved actual phase gauge. The preceding proof bounds the full norm,
including common form after leakage:
\[
 N_p^2=\|x_p\|_2^2+\|y_p\|_2^2,\qquad N_p<\frac9{512}
 \quad\hbox{at contact entry}.                             \tag{5}
\]
It does not replace the reached state by the stationary template.

Stationarity gives the exact identity
\(S_p(\Theta_p+y_p)=-K_p(y_p)y_p\), with \(K_p\) the integrated edge
Hessian, not a linearization. The support and held weights give
\(\|H_p\|_2,\|K_p\|_2\le18\), hence \(\|H_p-K_p\|_2\le36\).
For \(\mathcal N^2=N_1^2+N_2^2\), both rows of (1) imply
\[
 \begin{split}
 \tfrac12(\mathcal N^2)'={}&
 \sum_{p=1}^2\left[-\frac{x_p^TH_px_p}{9}
       +\frac{\gamma}{9}x_p^T(H_p-K_p)y_p\right]\\
 &-\sum_{j\in A}(x_{1,j}-x_{2,j})^2
 \ \le 2g\mathcal N^2.
 \end{split}                                               \tag{6}
\]
The upper norm derivative obeys \(\mathcal N'\le2g\mathcal N\),
including at zero by continuity. For \(0\le s\le T\),
\[
 \mathcal N(s)
 <\sqrt2\,\frac9{512}e^{2gT}
 <\frac{17}{12}\frac9{512}\frac{48000}{47999}
 <\frac1{40}<R.                                           \tag{7}
\]
Here \(2gT=1/48000\), and \(e^z\le(1-z)^{-1}\) for \(0\le z<1\).
Each copy is bounded by this joint norm. It need not retain its smaller
individual entry bound \(9/512\) during reciprocal exchange.

The source and prefix bounds, followed by (7), retain the original phase
charts and all four C9 windings. The marked-gap derivative bound is \(4gR\).
Over all three finite stages,
\[
 |\hbox{marked gap change}|
 \le4gR(L+h+T)=\frac1{256000}<\frac{\Delta}{8}.              \tag{8}
\]
Readiness therefore keeps all eighteen original marked gaps below
\(\Delta/4\). This retains both original objects; it does not establish
an outer phase identity between them.

Norm dissipation must not be confused with the fine storage balance.
For the earlier internal storage \(V_p\) and \(q_p=H_px_p\),
\[
 V_p'=-\frac{\|q_p\|_2^2}{9}+q_p^Tf_p,\qquad
 (V_1+V_2)'_{\rm added}
 =-\sum_{j\in A}(q_{1,j}-q_{2,j})(x_{1,j}-x_{2,j}).          \tag{9}
\]
The added term is not generally nonpositive. Opposite form inputs do not
establish physical energy conservation or passivity of this storage.
For example, Cauchy--Schwarz bounds its absolute value by
\(36\mathcal N^2\), so its integrated absolute work is bounded by
\(36T/40^2\) on this window. There is no support or state jump at switching,
but the continuing supplied input and its work remain explicit.
Retention uses (6), not a false monotonicity assertion for (9).

## 4. Carrying initialization through the previous leak

Let \(\mu_p\) be copy \(p\)'s original pre-leak regional reading,
associated with error \(\epsilon_p\le10^{-6}\). At that earlier instant,
balanced preparation supplied global mean zero, so the actual pair was
\((m_{pA},-m_{pA})\). Its reading interval must intersect
\([-3/512,3/512]\); this consistency check does not clamp the reading or
authenticate a source.

Define the preceding leak reference and its propagated center by
\[
 M=\begin{pmatrix}-1-k_0&k_0\\k_0&-k_0\end{pmatrix},\quad
 P=e^{hM},\qquad \bar z_p=P(\mu_p,-\mu_p)^T.                \tag{14}
\]
The Metzler matrix \(M\) has nonpositive row sums, so its semigroup
contracts the maximum norm. The earlier residual has magnitude at most
\(F\); therefore the actual contact-entry pair satisfies
\[
 \|z_p(0)-\bar z_p\|_\infty\le\epsilon_p+hF.                \tag{15}
\]
Both coordinates must be carried. Leakage changes total form:
generally \(\bar z_{pB}\ne-\bar z_{pA}\), and the actual endpoint likewise
has no opposite-means constraint. Reimposing it resets information lost
through the preceding boundary.

Stack the pairs to obtain \(\bar z\), propagate \(e^{sJ}\bar z\), and
define the mathematical reference output
\[
 Q_{\rm ref}=9(1,1,0,0)\,[I-e^{TJ}]\,\bar z.                \tag{16}
\]
This is the conservative reference's balance (4), equivalently its signed
flux integral. An accumulator is computational, not another constituent.
A signed output row must not become a negative off-diagonal entry of a
supposedly Metzler generator.

Put \(\epsilon_*=\max(\epsilon_1,\epsilon_2)\). Contraction gives
\[
 \|z(s)-e^{sJ}\bar z\|_\infty\le\epsilon_*+hF+sF.
\]
Apply this to the original flux \(9(m_{1A}-m_{2A})\), instead of bounding
the two endpoint totals independently:
\[
 |Q-Q_{\rm ref}|\le18T(\epsilon_*+hF)+9T^2F.               \tag{17}
\]
This prices hidden initialization, prefix leakage and the new responding
partner. It consumes no new contact-entry reading, previous measured
transfer, future mean or actual fine trajectory.

Enclose both exponentials and signed products in (14)--(16) with the
shared rational Metzler owner. Let \(\widehat Q\) be the midpoint of the
resulting reference-output interval, whose radius must be at most
\(10^{-35}\). This allowance covers prefix propagation, contact propagation
and output subtraction. It is added once, not silently discarded:
\[
 |Q_{\rm obs}-\widehat Q|
 \le18T(\epsilon_*+hF)+9T^2F+\epsilon_Q+10^{-35}
 \le\frac{187}{102400000}+10^{-35}<2\,10^{-6}.              \tag{18}
\]
The fraction is the fixed worst case with both reading bounds at
\(10^{-6}\). Two original aggregate readings suffice together with the
stated source, law and boundary information. Four reference means evolve;
no universal minimal-state or one-coordinate closure claim follows.

## 5. Actual partner histories a weaker description cannot resolve

These are two supplied histories from a shared source, not arbitrary points
in a containing norm ball or an imposed trajectory of the first object.

Initialize charging references from the same actual regional pair.
With \(v=(1,-1)^T\), their difference after charging is \(dv\), where
\[
 d=2a_{\max}\int_0^L e^{-2k_0s}\,ds
 \ge2a_{\max}L(1-L/2).                                    \tag{19}
\]
Their leak-reference difference is \(dPv\). During contact the

\[
 D=\begin{pmatrix}-k_0-2&k_0\\k_0&-k_0\end{pmatrix}.        \tag{20}
\]
This identity follows from the full four-mean reference; it does not

Both \(e^{sM}\) and \(e^{sD}\) contract the maximum norm.

the first-row derivative under \(M\) has magnitude at most
\(1+2k_0<2\). Starting from \(w\), the corresponding derivative under

\[
 9\int_0^T e_A^Te^{sD}Pv\,ds
 \ge9T(1-2h-3T/2)>0.                                    \tag{21}
\]
Higher right loading decreases outgoing \(Q\). The positive comparison
is therefore \(Q_{\text{right }-}-Q_{\text{right }+}\).

Each charging-plus-leak reference has per-coordinate discrepancy at most

 \begin{split}
 Q_{\text{right }-,{\rm obs}}-Q_{\text{right }+,{\rm obs}}
 \ge{}&18a_{\max}LT(1-L/2)(1-2h-3T/2)\\

 \end{split}                                               \tag{22}

The first term is \(32319/33554432>19/20000\).
The losses are less than \(3\,10^{-6}\), so the bound exceeds
\(947\,10^{-6}\), itself above the frozen target.
These analytical inequalities do not evaluate nonlinear endpoints.

The weak decoder retains the first copy's information and pre-contact
history, the common second-copy source before charging, known law,

The positive predictor retains both original readings and avoids this

not failure of every frozen-partner approximation, a universal minimum

## 7. The fixed transfer does not resolve phase feedback at its noise allowance

The preceding successful predictor bounds the live phase contribution but

### Shared preparation and a supplied comparator switch

Retain both original sources, their actual weights and all charge-and-leak
histories through the contact-entry state. Both hypotheses therefore have
the same two original readings and the same complete seventy-two-coordinate
entry state. At this instant supply a mathematical switch for the comparator
only. The actual model continues (1)--(3), while the comparator obeys
\[
 \widetilde x_p'=-\frac{H_p\widetilde x_p}{9}
                        +\widetilde f_p,\qquad
 \widetilde\theta_p'=\frac{\gamma H_p\widetilde x_p}{9}.
                                                               \tag{23}
\]
Here \(\widetilde f_1=\widetilde x_2-\widetilde x_1\) and
\(\widetilde f_2=-\widetilde f_1\) on the nine matched \(A\)-slots, and
both vanish on \(B\). Each hypothesis has two responding partners.
Only \(\gamma S_p/9\) in the form row is removed. The phase row is live,
with the same nonzero \(\gamma\); it is not clamped or assigned zero.

The clock, capacities, internal support, weights and input registration
are unchanged. No contact edge is inserted into \(H_p\) or \(S_p\).
The switch is a supplied intervention on an actual source formed under
the original law, not a claim that (23) independently formed that source.

Both copies' independent phase gauges remain conserved.

### A bound on the true full-law difference

Stack the thirty-six forms, and define
\[

Write \(e_x=x-\widetilde x\) and \(e_\theta=\theta-\widetilde\theta\).
The exact shared entry gives \(e_x(0)=e_\theta(0)=0\).
Subtracting both complete systems yields
\[
 e_x'=Ae_x+\frac{\gamma}{9}S(\theta),\qquad
 e_\theta'=\frac{\gamma}{9}{\cal H}e_x.                    \tag{28}
\]
The force in (28) is the **actual nonlinear sine current**, not a

\]

## 19. Persistent registered identity and full relaxation under the same port

Continue the same unit-strength A contact without changing any evolution
row. Let \(t=0\) here denote its original activation, after the full-contact
prefix \(T=2000000\) and paid cut \(U=10000\), in the same structural clock
\(\tau\). The reached state at \(t=1/2\) is not reinitialized. At activation
alone the prefix laws give \(a(0)=0\); the subsequent common mode stays live.
All original source-error pairs, actual weight associations, phase gauges,
unit capacities, branches and eighteen marked observations are retained.

The [moving-reference storage method](SINE_PREPARATION_ROBUSTNESS.md#sine-relative-preparation-storage)
already explains the Bregman cancellation and the need to establish its
whole-segment domain. The [actual successor convergence result](ORGANIZED_PATTERN_CONSTITUTIVE_INHERITANCE.md#mixed-coupling-successor)
already uses a compact invariant sublevel and the full zero-loss equations.
Their model-specific constants do not transfer. Here the new ingredients
are a regional reference with finite total variation, the actual transport
metric in its comparison, and a source-inclusive uniform registration bound.

### Establish the actual chart before comparing states

At A activation, \(E_+=E_{\rm old}\), because \(a(0)=0\). The actual full
contact and cut retain the upper bound (132), so
\[
 E_+(0)\le E_0=\tfrac{27}{2}\Delta^2+486\eta+18\eta^2+9r_*^2,
 \qquad 5E_0<1/10000<R^2,\qquad R=1/32.                 \tag{202}
\]
Initial inclusion is inherited from that same reached history. Before a
first exit from the original acute ball, (192) and (196) give
\[
 N(t)^2\le5E_+(t)\le5E_0<1/10000.                       \tag{203}
\]
This contradicts any exit through \(N=R\). The smooth held field therefore
remains in this compact, mean-fixed acute neighborhood for every \(t\ge0\).
The whole form mean and both phase gauges remain fixed; no unbounded mean
direction has been removed without its conservation law. All four original
cycle windings persist. Tight marked registration still requires a different
argument: (203) alone is not the \(\Delta/4\) bound.

### A nominal cone survives the generated common-form mode

Continue the carried nominal regional family from the same activation,
retaining its inherited phases and velocities. Put
\[
 s=q_D+q_R,\quad z=q_D-q_R,\quad
 u=-a,\quad b_v=-(v_D-v_R),\quad V=v_D+v_R,
 \quad A=4\gamma^2kC/9.
\]
The symbol \(b_v\) is a velocity coordinate, distinct from the original
section level \(b=3\Delta/16\). The full nominal rows give
\[
 \begin{split}
 s''+2ks'+2A\sin(s/2)\cos(z/2)&=0,\qquad s'=4\gamma kV,\\
 z'&=-4\gamma k b_v,\\
 u'&=-u+b_v,\\
 b_v'&=-(2k+1)b_v+u+(2\gamma C/9)\sin(z/2)\cos(s/2).
 \end{split}                                               \tag{204}
\]
For \(q_{\rm hi}=b+\epsilon/2\), the invariant cone is
\[
 0\le s,z\le q_{\rm hi},\quad -\gamma^2s\le s'\le0,
 \quad0\le b_v\le\gamma z,\quad0\le u\le(6/5)\gamma z.
                                                               \tag{205}
\]
At activation the old cut wedges give these position and \(s'\) bounds,
\(b_v=-z'/(4\gamma k)\le\gamma z/(4k)<\gamma z\), and \(u=0\).
The lower velocity/common-mode faces are inward. On the upper \(b_v\) face,
the outward derivative divided by \(\gamma z\) is at most
\(-2k+1/5+C/9+4\gamma^2k<0\). On the upper \(u\) face it is at most
\(-1/5+(24/5)\gamma^2k<0\). The \(s\) wedge follows from
\(2k/3-\gamma^2>0\), as in (138). The zero-position faces force their
associated velocities to zero. These inequalities use \(4/9<k<1/2\),
\(0<C<3\), \(\gamma<1/3000\); they cover intersections of the faces too.
Thus \(s,z\) are nonnegative and nonincreasing, even though \(u\) is generated.

The exact regional norm identities are
\[
 \|\bar y\|=\tfrac32\sqrt{s^2+z^2},\qquad
 \|\bar x\|=3\sqrt{u^2+b_v^2+V^2}.
\]
Consequently \(\|\bar y\|\le3q_{\rm hi}\),
\(\|\bar x\|<9gq_{\rm hi}\), and the nominal joint norm is below
\(4q_{\rm hi}<1/100\). This establishes its chart independently of (203).
Actual/reference phase segments therefore remain in the same convex chart.

More than pointwise smallness is available. Since \(u(0)=0\), its convolution
law and nonnegative \(b_v\) imply \(\int_0^\infty u\le\int_0^\infty b_v\).
Integrating the monotone \(s,z\) equations gives
\[
 \begin{split}
 \int_0^\infty\|\bar y'\|\,dt
 &\le\tfrac32\int_0^\infty(|s'|+|z'|)\,dt
 \le3q_{\rm hi}=:L_y,\\
 \int_0^\infty\|\bar x\|\,dt
 &\le3\int_0^\infty(u+b_v+|V|)\,dt

 \end{split}                                               \tag{206}

These are uniform path and integral bounds, not evaluated trajectories.
