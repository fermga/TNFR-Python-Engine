# Actual Coupling between two maintained sine organizations

This admission connects two independently prepared organizations through the
registered UM action. It retains the [complete word and weighted continuation](SINE_PAIR_COUPLING.md),
the [sufficient pair-state criterion](SINE_PAIR_STATE.md#sine-pair-support-symmetry)
and the [ontology's separate identity and formation obligations](../EMERGENT_ONTOLOGY.md).
The resulting connection, constituent retention and acquisition of a new
common identity are different claims.

  Supply the same form origin `9/32<m<11/32` and phase origin zero in both
  components. Independently in each component, supply centered deviations
  `xi,eta` with `sum xi=sum eta=0` and `||xi||^2+||eta||^2<z^2`, so
  `x=m+xi`, `theta=theta_*+eta` on the declared lifts. Initial capacities

- The two nominal static sources have `m=5/16` and exact target midpoint

  the other has every internal split zero. Materialize the represented
  controls with the previously frozen dyadic twist
  `alpha_hat=674651885/536870912`. Preserve represented primitives and
  arithmetic defects separately from the exact-real theorem.
- Predict thirteen actual added edges: the internal `A_0` edge and all
  twelve edges from its two members to the six members of `B_0,B_1,B_4`.
  Retain every generated conductance. Predict bare degrees eleven on
  `A_0`, six on those three B pairs, and four elsewhere. No new edge is
  inserted by the analysis and no actual weight is repaired.

  beyond the old unordered pair coordinates. Compare an actual split output

  identity tube by `t=3/8`, through a signed pair-mean form contrast.
  These are elapsed structural times after the completed word; the word's
  stage count is not elapsed continuous time.
- Use exact algebra and static independent controls with the preceding
  controls' 100-digit arithmetic and `2^-46` ordinary response tolerance;
  smaller algebraic contrasts retain their separately stated error bounds.
  No trajectory, parameter search, event selector, varying-weight law,
  reservoir or physical identification belongs to this protocol. A tube

The common initial registration, supplied word and selected complete law are
premises. They do not derive why contact occurs. Static controls cannot
replace the all-source inequalities or certify an unproved later behavior.

## 2. Actual candidate inventory, reset and contact support

The separate source components satisfy the existing conservative winding
barrier before contact. Their independent laws and mean leaves are retained;
connectivity of their disjoint union is not assumed. Their common phase
registration is needed by the following cross-component candidate argument.

Until UM commits new support, every selected target has exactly its four
old A neighbors. Consequently its consensus, phase proposals, capacity blend,
stored-pressure relief and subsequent SHA reset are exactly the maps in
[the previous word theorem](SINE_PAIR_COUPLING.md#collective-coupling-map).
In particular, UM reads the old neighbors, not its prospective new contacts.
The B state, history and lifecycle are unchanged by every reset. Selected
capacities become `1/2`, all other capacities remain one, and only the two
selected forms increase by `b=1/1024`.

For a selected A member, the snapshot U3 gate admits its own pair partner
and all members of `B_0,B_1,B_4`. Their phase gaps are at most
`alpha+2*z<pi/2`. The A distance-two pairs and `B_2,B_3` have wrapped
gaps at least `2*alpha-2*z>pi/2`, so they fail before any phase proposal.
Existing A edges are not new-link candidates. The all-candidate policy
therefore proposes precisely one internal edge and twelve cross edges.

Let `theta'` be the merged phases and let `x'` be the post-AL forms.
For each admitted cross edge `(i,j)`, the actual compatibility weight in
exact real arithmetic is

\[
W_{ij}=1-
 \frac{|\operatorname{wrap}(\theta_i'-\theta_j')|}{2\pi}
 -\frac{|x_i'-x_j'|}{4(|x_i'|+|x_j'|+\epsilon_E)},
\]

where `epsilon_E` is the represented positive implementation regularizer
`1e-9`. The homogeneous `Si` contribution cancels. No resulting weight is
replaced by this formula's rounded or unit limit in the continuation.

The selected phase increment is below `3*h*z`, while B phases do not move.
Thus a final cross phase gap is below `alpha+3*z`, and its form difference
is below `2*z+b=5*z/2`. Both endpoint forms exceed `1/4`, giving

\[
W_{ij}>
1-\frac{\alpha+3z}{2\pi}-\frac{5z}{4}
>\frac45-2z>\frac34.
\]

The previous internal-edge bound gives `W_internal>1-sqrt(2)*z>3/4`.
Every predicted candidate therefore passes both the final phase gate and
the score threshold. The output has exactly 53 edges: forty retained unit
edges and thirteen actual new edges, all with positive weight at most one.
New edges have the actual `weight` attribute and no inserted `length`.

The two selected degrees are eleven. The six B members in pairs `0,1,4`
have degree six, while every other degree remains four. This is a connected
support. The connection is a six-member contact fan, even for the nominal
synchronized-tip control; the unchanged `pi/2` phase gate and `3/4`
threshold do not select B pair zero alone.

Only the selected targets execute the word's lifecycle/history transitions.
The four old A neighbors receive merged phase writes; new B contacts do not
receive such writes during this same UM stage. Selected stored pressure is
relieved using the original four neighbors. The new continuous pressure
later uses all actual neighbors and is a separate evaluation. These timing
and support distinctions are part of the model, not implementation noise.

## 3. Sufficient boundary state and an explicit loss of pair closure

Every independent pair interchange preserves the new bare support. It need
not preserve its actual conductances: the compatibility score depends on
the constituent form and phase at each endpoint. For generic source states,
the four boundary pairs `A_0,B_0,B_1,B_4` therefore have nonconstant rows or
columns in their cross-conductance blocks. Equal bare neighbor counts do
not restore the missing weighted symmetry.

The full state with its weighted incidence and associated execution records
remains sufficient. A mixed collective description can retain the original
unordered coordinates for nonboundary pairs and the already available
[marked-port offsets](SINE_PAIR_INTERACTION.md#sine-pair-emission-descent)
`u_p=x_p-X`, `v_p=sin(theta_p-Theta)` for a marked member of each boundary
pair, together with the actual cross matrices and their association to those
members. Since `R>0`, these offsets reconstruct both member states without
division by a possibly vanishing split. Quotienting the joint state and
boundary data by simultaneous relabeling is also sufficient. Discarding
the association between a weight row and a member state is not.

An exact obstruction is already present at the nominal split source.
The target consensus is symmetric, so after UM its phases are `+/-delta'`.
The B zero-pair phases are zero, and its member forms are `m`. Put
`D_E=2*m+b+epsilon_E`. The cross weights from the plus and minus A members
to either B zero-pair member are

\[
w_\pm=1-\frac{|\delta'|}{2\pi}
 -\frac{b\pm u}{4(D_E\pm u)},\qquad
w_+-w_-=-\frac{u(2m+\epsilon_E)}{2(D_E^2-u^2)}<0.
\]

Here the frozen `0<u<b` ensures that both form differences have the
displayed sign. Hold the actual weighted graph fixed and interchange both
`x,theta` of the two A target members. Every old retained pair coordinate
`X,Theta,R,U,Q` is unchanged. The two B zero-pair nodes each have degree
six and capacity one. Their mean phase rate nevertheless changes by

\[
\boxed{\Delta\dot\Theta_{B_0}
 =\frac{u(w_+-w_-)}{3\pi}
 =-\frac{u^2(2m+\epsilon_E)}{6\pi(D_E^2-u^2)}\ne0.}
\]

The difference follows directly from the two weighted form-gradient terms:
their change is `2*u*(w_+-w_-)` at each B node. All other incident terms
are unchanged. This supplies an obstruction to a future law on the old
unordered pair state alone on this same weighted graph. It is not an
obstruction to the retained full-state law or to the connected organization.

If the two weight rows and associated records are interchanged with the A
member states, the full continuous field is instead relabeled covariantly.
Likewise the actual all-candidate word maps a jointly relabeled source to
the corresponding jointly relabeled output in exact arithmetic. The
complete event therefore descends on the orbit of state and boundary data,
while its output generally leaves the old fixed-weight independent-swap
stratum. Finite binary64 controls retain their arithmetic tolerance rather
than asserting universal bitwise equivariance.

## 4. Admitted continuation and complete work accounting

Let `U` be the actual connected bare support, `W` its held conductances,
`d_i` its bare degrees and `nu_i` its post-word held capacities. The
selected [weighted completion](SINE_PAIR_COUPLING.md#collective-coupling-weighted-law)
has, in the original structural clock,

\[
q=L_Wx,\quad S_i=\sum_{j\in N_U(i)}\sin(\theta_j-\theta_i),\quad
M=\operatorname{diag}\frac{\nu_i}{\pi d_i},\qquad
\dot x=MS,\quad\dot\theta=Mq.
\]

Its independently specified pressure is `p_i=S_i/(pi*d_i)`. The stored UM
pressure does not replace it. The exact full storage is

\[
H=\frac12x^TL_Wx+
 \sum_{\{i,j\}\in U}[1-\cos(\theta_j-\theta_i)],
\qquad \dot H=q^TMS-S^TMq=0.
\]

The old unit-only reader still correctly rejects generic output weights.
The new completion admits them without weakening that separate model.
For `rho_i=d_i/nu_i`, the global lifted form and phase means weighted by
`rho_i` are conserved. Separate constituent means and storages need not be
conserved after contact. Reusing their pre-contact conservation as an
identity certificate would omit the newly acquired boundary exchange.

For example, let `q_A,S_A` denote sums over A's internal support, including
its new within-pair edge, and `q_C,S_C` the cross-contact sums at A. Its
internal storage obeys

\[
\dot H_A=q_A^TM_A S_C-S_A^TM_Aq_C.
\]

The corresponding formula holds for B; together with the derivative of
the cross-contact storage they sum to zero. The actual total-degree
normalization remains in `M_A,M_B`. The boundary terms explain how a
conservative total law can change a constituent's old identity geometry.

For the supplied word, let `E_A,E_B` be the forty old edges and `C` the
twelve cross contacts. AL acts before any added support, with work

\[
\Delta H_{\rm AL}
=4b\sum_{j\sim0}(X_{A,0}-X_{A,j})+4b^2.
\]

UM's phase work on old edges is

\[
\Delta H_{\rm UM,old}
=\sum_{\{i,j\}\in E_A\cup E_B}
 [\cos(\theta_j-\theta_i)-\cos(\theta_j'-\theta_i')].
\]

Here phases in the first term are the post-AL snapshot; AL leaves them
unchanged. B contributes zero because its state does not reset. The full
attachment cost, evaluated at the actual common post-UM endpoint, is

\[
\Delta H_{\rm attach}
=2\omega u_{A,0}^2+2\sin^2\delta_{A,0}'
+\sum_{(i,j)\in C}
 \left[\frac{W_{ij}}2(x_i'-x_j')^2+
             1-\cos(\theta_j'-\theta_i')\right],
\]

where `omega` is the actual internal-edge conductance. Thus
`H_after-H_before=DeltaH_AL+DeltaH_UM,old+DeltaH_attach` exactly.
Capacity synchronization, pressure relief and SHA change subsequent rates
but add no direct term to this storage. No sign is assigned to the old-edge
phase work and no reservoir pays for the new edges. Conservation between
events does not make the supplied contact passive.

## 5. Uniform finite retention of both original identities

For each component let `P` be its ten-node arithmetic-centering projection
and define the original geometric identity radius along continuous lifts by

\[
Z_C(t)^2=\|P x_C(t)\|^2+
          \|P(\theta_C(t)-\theta_*)\|^2,\qquad C\in\{A,B\}.
\]

This removes each component's instantaneous common form and phase origin;
it does not assume those means remain conserved. The identity tube is
`Z_C<r=1/16`. It implies the old winding neighborhood, acute old edges
and nonantipodal pair chart. It is a particular declared identity, not a
definition of every possible organized state.

Use elapsed time zero immediately after the whole word. The preceding
reset bounds give

\[
Z_A(0)<\frac{13z}{4},\qquad Z_B(0)<z,
\qquad R_0=2z+b=\frac{5z}{2},
\]

where `R_0` bounds the initial global form range. Because all actual
conductances satisfy `0<W<=1` and `0<nu<=1`, the complete rows imply
globally

\[
|\dot x_i|\le\frac1\pi,\qquad
\max_i x_i(t)-\min_i x_i(t)\le R_0+\frac{2t}{\pi},
\]

\[
|\dot\theta_i(t)|\le\frac1\pi
 \left(R_0+\frac{2t}{\pi}\right),\qquad
|\theta_i(t)-\theta_i(0)|
 \le\frac{R_0t}{\pi}+\frac{t^2}{\pi^2}.
\]

These bounds also exclude finite-time escape of the smooth lifted solution;
they require no local acute-domain premise. Since centering is a contraction,
each constituent's full centered-state displacement is at most

\[
B(t)=\frac{\sqrt{10}\,t}{\pi}
 \sqrt{1+\left(R_0+\frac t\pi\right)^2}.
\]

For `0<=t<=1/64`, use `sqrt(10)<10/3`, `pi>3` and
`sqrt(1+(R_0+t/pi)^2)<101/100` to obtain

\[
Z_A(t),Z_B(t)
 <\frac{13}{2048}+\frac{101}{5760}
 =\frac{2201}{92160}<\frac1{32}<r.
\]

Both source identities therefore survive the actual contact for the full
frozen finite interval. In particular, old edge gaps remain below
`alpha+sqrt(2)*r<pi/2` and internal pair gaps below `sqrt(2)*r`.
The exact fine flow and retained boundary state remain sufficient throughout
this interval. This theorem does not identify a new common identity or
transfer an all-time independent-component barrier to the joined graph.

## 6. The receiver necessarily leaves its original identity tube

The connection changes the complete field even at the original exact
twists. Each node of B pair one acquires two zero-phase A neighbors, giving
an additional sine sum `-2*sin(alpha)`; B pair four acquires the opposite
sum. Their old internal sine sums vanish at that reference. Thus the old
reference is not a critical point of the joined phase storage. A copied
pre-contact critical-point barrier cannot prove joined maintenance.

A stronger conclusion follows from the full rows without a trajectory.
Let

\[
D(t)=X_{B,1}(t)-X_{B,4}(t)
 =\frac{x_{12}+x_{13}-x_{18}-x_{19}}2.
\]

Its four nonzero coefficients sum to zero and have Euclidean norm one,
so `|D(t)|<=||P x_B(t)||<=Z_B(t)` and `|D(0)|<z`.
Every absolute phase error from the original common-reference twist after
the word is bounded by

\[
a_0=z+\frac{4h}{3}=\frac{5z}{3}.
\]

Indeed this covers the four old A-neighbor phase writes, the smaller target
writes and the unchanged B coordinates. The global phase bound above then
gives, for all twenty retained lifted phases,

\[
|\theta_i(t)-\theta_{*,i}|
 \le a(t)=a_0+\frac{R_0t}{\pi}+\frac{t^2}{\pi^2}.
\]

Each of the six sine terms at a B pair-one or pair-four member differs
from its reference value by at most `2*a(t)`, using the global Lipschitz
bound for sine. Their capacities are still one and their degrees six.
Consequently the difference of their pair-mean form rates satisfies

\[
\dot D(t)\le-\frac{2\sin\alpha}{3\pi}
             +\frac{4a(t)}\pi.
\]

Integration retains the signed nominal response and bounds its whole
nonlinear remainder:

\[
D(T)<z-\frac{2\sin\alpha}{3\pi}T
 +\frac{4a_0}\pi T+
 \frac{2R_0}{\pi^2}T^2+
 \frac4{3\pi^3}T^3.
\]

At the frozen `T=3/8`, the elementary strict bounds
`sin(2*pi/5)>19/20` and `3<pi<22/7` give the entirely rational estimate

\[
\boxed{D(3/8)<-\frac{374311}{5406720}
 <-\frac1{16},\qquad
Z_B(3/8)>\frac1{16}.}
\]

The sine bound follows directly from
`sin(2*pi/5)=sqrt(10+2*sqrt(5))/4`. The rational comparison retains
strict margin `36391/5406720` beyond the tube radius. The argument does
not stop at a local derivative or assume the state stayed in that tube;
its form-range and phase-displacement bounds are global on the declared
fixed-support segment. Thus every exact source in the frozen family has
left the old B identity tube by `t=3/8`, although both identities were
retained throughout `0<=t<=1/64`.

This is a precise obstruction to all-time retention of the original small
identity tube for this supplied contact fan and complete law. It is not a
proof of winding loss, disappearance of all organized structure, failure
of UM, or failure of the weighted law. A deformed continuing organization
or a larger common identity would need its own state, identity definition
and maintenance argument. Replacing that target after evaluating this
response would not convert the present scoped exit into a retained-source
success.

## 7. Static controls and the resulting admission boundary

The [independent static controls](../../tests/operators/test_collective_organization_contact.py)
retain both frozen nominal preparations. They check each separate source's
barrier, actual whole-word support and associated writes, all generated
weights, the admitted connected field, complete event work, fixed-boundary
state-swap discrimination and joint relabeling. Exact rational and interval
controls also check the finite-retention and signed-exit bounds. No
continuous trajectory is evaluated. Source preparation, represented input
defects and the exact-real family remain separately identifiable.

The independent reset/weight oracle uses 100-digit arithmetic with absolute
error allowance `2^-46`. The smaller boundary discriminator instead compares
exact rational phase-rate numerators reconstructed from the stored forms and
conductances; its algebraic equality has no floating tolerance. It does not
substitute the exact symmetric-source weight formula for represented weights.
The dyadic twist also has a small common phase-origin and shape defect
relative to `k*(2*pi/5)`. The source controls re-admit its centered geometry;
they do not assert equality to the exact frozen coordinates. The retention
and exit inequality controls use the exact mathematical twist independently
of that materialization. Common phase rotation is an exact-law symmetry,
not a universal bitwise guarantee for the represented event implementation.

The gate therefore admits an actual supplied connection with sufficient
boundary state and finite retention, and excludes perpetual retention of
the declared original small receiver tube for this family. The exact result
requires no higher-precision response campaign. Its remaining mathematical
question is what continuing identity, if any, is supported by the actual
joined system after this obligatory deformation; neither connectivity,
conservation nor the old pair quotient answers that question alone.
