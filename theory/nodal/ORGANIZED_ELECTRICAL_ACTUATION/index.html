    A finite input on the actual five-child organization¶

    Prospective declaration¶

    The electrical identification owner excludes a literal instantaneous relative-phase jump with finite work in the fixed passive reference. This construction replaces the first positive intervention with one finite, externally supplied form input. It does not derive the occurrence of that input from an autonomous TNFR source.

Retain the reached state, all fine directions and conserved means
    Entry history Continue the host unforced for the original first observation window h0=1e-5; intervene immediately before its first +epsilon*k0 event. No donor, later phase event, contact or old response verdict is a prerequisite
    Support and law C9-by-C5, nodes 9*c+j, all 90 unit edges, held unit capacities; x'=(-L*x+gamma*S(theta))/4+T*f, theta'=gamma*L*x/4, gamma=1/(1023*pi), full-circle pairwise sine

    1. Independently supplied waveform and complete evolution¶

    Write T for repetition over the nine slots and L=I5 tensor L9 + L5 tensor I9. The marked vector k is neutral. On C5,

    \[ v=L_5^+k=(2/5,0,-1/5,-1/5,0)^T,\quad L_5v=k. \tag{1} \]

    This is the inverse on the neutral sector, with no grounded charge or discarded common phase. Define prescribed offsets and forcing by

    \[ \begin{split} h(u)&=a k s(u/d),\\ z(u)&=\frac{4a}{\gamma d}v s'(u/d),\\ f(u)&=\frac{4a}{\gamma d^2}v s''(u/d) +\frac{a}{\gamma d}k s'(u/d). \end{split} \tag{2} \]

    The primes on s denote differentiation of its dimensionless argument. Exactly h'=gamma*L5*z/4 and z'=-L5*z/4+f. Both z endpoints vanish; h starts at zero and ends at a*k. Every vector in (2) has zero sum. Bounded jumps of f when it is switched on or off are allowed; the states remain continuous. Finite bandwidth and tracking error have not been admitted.

    Equation (2) is an executable time function, independent of the response. It is not asserted to solve the nonlinear unforced model. For the actual solution define

    \[ y=x-Tz,\quad \eta=\theta-\theta_{\rm entry}-Th. \]

    The complete equations give, without linearizing the sine,

    \[ y'=-Ly/4+\gamma S(\theta)/4,\qquad \eta'=\gamma Ly/4,\qquad y(0)=x_{\rm entry},\ \eta(0)=0. \tag{3} \]

    The phase row is live; its background drift is part of the endpoint error. The supplied signal preserves both global means. Smooth finite-dimensional linear growth and bounded sine forcing give a unique solution on the whole finite interval. No numerical integration is needed for the bounds below.

    The earlier collective construction also used a uniform input and a same-input comparison, but consumed initial collective observations on a different support. Here (2) consumes none of those observations; its coefficients and source bounds are derived on C5. This is detached continuous-law analysis, not a registered hybrid operator or a change to the native relational runtime.

    2. Actual source, residual state and identity¶

    Rebuild isolated recovery from its primitive allowances and clock, then the outer source from the recovered radius. One equal recovery bound can cover the five independent sources without equating their actual states. Retain both capture branches, acquisition identity, complete law and attachment/storage premises. The outer theorem supplies full Euclidean departures R,Z from its carrier and nominal form bound M*. These cover the outer-zero control as well.

    With g=gamma_upper, the initial unforced prefix admits

    \[ M_0=M_*+R+g h_0, \quad A_0,B_0=\mathcal T(R,Z,2g h_0), \tag{4} \]

    where the shared hidden-transport function is

    \[ \mathcal T(A,B,q)=\left(\frac{A+qB}{1-q^2}, \frac{B+qA}{1-q^2}\right),\qquad q<1. \tag{5} \]

    Its proof belongs to the source comparison. Let A be an upper bound on the actual principal mean-phase gaps at pulse entry: the largest nominal outer-one gap plus 2Z+4g*M0*h0 also bounds the outer-zero control. Prefix internal gaps use B0 and external gaps retain the same mean and fine errors. This does not select a nominal state in place of an acquired one.

    The maximum degree is four, ||L||_infinity<=8, and |S_i|<=4. Heat contraction applied to (3) yields

    \[ \|y(u)\|_\infty\le M=M_0+gd,\quad \|\eta(u)\|_\infty\le D=2gMd. \tag{6} \]

    Since both z endpoints vanish, the endpoint bounds are

    \[ \|x(d)\|_\infty\le M,\quad \|x(d)-x(0)\|_\infty\le (2M+g)d,\quad \|\theta(d)-\theta(0)-Ta k\|_\infty\le D. \tag{7} \]

    No small form bound is claimed during the pulse: z can be large. For the within-child orthogonal projection Q, QTf=0, Q commutes with L, and QS(psi+T*Phi)=0. Consequently (5) still transports the actual hidden form and phase norms under the input, with

    \[ A_h,B_h=\mathcal T(A_0,B_0,2gd). \tag{8} \]

    Thus all internal gaps lie between 2*pi/9-2Bh and 2*pi/9+2Bh. The strict collar margin is pi/2-2Bh. An external fine gap stays at magnitude at most A+|a|+2B0+2D. Strictly positive internal gaps below pi/2 and external magnitudes below pi/2 preserve every declared cycle winding throughout the pulse. Winding retention is a finite identity result, not all-future maintenance or invariance of the original observation prior.

    There is a sharper comparison with the unforced continuation of the same actual entry state. It is a proof device, not a measured branch required by the actuator. Subtract that continuation shifted by (Tz,Th) from the actual forced solution. A child-common phase displacement changes only two external edges per slot, so its sine-current increment has infinity norm at most 2|a|s. Using integral_0^1 s=1/2, the sine Lipschitz bound 8 and the same domain 2gd<1,

    \[ E_x\le\frac{g|a|d}{4(1-(2gd)^2)},\qquad E_\theta\le2gd E_x. \tag{9} \]

    At release this compares with J_a(F_d(state_entry)): first the real unforced elapsed duration, then the phase translation. The order matters. Neither (7) nor (9) identifies the endpoint with the old instantaneous event or admits the original observation inverse.

    3. Full storage, supplied work and unavoidable cost¶

    The unchanged full storage is

    \[ H=\tfrac12x^TLx+\sum_{e=1}^{90}(1-\cos\delta_e), \quad H'=-\tfrac14\|Lx\|_2^2+(Lx)^TTf. \tag{10} \]

    Define W=integral (Lx)^T Tf, Wabs=integral |(Lx)^T Tf|, Wplus=integral max(0,(Lx)^T Tf) and Loss=integral ||Lx||^2/4. Then H(d)-H(0)=W-Loss and Wplus<=Wabs. None is paid from signed hidden storage or the old jump-work estimate. The exact uniform port identity (Lx)^T Tf=9(L5 X)^T f remains valid off the carrier; independent physical calibration is still required to assign amperes, seconds or joules.

    For ell=gamma_lower>0, the cubic extrema give

    \[ Z_m=\frac{12|a|}{5\ell d},\quad J_z=\frac{24|a|}{5\ell d},\quad F_m=\frac{48|a|}{5\ell d^2}+\frac{6|a|}{5\ell d},\quad J=J_z+8M. \tag{11} \]

    These bound respectively ||z||infinity, ||L5 z||infinity, ||f||infinity and ||Lx||infinity. Counting all nodes and edges gives

    \[ \sup H\le180(Z_m+M)^2+180,\quad Loss\le45dJ^2/4,\quad W_{abs}\le45dJF_m. \tag{12} \]

    The last bound also bounds the sum of positive supplied work over the individual channels: it was obtained by summing absolute node powers before integration, so it does not require cancellation between supplying and absorbing channels.

    Finite upper bounds alone could conceal a costly construction. The phase row also forces a lower cost for the actual collective increment Delta Phi=a*k+e, with ||e||infinity<=D. Orthogonal decomposition gives

    \[ Loss\ge\frac{36}{\gamma^2d}\|\Delta\Phi\|_2^2,\qquad \sup\tfrac12x^TLx\ge \frac{72}{\gamma^2d^2}\Delta\Phi^TL_5^+\Delta\Phi. \tag{13} \]

    These are the C5 electrical pulse inequalities in structural units, with the actual lift and all fine storage retained. Put v=max(0,|a|-2D). The marked-minus-adjacent increment has magnitude at least v; ||e0-e1||^2=2 and (e0-e1)^T L5 (e0-e1)=6. Therefore

    \[ Loss\ge18v^2/(g^2d),\qquad \sup\tfrac12x^TLx\ge12v^2/(g^2d^2). \tag{14} \]

    They are necessary dissipative and peak-storage costs, not independently measured energies or an optimized input. Net work additionally depends on the initial storage through (10); in particular Wplus>=max(0,Loss_lower-H_entry_upper) if the actual entry storage upper bound is retained. The tiny supplied gamma makes a rapid phase translation require a large transient form; an instantaneous jump's small storage difference cannot account for this continuous cost.

    4. Instrument and evidence boundary¶

    The pulse and admission owner admits represented-real primitives before arithmetic, evaluates the neutral waveform and bounds the full source. It reuses the original recovery, outer-acquisition and hidden-transport owners without changing frozen producers. The single-assessment driver uses the shared exclusive source/configuration receipt lifecycle. The independent controls exercise algebra, full balances, invalid domains and synthetic source failures; they do not evaluate the reserved source decision.

    An externally prescribed input and a derived collective interaction are different claims. A future autonomous actuator would require its own TNFR state, preparation, coupling and joint energy balance; (2) derives none of those. Likewise this conditional finite intervention supplies no physical identification of electric charge or fundamental matter.

    The execution plan owns continuation. A complete finite observation protocol must retain the elapsed intervals, actual release forms, cumulative work and inference errors. Reusing the frozen instantaneous word or its verdict would omit the very state evolution established here.
