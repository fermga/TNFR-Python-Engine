    Phase-nearest UM contact and joint identity maintenance¶

    This comparison changes one existing UM policy parameter in the actual organization-contact protocol: UM_CANDIDATE_COUNT=3 replaces its unrestricted value 0. The source family, ordered word, remaining parameters, actual generated weights, continuous law and structural clock retain their declared premises. The earlier unrestricted-support results remain valid for their own model.

    1. Frozen comparison protocol¶

    Before evaluating the actual word or its reserved static controls, freeze the following comparison. Reuse the complete source and operator settings in the original protocol, including independently perturbed doubled-C5 components A and B, their common prepared form mean and phase origin, z=1/512, the strict full-state source balls, the actual target pair (0,1) and the ordered word emission,coupling,silence. Change only UM_CANDIDATE_COUNT to 3; retain proximity selection and the complete candidate inventory. No receiver action, new operator, controller, input or continuous loss is introduced. The actual event remains supplied.

    The prospective support prediction is the old forty edges plus five: the internal A0 pair edge and all four A0-to-B0 edges. Every generated conductance must retain its actual value and endpoint association. Admit all actual operator preconditions and the full weighted continuation, including changed degree normalization and capacity. Do not substitute a pair-constant dynamics for generic boundary conductances.

    The prospective common identity uses one twenty-node arithmetic projection P=I-11^T/20, the original common-aligned twists theta_*, and

    \[ Z^2=\|Px\|^2+\|P(\theta-\theta_*)\|^2<r^2, \qquad r=1/16. \]

    The phases in this expression are declared continuous lifts of the original twists. One common origin is removed; the relative A/B form and phase origins remain observable identity coordinates. The target is maintenance of this identity for the entire unchanged source family under the fixed post-word law, not just existence or linear stability of a symmetric reference. Establish a common stationary reference, exact balances and a strict energy barrier, or record precisely which obligation fails. Do not enlarge the identity radius, shrink the source or retune the word after examining a response.

    Reuse the original split and unsplit nominal controls, source representation, seed, timestamps, execution order and independent 100-digit static oracle with ordinary response allowance 2^-46. Add independent exact algebraic controls for support, mean weights, stationarity, coercivity, full-source energy bounds and event-work accounting. Analytical bounds apply to the exact family; represented controls re-admit their own input defects. No continuous trajectory, time sweep, topology search or physical calibration is part of this gate. No frozen response from the unrestricted-support study is regenerated or reinterpreted as a result of this comparison.

    2. Actual candidate selection and the five added edges¶

    Write alpha=2*pi/5 and retain b=h=1/1024=z/2, where b is the AL form increment and h the UM phase push. Before UM, each selected A member has its four original A neighbors. The shared candidate reader excludes the target and existing neighbors, applies the snapshot U3 phase gate, then orders admitted missing neighbors by absolute circular phase gap, using the retained node rank only to break ties.

    The selected member's partner and the two B0 members have snapshot gaps at most 2*z. Every member of B1 or B4 instead has gap at least alpha-2*z>2*z; the distance-two A and B pairs fail the unchanged pi/2 phase gate. Thus the three nearest candidates are exactly the partner and both B0 members for every source in the unchanged family. Their internal ranking can vary without changing the selected set. The two target proposals create one common internal edge and four cross edges, not duplicate undirected edges. No external candidate list or new neighbor-selector law is supplied.

    The selected candidate count changes only these link proposals. The existing-neighbor consensus, four bidirectional neighbor phase writes, capacity blend, selected stored-pressure relief and SHA reset are still the actual primary map. They read the immutable old support, not prospective new edges. B node state and execution history do not reset during this word. All inherited live form/capacity, circular phase, grammar and associated-history preconditions retain their original strict margins.

    The final compatibility score uses the actual post-AL forms and merged phases, with homogeneous Si=1/2 as before. A new cross gap is below 2*z+3*h*z<3*z, its form difference is below 2*z+b=5*z/2, and both forms exceed 1/4. The shared score therefore satisfies

    \[ W_{ij}>1-\frac{3z}{2\pi}-\frac{5z}{4}>\frac34. \]

    The internal edge retains its previous bound omega>1-sqrt(2)*z>3/4. Every selected candidate therefore passes the final phase and score gates. Every generated weight lies in (3/4,1] and remains attached to its actual endpoints. New edges have the actual weight attribute; no length or unit conductance is inserted by the analysis.

    The resulting connected support has 45 edges: forty retained unit edges, the internal A0 edge, and the complete four-edge A0--B0 block. Selected A0 members have bare degree seven, B0 members have degree six, and all other nodes have degree four. Their post-word capacities are respectively 1/2,1,1. Consequently the actual mean weights are

    \[ \rho_i=\frac{d_i}{\nu_i}= \begin{cases} 14,&i\in A_0,\\ 6,&i\in B_0,\\ 4,&\text{otherwise}, \end{cases} \qquad \sum_{i\in A}\rho_i=60,\quad \sum_{i\in B}\rho_i=44,\quad \sum_i\rho_i=104. \]

    The old unrestricted fan's degree normalization and mean weights do not transfer to this graph. For the nominal post-word forms the weighted form mean is m+7*b/26; arbitrary admitted deviations retain their own weighted correlations rather than being replaced by that nominal value.

    3. Complete law, reference and joint identity¶

    Keep the conservative weighted-form/bare-phase completion with e=0,w=beta=1, zero forcing, held actual support and conductances, held post-word capacities and the original structural clock:

    \[ S_i=\sum_{j\sim i}\sin(\theta_j-\theta_i),\quad q=L_Wx, \quad M=\operatorname{diag}\frac{\nu_i}{\pi d_i},\qquad \dot x=MS,\quad\dot\theta=Mq. \]

    The independently specified pressure is p_i=S_i/(pi*d_i), evaluated on the new support. It is distinct from the stored pressure relieved by UM on the old support. The actual storage and conserved means are

    \[ H=\frac12x^TL_Wx+\sum_{\{i,j\}\in U}(1-\cos(\theta_j-\theta_i)), \qquad \dot H=q^TMS-S^TMq=0, \]
    \[ \frac d{dt}(\rho^Tx)=0,\qquad \frac d{dt}(\rho^T\theta)=0. \]

    Use the common-aligned reference x_*=m*1 and theta_*(A_(k,s))=theta_*(B_(k,s))=k*alpha, on the original lifts. Every old fine current cancels at that twist. All five new edges have zero reference form and phase differences. The reference is therefore stationary under the complete actual weighted law, with

    \[ H_*=40(1-\cos\alpha). \]

    Either common origin can be translated to match a particular source's conserved weighted mean, without changing H or the identity. The relative geometry uses one twenty-node arithmetic projection,

    \[ P=I-\frac{11^T}{20},\qquad Z^2=\|Px\|^2+\|P(\theta-\theta_*)\|^2. \]

    If eta=theta-theta_*, this identity separates exactly as

    \[ Z^2=Z_A^2+Z_B^2+ 5\left[(\overline x_A-\overline x_B)^2+ (\overline\eta_A-\overline\eta_B)^2\right]. \]

    Here Z_A,Z_B are the earlier ten-node centered constituent norms. Thus the joint identity retains relative form and phase origins rather than quotienting them away independently. A bound on Z protects both constituent geometries and their relative registration. The arithmetic means used in this observation are not asserted to be conserved.

    The whole reference has zero first circular phase resultant: sum_i exp(i*theta_*,i)=4*sum_(k=0)^4 exp(i*k*alpha)=0. There is no defined global resultant angle there. The arithmetic lift projection is the declared geometric observation; it is not a new primitive collective phase or a circular-mean reconstruction.

    Generic actual cross conductances still require the member-to-boundary association identified by the earlier contact result. The proof below works on the complete fine state. Equal reference phases or equal bare neighbor counts do not turn the actual weighted evolution into a closed pair-symmetric model.

    4. A full relative spectral bound for comparison matrices¶

    Let L_0 be the unit Laplacian of the two disjoint original doubled C5 graphs, and N the unit Laplacian of the five added edges. The actual bare Laplacian is L_U=L_0+N. Since all actual edge weights exceed 3/4,

    \[ L_W\succeq\frac34 L_U. \]

    This is a quadratic-form comparison, not a replacement of W in the law. To lower-bound lambda_2(L_U), first omit the internal A0 edge from a unit comparison graph. The remaining graph is the two-member doubling of a ten-vertex base graph consisting of two C5 cycles joined by the single A0--B0 base edge.

    Only this unit comparison matrix is decomposed by its symmetries. Fine pair-antisymmetric directions have eigenvalues four or six. On pair-common directions its Laplacian acts as twice the base Laplacian. Exchanging the two identical base cycles splits that base matrix into the symmetric block L_C5 and the antisymmetric block L_C5+2*e_0*e_0^T. The first block has one common zero mode and its next eigenvalue is (5-sqrt(5))/2>1/5.

    For the second block, five times its shift by I/5, in cyclic node order, is the exact matrix

    \[ 5\left(L_{C5}+2e_0e_0^T-\frac15I\right)= \begin{pmatrix} 19&-5&0&0&-5\\ -5&9&-5&0&0\\ 0&-5&9&-5&0\\ 0&0&-5&9&-5\\ -5&0&0&-5&9 \end{pmatrix}. \]

    Its leading principal minors are 19,146,839,3901,909, all positive. Sylvester's criterion gives lambda_min(L_C5+2*e_0*e_0^T)>1/5. Restoring the omitted internal edge adds a nonnegative quadratic form. Consequently

    \[ \boxed{\lambda_2(L_U)>\frac25.} \]

    The argument controls every relative fine direction, including independent member splits and the relative A/B origin. It imposes no symmetry on the actual W, source perturbations or future trajectory.

    For the source upper bound below, two further simple facts suffice: lambda_max(L_0)<=8 from its degree-four support, and lambda_max(N)=4. The latter follows by restricting to the four A0/B0 members: N is the Laplacian of K4 with the B0 internal edge removed, with eigenvalues 0,2,4,4; the other sixteen directions are zero.

    5. Lower storage barrier on the unchanged joint radius¶

    Take the frozen r=1/16. On Z<=r, any edge phase error from the reference is at most sqrt(2)*r. Every old principal reference gap has magnitude alpha and every added reference gap is zero. The old gaps remain acute, and their cosines obey

    \[ \cos(\alpha+\sqrt2r) \ge\cos\alpha-\sqrt2r >\frac3{10}-\frac3{32} =\frac{33}{160}>\frac15. \]

    Here cos(alpha)=(sqrt(5)-1)/4>3/10, sqrt(2)<3/2, and alpha+sqrt(2)*r<pi/2. The added-edge cosines exceed the same lower bound. Thus the phase Hessian throughout this whole radius obeys L_(cos delta) >= L_U/5. The actual form block also dominates L_U/5 because L_W >= 3*L_U/4.

    Translate the common origins away and Taylor-expand about the stationary reference along the straight centered-state segment, which remains in the radius. The full Hessian comparison and Section 4 give

    \[ \boxed{H-H_*\ge\frac{Z^2}{25}\qquad (Z\le r).} \]

    At the radius boundary this requires

    \[ H-H_*\ge\frac{r^2}{25}=\frac1{6400}. \]

    The bound uses both complete rows and all fine directions. The same reference is a strict relative-storage minimum, but its existence alone would not establish retention of the actual post-word source.

    6. Upper storage bound for the entire actual post-word family¶

    Let v_0=(xi,eta) be the initial twenty-node deviation from the common reference, before the supplied word. The independent constituent source balls give

    \[ \|v_0\|^2<2z^2. \]

    Let a=b*1_(A0) be the actual AL form increment and let d be the actual UM phase increment, with all other phase entries zero. The previously admitted consensus bounds give two selected increments below 3*h*z in absolute value and four old-neighbor increments below 4*h/3. Consequently

    \[ \|d\|^2<\left(\frac{64}{9}+18z^2\right)h^2<9h^2. \]

    B phases are unchanged; SHA changes capacity only. The full post-word deviation is exactly v_1=v_0+(a,d), with its retained phase lifts. Centering is a contraction, so Z_1<sqrt(2)*z+sqrt(11)*h<7*h<r. This admits the initial state to the frozen radius before using an energy certificate.

    A sharper storage estimate keeps the different form and phase curvatures. The full post-word phase-error norm is below sqrt(2)*z+3*h<6*h. Therefore every old-edge error is below 6*sqrt(2)*h<1/64. Along the straight segment from the reference to this actual endpoint, the old-edge cosines are at most

    \[ \cos\alpha+\frac1{64} <\frac5{16}+\frac1{64}=\frac{21}{64}<\frac13. \]

    Every new-edge cosine is at most one. Actual conductances are at most one. Define the positive-semidefinite comparison form

    \[ \mathcal B=\operatorname{diag} \left(L_0+N,\frac13L_0+N\right),\qquad \mathcal Q(v)=\frac12v^T\mathcal Bv. \]

    Taylor's formula at the stationary reference then yields H_post-H_*<=Qcal(v_1). The use of 1/3 is local to this small source segment; it is not asserted as an upper cosine bound throughout the larger radius-r identity.

    Since lambda_max(B)<=12, the original source contribution is

    \[ \mathcal Q(v_0)<12z^2=48h^2. \]

    The AL increment has eight old boundary edges and four new cross edges; the internal edge has zero increment difference. Its exact comparison form cost is therefore

    \[ \frac12a^T(L_0+N)a=6b^2=6h^2. \]

    For the old phase part, the norm bound above gives d^T*L_0*d/6<12*h^2. On the five new edges only the two selected phase increments contribute, each of magnitude below 3*h*z. If those increments are d_+,d_-, then

    \[ d^TNd=(d_+-d_-)^2+2d_+^2+2d_-^2 \le4(d_+^2+d_-^2)<72h^2z^2. \]

    Hence the complete reset comparison cost satisfies

    \[ \mathcal Q(a,d)<(18+36z^2)h^2<19h^2. \]

    The square root of a nonnegative quadratic form is a seminorm, so its triangle inequality retains the worst allowed correlation between the initial source and the reset:

    \[ \boxed{ H_{\rm post}-H_*\le\mathcal Q(v_1) <(\sqrt{48}+\sqrt{19})^2h^2 <128h^2=\frac1{8192}<\frac1{6400}. } \]

    For the last strict algebraic inequality, (sqrt(48)+sqrt(19))^2=67+2*sqrt(912)<128, since 4*912<61^2. No source correlation, selected-member asymmetry or actual boundary conductance has been averaged away. The bound covers the entire original source family with the frozen radius and word constants.

    7. Joint identity is maintained for all structural time¶

    The actual completed source has Z_1<r and storage excess below 1/8192, while any first radius exit would require storage excess at least 1/6400. The complete fixed-support flow conserves H, so such a first exit is impossible. Therefore

    \[ \boxed{Z(t)<\frac1{16}\quad\text{for every }t\ge0} \]

    for every exact source in the frozen family, with time measured after the completed word in the original structural clock. All old and new fine edges remain acute, both original constituent winding neighborhoods and nonantipodal member charts persist, and the relative form and phase origins remain inside the joint bound. A common translation was removed; relative A/B motion was retained rather than assumed absent.

    The conserved actual weighted means control the remaining common lifted coordinates. Together with the bounded relative state they prevent finite escape. Equivalently, the complete finite-dimensional field has bounded form rates and phase rates linear in the form range, giving global smooth continuation. The barrier is thus an all-time result, not a bound that silently stops at an unproved existence horizon.

    This is conservative geometric maintenance. It does not imply convergence to the stationary reference, a period, asymptotic attraction, synchronization at a specified time, or acquisition of an initially absent organization. The two constituent organizations and their near-common registration were prepared, and their connection was supplied by the declared word.
