## 4. Work and all-time identity of the postword continuous law

Let `H=x^T L_f x/2+V(theta)` and let `H_*` be its winding-one target value.

eigenvalue four. The target is a phase critical point. Along the straight
segment of declared phase deviations, the potential Hessian is
`B diag(cos(edge difference)) B^T <= L_f`. Taylor's integral remainder
therefore gives the uniform **upper** bound

\[
H-H_*\le\frac{\lambda_{\max}(L_f)}2 Z_f^2<4z^2.
\]

No unmeasured source energy ceiling is needed in addition to the stated
norm domain. For the whole-pair increment `h=b*1_pair`, the existing exact

\[
\Delta H=(L_fx)^T h+\tfrac12h^TL_fh
=4b\sum_{\ell\sim k}(X_k-X_\ell)+4b^2.

It is invariant under the pair swaps and is thus a well-defined collective

\[
Z_f^+<\frac1{512}+\frac2{1024}=\frac1{256}<r,
\qquad
H^+-H_*<4z^2+7bz+4b^2=\frac{17}{524288}.

\[
\kappa_f=\frac{5-\sqrt5}{2}

\[

## 5. Exact real domain, represented execution and remaining scope

The open-domain theorem concerns exact real state. Binary64 values are a
discrete set; sampling them does not prove openness. Conversely, a source
already materialized in binary64 and independently certified inside the
stated domain has particularly simple primary arithmetic:

1. Every source form and its boosted result lie in the same binade
   `[1/4,1/2)`, whose spacing is `2^-54`. The boost `2^-10` is an integer
   multiple of this spacing. The exact sum is representable within the
   same binade, so round-to-nearest addition introduces no AL defect.
2. Halving a normal capacity in `(1/2,2)` is exactly representable and
   remains normal in `(1/4,1)`. SHA cannot underflow on this domain.

Thus this represented subdomain realizes the same primary resets, without
pretending every requested boost or attenuation is exact elsewhere.
Admission concerns the materialized source; converting an arbitrary
rational or decimal input may change it. Trigonometric pair coordinates,
source norm relative to the irrational twist, storage and barrier verdicts
retain independently checked enclosures. Equal floating summaries do not
authenticate a joint physical/history orbit. Outside the binade domain use
actual represented increments and the general reset identity instead.
