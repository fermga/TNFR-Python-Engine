    9. A finite-amplitude internal pulse with persistent collective identity¶

    Keep the complete double-replica C5, the same conservative sine law e=0,w,beta>0, no inputs or events, and now a common strictly positive held capacity nu at all ten fine nodes. This additional capacity premise is needed for the invariant family below; paired equality alone was enough for sections 7 and 8.

    9.1. The exact invariant preparation¶

    Let alpha=2*pi/5 and c_alpha=cos(alpha)>0. Supply the exact collective state

    \[ X_i=\bar X,\qquad \Theta_i=\bar\Theta+i\alpha\pmod{2\pi}, \qquad i=0,\ldots,4, \]

    and identical signed internal coordinates

    \[ u_i=u,\qquad \delta_i=\delta \]

    for all five pairs. The constants bar X and bar Theta specify the common form and phase origins. The target angles are exact turns i/5; replacing them by rounded radian values and accepting a small balance residual would not establish this invariant family.

    At each coarse node the two phase differences are +alpha and -alpha modulo 2*pi. Their sine currents cancel, while their cosines add. The exact retained-coordinate rows of section 7 therefore give

    \[ \dot X_i=0,\qquad \dot\Theta_i=0,\qquad \dot u_i=-a\nu c_\alpha\cos\delta\sin\delta,\qquad \dot\delta_i=b\nu u. \]

    The internal derivatives are identical at all five pairs, so smooth uniqueness proves that the full preparation remains in this family. Its complete nonlinear evolution reduces exactly to

    \[ \boxed{\quad \dot u=-a\nu c_\alpha\cos\delta\sin\delta,\qquad \dot\delta=b\nu u . \quad} \]

    The ten constituent coordinates remain x_i,plus=bar X+u, x_i,minus=bar X-u and theta_i,plus/minus=bar Theta+i*alpha+/-delta. Static collective means do not mean that the constituents are stationary. Neither an external periodic input nor an additional oscillator equation has been installed: the two displayed rows are an invariant restriction of the already supplied complete law.

    9.2. Conserved amplitude and exact periods¶

    Because a=beta*b, the internal quantity

    \[ \boxed{\quad H=u^2+\beta c_\alpha\sin^2\delta\quad} \]

    is conserved:

    \[ \dot H =-2a\nu c_\alpha u\cos\delta\sin\delta +2\beta b\nu c_\alpha u\sin\delta\cos\delta=0. \]

    It is part of the existing full storage, not a second independently postulated energy. Indeed, section 7 gives exactly

    \[ E_f=4E_{*,C5}+20H,\qquad E_{*,C5}=5\beta(1-c_\alpha). \]

    Define

    \[ \Omega=\nu\sqrt{ab c_\alpha} =\frac{w\nu\sqrt{c_\alpha}}{\pi\sqrt\beta}, \qquad m=\frac{H}{\beta c_\alpha}. \]

    For 0<m<1 and a preparation in |delta|<pi/2, the level set is a nonstationary closed curve around (u,delta)=(0,0). Its amplitudes are

    \[ |\delta|_{\max}=\arcsin\sqrt m<\pi/2,\qquad |u|_{\max}=\sqrt H . \]

    The pair chart consequently remains valid for the entire motion. Set eta=2*delta; differentiating the actual phase row gives

    \[ \ddot\eta+\Omega^2\sin\eta=0 . \]

    This pendulum equation is a derived form of the retained internal system. The conserved level already proves that the orbit is a libration, and direct quadrature gives its period. With the elliptic parameter m, define

    \[ K(m)=\int_0^{\pi/2}\frac{d\psi}{\sqrt{1-m\sin^2\psi}}. \]

    Since sin(delta)=sqrt(m)*sin(psi) on a quarter orbit,

    \[ \boxed{\quad T_{\rm labeled}=\frac{4}{\Omega}K(m) =\frac{4\pi\sqrt\beta}{w\nu\sqrt{c_\alpha}}K(m). \quad} \]

    This is the period of the full labeled circular fine state, not merely of a tangent mode. On a nonzero libration the half-period map is (u,delta)->(-u,-delta). It swaps the two members of every pair while leaving X,Theta fixed. The unordered state of section 8 therefore has the smaller fundamental period

    \[ \boxed{\quad T_{\rm unordered}=T_{\rm labeled}/2.\quad} \]

    To see why it is the fundamental unordered period, the nonstationary closed energy curve is traversed once in T_labeled and each unordered state has exactly two fine lifts on it. Those lifts are related by the half-period swap; there is no fixed lift on a positive-energy orbit. The invariants R,U,Q change along this orbit, so the unordered state is not stationary despite the fixed collective means.

    The same elementary integral bounds used for the two-node pulse give

    \[ T_0=\frac{2\pi}{\Omega},\qquad T_0<T_{\rm labeled}\le\frac{T_0}{\sqrt{1-m}}, \]

    and half of each bound for the unordered period. The strictly positive amplitude is essential: T_0 is the small-amplitude limiting period, not a period assigned to a stationary state. In particular, the pulse frequency depends on the supplied capacity, coefficients, geometry and amplitude. As m approaches one, K(m) diverges. This is a persistent periodic internal motion under a declared law, not a universal constant frequency.

    EPI as the projection of an internal circulation¶

    The existing pulse has an exact circular representation. This is a coordinate consequence of the two retained rows, not an additional oscillator, complex EPI variable or physical spatial rotation. With H>0, 0<m<1 and |delta|<pi/2 as above, put

    \[ A=u,\qquad B=\sqrt{\beta c_\alpha}\sin\delta,\qquad Z=A+iB . \]

    Both real coordinates have form units. The original conservation identity and the independently specified evolution rows give

    \[ A^2+B^2=H,\qquad \dot A=-\Omega\cos\delta\,B,\qquad \dot B=\Omega\cos\delta\,A,\qquad \boxed{\dot Z=i\Omega\cos\delta\,Z.} \]

    For example, the first row follows from a=beta*b and Omega=b*nu*sqrt(beta*c_alpha); the second follows by differentiating sin(delta) using delta_dot=b*nu*u. Thus the two-coordinate description follows from the full law rather than reconstructing a pressure from its measured response.

    The map (u,delta)->(A,B) is invertible on this pair chart, since its Jacobian determinant is sqrt(beta*c_alpha)*cos(delta)>0. A continuous angular lift psi=arg(Z) therefore obeys

    \[ \dot\psi=\Omega\cos\delta =\Omega\sqrt{1-m\sin^2\psi},\qquad 0<\Omega\sqrt{1-m}\le\dot\psi\le\Omega . \]

    The constituent forms are exactly

    \[ x_{i,+}=\bar X+\sqrt H\cos\psi,\qquad x_{i,-}=\bar X-\sqrt H\cos\psi . \]

    EPI is consequently a projection of circulation in this internal state plane. Its angular speed is generally variable in the declared structural clock. The primitive phase difference delta still librates; the internal angle psi is not that primitive phase. The angle is unavailable at H=0, where the state is stationary. Neither a rotation in physical space nor spin, a universal frequency or a laboratory time unit follows.

    This also gives an exact information-loss control. Choose 0<d<pi/2 and u_0 with 0<u_0^2+beta*c_alpha*sin(d)^2<beta*c_alpha. The two admitted preparations (u_0,+d) and (u_0,-d) have the same complete fine EPI snapshot, the same means and the same storage H, but

    \[ \dot u\big|_{+d}=-a\nu c_\alpha\cos d\sin d<0,\qquad \dot u\big|_{-d}=+a\nu c_\alpha\cos d\sin d>0 . \]

    Instantaneous EPI and storage alone therefore do not determine even the direction of its next change. The retained relative phase distinguishes these states. Observing only the collective mean hides the entire pulse; forgetting constituent labels instead changes the exact period to T/2 as already proved above. A visually repeated scalar trace alone neither identifies these internal coordinates nor proves periodicity of the full state.

    Common phase shifts leave every phase difference, and hence these form rows, unchanged. A time-dependent common phase rotation requires its own complete phase-law admission; it is not a source of this relative EPI pulse.

    There is already a broader maintenance result: Section 12 proves continuing internal turns on an open full-state family, with interaction-generated angular speeds and without a common period. It does not require the exact circles or common waveform of this special preparation. Its trapping, activity, almost-everywhere recurrence and no-capture boundaries remain distinct. In particular, that robust organization is compatible with the small-amplitude orbital instability of the synchronized waveform. This circular representation supplies no new stability or formation certificate and does not select the zero-loss law.

    9.3. Collective identity and the stronger fine-edge condition¶
