## 1. Full lifted cell, stationary target and storage

Write \(\alpha=2\pi/9\), \(\psi_j=\alpha(j-4)\), and
\(\theta_{c,j}=\psi_j+\delta_{c,j}\). On every oriented internal
cycle edge use the continuous angle

\[
 a_{c,j}=\alpha+\delta_{c,j+1}-\delta_{c,j},
 \qquad j+1\pmod9,
 \qquad \sum_j a_{c,j}=2\pi .
 \tag{1}
\]

This includes the fixed closing-edge turn. On a matching edge between
children \(c,b\) at slot \(j\), use
\(a_{cb,j}=\delta_{b,j}-\delta_{c,j}\). The declared cell is the
intersection of all 90 strict inequalities \(|a_e|<\pi/2\), on the
leaf

\[
 \sum_{c,j}x_{c,j}=0,\qquad
 \sum_{c,j}\delta_{c,j}=9/8.
 \tag{2}
\]

All 90 fine coordinates are retained; the two linear conservation laws
remove only their common origins. This affine phase cell is convex.
Its closure has \(|a_e|\le\pi/2\), and every boundary point has at
least one saturated edge. It is the closure of the open cell: mixing
with the strict target below approaches every closed-cell point from
the interior. The five marked cycle sums are fixed by (1), rather than
inferred from selected phase snapshots.

For the actual 90-edge Laplacian and sine current, the complete law is

\[
 x_\tau=\tfrac14[-Lx+\gamma S(\theta)],\qquad
 \theta_\tau=\tfrac\gamma4Lx,
 \qquad \gamma=\frac1{1023\pi}.
 \tag{3}
\]

The coefficients remain uniform despite unequal node degrees. The first
row supplies the pressure at held unit capacity. Both rows, and the
storage derivative below, acquire the factor \(1023/1024\) in original
time \(t\); the phase row is not exempt from this clock change.

Set

\[
 V_9=9(1-\cos\alpha),\qquad
 H_*=5V_9,\qquad
 x^*=0,\qquad \theta^*_{c,j}=\psi_j+1/40.
 \tag{4}
\]

The two internal sine currents cancel at every fine node of this target;
all matching currents vanish separately. Thus \(S(\theta^*)=0\),
and both rows in (3) vanish. All internal angles are \(\alpha\),
all matching angles are zero, and (2) holds. This verifies a fine-state
equilibrium with five winding-one cycles, independent of carrier closure.

The storage and exact identities are inherited from the
[source-port balance](ORGANIZED_SOURCE_PORT.md#1-fine-law-normalization-and-attachment-storage):

\[
 H=\tfrac12x^TLx+\sum_e(1-\cos a_e),\qquad
 H_\tau=-\tfrac14\|Lx\|_2^2,\qquad
 (\mathbf1^Tx)_\tau=(\mathbf1^T\delta)_\tau=0.
 \tag{5}
\]

There is no later input or support event. The given vector field is
globally Lipschitz in finite real lifts, so every finite initial state
has a unique complete solution. The proof below concerns this supplied
continuous law, not a numerical time step or a native operator execution.

## 2. Every acute boundary face has a positive excess

The function \(f(a)=1-\cos a\) is convex on
\([-\pi/2,\pi/2]\). Jensen's inequality and each exact cycle sum in
(1) give \(\sum_j f(a_{c,j})\ge V_9\) on the whole closed cell.
The five cycle edge sets are disjoint. All other edge costs and the
form storage are nonnegative. In particular, \(H-H_*\ge0\).

If an internal edge reaches \(+\pi/2\), the other eight angles of
that cycle sum to \(3\pi/2\). Its cycle storage is therefore at least

\[
 b_9=1+8[1-\cos(3\pi/16)].
 \tag{6}
\]

If it reaches \(-\pi/2\), the remaining angles sum to \(5\pi/2\)
and its lower bound is \(1+8[1-\cos(5\pi/16)]>b_9\).
These arguments cover both signs of every one of the 45 internal edges.
No feasibility of an independently minimized face is required: a lower
bound valid on the larger cycle domain is valid on the actual full face.

If instead any of the 45 matching edges reaches either acute boundary,
that edge contributes one, while the five internal cycles still contribute
at least \(5V_9\). Other matching edges can only add storage. Consequently
all 180 signed faces satisfy

\[
 H-H_*\ge\beta_{\rm face},\qquad
 \beta_{\rm face}=\min\{b_9-V_9,1\},\qquad
 b_9-V_9=9\cos(2\pi/9)-8\cos(3\pi/16).
 \tag{7}
\]

The shared outward 128-bit rational trigonometric bounds give
\(6/25<b_9-V_9<1\), hence \(\beta_{\rm face}>6/25\).
For orientation, this excess is approximately \(0.24264308965044\).
The strict rational inequality, not the displayed decimal, is used below.
This is a complete boundary lower bound, not a claim that a full-support
boundary minimizer attains it. Additional cycle correlations may strengthen
it but are unnecessary for this source.

## 3. Re-admitting the actual reached family and its complete storage

Rebuild the
[attachment source](ORGANIZED_SOURCE_PORT.md#6-actual-donor-preparation-host-handoff-and-event-work)
from all ten primitive allowances. The host retains its reached state at
\(s=4\) and its moving reference

\[
 \begin{split}
 x^H_{g,a,j}&=\tfrac12[(2g-1)u+(2a-1)v]+r_{g,a,j},\\
 \delta^H_{g,a,j}&=\tfrac12[(2g-1)\Delta+(2a-1)B]+z_{g,a,j},\\
 |v|&\le\bar\gamma A\,\overline B,\qquad
 |u|\le\bar\gamma A\,\overline\Delta,\qquad
 A=1+2\bar\eta,\\
 0<B&\le\overline B<1/64,\qquad
 0<\Delta\le\overline\Delta<1/640,\qquad
 \|r\|_2\le R<10^{-60},\quad \|z\|_2\le Z<10^{-60}.
 \end{split}\tag{8}
\]

Here \(\bar\gamma\) and \(\bar\eta\) are the outward upper bounds on
\(\gamma\) and \(\gamma^2\), respectively, using the shared interval
square for the latter. The overlined gaps are the freshly rebuilt host entry bounds.
The original host proof supplies \(R,Z\) for the four independent
recovered children. They bound all actual fine deviations; (8) does
not set them to zero or require them to match between children.

The independent donor retains
\(x^D=r_D\), \(\delta^D=1/8+z_D\), with separate exact zero
means of \(r_D,z_D\) and
\(\|r_D\|_2,\|z_D\|_2\le q=10^{-70}\). Its quarter-factor
isolated dynamics is the original half-factor dynamics at half speed
in both rows. Thus its formation time 200 and recovery dwell
\(2\cdot10^{13}\) are the already declared transformed durations,
not a new simulation or an additional waiting period. All five original
families, and their 80 relative initial directions, remain in this source.

Initial cell membership follows on every edge: internal angles lie in
\([\alpha-2\max(Z,q),\alpha+2\max(Z,q)]\subset(0,\pi/2)\);
host matching angles have absolute value at most
\(\max(\overline B,\overline\Delta)+2Z<\pi/2\); donor-contact
angles satisfy the strictly positive acute bounds from the fresh attachment.
The event changes no coordinate, so this is the actual post-event state.

For the host's four internal cycles the reference forms are constant
and the reference phase gradients cancel at each node. The block cycle
Laplacian has norm at most four. The internal form storage and the
Taylor remainder of the internal phase storage are therefore at most
\(2R^2\) and \(2Z^2\), respectively. This phase bound uses cancellation
of the whole cycle's first variation, rather than treating each edge
as individually stationary.

There are 18 host matching edges with reference gap \(B\), and 18
with reference gap \(\Delta\). On each such edge the actual form gap
has magnitude at most \(\bar\gamma A d+2R\), and its phase gap
at most \(d+2Z\), for the corresponding upper bound \(d\).
Using \(1-\cos a\le a^2/2\) yields the source-inclusive host excess

\[
 \begin{split}
 H_H-4V_9&\le U_H,\\
 U_H&=2(R^2+Z^2)+9\sum_{d\in\{\overline B,\overline\Delta\}}
       [(\bar\gamma A d+2R)^2+(d+2Z)^2].
 \end{split}\tag{9}
\]

This estimate needs no childwise residual mean conservation after host
assembly. Its nominal part is
\(9[1+(\bar\gamma A)^2](\overline B^2+\overline\Delta^2)\);
all remaining nonnegative cross terms and squares are retained exactly,
including terms below the trigonometric interval grid. The donor excess
is at most \(4q^2\) by the same internal-cycle calculation.

Write \(b=(\overline B+\overline\Delta)/2\), and retain the actual
new-edge work upper bound

\[
 \begin{split}
 W_+&=\tfrac92\{[\bar\gamma A b+R+q]^2+
                         [1/8+b+Z+q]^2\},\\
 0\le H(0)-H_*&\le U_+:=U_H+4q^2+W_+<1/10.
 \end{split}\tag{10}
\]

The last strict inequality already follows with the coarse rational
bounds in (8), \(\bar\gamma<1/3000\),
\(\bar\eta<1/9000000\), and \(q=10^{-70}\):
the resulting upper bound is less than \(0.082533\). The calculator
uses the actual outward host entry bounds, preserving the stronger
source association. An old upper bound before host acquisition is not
substituted for (9).

Equation (10) adds the donor's complete reached storage and the actual
contact work to the reached host storage. The separately admitted positive
work \(1/16<H_B<1/10\), five primitive preparation costs, original host
assembly work and prior dissipation keep their
[source owner's accounting](ORGANIZED_SOURCE_PORT.md#6-actual-donor-preparation-host-handoff-and-event-work).
Past loss funds none of these supplied events. Neither (9) nor (10)
changes a source coordinate or introduces a work reservoir.

## 4. Permanent winding retention and full-state convergence

Combining (7) and (10) gives a strict whole-source separation:

\[
 \beta_{\rm face}-U_+>\frac6{25}-\frac1{10}=\frac7{50}>0.
 \tag{11}
\]

Suppose a member of the admitted family first reached the cell boundary.
By (5), its storage could not exceed its initial value, while every
boundary face requires at least \(H_*+\beta_{\rm face}\). This
contradicts (10)--(11). The strict gap also excludes accumulation at the
boundary. Hence every source member remains in the acute fixed-branch
cell for all elapsed post-attachment \(\tau\ge0\), and all five
marked windings remain exactly one.

For convergence, the support is connected. On the fixed zero-form-mean
leaf, bounded \(x^TLx\) bounds the form coordinates. Bounded fixed-branch
edge angles and the fixed lifted mean similarly bound all phase offsets.
The trapped storage sublevel is thus compact and separated from the phase
boundary. On its interior the phase Hessian is the weighted graph Laplacian
with edge weights \(\cos a_e>0\). It is positive definite on the
fixed-mean tangent space. Strict convexity therefore makes the stationary
phase configuration (4) the only critical point in this cell and mean leaf.

In the zero-loss set of (5), \(Lx=0\); the form mean then forces
\(x=0\), and the phase row vanishes. To remain in that set, the form
row further requires \(S(\theta)=0\). Its largest invariant subset
is consequently the single equilibrium (4). The invariance principle on
the compact trapped sublevel proves, for every independently perturbed
member of the original prepared family,

\[
 x_{c,j}(\tau)\longrightarrow0,\qquad
 \theta_{c,j}(\tau)\longrightarrow\psi_j+1/40
 \quad(\tau\longrightarrow\infty).
 \tag{12}
\]

The common phase origin follows from the retained raw total \(9/8\)
divided by 45. The result concerns every fine coordinate; it is not an
inference from five observed means or the ten-coordinate restriction.
It reuses the method of the
[acute-sector capture theorem](SINE_PATTERN_DYNAMICS.md#sine-target-free-sector-capture)
with the present support, uniform quarter mobility, source and all edge faces
proved explicitly. No global settling time or finite readout is supplied.

## 5. A collective registration distinguished by the control

Define the lifted relative-registration observation

\[
 \rho(\tau)=\frac19\sum_{j=0}^8\delta_{4,j}(\tau)
       -\frac1{36}\sum_{c=0}^3\sum_{j=0}^8\delta_{c,j}(\tau).
 \tag{13}
\]

It is invariant under a common change of phase origin and retains the
specified lifts. At the actual initial source, the host mean is zero and
the donor mean is \(1/8\), so \(\rho(0)=1/8\) for the whole family.
For the connected law, (12) gives \(\rho(\tau)\to0\).

In the disconnected control only the nine donor contacts are omitted.
Both complete rows keep their uniform quarter factor, the same structural
clock, held capacities and every reached coordinate. Edge cancellation
now separately conserves each component's form sum and lifted phase sum.
The host mean is therefore zero forever, the donor mean is \(1/8\)
forever, and

\[
 \rho_{\rm disconnected}(\tau)=1/8\qquad(\tau\ge0).
 \tag{14}
\]

Thus this control cannot converge to any state with a common child phase
origin, including (4). No disconnected convergence theorem or finite
response calculation is needed for the obstruction. This alternative
starts from the same reached coordinates on its own declared support;
it is not a later removal event with an unaccounted storage jump.

The joined whole approaches common relative registration that the unchanged
separate components cannot approach together. This is a conditional
collective property caused by the admitted interaction. The child windings
already existed; their permanent retention is a separate conclusion.
The result supplies no new primitive state variable or fundamental law,
and does not make fine means an exact autonomous reduction. Continuous
retention of the two earlier parent registration neighborhoods, autonomous
contact selection, physical identification and emergence at other sizes
remain outside this theorem.
