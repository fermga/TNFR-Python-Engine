    Recursivity: associated history and collective sine dynamics¶

    REMESH has a precise conditional role on the organized sine objects. Its registered node-level action records an advisory; its separately invoked network map mixes each member's present form with that member's delayed forms. The latter is a genuine hybrid form reset, with a calculable effect on storage and subsequent phase motion. It does not derive a delay law from continuous nodal dynamics or automatically select when to apply it.

    This owner specializes existing mechanisms to the weighted sine law and contextual collective chart. The research program owns task order; the runtime REMESH derivation and event contracts retain the general map, transactions, history advancement and recurrence results.

    1. Three distinct operations and their admission¶

    The public Recursivity(depth=...) operator and its atomic execute_recursivity_stage leave structural form, phase, capacity, pressure and support unchanged before any separately supplied refresh callback. They record the target's actual glyph/lifecycle context and one graph-level advisory per runtime ordinal (the legacy retained-sample index outside that runtime). Equal physical states with different histories or advisory ordinals therefore need not have equal execution descriptions.

    REMESH is a registered generator/closure. Declared depth must be a positive integer; depth greater than one invokes the actual word's U5 stabilizer requirement. A validated word and live admission remain separate from the availability of delayed forms. Passing the advisory does not execute a temporal echo, create a child, prove fractal structure or select a delay. When the optional application precondition is enabled, REMESH_MIN_NODES is a strict nonnegative integer (default 2); zero remains an admitted configuration. Boolean, textual, floating or negative counts reject before the advisory writes. This uses the shared operator argument validator.

    The explicit apply_network_remesh operation instead consumes graph _epi_hist, a uniform factor 0<a<=1, strict positive integer local/global delays, and the clipping policy. It does not execute the glyph or supply its grammatical justification. Its caller must declare any composition with a grammatical word and its event schedule. No implicit bridge from advisory to mixing is assumed here.

    The optional apply_topological_remesh helper is yet another supplied operation. A change of support would change degrees, weights, pressure, storage and the collective chart. It is excluded from every fixed-support formula below and cannot be inferred from the delayed-map name.

    The delayed map requires at least max(tau_local,tau_global)+1 indexed snapshots. It returns an explicit no-op for insufficient history or empty live support. Selected lag entries must contain exactly the current node support, with admitted signed scalar forms (including uniform-real BEPI). Missing or invalid historical members cannot be replaced by current values. Both selected rows are admitted even when a coefficient vanishes at a=1. The runtime reads lag tau at index -(tau+1); the newest history row is not substituted for live x. Local/global label temporal lags, not a neighborhood average and a network-wide mean.

    2. Complete state and the actual reset¶

    Between events retain a fixed finite connected simple undirected support with at least two nodes and no self-loops, actual nonnegative conductances W, bare degrees d, primitive circular phase theta, held nonnegative capacities nu, zero forcing/loss and the declared structural clock:

    \[ q=L_Wx,\quad S_i=\sum_{j\sim i}\sin(\theta_j-\theta_i),\quad M=\operatorname{diag}\frac{\nu_i}{\pi d_i},\quad p_i=\frac{S_i}{\pi d_i},\quad \dot x=MS,\quad\dot\theta=Mq. \tag{1} \]

    For the event retain its selected node-associated lag fields y,z, row order, history support, delays, primitive representations, clipping bounds/mode, factor source, runtime clock and consumed execution context. For repeated use retain the full required indexed suffix and its advancement rule; the two currently selected rows alone need not determine the next selection. Association matters: a multiset of past values does not say which member receives which value. _epi_hist is distinct from per-node mutation evidence and glyph history. A supplied temporal sampling model is necessary before an index delay is interpreted as elapsed structural or laboratory time.

    In exact real arithmetic set

    \[ b=(1-a)^2,\quad c=a(1-a),\quad e=a,\quad b+c+e=1, \qquad r=bx+cy+ez. \tag{2} \]

    The implementation evaluates the nested binary64 expression (1-a)*((1-a)*x+a*y)+a*z, then structural clipping. It exposes raw and bounded node proposals. Let x+ be the actual admitted committed endpoint, including those interventions, and h=x+-x. Form changes; theta, nu, support and stored pressure do not. The executor does not append or shift _epi_hist. Its timestamped EPI boundary recorder prevents a same-time form jump from being misread as a continuous two-sample Mutation rate. That recorder is not the delayed-history advancement rule.

    The shared executor plans before writing and protects graph-owned state against commit/observer failure. External callback effects have their own limits. The formulas use the existing executor and sine field reader; no new reset implementation or alternate history interpreter is introduced.

    3. Exact storage work and a history-dependent passivity condition¶

    Use the selected storage

    \[ H(x,\theta)=F(x)+V(\theta),\qquad F(x)=\tfrac12x^TL_Wx,\qquad V(\theta)=\sum_{\{i,j\}}(1-\cos(\theta_j-\theta_i)). \]

    Phase is fixed during this jump, so the existing form-reset identity gives

    \[ \Delta H=q^Th+\tfrac12h^TL_Wh. \tag{3} \]

    This is exact for the actual represented/clipped endpoint interpreted as a real vector. Nonpositive work holds if and only if q^T h <= -h^T L_W h/2; it is not guaranteed by the operator label.

    For the ideal unclipped endpoint r, quadratic convexity gives the stronger history-resolved identity, writing (v_0,v_1,v_2)=(x,y,z) and (c_0,c_1,c_2)=(b,c,e):

    \[ F(r)=\sum_k c_k F(v_k) -\frac12\sum_{k<l}c_kc_l (v_k-v_l)^TL_W(v_k-v_l). \tag{4} \]

    Thus F(y)<=F(x) and F(z)<=F(x) are sufficient for passive ideal mixing. They are not necessary. A common hard clipping interval is nonexpansive on every pair of real scalar values, so F(clip(r))<=F(r); the same sufficient condition survives ideal hard clipping. It does not remove the binary64 evaluation residual. Soft clipping and arbitrary represented outputs must use (3) or their explicitly justified bounds.

    There is no passive guarantee from current form alone. On the weighted path 0--1--2 with conductances 1 and 2 and capacities one, take x=0, y=z=(1,0,-1) and a=1/2, with clipping inactive. All three histories have zero mean in the conserved metric rho=(1,2,1), but

    \[ x^+=\tfrac34(1,0,-1),\qquad \Delta H=F(x^+)=\frac{27}{32}>0. \tag{5} \]

    The phase may be any admitted acute phase configuration and is unchanged. These supplied histories are a counterexample to unconditional passivity, not a claim that this particular triple is sampled from one sine trajectory. Convex mixing can import stored historical disagreement into a current consensus. The augmented-history Lyapunov theorem is not contradicted: it consumes a different energy and an explicitly supplied history shift.

    4. Both continuous rows and their means after the jump¶

    Because (1) obtains pressure from phase and bare support,

    \[ p^+=p,\qquad \dot x^+=\dot x,\qquad q^+=q+L_Wh,\qquad \dot\theta^+-\dot\theta=ML_Wh. \tag{6} \]

    These are refreshed-law comparisons. An unrelated stored-pressure alias or opaque refresh cannot replace (1). An unchanged instantaneous form rate does not imply unchanged subsequent motion: its derivative changes by M DS(theta) M L_W h when both continuations use the same held law. For positive capacities and a connected positive-conductance graph, the phase-rate change vanishes exactly when h is spatially uniform. With zero conductances or capacities use the actual kernel of M L_W instead.

    At positive capacities let rho_i=d_i/nu_i and define means using the retained phase lifts. At the jump

    \[ \overline\theta^+=\overline\theta,\qquad \overline x^+-\overline x=\frac{\sum_i\rho_i h_i}{\sum_i\rho_i}. \tag{7} \]

    For ideal (2), the form mean is b*mean(x)+c*mean(y)+e*mean(z). If y,z really come from the same event-free fixed-support, held-capacity conservative flow, its conserved weighted form mean makes this equal to the current mean. Earlier jumps, changed metrics or arbitrary history do not supply that premise. Rounding/clipping require their actual residual. Equal historical means alone do not imply passive work, as (5) shows.

    The phase mean is unchanged immediately and remains the appropriate conserved lifted mean during each subsequently admitted continuous segment. Neither mean is a complete collective state. In particular, a nonuniform reset can preserve both means while changing the internal phase row.

    5. A sufficient collective domain and a symmetry obstruction¶

    Use the marked twenty-node contact and its nine physical twin pairs from the contextual chart. Keep its support/conductance/capacity involution and marked acute phase sector. Require current x and both selected history rows to have equal forms within each twin pair. Uniform factor/delays and one scalar clipping map then produce equal outputs for each pair, even under the same deterministic represented arithmetic. Phase and capacity equalities, marked support and cycle windings are unchanged by the jump.

    For a positive-width subdomain, take every selected form in (1/4,3/8), hard bounds [-1,1], any a in (0,1], and any admitted acute phase in the existing twin chart. Ideal outputs stay in that interval by convexity. This proves immediate preservation of that organizational class for a whole history family, not one replayed source. At an admitted actual endpoint, smooth uniqueness and phase-sector openness also supply a possibly endpoint-dependent nonzero continuation interval in the class. No uniform lifetime, passive work or formation result follows. A separate barrier or enclosure is needed for a claimed longer interval.

    Current twin symmetry alone is insufficient. In the unclipped exact model, for paired i,j with x_i=x_j the output difference is

    \[ x_i^+-x_j^+=c(y_i-y_j)+e(z_i-z_j). \tag{8} \]

    Vanishing of this combination is the exact single-event condition. Equal lag rows are a reusable sufficient condition; cancellation can admit a larger family. At a=1 only the global difference contributes to the ideal output, although both rows remain runtime inputs. Clipping can erase a difference but does not justify forgetting the original history/context.

    The eleven-cell physical chart therefore extends by associated histories. One may compress pairwise-equal history coordinates and lift them back to the same fine members; the actual history order, multiplicities and all nonphysical member records remain. On that stratum the uniform temporal map commutes with this lift. For arbitrary linear observations, (2) also commutes with the observation in exact arithmetic, but clipping and nonlinear sine evolution do not inherit arbitrary linear closure from that fact. The exact P5 diffusion/echo composition in Derived EPI memory has a different generator and quotient; its coefficients and stability verdicts cannot be transferred to this complete sine model.

    6. Verification and resulting boundary¶

    Synthetic controls exercise the existing advisory, word admission, delayed planner/executor, history support, storage, means, refreshed rows, clipping and the contact's history-dependent symmetry. Their supplied histories are labeled controls, not new continuous response evidence or retained-producer replays.

    The result admits a canonical temporal form operation on a declared family of collective objects with sufficient associated history. Its precise limits include missing history, unsupported history association, active clipping, and event work that can be positive. Runtime validity, grammar, organizational identity, passivity and selected occurrence remain distinct. Completing the event contract does not require every object to admit every operator or every grammatical word to preserve the same identity.
