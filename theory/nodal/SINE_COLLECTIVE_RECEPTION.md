it mixes form over the actual bare incoming neighbors while retaining the
physical twin stratum. Its event work depends on the separate conductance
weights and need not be nonpositive. The selected conservative sine law

but can change the phase-rate row. These are conditional analytical results,
not an evaluation of a new retained-source word or trajectory. The
[research program](../RESEARCH_PROGRAM.md#research-current-question)
alone selects any subsequent investigation.

## 3. Exact event work and its sign

For every admitted actual endpoint `x^+=x+h` with phase and support fixed,
including a represented or clipped endpoint, direct expansion of (2) gives

\[
 \Delta H=q^Th+\tfrac12h^TL_Wh
 =\sum_{\{i,j\}\in U}W_{ij}
   \left[(x_i-x_j)(h_i-h_j)+\tfrac12(h_i-h_j)^2\right].
 \tag{5}
\]

Thus the necessary and sufficient nonpositive-work criterion is
`q^T h <= -h^T L_W h/2`. It is a condition on the full event, not an
operator-name guarantee. This storage has no additional capacity term.

For one unclipped target, let `s_i=sum_j W_ij>0` and let
`v_i=sum_j W_ij*x_j/s_i` be its conductance-weighted neighbor mean. Then
`q_i=s_i*(x_i-v_i)` and

\[
 \Delta H=m_i b_i q_i+\tfrac12m_i^2s_i b_i^2
 =\frac{s_i}{2}\left[(x_i+m_i b_i-v_i)^2-(x_i-v_i)^2\right].
 \tag{6}
\]

For `m_i>0`, this is nonpositive exactly when

\[
 b_iq_i\leq-\tfrac12m_i s_i b_i^2.
 \tag{7}
\]

The statement includes the zero-update case. When the update is nonzero,
its direction must oppose q_i and its length must not overshoot the
equal-storage endpoint about v_i. Reception moves toward u_i, whereas
weighted form storage is minimized at v_i; these centers can differ.

With a fixed mix `0<m_i<=1`, fixed positive incident weights and arbitrary
target/neighbor forms in any nonempty open interval, **single-target EN has
nonpositive work for all such forms if and only if those weights are
equal**. For equal weights, u_i=v_i and (6) reduces to

\[
 \Delta H=-m_i(1-m_i/2)\,q_i^2/s_i\leq0.
 \tag{8}
\]

Conversely, unequal weights make the weighted and bare averaging linear
functionals different. Choose neighboring forms within the interval for
which u_i differs from v_i, and choose the target form x_i=v_i. Then q_i=0,
the convex mixture stays in the interval and
`Delta H=m_i^2*s_i*(u_i-v_i)^2/2>0`. The zero mix is the identity and is the
explicit exception. This proves a universal-scope obstruction; a particular
unequally weighted source can still satisfy (7).

This equivalence allows independent neighbor forms. If forms are constrained
to be equal within fixed cells, equality of the two mean functionals instead
requires, for every incident cell a with per-member bare count n_ia,

\[
 \frac{\sum_{j\in a\cap N_i}W_{ij}}{n_{ia}}=\frac{s_i}{d_i}.
\]

Only these per-cell average weights are then observable by the numeric
mixture. The contact's admitted involution also makes the two weights from
a port into each twin cell equal. Its old unit-weight cells and newly added
subunit-weight cells can still violate this restricted condition, as the
explicit witness below does.

For simultaneous targets T, let `W_ij=0` for absent edges. Equation (5)
can also be written

\[
 \Delta H=\sum_{i\in T}
    \left(q_i h_i+\tfrac12s_i h_i^2\right)
    -\sum_{\{i,j\}\subset T}W_{ij}h_i h_j.
 \tag{9}
\]

The final sum is the cross term omitted by adding isolated-target work.
It is present at the actual internal port edge. Its sign depends on the
two increments; single-target criteria do not decide a simultaneous stage.
Whole-word work must additionally include IL's phase reset and any other
actual intervening event, rather than attributing their sum to EN.

## 4. Fresh rows, means and pressure provenance

At the EN event, support, phase and capacity are fixed. Hence the complete
selected law gives

\[
 p^+=p,\qquad \dot x^+=\dot x,\qquad
 q^+=q+L_Wh,\qquad
 \dot\theta^+-\dot\theta=M L_Wh.
 \tag{12}
\]

An untargeted neighbor can acquire a different phase rate through the
corresponding off-diagonal entry of L_W h. No phase jump is being inferred
from this rate change. On this connected positive support, a nonzero
increment supported on the proper port subset is not a constant vector,
so L_W h is nonzero; positive capacities make the phase-rate change
nonzero somewhere. Equation (11) therefore gives a nontrivial subsequent
phase-rate response even though both port gradients were initially zero.

There is also a local consequence for form motion. Let C(theta) be the bare
Laplacian with edge coefficients `cos(theta_j-theta_i)`. On either smooth
continuation with held support and capacity, differentiation of (1) yields

\[
 \ddot x=-M C(\theta)M q,\qquad
 \ddot x^+-\ddot x=-M C(\theta)M L_W h.
 \tag{12a}
\]

Within the acute connected sector, C has only the constant null direction.
If the acceleration difference vanished, M L_W h would be constant. Since
`1^T L_W h=0` and M is positive, that constant must be zero. Connected
positive-conductance support then forces h to be constant. Thus a nonzero
port-only h changes both the phase rate and the form acceleration, although
it leaves the instantaneous form rate unchanged. These are local derivative
identities, not a finite-horizon response or maintenance prediction.

The EN primary map leaves stored pressure and the stored `dEPI` channel
unchanged; optional diagnostics are separate observations, not a new
evolution row. If the source pressure was fresh, it remains fresh for (1).
If the source pressure
was stale, EN preserves that same discrepancy; an explicit refresh is still
needed before claiming the constitutive row. This differs from the
[pure-EPI realization bridge](../../src/tnfr/physics/reception_realization.py),
whose pressure depends on form and generally changes after EN. The bridge's
numerical kernels can be reused; its pressure-manifold defect and diffusion
stability certificate are not certificates for (1).

Let `rho_i=d_i/nu_i` and `R=sum_i rho_i`. Capacity and support do not change,
so the two means obey

\[
 \mu_x^+-\mu_x=\frac{\rho^T h}{R},\qquad
 \mu_\theta^+=\mu_\theta.
 \tag{13}
\]

The continuing conservative law preserves its new form-mean leaf. Local
Reception is not generally a mean-conserving transfer; no compensating
source decrement, reservoir or autonomous event selector is installed by
the blend. A supply interpretation must account separately for (5).
