    One collective Resonance word on the retained pair object¶

    This result extends the admitted Emission--Silence word by one actual neighbor-dependent Resonance stage. It uses the unordered pair state, the RA contract, and the partial-transition criterion.     \[ b_{\rm AL}=\frac1{1024},\qquad d_{\rm RA}=\frac18,\qquad c_{\rm RA}=\frac1{16},\qquad a_{\rm RA}=\frac1{32},\qquad a_{\rm SHA}=\frac12. \]

    The supplied word is emission,resonance,silence on one marked whole pair k. Use the explicit core grammar profile and validated per-prefix contexts, the canonical public classes and the shared selected-target snapshot stage.     2. Uniform admission and a shared nonzero neighbor resultant¶

    \[ \alpha+|\eta_i-\eta_j| \le\alpha+\sqrt2\,Z_f <\alpha+\sqrt2\,z<\frac\pi2. \]

    For example pi/2-alpha=pi/10>3/10, whereas sqrt(2)*z<3/1024. Thus every one of the four neighbors is individually U3-compatible for both selected members, with a strict margin. Their neighbor sets are the same, and neither target is its partner's neighbor. AL therefore changes none of the neighbor data consumed by this RA stage. This statement depends on the supplied complete doubled support and canonical limit; arbitrary pruning or a tighter limit needs a fresh calculation.

    Let the pre-AL pair variables be X,Theta,R,U,Q, with associated signed representatives u,delta, so R=cos(delta)>0, U=u^2 and Q=u*sin(delta). The shared neighbor form mean and complex resultant are

    \[ A_k=\frac{X_{k-1}+X_{k+1}}2,\qquad S_k=\frac{R_{k-1}e^{i\Theta_{k-1}} +R_{k+1}e^{i\Theta_{k+1}}}{2}. \]

    \[ |\mu_k-\theta_{*,k}-\gamma| \le\arctan\frac{z/2}{\cos\alpha-z/2} <\frac{2z}{1-2z}<3z. \]

    Both target-to-mean differences consequently have magnitude below 4z, so the runtime's shortest-arc interpolation uses precisely this same lift. The mean-alignment advisory also has a strict passing margin. The exact antipodal and zero-resultant branches are absent on this domain.

    Every source form is in (143/512,177/512). AL remains unclipped and places the selected forms above 1/4.     \[ \mu_0-\gamma =\arctan\left( \frac{(\cos\epsilon-1)\sin\alpha} {(\cos\epsilon+1)\cos\alpha}\right) =\arctan\left[-\tan^2(\epsilon/2)\tan\alpha\right]<0. \]

    The unperturbed argument offset is zero. Thus the target collective phase after the word differs by (mu_0-gamma)/16, strictly, while its form mean and capacity outputs coincide.     Conditional passivity of this RA stage¶

    5. Representation, verification and remaining scope¶

    The formulas and positive-width theorem above use exact real arithmetic. AL has the exact binade addition property proved by the earlier word, but that property does not make the RA blend or its circular mean exact. Even the rational capacity multiplier 33/32 can round on an arbitrary binary64 capacity. Equal input capacities still undergo the same calculation, whereas a floating-point primary result must be admitted on its actual stored value.

    For a represented execution, independently record the actual total form increment and circular phase displacement, actual capacities, kind, histories, and pressure provenance. Rebuild the consumed collective state and structural work. If an error enclosure against the exact map is used, retain the separately bounded centered form and phase defects e_x,e_theta; a sufficient replacement norm bound is

    \[ Z_{\rm represented}' \le\frac{69}{16384}+\|e_x\|+\|e_\theta\|. \]

    Recheck the strict source, U3, positive-capacity, radius and energy premises using certified values. A static successful control is not a symbolic proof that all transcendental calls are exact, and a conservative bound failure does not demonstrate loss of identity. Neighbor reduction order and circular projection are part of the represented execution protocol; exact-real permutation invariance alone does not establish bytewise equality for all floating results. The associated history quotient also cannot be authenticated by equal rounded summaries.

    The static selected-stage controls exercise the supplied complete word against independently reconstructed collective rows, associated histories, the internal-neighbor distinction, graph-edge work, rejected-prefix preservation and the existing same-law identity reader. They run no trajectory and retain finite precision and source admission separately from this exact-real proof.

It reuses the earlier persistent-response bounds; it neither executes RA nor equates its factors with a continuous elapsed time. The execution plan owns the next investigation.
