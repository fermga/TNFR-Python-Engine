    1. Complete support, normalization and state¶

    Let \(p=0,1\) index the same-slot and registered interfaces, and define

    \[ k_c^{(0)}=0,\qquad k_c^{(1)}=2(c-2),\qquad \sigma_{p,c}(j)=j+k_c^{(p)}\pmod9. \tag{1} \]

    For each c this is a permutation of all nine donor slots. Thus every host node gains exactly one contact and every donor node gains exactly five. The complete graph is undirected, connected and has 54 nodes and 144 unit edges, with degrees five and seven. No contact is repeated. The internal C9 supports and the original outer C5 slot cycles are identical between the two contacts; the mixed contact cycles can differ.

    All signed forms and primitive phases remain in the state. With \(L_p\) the actual graph Laplacian and \(S_p\) its complete sine current, the rows and storage are

    \[ \begin{split} x'&=(-L_px+\gamma S_p(\theta))/4,& \theta'&=\gamma L_px/4,\\ H_p&=\tfrac12x^TL_px+\sum_{e\in E_p}(1-\cos\delta_e),& H_p'&=-\|L_px\|^2/4. \end{split} \tag{2} \]

    Both global form and lifted phase sums are conserved. At contact they are zero and \(9\beta\), respectively. The uniform quarter factor is unchanged in both rows; it is not inverse degree. The two contact graphs define different subsequent full vector fields. Equal degrees or an abstract graph relabeling do not establish equality at the same retained source and marked readout. The isolated origin symmetry does not erase relative registrations after attachment.

    For an edge from host \((c,j)\) to donor \(\sigma_{p,c}(j)\), write

    \[ u_{c,j}=x_{D,\sigma_{p,c}(j)}-x_{H,c,j},\quad \delta_{c,j}=\theta_{D,\sigma_{p,c}(j)}-\theta_{H,c,j}, \quad f_{c,j}=(u_{c,j}+\gamma\sin\delta_{c,j})/4, \quad g_{c,j}=-\gamma u_{c,j}/4. \tag{3} \]

    The host receives \((f,g)\) and the contacted donor node receives \((-f,-g)\). These are both boundary channels, including backreaction. Their edge storage is \(u^2/2+1-\cos\delta\); its derivative plus both regional derivatives gives (2). No auxiliary scalar forcing or one-sided donor waveform replaces these reciprocal rows.

    2. Permutation matching preserves the exact mean equation¶

    Set \(R_D=(1/9)\sum_jx_{D,j}\), and

    \[ \mathcal A_p=\frac19\sum_{c,j}\sin\delta_{c,j}. \tag{4} \]

    Every internal current cancels in the donor mean. Bijection of each \(\sigma_{p,c}\) gives \(\sum_{c,j}x_{D,\sigma_{p,c}(j)}=45R_D\). The host form total is \(-9R_D\), from the conserved zero whole form. Summing (3) therefore gives, for arbitrary admitted fine states,

    \[ R_D'=-3R_D/2-\gamma\mathcal A_p/4,\qquad \Psi_D'=3\gamma R_D/2,\qquad R_D(0)=0. \tag{5} \]

    Here \(\Psi_D\) is the donor's actual lifted phase mean. The matching permutations preserve the exact form cancellation; an arbitrary contact weight profile would not follow from this argument. The nonlinear moment \(\mathcal A_p\) still consumes the full boundary phase state.

    Let \(X=1/100\), \(T_o=1/5000\), \(q=10^{-8}\), \(\gamma_+\ge\gamma\), and

    \[ B_o=X+\gamma_+T_o,\qquad B=\max\{B_o,q\}+7\gamma_+h/4. \tag{6} \]

    The carried host has \(\|x_H(0)\|_\infty\le B_o\), and the donor has \(\|x_D(0)\|_\infty\le q\). The graph heat semigroup contracts the maximum norm and \(\|S_p\|_\infty\le7\), so \(\|x(t)\|_\infty\le B\) up to h for either interface. The phase row bounds host and donor rates by \(5\gamma_+B/2\) and \(7\gamma_+B/2\). Hence

    \[ |\mathcal A_p(t)-\mathcal A_p(0)|\le30\gamma_+Bt. \]

    Since \(|\mathcal A_p|\le5\), the nonnegative damping kernel in (5) gives \(|R_D(t)|\le5\gamma_+t/4\). Integrating the complete mean equation, including damping and phase motion, yields

    \[ \left|R_D(h)-R_D(0)+\frac{\gamma h}4\mathcal A_p(0)\right| \le E:=\frac{15}{16}\gamma_+h^2+ \frac{15}{4}\gamma_+^2Bh^2. \tag{7} \]

    Thus the previous form of the finite remainder is justified on each new graph by its own permutation, conservation and degree premises. It is not transferred merely because the contact counts agree.

    3. Actual source correlations and finite matrix entries¶

    The joint-resource proof rebuilds all six original sources, their uninterrupted recovery, actual outer acquisition and the complete sequential word.     Write \(\psi_j=\alpha(j-4)\), \(\alpha=2\pi/9\). The actual host offsets belong to \(P_{w,c}+[-Z-m_o,Z+m_o]\), while the donor has offsets \(\beta+z_{D,j}\), \(|z_{D,j}|\le q\). For an integer \(n_{p,c,j}\),

    \[ \psi_{\sigma_{p,c}(j)}-\psi_j =\alpha k_c^{(p)}-2\pi n_{p,c,j}. \tag{8} \]

    The turn term drops out of sine and cosine exactly. No actual lifted coordinate or phase total is changed by using the smaller signed k in the trigonometric argument. At the supplied \(\beta=\pi/2\),

    \[ \mathcal A_{w,p}(0)\in C_{w,p}:= \sum_{c=0}^4\cos\bigl(P_{w,c}-\alpha k_c^{(p)}+[-e,e]\bigr). \tag{9} \]

    This is a source-family enclosure of the actual slot correlations.     \[ R_{w,p}=R_{D,w,p}(h)-R_{D,w,p}(0)\in T_{w,p}:=-\frac{h[\gamma]}4C_{w,p}+[-E,E]. \tag{10} \]

    All four entries describe different complete continuations of retained precontact states. The same actual donor member is fixed in all four. The paired host members retain their shared primitive preparation errors; their distinct reached states are never identified with one another.

    5. Scalar factorization, noise and admissible correction¶

    Fix one actual paired host preparation and one donor member. If real functions \(a(s)\) and \(b(p)\) represent the four true responses by \(R(s,p)=a(s)b(p)\), then

    \[ \det R=R_{0,0}R_{1,1}-R_{0,1}R_{1,0}=0. \tag{14} \]

    The source factor may depend on every coordinate of the actual host member; the probe factor may consume the fixed donor and protocol, but cannot depend on which host is presented. Excluding zero from an outward determinant interval constructed from (10) contradicts (14). This tests multiplication of two real factors; a pure complex product also has zero determinant and is excluded by the same argument. It does not exclude an affine response, arbitrary nonlinear dependence on one scalar, multiple channels or memory-dependent descriptions. In particular, a real-part observation of a complex product need not have rank one: \(\operatorname{Re}(ab)=\operatorname{Re}(a)\operatorname{Re}(b) -\operatorname{Im}(a)\operatorname{Im}(b)\) is a sum of two real products.

    Let the observed increment matrix be \(Y_{w,p}=R_{w,p}+n_{w,p}\). Each increment uses two raw errors, so \(|n_{w,p}|\le2\eta\), with \(\eta=10^{-9}\). All eight raw errors can be correlated. Define

    \[ O_{w,p}=T_{w,p}+[-2\eta,2\eta],\qquad K_{w,p}=T_{w,p}+[-4\eta,4\eta]. \tag{15} \]

    Then \(Y\in O\). For any particular allowed observed record, every candidate corrected true matrix in its componentwise error box satisfies

    \[ Y_{w,p}+[-2\eta,2\eta]\ \subseteq\ K_{w,p}. \tag{16} \]

    The extra enlargement in K accounts for possible corrections of a noisy record; it introduces no additional readings or actual physical errors. For a matrix of intervals J define its outward determinant interval by \(\mathcal D(J)=J_{0,0}J_{1,1}-J_{0,1}J_{1,0}\). The prospective strong decision requires

    \[ 0\notin\mathcal D(T),\qquad 0\notin\mathcal D(K). \tag{17} \]

    The second condition makes the first redundant mathematically, but both evidence levels are reported. It ensures that every allowed observed record has no rank-one correction under the declared error model. A nonzero determinant of one noisy point matrix, by itself, would not reject a true rank-one response. Likewise, a determinant interval containing zero proves neither factorization nor existence of a jointly realizable correction.

    These uniform statements concern the same-source counterfactuals specified above. Four independently prepared laboratory records need a separate preparation/matching admission: different states cannot silently share the same source factor. The theorem supplies neither physical copies nor record authentication. Missing law, source, work or identity premises keep the combined result unavailable even if an algebraic determinant is separated.

    6. Assessment and scope boundary¶

    Re-admit the maximum corner of the joint resource box before constructing the four fresh source enclosures. Its source radii and cost bounds are nondecreasing in primitive allowances. The fixed q bounds every admitted recovered member; the supplied observation word does not depend on its reading errors. All downstream intervals therefore enclose the smaller allowance families as well, while (15) grows with contact reading error. No assessment of smaller corners or alternative registrations is needed.

    The detached proof consumer belongs in _organized_interface_factorization.py. It must rebuild primitive source and complete-support evidence, retain the true/observed/correction distinction, and preserve explicit unavailable conditions. Shared arithmetic and field kernels do not establish source identity, an occurrence law or an independent physical measurement bridge.

    A separated determinant would identify a concrete restriction on carrying one real multiplicative coupling across these normalized probes. It would not identify electric charge, disprove intrinsic properties generally or establish that every physical interaction must satisfy (14). An unresolved test would remain a finite admission result. Neither outcome selects a unique TNFR law, supplies indefinite outer-one maintenance or initiates a new hierarchy of closure moments.

    7. Retained finite factorization obstruction¶

    The declaration and complete consumer were committed at 9548b8e1 before the single assessment. The attempt retains the committed source inventory, declaration and arithmetic.
