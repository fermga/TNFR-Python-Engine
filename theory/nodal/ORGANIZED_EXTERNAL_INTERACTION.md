## 1. Complete state, support and balances

Let \(H\) denote the 45-node host and \(D\) the nine-node external
constituent, called the donor below only to distinguish its coordinates.
The sign of its measured form exchange is a conclusion, not a premise of
that name. The joined support is \(C_9\mathbin\square W_6\), where
\(W_6\) consists of the five-cycle and one hub connected to every cycle
vertex. It has 54 nodes and 144 undirected unit edges. The complete rows are

\[
 x'=(-Lx+\gamma S(\theta))/4,\qquad
 \theta'=\gamma Lx/4,\qquad
 S_i(\theta)=\sum_{b\sim i}\sin(\theta_b-\theta_i).
 \tag{1}
\]

Here prime denotes elapsed contact time in the declared tau clock. Forms
are signed, all capacities are held at one, and the phase law is smooth on
the full circle; real lifts are retained for the stated means. The form row
specifies pressure independently of the response. The supplied uniform
quarter factor applies to both rows even though the two vertex degrees
are five and seven. Degree normalization would define a different model.
The original clock multiplies both rows by \(1023/1024\).

Common form and common phase translations leave the complete field and
difference storage unchanged. Vertex phase representatives may change by
integer turns with their retained lifts transported consistently. Simultaneous
cyclic slot relabeling of all six children, and cyclic relabeling of the five
host children, are support symmetries when the state and marked observations
are relabeled together. Independent child phase translations cease to be
symmetries after their contacts are present; the relative donor origin is
therefore consumed boundary information.

Reciprocity gives the full storage and conserved totals:

\[
 \begin{split}
 H_{\rm all}&=\frac12x^TLx+
       \sum_{\{a,b\}}[1-\cos(\theta_b-\theta_a)],\\
 H_{\rm all}'&=-\frac14\|Lx\|_2^2,\qquad
 (\mathbf1^Tx)'=(\mathbf1^T\theta)'=0.
 \end{split}\tag{2}
\]

Indeed the phase gradient of storage is \(-S\), so the two mixed terms
cancel directly. Finite-dimensional global Lipschitz continuity of (1)
gives a unique complete solution. The proof needs neither acute new edges
nor native U3 admission. This remains the detached smooth-sine comparison,
not an implementation of a registered operator or its occurrence law.

## 3. Both port channels and the carrier restriction

Orient each new edge from host node \((c,j)\) to donor node j, and set

\[
 u_{c,j}=x_{D,j}-x_{H,c,j},\qquad
 \delta_{c,j}=\theta_{D,j}-\theta_{H,c,j},\qquad
 f_{c,j}=\frac{u_{c,j}+\gamma\sin\delta_{c,j}}4,\qquad
 g_{c,j}=-\frac{\gamma u_{c,j}}4.
 \tag{6}
\]

Let \(P\) sum contact coordinates over c at each donor slot j. With
\(L_H,S_H\) including all 90 original host edges and \(L_D,S_D\)
the donor internal cycle, the full rows split exactly as

\[
 \begin{array}{ll}

 \end{array}\tag{7}

\[

## 4. Exact donor mean and a finite whole-law remainder

\[
 R_D'=-\frac32R_D-\frac\gamma4\mathcal A,

 \tag{12}
\]

## 8. Collective information and interpretation boundary

On the carrier, the contact phase moment is
\(\sum_c\sin(\Psi_D-\Phi_c)\). Thus the complete external exchange
depends on the collective boundary registration, even when all child
windings and global form means agree. Identifying an outer winding does not
reconstruct that registration or the fine contact correlations.

For an explicit sufficiency counterexample, hold all forms zero and the
donor origin at \(\pi/2\). Compare host origins
\((0,0,0,0,0)\) and \((a,-a,0,0,0)\), with
\(0<a<\pi/4\), and no fine residuals. Both have the same zero mean
lifted origin, outer winding zero and five internal winding-one identities.
Their contact moments are respectively 5 and \(3+2\cos a\), hence
their donor form rates differ by (12). This is a counterexample to
mean-and-winding sufficiency on the stated state domain, not a claim that
these two configurations are reached by the frozen source preparation.

Even retaining the full complex contact moment does not generally close
the dynamics with only the two organizations' mean forms and lifted phase
means. On the carrier define

\[
 Q=\sum_{c=0}^4e^{i(\Psi_D-\Phi_c)},\qquad
 Q'=\frac{i\gamma}{4}\sum_c e^{i(\Psi_D-\Phi_c)}
                           [(KX)_D-(KX)_c].
 \tag{24}
\]

Fix host origins \((a,-a,0,0,0)\), \(0<a<\pi/4\), and donor
origin \(\pi/2\). Compare all forms zero with host forms
\((b,-b,0,0,0)\) and donor form zero, where \(b\ne0\). Both
states have the same host and donor mean forms, the same mean phases,
the same marked windings, and \(Q=i(3+2\cos a)\). They lie on
the same conserved form and lifted phase leaves as the declared contact.
For the second state the host entries of KX are
\((4b,-4b,b,0,-b)\), while the donor entry is zero. Equation (24)
therefore gives

\[
 Q'_{\rm zero\ form}=0,\qquad
 Q'_{\rm changed\ form}=-2i\gamma b\sin a\ne0.
 \tag{25}
\]

Their instantaneous donor form rates agree, but differentiating (12)
shows that their donor form accelerations differ by
\(\gamma^2b\sin a/2\). The retained moment and pair means thus
do not define an autonomous complete port state on this domain. The
missing information includes the phase/form correlation in (24); the
twelve-coordinate carrier state retains it. This independent algebraic
counterexample concerns the admitted model's state domain, not two
asserted members of the frozen acquired-source family.

Likewise, an independently changed donor registration changes its external
interaction; it is not determined by the host's winding label. The carrier
law (9) and general fine-state port (7) retain the information responsible.
No scalar coarse nodal law, winding-only force, nonadditivity or unique
physical constitutive law is inferred. Any physical comparison still needs
