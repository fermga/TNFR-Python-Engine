    Observing collective winding with finite form inputs¶

    Prospective protocol and decision¶

    The single finite pulse admitted one approximate phase intervention. This question admits a complete continuing observation history and derives its inverse anew. Known inputs and collective form measurements must suffice; actual phases, source labels and hypothetical reference readings are not inverse inputs.

    1. One continuous history and the global phase bound¶

    Let R repeat a child vector over nine slots (called T in the complete row in the declaration), and use L=I5 tensor L9+L5 tensor I9. For marked child c set k_c=e_c-1/5 and v_c=L5^+ k_c. On a ramp with signed increment a and local fraction r=u/d, the reused waveform is

    \[ s(r)=3r^2-2r^3,\quad z(u)=\frac{4a}{\gamma d}v_c s'(r),\quad f(u)=z'(u)+L_5z(u)/4. \tag{1} \]

    The command H evolves from its current value by a*k_c*s; it is constant on the plateaus. Both z endpoints vanish. The complete H is continuous, with H'=gamma*L5*z/4, starts and ends at zero, and never changes any edge by more than epsilon. The middle increment has magnitude 2*epsilon, but crosses from +epsilon to -epsilon; this is not an excursion of 2*epsilon. The fine input Rf is zero on plateaus and after release. Bounded switches in f jump no state. Endpoint readings are state values; the plateau estimates use the unforced one-sided dynamics.

    For the one actual solution throughout the entire program, put

    \[ y=x-Rz,\qquad e=\theta-\theta(0)-RH. \]

    Every switching boundary retains y and e continuously. The exact rows are

    \[ y'=-Ly/4+\gamma S(\theta)/4,\quad e'=\gamma Ly/4=-\gamma y'+\gamma^2S(\theta)/4. \tag{2} \]

    With g=gamma_upper, heat contraction and ||S||infinity<=4 give

    \[ \|y(t)\|_\infty\le M=X+gT_{obs},\qquad e(t)=\gamma\{x(0)-y(t)\} +\frac{\gamma^2}{4}\int_0^t S(\theta(u))\,du, \]
    \[ \boxed{\|e(t)\|_\infty\le E=2gX+2g^2T_{obs}}. \tag{3} \]

    This exact integration uses the live phase row and form diffusion. It avoids treating a transient large prescribed form offset as uncontrolled phase drift. No stationary background, phase feedback or reset is used. Global form and lifted-phase sums are conserved because every input is neutral and the edge currents sum to zero. Common means need not vanish in the larger inference class; the actual acquired hosts retain their original zero totals.

    At every sampling endpoint z vanishes, so abs(X_c)<=M. At final release both z and H vanish, yielding ||x_end||infinity<=M and ||theta_end-theta_initial||infinity<=E. For example ||x_end-x_initial||infinity<=M+X; neither state is restored exactly. The subsequent unforced flow must start at this actual endpoint.

    2. Complete identity and actual-source inclusion¶

    Initial internal fine gaps are 2*pi/9 plus differences of z; outer fine gaps are the admitted principal mean gaps plus those residual differences. Using (3), sufficient whole-history identity margins are

    \[ \begin{split} m_{internal,+}&=2\pi/9-2\zeta-2E,\\ m_{internal,acute}&=\pi/2-2\pi/9-2\zeta-2E,\\ m_{outer,acute}&=\pi/2-A-\epsilon-2\zeta-2E. \end{split} \tag{4} \]

    Strict positivity prevents a cut crossing on each of the five internal and nine outer marked cycles. Every initial outer identity is retained, including negative winding in the larger admitted class. Removing the current child mean from e changes a within-child residual by at most 2E, so a sufficient strict circular-collar margin is

    \[ m_{collar}=\pi/2-2\zeta-4E. \tag{5} \]

    The actual source is rebuilt from the recovery and resource primitives and outer acquisition, not from the old observation or contact reports. If the latter proof gives nominal form bound M, nominal outer-gap bound A, and full Euclidean departures R,Z, the inclusion checks are

    \[ M_*+R<X,\qquad Z<\zeta,\qquad A_*+2Z<A. \tag{6} \]

    Within-child mean removal is an orthogonal projection, so Z still bounds its residual; there is no state change in this decomposition. These bounds also cover the outer-zero control with stationary nominal carrier. Independent child acquisition/recovery, matching law and clock, support, capture, and historical storage/work premises remain necessary. Equality of their five resource bounds does not equate five actual preparations. The first plateau supplies the unforced first window; no extra prefix is inserted before it.

    3. Form readings isolate the interaction current¶

    Let U be the five actual child mean forms. Internal sine currents cancel exactly within a child, including off the carrier, leaving two external currents per child. On an unforced plateau,

    \[ U'=-L_5U/4+\gamma\bar S/4. \tag{7} \]

    The observer supplies two ordered vectors P,Q of measured mean forms at that plateau's endpoints. For the receiver r=(c+1) mod 5, form the exact primitive combination

    \[ D=Q_r-P_r+\frac h8\{L_5(P+Q)\}_r. \tag{8} \]

    All terms are in form units. The second term compensates diffusion rather than equating the observed increment to a sine-current response. Integrating (7) shows that its true-value counterpart equals gamma/4 * integral(bar S_r) plus the trapezoid error in the diffusion term. The fine unforced rows bound each mean's second derivative by

    \[ M_2=4M+2g+4g^2M. \tag{9} \]

    Since ||L5||infinity=4, the trapezoid error in D is at most M2*h^3/12. During any plateau the command is fixed. Each of the two external phase differences differs from its initial shifted counterpart by at most 2E, so the mean current differs by at most 4E. The plateau integral may therefore be compared to the initial shifted current with error at most g*h*E, irrespective of its block's position.

    The two receiver-reading coefficients in (8) are -1+h/4 and 1+h/4; the four neighboring endpoint coefficients are -h/8. With h<4, their absolute sum is exactly 2+h/2. Correlations need not be discarded. For every measured corrected increment,

    \[ D=\frac{\gamma h}{4}\bar S_r(\theta(0)+RH_q)+\delta, \quad |\delta|\le b=g h E+M_2h^3/12+(2+h/2)\eta_{raw}. \tag{10} \]

    No derivative or phase is measured. Reads from different plateaus belong to one carried state history; the initial state in (10) is a proof reference, never a copied or observed branch.

    4. Quadratures, circular geometry and the initial winding¶

    For interface c define the initial fine edge phasor by

    \[ C_c+iS_c=\frac19\sum_j e^{i d_{c,j}},\quad d_{c,j}=a_c+z_{c+1,j}-z_{c,j},\quad |a_c|\le A. \tag{11} \]

    The three plateau levels of block c affect just its receiver's boundary with marked child c. Their initial currents satisfy

    \[ \bar S_r^{\pm}-\bar S_r^0 =\mathord\pm\sin\epsilon\,C_c +(1-\cos\epsilon)S_c. \]

    Use the three new corrected increments to compute

    \[ O_c=D_+-D_-,\quad V_c=D_++D_--2D_0. \]
    \[ O_c=\frac{\gamma h\sin\epsilon}{2}C_c+\delta_C, \quad V_c=\frac{\gamma h(1-\cos\epsilon)}2 S_c+\delta_S, \quad |\delta_C|\le2b,\quad |\delta_S|\le4b. \tag{12} \]

    The coefficients and initial class in (12) exactly match the shared quadrature-to-cycle inverse. Consequently its existing geometry and circular kernel apply unchanged after the new primitive reading combinations and new complete-history errors have been established. This transfers only that algebraic inverse, not the earlier matched-branch or instantaneous sequential theorem.

    In particular C_c>=cos(A+2*zeta)>0, the phasor modulus is at least cos(2*zeta), and its circular argument differs from a_c by at most 2*zeta. The kernel intersects those constraints, bounds each mean gap, and tests their sum against 2*pi*w, w in {-1,0,1}. It retains shared outward rounding and the existing angle-arithmetic allowance. A uniform returned cycle width strictly smaller than 2*pi admits at most one winding; an actual valid history supplies its included winding. This proves conditional identification for every admitted state and error record.

    Malformed primitive readings reject before arithmetic. Impossible raw magnitudes, incompatible phasor annuli or absent cycle candidates remain explicit. A candidate or even a singleton for arbitrary supplied records does not authenticate their origin, their law, their error bounds or their joint realizability. Noise that defeats the uniform certificate leaves protocol admission unavailable, even if particular data look decisive.

    5. Complete continuous resource account¶

    The storage and balance remain

    \[ H=\tfrac12x^TLx+\sum_e(1-\cos\delta_e),\qquad H'=-\|Lx\|_2^2/4+(Lx)^TRf. \tag{13} \]

    Let ell=gamma_lower. The largest ramp has amplitude 2*epsilon and duration one. With the full-history residual M, the pulse extrema give

    \[ Z_m=\frac{24\epsilon}{5\ell},\quad J=\frac{48\epsilon}{5\ell}+8M,\quad F_m=\frac{108\epsilon}{5\ell}. \]
    \[ \sup H\le180(Z_m+M)^2+180,\quad Loss\le45T_{obs}J^2/4,\quad W_{abs}\le45\cdot15 JF_m. \tag{14} \]

    The input is present only over the fifteen ramps. The last bound also bounds total positive supplied work, including the sum of positive individual channel contributions. It uses absolute powers before summing, so simultaneous supplying and absorbing channels do not conceal costs. Storage change, loss and supplied work are distinct ledger quantities; old event work and signed hidden storage supply no reservoir.

    Returning the command to zero does not erase the cost. On each ramp, the actual collective phase increment is its command plus at most 2E componentwise. The marked-minus-adjacent increment has magnitude at least delta_a=max(0,abs(a)-4E). The previously proved C5 contrast inequalities then imply, on disjoint ramp intervals,

    \[ Loss\ge\frac{90}{g^2} (2\delta_{\epsilon}^2+\delta_{2\epsilon}^2),\qquad \sup\tfrac12x^TLx\ge12\delta_{2\epsilon}^2/g^2. \tag{15} \]

    Plateau loss is nonnegative. Since initial storage is at most 180X^2+180, positive supplied work is at least max(0,Loss_lower-180X^2-180). These structural bounds prove neither calibrated joules nor optimal cost. Finite bandwidth, tracking uncertainty and physical sampling remain additional premises before interpreting this protocol as a realizable measurement.

    6. Instrument and handoff boundary¶

    The pure protocol, word and inverse admit only their primitive time, noise and ordered form readings. The source assessor separately rebuilds actual acquisition and inclusion, then assesses the fixed corner through the exclusive driver. The independent controls exercise synthetic algebra and source failures without generating a reserved response. No existing frozen driver or inverse is rewritten.

    The final state belongs to this new finite-input history. Neither the old post-observation contact response nor its source-specific electrical error certificate transfers merely because the same winding was retained. Their full source, means, support, law and clock must match the new endpoint. The execution plan alone selects the next dependency. No physical dataset, autonomous actuator, laboratory calibration or material identity is admitted by the inverse.
