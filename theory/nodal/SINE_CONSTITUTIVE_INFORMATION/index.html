    11.6 Global closure obstruction and a normalized-sine comparison¶

    The finite-time boundary audit requires distinguishing a limited-domain law from a globally defined one. The pressure owner already proves that principal-argument pressure has no continuous extension through either an antipodal branch or a zero neighbor resultant. With w*nu_i>0 the form row inherits that discontinuity. Changing only phase evolution, storage or locality cannot repair it while leaving that form row unchanged. The reflected boundary additionally has path-dependent phase-rate limits from positive resultants. A regular invertible chart change removes neither obstruction.

    The incompatibility is stronger than the local-completion theorem¶

    Take P2 with r=x_0-x_1!=0, delta=theta_1-theta_0 in (-pi,pi), c=nu_0+nu_1>0 and arbitrary phase velocities F_0,F_1. The native form row and current storage give, before any locality assumption,

    \[ E=r^2/2+\beta(1-\cos\delta),\qquad \dot E=c[-er^2+wr\delta/\pi]+\beta\sin\delta(F_1-F_0). \]

    Preserving the prescribed exact loss forces

    \[ F_0-F_1=\frac{wcr\delta}{\beta\pi\sin\delta}. \]

    This relative rate diverges as delta->pi at fixed nonzero form contrast. It is unaffected by a common phase clock. Dropping capacity separability or allowing nonlocal phase rows therefore cannot supply a bounded completion with the same pressure, storage and loss.

    Even weakening exact loss to total passivity is insufficient. If F were bounded near this antipodal state, its phase-work term would tend to zero, leaving lim Edot=c*(-e*r^2+w*r). For e>0 choose 0<r<w/e; for e=0 any r>0 suffices. The limit is positive, contradicting passivity. This argument concerns all-state work identities, not an assertion that these fixed-form paths are trajectories. Actual finite-time collisions have their separate proof. The bounded rho*Ng and fixed-form nonlinear phase corrections above retain their regular-domain results but cannot cure this global obstruction.

    That pressure, cosine storage and exact global loss Unbounded relative phase response near antipodal P2, without needing locality

    Reuse an existing phase reading with an explicit changed premise¶

    The repository already distinguishes J_phi/pi from argument pressure. Write S_i=sum_j sin(theta_j-theta_i), q=Bx and D=diag(d_i) on fixed connected simple unit support. The following comparison law uses the same signed form, circular phase, held nonnegative capacity and clock:

    \[ s_i=\frac{S_i}{\pi d_i},\qquad \boxed{\dot x=N(-eD^{-1}q+ws),\quad \dot\theta=\frac{w}{\beta\pi}ND^{-1}q.} \]

    Its phase-to-form pressure is s=J_phi/pi and its response metric is H_s=pi*D. This replaces both native phase-pressure response and its matching exchange row. It is not the native law with a different domain setting, and not the separately supplied Kuramoto/U3 sine phase clock: here sine enters form pressure, while form contrast drives phase. Existing current telemetry does not select its use as a dynamical law.

    On the native regular domain,

    \[ s_i=\frac{|z_i|}{d_i}\operatorname{sinc}(\operatorname{Arg}z_i)\,g_i. \]

    Thus the existing geometric relation directly relates the two responses. The comparison retains cancellation strength as well as direction, and has no division by a resultant or its argument. It remains defined when phases cancel. No new primitive variable, cutoff, fitted gain or boundary event is introduced. Choosing this pressure is nevertheless an additional premise.

    Complete balance, continuation and inherited geometry¶

    With the unchanged storage E=E_D+beta*V_phi, grad V_phi=-S gives

    \[ \dot E_D=-e q^TND^{-1}q+\frac w\pi q^TND^{-1}S,\qquad \beta\dot V_\phi=-\frac w\pi S^TND^{-1}q. \]

    The exchange cancels exactly, leaving the same loss -e q^TND^{-1}q. On any fixed finite graph the complete vector field is globally Lipschitz on the form/phase lift: its form dependence is linear with constant coefficients, and sine has bounded derivative. Hence every finite initial state has a unique global solution, descending to circular phases. This mathematical theorem concerns the comparison, not the native executor.

    For strictly positive held capacities, reciprocity supplies two identities:

    \[ \frac{d}{dt}\sum_i\frac{d_i}{\nu_i}x_i=0,\qquad \frac{d}{dt}\sum_i\frac{d_i}{\nu_i}\theta_i=0. \]

    The second uses a chosen continuous phase lift; it is not in general a single-valued scalar observation on the phase torus. At zero capacity these weighted expressions are unavailable, although the smooth law and work balance remain valid. The native law generally conserves neither mean.

    With all capacities positive, equilibria are exactly q=0,S=0. All native regular equilibria therefore survive, but the complete set grows: zero and negative-real resultants with S=0 are now allowed. The seven-equilibrium classification must not be transferred as exhaustive. At each equilibrium the existing full-network stiffness proof applies with H=pi*D: positive quotient cosine Hessian gives local exponential recovery for e,w,beta>0, and negative stiffness gives growing real modes. Consensus has exactly the native linearization. Away from consensus, equal geometry and stability signs do not give equal rates, memory or basins.

    For e>0 and positive capacities, storage and conserved weighted form mean give precompactness on form times the phase torus. On the largest invariant subset with zero loss, q=0 and BND^-1 S=0, so ND^-1 S=c*1. Reciprocity sum_i S_i=0 forces c=0. LaSalle therefore gives approach to the equilibrium set. This does not prove convergence to consensus, to one equilibrium, or to a physical entity.

    Conditional selection and remaining freedom¶

    There is a more structural selection route than assuming a constant metric. Keep cosine storage, the same diffusion channel and exact loss on the full phase torus for every finite connected simple unit graph and every nonnegative capacity vector. Require zero-capacity phase freezing, own-capacity separability and a finite continuous phase law. The additional pressure premise is normalized pairwise superposition,

    \[ g_i(\theta)=d_i^{-1}\sum_{j\sim i}f(\theta_j-\theta_i), \]

    with one continuous, periodic, odd kernel f across supports. On a star with only the root active and arbitrary q_i!=0, exact loss gives beta*S_i*F_i=w*nu_i*q_i*g_i. Thus every finite family with sum_j sin(delta_j)=0 must also have sum_j f(delta_j)=0. All phases, including cancellations and antipodal leaves, are admitted by the proposed global completion; no regular-domain padding is required.

    Reuse the balanced-star algebra with a different object being classified. The two gaps delta,-arcsin(sin(delta)) give f(delta)=f(arcsin(sin(delta))). Put h(s)=f(arcsin(s)). Three gaps with sines a,b,-(a+b) give h(a+b)=h(a)+h(b) whenever all arguments lie in [-1,1]. Continuity implies h(s)=k*s and therefore f(delta)=k*sin(delta). Matching the native consensus tangent fixes k=1/pi. Capacity isolation now forces F_i=w*nu_i*q_i/(beta*pi*d_i) whenever S_i!=0; own-capacity separability transfers this row to general capacities. Nonzero S_i states are dense by perturbing a neighbor phase, including near zero resultants, so continuity extends the row everywhere. Varying only the root phase would not justify that density at a zero neighbor resultant.

    This derives the metric and phase independence instead of assuming them. It does not derive pairwise superposition, which the native collective Arg source does not obey. A separate sufficient selection premise is to require the phase row itself to be phase-independent while leaving the phase-only pressure initially unrestricted: the same isolated-capacity identity and consensus tangent then also force the displayed comparison.

    Neither selection premise follows from smoothness, storage or circular symmetry. For example, the comparison family

    \[ m_i(\theta)=\frac{1+\epsilon(S_i/d_i)^2}{\pi d_i},\quad g_i=m_iS_i,\quad F_i=(w/\beta)\nu_i m_i q_i,\qquad \epsilon\ge0 \]

    retains smoothness, positive mobility, exact loss, the same equilibria and the same consensus Jacobian. It supplies a counterexample to unique selection, not another production setting. The frozen pairing comparison reuses its zero-loss members to separate a robust local grouping direction from different relative response rates. The family generally violates the common pairwise-superposition premise. The original storage classification assumed native pressure; changing that premise means cosine storage is retained here explicitly, not rederived by that old theorem.

    An exact discriminator before any trajectory¶

    On a three-leaf star, take center phase zero, leaf phases (0,0,pi/2), uniform form and positive held capacities. All native resultants are regular. Put W=sum_i d_i/nu_i and let M be the degree/inverse-capacity weighted form mean. At this same initial state,

    \[ \dot M_{\rm native} =\frac wW\left[\frac{3\arctan(1/2)}{\pi}-\frac12\right]<0,\qquad \dot M_{\rm sine}=0. \]

    The sign follows from atan(1/2)<1/2<pi/6. Both initial phase rates are zero, and both laws dissipate zero storage initially; the mean response still distinguishes them. This is an exact prospective initial-rate comparison with identical preparation, coefficients and clock, not a fitted response or physical validation. The older same-mean/different-spread pressure test remains useful; no frozen acquisition is rerun.

    There is also a purely spectral distinction at the same maintained C5 geometry. For a uniform acute cycle twist with c=cos(kappa)>0, common capacity nu and nonzero combinatorial Laplacian eigenvalue ell, write z=2*s/(nu*ell) for the scaled temporal exponent. The two characteristic polynomials are

    \[ z^2+ez+\frac{w^2}{\beta\pi^2c}=0\quad\text{(native)},\qquad z^2+ez+\frac{w^2c}{\beta\pi^2}=0\quad\text{(comparison)}. \]

    For winding-one C5, e=w=1/2,beta=1 and c=cos(2*pi/5)<1/3. The native discriminant 1/4-1/(pi^2*c) is negative, whereas the comparison discriminant 1/4-c/pi^2 is positive. Both recover locally, but one has damped oscillatory linear modes and the other distinct negative real modes. Local pulse character therefore depends on the constitutive response, even when the stable geometry and storage agree. This is a tangent theorem, not a computed nonlinear response or a transfer of earlier memory records.

    11.7 Common exchange geometry, composition and a controlled common regime¶

    The two complete laws above belong to one mathematical family. This is an identity between declared models, not a derivation of a uniquely selected microscopic law. Keep the same support, clock, held capacities, coefficients and storage. Write M=diag(m_i) with finite positive entries on its stated domain. Then

    \[ \dot x=N(-eD^{-1}q+wMS),\qquad \dot\theta=(w/\beta)NMq \]

    gives Edot=-e*q^T*N*D^-1*q for every such mobility. In fact grad_x E=q and grad_theta E=-beta*S, so the two exchange work terms cancel by symmetry of NM. No derivative of M enters this first storage derivative, even if M depends on the state. Existence, regularity, symmetries and higher derivatives still require their own hypotheses. Equal loss functions do not imply equal storage histories along different trajectories.

    The native choice is m_i=1/H_i, while the normalized-sine comparison uses m_i=1/(pi*d_i). The common skew work structure therefore leaves precisely the constitutive mobility being investigated. It does not by itself give a Poisson bracket: the earlier Jacobi audit distinguishes that extra requirement from work cancellation.

    Geometric attenuation is not generally a change of clock¶

    On the native regular domain put z_i=R_i*exp(i*alpha_i). The exact ratio is

    \[ a_i=\frac{m_i^{\rm sine}}{m_i^{\rm native}} =\frac{R_i}{d_i}\operatorname{sinc}\alpha_i =\frac{H_i}{\pi d_i},\qquad 0<a_i\le1. \]

    Here R_i<=d_i and 0<sinc(alpha_i)<=1. Equality requires all neighbors to have the center's phase modulo a turn. The sine law scales both nodal exchange terms by a_i and leaves form diffusion unchanged. Different nodes generally have different attenuations.

    Even P2 with positive diffusion rules out a common clock interpretation. Set r=x_0-x_1, delta=theta_1-theta_0 in (-pi,pi) and c=nu_0+nu_1>0. In these two relative coordinates the native and sine velocity vectors are

    \[ F_n=\left(c[-er+w\delta/\pi], -\frac{cw r\delta}{\beta\pi\sin\delta}\right),\qquad F_s=\left(c[-er+w\sin\delta/\pi], -\frac{cw r}{\beta\pi}\right). \]

    Thus

    \[ \det(F_n,F_s)=\frac{c^2ewr^2}{\beta\pi} \left(1-\frac{\delta}{\sin\delta}\right)\ne0 \]

    when e>0,r!=0,0<|delta|<pi. A regular common clock change multiplies the whole vector field by one positive scalar, so it cannot identify these fields, even after removing common offsets. For e=0 on P2 only, F_s=sinc(delta)*F_n: there is a restricted orbit equivalence. It is not general on larger graphs; the preceding star's weighted-mean discriminator also applies at e=0 and forbids a positive common multiplier there.

    A controlled near-alignment regime¶

    Suppose all wrapped incident gaps satisfy |delta_ij|<=epsilon<pi/2. Then C_i>=d_i*cos(epsilon) and |alpha_i|<=epsilon. Since H_i/pi=C_i*tan(alpha_i)/alpha_i>=C_i with the removable value at zero,

    \[ \cos\epsilon\le a_i\le1,\qquad |g_i-s_i|\le\frac{(1-\cos\epsilon)\epsilon}{\pi}, \]
    \[ |\dot\theta_i^{\,n}-\dot\theta_i^{\,s}| \le\frac{w\nu_i|q_i|}{\beta\pi d_i}(\sec\epsilon-1),\qquad |\dot x_i^{\,n}-\dot x_i^{\,s}| \le\frac{w\nu_i}{\pi}(1-\cos\epsilon)\epsilon. \]

    These are instantaneous bounds at a shared state, not trajectory enclosures. The phase discrepancy is O(epsilon^2*|q_i|); the complete discrepancy is cubic only when form contrasts are also O(epsilon). A future error estimate must additionally bound the field Lipschitz constant and preserve the neighborhood throughout the comparison horizon. The common small-signal regime is derived; the ratio does not supply an autonomous switching rule.

    Composition exposes an independent premise¶

    11.8 Closed form balance can select primitive pressure composition¶

    Whole-graph balance is equivalent to an odd pairwise source¶

    Minimal regular information for every relative registration¶
