    Interaction at a marked boundary of a critical parent¶

    A critical parent can transmit its internal registration to an additional organized neighbor through an explicitly marked boundary. The inherited local state has three coordinates at zero contact. At positive contact two of these modes become stable, while one critical mode remains. The neighbor changes that mode's relaxation coefficient through the geometry of the joined state.

    This is a conditional, local theorem for supplied support and prepared organizations. It does not establish the occurrence of contact, acquisition of the additional neighbor, or entry of any original source into the local domain.

    Premise Declaration
    Parent The 54-node critical organization in the transition owner, at fixed \(0<\eta\le10^{-12}\) and \(\lambda=\lambda_c(\eta)\)
    Neighbor One additional C9 with the same internal carrier and constitutive law
    Boundary Nine same-slot edges from host child c=2 to the neighbor, all of supplied weight epsilon
    Dynamics Both rows use all edge weights; unit capacity, gamma, quarter factors and tau remain unchanged
    Local state All 63 forms and phases, zero total form, fixed total phase, and all fine coordinates
    Small-contact description A local three-dimensional center manifold at epsilon=0; constants and neighborhoods may depend on fixed eta
    Positive-contact description Local critical attraction at each fixed epsilon>0, with its own neighborhood and transverse rates
    Evidence Exact fields, projection, stationary elimination, center-manifold coefficients and static controls; no trajectories, scans or source replay

    1. Complete joined law and the cost of contact¶

    Keep the parent labels c=0,...,5 and add c=6 for the neighbor. Each child has j=0,...,8, with primitive node order 9*c+j. The parent's 144 edges retain their weights: 99 old internal/rim edges of weight one and 45 spokes of weight eta. The neighbor has nine internal edges of weight one. Add \(\{(2,j),(6,j)\}\), j=0,...,8, with weight epsilon. Write \(L_\epsilon=L_0+\epsilon L_{\rm b}\).

    All edges use the same potential and current:

    \[ V_\lambda(a)=1-\cos a+\lambda\frac{(1-\cos9a)^2}{6\,9^4}, \qquad j_\lambda(a)=\sin a+ \lambda\frac{\sin9a-\tfrac12\sin18a}{2187}, \qquad \lambda=\lambda_c(\eta). \tag{1} \]

    For the full phase potential \(U_\epsilon=\sum_e w_eV_\lambda(\theta_b-\theta_i)\), the exact equations are

    \[ x'=\frac{-L_\epsilon x-\gamma\nabla U_\epsilon}4,\qquad \theta'=\frac\gamma4L_\epsilon x,\qquad H_\epsilon=\frac12x^TL_\epsilon x+U_\epsilon,\qquad H_\epsilon'=-\frac14\|L_\epsilon x\|^2. \tag{2} \]

    Here \(\gamma=1/(1023\pi)\) and prime means the unchanged tau clock. Total form and total lifted phase are conserved. The form row supplies the pressure under held unit capacity. There is no external input or later event in these equations. Work on the zero-total-form leaf and a fixed-total-phase local chart.

    If the boundary is inserted without changing the primitive state, its storage jump is exactly

    \[ \Delta H=\epsilon\sum_{j=0}^8 \left[\frac12(x_{6,j}-x_{2,j})^2+ V_\lambda(\theta_{6,j}-\theta_{2,j})\right]\ge0 . \tag{3} \]

    This is supplied event work. Continuous loss is not its reservoir, and the event is not declared passive or autonomously selected. The jump is zero at the exactly aligned equilibrium, which does not by itself explain why the event occurs.

    The local identity chart uses alpha=2*pi/9, \(\theta_{c,j}=\alpha(j-4)+\Phi_c+q_{c,j}\), with \(\sum_jq_{c,j}=0\), and the parent's original compatible lifts. For example, the open neighborhood

    \[ \|h\|_2<6\eta,\qquad \|q\|_\infty<1/100,\qquad |t|<1/20,\qquad |d|<1/20 \tag{4} \]

    has the same parent marked class, neighbor winding one, and zero winding on the eight independent new mixed slot cycles. Here t and d are defined below; the old host shape is \(h_c=\Phi_c-\Phi_5-t-b_c\), with \(b_c=(2\pi/5)(c-2)\) and \(\sum_{c=0}^4h_c=0\). Every new boundary gap has magnitude less than \(1/20+1/120+6\eta+1/50<2/25\). Thus the boundary remains in a single circular chart with \(j_\lambda'>9/10\), using \(\cos a-2/243\le j_\lambda'(a)\). The old internal and rim bounds remain those of the transition owner. This is a local identity domain, not a source-entry certificate.

    2. Exact carrier and collective boundary equations¶

    The seven-child carrier \(q=0\), with forms constant within each child, is exactly invariant: internal currents cancel and every remaining edge joins equal slots. Its equations use the weighted seven-vertex graph in both rows, with the same quarter factors. This is a twelve-dimensional relative system, not a stationary approximation. The other 112 relative fine form/phase directions are retained when testing its local stability.

    Let \(X_c\) be the child mean form, and define

    \[ \begin{split} A&=\frac16\sum_{c=0}^5X_c,& B&=X_6,& r&=B-A,\\ \Omega&=\frac16\sum_{c=0}^5\Phi_c,&\Psi&=\Phi_6,&d&=\Psi-\Omega,\\ t&=\frac15\sum_{c=0}^4\Phi_c-\Phi_5,& \xi&=X_2-A,&s&=\Phi_2-\Omega . \end{split} \tag{5} \]

    The mean coordinates r and d are neighbor minus parent. On fixed global means, \(A=-r/7,\ B=6r/7\), and the phase means are \(\Omega=-d/7,\ \Psi=6d/7\), up to the fixed common origin. The actual marked port has form offset xi and phase offset s.

    On the carrier, direct summation of the complete rows gives

    \[ \begin{split} r'&=-\frac{7\epsilon}{24} [r-\xi+\gamma j_\lambda(d-s)],\\ d'&=\frac{7\gamma\epsilon}{24}(r-\xi),\\ t'&=\frac{3\gamma\eta}{2} \left(\frac15\sum_{c=0}^4X_c-X_5\right) -\frac{\gamma\epsilon}{20}(r-\xi). \end{split} \tag{6} \]

    For a noncarrier state, the first line uses the actual averaged boundary current

    \[ \overline j_{\rm b} =\frac19\sum_jj_\lambda(d-s+q_{6,j}-q_{2,j}) \tag{7} \]

    in place of \(j_\lambda(d-s)\); the other two mean rows are still exact as written. Thus (6) is not an unqualified closure in t,r,d. The zero fine means cancel the linear Taylor term, giving the own-law error bound

    \[ |\overline j_{\rm b}-j_\lambda(d-s)| \le\frac{J_2}{18}\|q_6-q_2\|_2^2,\qquad J_2=\frac{10}{9}. \tag{7a} \]

    The isolated parent's critical phase curve, at fixed parent mean, has host origins \(t/6+b_c+u_c(t)\) and donor origin \(-5t/6\). At t=0 the marked port c=2 has \(u_2(0)=0\) by reflection. Let w be that curve's tangent and put

    \[ N=\|w\|_2^2=\frac{15}{2}+9\|u_t(0)\|_2^2,\qquad h_\eta=w_{2,j}=\frac16+u_{2,t}(0),\qquad m_\eta=\frac{9\gamma^2}{4N}. \tag{8} \]

    The own-law bound \(\|u_t(0)\|_2<6\eta\) gives \(h_\eta>1/6-6\eta>1/7\). Extend w by zero on the neighbor. The critical spectral coordinate

    \[ \zeta=\frac{w^T(\theta_P-\theta_{P,*}+\gamma x_P)}N \tag{9} \]

    ignores the parent common means because w has zero sum. Projecting the actual joined rows gives

    \[ \zeta'=-\frac{\gamma^2}{4N}w^T\nabla U_P(\theta_P) +\epsilon m_\eta h_\eta\,\overline j_{\rm b}. \tag{10} \]

    Every boundary form-diffusion term cancels in this row. The cancellation requires the same boundary weights in both complete rows. Physical t instead has the explicit form term in (6); it is not identical to zeta on arbitrary states.

    3. Three local coordinates at zero contact¶

    At epsilon=0, fix the parent's critical state and the aligned neighbor carrier, with all forms zero. The full relative state has 124 dimensions. The parent contributes one center direction and 105 stable directions. The neighbor's eight relative phases and eight relative forms are stable, since its phase Hessian is \(\cos\alpha L_9>0\) on its relative space. The two independent component-mean differences r,d have zero rows. There are therefore three semisimple zero eigenvalues and 121 strictly stable eigenvalues. On the exact carrier the count is three center and nine stable directions.

    For each fixed eta, the finite-smoothness center construction used in the local parent dynamics applies after adjoining \(\epsilon'=0\). Choose a finite \(C^k\) manifold, k at least five, equivariant under reflection and with the product of the parent center curve and the two mean coordinates at epsilon=0. The local spectral gap exists at fixed eta; no lower bound uniform in eta is asserted. The analytic extension to negative epsilon needed for the parameter theorem is only a proof device, not an admitted negative-weight model.

    Use physical t,r,d as center coordinates and write z=(t,r,d). All three reverse under the reflection fixing c=2, while epsilon is unchanged. The aligned state is an exact equilibrium for every epsilon. The local rows are therefore odd in z. At zero contact the actual center port has

    \[ s=h_\eta t+O_\eta(t^3),\qquad \xi=O_\eta(t^3),\qquad \zeta=t+O_\eta(t^3). \tag{11} \]

    The equalities concern the invariant center curve. The stationary shape supplies the same tangent, but not an invariant trajectory. At nonzero epsilon the graph corrections in (11) are \(O_\eta(\epsilon\|z\|)\).

    Let \(b_\eta>0\) be the parent's critical cubic coefficient, already derived in its unchanged tau clock:

    \[ b_\eta=m_\eta A_5\eta^5 B_s(0,\mu_c,\eta),\qquad A_5=5\varphi^{10},\qquad 55<B_s(0,\mu_c,\eta)<70 . \tag{12} \]

    The notation A_5 here is the torque coefficient, not the mean A in (5). Put c=7/24. Projection of the full field gives

    \[ \begin{split} t'&=-b_\eta t^3+ \epsilon m_\eta h_\eta(d-h_\eta t)+R_t,\\ r'&=-c\epsilon[r+\gamma(d-h_\eta t)]+R_r,\\ d'&=c\gamma\epsilon r+R_d,\\ R_t&=O_\eta\!\left(|t|^5+ \epsilon\|z\|^3+\epsilon^2\|z\|\right),\\ R_r,R_d&=O_\eta\!\left( \epsilon\|z\|^3+\epsilon^2\|z\|\right). \end{split} \tag{13} \]

    For the first row, the parent linear field vanishes under its left center vector. Consequently a first-order correction to the stable graph cannot add a term linear in epsilon*z to (10). The direct boundary term is the one displayed in (13), since \(j_\lambda'(0)=1\). Changing from zeta to physical t changes the derivative only by \(O_\eta(\epsilon^2\|z\|+\epsilon\|z\|^3+|t|^5)\), using (11). This explains why the explicit raw form term in (6) is absent from the leading center t row; it has not been dropped from the full law. The other rows follow from (6), and reflection excludes quadratic state remainders.

    The leading linear contact matrix, in the order (t,r,d), is

    \[ \epsilon \begin{pmatrix} -m_\eta h_\eta^2&0&m_\eta h_\eta\\ c\gamma h_\eta&-c&-c\gamma\\ 0&c\gamma&0 \end{pmatrix}. \tag{14} \]

    It has one zero eigenvalue and two with negative real part: the remaining polynomial is

    \[ s^2+(c+m_\eta h_\eta^2)s+ c m_\eta h_\eta^2+c^2\gamma^2 . \tag{15} \]

    The two additional decay rates are of order epsilon at fixed eta. Eliminating these mean coordinates at epsilon=0 would erase genuine neutral state and cannot give a uniform instantaneous scalar law.

    At epsilon=0 the leading full-state center initialization is \((\Pi_\eta(\theta_P,x_P),r,d)\), where \(\Pi_\eta=\zeta+O_\eta(\|(\delta\theta_P,x_P)\|^2)\). Here the parent map consumes its mean-subtracted phase and form coordinates; the linear w projection already performs that removal. For small contact, the three-dimensional slow spectral subspace is separated from the other 121 directions and changes by O_eta(epsilon). Its linear initialization, expressed in physical coordinates on that subspace, is \(P_{3,\epsilon}\delta Z\), with \(P_{3,\epsilon}=P_{3,0}+O_\eta(\epsilon)\) and \(P_{3,0}\delta Z=(\zeta,r,d)\). The corresponding nonlinear tracking initialization retains the discarded-state correction in all three coordinates. In particular, availability of r and d as physical coordinates on the invariant graph does not make its tracking initial means equal to the raw means of an arbitrary off-manifold state. No uniform nonlinear initialization error, neighborhood or tracking rate as eta or epsilon tends to zero is asserted.

    4. A fixed positive contact changes the critical relaxation¶

    There is a stronger exact local conclusion at every fixed epsilon>0. At the aligned state the new gaps are zero and \(j_\lambda'(0)=1\). Its phase Hessian is

    \[ M_{63}=\operatorname{diag}(M_{P,*},\cos\alpha L_9) +\epsilon L_{\rm b}. \tag{16} \]

    All three terms are positive semidefinite. Before adding the boundary, their kernel consists of the parent critical tangent and the two component rotations. On the fixed-global-phase space, the boundary eliminates one linear combination. Its remaining critical vector is

    \[ \begin{split} \widetilde w_P&=w-\frac{h_\eta}{7}\mathbf1_P,& \widetilde w_N&=\frac{6h_\eta}{7}\mathbf1_N,\\ \widetilde N&=\|\widetilde w\|^2 =N+\frac{54}{7}h_\eta^2. \end{split} \tag{17} \]

    Its two endpoints agree on every new edge. Conversely, equality on those edges imposes d=h_eta*t on the two old relative kernel directions, leaving only (17). Thus the relative phase Hessian has 61 positive directions and one zero. The complete 124-dimensional pencil

    \[ s^2L_\epsilon^{-1}+\frac s4I+\frac{\gamma^2}{16}M_{63} \tag{18} \]

    has one simple zero eigenvalue and 123 eigenvalues with negative real part, by the scalar quadratic and kernel Schur-complement arguments applied to this actual joined Hessian.

    The stationary normal elimination can also be identified exactly. In the local chart, the 56 fine phase directions remain positive. The old 48 directions and the neighbor's eight directions already have positive internal curvature; new edges add nonnegative curvature because their gaps satisfy (4). At the carrier their fine gradients vanish. Integrating the fine Hessian therefore forces q=0 at any nearby normal stationary state.

    At fixed physical t, neighbor stationarity then requires \(j_\lambda(d-s)=0\). The strict local monotonicity of this current gives d=s. All boundary currents vanish, and the parent's projected host equations uniquely recover its unchanged u(t). Let \(s_*(t)=t/6+u_2(t)\). With global phase fixed, the joined stationary curve is exactly

    \[ \widetilde\Theta_P(t)=\Theta_P(t)-\frac{s_*(t)}7\mathbf1_P, \qquad \widetilde\Theta_N(t)= \alpha(j-4)+\frac{6s_*(t)}7+\text{fixed common origin}. \tag{19} \]

    Its boundary energy is zero. Its reduced phase potential is the same \(9\mathcal W(t)\) as the parent, plus the constant internal neighbor potential. The positive normal Hessian ensures local uniqueness of this normal elimination at fixed t.

    Apply the invariant-center calculation to this joined system. The normalized left vector is \((\widetilde w,\gamma\widetilde w)/\widetilde N\). As in the parent derivation, the actual invariant curve agrees with the stationary curve through degree two; its cubic normal correction is annihilated by \(\widetilde w^TM_{63}=0\). The resulting cubic coefficient is

    \[ t'=-b_{\eta,\epsilon}^{\rm joined}t^3+O_{\eta,\epsilon}(t^5), \qquad b_{\eta,\epsilon}^{\rm joined} =b_\eta\frac{N}{N+(54/7)h_\eta^2}>0 . \tag{20} \]

    For each fixed positive epsilon this coefficient has the stated exact value, independent of epsilon. Neighborhoods and transverse decay rates do depend on epsilon. Since u_t=O(eta),

    \[ \frac{b_{\eta,\epsilon}^{\rm joined}}{b_\eta} \longrightarrow\frac{35}{36}\quad\hbox{as }\eta\to0 \tag{21} \]

    along the proved critical family, without a uniform dynamical-limit claim. The effect comes from the larger tangent norm in (17); the stationary quartic potential is unchanged. This is an inherited change in relaxation, not a physical mass identification or a change to primitive capacity.

    5. Joined initialization, identity and asymptotic decay¶

    The positive normal Hessian and the unchanged strictly positive quartic reduced potential make the aligned joined state a strict local minimum of H_epsilon on the fixed-mean leaf. At each fixed positive eta and epsilon, a small compact sublevel is invariant and lies inside the identity chart. Storage loss and connected support force x=0 on its zero-loss invariant set; local stationary classification leaves only the critical equilibrium. Thus the full joined state is Lyapunov stable and asymptotically attracting, including all fine perturbations admitted locally.

    The contact work can be included in an explicit sufficient admission condition. Choose a small closed full relative ball \(\mathcal B\) inside the identity chart and this strict-minimum neighborhood, and set

    \[ \mathcal E_{\eta,\epsilon} =\min_{\partial\mathcal B} [H_\epsilon(Z)-H_\epsilon(Z_*)]>0 . \tag{21a} \]

    If the primitive state immediately before the event is already inside this ball and

    \[ H_0(Z_0)-H_0(Z_*)+\Delta H(Z_0) <\mathcal E_{\eta,\epsilon}, \tag{21b} \]

    the joined trajectory cannot leave the ball. The reference has zero boundary work, so \(H_\epsilon(Z_*)=H_0(Z_*)\), and (21b) is exactly the post-event sublevel condition with the paid jump (3). This is a local, sufficient admission; neither a numerical ball radius nor a global low-energy capture criterion is supplied.

    Finite-smoothness center reduction and stable tracking apply with 123 stable directions. The same graph-slope and quadratic-defect argument as in the parent initialization proof gives

    \[ \begin{split} \Pi_{\eta,\epsilon}^{\rm joined}(\delta\theta,x) &=\frac{\widetilde w^T(\delta\theta+\gamma x)} {\widetilde N} +O_{\eta,\epsilon}(\|(\delta\theta,x)\|^2)\\ &=\frac{N\zeta+(54h_\eta/7)(d+\gamma r)} {N+(54/7)h_\eta^2} +O_{\eta,\epsilon}(\|(\delta\theta,x)\|^2). \end{split} \tag{22} \]

    The linear identity uses the physical component means, so it retains the neighbor's form and phase rather than replacing them with their stationary values.
