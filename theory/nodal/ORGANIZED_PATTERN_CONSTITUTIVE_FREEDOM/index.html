    Constitutive freedom in the interaction of matched organized patterns¶

    The same organized targets, target storage values, complete tangent and quadratic dynamics can support opposite local nonlinear interactions. This owner proves that statement for the existing positive-loss three-C9 support. It supplies an explicit analytic alternative to the sine potential; it neither selects that alternative nor repeats a finite response calculation. An own-law acquisition argument below also places the original isolated-C9 source families in their capture neighborhoods for every member of this potential family, at the original common time and error budgets.

    The separate source-path robustness theorem also carries the original uncertain interior Cayley corridor through its phase passage into winding-one capture for every member of this family. Its own-law current and storage bounds make that formation outcome non-discriminating. The nominal interaction distinction below retains its scope; neither formation result transfers it to an acquired residual state or a finite response.

    The carried-state observation below closes that separate obligation for the two endpoint laws and the original class-one ramp sources. Own-law recovery, prescribed attachment work and a real nonlinear remainder give opposite finite measured signs under an explicit mathematical error allowance. No laboratory resolution or physical clock is inferred from that allowance. The scalar calibration theorem then separates preservation of those signs from separation of complete recorded observations under a common independently specified sensor.

    The earlier mobility counterfamily and smooth local continuation already establish other nonselection results. Here mobility stays constant, loss stays positive, and a target-matched analytic potential changes the distinct-neighbor interaction of the present joined objects. The observation and supplied interventions retain their original coordinates.

    Complete state, potential and reciprocal law¶

    Keep all 27 nodes, the 27 internal cycle edges and the two bridges (4,13) and (13,22). All edges have unit weight. Node order is 9*c+j, with component index c=0,1,2 and local index j=0,...,8. Let \(L\) be the unnormalized graph Laplacian,

    \[ D=\operatorname{diag}(d_i),\quad A=D^{-1}L,\quad (d_4,d_{13},d_{22})=(3,4,3),\quad \sum_i d_i=58. \tag{1} \]

    The complete state is signed real form \(x\in\mathbb R^{27}\) and 27 primitive circular phases, represented by continuous lifts when taking derivatives and means. Every capacity is held at one. Support, loss, mobility and clock are held; no controller, support selector or capacity evolution is inferred.

    For \(0\le\lambda\le1\), define

    \[ \begin{split} W(\delta)&=\frac{(1-\cos9\delta)^2}{6\,9^4},\\ V_\lambda(\delta)&=1-\cos\delta+\lambda W(\delta),\\ j_\lambda(\delta)=V_\lambda'(\delta) &=\sin\delta+\frac{\lambda}{2187} \left(\sin9\delta-\frac12\sin18\delta\right). \end{split}\tag{2} \]

    The potential is even, nonnegative, real analytic and \(2\pi\)-periodic. It vanishes at zero. Its current is odd and periodic. The parameter and the ninth harmonic are declared constitutive choices, constructed to test this support's target constraints; they are not inferred universal constants.

    Set \(U_\lambda(\theta)=\sum_{\{i,j\}}V_\lambda(\theta_j-\theta_i)\),

    \[ q=Lx,\qquad S_{\lambda,i}=\sum_{j\sim i}j_\lambda(\theta_j-\theta_i),\qquad \nabla U_\lambda=-S_\lambda. \tag{3} \]

    Use the same structural normalization

    \[ e=\frac{1023}{1024},\quad w=\frac1{1024},\quad \tau=e t,\quad \gamma=\frac{w}{e\pi}=\frac1{1023\pi}. \]

    In the original clock and the structural clock respectively, the complete rows are

    \[ \begin{array}{ll} \displaystyle\frac{dx}{dt}=-eD^{-1}q+\frac w\pi D^{-1}S_\lambda, &\displaystyle\frac{d\theta}{dt}=\frac w\pi D^{-1}q,\\[3pt] x'=-Ax+\gamma D^{-1}S_\lambda, &\theta'=\gamma Ax. \end{array}\tag{4} \]

    Thus the pressure for the held unit-capacity nodal row is specified by the first original-time expression, independently of the evaluated response. Both evolution rows transform under the clock change. All coefficients use the adopted structural units: \([p]=[x]/([\nu_f][t])\). No laboratory time, energy unit, inverse-mass interpretation or physical identity is supplied.

    For

    \[ H_\lambda=\frac12x^TLx+U_\lambda(\theta), \]

    the constant reciprocal exchange block is

    \[ \begin{pmatrix}x'\\\theta'\end{pmatrix} =\left[ \begin{pmatrix}0&-\gamma D^{-1}\\\gamma D^{-1}&0\end{pmatrix} -\begin{pmatrix}D^{-1}&0\\0&0\end{pmatrix} \right]\nabla H_\lambda. \tag{5} \]

    The skew part cancels between the two rows. Consequently, on the actual full state,

    \[ H_\lambda'=-(Lx)^TD^{-1}(Lx)\le0,\qquad \frac{dH_\lambda}{dt}=-e(Lx)^TD^{-1}(Lx). \tag{6} \]

    The same positive loss law is retained, not merely its value on a target. Odd edge currents and Laplacian row sums also give

    \[ (\mathbf1^TDx)'=0,\qquad (\mathbf1^TD\theta)'=0. \tag{7} \]

    These are lifted means in the declared coordinates, not circular-resultant angles. Adding the same constant to all forms or to all phases preserves the dynamics and storage differences.

    Global admission and constitutive derivative bounds¶

    For every real gap and integer \(r\ge0\), differentiating (2) gives

    \[ |j_\lambda^{(r)}(\delta)| \le1+\frac{\lambda}{2187} \left(9^r+\frac{18^r}{2}\right). \tag{8} \]

    In particular, \(|j_\lambda'|\le1+2\lambda/243\). Thus the complete real-lift field (4) is globally Lipschitz: its form-row difference is bounded by \(2\|\Delta x\|_\infty+ 2\gamma(1+2\lambda/243)\|\Delta\theta\|_\infty\), and its phase-row difference by \(2\gamma\|\Delta x\|_\infty\). It has a unique global solution from every finite full state, in either time direction, between the explicitly supplied events. This does not assert global convergence to one winding target.

    Equation (8) supplies real derivative bounds for this law. Although (2) also extends analytically to complex phases, its ninth and eighteenth harmonics have different complex growth from sine. The earlier sine Cauchy tails, source bounds, finite-time intervals and error budgets do not transfer without a new estimate.

    Target storage and full local dynamics agree¶

    Let \(\alpha=2\pi/9\), choose mediator class \(k\in\{1,2\}\), and use the nominal joined target

    \[ x=0,\qquad \Theta_{k,9c+j}=k_c\alpha(j-4),\qquad (k_0,k_1,k_2)=(1,k,1). \tag{9} \]

    Each cycle's principal edge gaps are \(\pm k_c\alpha\); the bridges have gap zero. The closing-edge lifts differ by known full turns, which do not change the periodic current or its derivatives. Direct differentiation gives

    \[ \begin{split} W''(\delta)&=\frac{\cos9\delta-\cos18\delta}{243},\\ W'''(\delta)&=\frac{-\sin9\delta+2\sin18\delta}{27},\\ W''''(\delta)&=\frac{-\cos9\delta+4\cos18\delta}{3}. \end{split}\tag{10} \]

    At every \(\delta=2\pi m/9\), \(m\in\mathbb Z\),

    \[ W=W'=W''=W'''=0,\qquad W''''=1. \tag{11} \]

    Therefore both classes have the same equilibrium, target storage value, gradient and full phase Hessian for every \(\lambda\). In particular,

    \[ H_{*,k}=18(1-\cos\alpha)+9(1-\cos k\alpha),\qquad L_{c,k}=\nabla^2U_\lambda(\Theta_k) \]

    are independent of \(\lambda\). Opposite internal currents cancel at every cycle node, and both bridge currents vanish. The full 54-coordinate tangent is

    \[ J_k=\begin{pmatrix}-A&-\gamma D^{-1}L_{c,k}\\ \gamma A&0\end{pmatrix}. \tag{12} \]

    The entire quadratic field is also identical: it consumes \(j_\lambda''=V_\lambda'''\), whose target values do not change. The cubic field consumes \(j_\lambda'''=V_\lambda''''\), and does change. No hidden quadratic mode is removed by this matching statement.

    A locally re-admitted identity and exact event work¶

    Let \(y=\theta-\Theta_k\), \(P_0=I-\mathbf1\mathbf1^T/27\), and \(Z^2=\|P_0x\|_2^2+\|P_0y\|_2^2\). This is a quotient radius, not an operation that resets the state or its weighted means. Within \(Z\le r=1/12\), every edge residual has magnitude at most \(\sqrt2r\). The graph diameter is ten: four cycle edges, the two bridges, then four cycle edges suffice between any pair. For any real vector \(v\), its range and the variance bound give \(\|P_0v\|_2^2\le27\operatorname{range}(v)^2/4 \le(135/2)v^TLv\), by Cauchy-Schwarz along a shortest path joining its extreme values. Thus \(\lambda_2(L)\ge2/135\), independently of the phase law. The existing angular bound is \(\cos(4\pi/9+\sqrt2r)>1/20\). It also follows from \(\pi>31/10\), \(\sqrt2<17/12\) and \(\sin(13/240)>13/240-(13/240)^3/6>1/20\).

    Writing \(c=\cos9\delta\), equation (10) gives \(W''=(1+c-2c^2)/243\ge-2/243\). Hence throughout this chart,

    \[ V_\lambda''>\frac1{20}-\frac2{243} =\frac{203}{4860}>\frac1{25}. \tag{13} \]

    Integrating the Hessian along the segment from the target yields

    \[ H_\lambda-H_{*,k} \ge\frac12x^TLx+\frac1{50}y^TLy \ge\frac{Z^2}{3375}. \tag{14} \]

    An initial state inside the chart with relative storage below \(r^2/3375=1/486000\) cannot reach its boundary under unforced flow, by (6) and (14). The target is the unique phase critical orbit there. On a fixed weighted-mean leaf the trapped sublevel is compact. The largest invariant subset with zero loss has \(Lx=0\); invariance then requires \(D^{-1}\nabla U_\lambda\) to be common across nodes. Its degree-weighted sum is zero, so that gradient vanishes. Thus LaSalle's argument gives local convergence to the corresponding target orbit. No common form or phase is forced back to zero.

    For any prescribed form jump \(f\), phases do not jump, and each law has the exact work

    \[ \Delta H_\lambda=f^TLx^-+\frac12f^TLf. \tag{15} \]

    For simultaneous donor and receiver jumps \(f=ae_4+be_{22}\), \(L_{4,22}=0\), so (15) is \(a(Lx^-)_4+b(Lx^-)_{22}+3(a^2+b^2)/2\). It is the same functional in each model, but later pre-event states need not coincide. The weighted form mean changes by \(3(a+b)/58\), with the phase mean unchanged.

    From the prescribed nominal source (9), the existing amplitudes \(a=b=m=7/10000\) give work \(3m^2<1/486000\) and \(Z_+^2=50m^2/27<r^2\). The single-input and zero-input branches also satisfy these bounds. All four branches consequently retain the joined quotient identity for both laws, without borrowing an acquisition theorem. Any support change needs its own edge-storage work; continuous loss is not an undeclared event reserve.

    A changed interaction of the same prescribed organized states¶

    Use the same complete nominal pre-input state (9) in four histories with form jumps \(0,ae_4,be_{22},ae_4+be_{22}\). Observe the mediator form and define, as in the distinct-neighbor owner,

    \[ M_{\lambda,k}(a,b,T)=x_{13}^{11}(T)-x_{13}^{10}(T) -x_{13}^{01}(T)+x_{13}^{00}(T). \tag{16} \]

    Immediately after the joint jump, the phase slopes are \(\theta'_4=\gamma a\), \(\theta'_{22}=\gamma b\), \(\theta'_{13}=-\gamma(a+b)/4\), and \(\theta'_{12}=\theta'_{14}=0\). Thus the four incident gap slopes into node 13 are \(\gamma(5a+b)/4\), \(\gamma(a+5b)/4\), and twice \(\gamma(a+b)/4\). The first two edges are bridges; the other two are internal mediator edges.

    The form derivatives through order two are linear in the impulses. The third derivative is at most quadratic. In the fourth derivative, the only cubic contribution is the current's third derivative applied to three initial gap slopes; every other term is at most quadratic. Port-fixing ring reflection with simultaneous form/phase-deviation sign reversal is a symmetry of every even potential (2). At the nominal source, the central output is odd in \((a,b)\), so its quadratic contributions vanish. Hidden quadratic modes generally remain nonzero.

    The mixed bridge cubes sum to \(90ab(a+b)\), and the two mixed internal cubes sum to \(6ab(a+b)\). Including the degree-four normalization, gap denominators and \(4!\) gives the complete nonlinear local onset

    \[ \begin{split} M_{\lambda,k}(a,b,T) &=\frac{\gamma^4ab(a+b)}{1024} [15j_\lambda'''(0)+j_\lambda'''(k\alpha)]T^4+O(T^5)\\ &=\boxed{\frac{16\lambda-15-\cos(k\alpha)}{1024} \gamma^4ab(a+b)T^4+O(T^5)}. \end{split}\tag{17} \]

    For fixed positive \(a,b\), the sine member \(\lambda=0\) has negative onset, whereas \(\lambda=1\) has positive onset, since \(1-\cos(k\alpha)>0\) for both classes. Analyticity supplies a positive local time neighborhood in which the corresponding exact signs hold; (17) supplies no declared numerical horizon or measurement resolution.

    The leading coefficient vanishes at \(\lambda_{\mathrm{crit},k}=(15+\cos k\alpha)/16\). Since \(\cos2\alpha<\cos\alpha\), \(\lambda_{\mathrm{crit},2}<\lambda_{\mathrm{crit},1}\). Between these two values the classes have opposite local orientations under one law. For example, the fixed rational \(\lambda=31/32\) gives the factor \(1/2-\cos(k\alpha)\): it is negative for class one and positive for class two because \(\alpha<\pi/3<2\alpha\). At \(\lambda=1\), the bridge contribution vanishes and the entire positive onset comes from the internal mediator edges. Omitting those edges would therefore give a false null. Within this particular family, the normalized class-one-minus-class-two coefficient remains \((\cos2\alpha-\cos\alpha)/1024\); that robustness is not asserted for every matched-target potential. At a critical value only the order-four coefficient is known to vanish; exact additivity does not follow. Zero input in either channel cancels (16) for every common source. At the nominal source, \(a=-b\) is also an exact null by outer-ring exchange and the sign symmetry.

    Own-law acquisition from the original isolated C9 sources¶

    This section changes support explicitly to one isolated unit C9, with nodes \(0,\ldots,8\), edges \(j--(j+1\bmod9)\), degree matrix \(D=2I\), capacities one and the same \(e,w,\gamma\) and clocks as (4). It does not apply the joined graph's spectral bound to an isolated component or identify the two supports' reached states. The original two-class preparation supplies the following unchanged experiment for each law:

    • Classes \(k=1,2\), rational slope \(m=(2046/9)(355/113)^2\), initial form \(x_j(0)=km(j-4)+\xi_j^x\), and initial phase lifts \(\theta_j(0)=\xi_j^\theta\).
    • Bounds \(|\xi_j^x|\le\rho_x=10^{-10}\) and \(|\xi_j^\theta|\le\rho_\theta=10^{-10}\), with separate exact zero sums \(\sum_j\xi_j^x=\sum_j\xi_j^\theta=0\). Each class has a complete sixteen-dimensional relative source neighborhood, not an independently selectable eighteen-dimensional ambient box.
    • Acquisition time \(T=100\) in \(\tau\), original time \(102400/1023\), radius \(r=1/12\), and common whole-source storage budget \(10^9\). No input, support event, state reset or coefficient change occurs.
    • Target \(x^*=0\), \(\theta_{k,j}^*=k\alpha(j-4)\), with \(\alpha=2\pi/9\). Targets label the endpoint comparison, not replacement endpoint states.

    Write \(A=L/2\), \(z=\gamma x\), \(\eta=\gamma^2\), and \(f_\lambda=S_\lambda/2\). The complete scaled law is

    \[ z'=-Az+\eta f_\lambda(\theta),\qquad \theta'=Az,\qquad (\theta+z)'=\eta f_\lambda(\theta). \tag{18} \]

    Odd currents preserve both zero means. The full real-lift field is globally Lipschitz by (8), including nonacute states during acquisition. Continuous lifts are therefore retained through any winding changes.

    For \(P_9=I-\mathbf1\mathbf1^T/9\) and the norm \(\|u\|_{2I}=\sqrt2\|u\|_2\), the C9 graph gives the same law-independent semigroup gap \(g=1/5\): \(\|e^{-A\tau}P_9\|_{2I}\le e^{-g\tau}\). Indeed \(1-\cos(2\pi/9)>1/5\), so \(\lambda_2(L)>2/5\), while \(\lambda_{\max}(L)\le4\). The forcing and Hessian constants must instead be re-admitted:

    \[ \begin{aligned} J_\lambda&=1+\lambda/1458,& \|f_\lambda(\theta)\|_{2I}&\le F_\lambda=\sqrt{18}\,J_\lambda,\\ u_\lambda&=1+2\lambda/243,& \nabla^2U_\lambda(\theta)&\preceq u_\lambda L . \end{aligned}\tag{19} \]
