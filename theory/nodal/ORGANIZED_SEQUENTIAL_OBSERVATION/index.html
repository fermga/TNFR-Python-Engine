    Observing outer winding on one continuing organization¶

    Declared single-history observation contract¶

    The matched-source inverse identifies a collective winding but assumes eleven continuations of one initial state. This gate asks whether one prescribed observation history can remove that premise while retaining unknown background motion and probe backaction.

    Item Fixed choice and scope

    Clock tau=(1023/1024)*t; elapsed tau starts at the actual unknown initial source; all intervals and both continuous rows use this clock
    Initial class Exactly the earlier admitted class: every abs(x_i)<=X=1/100; theta[c,j]=2*pi*(j-4)/9+Phi_c+z[c,j], unknown lifted origins Phi, zero child-mean z and every abs(z[c,j])<=zeta=1e-6; principal adjacent origin gaps have magnitude at most A=7/5 and common winding w in {-1,0,1}

    The analytical task is to justify the sequential cancellation under the complete rows, not to substitute sequential samples into the earlier matched-source formula. A successful result would remove a mathematical preparation dependency while retaining externally supplied interventions, known law and clock, and an independently uncalibrated observation model.

    The declaration above was committed as 8c0d4cca before assessment. The following proof evaluates its fixed rational bounds without integrating a trajectory or changing the protocol after observing the result.

    1. One actual history and the initial identity¶

    Write \(\psi_j=2\pi(j-4)/9\), and let \(a_c=\operatorname{wrap}(\Phi_{c+1}-\Phi_c)\) denote the initial child-origin gaps. The initial fine boundary gaps and their moments are

    \[ d_{c,j}^0=a_c+z_{c+1,j}-z_{c,j},\qquad C_c^0=\frac19\sum_j\cos d_{c,j}^0,\qquad S_c^0=\frac19\sum_j\sin d_{c,j}^0. \tag{1} \]

    The actual initial fine errors telescope around every outer slot cycle; each has sum \(2\pi w\), with the same unknown integer \(w\in\{-1,0,1\}\). The internal C9 cycles have winding one. These are identities of all 45 actual phase coordinates, not five assigned labels. The matched-source state argument establishes this initial class and includes both previously acquired sources without resetting their residuals. The present argument does not require the larger class to have a demonstrated acquisition mechanism.

    Let \(g\) be the upper endpoint of the shared outward interval for \(\gamma\), and set

    \[ T=20h,\qquad B=X+gT,\qquad D=4gBT. \tag{2} \]

    Heat contraction for \(-L/4\), together with \(\|S(\theta)\|_\infty\le4\), gives \(\|x(t)\|_\infty\le X+gt\le B\) along the entire history. Phase events leave x unchanged. The full phase row gives \(\|\dot\theta\|_\infty\le2gB\), hence every edge accumulates continuous phase drift of magnitude at most D. Remove only the explicit cumulative commanded offset from the phase lift; its remaining drift is continuous through all fifteen events. At the start of every block the cumulative command is zero, but the evolved form and phase drift remain.

    For a block starting at \(s_c=4ch\), let \(C_c^s,S_c^s\) be its actual boundary moments before any of that block's events. The global Lipschitz bounds for sine and cosine imply

    \[ |C_c^s-C_c^0|\le D,\qquad |S_c^s-S_c^0|\le D. \tag{3} \]

    Thus the proof can transport local information back to the original moments. It does not identify the starts of different blocks or reuse a state discarded by an earlier observation.

    2. Exact cancellation and the unobserved comparison¶

    For one block let \(Y_k\) be its five actual receiver mean forms, temporarily without readout error. In endpoint coordinates the two fixed combinations are

    \[ \begin{split} D_C&=(Y_0-4Y_1+6Y_2-4Y_3+Y_4)/3,\\ D_S&=Y_0-2Y_1+2Y_3-Y_4. \end{split}\tag{4} \]

    Both annihilate every quadratic polynomial in time. Their raw coefficient absolute sums are \(16/3\) and 6, respectively, giving exactly the declared worst-case reading-error bounds. A common endpoint is one reading, even when it occurs in two adjacent increments.

    For the proof alone, continue the block's actual initial state without its three local events for a time \(H=4h\). Denote this hypothetical reference by \((\bar x,\bar\theta)\), and its receiver mean form by b. It is not measured and is never passed to the inverse. Its form bound is also B: it begins with \(\|x(s_c)\|_\infty\le X+gs_c\), and \(s_c+H\le T\). In particular, each block's reference retains all history accumulated before that block.

    The degree-four full rows imply

    \[ \begin{split} \|\dot{\bar x}\|_\infty&\le2B+g,\\ \|\ddot{\bar x}\|_\infty&\le4B+2g+4g^2B,\\ |b'''|\le\|\bar x'''\|_\infty &\le M_3:=8B+4g+16g^2B+4g^3+16g^3B^2. \end{split}\tag{5} \]

    Indeed, each edge phase rate is at most \(4gB\), each edge phase acceleration at most \(4g(2B+g)\), and differentiating the four sine terms gives \(\|S''\|_\infty\le64g^2B^2+16g(2B+g)\). Together with \(\|L/4\|_\infty=2\), these yield (5). No equilibrium or constant background is assumed.

    Writing \(E_h\) for time translation by h, the first combination on b is \((E_h-1)^4b/3\), and the second is \(-(E_h+1)(E_h-1)^3b\). A third finite difference is an integral of \(b'''\) over a cube of side h. Bounding the two third differences in each expression therefore gives

    \[ |D_C[b]|\le\frac23M_3h^3,\qquad |D_S[b]|\le2M_3h^3. \tag{6} \]

    This is the residual unknown background motion after the exact algebraic cancellation. It differs from the matched-source cancellation, where the same independently measured endpoint baseline cancels directly.

    3. Complete nonlinear backaction within each block¶

    At local time t let \(q(t)\in\{0,+\epsilon,-\epsilon,0\}\) be the commanded amplitude in its four windows. Write

    \[ y=x-\bar x,\qquad v=\theta-\bar\theta-q(t)k_c. \tag{7} \]

    The explicit event is removed from v, so both y and v are continuous and start at zero. Their differential equations, away from the event times, retain the full form diffusion and phase feedback. The phase row yields \(\|v(t)\|_\infty\le2g\int_0^t\|y(r)\|_\infty\,dr\). Every edge of the commanded offset has magnitude at most \(\epsilon\), and the global sine Lipschitz bound yields

    \[ \|y(t)\|_\infty \le g\epsilon t+2g\int_0^t\|v(r)\|_\infty\,dr. \tag{8} \]

    If \(2g^2H^2\le1/2\), a supremum argument at every time up to H gives

    \[ \|y(t)\|_\infty\le2g\epsilon t,\qquad \|v(t)\|_\infty\le2g^2\epsilon t^2. \tag{9} \]

    These estimates do not reset y or v at an event. In particular, setting q back to zero in the fourth window leaves the accumulated backaction.

    Freeze only the reference phase at the block's initial state when defining the leading vector slope

    \[ F_q=\frac\gamma4 \{S(\bar\theta(0)+qk_c)-S(\bar\theta(0))\}. \tag{10} \]

    The function \(\sin(a+u)-\sin a\) has derivative with respect to a of magnitude at most \(|u|\). The reference edge drift at time t is at most \(4gBt\); the additional phase v has edge magnitude at most \(2\|v\|_\infty\). Using all four incident edges and (9),

    \[ \|\dot y-F_q\|_\infty \le J(t):=4g\epsilon(1+gB)t+4g^3\epsilon t^2. \tag{11} \]

    The first term includes \(\|Ly/4\|_\infty\), not just the pressure perturbation. This keeps the complete finite evolution rather than replacing a mean-form increment by an onset derivative.

    For the receiver child \(c+1\), the mean of (10) at the two nonzero amplitudes is exactly

    \[ A_\pm=\frac\gamma4 \{\mathord\pm\sin\epsilon\,C_c^s +(1-\cos\epsilon)S_c^s\}. \tag{12} \]

    The four leading mean slopes are consequently \(0,A_+,A_-,0\). Integrate (11) using the absolute increment weights \((1/3,1,1,1/3)\) and \((1,1,1,1)\). Their first time moments are \((16/3)h^2\) and \(8h^2\), and their second time moments are \((116/9)h^3\) and \((64/3)h^3\). The resulting backaction remainders are

    \[ \begin{split} E_C&=\frac{64}{3}g\epsilon(1+gB)h^2 +\frac{464}{9}g^3\epsilon h^3,\\ E_S&=32g\epsilon(1+gB)h^2 +\frac{256}{3}g^3\epsilon h^3. \end{split}\tag{13} \]

    This argument applies independently to every block's actual carried start; the uniform constants retain all earlier effects through B and D.

    4. Initial quadratures and uniform winding identification¶

    Define positive outward response factors \(f_C=g h[\sin\epsilon]^+/2\) and \(f_S=g h[1-\cos\epsilon]^+/2\), where a plus denotes the interval upper endpoint. Combining the background terms (6), the backaction terms (13), the exact reading coefficients, and the transport bound (3) gives

    \[ \begin{split} D_C&=\frac{\gamma h\sin\epsilon}{2}C_c^0+\delta_C, &|\delta_C|&\le b_C:=E_C+\frac23M_3h^3 +\frac{16}{3}\eta+f_CD,\\ D_S&=\frac{\gamma h(1-\cos\epsilon)}2S_c^0+\delta_S, &|\delta_S|&\le b_S:=E_S+2M_3h^3+6\eta+f_SD. \end{split}\tag{14} \]

    The two inferred moments are now explicitly those of the original unknown state. Later blocks need no pretended common preparation. Both errors are uniform over all allowed initial forms, fine phases and reading errors.

    The initial-moment inverse therefore applies with these new error bounds: scale the raw combinations by \(K_C=2/([\gamma]h[\sin\epsilon])\) and \(K_S=2/([\gamma]h[1-\cos\epsilon])\), and intersect with the original cosine prior \([c_0,1]\), \(c_0=[\cos(A+2\zeta)]^->0\), and sine prior \([-1,1]\). The original annulus \(\cos(2\zeta)\le|C_c^0+iS_c^0|\le1\) remains a necessary check because (14) transports to the initial moments. Every raw form reading must also have magnitude at most \(B+\eta\).

    Take the outward argument of each surviving moment rectangle, enlarge by \(2\zeta\) on each side, and clip to \([-A,A]\). This encloses each original origin gap \(a_c\). Sum the five intervals and retain the candidate sectors whose outward \(2\pi w\) intervals intersect that sum. Under the actual-source premises the true sector is retained. A singleton then identifies it; the filters and sector intersections alone do not prove that arbitrary raw records arise from one admitted trajectory.

    For clarity, the uniform interval-width bound uses the same shared outward rounding allowances as the matched-source proof. With \(u=2^{-128}\),

    \[ \begin{split} \rho_K&=(2K^++\operatorname{wid}(K)+2)u,\\ w_C&=2K_C^+b_C+\operatorname{wid}(K_C)(f_C+2b_C)+\rho_{K_C},\\ w_S&=2K_S^+b_S+\operatorname{wid}(K_S)(f_S+2b_S)+\rho_{K_S},\\ d&=w_S/c_0+w_C/c_0^2+4\zeta+10^{-20}. \end{split}\tag{15} \]

    Here d bounds each initial-angle interval width, and \(5d\) bounds the cycle-sum width. The existing argument-evaluation proof applies on the same domain \(c_0>1/6\), \(|S|\le1\); no precision or transcendental kernel is changed. The fixed exact-rational outward assessment gives conservative strict bounds

    \[ \begin{split} B&<0.010000063,&D&<2.489243\cdot10^{-9},&M_3&<0.081245127,\\ b_C&<7.176748\cdot10^{-14},&b_S&<1.057319\cdot10^{-13},&c_0&>0.169965,\\ w_C&<0.000924139,&w_S&<0.027207175,&d&<0.192069229,\\ 5d&<0.960346143,&2\pi-5d&>5.322839. \end{split}\tag{16} \]

    The separated winding values are more than 6.28 apart, whereas the cycle interval is less than 0.961 wide. Outward widths of the three fixed \(2\pi w\) enclosures are smaller than the positive separation margin. Thus every actual admitted initial state and every allowed reading error yields exactly one necessary sector. This is a uniform conditional identification theorem, not a classification of sampled sources.

    5. Signed event work and retention of the continuing identity¶

    Each event changes eighteen outer edges and no internal gaps or forms. For its actual pre-event gaps, the signed storage jump is

    \[ W=\sum_{e\ \mathrm{changed}} \{\cos d_e-\cos(d_e+\Delta q\,\Delta k_{c,e})\}. \tag{17} \]

    The cosine Lipschitz bound gives \(|W|\le18|\Delta q|\). The three event amplitudes in each block have magnitudes \(\epsilon,2\epsilon,\epsilon\); hence every absolute work is at most \(36\epsilon=3.6<4\), and

    \[ \sum_{\text{15 events}}|W| \le5\cdot18(\epsilon+2\epsilon+\epsilon)=36<40. \tag{18} \]

    These bounds evaluate the storage law on each event's actual state without prescribing the sign of its work. The final inverse command does not undo the middle event's payment or return the evolved state. No continuous loss is credited as a reserve, and no reservoir or event-occurrence law is deduced.

    At any point in the word, each commanded outer edge offset has magnitude at most \(\epsilon\), including the endpoints of the larger middle jump. The full continuous drift is bounded by D. Thus

    \[ \begin{split} \alpha-2\zeta-D&>0.698129698,\\ \pi/2-\alpha-2\zeta-D&>0.872662623,\\ \pi/2-A-2\zeta-\epsilon-D&>0.070794324, \qquad \alpha=2\pi/9. \end{split}\tag{19} \]

    Internal gaps remain positive and acute; outer gaps remain strictly acute at all times and on both sides of every event. Each supplied event is a single-valued vertex phase offset, so its increments telescope around every marked cycle. Continuous drift cannot cross a phase-chart boundary. All five internal winding-one identities and all nine original outer windings therefore persist throughout \([0,T]\). Identification of the original winding consequently identifies the continuing object's winding over this finite history.
