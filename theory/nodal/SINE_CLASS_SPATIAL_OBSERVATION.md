# Spatial observation of an acquired mediator's nonlinear response

<a id="sine-class-spatial-observation"></a>

The [complete cubic central response](SINE_CLASS_CUBIC_RESPONSE.md#sine-cubic-finite-result)
has a resolved organization-dependent true sign, but the fixed recording
budget permits cancellation of its scalar contrast. This owner admits a
different receiver observation by deriving its symmetry, locality and
complete error budget before assessing its coefficient. A lower amplitude
degree alone does not guarantee greater organization sensitivity.

The law, acquired families, support, clock, events and recording budget
remain unchanged. This is a conditional mathematical observation study,
not a physical identification or a new formation or nonlinear-response
campaign. The [execution plan](../research/FIVE_STAGE_EXECUTION_PLAN.md#current-g3-gate)
owns its status.

## Complete model and scalar observation

Use all 54 original coordinates on three C9 rings, contacts \((4,13)\)
and \((13,22)\), unit capacities and nominal classes \((1,k,1)\),
\(k=1,2\). In the previously declared structural clock,
\[
 x'=-Ax+\gamma f_k(y),\qquad y'=\gamma Ax,\qquad
 A=K L,\quad f_k(y)=K S(\Theta_k+y),\quad
 \gamma=\frac1{1023\pi},\quad \tau=et,\quad e=\frac{1023}{1024}.
 \tag{1}
\]
Here \(y=\theta-\Theta_k\) is a continuous lift relative to that
class's equilibrium, \(K\) is the inverse actual degree matrix and
\(\Theta_{k,c,j}=k_c(2\pi/9)(j-4)\). Thus \(f_k(0)=0\).
The central donor, mediator and receiver have degrees \(3,4,3\).
All original phase and form residuals remain in the source.

The same four histories within each class share one complete actual
acquired source, with separate component zero sums and Euclidean form
and target-phase errors at most \(\epsilon\). The two classes may have
independent source residuals. Their original
[preparation, event work and identity obligations](SINE_CLASS_NONLINEAR_ORGANIZATION.md#sine-nonlinear-organization-events)
remain premises; selecting an observation installs no event or reset.
Keep
\[
 a=b=\frac1{2000},\qquad s=1,\qquad T=2,\qquad
 \epsilon=10^{-32},\qquad\delta=10^{-30},\qquad r=\frac1{12}.
 \tag{2}
\]
The donor form jumps are at zero and \(s\), with the appropriate absent
jump in each control history. Every coordinate carries across \(s\).

Let \(\ell x=x_{23}-x_{21}\), the difference between the two receiver
neighbors of its center \(22\). Define
\[
 N_k=\ell x_{k,11}(T)-\ell x_{k,10}(T)
                    -\ell x_{k,01}(T)+\ell x_{k,00}(T),
 \qquad D_{\mathrm{sp}}=N_1-N_2.
 \tag{3}
\]
This observation uses two nodal readings per history, hence sixteen
readings in total. Each retains its separate bound \(\delta\).
Without assuming correlations or a separately calibrated direct
difference sensor,
\[
 |\widehat D_{\mathrm{sp}}-D_{\mathrm{sp}}|\le16\delta.
 \tag{4}
\]
The observation is at the final time; no temporal derivative or hidden
state is measured as an extra channel.

## Parity and the central-contact restriction

Let \(\mathcal R\) reflect each ring by \(j\mapsto8-j\). It fixes
all ports, the graph and both supplied donor impulses. On a nominal
source, scaling all impulses of a history by an auxiliary parameter
\(\zeta\) gives the exact relation
\[
 x_k(-\zeta,t)=-\mathcal R x_k(\zeta,t),\qquad
 y_k(-\zeta,t)=-\mathcal R y_k(\zeta,t).
 \tag{5}
\]
It follows from the sine law and uniqueness, with the reflected target
phases interpreted through their circular differences. Since
\(\ell\mathcal R=-\ell\), this spatial response is even in
\(\zeta\). Its nominal constant term is zero and every odd amplitude
coefficient vanishes. Actual source residuals need not obey (5); they
are handled separately below.

Let \(P_R^-\) be the projection onto the receiver-supported
reflection-odd subspace: take the receiver restriction, antisymmetrize
it, and set the other components to zero. In the maximum norm,
\(\|P_R^-\|\le1\), \(\ell P_R^-=\ell\), and
\[
 P_R^- A=A P_R^-.
 \tag{6}
\]
Receiver-odd vectors have zero receiver-center coordinate; the two
internal neighbor contributions cancel at that center. Thus no bridge
term enters or leaves this subspace through its single central contact.
Equation (6) concerns the heat/tangent operator and projected equations.
It does not assert a decoupled nonlinear receiver law: local nonlinear
forcing can depend on receiver-even phases delivered through the port.

For example, in the complete quadratic amplitude hierarchy below,
mediator-odd internal forcing cannot itself diffuse into receiver-odd
coordinates. At \(\eta=\gamma^2=0\) in the scaled hierarchy, the
first-level heat and phase fields are class blind. The receiver's
quadratic forcing then uses only its own fixed target sine, and its
projected propagation is also class blind. Consequently the leading
heat-only quadratic spatial response is identical for \(k=1,2\) at
every finite time. Exposing an odd quadratic mode is therefore
insufficient to demonstrate organization sensitivity.

## Complete quadratic response and a nonzero locality check

Use the ordinary amplitude coefficients and maps from the
[complete triangular variation law](SINE_CLASS_CUBIC_RESPONSE.md#sine-cubic-triangular-variation).
Only its first two levels are needed by the new observation:
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 (u_1,w_1)'&=\mathcal J_k(u_1,w_1),\\
 (u_2,w_2)'&=\mathcal J_k(u_2,w_2)+(Q_k(w_1,w_1),0),\\
 \mathcal J_k&=
 \begin{pmatrix}-A&-\eta C_k\\A&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
 x_1=u_1,\ y_1=\gamma w_1,\quad
 x_2=\gamma^3u_2,\ y_2=\gamma^4w_2,
 \end{aligned}
 \tag{7}
\]
where
\[
 Q_k(v,w)_i=-\frac1{2d_i}\sum_{j\sim i}
      \sin(\Theta_{k,j}-\Theta_{k,i})(v_j-v_i)(w_j-w_i).
 \tag{8}
\]
Each present form jump changes only \(u_{1,4}\). All second-level
coordinates and all phase coefficients carry continuously at the second
event. The complete quadratic contrast is
\[
 D_{\mathrm{sp}}^{(2)}
 =\gamma^3\ell\left[
  (u_{2,1,11}-u_{2,1,10}-u_{2,1,01})
 -(u_{2,2,11}-u_{2,2,10}-u_{2,2,01})\right](T).
 \tag{9}
\]
No tangent phase feedback is dropped. The nominal unprobed coefficient
is exactly zero.

The quadratic contrast is not identically class blind once this
feedback is retained. A local formal check makes the first surviving
mechanism explicit. Put
\(\alpha=2\pi/9\), \(d_c=\cos\alpha-\cos2\alpha>0\), and
\(u=T-s\). Under joint time scaling \(s\mapsto\lambda s\),
\(T\mapsto\lambda T\), the degree-six term of (9) is
\[
 \lambda^6\,
 \frac{\gamma^5 d_c\sin\alpha\,ab}{3456}
 \left(\frac{s^3u^3}{3}+s^2u^4+su^5+\frac{u^6}{3}\right).
 \tag{10}
\]
For one impulse \(a\), the receiver-center first phase coefficient
starts as \(w_{1,22}=-at^2/24+\cdots\); its class-one-minus-class-two
difference starts as \(-\eta d_c at^3/144+\cdots\). Receiver
neighbors start one time degree later. The center-to-neighbor gap is
therefore \(at^2/24+\cdots\), with class difference
\(\eta d_c at^3/144+\cdots\). The leading projected receiver
forcing in (8) is \(\sin\alpha\) times half the squared gap.
After cancelling the single-impulse histories, its class difference is
\[
 \frac{\eta d_c\sin\alpha\,ab}{3456}
       [t^2(t-s)^3+t^3(t-s)^2],
 \tag{11}
\]
whose integral yields (10). Other receiver edges and subsequent heat
propagation contribute at later joint time degrees.

Equation (10) is a check on the complete quadratic amplitude
coefficient, including its \(\gamma^5\) suppression. It is neither
a finite-horizon sign prediction nor a claim about the leading time
degree of every amplitude order of the full nonlinear response.

## A correlated bound on all even orders at least four

A separate remainder for each class would lose the useful locality
in (6). Instead compare their nominal full histories directly on a
common complex amplitude disk. This is only an analytic proof device;
the supplied nodal model and its source remain real.

For a history with total absolute impulse budget \(A_*>0\), fix
\(g=1/3000>\gamma\) and
\[
 \rho=\frac1{2gA_*}>1,\qquad 16g^2T^2<1.
 \tag{12}
\]
The [closed-disk bound](SINE_CLASS_CUBIC_RESPONSE.md#sine-cubic-amplitude-tail)
already proves \(\|y_k\|_\infty<1\) for \(|\zeta|\le\rho\),
both classes and every time up to \(T\). It retains all event
amplitudes and gives holomorphic dependence on a neighborhood of the
closed disk. Normalized sine forcing there obeys
\(\|f_k(y_k)\|_\infty\le8\), since \(\cosh2<4\).

The two class histories have identical nominal form impulses and zero
initial deviations. Write
\(X_\Delta(t)=\|x_1(t)-x_2(t)\|_\infty\) and
\(Y_\Delta(t)=\|y_1(t)-y_2(t)\|_\infty\). The heat semigroup
is a contraction, so directly
\[
 X_\Delta(t)\le16gt,\qquad
 Y_\Delta(t)\le2g\int_0^t X_\Delta(v)\,dv
                  \le16g^2t^2.
 \tag{13}
\]
These bounds use the already closed individual phase disks. They need
no further comparison denominator or hypothesis that the two nonlinear
class fields coincide.

The receiver base phase gaps, including its bridge, are identical in
both classes. Along the straight segment between the two complex phase
states their edge imaginary parts remain bounded by two. Hence the
receiver sine difference is at most \(8Y_\Delta\). Projecting the
form difference onto (6) eliminates incoming form and bridge forcing.
Another heat-contraction estimate gives
\[
 \|P_R^-(x_1-x_2)(T)\|_\infty
 \le8g\int_0^T Y_\Delta(t)\,dt
 \le\frac{128}{3}g^3T^3,\qquad
 |\ell(x_1-x_2)(T)|\le\frac{256}{3}g^3T^3.
 \tag{14}
\]
Thus the receiver difference benefits from locality even though the
global class difference does not.

The scalar in (14) is holomorphic and even in \(\zeta\), with
zero constant term. Cauchy's estimate summed over amplitude degrees
four, six and higher proves the cross-class per-history bound
\[
 R_{4,\Delta}(A_*,T)
 =\frac{4096\,g^7 A_*^4T^3}
        {3(1-4g^2A_*^2)}.
 \tag{15}
\]
For \(A_*=0\) the nominal histories are stationary and this remainder
is exactly zero. This is already a difference between classes. The
mixed contrast therefore uses
\(R_{4,\Delta}(|a|+|b|,T)+R_{4,\Delta}(|a|,T)+R_{4,\Delta}(|b|,T)\),
without another factor of two.

## Actual sources and recording criteria

Let \(J_{\mathrm{sp}}^{(2)}\) enclose (9), including all coefficient
arithmetic and finite time tails. Real full-law heat comparison bounds
each source perturbation in the original coordinates by
\(\epsilon/(1-2\bar\gamma T)\), where
\(\gamma\le\bar\gamma\le g\). Applying the two-node observation
to eight histories gives the uniform true contrast enclosure
\[
 D_{\mathrm{sp}}\in J_{\mathrm{sp}}^{(2)}+[-E,E],\qquad
 E=\sum_{h\in\{11,10,01\}}R_{4,\Delta}(A_h,T)
       +\frac{16\epsilon}{1-2\bar\gamma T}.
 \tag{16}
\]
Here \(A_{11}=|a|+|b|\), \(A_{10}=|a|\) and \(A_{01}=|b|\);
the indexed histories retain both single-impulse contributions when
\(a=b\).
This retains arbitrary actual source residuals, with no imposed
reflection symmetry or cross-class correlation. At (2), exact rational
arithmetic independently of any coefficient gives
\[
 \sum R_{4,\Delta}<5.619\,10^{-33},\qquad
 \frac{16\epsilon}{1-2\bar\gamma T}<1.603\,10^{-31},\qquad
 E<1.66\,10^{-31}.
 \tag{17}
\]
The bound is sufficiently small to assess an organization contrast
without the former independent-class remainder. It does not establish
the size or sign of the as-yet-unassessed spatial coefficient.

If the true interval in (16) lies strictly beyond \(\pm16\delta\),
the recorded statistic has the same sign for every admitted error
vector. Excluding a separately noisy sixteen-record null model through
this statistic requires the different sufficient condition beyond
\(\pm32\delta\). Neither conclusion follows merely from (10).

If instead (16) proves \(|D_{\mathrm{sp}}|\le16\delta\), each
actual source pair admits errors
\(e_j=-\sigma_jD_{\mathrm{sp}}/16\), where \(\sigma_j\) are the
sixteen signs in (3), making the recorded statistic exactly zero.
This is a scalar cancellation witness, not equality or overlap of the
whole record vectors and not blindness of every other observation.
Failure of either test is an unresolved sufficient bound, not an exact
nonidentifiability theorem.

## Fixed analytic projection policy

Before assessing this observation, retain exactly the prior numerical
policy: order 64, dyadic128 outward arithmetic, full 54-coordinate
amplitude levels one through three, eight carried analytic segments
and their proved time tails. No new solver, subdivision, precision,
source, pulse, horizon or noise choice is introduced. Use the fixed
weights \((23,+1),(21,-1)\), project level two, subtract both class
mixed coefficients and only then multiply by the shared
\(\gamma^3\) enclosure.

The previous coefficient evaluation retained every coordinate at all
three amplitude levels. Those records are prior information. Reusing
their justified enclosures for this separately declared projection is
a conditional analytic assessment, not an unseen reserved full-response
experiment. Its finite outcome must be retained separately; this
admission evaluates neither those off-center entries nor a new response.

The event work and identity ledger is inherited without alteration.
There is no extra preparation or stimulus cost from a passive mathematical
observation, while realizing or calibrating such an observation physically
remains a separate obligation.

## Finite analytic result at the unchanged design

The first declared assessment completed the fixed calculation and
projection. Both retained full-coordinate parameter and coefficient
arrays matched the previous analytic evidence exactly. The new complete
quadratic contrast obeys the outward bound
\[
 J_{\mathrm{sp}}^{(2)}
 \subset[7.95100431108890,7.95100431108891]\,10^{-30}.
 \tag{18}
\]
Its exact rational width is less than \(7.396\,10^{-46}\).
The higher-amplitude allowance is less than \(5.619\,10^{-33}\)
and the actual-source allowance is less than \(1.602\,10^{-31}\).
Reconstructing (16) from the exact retained quantities proves
\[
 0<7.785\,10^{-30}<D_{\mathrm{sp}}<8.117\,10^{-30}.
 \tag{19}
\]
Thus the true spatial mixed response depends on mediator organization
through the complete nonlinear law on the admitted actual source
families. Its sign is positive in the declared class-one-minus-class-two
orientation. The original work allowances and identity guards also
remain satisfied; this sign supplies no replacement for their premises.

The recording allowance is \(16\delta=16\,10^{-30}\), however.
The retained recorded interval has the outward enclosure
\[
 \widehat D_{\mathrm{sp}}\in[-8.215,24.117]\,10^{-30}.
 \tag{20}
\]
Equation (19) lies wholly inside \((-16\delta,16\delta)\), so the
scalar cancellation witness following (17) applies to every admitted
actual pair. Its exact margin exceeds \(7.883\,10^{-30}\).
This observation resolves a true sign without guaranteeing a recorded
sign or excluding the separately noisy null alternative. Selecting a
lower amplitude degree has not overcome the inherited observation
limit: locality suppresses the organization-sensitive part, and the
new statistic also consumes twice as many nodal readings.

## A joint limitation of the two admitted statistics

The [central statistic](SINE_CLASS_CUBIC_RESPONSE.md#sine-cubic-finite-result)
and (3) are defined on the same complete class histories, events and
source family. Their bounded-error witnesses can hold simultaneously.
Write \(D_{\mathrm c}\) for the central statistic. For each actual
source pair choose
\[
 e^{\mathrm c}_j=-\sigma^{\mathrm c}_jD_{\mathrm c}/8
 \quad\text{on the eight node-22 readings},\qquad
 e^{\mathrm{sp}}_j=-\sigma^{\mathrm{sp}}_jD_{\mathrm{sp}}/16
 \quad\text{on the sixteen node-21/23 readings}.
 \tag{21}
\]
The two sets of error coordinates are disjoint. The proved bounds
\(|D_{\mathrm c}|<8\delta\) and
\(|D_{\mathrm{sp}}|<16\delta\) make all 24 errors admissible
under the same per-reading budget. Thus
\[
 (\widehat D_{\mathrm c},\widehat D_{\mathrm{sp}})=(0,0)
 \tag{22}
\]
is an allowed pair of statistics for every actual source pair.
No homogeneous linear combination of these two statistics can
guarantee a nonzero class contrast under that error model. This is
stronger than merely observing that two separate sufficient tests fail,
but remains precisely scoped: it does not prove overlap of the full
24-record vectors, cancellation under correlated or differently
calibrated errors, blindness of other receiver observations, or
vanishing of the true organization-dependent dynamics.
