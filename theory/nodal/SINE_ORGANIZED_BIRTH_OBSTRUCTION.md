## 3. A uniform complete-law acceleration envelope

rows of (2) gives the
[complete-law acceleration](SINE_COLLECTIVE_HIERARCHY.md#2-acceleration-predicted-by-this-law-and-acceleration-actually-observed)

\[
 \ddot x_i=m_i\sum_{j\sim i}\cos(\theta_j-\theta_i)
                         (m_jq_j-m_iq_i).
 \tag{7}
\]

This uses the actual phase velocity. It does not infer a pressure from the
observed response or replace temporal evidence by a current RHS value.

For any two nodes, (3) and Cauchy--Schwarz imply

\[
 |x_i-x_j|=|(e_i-e_j)^TPx|
 \leq\sqrt2\|Px\|_2<\sqrt2r.
 \tag{8}
\]

Using the actual nonnegative conductances bounded by one,

\[
 |q_i|\leq\sum_{j\sim i}W_{ij}|x_i-x_j|
 \leq d_i\sqrt2r,
 \qquad |\dot\theta_i|\leq\frac{\nu_i\sqrt2r}{\pi}.
 \tag{9}
\]

Keeping the two phase-velocity contributions in (7) and using
`abs(cos)<=1` yields

\[
 |\ddot x_i|
 \leq B_i:=\frac{\nu_i\sqrt2r}{\pi^2}
    \left(\nu_i+\frac1{d_i}\sum_{j\sim i}\nu_j\right)
 \leq\frac{\nu_i(\nu_i+\nu_{N_i}^{\max})\sqrt2r}{\pi^2}.
 \tag{10}
\]

No form/phase independence, symmetry or tangent approximation is assumed.
The envelope is conservative and need not be attained; discarding a useful

 \tag{11}

All nodes have capacity at most one, so a second uniform corollary is

\[

\]

These strict rational margins apply for all structural time by (6).

## 4. Exact three-sample windows are positive averages of acceleration

Let `f(t)=x_i(t)` along any solution admitted above, with
`7<=t_0<t_1<t_2`, `h_1=t_1-t_0`, `h_2=t_2-t_1`, and `H_t=h_1+h_2`.
The true timestamped observation uses the same convention as the shared
[acceleration reader](../../src/tnfr/operators/nodal_equation.py):

\[
 \widehat A_i=
 \frac2{H_t}\left[
  \frac{f(t_2)-f(t_1)}{h_2}
  -\frac{f(t_1)-f(t_0)}{h_1}\right].
 \tag{13}
\]

For a twice continuously differentiable f, define the piecewise-linear kernel

\[
 K(s)=\begin{cases}
 \dfrac{2(s-t_0)}{h_1H_t},&t_0\leq s\leq t_1,\\[6pt]
 \dfrac{2(t_2-s)}{h_2H_t},&t_1\leq s\leq t_2.
 \end{cases}
 \tag{14}
\]

Its values agree at `t_1`, it is nonnegative, and its two areas are
`h_1/H_t` and `h_2/H_t`. Integrating `K(s)f''(s)` by parts on each
subinterval cancels the two terms involving `f'(t_1)` and gives exactly

\[
 \widehat A_i=\int_{t_0}^{t_2}K(s)\ddot x_i(s)\,ds,
 \qquad \int_{t_0}^{t_2}K(s)\,ds=1.
 \tag{15}
\]

Thus this finite-window observation is a positive normalized average of
the full acceleration, without a small-window approximation. Equations
(11)--(12) imply

\[
 \boxed{|\widehat A_0|<1/128<\tau,\qquad
        |\widehat A_i|<1/48<\tau\quad\text{for every node }i.}
 \tag{16}
\]

This holds for the frozen window (4) and for every later unequal-spaced
window wholly on the same continuation. Neither a change of sampling times
nor arbitrarily fine exact sampling can create a crossing. A window through
a supplied form, phase, capacity or support jump has different premises;
(15) cannot be applied to it as a smooth trajectory of (2).

## 5. Represented observations need a separate error allowance

The result above concerns exact source states and true trajectory values.
It is not a claim that every floating-point record accepted by a history
reader is a certified sample of that trajectory. Source materialization,
solver enclosures, timestamps, arithmetic and target/clock association
retain their separate obligations.

At fixed exact timestamps, let each recorded form differ from its true value
by at most `epsilon_k`. Linearity of (13) gives the exact endpoint-error bound

\[
 E_{\rm samples}=\frac2{h_1+h_2}
 \left(\frac{\epsilon_0+\epsilon_1}{h_1}
       +\frac{\epsilon_1+\epsilon_2}{h_2}\right),\qquad
 |\widehat A_{\rm recorded}-\widehat A_{\rm true}|
 \leq E_{\rm samples}.
 \tag{17}
\]

For (4), a separately certified common endpoint allowance

\[
 E_{\rm samples}\leq64\epsilon=2^{-34}.
 \tag{18}
\]

This is an illustrative observation budget, not an evaluated solver accuracy
or a new trajectory. Any time and arithmetic errors must also be bounded.
If their combined effect on the reported acceleration is `E_other`, a
sufficient no-crossing check at parent 0 is

\[
 \frac1{128}+2^{-34}+E_{\rm other}\leq\tau.
 \tag{19}
\]

For this particular fixed window, the reader's binary64 arithmetic also
admits a separate bound under explicit represented-input premises. Immediately

positive-rho weighted form mean lies in the same interval. At any later time,

The represented timestamps in (4), their differences `1/4`, and their span

\]

No sample producer has supplied the endpoint allowance in this calculation;
