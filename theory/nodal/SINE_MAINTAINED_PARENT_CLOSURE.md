## 3. Changed mobility, means and continuation

With positive represented capacity, only the parent coefficient changes:

\[
M_2^+=rM_2^-,\qquad \rho_2^+=\rho_2^-/r,
\qquad \rho_i=\frac{d_i}{\nu_i}.
\]

All other instantaneous rows remain unchanged. On this source the predicted
parent form rate changes approximately `0.01647756447` to `0.01516632156`,
and phase rate `0.3963025696` to `0.3647658132`. Both remain nonzero.
The same instantaneous neighbor rates do not imply unchanged future motion,
because later phase and form evolve through the altered coupled field.

For either retained coordinate vector y, let
`m^-=sum(rho_i^- y_i)/R`, `R=sum(rho_i^-)` and
`a=rho_2^-(1/r-1)`. Then the reset changes the corresponding mean by

\[
m^+=m^-+\frac{a}{R+a}(y_2-m^-).
\]

This is reweighting with unchanged coordinates, not an unrecorded form or
phase jump. The phase mean uses the declared real lifts, including the
unchanged negative lifts at nodes 10 and 11. Each mean is conserved on its
own positive-capacity held-law segment; a common value across the event is
not generally conserved. H remains the same storage functional, with the

The general scalar SHA contract allows intentional zero attenuation or

However, `rho_2=d_2/nu_2` and the positive-weight mean theorem are unavailable.
The nodal identity alone would freeze only the unforced form row, not every

The new parent capacity lies strictly outside the narrow parameter family
