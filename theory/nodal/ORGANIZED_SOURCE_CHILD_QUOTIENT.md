## 6. A finite first-hit certificate near the sector

Its useful conclusion is the fixed-level bracket, beyond the quadratic
projected-error mechanism already available in
[repeated composition](REPEATED_ORGANIZED_COMPOSITION.md) and

and continuous raw phase offsets. Initialize a full solution z by arbitrary

\[

### Quadratic defect and uniform state allowance

The fine Laplacian commutes with Pi. Therefore the projected phase row has
zero direct defect, and only the sine term of the form row contributes.
Let `vartheta=Pi*Theta`, with child orbit labels A, B, C, D as in section 1.
For example the defect at A and at B is

\[
\begin{aligned}
 d_{A,j}&=2\sin(\vartheta_{B,j}-\vartheta_{A,j})(\cos b_j-1),\\

                 [\cos(b_k-b_j)-1]\\
 &\quad+\sum_{U=A,C}\sin(\vartheta_{U,j}-\vartheta_{B,j})(\cos b_j-1).
\end{aligned}
\]

The C row has the same paired-neighbor expression as A and the D row is
zero. Since `1-cos(s)<=s^2/2`, the two cycle terms in a B row each contribute
at most `2*||b||_infty^2` and its two matching terms at most
`||b||_infty^2/2` each. Thus

\[
 \|\Pi S(\Theta)-S(\Pi\Theta)\|_\infty\le5\|b\|_\infty^2.
\]

Let g be a certified upper bound for gamma and set
`L=5*(1+g)/2`, `c=5*g/4`. The fine degree is at most five, so
`||L_f||_infty<=10`, the sine map is 10-Lipschitz in the infinity norm,
and the complete joint vector field is L-Lipschitz. Comparison of z with
Jz gives `||(I-Pi)z(t)||_infty<=h0*exp(L*t)`.

Choose outward upper bounds `G>=exp(L*T)` and
`Phi>=(exp(L*T)-1)/L`, and define the allowances

\[
 H=h_0G,\qquad e=cH^2\Phi.
\]

On the entire interval the hidden state is bounded by H, while
`||Pi*z(t)-w(t)||_infty<=e`. Indeed the latter error starts at zero and
satisfies the upper-Dini comparison `e'(t)<=L*e(t)+c*H^2`.
Consequently fine reconstructed error is bounded by `H+e`, not by e alone.
Hidden form cannot be omitted: it generates hidden phase through the second
row even when `b(0)=0`. No attraction toward the symmetric sector is asserted.

### Energy and whole-bracket loss from primitives

`a^T K a+b^T K b<=54*(||a||_infty^2+||b||_infty^2)<=108*H^2`.
It need not be positive outside an acute cell. The same splitting gives
the exact instantaneous loss identity
`rho(z)-rho(Pi*z)=||K*a||_2^2/2`; this does not equate the projected state
with the separately evolved reference.

### Same fixed level, existence and timing

\[
 E_f^->\ell,\qquad E_c^->\ell,\qquad r_f>0,\qquad r_c>0,

\[
 |\tau_f-\tau_c|\le\frac{B_E}{\min(r_f,r_c)}.

unavailable; it does not establish absence of a crossing.

The one-word mean/rail budget supplies unit rails and strict Reception

### Separate the timing contribution from the reset defect

Let H, e, X and Delta denote the section 6 hidden-state, projected-state,

\[

\]

Comparing at one time using e, then integrating the reference incoming row
between the two hit times, proves

\[
 \|\Pi z(\tau_f^-)-w(\tau_c^-)\|_\infty\le D.
\]

Only the mathematical incoming extension is used in that integration; no
actual reset trajectory is silently continued by the old law.

Reception is a convex linear form map for `0<beta<=1`. It commutes with
Pi because the marked targets are fixed and both swapped neighbors are
retained with their inherited counts. Its infinity-norm Lipschitz bound
is one. It changes no phase, so the complete-word projected form allowance
will remain D.

For Coherence at target i, the only hidden contribution is the paired
matching neighbor from children one and two. With symmetric relative phase
u and hidden phase b at that slot, its phasor difference is exactly

\[
 Z_i(z)-Z_i(\Pi z)=2e^{iu}(\cos b-1),\qquad
 |Z_i(z)-Z_i(\Pi z)|\le b^2\le H^2.
\]

The target itself is a singleton, so its input lift has no hidden part.
The argument margin gives a direct phase-reset defect at most
`lambda*H^2/4`. Between the projected fine and reference inputs, each
relative angle changes by at most `2D`; five neighbors yield a phasor
difference at most `10D`. Including the target's retained input lift,
the resulting complete-word bounds are

\[
 F=D,\qquad P=\left(1+\frac{5\lambda}{2}\right)D+
                     \frac{\lambda H^2}{4},
\]

\[
 \|\Pi x_f^+-x_c^+\|_\infty\le F,\qquad
 \|\Pi\delta_f^+-\delta_c^+\|_\infty\le P,
 \qquad \nu_f^+=\nu_c^+.
\]

Silence establishes the exact last equality while holding form and phase.
All three stages leave the hidden child coordinates unchanged. Thus full
fine reconstruction has joint error at most `H+max(F,P)`, whereas each
marked target directly has the finer form/phase allowances F and P.

At a fixed horizon and bounded X, the projected allowances are quadratic
in the initial hidden size provided the two loss floors remain uniformly

allowance alone need not be uniformly quadratic as transversality degenerates.
The fine reconstruction remains generally first order in hidden size.

\]

Reception keeps both form extrema within X, and subsequent stages hold form.
The hidden energy term is unchanged in structure, with both hidden norms
at most H. Each actual ideal successor separately has `E^+<=ell` by word
passivity. These are energy bounds at the aligned successors, not a balance
over an interval spanning different hybrid laws.

### A nonzero quadratic Coherence defect

The approximation is not an exact off-sector word descent. A static
control makes this explicit without claiming to know either actual hit state.
Set form to zero, give node 9 offset `phi+b` and node 18 offset `phi-b`,
and set every other offset to zero. Fix `phi=1/128`, `b=2^-20`.
Both the full and projected preparations have acute edge angles, zero form
mean and excess at most `4*(phi^2+b^2)<1/100`.

At target zero, with `r=1+2*cos(alpha)`, their relative resultants are

\[
 Z_f=r+2e^{i\phi}\cos b,\qquad Z_c=r+2e^{i\phi},\qquad
 \Im(Z_f\overline{Z_c})=2r(\cos b-1)\sin\phi<0.
\]

Both lie in the right half-plane, so their arguments differ. More precisely,
as b tends to zero at fixed positive phi,

\[
 \operatorname{Arg}Z_f-\operatorname{Arg}Z_c
 =-\frac{r\sin\phi}{|Z_c|^2}b^2+O(b^4).
\]

The default positive lambda preserves this nonzero quadratic correction in
the target phase. A carrier-only control with phi zero would miss it. The
sign here belongs to the supplied static pair, not to the separate unknown
states at the two actual crossings. This distinguishes a useful finite
approximation from exact closure after hidden phase has been discarded.

### Detached implementation and acceptance boundary

The [word-error reader](../../src/tnfr/physics/_organized_source_child_word_error.py)
rebuilds the incoming certificate from primitives and resolves the registered
default factors. It reuses the source barrier, shared offset-edge geometry
and one-word reuse budget to check the mathematical domain and applicable
scalar margins. The same offset helper serves the existing reflection
retention reader; it retains the closing-edge lift and never substitutes
rounded raw phases for the exact carrier.

Conditional endpoint bounds are absent when a source, crossing or sufficient
word-domain margin fails. The report retains the available evidence and
reasons; an unavailable certificate does not prove that an event fails or
that its successor loses organization. It installs no selector, performs no
ideal or represented reset, and neither locates a hit nor integrates flow.
Binary64 event errors, stale pressure, callbacks and runtime history require
their own admission outside this mathematical certificate.

[Independent controls](../../tests/physics/test_organized_source_child_word_error.py)
check the branch-sensitive source domain, inherited factors and capacities,
the local nonlinear word algebra, exact symmetry, tiny nonzero deviations,
and unavailable conditions. The first-hit static preparation from section 6
is fixed unchanged for the successor certificate; the separate off-carrier
control above tests the nonzero reset defect. Neither control evaluates a
reserved trajectory or an actual first-word response.

For the fixed first-hit preparation, all successor-certificate conditions
pass. Conservative bounds on the aligned projected outputs are `F<9.5e-10`
and `P<1.7e-9`; full fine reconstruction is bounded by `9.8e-7` and the
aligned energy discrepancy by `3.1e-7`. These are bounds in the declared
model coordinates, not evaluated errors. The independent static phase
control has the exact negative sign proved above; its high-precision
default Coherence increment difference is approximately `-2.62782e-16`.
That value belongs only to the supplied static pair.
