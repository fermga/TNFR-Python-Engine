## 2. U3 admits the internal edge and excludes new interpair contacts

Write `k=0` for the marked pair, `b=h=1/1024`, and use the source's
signed representatives `x_(j,+/-)=X_j+/-u_j` and
`theta_(j,+/-)=Theta_j+/-delta_j`. The AL stage changes only the two
selected forms and their associated execution records. All four existing
neighbors of each selected member have phase gaps bounded by

\[
\alpha+\sqrt2z<\pi/2.
\]

Every missing edge from a selected member is either its partner or a member
of a distance-two base pair. The partner gap is at most `sqrt(2)*z`, whereas
the wrapped gap to every distance-two pair is at least

\[
2\alpha-\sqrt2z>\pi/2.
\]

The UM candidate reader applies U3 to the snapshot phases **before** its
phase proposals. Thus each target has exactly one possible new neighbor,
its partner. Moving other phases later in the same stage cannot introduce
a candidate that failed this first gate. The two proposals concern the
same undirected internal edge; the merge creates one edge, not two.

For each selected member `s in {+,-}`, the bidirectional consensus is

\[
C_s=\arg\left(e^{i(\Theta_k+s\delta_k)}
       +2R_{k-1}e^{i\Theta_{k-1}}+2R_{k+1}e^{i\Theta_{k+1}}\right),
\qquad R_j=\cos\delta_j.
\]

Choose its lift near `theta_(*,k)+gamma`. At the reference twist, the
resultant is `(1+4*cos(alpha))*exp(i*(theta_(*,k)+gamma))`, of magnitude
`sqrt(5)`. The five constituent phase deviations give a chord error at
most `sqrt(5)*z`. Consequently the resultant is nonzero and

\[
|C_s-\theta_{*,k}-\gamma|
\le\arctan\frac{z}{1-z}<2z.
\]

Target-to-consensus displacements are below `3*z`. For each of the four
neighbors, the displacement to either consensus on the nearest lift is
below `alpha+3*z<4/3`. These statements fix the shortest-arc branches
consumed by the actual stage, including the edge crossing the displayed

admission bounds.

After AL, all selected forms remain in `(1/4,1/2)`. Capacities remain in

## 4. Actual edge weight and the unit-support boundary

Let `epsilon_E=1e-9` denote the implementation's positive form-similarity
regularizer. With the homogeneous `Si=1/2`, the shared compatibility formula

\[
\boxed{W=1-
 \frac{|\operatorname{wrap}(\theta_{k,+}'-\theta_{k,-}')|}{2\pi}
 -\frac{|x_{k,+}+b-x_{k,-}-b|}
 {4(|x_{k,+}+b|+|x_{k,-}+b|+\epsilon_E)}.}
\]

The cancelling boosts in the numerator emphasize that UM leaves the
post-AL form difference unchanged. Both directed candidate proposals have
this same symmetric score. Phase contraction, `x+b>1/4` and the source
norm imply `1-W<sqrt(2)*z<1/4`. Thus the internal edge is indeed added,
with `W>3/4`; the finite candidate score does not merely leave its existence
undecided. No interpair edge is added.

Every deficit in this expression is nonnegative. Therefore

\[
\boxed{W=1\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad u_k=0,\ \delta_k=0.}
\]

Without the frozen homogeneous `Si` premise, equality of the two supplied
`Si` values would be a third necessary condition. The exact unit-edge
family is a synchronized-member stratum. It retains positive relative
width in the other pairs' internal states, all mean states and paired

with nonzero selected form or phase split produces a strictly subunit edge

The current complete normalized-sine reader requires unit conductances.

These exact-real equalities are not authentication rules for rounded data.

## 5. Unit-edge continuation: new rows and an identity barrier

Restrict now to `u_k=delta_k=0`. The two consensuses coincide, the new edge
has unit weight, and the selected members retain identical forms, phases
and capacities. Write `E'=E union {{k,+},{k,-}}`, let `L'` be its unit
Laplacian and `D_i` its actual fine degrees. They are five on the selected
pair and four elsewhere. The post-word capacities are the positive held
values from Section 3. The admitted complete continuation is

\[
\dot x_i=\frac{\nu_i}{\pi D_i}
 \sum_{j\sim_{E'}i}\sin(\theta_j-\theta_i),\qquad
\dot\theta_i=\frac{\nu_i}{\pi D_i}(L'x)_i.
\]

Pressure is the independently specified sine sum in the first row.
These are original-clock rows on the new support; neither stored UM
pressure nor the old degree-four normalization is substituted.

The existing [internal-edge derivation](SINE_PAIR_STATE.md#sine-pair-support-symmetry)
extends directly from common capacity to the held paired capacities, which
multiply each pair's two fine rows equally. With `e_j=1` for `j=k`, zero
otherwise, `d_j=2`, `D_j=4+e_j`, and

\[
J_j=\sum_{\ell\sim j}R_\ell\sin(\Theta_\ell-\Theta_j),\quad
C_j=\sum_{\ell\sim j}R_\ell\cos(\Theta_\ell-\Theta_j),\quad
q_j=\sum_{\ell\sim j}(X_j-X_\ell),
\]

the exact pair rows are

\[
\begin{aligned}
\dot X_j&=\frac{2v_j}{\pi D_j}R_jJ_j,&
\dot\Theta_j&=\frac{2v_j}{\pi D_j}q_j,\\
\dot u_j&=-\frac{2v_j}{\pi D_j}\sin\delta_j(C_j+e_jR_j),&
\dot\delta_j&=\frac{2v_j}{\pi D_j}(2+e_j)u_j.
\end{aligned}
\]

Every independent pair interchange is still a support automorphism.
The exact unordered description therefore persists. At the selected tip,
`u_k=delta_k=0` makes both internal rates zero, so that tip remains invariant.
As a useful exact reuse, throughout this invariant stratum the internal
edge contributes zero to both fine sums. The entire new-support flow then
equals the old doubled-C5 flow with auxiliary effective target capacity
`(4/5)*v_k`, and all other capacities unchanged. This is a restricted
equivalence of complete rows, not a modification of the actual stored
capacity or an admission of the new graph by the old strict replica reader.
At the same fine state, the actual selected pressure itself is `4/5` of
the old-support pressure; the auxiliary-capacity representation instead
keeps that old pressure and puts the same factor into its capacity. The
two representations agree on both continuous rates, not on every primitive
label. Representing the auxiliary rational capacity in binary64 introduces
its own rate defect and must not be asserted exactly equal by floating-point
coincidence.

For completeness, the identity barrier can be checked directly on the
actual new graph. Its storage is

\[
H_{E'}=\frac12 x^TL'x+
 \sum_{\{i,j\}\in E'}[1-\cos(\theta_j-\theta_i)].
\]

Differentiating cancels the two rows term by term, so `dH_(E')/dt=0`.
The old winding target remains a critical point: the new internal edge
has zero target form and phase differences. It adds zero target storage,
so the same target value `H_*` applies. With `l=e_(k,+)-e_(k,-)`,

\[
L'=L+ll^T,\qquad
\lambda_2(L')\ge5-\sqrt5,\qquad
\lambda_{\max}(L')\le2\max_iD_i=10.
\]

The phase changes of Section 2 have Euclidean norm below `3*h`: there are
two selected displacements below `3*h*z` and four neighboring displacements

Centering can only reduce these norms. Hence the post-word full centered

\[
Z_f' < z+\sqrt{8/5}\,b+3h
       <\frac{13}4z<\frac1{128}.
\]

Take `r=1/16`. All old edge target gaps are `alpha`, and the new target
gap is zero. On the radius-`r` chart the storage Hessian therefore gives

\[
H_{E'}-H_*
\ge\kappa (Z_f)^2,\qquad
\kappa=\frac{5-\sqrt5}{2}\cos(\alpha+\sqrt2r)>\frac18,
\]

with `alpha+sqrt(2)*r<pi/2`. The global Hessian upper bound and criticality

\[
H_{E'}(z')-H_*\le5(Z_f')^2
 <\frac5{16384}<\frac1{2048}<\kappa r^2.

Conservation prevents a first radius exit, which would require storage

## 8. Complete weighted law, balance and retained state

For this section let `beta>0`; the word application fixes `beta=1`. Let `U`
be fixed simple connected undirected support without loops, with bare degrees
`d_i>0`. Supply a finite symmetric nonnegative conductance `W_ij` on every
edge, with its declared dimensionless scale, and fixed positive capacities
`nu_i`. A supported zero-weight edge remains in `U`. Write

\[
q=L_Wx,\qquad
S_i=\sum_{j\in N_U(i)}\sin(\theta_j-\theta_i),\qquad
V_U=\sum_{\{i,j\}\in U}[1-\cos(\theta_j-\theta_i)],\qquad
M=\operatorname{diag}\frac{\nu_i}{\pi d_i}.
\]

The supplied complete rows and storage are

\[
\boxed{
p_i=\frac{S_i}{\pi d_i},\qquad
\dot x=MS,\qquad
\dot\theta=\beta^{-1}Mq,\qquad
H_W=\frac12x^TL_Wx+\beta V_U.}
\]

This retains signed real form, primitive circular phase, the original
structural clock and zero forcing. Capacity, support and conductance have
zero derivative on each declared continuous segment. The independently
evaluated pressure is not the UM-stored pressure after relief. No pressure
is reconstructed from a response, and no supported zero edge is discarded.
Conductance enters the form gradient in the phase row; the conservative
form row has bare phase pressure and no separate diffusive form-pressure
term. Positive-loss transport would need its own normalization and complete
admission and is outside this result.

Since `grad_x H_W=q` and `grad_theta H_W=-beta*S`, symmetry of the diagonal
`M` gives the exact cancellation

\[
\dot H_W=q^TMS-S^TMq=0.
\]

Equivalently, these rows have the constant skew structure

\[
\begin{pmatrix}\dot x\\\dot\theta\end{pmatrix}
=\begin{pmatrix}0&-M/\beta\\M/\beta&0\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}\nabla_xH_W\\\nabla_\theta H_W\end{pmatrix}.
\]

This identifies a conditional conservative completion; it does not identify
`H_W` with physical energy. Its form and phase units inherit the source's
declared structural chart and `beta` conversion. The method reuses the
[weighted balance completion](RELATIONAL_EFFECTIVE_CONNECTIONS.md#connection-mechanisms-and-mediators):
that owner's native resultant pressure and phase metric also cancel through
`H_U*g_U=-grad V_U`. Here the sine model instead specifies
`g_U=S/(pi*d)` and metric `pi*d`. The common cancellation method does not
make these different complete laws identical.

For `rho_i=d_i/nu_i`, edge antisymmetry gives

\[
\frac{d}{dt}\sum_i\rho_i x_i=\frac1\pi\sum_iS_i=0,
\qquad
\frac{d}{dt}\sum_i\rho_i\theta_i
=\frac1{\beta\pi}\sum_iq_i=0.
\]

The second statement uses declared continuous lifts. Thus both weighted
means use bare degree divided by capacity. Conductance strength cannot
replace this weight. Form-origin shifts, common circular phase rotations
and joint relabeling of state, `U`, `W` and capacities preserve the law.
At `W_ij=1` it recovers both rows and storage of the earlier unit model.
If a bare edge has zero weight, it still changes `S`, `V_U` and degree;
deleting it is a different support event. No regular limit of conductance
alone establishes the absence of that phase relation.

Conservation does not uniquely select this completion. With the displayed
form row fixed, an additional supplied phase row `z` satisfying `S^T*z=0`
would leave this scalar balance unchanged. Extra restrictions would be
needed to exclude every such row. Conversely, weighting the sine pressure
and phase storage by `W` would define another conservative model but would
change the bare phase-channel premise retained here. The selected reciprocal
diagonal phase row is an explicit constitutive choice, not a law inferred
from the nodal equation, unit-limit agreement or conservation alone.

edge and `omega_j` for its conductance. Absent edges have both zero;
present zero-weight edges have `epsilon_j=1, omega_j=0`. Let `d_j` count
adjacent base pairs, `D_j=2*d_j+epsilon_j` be the fine bare degree, and
`v_j` be the held equal-member capacity. Use the neighboring sums `J_j,C_j,q_j`
from Section 5. Direct averaging of both fine rows gives

\[
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
\dot X_j&=\frac{2v_j}{\pi D_j}R_jJ_j,&
\dot\Theta_j&=\frac{2v_j}{\beta\pi D_j}q_j,\\
\dot u_j&=-\frac{2v_j}{\pi D_j}
             \sin\delta_j(C_j+\epsilon_jR_j),&
\dot\delta_j&=\frac{2v_j}{\beta\pi D_j}
                  (d_j+\omega_j)u_j.
\end{aligned}}
\]

The internal edge contributes `-sin(2*delta_j)` to the plus-member bare
sine sum whenever present, but `2*omega_j*u_j` to its weighted form
gradient. Thus its weight changes the internal phase row without multiplying
its bare phase-pressure contribution. Its contributions to both pair means
cancel; the new degree still normalizes those means. Putting
`omega_j=epsilon_j` recovers the unit internal-edge rows of Section 5.

For

\[
A_j=\frac{2v_j}{\pi D_j}(C_j+\epsilon_jR_j),\qquad
c_j=\frac{2v_j}{\beta\pi D_j}(d_j+\omega_j),
\]

the unordered internal rows are smooth:

\[
\dot R_j=-c_jQ_j,\qquad
\dot U_j=-2A_jQ_j,\qquad
\dot Q_j=-A_j(1-R_j^2)+c_jU_jR_j.
\]

They preserve the derivative of `Q_j^2-U_j*(1-R_j^2)` exactly. More
strongly, evolving any admitted fine representative and projecting gives

including synchronized tips. The derivative identity by itself would not

The collective state includes the actual `U,W` and held capacities along

\]

The two coefficients retain the distinct conductance and bare-support roles.
