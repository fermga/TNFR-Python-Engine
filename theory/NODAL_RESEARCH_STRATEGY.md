# Nodal research strategy: emergence, model admission and reuse

This document explains the scientific dependencies and boundaries of the
research. The [execution plan](research/FIVE_STAGE_EXECUTION_PLAN.md#current-g3-gate)
is the sole task queue; the [portfolio](../TNFR_lineas_de_investigacion.txt)
classifies branches. The [theory-to-execution map](README.md#theory-to-execution)
locates proofs, implementations and tests. A reusable result or an open
question below does not start an additional campaign.

## 1. Scientific objective and interpretation

The generative hypothesis is that observable constituents and their properties
could emerge from interacting TNFR patterns. A larger NFR would organize
smaller constituents while retaining their influence. Primitive EPI need not
be a sensor reading; physical predictions still need an independent collective
measurement bridge. [Emergent ontology](EMERGENT_ONTOLOGY.md) owns this hypothesis.

A supplied triangle, lattice, partition or embedding is a preparation, not a
discovered constituent. Organization can retain an identity while internal
coordinates move; identity need not mean equilibrium or one periodic waveform.
A useful finite lifetime is a legitimate outcome. Creation from nothing,
eternal lifetime and a universal clock are not prerequisites for every study.

The dependencies are sufficient state and complete laws, then formation,
identity and interaction under those laws, then independently testable physical
identification. The nodal identity alone selects neither pressure nor every
companion row. A negative result can clarify these dependencies as much as a
successful preparation.

The form-balance/common-origin question connects the first two. Its useful
output is an explicit account of which relative dynamics, absolute observations
and primitive information are preserved by a proposed recentering. Constant
translation symmetry does not by itself justify time-dependent recentering or
make an observed absolute coordinate irrelevant. A complete-law change must
still meet its locality and capacity contract.
The [asymptotic-origin construction](nodal/RELATIONAL_PATTERN_MEMORY.md#asymptotic-origin-coordinate-scope)
can supply a local nonlinear conserved coordinate under explicit recovery and
regularity assumptions even when a bare mean drifts. It neither establishes
raw linear form balance nor independently selects a primitive law.

The same account informs composition: eliminating one global origin can
preserve every inter-node difference, whereas independently discarding each
component's origin loses offsets needed at a later join. Keep these offsets
or declare them unavailable; do not reconstruct them from the desired contact
outcome. [Retained-frame admission](research/FIVE_STAGE_EXECUTION_PLAN.md#retained-relative-frame-gate)
uses this distinction without requiring a new generic maintenance proof.
The native [retained phase record](nodal/RELATIONAL_PATTERN_MEMORY.md#relational-retained-phase-memory)
already makes a collective origin meaningful through a declared reference or
contact. Its observation lesson is reusable; its response bounds retain their
own law and preparation.
Eliminating internal state instead can generate memory and nonlocal
responses. The [hidden-inventory identities](nodal/RELATIONAL_PATTERN_MEMORY.md#conserved-hidden-inventory)
retain both the memory term and hidden initial source in the fixed linear
model; zero collective charge exchange need not mean absent hidden dynamics.
Those effective terms do not automatically define primitive laws.

Capacity and support provide another shared boundary. The form-balance weights
contain both degree and inverse capacity. A changing capacity or a bridge event
therefore needs its own continuous or jump accounting before a held-support
invariant can be reused. The [capacity, clock and event accounting](FORCED_SUPPORT_BALANCE.md#prescribed-capacity-and-event-mean-balance)
distinguishes raw form charge from its normalized mean under prescribed changes.
Storage loss, event work and an independently declared
reservoir also remain distinct. Neither fixed-support recovery nor an admissible
event budget selects when a connection occurs.
The existing [capacity-asymmetry result](TNFR_SCALE_GEOMETRY_AND_BRIDGE.md#sine-replica-capacity-asymmetry)
also distinguishes protection of collective geometry from uninterrupted internal
motion; heterogeneous capacity need not change only the speed of a clock.

Physical discrimination remains the portfolio's supporting branch F: each
mathematical alternative needs independent preparation, measurement and clock
admission before a physical conclusion. Numerical admission and operator
contracts support these questions without supplying a fundamental selector.
The [execution plan](research/FIVE_STAGE_EXECUTION_PLAN.md#current-g3-gate)
alone opens the next bounded gate; this grouping does not reactivate receiver
transfer, recurrence, pulse estimates or archived runtime campaigns.

### Use case studies to expose transferable mechanisms

Refine a case study when the missing estimate decides a named formation claim
or supplies a reusable mechanism. A complete basin atlas of one graph is not
a prerequisite to the generative objective. Once a bounded question is answered,
retain its proof and limits instead of optimizing its radius or collecting
another trajectory without a new discriminator.

The [sine dynamics owner](nodal/SINE_PATTERN_DYNAMICS.md) connects composition,
prepared acquisition, retained-state memory, controlled reduction and
preparation geometry under one complete law. Its reading map owns that chain.
The methodological lesson is that sufficient storage, geometric compatibility,
actual formation and autonomous selection are separate obligations.

**Relation to established work.** [Synchronization](https://arxiv.org/abs/1511.07139),
[pattern formation](https://doi.org/10.1103/RevModPhys.65.851) and
[port-Hamiltonian graph systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/1107.2006) are comparison
frameworks. [Quantum Graphity](https://arxiv.org/abs/0801.0861) is a precedent for
emergent geometry, not a derivation of TNFR. The sine law's
[storage/Poisson representation](nodal/RELATIONAL_PATTERN_MEMORY.md#sine-relative-pattern-state)
is a conditional same-law identity, not physical energy identification.
Originality requires comparison of precise results, not new terminology.

### What the nine-point critique establishes

Continuing obligations are empirical validation, complete evolution,
pressure-law justification, measurement and clock bridges, auxiliary-model
scope, diagnostic incompleteness, configured policies, autonomous selection,
and the distinction between computation, proof and physics. These are not
interchangeable software defects. A closed model can still lack independent
justification; a successful physical mapping can remain equivalent to a
known model. Neither outcome should be overstated.

### Closure audit: equations, events and identity

Start from a declared full state and `x_dot=diag(nu)*P(z)`. Include only history
actually consumed. State which coordinates are held, evolved, observed or
changed by events. A regular chart, exact symmetry quotient and lossy
observation require different arguments.

### Pressure, coordinates, capacity and clock

[Locality, regularity and covariance](nodal/PRESSURE_CONSTITUTIVE_SCOPE.md#44-constitutive-admission-ledger-and-remaining-choices)
constrain pressure without uniquely choosing it. A
[chart change](FUNDAMENTAL_THEORY.md#26-a-structural-coordinate-must-transform-its-pressure)
must transform the full law. Primitive phase, form-derived orientation and
instrument phase remain distinct. [Clock changes](NODAL_PARAMETER_FOUNDATIONS.md#pressure-clock-full-state-closure)
transform every row; common capacity can rescale time in an admitted family,
while heterogeneous changes can alter trajectories. Proposed
[capacity relations](CAPACITY_LOCALIZATION_BALANCE.md#8-a-local-form-capacity-relation-dissipation-and-restoration-criteria)
require tangency and boundary/event checks. Synchronization need not be a
monotone clock, and capacity is not automatically an oscillation frequency.

The native and sine laws share a
[reciprocal exchange family](nodal/RELATIONAL_EXCHANGE_ADMISSION.md#common-exchange-geometry-and-composition),
but a common storage balance and consensus tangent do not select its mobility.
[Closed form balance](nodal/RELATIONAL_EXCHANGE_ADMISSION.md#closed-form-balance-and-source-selection)
is equivalent to odd pairwise source composition only within the specified
graph-uniform incident-gap information class. Combining this with exact
cosine-storage loss, the full phase domain and the stated phase-row premises
selects sine pressure conditionally; uniqueness of the companion phase row
retains its additional hypotheses. The cubic-sine countercontrol admits exact
exchange on every path and cycle but fails at degree three under fixed cosine
storage. [Changing its phase potential](nodal/RELATIONAL_EXCHANGE_ADMISSION.md#phase-storage-selection-boundary)
gives a smooth all-degree alternative with the same form balance, Poisson
tensor and consensus tangent. Branching therefore tests the fixed-storage
class; it does not select storage or a universal law. These alternatives
require scrutiny of the information, storage and support premises, beyond
additional trajectories of an already tested ring.
The [stationary mediator reduction](nodal/RELATIONAL_PATTERN_MEMORY.md#sine-mediator-common-geometry)
shows why this primitive information class need not survive elimination of
constituents: pairwise sine exchange can induce collective pressure, while
finite hidden capacity retains memory.

### Two immediate identifiability constraints

Equal instantaneous form rates can conceal different capacities and later
accelerations. Equal visible states can conceal different hidden phases and
future responses. Reconstructing pressure from the evaluated derivative
resolves neither issue. Reuse the [parameter counterexamples](NODAL_PARAMETER_FOUNDATIONS.md#32-an-epi-rate-need-not-identify-capacity-even-with-a-fixed-pressure-law)
and [hidden-state observability](nodal/RELATIONAL_PATTERN_MEMORY.md#sine-hidden-state-observability),
including their rank and uncertainty limits.

## 3. Reusable mechanisms and their boundaries

### Geometry inherited from dynamics

The [scale owner](TNFR_SCALE_GEOMETRY_AND_BRIDGE.md) distinguishes sufficient
collective state from a scalar observation. Actual support symmetries can remove
redundant labels while internal form, phase and capacity correlations remain.
Joint identity, autonomous compensated exchange and restored current at a zero
resultant are compatible conditional mechanisms; they do not establish an
operator selector, attraction or primitive edge birth.

The [complete-law resonance derivative](nodal/RESONANCE_FOUNDATIONS.md#resonance-tangent)
controls stability claims. For example, `n>=5` follows from strict acuteness for
one uniform winding-one cycle certificate; it is not a universal minimum NFR.
Reuse a geometric restriction only with its support and complete-law hypotheses.

### Formation must respect the conservation boundary

Formation means organization absent initially and present later under a stated
identity criterion. The [conservative obstruction](nodal/RESONANCE_FOUNDATIONS.md#conservative-formation-boundary)
separates finite observed episodes from attraction to an invariant family.
Discarding hidden constituents cannot create irreversible loss in the full law.

## 5. Physical interpretation and terrestrial evidence

### Measurement correspondence versus generative emergence

Mathematical variable definition, independent measurement and complete
evolution are separate obligations. P1–P5 require correct diagnostics,
separate calibration/evaluation, a measurement/clock/preparation bridge,
a frozen reserved response, and compatible memory/maintenance tests.
Retrospective pressure reconstruction cannot validate its own prediction.

[Passive transport](research/PASSIVE_TRANSPORT_PROTOCOL.md) and
[phase/amplitude measurement](research/PHASE_AMPLITUDE_MEASUREMENT_PROTOCOL.md)
own admission. No public dataset has passed the complete physical gate.
[TCLab](research/TCLAB_EXPLORATORY_PROTOCOL.md) remains evaluated exploration
with residual bias and incomplete identification; those observations cannot
become reserved data again. [Volts](research/PASSIVE_TRANSPORT_PROTOCOL.md#volts-model-boundary)
belongs to an earlier model, and [reported EEG evidence](research/archive/REPORTED_INTERFACE_OBSERVATIONS.md)
retains its external provenance.

Use public terrestrial data accessible by workstation, or measurements possible
at the inhabited surface with an ordinary research laboratory. Outer-space
data and large-facility programmes are outside scope. A plausible waveform
without support, input, clock and uncertainty information is insufficient.

### A physical atlas as a selection method

The [physical atlas](PHYSICAL_REGIME_CORRESPONDENCES.md) compares mechanisms,
standard equations, observations, possible TNFR reductions and discriminators.
It is a comparison library, not a second task queue. A phase variable does
not establish Josephson physics; a correlation diagnostic does not establish
criticality or Kibble–Zurek scaling. A useful equivalence may validate one
bridge without selecting a fundamental ontology. New physics requires an
independently justified prediction that distinguishes relevant alternatives.

## 6. Ownership, stopping and reproducibility

Use the plan's F1–F4 admission method: sufficient state; complete laws and
premises; joint consistency; then a discriminating proof or frozen prediction.
Choose bounded questions that remove a genuine uncertainty. Stop when the
stated claim is proved, excluded, or limited by a precisely identified missing
premise. Do not replace a closed obstruction with an unbounded parameter scan.

Maintain proofs with their owners, execution with shared engine contracts,
and examples with the public workflow. Retain source/configuration, support,
clock, inputs, precision, uncertainty and original verdicts. Exact theorems,
validated finite enclosures, ordinary numerical observations and diagnostics
provide different evidence. Passing tests is not a physical identification.
Only the [execution plan](research/FIVE_STAGE_EXECUTION_PLAN.md#current-g3-gate)
can reactivate a deferred branch; archive documents remain evidence.
