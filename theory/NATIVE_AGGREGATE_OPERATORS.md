# Aggregate operators and native transitions

This owner asks when several registered constituent actions are exactly one
registered action on a specified aggregate. It uses the existing shared
operator implementation and [ordered-language descent criterion](STRUCTURAL_OPERATORS.md#collective-grammar-descent).
The [native collective calculus](NATIVE_COLLECTIVE_CALCULUS.md) instead retains
the constituent state needed to describe native execution. A sufficient
coordinate description does not itself establish operator inheritance.
This owner also identifies the state needed when an inherited partial
transition reaches its clipping boundary.
The [research program](RESEARCH_PROGRAM.md#research-current-question)
owns the research queue.

The results below concern a declared arithmetic aggregation and restricted
event or native-transition domains. They neither define the only possible
collective NFR nor derive a universal collective law, organizational identity
or physical event occurrence. A configured selector remains a supplied policy
even when its decisions descend to the aggregate.

<a id="native-resonance-inheritance-domain"></a>
## A sufficient domain for one aggregate Resonance

**Conditional result.** Two consecutive Resonance (RA) actions on a pair of
nonadjacent twins can inherit exactly one registered RA on their aggregate,
with unchanged factors. Internal form and capacity contrasts can both be
nonzero. Paired phases, a common strict form sign and semantic kind, and
duplicated consumed grammar context supply one sufficient domain. These are
additional premises, not consequences of averaging or necessities of TNFR.

### State, support and execution domain

Use the unit, undirected doubled C5 support from the native calculus: block
`i` contains two nonadjacent nodes, each connected to both members of blocks
`i-1` and `i+1` (indices modulo five). The comparison graph is the simple
unit C5. Its node `i` is assigned the following aggregate channels:

```text
x_(i,-)=M_i-d_i,       x_(i,+)=M_i+d_i,
v_(i,-)=V_i-e_i,       v_(i,+)=V_i+e_i,
theta_(i,-)=theta_(i,+)=mu_i,
Q_i=(M_i,V_i,mu_i,kind_i,context_i).
```

Here `x=EPI` is signed form and `v=nu_f` is capacity. The phase coordinates
use an explicit common real lift of width less than both mathematical pi
and the represented runtime pi. They are not form-derived angles. Each
coarse edge represents one neighboring block; equal fine multiplicity,
rather than summed conductance, supplies the neighbor-average identity.
This statement is about RA's actual unweighted compatible-neighbor reader,
not a proposed diffusion law.
The exact phase argument uses the ideal circle of period `2*pi`, with
represented coefficients and cutoff, and compares endpoints on that common
lift. Runtime modulo by represented `2*pi` and trigonometric rounding are
separate realization errors, not exact circle identities.

Require the following common domain at each completed-pair boundary:

- Every fine form belongs to one convex interval strictly inside the shared
  hard-clipping rails. The interval is either wholly above the operational
  trigger `T=1e-9` or wholly below `-T`. All nodes have one common nonempty
  `epi_kind`. Thus a convex blend preserves both strict sign and kind.
- All primitive values and configuration consumed by the selected path pass
  their actual admission. In particular `V_i>|e_i|`, and the fine and coarse
  amplified capacity proposals have finite representable arithmetic. A
  finite continuation needs sufficient headroom at every occurrence; direct
  RA does not itself impose the runtime's later `VF_MAX` clamp.
- Fine and coarse graphs share the admitted factors
  `rho=RA_epi_diff`, `b=RA_vf_amplification`, `c=RA_phase_coupling`, with
  `0<rho<=1`, `b>0`, `0<c<=1`, and the same admitted U3 phase limit `L`.
  Every requested block has at least one live compatible neighbor.
- Both twins and their coarse node have equal, separately owned consumed
  grammar context: bounded trace, persistent U2 debt, prior-Coherence flag,
  effective local operator ordinal and retention policy. The common context
  must admit RA under the shared grammar policy. For example, supplied
  Emission (AL), Coherence (IL) history with zero debt is sufficient; it is
  not a necessary precursor or a derived history.

Use the strict caller `enforce_canonical_grammar` followed by `apply_glyph`,
with `GRAMMAR_REJECTION_MODE="raise"`. A supplied coarse target expands into
the two fine targets consecutively. There is no interleaved action, flow,
pressure preparation, phase coordinator, capacity adaptation or callback
between them. Support stays fixed. Both actions retain the direct path's
secondary effects; this is neither a public-class equivalence nor an atomic
stage. Existing valid stored pressure can be retained, but no pressure law
is inferred or refreshed by these RA requests.

Optional telemetry may be collected and compared separately. It is not
included in `Q`: two actual fine occurrences do not become one occurrence
record. Exclude custom observers that feed additional state into execution.

### Same selected data, same registered action

On the declared common lift, the live admitted block set and its selected
means are

```text
J_i={j in {i-1,i+1}: abs(mu_j-mu_i)<=L},       J_i nonempty,
S_i=(sum_(j in J_i) M_j)/|J_i|,
eta_i=Arg_I(sum_(j in J_i) exp(i*mu_j)).
```

The phase lift of width less than pi makes the selected resultant nonzero
and fixes its angle in the same interval. U3's wrapped differences give
exactly this `J_i`. Both fine targets select both members of each block in
`J_i` and no others. Consequently their selected form mean is

```text
sum_(j in J_i) ((M_j-d_j)+(M_j+d_j))/(2*|J_i|) = S_i.
```

Their selected phase resultant is twice the coarse resultant, so their
phase target is exactly `eta_i`. The two twins are nonadjacent: the first
action changes none of the second's neighbors. Both consume these same
selected data despite executing sequentially. The common strict form
interval gives `abs(S_i)>T`, so every target uses the same registered
capacity factor `f=1+b`. Common kind and convex, unclipped form mixing
admit all fine identity proposals and the coarse proposal.

Applying the actual RA row to each twin and taking its mean and difference
therefore gives

```text
M_i'=(1-rho)*M_i+rho*S_i,       d_i'=(1-rho)*d_i,
V_i'=f*V_i,                    e_i'=f*e_i,
mu_i'=(1-c)*mu_i+c*eta_i,       a_i'=0.
```

These three mean rows and the unchanged kind are exactly one registered
coarse RA with the same factors. All other blocks are unchanged. In ideal
real arithmetic, writing `P_i` for the completed fine pair and `R_i` for
the registered coarse action, this proves

```text
Q(P_i(s)) = R_i(Q(s))
```

on the stated domain, including the consumed local grammar projection
below. This is stronger than copying identical nodes: for `rho<1`, a nonzero
internal form contrast survives, and any nonzero capacity contrast is
amplified. Their event response need not be visible in the coarse primary
channels for those channels to obey the same registered event rule.

### Admission, context and finite composition

For the compared actions, sign, kind, factor and represented-arithmetic
admission are premises on the whole fine domain, not deductions from a
coarse pass. With these premises, paired phases identify U3 admission on
both sides. An empty live `J_i` rejects both paths before the first fine
event. Identical valid contexts identify the remaining incremental grammar
decisions. Resonance is U2-neutral and has no U4 transformer/trigger
requirement; it does not erase an already excessive persistent debt.

Each twin appends one RA and advances its local ordinal from `k` to `k+1`;
the coarse target does the same. Persistent debt and prior-Coherence remain
unchanged. The projected history is the common associated local history,
not a concatenation of both traces or a sum of their ordinals. This supplies
the consumed context equality needed by the partial-transition criterion.

Only completed pairs belong to this projection domain. After the first
fine event, target phases and histories can differ. That intermediate state
retains its fine description and pending second target; it is not the
coarse endpoint. A second-target rejection outside the sufficient domain
does not roll back the accepted first target.

Convex form mixing and phase motion preserve their respective common
intervals, common kind and paired phases at completed-pair boundaries.
Induction therefore gives the same projected successors for any finite
supplied sequence of complete pairs, provided live U3 admission and output
representability remain valid. Recompute `J_i`, `S_i` and `eta_i` after
earlier completed pairs; adjacent targets consume the updated state.
If the initial lift diameter is strictly less than `L`, phase convexity
keeps every neighbor compatible throughout such a finite sequence. This
stronger sufficient corollary removes the U3-renewal obligation, but not
finite capacity headroom. Repeated RA fragments are not automatically
closed grammatical sentences; incremental admission does not discharge
unconsumed U5/U6 or complete-word closure obligations.

### Exact scope and represented controls

The algebra above is exact-real mathematics with the specified coefficients.
Binary64 reductions of two versus four selected nodes, two-stage blends,
trigonometric means and capacity products need not be bit-identical.
Threshold-adjacent selection/trigger decisions and overflow require their
own margins. A finite coarse capacity alone cannot certify finite fine
proposals. This result makes no unrestricted binary64 admission-equivalence
or numerical-enclosure claim.

The [shared-runtime controls](../tests/physics/test_native_aggregate_resonance.py)
freeze independent predictions before dispatch on one rational source and
its all-negated form counterpart. Both have nonzero internal form and
capacity contrasts, paired phases and supplied AL/IL contexts. They execute
block 0 followed by adjacent block 1, using unchanged default factors.
Block 0 admits two neighboring blocks; block 1 admits only the updated
block 0, excluding its other neighbor by the actual U3 gate. Thus the
comparison tests live partial selection rather than requiring all edges
to be compatible. It verifies the mean rows, surviving contrasts, kind,
local context and singleton-prefix boundary with an explicit floating
residual tolerance. The controls stay in a positive non-wrapping phase
chart; they do not certify runtime wrap equivalence to the ideal circle.

Separate sign, kind and persistent-debt controls keep the coarse request
admitted while the second fine request rejects and the first remains
committed. Their aggregate primary channels are rebuilt from the modified
fine primitives. A heterogeneous kind or history has no proved aggregate
map: those negative controls explicitly supply the first member's label
and context. They demonstrate the insufficiency of that choice outside
the theorem, not an alternative canonical projection.

Optional RA metrics expose four fine event records versus two coarse
records for the two-block sequence, with twice as many selected neighbors
per fine target. Stored pressure and structural time remain unchanged;
these direct events create no physical temporal secants. No native selector,
complete runtime call or trajectory is executed by these controls.

### Why one inherited operator is not a complete collective law

The existing [joint form/capacity calculus](NATIVE_COLLECTIVE_CALCULUS.md#native-joint-collective-calculus)
already separates event inheritance from the subsequent nodal row. With
paired phases, write each prepared fine pressure as `p_-=B+C`, `p_+=B-C`,
where `C=k*d+b_v*e` on the declared regular support. Its mean pressure can
equal the ordinary coarse native pressure `B`. Nevertheless, after a
common RA boost `f`, retaining that prepared pressure through the flow gives

```text
fine mean form rate = f*(V*B-e*C),
ordinary coarse form rate = f*V*B.
```

The covariance `-f*e*C` generally remains nonzero in the positive event
domain. This reuses the existing held-pressure result; it supplies no new
pressure law. Constituent pressure peaks, normalized Si, temporal samples
and native request selection have further information requirements. They
do not descend merely because mean pressure or this event descends.

The admitted aggregate action consequently establishes a restricted
cross-scale operator correspondence. A complete collective dynamical law,
organizational identity, its maintenance or acquisition, and the occurrence
of the supplied paired requests remain separate questions under the
[NFR ontology](EMERGENT_ONTOLOGY.md#collective-memory-and-operators).

## Fine Resonance does not universally inherit one aggregate Resonance

The [complete-call map](NATIVE_COLLECTIVE_CALCULUS.md#native-split-phase-call) retains associated constituent state. A stronger claim
would identify the admitted two-target Resonance (RA) action with **one**
registered RA on a collective node whose capacity is the arithmetic mean
`V=(v_-+v_+)/2`, using the same amplification factor b. This is a specified
aggregation candidate, not a definition required by TNFR. Its capacity row
alone has a necessary obstruction, independently of how aggregate form,
phase, kind, neighbors or grammar might be completed.

Assume both constituent capacities are positive and exactly one selected
signed mean activates amplification. In exact-real arithmetic with the
represented factor, the admitted fine action gives

```text
V_star=V+b*v_active/2,
0<v_active/2<V,
V < V_star < (1+b)*V,                       b>0.
```

These are ideal inequalities, not universal binary64 strict inequalities:
an amplification can round away at a small factor or capacity. Represented
separation needs its own margin, as in the default-sized control below.

One registered RA with input capacity V has only the two possible capacity
outputs `V` and `(1+b)*V`, according to its own selected-mean trigger. Neither
equals the fine aggregate above. Therefore no choice of aggregate trigger,
retained hidden information or grammar can make these capacity rows agree
while keeping this capacity map and the unchanged registered factor.
This is failure of that universal operator-intertwining claim, not another
claim that means omit information. It already holds for an event endpoint,
before flow, coordination or capacity adaptation.

The [existing composition source](../tests/physics/test_native_split_phase_composition.py) supplies an admitted control: `v_-=7/8`,
`v_+=9/8`, `V=1`, and only the odd constituent activates amplification. Thus
`V_star=1+9*b/16`, strictly between the two aggregate outputs. The control
compares the existing fine response with both branches of the shared
registered capacity proposal; it introduces no new source or trajectory.

When both triggers agree, this capacity obstruction disappears. Matching
capacity is not sufficient: aggregate form/sign/kind, phase action, U3 and
associated grammar still need their own common-domain proof under the
[ordered-action criterion](STRUCTURAL_OPERATORS.md#collective-grammar-descent).
An effective factor `b*v_active/(2*V)` would reproduce this one capacity row,
but would be a state-dependent collective rule, not the unchanged registered
RA factor. Different aggregate capacity maps also require independent
justification. The result does not exclude richer collective NFRs, formation,
or all forms of operator inheritance across scales.

## An actual selected native transition at uniform capacity

**Conditional result.** On a separate uniform-capacity sector, a fine
doubled-C5 network and an ordinary coarse C5 have the same projected ideal
native transition under the existing default selector, without supplying an
operator word. Nonzero internal form contrasts are allowed. The transition
retains form means, common capacity, paired primitive phases, semantic kind,
associated grammar/lag context, phase gains and clock. It need not retain
fine pressure normalization, Si, stability counters or complete temporal
histories. This is a partial reduction of a specified runtime model, not an
identification of the two complete graph states.

The proof combines the [native writer domain](nodal/PRIMITIVE_PHASE_CLOSURE.md#native-phase-writer-closure),
the [conditional moment rows](NATIVE_COLLECTIVE_CALCULUS.md#native-collective-calculus)
and the [ordered-language criterion](STRUCTURAL_OPERATORS.md#collective-grammar-descent).
The new obligation is descent of the **actual selected** transition to an
ordinary coarse runtime, after discarding internal form contrast. A
conditional common request or equal mean pressure alone did not prove it.

### Selection and associated grammar really descend

makes its capacity term the full default normalized weight `alpha`, where
`alpha>si_hi` (approximately `0.7585>0.5`). Consequently every unforced base
decision is Coherence (IL), even if fine and coarse pressure maxima and Si
values differ. This uses the default selector, which does not apply the
parametric selector's soft repetition policy.

The batch increments lags before comparison and then chooses

```text
g_i = AL  if a_i+1 > AL_MAX_LAG,
      EN  else if r_i+1 > EN_MAX_LAG,

```

Emission (AL) has priority when both limits are exceeded. With the actual
defaults the limits are five and three. These decisions depend only on
retained counters and are frozen before the ordered event sweep. Duplicated

and advance the same local ordinal once, with the shared debt/prior-Coherence

```text
IL: (a_i',r_i')=(a_i+1,r_i+1).
```

Neither concatenated twin histories nor a doubled local ordinal is used.
The actual number of fine events remains twice the coarse count.

### Mean flow and hidden contrast

```text
p_(i,-)=B_i+k*d_i,       p_(i,+)=B_i-k*d_i.
```

Capacity contrast is zero; regular degree contrast is also zero on both

held through this call's events and integration, not refreshed after an

For Reception, earlier complete pairs have already changed the live neighbor

```text
M_i'=Y_i+delta*nu*H_i*B_i,
```

The first row is the ordinary coarse held-pressure form step. The multiplier

Old rate and physical history still determine individual accelerations and
stored temporal records, but do not change this selected primary successor.

### Phase gains and capacity feedback

Events in this sublanguage leave primitive phase and capacity unchanged.
Duplicated phases give the same normalized global resultant magnitude and
angle on both graphs, and the same local neighbor-resultant angle. Equal
per-block glyph histories duplicate every raw count while preserving every
proportion, including the disruptor fraction. The actual phase controller
therefore sees the same inputs and previous gains, takes the same branch
and produces the same updated gains `k_G,k_L`.

On the common lift its ordinary coordinator is

```text
mu_i'=(1-k_G-k_L)*mu_i+k_G*beta+k_L*N_i(mu),
beta=Arg_I(sum_j exp(i*mu_j)).
```

The default nonnegative gain bounds have sum below one, so the lifted phase
hull remains inside its incoming interval and fine phases stay paired.
This is the actual configured relaxation step, not a new continuous phase
law or an identification of capacity with angular velocity.

Capacity qualification can differ: it consumes prepared Si and held
post-event pressure. The counts and write eligibility therefore need not
descend. Nevertheless every immutable neighboring capacity mean equals
`nu`, so both an eligible proposal and an ineligible no-write leave capacity
equal to `nu`. The shared fixed-point guard preserves this equality in the
represented controls too. No assumption of equal counters or equal due flags
is needed for the primary capacity successor. This statement depends on
global uniformity, not merely equal capacities inside each pair.

### An aggregate-testable margin and its stopping boundary

Let `(x_min,x_max)` be the common form rails. Admit a step only when all
incoming means, live event means and predicted flow means satisfy

```text
x_min+D < M_i, Y_i, M_i' < x_max-D,
abs(A_i-delta*nu*H_i*k) <= 1.
```

All quantities in this test use retained state, configuration and the
already determined lag choices; no hidden fine response is read. For the
defaults `0<m<1` and `0<h<1`, the common sufficient step bound
`delta*nu*k<=2-m` implies the second inequality for all three actions.
The event multipliers obey `0<=A_i<=1`. Hence every fine lift with
`|d_i|<=D` stays inside the rails at events and flow endpoints, and
`|d_i'|<=D`. For a single Euler interval the held form interpolation also
stays inside those rails. Inactive clipping justifies, rather than assumes
from a single evaluated lift, the affine rows used in the proof.

The retained context, gains, common capacity and phase domain are preserved.
At the successor, evaluate the next margin using the new retained state.
Induction gives a unique projected transition along any finite segment for
which the margins continue to hold. Margin failure stops this certificate;
it does not claim that the native engine rejects or that no larger domain
can work. An empty interior interval, changed callbacks/selector/support,
invalid temporal evidence or nonrepresentable arithmetic cannot be repaired
by a coarse pass. No all-time form-rail invariant is asserted.

lift. Its two post-IL pressures fall on opposite sides of the default

are checked against an explicit tolerance, not promoted to bit-exact

The theorem establishes a nontrivial lossy native transition with retained

## Finite validity horizon under recurrent Emission

**Conditional result.** The default lag policy prevents indefinite renewal
of the strict, unclipped domain of the
[uniform-capacity transition](#native-uniform-capacity-transition).
Emission (AL) adds positive form recurrently. Ordered Reception (EN) and
the held form flow retain a positive part of that input, while the adverse
phase contribution has a finite total bound. Consequently a strict form
margin must fail after finitely many calls, unless another premise fails
earlier. This is a limit of that partial affine description under the
specified policy, not a failure of the engine or an exclusion of collective
organization in other domains.

Keep all premises of the preceding ideal transition, including the actual
default selector, strict grammar, globally uniform capacity, paired phases,
common kind/context, target order, no callbacks and REMESH abstention.
The argument uses the ideal real-arithmetic stage formulas with resolved
represented coefficients. It does not assert a validated binary64 horizon.
No additional trajectory, source search or continuation law is used.

### Ordered events retain positive input

Index calls by `n=0,1,...`, so `M_n` is the vector of incoming block means.
Let `N` be the C5 neighbor-mean matrix, and put

```text
z=delta*nu*k,      lambda=1-m-z,
b_AL=GLYPH_FACTORS['AL_boost']>0,
m=GLYPH_FACTORS['EN_mix'],      h=GLYPH_FACTORS['IL_dnfr_factor'].
```

Here `k` is the resolved default EPI pressure weight and `delta=1/16`.
The proof requires `lambda>0`, a stronger bound than the earlier contrast
nonexpansion criterion. The actual defaults satisfy it throughout the
admitted positive capacity interval: using the represented `VF_MAX`,
`z_max<0.071925`, `1-m>0.758546` and `lambda>0.686622`.
These are outward rational comparisons, not identities between separately
rounded constants. In particular the proof does not require `h=1-m`.

The complete ordered event sweep has an affine representation

```text
Y_n=E_n*M_n+b_n.
```

Each Reception updates its row to a convex combination of its current
value and the two live neighboring rows. Emission and Coherence (IL) have
identity linear parts on form. Products of these updates give

```text
E_n>=0,       E_n*1=1,       (E_n)_(i,i)>=1-m,
b_n>=0,       (b_n)_i=b_AL whenever block i executes AL.
```

Every target is updated only once. Immediately before its own Reception
update its row is still the corresponding identity row, so its diagonal
keeps at least `1-m`; previous neighbor updates can add more. There is no
factor `(1-m)^5` at this stage. Similarly a block's own Emission input is
not removed by later updates of other targets. Neighboring Reception may
carry that positive input into additional rows.

Let `H_n` have entries one for AL/EN and `h` for IL. The independent pressure
was prepared **before** these events, so the held Euler transition is

```text
M_(n+1)=W_n*M_n+b_n+f_n,
W_n=E_n+z*diag(H_n)*(N-I),
f_n=delta*nu*(w_phase/pi_rep)*diag(H_n)*(N-I)*mu_n.
```

It is not a diffusion matrix applied to `E_n*M_n+b_n`. That alternative
would refresh pressure from event-modified form and change the model.
Since `0<h<1`, the off-diagonal entries of `W_n` are nonnegative, its
diagonal is at least `lambda`, and its row sums equal one. Thus `W_n` is
nonnegative and row-stochastic. Such a matrix preserves a constant lower
bound and does not increase the sup norm of an adverse forcing term.
Neither the event sweep nor this argument assumes conservation of the
arithmetic sum of forms; ordered Reception and target-dependent pressure
retention do not supply that conservation law.

### Six-call recurrence and a finite phase budget

The actual Emission counter increments before comparison with five and
resets only on an accepted AL. With an admitted nonnegative initial lag
`a_i`, its first AL is on one-based call `max(1,6-a_i)`, then every sixth
call. Reception never resets this counter, and AL has priority when both
lags exceed their limits. Hence each block receives an AL in every six
consecutive successful calls. No assumption about a periodic phase
controller or an externally supplied word is needed.

Write `Delta_n` for the width of the inherited phase lift at call `n`.
These three event types write no phase. The
[coordinator diameter bound](nodal/PRIMITIVE_PHASE_CLOSURE.md#native-phase-contrast-budget) gives

```text
g_min=PHASE_ADAPT['kG_min']>0,       rho=1-g_min in (0,1),
Delta_(n+1)<=rho*Delta_n,
F0=delta*nu*(w_phase/pi_rep)*Delta_0,
||f_n||_infinity<=F0*rho^n,
sum_(n>=0) ||f_n||_infinity<=F=F0/g_min.
```

Use the stored gain floor, not its nominal pi-based expression. The shared
default controller clamps the updated gain above that floor for every
branch. The proof only bounds phase forcing; it does not set it to zero
or identify it as an emitted form input. Gamma being zero during flow
likewise does not remove the separate hybrid Emission jumps.

Within a six-call block, each coordinate has its own AL input at some
position. At most five subsequent matrices follow it. Their diagonal
entries retain at least

```text
J=b_AL*lambda^5>0
```

of that input in its original coordinate. All other propagated emission
contributions are nonnegative. Products of the stochastic matrices preserve
the incoming minimum and bound the negative phase contribution in sup norm.
Therefore, for any prefix that keeps the stated affine domain,

```text
min_i M_(6K,i) >= min_i M_(0,i) + K*J
                 - F0*(1-rho^(6K))/(1-rho)
              >= min_i M_(0,i) + K*J - F.
```

The factor `lambda^5` is a conservative self-contribution bound. It requires
neither equal lag phases across blocks nor a particular Reception schedule.
The phase sum may oppose the increase, but cannot offset an unlimited
number of positive six-call contributions.

### The exact pair projection and its branches

### Ordered events and the incoming pressure association

At the start of a call, orient each pair for the calculation as


The two twins are nonadjacent and have identical neighboring pairs. The

The compact pair formula for IL simply returns its admitted input.

```text
u_i=Y_i+delta*nu*H_i*B_i,
v_i=T_i-z*H_i*s_i,       z=delta*nu*k,
M_i'=C(u_i,abs(v_i)),    s_i'=S(u_i,abs(v_i)).
```

The subtraction consumes **incoming** `s_i`, not the event spread `T_i`.
The raw constituent values are `u_i-v_i` and `u_i+v_i`, associated with
the initially lower and upper form respectively. `v_i` can be negative:
full Emission saturation has `T_i=0`, yet held pressure can recreate a
spread and reverse that ordering during flow. Replacing `s_i` by `T_i`
would lose this response. Taking `abs(v_i)` in the final symmetric pair
projection is legitimate; discarding the incoming association before
forming `v_i` is not. A mid-call continuation would need to retain the
incoming held pressure information explicitly.

refreshed pressure step. In particular the stored rates remain

### Fine deficits retain both pressure stages

Use individual constituent indices `j`, not only block means. Write

```text
R=U-L>0,       y_j=U-x_j in [0,R],       D_n=max_j y_(n,j),
```

`N_j` denotes the mean over the four fine neighbors. Because the support


Every target is visited once. Emission cannot increase a deficit, and

```text
y_j'=clip_[0,R](y_j^E-z*H_j*y_j+z*H_j*N_j(incoming y)-f_(n,j)).
```

The neighbor values in the event and flow terms belong to different

### One-call bounds without a monotone-map assumption

The complete clipped map is not generally order-preserving. For example,
use the default rails `[-1,1]`, zero internal spread, uniform phase and

```text
y_j'<=max(D_n-b,z*D_n)+F_n <= a*D_n+F_n,

```

Indeed, the first branch is bounded by `(1-z)*D_n+z*D_n-b`, and the

Both coefficients retaining the target's incoming deficit are positive
and at least `lambda`: `1-z*h>=1-m-z`. The right-hand sides are
nonnegative, so the final projection is again harmless for these upper
bounds. In particular all three cases imply

```text
D_(n+1)<=D_n+F_n.
```

This estimate allows temporary increases in the largest deficit; it does
not assume that every form increases at every call.

### A strict improvement in every six-call block

Fix a block of six calls starting at `n`, and define its accumulated
forcing allowance

```text
E_j=sum_(r=0)^(j-1) F_(n+r),       E_0=0.
```

The running maximum is bounded by `D_(n+j)<=D_n+E_j`. The actual lag
policy gives each constituent exactly one accepted Emission in those six
calls, even from an arbitrary admitted initial lag. If its Emission is at
offset `j`, its next deficit satisfies

```text
y_(n+j+1)<=a*(D_n+E_j)+F_(n+j)
         <=D_n+E_(j+1)-(1-a)*D_n.
```

There are at most five later calls in the block, and none is another
Emission at that target. For either of the remaining choices, a gap `g`
below the running allowance is retained by at least `lambda*g`. The
next forcing contribution is included in the next allowance. Thus every
constituent ends the block at most
`D_n+E_6-(1-a)*lambda^5*D_n`, and

```text
D_(n+6)<=Q*D_n+sum_(r=0)^5 F_(n+r),
Q=1-(1-a)*lambda^5 in (0,1).
```

This argument uses the true per-target lag recurrence and scalar row
bounds. It does not require synchronized lag phases, conservation of
total form, or monotonicity of the complete transition. In particular it
remains valid when an Emission saturates and held pressure subsequently
moves the same form away from the rail.

### Convergence along an indefinitely admitted ideal continuation

Set `tau=rho^6` and `A=F0*sum_(r=0)^5 rho^r`. Iterating the block bound

```text
D_(6K)<=Q^K*D_0+A*sum_(j=0)^(K-1) Q^(K-1-j)*tau^j.
```

the stated constituent and block-mean limits. The internal spread

theorem, not an extension of the smaller uniform-capacity quotient.

Use the resolved represented coefficients as exact constants in the ideal
model, rather than replacing them by intended decimal or symbolic values:

```text
c=w_vf>0,  k=w_epi>0,  ell=w_phase/pi_rep,
Z=delta*V*k,  lambda=1-m-Z>0,
a=max(1-b/(U-L),Z),  Q=1-(1-a)*lambda^5,
C(V)=c*(1+6*Z/(1-Q)).
```

The default parameters give `0<Z<min(1/2,1-m)` and `0<a,Q<1` throughout
this capacity range. The additional sufficient restriction is strict:

```text
C(V)*epsilon < EPS_DNFR_STABLE.
```

It is not a necessary boundary of convergence. Failure of this sufficient
inequality leaves the behavior undecided.

### Invariant selection with arbitrary eligible adaptation subsets

The actual [capacity adapter](../src/tnfr/dynamics/adaptation.py) proposes
`(1-mu)*nu_j+mu*N_j(nu)` from one immutable snapshot at eligible nodes and
leaves the other capacities unchanged. Its admitted coefficients make
every proposal convex. Thus any subset of eligible writes preserves the
initial capacity hull; this does not yet prove consensus or conservation
of the initial mean.

Fresh Si has nonnegative components and satisfies
`Si_j>=alpha*nu_j/max_l(nu_l)>=alpha*v_-/v_+>si_hi`.
The default selector therefore proposes Coherence (IL); the retained lags
override it only by Emission (AL) or Reception (EN). These three direct
events preserve capacity. This establishes an induction across complete
calls: selection preserves the hull before adaptation, and adaptation
preserves it afterward. No later selection or successful adaptation is
assumed in advance. Unequal capacities or stability counts do not break
paired choices in this high-Si sector. Phase coordination consumes no
capacity and preserves the inherited paired-phase contraction.

### A pressure bound before permanent qualification

At each call, the held flow uses its own incoming capacity, with
`z_(n,j)=delta*nu_(n,j)*k<=Z`. The pressure capacity channel is the signed
neighbor contrast `c*(N_j(nu)-nu_j)`, not a normalized contrast. Hull
preservation bounds its magnitude by `c*epsilon` under every eligible mask.
Keep `D_n=max_j(U-x_(n,j))` and the preceding phase diameter bound
`Delta_n<=rho^n*Delta_0`. The two form forcing allowances are

```text
F_n=delta*V*ell*Delta_0*rho^n,
G=delta*V*c*epsilon.
```

The pressure retained by a selected event is multiplied by `H_j=1` or
`h<1`. The [fine-deficit proof](#native-uniform-capacity-limit) therefore
applies with variable `z_(n,j)<=Z`: Emission gives at most
`a*D_n+F_n+G`; subsequent Reception or Coherence retains a target's gap
below the running allowance by at least `lambda`. Every constituent still
has one accepted Emission in every six-call window. Consequently, for
**every** starting call `n`,

```text
D_(n+6) <= Q*D_n + sum_(r=0)^5 F_(n+r) + 6*G.
```

Iterate on each of the six residue classes. The initial term and geometric
phase convolution vanish; the constant forcing sum is bounded by
`6*G/(1-Q)`. Thus

```text
limsup_(n->infinity) D_n <= 6*G/(1-Q).
```

No additional allowance for intervening calls is needed because the same
recurrence holds on every residue class. Neither capacity convergence nor
a nonempty eligible mask has been used.

The freshly prepared pressure satisfies

```text
max_j abs(p_(n,j)) <= k*D_n+c*epsilon+ell*Delta_n.
```

Indeed, all incoming forms lie in `[U-D_n,U]`, so their difference from a
neighbor mean is at most `D_n`, not `2*D_n`. Regular support removes the
topology contrast. Applying the preceding bound gives

```text
limsup_(n->infinity) max_j abs(H_j*p_(n,j)) <= C(V)*epsilon
                                                   < EPS_DNFR_STABLE.
```

This is the pressure actually consumed by adaptation: prepared pressure
after its glyph retention, without refresh after flow or phase coordination.
The adapter also consumes the incoming prepared Si, whose strict floor
already holds. The strict limiting pressure margin implies that from
some finite call onward both gates qualify at every node on every call.
An equality at the threshold would not suffice: the abstract sequence
`EPS_DNFR_STABLE+1/(n+1)` approaches the threshold without ever passing.
This is a counterexample to that limiting inference, not a native response.

### Permanent averaging and a vanishing forcing remainder

Qualification increments each stability count; a successful write does
not reset it. After at most `VF_ADAPT_TAU` further qualifying calls, all
nodes therefore update on every call. With `mu=VF_ADAPT_MU` and the fixed
fine neighbor-mean matrix `N`, the ideal recurrence becomes

```text
nu_(n+1)=P*nu_n,        P=(1-mu)*I+mu*N.
```

On the doubled unit `C5`, `P` is doubly stochastic. The entries of `P^2`
in each row have four classes:

| Target relative to the row node | Count | Entry |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Same node | 1 | `(1-mu)^2+mu^2/4` |
| Its twin | 1 | `mu^2/4` |
| Adjacent blocks | 4 | `mu*(1-mu)/2` |
| Nonadjacent blocks | 4 | `mu^2/8` |

For the actual default `0<mu<4/5`, all entries are at least
`eta=mu^2/8>0`. Subtracting `eta` from every entry leaves a nonnegative
matrix with each row sum `1-10*eta`. Its constant component cancels in
differences, proving directly

```text
osc(P^2*nu) <= (1-10*eta)*osc(nu),       0<1-10*eta<1.
```

Hence capacities converge to one positive value in the initial hull.
Double stochasticity preserves the mean once full updating starts. The
limit is that mean, not necessarily the initial mean: earlier partial
writes need not conserve total capacity. No externally selected target
capacity or physical scale follows.

Now the actual width `epsilon_n=osc(nu_n)` tends to zero. Replace `G` in
the six-call bound by the actual per-call allowance
`G_n=delta*V*c*epsilon_n`. Both phase and capacity forcing vanish. For
each residue class, a recurrence with uniform `Q<1` and a vanishing
nonnegative remainder tends to zero: split its geometric convolution
into a finite initial part and a tail bounded by the remainder supremum
divided by `1-Q`. Therefore `D_n->0`. The inherited nested phase intervals
converge to one angle, and the complete prepared pressure tends to zero.

### An explicit nonzero neighborhood without source search

Set `v0=1/2` and use the conservative envelope `Vbar=1`. Compute `Cbar`
from `C(Vbar)` above and define

```text
r_star=min(v0/2,
           v0*(alpha-si_hi)/(2*(alpha+si_hi)),
           EPS_DNFR_STABLE/(4*Cbar)).
```

Every term is positive under the unchanged defaults. For any fine initial
capacities in `[v0-r_star,v0+r_star]`, the lower endpoint is positive and
the upper is at most `3/4<Vbar<VF_MAX`. The second term leaves a strict
Si margin; the width is at most `2*r_star`, so the limiting pressure
bound is at most half the stability threshold. The construction admits
arbitrary fine forms within the inherited rails and retains the other
phase, grammar, history and execution premises. It selects no response,
trajectory, natural scale or new law.

The [capacity robustness controls](../tests/physics/test_native_capacity_robustness.py)
check the derived threshold with exact represented coefficients, masked
hull and mean behavior, isolated actual counter/write transitions and the
exact averaging contraction. They do not execute a native trajectory or
replay earlier frozen evidence. The proof is conditional on indefinitely
admitted ideal execution. Finite arithmetic can reach rounding stasis;
indefinite counter/clock representability, a finite consensus time, full
runtime stationarity, collective formation and physical identification
remain outside this result.
