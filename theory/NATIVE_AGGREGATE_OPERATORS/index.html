    Aggregate operators and native transitions¶

    This owner asks when several registered constituent actions are exactly one registered action on a specified aggregate. It uses the existing shared operator implementation and ordered-language descent criterion. The native collective calculus instead retains the constituent state needed to describe native execution. A sufficient coordinate description does not itself establish operator inheritance. This owner also identifies the state needed when an inherited partial transition reaches its clipping boundary. The research program owns the research queue.

    The results below concern a declared arithmetic aggregation and restricted event or native-transition domains. They neither define the only possible collective NFR nor derive a universal collective law, organizational identity or physical event occurrence. A configured selector remains a supplied policy even when its decisions descend to the aggregate.

    A sufficient domain for one aggregate Resonance¶

    Conditional result. Two consecutive Resonance (RA) actions on a pair of nonadjacent twins can inherit exactly one registered RA on their aggregate, with unchanged factors. Internal form and capacity contrasts can both be nonzero. Paired phases, a common strict form sign and semantic kind, and duplicated consumed grammar context supply one sufficient domain. These are additional premises, not consequences of averaging or necessities of TNFR.

    State, support and execution domain¶

    Use the unit, undirected doubled C5 support from the native calculus: block i contains two nonadjacent nodes, each connected to both members of blocks i-1 and i+1 (indices modulo five). The comparison graph is the simple unit C5. Its node i is assigned the following aggregate channels:

    x_(i,-)=M_i-d_i,       x_(i,+)=M_i+d_i,
v_(i,-)=V_i-e_i,       v_(i,+)=V_i+e_i,
theta_(i,-)=theta_(i,+)=mu_i,
Q_i=(M_i,V_i,mu_i,kind_i,context_i).

    Here x=EPI is signed form and v=nu_f is capacity. The phase coordinates use an explicit common real lift of width less than both mathematical pi and the represented runtime pi. They are not form-derived angles. Each coarse edge represents one neighboring block; equal fine multiplicity, rather than summed conductance, supplies the neighbor-average identity. This statement is about RA's actual unweighted compatible-neighbor reader, not a proposed diffusion law. The exact phase argument uses the ideal circle of period 2*pi, with represented coefficients and cutoff, and compares endpoints on that common lift. Runtime modulo by represented 2*pi and trigonometric rounding are separate realization errors, not exact circle identities.

    Require the following common domain at each completed-pair boundary:

    • Every fine form belongs to one convex interval strictly inside the shared hard-clipping rails. The interval is either wholly above the operational trigger T=1e-9 or wholly below -T. All nodes have one common nonempty epi_kind. Thus a convex blend preserves both strict sign and kind.
    • All primitive values and configuration consumed by the selected path pass their actual admission. In particular V_i>|e_i|, and the fine and coarse amplified capacity proposals have finite representable arithmetic. A finite continuation needs sufficient headroom at every occurrence; direct RA does not itself impose the runtime's later VF_MAX clamp.
    • Fine and coarse graphs share the admitted factors rho=RA_epi_diff, b=RA_vf_amplification, c=RA_phase_coupling, with 0<rho<=1, b>0, 0<c<=1, and the same admitted U3 phase limit L. Every requested block has at least one live compatible neighbor.
    • Both twins and their coarse node have equal, separately owned consumed grammar context: bounded trace, persistent U2 debt, prior-Coherence flag, effective local operator ordinal and retention policy. The common context must admit RA under the shared grammar policy. For example, supplied Emission (AL), Coherence (IL) history with zero debt is sufficient; it is not a necessary precursor or a derived history.

    Use the strict caller enforce_canonical_grammar followed by apply_glyph, with GRAMMAR_REJECTION_MODE="raise". A supplied coarse target expands into the two fine targets consecutively. There is no interleaved action, flow, pressure preparation, phase coordinator, capacity adaptation or callback between them. Support stays fixed. Both actions retain the direct path's secondary effects; this is neither a public-class equivalence nor an atomic stage. Existing valid stored pressure can be retained, but no pressure law is inferred or refreshed by these RA requests.

    Optional telemetry may be collected and compared separately. It is not included in Q: two actual fine occurrences do not become one occurrence record. Exclude custom observers that feed additional state into execution.

    Same selected data, same registered action¶

    On the declared common lift, the live admitted block set and its selected means are

    J_i={j in {i-1,i+1}: abs(mu_j-mu_i)<=L},       J_i nonempty,
S_i=(sum_(j in J_i) M_j)/|J_i|,
eta_i=Arg_I(sum_(j in J_i) exp(i*mu_j)).

    The phase lift of width less than pi makes the selected resultant nonzero and fixes its angle in the same interval. U3's wrapped differences give exactly this J_i. Both fine targets select both members of each block in J_i and no others. Consequently their selected form mean is

    sum_(j in J_i) ((M_j-d_j)+(M_j+d_j))/(2*|J_i|) = S_i.

    Their selected phase resultant is twice the coarse resultant, so their phase target is exactly eta_i. The two twins are nonadjacent: the first action changes none of the second's neighbors. Both consume these same selected data despite executing sequentially. The common strict form interval gives abs(S_i)>T, so every target uses the same registered capacity factor f=1+b. Common kind and convex, unclipped form mixing admit all fine identity proposals and the coarse proposal.

    Applying the actual RA row to each twin and taking its mean and difference therefore gives

    M_i'=(1-rho)*M_i+rho*S_i,       d_i'=(1-rho)*d_i,
V_i'=f*V_i,                    e_i'=f*e_i,
mu_i'=(1-c)*mu_i+c*eta_i,       a_i'=0.

    These three mean rows and the unchanged kind are exactly one registered coarse RA with the same factors. All other blocks are unchanged. In ideal real arithmetic, writing P_i for the completed fine pair and R_i for the registered coarse action, this proves

    Q(P_i(s)) = R_i(Q(s))

    on the stated domain, including the consumed local grammar projection below. This is stronger than copying identical nodes: for rho<1, a nonzero internal form contrast survives, and any nonzero capacity contrast is amplified. Their event response need not be visible in the coarse primary channels for those channels to obey the same registered event rule.

    Admission, context and finite composition¶

    For the compared actions, sign, kind, factor and represented-arithmetic admission are premises on the whole fine domain, not deductions from a coarse pass. With these premises, paired phases identify U3 admission on both sides. An empty live J_i rejects both paths before the first fine event. Identical valid contexts identify the remaining incremental grammar decisions. Resonance is U2-neutral and has no U4 transformer/trigger requirement; it does not erase an already excessive persistent debt.

    Each twin appends one RA and advances its local ordinal from k to k+1; the coarse target does the same. Persistent debt and prior-Coherence remain unchanged. The projected history is the common associated local history, not a concatenation of both traces or a sum of their ordinals. This supplies the consumed context equality needed by the partial-transition criterion.

    Only completed pairs belong to this projection domain. After the first fine event, target phases and histories can differ. That intermediate state retains its fine description and pending second target; it is not the coarse endpoint. A second-target rejection outside the sufficient domain does not roll back the accepted first target.

    Convex form mixing and phase motion preserve their respective common intervals, common kind and paired phases at completed-pair boundaries. Induction therefore gives the same projected successors for any finite supplied sequence of complete pairs, provided live U3 admission and output representability remain valid. Recompute J_i, S_i and eta_i after earlier completed pairs; adjacent targets consume the updated state. If the initial lift diameter is strictly less than L, phase convexity keeps every neighbor compatible throughout such a finite sequence. This stronger sufficient corollary removes the U3-renewal obligation, but not finite capacity headroom. Repeated RA fragments are not automatically closed grammatical sentences; incremental admission does not discharge unconsumed U5/U6 or complete-word closure obligations.

    Exact scope and represented controls¶

    The algebra above is exact-real mathematics with the specified coefficients. Binary64 reductions of two versus four selected nodes, two-stage blends, trigonometric means and capacity products need not be bit-identical. Threshold-adjacent selection/trigger decisions and overflow require their own margins. A finite coarse capacity alone cannot certify finite fine proposals. This result makes no unrestricted binary64 admission-equivalence or numerical-enclosure claim.

    The shared-runtime controls freeze independent predictions before dispatch on one rational source and its all-negated form counterpart. Both have nonzero internal form and capacity contrasts, paired phases and supplied AL/IL contexts. They execute block 0 followed by adjacent block 1, using unchanged default factors. Block 0 admits two neighboring blocks; block 1 admits only the updated block 0, excluding its other neighbor by the actual U3 gate. Thus the comparison tests live partial selection rather than requiring all edges to be compatible. It verifies the mean rows, surviving contrasts, kind, local context and singleton-prefix boundary with an explicit floating residual tolerance. The controls stay in a positive non-wrapping phase chart; they do not certify runtime wrap equivalence to the ideal circle.

    Separate sign, kind and persistent-debt controls keep the coarse request admitted while the second fine request rejects and the first remains committed. Their aggregate primary channels are rebuilt from the modified fine primitives. A heterogeneous kind or history has no proved aggregate map: those negative controls explicitly supply the first member's label and context. They demonstrate the insufficiency of that choice outside the theorem, not an alternative canonical projection.

    Optional RA metrics expose four fine event records versus two coarse records for the two-block sequence, with twice as many selected neighbors per fine target. Stored pressure and structural time remain unchanged; these direct events create no physical temporal secants. No native selector, complete runtime call or trajectory is executed by these controls.

    Why one inherited operator is not a complete collective law¶

    The existing joint form/capacity calculus already separates event inheritance from the subsequent nodal row. With paired phases, write each prepared fine pressure as p_-=B+C, p_+=B-C, where C=k*d+b_v*e on the declared regular support. Its mean pressure can equal the ordinary coarse native pressure B. Nevertheless, after a common RA boost f, retaining that prepared pressure through the flow gives

    fine mean form rate = f*(V*B-e*C),
ordinary coarse form rate = f*V*B.

    The covariance -f*e*C generally remains nonzero in the positive event domain. This reuses the existing held-pressure result; it supplies no new pressure law. Constituent pressure peaks, normalized Si, temporal samples and native request selection have further information requirements. They do not descend merely because mean pressure or this event descends.

    The admitted aggregate action consequently establishes a restricted cross-scale operator correspondence. A complete collective dynamical law, organizational identity, its maintenance or acquisition, and the occurrence of the supplied paired requests remain separate questions under the NFR ontology.

    Fine Resonance does not universally inherit one aggregate Resonance¶

    The complete-call map retains associated constituent state. A stronger claim would identify the admitted two-target Resonance (RA) action with one registered RA on a collective node whose capacity is the arithmetic mean V=(v_-+v_+)/2, using the same amplification factor b. This is a specified aggregation candidate, not a definition required by TNFR. Its capacity row alone has a necessary obstruction, independently of how aggregate form, phase, kind, neighbors or grammar might be completed.

    Assume both constituent capacities are positive and exactly one selected signed mean activates amplification. In exact-real arithmetic with the represented factor, the admitted fine action gives

    V_star=V+b*v_active/2,
0<v_active/2<V,
V < V_star < (1+b)*V,                       b>0.

    These are ideal inequalities, not universal binary64 strict inequalities: an amplification can round away at a small factor or capacity. Represented separation needs its own margin, as in the default-sized control below.

    One registered RA with input capacity V has only the two possible capacity outputs V and (1+b)*V, according to its own selected-mean trigger. Neither equals the fine aggregate above. Therefore no choice of aggregate trigger, retained hidden information or grammar can make these capacity rows agree while keeping this capacity map and the unchanged registered factor. This is failure of that universal operator-intertwining claim, not another claim that means omit information. It already holds for an event endpoint, before flow, coordination or capacity adaptation.

    The existing composition source supplies an admitted control: v_-=7/8, v_+=9/8, V=1, and only the odd constituent activates amplification. Thus V_star=1+9*b/16, strictly between the two aggregate outputs. The control compares the existing fine response with both branches of the shared registered capacity proposal; it introduces no new source or trajectory.

    When both triggers agree, this capacity obstruction disappears. Matching capacity is not sufficient: aggregate form/sign/kind, phase action, U3 and associated grammar still need their own common-domain proof under the ordered-action criterion. An effective factor b*v_active/(2*V) would reproduce this one capacity row, but would be a state-dependent collective rule, not the unchanged registered RA factor. Different aggregate capacity maps also require independent justification. The result does not exclude richer collective NFRs, formation, or all forms of operator inheritance across scales.

    An actual selected native transition at uniform capacity¶

    Conditional result. On a separate uniform-capacity sector, a fine doubled-C5 network and an ordinary coarse C5 have the same projected ideal native transition under the existing default selector, without supplying an operator word. Nonzero internal form contrasts are allowed. The transition retains form means, common capacity, paired primitive phases, semantic kind, associated grammar/lag context, phase gains and clock. It need not retain fine pressure normalization, Si, stability counters or complete temporal histories. This is a partial reduction of a specified runtime model, not an identification of the two complete graph states.

    The proof combines the native writer domain, the conditional moment rows and the ordered-language criterion. The new obligation is descent of the actual selected transition to an ordinary coarse runtime, after discarding internal form contrast. A conditional common request or equal mean pressure alone did not prove it.

    Retained state and admitted completions¶

    Use the same fixed unit doubled C5 and simple C5 supports as above, with consecutive fine twins and corresponding coarse target order. Now impose one globally common positive capacity nu, interior to the runtime rails:

    x_(i,-)=M_i-d_i,      x_(i,+)=M_i+d_i,       |d_i|<=D,
v_(i,-)=v_(i,+)=nu,   theta_(i,-)=theta_(i,+)=mu_i.

    D>=0 is a fixed declared internal-form bound, not a fitted diagnostic. The common phase lift has width less than both mathematical and represented pi. As above, phase identities use the ideal circle with represented coefficients; runtime wrapping and reduction rounding have separate scope. Every node has the same nonempty semantic kind. At each block, retain one common, nonempty bounded grammar trace, persistent debt within its allowed capacity, prior-Coherence flag and local operator ordinal. Fine twins have separately owned copies. The shared positive retention window preserves a nonempty trace. Retain the two actual per-block lag counters a_i=since_AL and r_i=since_EN, the global phase gains, clock and runtime ordinal.

    Keep the existing default mixed pressure, fresh default Si, default selector, strict grammar rejection, direct glyph path, hard clipping, default phase controller/coordinator and snapshot capacity adaptation. The form step is one held-pressure Euler interval of length delta=1/16, with the live built-in Gamma source of type none. No overridden selector, supplied word, custom pressure/integrator, optional mathematics engine or callbacks are included. Default validators still run. The fine and coarse runtime use the same declared clock, step size, policy configuration and target order.

    All consumed primitive state, context, clocks and arithmetic must be admitted. Fine old rates, accelerations, physical histories and stability counts can differ; their validity is not inferred from aggregate admission. Existing temporal samples have valid times and signed forms. They are auxiliary completions of the retained state, not invented coarse observations.

    For automatic REMESH, retain an admitted stable_frac history whose length is less than the configured stability window. With the specified absence of callbacks, the built-in step does not append that stream: pressure/Si, coordination, adaptation and the EPI archive have different writers. Thus the short-history guard remains invariant and the actual REMESH gate abstains. The archive itself still updates.     AL: y_j^E=max(y_j-b,0),
EN: y_j^E=(1-m)*y_j+m*N_j(live event deficits),
IL: y_j^E=y_j.

    Every target is visited once. Emission cannot increase a deficit, and Reception is convex, so every live event deficit is at most the incoming D_n. The subsequent held-pressure flow is exactly

    y_j'=clip_[0,R](y_j^E-z*H_j*y_j+z*H_j*N_j(incoming y)-f_(n,j)).

    The neighbor values in the event and flow terms belong to different stages. Clipping an Emission event does not refresh the prepared pressure.

    One-call bounds without a monotone-map assumption¶

    The complete clipped map is not generally order-preserving. For example, use the default rails [-1,1], zero internal spread, uniform phase and neighbors of block zero at form zero and the default positive boost. Compare its incoming forms M_a=U-b/2 and M_b=U-b/4 under the same AL request. Both events saturate at U, while their incoming mean pressures are -k*M_a and -k*M_b. The resulting in-rail flow forms are U-z*M_a and U-z*M_b; increasing the input decreases that output by z*b/4. Thus the unclipped stochastic matrix cannot simply be reused as a global clipped-map comparison.

    Instead, bound each actual scalar row. At Emission, separate the two branches y_j>=b and y_j<b before final projection. They give

    y_j'<=max(D_n-b,z*D_n)+F_n <= a*D_n+F_n,
a=max(1-b/R,z) in (0,1).

    Indeed, the first branch is bounded by (1-z)*D_n+z*D_n-b, and the second by z*D_n. Since D_n<=R, D_n-b<=(1-b/R)*D_n. The maximum is nonnegative, so projection to [0,R] preserves the upper bound, including when the unprojected deficit is negative.

    For Reception and Coherence respectively, the same stage distinction gives

    EN: y_j'<=lambda*y_j+(1-lambda)*D_n+F_n,
IL: y_j'<=(1-z*h)*y_j+z*h*D_n+F_n.

    Both coefficients retaining the target's incoming deficit are positive and at least lambda: 1-z*h>=1-m-z. The right-hand sides are nonnegative, so the final projection is again harmless for these upper bounds. In particular all three cases imply

    D_(n+1)<=D_n+F_n.

    This estimate allows temporary increases in the largest deficit; it does not assume that every form increases at every call.

    A strict improvement in every six-call block¶

    Fix a block of six calls starting at n, and define its accumulated forcing allowance

    E_j=sum_(r=0)^(j-1) F_(n+r),       E_0=0.

    The running maximum is bounded by D_(n+j)<=D_n+E_j. The actual lag policy gives each constituent exactly one accepted Emission in those six calls, even from an arbitrary admitted initial lag. If its Emission is at offset j, its next deficit satisfies

    y_(n+j+1)<=a*(D_n+E_j)+F_(n+j)
         <=D_n+E_(j+1)-(1-a)*D_n.

    There are at most five later calls in the block, and none is another Emission at that target. For either of the remaining choices, a gap g below the running allowance is retained by at least lambda*g. The next forcing contribution is included in the next allowance. Thus every constituent ends the block at most D_n+E_6-(1-a)*lambda^5*D_n, and

    D_(n+6)<=Q*D_n+sum_(r=0)^5 F_(n+r),
Q=1-(1-a)*lambda^5 in (0,1).

    This argument uses the true per-target lag recurrence and scalar row bounds. It does not require synchronized lag phases, conservation of total form, or monotonicity of the complete transition. In particular it remains valid when an Emission saturates and held pressure subsequently moves the same form away from the rail.

    Convergence along an indefinitely admitted ideal continuation¶

    Set tau=rho^6 and A=F0*sum_(r=0)^5 rho^r. Iterating the block bound gives, for K>=1,

    D_(6K)<=Q^K*D_0+A*sum_(j=0)^(K-1) Q^(K-1-j)*tau^j.

    For Q!=tau the sum is (Q^K-tau^K)/(Q-tau); when Q=tau it is K*Q^(K-1). Both tend to zero because 0<Q,tau<1. The K=0 sum is empty. For intervening calls 0<=r<6,

    D_(6K+r)<=D_(6K)+F0*rho^(6K)*sum_(j=0)^(r-1) rho^j.

    Consequently D_n -> 0 along the entire admitted continuation, proving the stated constituent and block-mean limits. The internal spread contraction is consistent with this result but was not needed to replace fine dynamics by an approximate mean law.

    The phase coordinator preserves nested closed lift intervals with width at most rho^n*Delta_0. Their intersection is one angle, so all primitive phases converge to that common lifted angle. Common capacity remains fixed. The specified pressure therefore tends to zero as both form and phase differences vanish.

    At exactly uniform upper-rail form and common phase, the primary nodal coordinates remain stationary under each selected AL/EN/IL event and held flow. The nominal Emission proposal still adds b; its realized clipped form increment is zero. Operator occurrences, lag counters, runtime ordinals and the structural clock can continue advancing. The theorem neither identifies the complete execution state with a fixed point nor erases its retained policy memory.

    Static evidence and research consequence¶

    The limit controls check the two Emission branches, the separate incoming/live neighbor bounds, the nonmonotone saturated control, six-call allowance propagation and both forms of the geometric convolution. These are exact static comparison calculations, not executed histories. The upper-rail primary state has separate stationary-coordinate checks. Runtime and integrator execution are blocked, and earlier evaluated responses remain unchanged.

    This closes the limiting-organization question for this configured sector. It excludes a persistent nonuniform form or primitive-phase pattern there, but not finite transients, retained support, semantic identity or policy memory. It gives no finite saturation time or laboratory prediction and does not certify asymptotic binary64 behavior. A sustained organization outside this conclusion must fail at least one of the declared premises; changing one merely to obtain a preferred result would require a separate model and justification.

    Capacity adaptation preserves saturation in a nonzero neighborhood¶

    Conditional theorem. The preceding upper-rail limit is robust to a derived nonzero range of initial fine capacities, with the actual capacity adaptation enabled. All adaptation gates eventually qualify permanently; capacities converge to a common positive value, fine forms to U, and primitive phases to one common lifted angle. Gate qualification is proved before consensus is used. This excludes persistent form/phase contrast in this larger sector; it does not identify particles or a universal TNFR law.

    Retain the preceding doubled unit C5, direct native default policy, delta=1/16, zero Gamma, hard rails, paired phases in the common short lift, common kind, duplicated grammar/lag contexts, complete-pair event order, strict admission and REMESH abstention. Replace uniform capacity by

    0 < v_- <= nu_j(0) <= v_+ <= V < VF_MAX,
epsilon=v_+-v_-,
alpha*v_-/v_+ > si_hi.

    Here V is a fixed conservative upper envelope, alpha the resolved Si capacity weight, and si_hi the common default selection/adaptation threshold. Capacities may differ within pairs. Retain all consumed fine state, including valid nonnegative stability counts. This is a fine-state theorem, not an extension of the smaller uniform-capacity quotient.

    Use the resolved represented coefficients as exact constants in the ideal model, rather than replacing them by intended decimal or symbolic values:

    c=w_vf>0,  k=w_epi>0,  ell=w_phase/pi_rep,
Z=delta*V*k,  lambda=1-m-Z>0,
a=max(1-b/(U-L),Z),  Q=1-(1-a)*lambda^5,
C(V)=c*(1+6*Z/(1-Q)).

    The default parameters give 0<Z<min(1/2,1-m) and 0<a,Q<1 throughout this capacity range. The additional sufficient restriction is strict:

    C(V)*epsilon < EPS_DNFR_STABLE.

    It is not a necessary boundary of convergence. Failure of this sufficient inequality leaves the behavior undecided.

    Invariant selection with arbitrary eligible adaptation subsets¶

    The actual capacity adapter proposes (1-mu)*nu_j+mu*N_j(nu) from one immutable snapshot at eligible nodes and leaves the other capacities unchanged. Its admitted coefficients make every proposal convex. Thus any subset of eligible writes preserves the initial capacity hull; this does not yet prove consensus or conservation of the initial mean.

    Fresh Si has nonnegative components and satisfies Si_j>=alpha*nu_j/max_l(nu_l)>=alpha*v_-/v_+>si_hi. The default selector therefore proposes Coherence (IL); the retained lags override it only by Emission (AL) or Reception (EN). These three direct events preserve capacity. This establishes an induction across complete calls: selection preserves the hull before adaptation, and adaptation preserves it afterward. No later selection or successful adaptation is assumed in advance. Unequal capacities or stability counts do not break paired choices in this high-Si sector. Phase coordination consumes no capacity and preserves the inherited paired-phase contraction.

    A pressure bound before permanent qualification¶

    At each call, the held flow uses its own incoming capacity, with z_(n,j)=delta*nu_(n,j)*k<=Z. The pressure capacity channel is the signed neighbor contrast c*(N_j(nu)-nu_j), not a normalized contrast. Hull preservation bounds its magnitude by c*epsilon under every eligible mask. Keep D_n=max_j(U-x_(n,j)) and the preceding phase diameter bound Delta_n<=rho^n*Delta_0. The two form forcing allowances are

    F_n=delta*V*ell*Delta_0*rho^n,
G=delta*V*c*epsilon.

    The pressure retained by a selected event is multiplied by H_j=1 or h<1. The fine-deficit proof therefore applies with variable z_(n,j)<=Z: Emission gives at most a*D_n+F_n+G; subsequent Reception or Coherence retains a target's gap below the running allowance by at least lambda. Every constituent still has one accepted Emission in every six-call window. Consequently, for every starting call n,

    D_(n+6) <= Q*D_n + sum_(r=0)^5 F_(n+r) + 6*G.

    Iterate on each of the six residue classes. The initial term and geometric phase convolution vanish; the constant forcing sum is bounded by 6*G/(1-Q). Thus

    limsup_(n->infinity) D_n <= 6*G/(1-Q).

    No additional allowance for intervening calls is needed because the same recurrence holds on every residue class. Neither capacity convergence nor a nonempty eligible mask has been used.

    The freshly prepared pressure satisfies

    max_j abs(p_(n,j)) <= k*D_n+c*epsilon+ell*Delta_n.

    Indeed, all incoming forms lie in [U-D_n,U], so their difference from a neighbor mean is at most D_n, not 2*D_n. Regular support removes the topology contrast. Applying the preceding bound gives

    limsup_(n->infinity) max_j abs(H_j*p_(n,j)) <= C(V)*epsilon
                                                   < EPS_DNFR_STABLE.

    This is the pressure actually consumed by adaptation: prepared pressure after its glyph retention, without refresh after flow or phase coordination. The adapter also consumes the incoming prepared Si, whose strict floor already holds. The strict limiting pressure margin implies that from some finite call onward both gates qualify at every node on every call. An equality at the threshold would not suffice: the abstract sequence EPS_DNFR_STABLE+1/(n+1) approaches the threshold without ever passing. This is a counterexample to that limiting inference, not a native response.

    Permanent averaging and a vanishing forcing remainder¶

    Qualification increments each stability count; a successful write does not reset it. After at most VF_ADAPT_TAU further qualifying calls, all nodes therefore update on every call. With mu=VF_ADAPT_MU and the fixed fine neighbor-mean matrix N, the ideal recurrence becomes

    nu_(n+1)=P*nu_n,        P=(1-mu)*I+mu*N.

    On the doubled unit C5, P is doubly stochastic. The entries of P^2 in each row have four classes:

    Target relative to the row node Count Entry
    Same node 1 (1-mu)^2+mu^2/4
    Its twin 1 mu^2/4
    Adjacent blocks 4 mu*(1-mu)/2
    Nonadjacent blocks 4 mu^2/8

    For the actual default 0<mu<4/5, all entries are at least eta=mu^2/8>0. Subtracting eta from every entry leaves a nonnegative matrix with each row sum 1-10*eta. Its constant component cancels in differences, proving directly

    osc(P^2*nu) <= (1-10*eta)*osc(nu),       0<1-10*eta<1.

    Hence capacities converge to one positive value in the initial hull. Double stochasticity preserves the mean once full updating starts. The limit is that mean, not necessarily the initial mean: earlier partial writes need not conserve total capacity. No externally selected target capacity or physical scale follows.

    Now the actual width epsilon_n=osc(nu_n) tends to zero. Replace G in the six-call bound by the actual per-call allowance G_n=delta*V*c*epsilon_n. Both phase and capacity forcing vanish. For each residue class, a recurrence with uniform Q<1 and a vanishing nonnegative remainder tends to zero: split its geometric convolution into a finite initial part and a tail bounded by the remainder supremum divided by 1-Q. Therefore D_n->0. The inherited nested phase intervals converge to one angle, and the complete prepared pressure tends to zero.

    An explicit nonzero neighborhood without source search¶

    Set v0=1/2 and use the conservative envelope Vbar=1. Compute Cbar from C(Vbar) above and define

    r_star=min(v0/2,
           v0*(alpha-si_hi)/(2*(alpha+si_hi)),
           EPS_DNFR_STABLE/(4*Cbar)).

    Every term is positive under the unchanged defaults. For any fine initial capacities in [v0-r_star,v0+r_star], the lower endpoint is positive and the upper is at most 3/4<Vbar<VF_MAX. The second term leaves a strict Si margin; the width is at most 2*r_star, so the limiting pressure bound is at most half the stability threshold. The construction admits arbitrary fine forms within the inherited rails and retains the other phase, grammar, history and execution premises. It selects no response, trajectory, natural scale or new law.

    The capacity robustness controls check the derived threshold with exact represented coefficients, masked hull and mean behavior, isolated actual counter/write transitions and the exact averaging contraction. They do not execute a native trajectory or replay earlier frozen evidence. The proof is conditional on indefinitely admitted ideal execution. Finite arithmetic can reach rounding stasis; indefinite counter/clock representability, a finite consensus time, full runtime stationarity, collective formation and physical identification remain outside this result.
