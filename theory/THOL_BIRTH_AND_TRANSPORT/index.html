    Causal THOL birth and an admitted transport connection¶

    Status: Exact finite acceleration, edge-reset and declared-action selection obstructions, with bounded canonical runtime controls.     1. Deriving a birth trigger from actual nodal evolution¶

    Consider a fixed simple unit cycle with uniform held capacity nu>0, scalar EPI x, and a regular phase twist strictly inside the effective U3 gate and the neighbor semicircle boundary. Its phase, capacity and topology pressure gradients vanish. With the existing fully normalized EPI weight e=w_epi>0, define c=e*nu and L=I-W/2. The canonical nodal equation is

    \[ \dot x=-cLx. \]

    Two successive exact refreshed Euler segments of positive durations h1,h2 give

    \[ x_1=(I-ch_1L)x_0,\qquad x_2=(I-ch_2L)x_1. \]

    Their secants satisfy s1=-c*L*x0 and s2=-c*L*x1. The same three-point acceleration used by compute_d2epi_dt2 therefore has the exact reference identity

    \[ \widehat A=\frac{2(s_2-s_1)}{h_1+h_2} =\frac{2h_1c^2}{h_1+h_2}L^2x_0. \]

    The first duration occurs in the numerator because the second refreshed rate is evaluated at the endpoint of the first Euler segment. This is a finite solver identity, not an asserted exact second derivative of the continuous exponential trajectory. The sufficient convex conditions c*h_j<=1 keep all EPI endpoints in the convex hull of the initial field; clipping and runtime arithmetic still require observed residuals.

    On an even cycle let v_i=(-1)^i, so Lv=2v, and prepare x0=b*1+a*v. Then

    \[ \widehat A_i= \frac{8h_1c^2a}{h_1+h_2}v_i. \]

    The default THOL magnitude threshold is crossed when

    \[ \frac{8h_1(e\nu)^2|a|}{h_1+h_2}>\tau. \]

    This is a derived sufficient preparation condition. It leaves the existing threshold and operator coefficients unchanged and supplies no independent acceleration law. Runtime admission additionally requires the actual operator grammar and parent domain checks.

    3. Birth creates a node; Coupling must supply an actual edge¶

    The distinction established in THOL pressure feedback remains essential: public THOL creates an initially isolated child with a triad and hierarchy metadata. The node does not enter its parent's neighbor-pressure walk through the metadata alone. Its initial pressure is zero, capacity is the existing 0.95 multiple of the parent capacity, and phase is inherited. Its EPI is the bounded output of the existing metabolic amplitude map.

    This isolated birth also has an exact transport-energy boundary. Old EPI and conductance remain fixed, so for the Dirichlet matrix B=D-W,

    \[ B^+=\operatorname{diag}(B,0),\qquad x^+=(x,x_h),\qquad E_D(x^+)=\tfrac12(x^+)^T B^+x^+=E_D(x). \]

    The edge-based energy cannot see the isolated child's form. This is not a zero physical cost of creation. Although its capacity is positive, its reversible weight d_h/nu_h is zero until attachment; the connected, positive-metric forced-profile theorem cannot be applied at this intermediate state. The isolated-birth energy test checks the zero-block identity and rejects use of the same-support reset observer across the dimensional jump. After birth, that existing observer can account for UM on two snapshots of the same enlarged node set.

    An actual graph-backed Coupling operation can subsequently consider the child as a functional-link candidate. The candidate inventory must include the new node; a retained prebirth sample of graph nodes need not do so. The candidate must pass both the U3 phase condition and the existing compatibility threshold. There is no automatic parent-child edge implied by THOL's hierarchy.

    The parent target must already have a compatible neighbor when the hard U3 gate is checked, before functional candidates are considered. Targeting the isolated child, or including it as a target in an all-node stage, fails that gate despite its inherited phase alignment. The successful route targets the parent with its existing cycle neighbors.

    The Coupling proposal kernel computes link weight from final merged phases and the snapshot EPI/sense inputs. In its scalar regime this has the form

    \[ \omega=\tfrac12\left(1-\frac{|\operatorname{wrap} (\phi_i-\phi_j)|}{\pi}\right) +\tfrac14\left(1-\frac{|x_i-x_j|} {|x_i|+|x_j|+\epsilon}\right) +\tfrac14(1-|S_i-S_j|), \]

    with the existing epsilon and sense-index conventions. The implementation evaluates the EPI ratio with scaling to preserve its numeric domain. The default compatibility threshold is pi/(pi+1). The resulting positive conductance is an operator-derived weight, not an independently fitted parent-child coupling strength. Inherited equal phase alone is insufficient: the EPI and sense-index terms can still place the candidate below the compatibility threshold.

    Parent-target UM computes its phase, capacity and direct pressure proposals from the old compatible neighborhood before adding the functional edge. The newly considered child is therefore not silently included in that same call's original neighbor capacity mean. Default bidirectional UM can also change the parent's old neighbors' phases. The full before/after triads and the subsequent pressure refresh must be observed; a pure edge-only model does not describe every UM channel.

    4. What the new edge changes in the canonical pressure¶

    Keep scalar EPI fixed across a new undirected edge i-j with positive weight omega. For endpoint i, let d_i be its old weighted strength and mx_i its old weighted EPI neighbor mean. If d_i>0, the change of the normalized EPI gradient is

    \[ g^+_{x,i}-g^-_{x,i} =\frac{\omega}{d_i+\omega}(x_j-mx_i). \]

    For a former isolate, the new single-neighbor gradient is instead g_x^+=x_j-x_i, independently of the strictly positive incident weight. This is normalization of the actual adjacency, not infinite coupling.

    The capacity channel uses the unweighted unique neighbor support. With old neighbor count k_i>0 and mean capacity bar_nu_i, its edge-only change is

    \[ g^+_{\nu,i}-g^-_{\nu,i} =\frac{\nu_j-\bar\nu_i}{k_i+1}. \]

    A former isolate gets nu_j-nu_i. Zero-weight support edges can affect capacity, phase and topology while contributing no EPI conductance; the weighted EPI walk cannot replace those other channel conventions. If UM also changes capacity, evaluate these support-only identities on one declared held triad and account for the capacity write separately.

    For the actual unit-cycle parent joined to one isolated child, denote the parent's EPI by H, its old two neighbors' common EPI by J, and the child's EPI by z. For the ideal closed-form reference with parent capacity one and child capacity 19/20, the endpoint gradients are

    \[ g_{x,p}^+=\frac{2J+\omega z}{2+\omega}-H,\qquad g_{x,c}^+=H-z,\qquad g_{\nu,p}^+=-\frac1{60},\qquad g_{\nu,c}^+=\frac1{20}. \]

    The symmetric parent-target phase update leaves the child and parent phases equal in the exact preparation. Their phase pressures vanish, so the refreshed child pressure is

    \[ p_c=e(H-z)+\frac{w_{\nu}}{20}. \]

    The production damping coefficient is binary64 0.95, not the exact rational 19/20. For its exact represented capacity difference delta=1-Fraction(float(0.95)), replace -1/60 and 1/20 by -delta/3 and delta, respectively, including the pressure formula.

    The default topology weight is zero. For a configured nonzero topology channel, the new degree-neighbor gradients are -4/3 at the parent, 2 at the child, 1/2 at each old neighbor of the parent, and zero elsewhere. These formulas use a simple unit undirected cycle; arbitrary weighted or parallel-edge graphs require the implementation's actual degree and unique-support conventions.

    The old neighbors can acquire nonzero phase pressure after bidirectional UM. The newly connected whole graph therefore need not follow pure EPI diffusion, even when the parent and child formulas above simplify. Likewise the previous cycle coordinate x+(w_vf/e)*nu does not generally cancel pressure once weighted EPI transport and unweighted capacity transport differ.

    5. Connection has an exact energy reset, not a conservation claim¶

    On fixed ordered support, let W be symmetric conductance, D its strength diagonal and B=D-W. The existing EPI Dirichlet diagnostic is

    \[ F_W(x)=\tfrac12x^\top Bx =\tfrac12\sum_{\{i,j\}}w_{ij}(x_i-x_j)^2. \]

    Compare the graph after birth but before UM with the graph after the edge commit, including the child in both node orders. If UM leaves EPI unchanged, its exact edge reset is

    \[ F_{W^+}(x)-F_{W^-}(x) =\tfrac12\sum_{\{i,j\}}(w^+_{ij}-w^-_{ij})(x_i-x_j)^2. \]

    For the single new parent-child edge this is omega*(H-z)^2/2>=0. The change reflects a new conductance in the diagnostic while EPI is fixed; it is not proof of conserved energy or new EPI creation. It is also not a birth-energy theorem across different node supports.

    After the edge commit, a held-support nodal rate r=diag(nu)*p has

    \[ \dot F=(Bx)^\top r. \]

    The EPI-channel contribution alone is -e*(Bx)^T*diag(nu_i/d_i)*(Bx)<=0, with zero mobility at isolates. The capacity, phase and topology terms keep their own signs; a full multichannel decrease does not follow from the EPI term. A subsequent exact held-input Euler interval has the finite identity

    \[ F(x+h r)-F(x)=h(Bx)^\top r+\tfrac12h^2r^\top Br. \]

    These identities reuse the existing conductance and diffusion geometry. They measure how a created child becomes dynamically connected and how the changed structure enters the nodal channels.

    6. A precise boundary for the connected pattern's next evolution¶

    Freeze the connected post-UM support, capacities and phases before any later Silence or structural event. Assume positive capacities and fixed symmetric conductance on a connected graph. With D=diag(d_i), define the held non-EPI pressure contribution

    \[ F=w_{\nu}g_{\nu}+w_{\phi}g_{\phi}+w_{\mathrm{topo}}g_{\mathrm{topo}}. \]

    The refreshed canonical model, while its scalar chart remains valid, is

    \[ \dot x=\operatorname{diag}(\nu)(-eD^{-1}Bx+F). \]

    A zero-pressure equilibrium requires e*B*x=D*F. Since the symmetric connected Laplacian has kernel span{1}, the necessary and sufficient compatibility condition is

    \[ \mathbf1^\top DF=\sum_i d_iF_i=0. \]

    This is an exact balance condition for the actual channel conventions. It is not implied by each channel separately resembling neighbor averaging: the EPI channel's stationary weights are the conductance strengths, while the capacity channel uses unweighted support.

    In the ideal reflected C8 preparation, the parent-target bidirectional UM preserves reflection symmetry of the graph and capacities. Its phase gradient is antisymmetric under that reflection, with zero at the fixed nodes, so sum(d_i*g_phi_i)=0 inside the strict chart. With the default zero topology weight and ideal child capacity 19/20, the capacity contribution gives

    \[ \sum_i d_iF_i =w_{\nu}\left[-\frac{2+\omega}{60}+\frac{\omega}{20}\right] =\frac{w_{\nu}(\omega-1)}{30}<0. \]

    Here the observed link has 0<omega<1. Consequently this frozen ideal structure has no zero-pressure stationary EPI. Reflection cancellation is an exact-model statement; the actual binary64 weighted phase contribution must be measured as a residual. For the exact represented capacity difference delta above, the weighted capacity contribution is instead 2*w_vf*(omega-1)*delta/3, with the same strictly negative sign.

    The captured connected state confirms that sign: the weighted capacity pressure is approximately -0.0003191519727040398, while the weighted stored pressure is -0.0003191519727039513. Their weighted phase and arithmetic remainder is approximately 8.86e-17. The corresponding instantaneous represented nodal mean rate is -1.8015381161580725e-5. These quantities are read before the subsequent flow and final SHA; they are not observations of a future limiting drift.

    There is a different conditional possibility. Define

    \[ \bar v=\frac{\sum_i d_iF_i}{\sum_i d_i/\nu_i}. \]

    The weighted EPI mean has derivative bar_v. The equation

    \[ eBz=DF-\bar v\,(d_i/\nu_i)_i \]

    is compatible and determines a relative profile z up to a constant. Then x(t)=bar_v*t*1+z solves the held model. Subtracting that solution leaves homogeneous fixed-capacity EPI diffusion, so relative deviations decay on a connected graph. This conclusion concerns an unrestricted scalar chart with fixed coefficients. A nonzero mean drift eventually encounters finite EPI bounds; it gives no asymptotic claim for a clipped runtime or for a later UM/SHA policy.

    The next bounded question is therefore to distinguish a held-support relative profile with mean drift from restoration under further admitted structural operations. The frozen-support follow-up now implements the exact relative-profile reference, nonlinear forcing read-out and finite comparison. A repeated-policy theorem remains open. Neither birth, one successful connection nor this compatibility calculation establishes a persistent self-restoring localized entity.
