    Emergence: admissible claims and reusable mechanisms¶

    Status: research scope and reuse map. Conditional formation and maintenance are established for the supplied two-ring relational preparation below; universal pattern selection and identification with physical entities remain open. The execution plan owns priorities; this reference introduces no additional campaign.

    0. What counts as emergence here¶

    The nodal identity

    \[\dot{\mathrm{EPI}}=\nu_f\,\Delta\mathrm{NFR}\]

    relates form, capacity and pressure. It does not uniquely determine a pressure law, phase evolution, capacity evolution, support, clock or operator schedule. These choices must be stated and justified before interpreting their output. The parameter foundations own the assumptions and available derivations. Existing definitions and defaults are revisable.

    A derived consequence of a specified model can be useful without that model being uniquely selected. Conversely, renaming a supplied physical model, initial pattern or target value does not derive it from nodal dynamics. Distinguish four levels:

    Claim Required evidence Insufficient substitute
    Structural read-out Defined map from a supplied graph state, with domains and unavailable cases A suggestive field or topology name
    Dynamical consequence Explicit evolution law and assumptions, with proof or source-bound finite trajectory Static resemblance or an imposed endpoint
    Effective nodal emergence State map, closure or derived memory, and comparison with microscopic evolution Calling an arbitrary partition a macro-NFR
    Physical identification Independent measurement map and reserved prediction under a frozen model Relabeling measurements as EPI or reproducing fitted targets

    The mathematical owners below establish restricted results, not a derivation of the physical world. Empirical correspondence is separately governed by the measurement protocol; reported EEG observations retain the limits in the empirical record. Added physical programmes and circular type-signature instruments were retired, rather than maintained as parallel TNFR foundations.

    1. Substrate, formation and identity¶

    Research target: physical properties of collective patterns¶

    The primary hypothesis is that material constituents and their observable properties could arise from interacting TNFR patterns. It does not require one microscopic EPI to be a voltage, temperature, concentration or particle attribute. A prospective generative chain is fine nodal state/law -> collective identity and effective dynamics -> physical observations. The fine state includes form, phase, capacity, support and any history actually consumed by the law. The effective object may carry properties of their joint configuration and trajectory which no individual node has.

    A collective observation/measurement map is eventually needed to test that hypothesis, but it may act on a pattern and its interaction with a measurement system rather than on one primitive coordinate. Its latent-state assumptions and, where used, probability rule must be justified independently of the evaluated outcomes. Direct sensor-to-EPI transport experiments are supporting realizations; success there would not derive the sensor's material constituents.

    The user's spin example is a research question about such collective properties, not a proposed identification already established. A balanced configuration, phase rotation, winding integer or classical U(2)/Stokes representation is insufficient to establish physical spin. A spin claim would need the appropriate rotation action and quantitative measurement response, including the outcome statistics for the spin sector claimed; see the external spin-one-half reference. These are comparison obligations, not laws to insert into TNFR and then claim as derived. Equilibrium can also be relative or dynamical; a static balance alone does not prove reachability, robustness, composability or physical identity.

    The current engine assumes nodes, states and relations. That is the starting domain of this model, not evidence that the substrate is external to TNFR, nor a derivation of its own origin. A finer-scale TNFR realization would need the same explicit reduction obligations as any effective nodal description; see assumed substrate and emergence between scales.

    The deeper target can therefore be a hypothetical relational substrate whose collective configurations supply both effective objects and their environment. This does not require a second medium outside that structure, but it does require sufficient state and complete laws. The environment/pressure admission shows why an unobserved background, zero EPI and zero pressure are not interchangeable notions of vacuum. Connected fine support and its global unity remain premises, not consequences of the effective-link proof. The current pressure is a state-dependent driving term; a pressure-based description must retain whatever coordinates or memory its closure needs.

    NFRs participate in the same structural configuration¶

    The programme adopts the user's ontological clarification as a constitutive requirement: an NFR is an organization of the same structural configuration in which it acts. Its identity and interactions must be represented within that configuration. This participation in form is not, by itself, evidence that an NFR has been identified with observed physical matter.

    At an effective scale, write y=R(z) for the retained description of a pattern in the fine joint state z. On a smooth fixed-support interval, its rate must be inherited as y_dot=DR(z)*F(z). A closed law in y alone requires the existing closure conditions; otherwise retain boundary state, hidden coordinates or justified memory. Effective interaction must represent the same fine interaction, without counting it again as an independent force exerted by a second copy of the pattern. The collective interaction owner already shows why relations between patterns can carry necessary state.

    Here structural participation does not identify the entire joint state with one scalar EPI coordinate. The current representation also retains phase, capacity and support; reducing these to a richer form description would need a faithful map and an inherited law. Nor does causal participation require every NFR to change every variable, or to change continuously at equilibrium. The channels, reach and timing of its effects follow from the actual law. This requirement constrains model construction but does not select the cotangent premise, operator occurrence or a complete microscopic closure.

    1.1 NFR formation: definition, birth and collective dynamics¶

    The NFR definition is scale-relative. A node represents a micro-NFR; a coherent region is a candidate macro-NFR. Three distinct questions must be tested:

    • Graph birth: an admitted THOL action creates a child coordinate, its triad and hierarchy metadata. This is an implemented event under a supplied admission and environmental-amplitude rule.
    • Regional formation and persistence: a collection develops and retains a stated identity under surrounding interactions. This need not create vertices. A coherent snapshot, stationary minimum or topology label alone does not demonstrate temporal maintenance.
    • Autonomous effective description: aggregate coordinates predict their evolution, possibly with derived memory and a hidden-initial-state source. A failed reduction does not refute the region's existence in the full state.

    Operators specify transformations of existing state; they are not separate entities whose birth must be simulated. Deriving when a transformation occurs is a further closure problem. Likewise, telemetry does not become a causal law by being used to select an operator. In this structural interpretation, an operator is realized in NFR state or relations; "use" does not require an intentional actor or an additional NFR that commands the transformation. Consciousness is not an admission condition or a derived property of these contracts. Software callers and configured selectors can request events, but their requests do not establish autonomous occurrence in a proposed ontological realization. Each operator still requires its own live-state and composition conditions.

Its validity does not select the region or predict its indefinite maintenance.

    1.2 Can NFR properties justify a joint law?¶

    They can constrain one when the property has an independent mathematical meaning. Requiring a desired endpoint and choosing a force to attain it would instead prescribe the result. The distinction is particularly important for "resonance", "identity" and "fractality": none alone specifies a differential equation or a conserved quantity.

    Attributed property Independent structural requirement What remains to justify
    Identity through change A declared full-state observation and permitted symmetries; retain information needed to predict its response Its formation, restoration and lifetime under a complete law
    Relational interaction Explicit support, boundary exchange and response to neighboring state Pressure law, reciprocity and any state-dependent metric
    Circular phase A circle-valued coordinate, chart boundaries and transformation rules Its clock and relation to form; phase reflection is not automatically a redundancy
    Nested effective nodes A state map with exact closure, derived memory or controlled error Autonomous region selection and whether the same law survives reduction

    The existing scale bridge already supplies one useful positive example. On its reversible pure-EPI domain the inherited regional capacity is nu_eff=boundary_conductance/sum_i(d_i/nu_i). With common fine capacity this is that capacity times the ratio of boundary conductance to total regional degree. Thus internal and boundary structure determine a coarse response factor without fitting a function of EPI. A closed regional clock still requires the intertwining condition; nesting alone cannot remove hidden modes. This is a reused result, not a separate capacity-law campaign.

    A second, explicitly conditional route is the cotangent phase/form comparison. It asks whether reversible internal exchange can use the already derived phase metric. Choosing a conjugate-momentum interpretation then forces a geometric pressure correction and a reciprocal phase row. This supplies a testable complete comparison, not a deduction that every NFR must be Hamiltonian or that scalar EPI is physical momentum. The current engine's pressure is revised in that comparison, and held capacity is not claimed to emerge. The integration audit shows that those premises still allow different exchange scales. It also derives an effective scale from the multiplicity of exactly synchronized replicated blocks. This is a concrete connection to nested descriptions, with supplied fine dynamics and grouping; it neither selects the microscopic law nor shows that those groups form autonomously. NFR properties can impose checkable composition requirements without presupposing the desired pattern.

    The faithful scalar state remains the cylinder (x, exp(i*theta)). The representation controls already show why replacing it by x*exp(i*theta) loses relevant sign and zero-form phase information under the current multichannel law. A geometric proposal must retain this state or justify its changed quotient and pressure; calling the result an NFR does not supply that justification.

    2. Geometry and diagnostics do not close the dynamics¶

    The tetrad is a required diagnostic interface, not a proved complete state. Different capacities or hidden configurations can share its summaries and evolve differently. Its field definitions, distance conventions, availability and threshold scope belong to the field reference and minimal-state boundary.

    Graph spectra, phase winding and field symmetries can reveal constraints or suggest effective coordinates. A spectrum of a supplied graph does not derive that graph; a symmetry of an auxiliary field space does not prove that engine trajectories preserve it. The gauge reference and variational reference retain the useful algebra and its bridge obligations. The regime comparison separates diffusion from added inertial models. These tools may test a proposed nodal law; they do not provide missing laws by analogy.

    Arithmetic constructions have the same input discipline. Spectral properties of encoded divisibility are properties of that construction, not autonomous generation of arithmetic or physical matter. The arithmetic owner and spectral research scope retain those distinctions.

    3. Admission before expansion¶

    For the next proposed mechanism, record the supplied state and law, the structural principles that restrict it, the alternatives those principles still permit, and a response that distinguishes the alternatives before evaluation. Preserve counterexamples and null outcomes. A model that needs an added source must expose that source; a closure that loses information must expose the missing state or memory.

    The completed prospective finite-response discriminator between resultant-direction and current-amplitude phase sources on fixed P3 retains its finite engine checks. It does not select a unique pressure law or demonstrate autonomous NFR formation. It is not a pending task. The sole execution plan selects the remaining work. The conditional relational law, formation/control results and bounded memory prediction have their existing proof owners; their admission is not a pending law-selection campaign. Composition must retain the internal state needed by that law and declare any supplied support event. The event-premise result shows that even an additional zero-supply passivity premise does not select attachment. State-preserving pure addition then requires equal endpoint form and phase; an atomic edge exchange can instead lower declared storage on an open set. Neither observation proves autonomous occurrence or preservation of a pattern when its defining cycle is changed. The bridge-relocation theorem retains the internal cycles and proves recovery after one supplied passive exchange on an explicit open set. It demonstrates compatibility of reorganization and maintained phase identity, not an autonomous event law or an identification of the patterns with physical particles. The choice/clock audit now proves that the stated budget, recovery and covariance requirements leave different compatible event laws. It justifies no automatic selector. The complete-action revision permits nodal reorganization while connecting: actual UM can offset positive edge cost, and existing continuous dynamics can create phase admission. The operator map separates these mechanisms from supplied candidates and invocation. Whether sustained synchronization justifies activation remains a hypothesis; neither an instantaneous U3 match nor the SDK's auxiliary modal rhythm establishes it. The selected precursor is now an explicit nodal intermediary under the same form/phase law. Its derived memory retains both hidden coordinates, predicts a capacity-dependent transient and admits local recovery of the supplied composite geometry. The finite capacity intervention has passed its frozen prediction, while a shared frozen boundary supplies a control for apparent joint restoration without mutual influence. This is conditional interaction and restoration, not capture from initially disconnected support. A supplied return path admits distinct recoverable geometries for equal and opposite windings, with a necessary extra storage budget for the opposite equilibrium. It does not establish a maintained oscillation. Connection as a causally shared rhythm now has conditional local evidence: an ideal damped complex mode is proved and native causal residues resolve a receiver response. This does not show a perpetual pulse or a nonlinear limit cycle. Prepared synchronization alone is insufficient, and effective connection need not mean primitive edge creation. The connection-mechanism audit distinguishes hidden-node mediation, reinforcement and primitive support birth. The continuous contact comparisons and event-law comparisons leave multiple compatible laws under their respective premises. They prove neither impossibility of autonomous formation nor an exhaustive classification of future mechanisms. Primitive formation and selection of its complete law remain open; a supplied autonomous countermodel is not a derived occurrence law. The effective-link admission defines a scoped research criterion: mutual causal response and restoration of a declared joint geometry. Active paths, retained mediator memory and the existing recovery theorem yield sufficient conditions and an explicit local formation family. An unchanged frozen separator supplies a causal null even when the regions appear geometrically aligned. These are conditions for a collective relation on supplied fine support, not physical spatial binding or the origin of that support. A sustained common pulse is not required by this criterion. Autonomous coarse closure and transitions to another winding identity remain separate obligations. Completed collective-state and contact results retain their original hypotheses. The target is an effective-constituent mechanism, not yet a spin or particle identification.
