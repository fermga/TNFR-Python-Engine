    1.1 NFR formation: definition, birth and collective dynamics¶

    The NFR definition is scale-relative: a node represents a micro-NFR, while a coherent region can be studied as a macro-NFR. The model starts with a declared nodal substrate. Its initial existence is a premise of this description, not a claim that it is external to TNFR or irreducible at a finer scale. The foundation scope states the test for deriving an effective nodal substrate from finer TNFR. That test is distinct from the emergence of further organization in an already specified state. An operator describes a transformation of this state or its relations; it is not a separate entity whose appearance must be simulated. Its autonomous occurrence law remains a separate requirement.

    Three claims must remain separate:

    • Graph birth: an admitted public THOL action adds a child coordinate, triad and hierarchy metadata. This is an implemented structural event.
    • Formation of a coherent region: an existing collection reorganizes into a pattern with a stated identity, coupling and temporal persistence. This need not add graph vertices. A high coherence snapshot or a morphology label alone does not demonstrate its formation or continued maintenance.
    • An autonomous macro-NFR description: the proposed aggregate state predicts its evolution without unspecified microscopic variables. Exact closure, additional state or derived memory must be established for that claim. Failure of a particular reduction does not rule out a coherent region in the full state.

    3. Graph-derived geometric read-outs¶

    The canonical EPI channel propagates with L_rw = I − D⁻¹W (ΔNFR_epi = −L_rw·EPI, exact). For a symmetric nonnegative conductance graph without isolates, L_sym = D^(1/2) L_rw D^(−1/2): L_sym is a symmetric isospectral representative of L_rw, not the same nodal operator. Their eigenvalues agree, while their eigenvectors are related by degree scaling; the matrices coincide on regular connected components. The auxiliary graph-wave model must declare which representative supplies its stiffness. The isotropic harmonic substrate uses identity stiffness. Connectivity is primitive; every metric or spectral geometry below states which graph operator generates it.

    3.1 Graph metrics from declared conductance — DERIVED¶

    For a connected symmetric nonnegative conductance graph, the combinatorial Laplacian B=D-W defines the effective resistance R_eff(i,j) = B⁺_ii + B⁺_jj − 2B⁺_ij (a true metric; symmetric, non-negative, triangle inequality — effective_resistance, ex.124). It is not the hop count: it sees all parallel paths (transport difficulty). Measured — a ring's antipodal nodes give R_eff = 50 vs hops = 100 with unit conductance (two parallel paths halve it); a unit-conductance tree's two leaves give R_eff = hops = 12. On a weighted tree, resistance is the sum of reciprocal conductances along the unique path, which need not equal hop count. Disconnected components require separate treatment: cross-component resistance is infinite, not the finite value obtained by blindly applying that expression to the full disconnected pseudoinverse. Distance is derived from the declared conductance; uniformly scaling W changes resistance inversely while leaving L_rw unchanged. The tetrad potential instead uses shortest paths from explicit length, legacy weight, then unit lengths. It does not automatically use effective resistance. Neither choice has been derived as physical distance; edge semantics and the scale bridge own these separate geometries.

    3.2 Dimension readouts — MIXED: exact definitions and finite measurements¶

    There are several distinct dimension-like readouts. They can be compared on a declared graph family but are not generally one quantity.

    (a) The ambient spectral dimension d_s — the dimension an arbitrary network carries. From a selected heat-trace scaling window p(t)=Z(t)/n ~ t^{−d_s/2} (Z=Σ e^{−λ_k t}) it is estimated from the spectrum: measured d_s ≈ 1.01 (ring), 2.22 (2D torus), 3.36 (3D torus) — toward 1/2/3 with finite-size bias (ex.134). It is measured from the dynamics, not declared — but for a generic finite graph a stable scaling window need not exist (a selected tree gives ≈ 1.6 in the reported fit, emergent_base_dimension.py): no bare network singles out d = 3.

    (b) The simplex grade and representation multiplicity. A maximally-coupled cluster of k+1 mutually-resonant NFRs is the 1-skeleton K_{k+1} of the k-simplex; its Laplacian multiplicity k is the standard-irrep dim of S_{k+1} = the emergent cardinal readout (§0), while k is separately the simplex grade. These equal integers in that construction but remain different mathematical objects (emergent_simplex_dimension.py). The separate phase-threshold construction explicitly appends phase samples, rebuilds a graph with selected threshold pi/6 and applies an auxiliary phasor average with factor 0.4.
