    Organized NFRs: identity, dynamics and emergence¶

    Status: current ontology and admission criteria for organized TNFR objects. The objects are conditional mathematical constructions with declared state, laws and identity. Proofs, finite numerical evidence and open hypotheses retain distinct status. This owner defines how claims about those objects are admitted; the research program selects research.

    0. What counts as emergence here¶

    On an unforced continuous segment, the nodal identity

    \[\dot{\mathrm{EPI}}=\nu_f\,\Delta\mathrm{NFR}\]

    relates form, capacity and pressure. It does not uniquely determine a pressure law, phase evolution, capacity evolution, support, clock or operator schedule. These choices must be stated and justified before interpreting their output. Separately supplied continuous inputs and hybrid jumps retain the extended-row and event accounting; they are not absorbed into this unforced identity. The parameter foundations own the assumptions and available derivations. Existing definitions and defaults are revisable.

    A derived consequence of a specified model can be useful without that model being uniquely selected. Conversely, renaming a supplied model, initial pattern or target value does not derive it from nodal dynamics. Distinguish four levels:

    Claim Required evidence Insufficient substitute
    Structural read-out Defined map from a supplied graph state, with domains and unavailable cases A suggestive field or topology name
    Dynamical consequence Explicit evolution law and assumptions, with proof or source-bound finite trajectory Static resemblance or an imposed endpoint
    Effective nodal emergence State map, closure or derived memory, and comparison with microscopic evolution Calling an arbitrary partition a macro-NFR
    Autonomous structural occurrence A justified source, eligibility, timing and event law on the retained state A supplied schedule or an admissible passive event

    Mathematical experiments evaluate consequences under specified preparations, laws and numerical budgets. They can test a prediction, expose a counterexample or support a bounded numerical certificate. They do not by themselves provide independent empirical validation. The theory-to-execution map connects these claims to their proof, implementation and test owners.

    1. Substrate, formation and identity¶

    Organized objects as mathematical test cases¶

    Guiding physical hypothesis. Matter and the observed fundamental physical properties involved in its formation may emerge from organizations of TNFR structure and dynamics. The aim is a generative account: derive an organization's formation, identity, persistence and interaction properties, then establish how those properties are observed physically. This remains an open hypothesis. Conditional mathematical objects test its prerequisites; physical identification additionally needs justified observables, units, clocks and independent discriminating evidence. Neither a prepared pattern nor a compact prediction record establishes the formation of matter.

    Within this hypothesis, different physical entities could correspond to different reproducible organizations under the same complete dynamics. An account must derive their existence, stability or metastability, interactions, transformations and distinguishing observations. Persistent organization may include evolving patterns or cycles as well as stationary equilibria. A discrete catalogue is a possible outcome, not an assumption; assigning a separate law to each desired class would not establish the common mechanism. Current examples under different supplied laws do not yet form one dynamical classification of matter.

    Known experimental behavior constrains the intended explanation. Separate the measured response and its preparation from the present theory's account of it; an alternative ontology must recover those responses in their tested regime. Use the collective-property atlas to identify missing structural mechanisms. A common law must explain the claimed properties together, rather than assign an independent mechanism or fit to each desired number. A phase cycle or two stable outcomes alone does not establish the rotation and measurement behavior associated with spin.

    Mathematical sources of structural hypotheses. Investigate the possibility that arithmetic relations and recurring geometric constructions describe invariants, symmetries or composition rules of TNFR organizations. The stronger idea that numbers symbolically manifest an underlying TNFR order remains an open interpretive hypothesis. A mathematical theorem supplies evidence about its declared objects and premises; identifying those objects with nature additionally requires physical observations and their bridge.

    The arithmetic constructions and scale and geometry results provide mathematical tools and conditional examples. Distinguish a supplied number label, residue graph, polygon, coefficient or phase encoding from a structure selected by the dynamics. A preferred constant appearing after its geometry or normalization has been supplied does not establish its universal selection. Any proposed arithmetic emergence must specify which invariant, relation or operation is derived and which arithmetic premises it consumes.

    Constructions discussed as "sacred geometry" may motivate questions about precisely defined symmetries, proportions, tessellations, polyhedra or scale relations. Their cultural or symbolic significance is separate from their mathematical properties and causal consequences. Translate a proposed connection into an explicit construction, a dynamical mechanism and a discriminating consequence, with alternatives and failure conditions. Neither visual resemblance nor a numerical coincidence establishes the proposed order. The research selection procedure decides when such a question resolves a concrete foundational dependency.

    This objective permits physical observations to concern an organized whole. It does not require direct experimental access to every primitive TNFR coordinate, or identify EPI with a particular sensor signal. An effective EPI, phase or capacity of an organization needs a justified observation and predictive description, including retained memory where closure requires it. Calling a pattern an electron or giving it an effective EPI does not supply that description or its physical properties.

    Direct preparation and readout of primitive state is one possible comparison route. The native FO/CA access conditions remain binding for the specific prepared consumer that uses them; an insufficient apparatus bridge for that consumer is neither a refutation of the generative hypothesis nor a universal prerequisite for every foundation question. Independent empirical testing remains necessary for a physical explanation, including one whose microscopic coordinates are accessible only through collective responses.

    The four foundation prerequisites block new physical mapping studies until their evidence and review are complete. The physical target or a suggestive historical passage cannot override that prerequisite. The admissible work in the meantime concerns structure, causal dynamics, observation, identity and model discrimination.

    In that work, identity, activity and periodicity have separate observations. A maintained organization may retain reproducible interaction properties while its internal waveform changes. Exact predictive-state return is one specific pulse claim, not a universal NFR existence criterion. High coherence or phase synchronization alone establishes neither identity nor a clock. Formation still requires initial absence, acquisition and subsequent retention; changing an observation or assigning a label does not establish acquisition.

    Historical intuitions and current obligations. The Canon2 manuscript identified in the historical source audit describes identity through continued change and response (pp. 86--87, 113), and organization across scales (pp. 26, 125). The acquired native response now supplies a conditional instance of response with retained identity, including a local finite contract; quantitative source entry remains separate. The composite-identity account distinguishes maintained joint organization from initially absent acquisition. Nesting and scale labels still require sufficient collective state, including derived memory when needed for prediction.

    The same manuscript proposes local and network-emergent time (p. 219), while its pulse example introduces an oscillating factor Omega(t) (pp. 220--221). That supplies activity; it does not derive the oscillator or its synchronization. These intuitions retain the full-state clock obligations and the pulse's causal-account requirement. Persistent identity and synchronization alone establish no monotone clock. The preserved review is artifacts/native-response-feasibility-20261029/. Historical assertions motivate hypotheses, not instructions or additional canonical premises; the research program selects their next discriminating test.

    The unifying target includes electromagnetic, weak, strong and gravitational phenomena, together with quantum matter, spectra and transformations. Seek compatible effective regimes of one justified complete dynamics, with common observation and scale-matching contracts. The shared physical prerequisites connect experimental discriminators to this hypothesis. These targets remain open; binding, decay or directed circulation alone identifies none of them.

    Electromagnetic emergence is a highlighted physical target. The relevant question is whether interactions and organization can yield a field/source sector with justified flux constraints, propagation, work and matter coupling. Classical Maxwell behavior, quantum electromagnetic excitations and identification with physical light/charge retain distinct obligations. The electromagnetic audit owns the current evidence, manuscript clues and missing mechanisms. Its double-slit comparison additionally requires a derived or explicitly supplied propagation/detection probability law; primitive phase and visual interference alone do not establish quantum statistics. These targets orient the sole research program without prescribing their successful realization.

    The primary line of work develops the foundations of TNFR through organized mathematical objects: determine their sufficient state, identity, dynamics, interactions and admissible structural transformations, then establish what those premises imply and what they leave undetermined. The intended outcome is a justified account of the paradigm's capabilities and limits. No external identity is assigned to a pattern from its shape, label or suggestive response.

    The organized-source construction supplies one conditional object on which continuous recovery and a grammatical word can be studied with explicit state, work and numerical obligations. The theory catalog routes other models and their proofs. Different complete laws remain distinct test cases; their results cannot be combined solely because both contain an organized pattern.

    The existing repertoire of collective properties is not assumed exhaustive. When a more complex organization becomes admissible, reassess its identity, sufficient state and effective dynamics: a lower-level description may omit new relational information. A proposed property needs a defined observation and a control that distinguishes organization-dependent behavior from what the prepared parts already possess. An effective law derived on that domain is compatible with an unchanged fine law; it does not by itself select a new universal microscopic law.

    A common substrate could admit distinct, reproducible families of collective organization at different scales. This is stronger than a mere change of scale: the proposed families must have distinguishable dynamics, composition rules and observable responses. A shared substrate does not identify distinct organizations with one another or prove that every level has a sufficient autonomous description.

    The engine represents nodes, states and relations as the supplied domain of each model. A finer TNFR realization needs the same explicit reduction obligations as any effective description; see substrate and scale. Objects and their environments belong to the same declared configuration. An unobserved background, zero form and zero pressure are distinct conditions; the environment/pressure contract retains the state or memory required by the actual driver. Neither global connectedness nor the origin of that substrate follows from an interaction certificate.

    Formation, maintenance and composition¶

    A supplied identity, entry into that identity, persistence under a law, recovery after an intervention and autonomous occurrence are separate claims. An identity criterion states membership or equivalence, its observation domain, error margins and the claimed interval. An observation that is unavailable before a support change cannot be treated as measured absence. A valid preparation or stationary state does not by itself establish acquisition.

    The interacting patterns could reorganize into a common composite NFR whose identity depends on their relative position, phase and dynamics. Their shapes and trajectories may change; a finite-lived composite may move or oscillate jointly. This remains a hypothesis. A formation claim requires an independently defined actual-state identity that is initially absent with a margin, then acquired and retained for the declared interval, with relevant constituent and boundary state and a later interaction or response criterion. Attributing that formation specifically to nonlinear interaction additionally requires a matched linear control. Linear acquisition would limit that attribution without invalidating formation under its own law. Conversely, a nonzero superposition defect or extra Fourier modes may describe small deformation or radiation without joint retention. Repeated composite formation could support a hierarchy, but does not guarantee increasing complexity.

    The same complete fine law may govern the incoming patterns and a common organization. A single-pattern retention argument does not thereby prove retention of their relation. Two spatial lobes within one carrier band are not independent dynamical constituents merely because an envelope is written as their sum; a collective description must retain the relative and boundary information its identity and evolution consume.

    The program overview summarizes available capabilities; each linked model owns its premises and verdict. In particular, a missing sufficient certificate is not proof that an organization cannot exist. Source-dependent exclusions remain constraints on those source classes, not universal exclusions.

    NFRs participate in the same structural configuration¶

    The programme adopts participation in form as a constitutive requirement: an NFR is an organization of the same structural configuration in which it acts. Its identity and interactions must be represented within that configuration. A proposed effective description must establish how it satisfies this constitutive requirement.

    At an effective scale, write y=R(z) for the retained description of a pattern in the fine joint state z. On a smooth fixed-support interval, its rate must be inherited as y_dot=DR(z)*F(z). A closed law in y alone requires the existing closure conditions; otherwise retain boundary state, hidden coordinates or justified memory. Effective interaction must represent the same fine interaction, without counting it again as an independent force exerted by a second copy of the pattern. The collective interaction owner already shows why relations between patterns can carry necessary state. The reduced formed-class ports give a controlled approximation example: a smaller component state transfers to a different receiver with explicit finite error for discarded modes and initial uncertainty. This supplies neither an exact nonlinear quotient nor a complete identity proof; that proof remains a separate obligation.

    A larger organization retains the constituent and boundary information its identity and response consume. Removing interchangeable labels is different from removing continuous state. A closed law on an invariant family is also different from an exact quotient on a neighborhood of that family. The collective grammar criteria additionally admit action targets, histories, guards and ordered event effects; continuous closure alone does not discharge those requirements.

    Localized identity and transport on a supplied support¶

    A basic NFR is the lowest retained level of a declared model, not an established ultimate constituent. The substrate foundation states that distinction. Conditional research can ask whether interactions on a supplied support sustain a localized organization that moves across it while retaining a reproducible identity. Such a claim retains the chosen law and preparation; it does not require that the law be uniquely selected among all compatible models.

    Transport of a pattern changes which fixed sites carry it. It differs from internal phase rotation, oscillation within fixed constituent pairs, a redrawn graph layout, relabeling sites or creating a child node. A supplied cycle's winding and a moving maximum are not sufficient identity tests. Formation and transport also differ: transporting a prepared organization does not show its formation from a class lacking that identity.

    An admissible transport claim declares the following before its response:

    • Law and background. Retain every consumed coordinate, the full support, capacities, clock, inputs and events, with a specified reference background. An observation window does not remove interactions with its complement. Define a support metric separately from conductance; graph-layout positions have no dynamical meaning without a declared geometric model.
    • Localization. Define a nonnegative observation of departure from the background, its units, spatial width/tail criterion and a nonvanishing signal floor. This observation does not replace signed form in the law or turn a signed storage remainder into a positive local energy. State how multiple peaks, splitting and unavailable centers are handled.
    • Displacement and identity. Use one fixed support reference and a predeclared identity comparison with error bounds over the entire interval. If translations are used as exact dynamical equivalences, verify that their maps preserve support, coefficients and complete dynamics. Profile alignment used only as an observation must retain any mismatch and its error bounds. Preserve displacement in the record. Common phase-origin freedom is not spatial translation. Retain the internal and boundary state needed to distinguish continuing transport from decay followed by unrelated excitation elsewhere.
    • Preparation and robustness. Declare whether the initial class already contains the identity, any supplied directional asymmetry and independent disturbances. Symmetry of both the law and preparation cannot be discarded by a numerical tie-break and then called autonomous direction selection. Admit finite transport, persistent motion and collision as separate claims.

    The localized identity owner provides a conditional prepared-transport example. The common-pattern formation contract separates its retained carrier identity from the acquisition of a new common organization. Case-specific results, controls and evaluated responses belong to those owners rather than a cumulative inventory in this ontology.

    1.1 NFR formation: definition, birth and collective dynamics¶

    The NFR definition is scale-relative. A node represents a micro-NFR; a coherent region is a candidate macro-NFR. Three distinct questions must be tested:

    • Graph birth: an admitted THOL action creates a child coordinate, its triad and hierarchy metadata. This is an implemented event under a supplied admission and environmental-amplitude rule.
    • Regional formation and persistence: a collection develops and retains a stated identity under surrounding interactions. This need not create vertices. A coherent snapshot, stationary minimum or topology label alone does not demonstrate temporal maintenance.
    • Predictive effective description: retained coordinates, or derived memory with its hidden-initial-state source, predict the declared response. An autonomous law on current aggregate coordinates additionally requires closure on those coordinates. A failed reduction does not refute the region's existence in the full state.

    Operators specify transformations of existing state; they are not separate entities whose birth must be simulated. Deriving when a transformation occurs is a further closure problem. Likewise, telemetry does not become a causal law by being used to select an operator. In this structural interpretation, an operator is realized in NFR state or relations; "use" does not require an intentional actor or an additional NFR that commands the transformation. Software callers and configured selectors can request events, but their requests do not establish autonomous occurrence in a proposed ontological realization. Each operator still requires its own live-state and composition conditions.

    The operator realization contract distinguishes target, eligibility, request origin and completed action; configured state-dependent selection is a reusable mechanism, not yet a derivation of autonomous occurrence. A continuous NFR evolution also need not be reinterpreted as an unobserved succession of registered discrete events.

    The collective-action criterion makes this distinction testable: an NFR description must predict both its continuous response and any claimed event independently of discarded fine labels. Event targets, history and occurrence can require additional retained state even when a continuous quotient already closes. The native full-form formation obstruction also supplies a conditional phase-acquisition budget; it does not turn a storage-passive reset into an autonomous mechanism.

    The program overview distinguishes available capabilities and their boundaries; each model retains its positive results and exclusions. The theory-to-execution map locates proofs, code and tests. The ontological obligations can be stated without maintaining a second inventory of preparations:

    Claim about an organization Required distinction
    Formation Show entry from an initial set lacking the claimed identity, then its declared retention and response. Native validated transit and sine prepared acquisition have separate law/domain and uncertainty hypotheses.
    Maintenance and recovery Admit the initial identity and prove retention over its declared interval; recovery additionally needs a declared intervention and return criterion. These claims do not require that the identity was initially absent.
    Effective description Prove sufficient collective state, or retain hidden initialization through derived memory. Means, winding and instantaneous rates alone need not close.
    Interaction and composition Retain environmental influence, relative frames and causal controls. Compatible isolated identities need not remain compatible after attachment.
    Birth or structural action Admit state/support jumps and their costs separately from occurrence. A configured child, favorable budget or named operator does not derive its own activation.

    Failure of a sufficient maintenance certificate does not establish absence of an identity. An observation unavailable before a support change is not a measured absence; declare its cross-support meaning before claiming acquisition. The following linked studies make those distinctions precise on their own laws.

    3. Admission before expansion¶

    For the next proposed mechanism, record the supplied state and law, the structural principles that restrict it, the alternatives those principles still permit, and a response that distinguishes the alternatives before evaluation. Preserve counterexamples and null outcomes. A model that needs an added source must expose that source; a closure that loses information must expose the missing state or memory.

    The research program selects the next bounded question. Existing conditional laws, formation, controls and memory forecasts are reusable evidence; their presence does not start additional law-selection or contact campaigns.

    For structural contact, distinguish three independent obligations:

    • State and causal influence: keep full internal and boundary information, including relative origins. The effective-link criterion requires mutual causal response and restoration of a stated joint geometry. Hidden-node mediation can provide this on supplied fine support. A frozen separator is a causal null even when two regions look aligned.
    • Event and storage: event admission keeps continuous loss separate from support/reset work. Passive pure addition, edge exchange and nodal reorganization are different contracts. A supplied event can preserve a pattern without selecting why or when the event occurs.
    • Complete-law selection: continuous relation comparisons and support-law comparisons retain multiple compatible choices. Neither synchrony, an eligible contact, storage passivity nor a successful prepared response uniquely chooses one.

    A sustained shared pulse is not required by the effective-link criterion. A damped collective response, maintained geometry, primitive edge birth and a sufficient composite identity remain different claims. The target is a justified account of the organization's state, inherited dynamics and admitted transformations. Its emergent properties are conclusions to establish under those premises, not identities to impose in advance.
