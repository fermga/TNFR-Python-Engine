## 1. A hierarchy needs a composable state-and-law map

Barenblatt section 5.3 connects a specified generalized homogeneity of a
response with transformations of parameters and the dependent quantity. Its
exponents need additional model information; the perturbation expansion is
an independent step with its own reference case. The transformations must
stay in the domain where the asymptotics applies. This is not a theorem that
arbitrary graph aggregation produces a renormalization group. In particular,
lossy projection need not be invertible like the displayed transformations.

Sections 7.1-7.2 supply a useful compositional idea: direct subdivision and
subdivision through an intermediate resolution must agree under their stated
homogeneity/self-similarity assumptions. A functional equation then yields
the power form in that class. Local/asymptotic versions retain small remainder
terms and can vary across the curve. We use this as motivation to test a
*composition property*, not as a proof of a TNFR power exponent.

**Existing TNFR basis inspected.** The scale owner already requests direct
versus staged reduction. DERIVED_EPI_MEMORY section 9.2 contains an exact first
quotient and a memory-producing second observation. The registered inheritance
owner derives the full map of form, clock, potential, reciprocal coupling and
boundary input. Matching preserves its parameters; three offsets enlarge the
family and can leave the source-admitted interval. Its output is an effective
pair, not automatically another C9 input. ORGANIZED_NETWORK_COMPATIBILITY
already proves that individually valid pair charts can fail to assemble around
a three-constituent cycle, while a richer six-coordinate description closes.

**Constructive proposal.** Search for a family closed under *composition of
organized descriptions*, permitting retained interface registration and finite
internal state rather than requiring the same bare scalar graph at every level.
The first deliverable would be a matched direct/staged state-law diagram for
two admissible grouping steps, including source, observation, input, clock and
storage. A precise second-step obstruction is equally useful: it identifies
the smallest missing coordinate, boundary parameter or memory term.

This is a real extension beyond a one-step parameter map, not a proposal to
repeat the already known P5 or three-C9 result. Use those as controls. First
admit a second operation on the first output; if that fails, stop and identify
the incompatibility before simulating a larger hierarchy. Formation of those
organizations remains a separate question from this representation test.

**Implementation reuse.** structural_morphism.py owns intertwining and its
finite-time bound; _sine_constitutive_inheritance.py owns the actual organized
map and source-specific evidence. epi_memory.py and linear_observation.py
already supply internal realization/closure mechanisms. multiscale/hierarchical.py
instead manages supplied scale graphs and pressure couplings; its named levels
do not certify the proposed diagram. multiscale_coherence.py assesses a
declared U5 diagnostic. Neither should be renamed as an emergent hierarchy.
Do not build a new hierarchy engine until this composition contract has an

## 2. Investigate dynamical similarity separately from geometric dimension

Barenblatt's curve example makes resolution part of the observation. Equal
scaling exponents can omit prefactors and local variation. The last broad
identification of physical fractality with incomplete similarity on printed
132 is not imported as a universal TNFR definition: the concrete derivation
concerns that selected geometric measurement and its assumptions.

**Constructive proposal.** When a hierarchy question is selected, compare
organizations through their response after a justified transformation of
state, clock, boundary input and output. Ask whether preparation details lose
predictive importance on a declared scale/window. That would define a scoped
dynamical similarity class, even if the drawings differ. Conversely, matching
geometric exponents with different contact response would separate geometric
resemblance from dynamical universality.

**Minimum useful discriminator.** Choose one existing constitutive alternative
that matches a target and its local tangent but differs nonlinearly, such as
the family at COMPOSITION_COMPATIBLE_CONSTITUTIVE_LAWS. Use a fixed response
observable and independently specified finite input/amplitude domain. First
check whether an exact coordinate equivalence already explains the apparent
collapse; otherwise seek a uniform discrepancy bound or a separating response.
Only after that decide whether a second grouping reduces or preserves the
difference. A finite matched response class is weaker than an asymptotic
universality theorem, but is an informative result in its own right.

**Optimization.** Existing fields.py power-law fits are explicitly descriptive.
They do not need a new name or a new fitted exponent. Reuse original data and
observations only inside a newly admitted study. The useful next object is a
response comparison and its domain, not another attractive fractal drawing or
unbounded fit. A conservative norm bound can be too expensive for that purpose;
structural_morphism.finite_time_intertwining_bound already documents a generic
exponential envelope and the sharper contractive symmetric possibility. A
future consumer can justify a specialized contractive bound without changing
the all-matrix contract or pretending this review evaluated a speedup.

## 3. Compare passive retention and active reorganization

Wagensberg suggests three questions: whether an object persists by reducing
its exposure, by changing internal activity/exchange, or by acquiring a new
identity. This is a heuristic distinction, not a universal selection law.
No biological progress, thermodynamic claim, information inequality, or
surface/volume formula from the essay is adopted as a TNFR theorem.

**Existing basis.** NATIVE_FOUNDATIONAL_ACCOUNT already admits a supplied
autonomous capacity-diffusion completion, preserving positive capacities,
total capacity and the native reciprocal storage balance. This is not the
gated adaptation policy in dynamics/adaptation.py and does not replenish
capacity lost by a Silence event. The ontology already separates maintenance,
recovery and transformation of identity. These positive results make a
structured comparison possible without adding a primitive universal pulse.
Capacity diffusion responds to capacity contrasts, not an observed identity
error or environmental signal. It is a controlled baseline for redistribution,
not already a demonstration of active compensation for an external change.
That stronger claim would require an independently specified feedback law and
its complete balance; it cannot be inferred from Wagensberg's terminology.

**Constructive proposal.** Compare two independently admitted mechanisms for
retaining the same organization and useful response under one specified family
of surrounding changes: altered boundary transmission versus internal capacity
redistribution. Both mechanisms must have explicit laws and supplied resources;
do not tune a controller from the response it is meant to predict. Retention
alone can reward a completely unresponsive isolated object, so record retained
interactive capability alongside identity and work/state costs. The outcome
may be a tradeoff, not a scalar optimum or universal preference for complexity.

**Minimum useful discriminator.** Begin with the existing native positive-capacity
law as one scoped mechanism and its held-capacity control. Do not assume the
same metric or rate transfers to a new boundary event. Admit one perturbation
and a common response before asking which mechanism extends the retention or
response domain. If capacity redistribution only changes timing, record that
equivalence rather than claim a new identity. Add another boundary law only
if the first comparison identifies a question it can discriminate.

## Disposition

Priority within a future global selection: composable retained state and
interfaces first; finite response classes next; repeated scale transformation
and universality only once the first two are admitted. Adaptive persistence is
a separate useful comparison when the target capability consumes environmental
variation. This is a conceptual dependency assessment, not an activated queue.
No implementation defect, new scientific result or physical correspondence
has been established by this reading.
