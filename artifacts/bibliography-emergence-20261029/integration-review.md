## Substantive findings

1. **The broader request is met.** The appendix identifies five connected
   capabilities: predictive internal state across interactions, imperfect
   collective organization, functional timing response, composable scale
   descriptions and durable reorganization. The proposals go beyond the
   current two-port case and name concrete discriminators rather than only
   precautions. They do not redefine NFR or assert a universal constitutive law.
2. **Existing achievements remain achievements.** The weighted non-orbit star,
   exact phase-advance branch, distinct periods, finite pulse retention,
   deterministic Gram/product closure, native alternatives and existing
   mediator/registered composition are explicitly acknowledged. The new
   questions concern response, admitted handoff or quantitative approximation;
   they do not present those existing mechanisms as undiscovered.
3. **The star proposal now preserves its exact scope.** The final draft names
   weighted-form/bare-sine dynamics, fixed support and original clock. It calls
   the reset balance-breaking and distinguishes a primary capacity map from a
   canonical Silence execution. Actual represented outputs and context/history/
   grammar remain required. No native phasor quotient theorem is imported.
4. **The active-object proposal is concrete without importing attraction.**
   One admitted Emission and one preselected relative crossing-time response
   make the missing capability identifiable. The tangent decision precedes
   finite continuation. Amplitude, neutral shear, full perturbations, event
   pressure refresh, marked ports and available crossings remain explicit.
   Existing conservative retention does not become an attracting-cycle phase
   response model or a practical numerical amplitude certificate.
5. **Memory is tied to a consumer.** Exact fixed-generator realization and
   source initialization are reused. Dynamic product reduction remains a
   possible improvement over an already successful static approximation,
   requiring a real state/error advantage and propagated nonlinear remainder.
   The correction properly confines reservoir attraction to local fixed-J
   leaves of its supplied zero-leakage law. It derives no reservoir from
   actual constituents merely by citing that example.
6. **Scale and adaptation claims remain conditional.** Direct/staged reduction
   first requires an admissible second operation; pair charts and C9 outputs
   are not silently iterable. Finite response similarity is not universality.
   Capacity diffusion is not already identity-error feedback. The proposed
   acquired-state switch begins with a source-associated action-image/capture
   question, not a basin scan or a replacement of the reached state by an
   equilibrium. Same-law reorganization is not hysteresis or autonomous action.
7. **No incorrect stability shortcut is adopted.** My source note identifies
   the frozen-eigenvalue shortcut in Newman printed704–705 as unsuitable for
   general time-dependent nonnormal dynamics. The appendix instead requires
   an actual propagator or justified metric bound. It does not copy that
   shortcut or claim a newly found defect in maintained TNFR code.
