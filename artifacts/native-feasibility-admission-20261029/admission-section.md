## Admission of an independent access-feasibility literature audit

**Decision:** the established prepared foundation is sufficient to ask
whether independent physical access could support the one-bridge consumer.
Admit only the bounded documentary audit specified by the
[current program](../RESEARCH_PROGRAM.md#research-current-question), subject
to its exact final readiness review and the unchanged strict guard. This
does not admit a physical realization, an empirical comparison or the
application of the FO threshold to measured data.

The distinction is substantive: existence of an independently characterized
preparation, reference policy and readout is the question being investigated.
It is not assumed as a premise for calculating or validating a physical
response. Requiring that question to be answered before allowing its
investigation would be circular. Conversely, the completed mathematical
FO/CA results do not let an experiment consume an unsupported access premise.

The four original prerequisite scopes therefore remain intact. No extra
mathematical mechanism is assumed to exist merely to pose the documentary
question. Any candidate-specific preparation, actuator or observation
proposed as actually available must still be assessed on independent source
evidence; a later prediction using it requires a new exact-task admission.
The CA ideal port is a requirement to examine, not evidence that hardware
implements it.

### Scope and evidence ceiling

The program freezes at most three terrestrial apparatus families, six
primary documents and six targeted search queries. Existing oscillator
inventories provide starting metadata, with their historical exposure and
model-specific exclusions retained. The audit reads methods and acquisition
descriptions; it does not download signal archives, extract trajectories,
fit parameters, run models, build apparatus or score an empirical response.
No new law/variable correspondence is admitted merely by completing a table.

For each inspected apparatus, distinguish source-backed facts, unavailable
information, proposed identifications and claims that need an exact or
controlled reduction. A diagram of selective control does not establish
unchanged companion rows, absence of hidden memory or negligible reaction.
Separately calibrated signals do not automatically form one simultaneous
complete state. Missing documentation is not evidence of physical absence.

The maximum positive conclusion is **a documented candidate warranting a
separate bridge review**. Other admissible conclusions are a documented
incompatibility with a specified required access condition, or insufficient,
inaccessible or not yet comparable evidence. None of those outcomes
automatically changes a source's experimental-admission status.

Published uncertainties cannot be compared directly with FO's native
`2^-166` budget without a justified clock, unit and gain conversion.
That budget is sufficient, not necessary; failure to establish it is not a
physical impossibility theorem. An apparatus deliberately programmed to
execute the TNFR equations may demonstrate implementation or control, but
does not independently validate those equations as a natural law or derive
its material constituents.

### Exact-task gate and transition

This documentary investigation is treated as physical activity for workflow
purposes. It is not exempted by calling it feasibility or source reading.
The final readiness review must bind the final program bytes and all four
current prerequisite reviews, assess the joint scope above, and explicitly
exclude broader empirical work. The strict prerequisite command must pass
before any external source inspection for this task.

The checker's `physical mapping ready` message then means readiness only
for that frozen documentary task. It does not interpret the study budget or
grant general permission for physics. A changed program or pillar review
invalidates its binding; any later bridge derivation, fit, empirical test or
different consumer needs its own substantive admission.

The exact-scope decision and independent reviews are retained in
`artifacts/native-feasibility-admission-20261029/`. This admission review
has not searched external sources or evaluated a physical response. The
program alone owns execution and the next decision after the bounded audit.
