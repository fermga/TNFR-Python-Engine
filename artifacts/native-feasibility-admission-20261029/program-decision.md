obstructions. Its [exact-scope admission](nodal/NATIVE_FOUNDATIONAL_ACCOUNT.md#native-feasibility-admission)

The sole active question is **whether independently published apparatus
descriptions justify a candidate for a later realization/measurement-bridge

none of the inspected cases justifies such a review. It is not an empirical
test of TNFR, nor a claim that an apparatus realizes the complete law.

### Frozen scope and source budget

Before external source inspection, run
`python scripts/check_research_prerequisites.py --require-physical-mapping`
with the admitted isolated interpreter. The final readiness must still bind
this exact program and its four current reviews. Treat this source inquiry
as physical activity, without changing the checker or relying on a generic
`ready` message to permit other work.

Use the [existing apparatus inventory](research/PHASE_AMPLITUDE_MEASUREMENT_PROTOCOL.md#primitive-phase-information-boundary)
as starting metadata. Review at most **three apparatus families**, in this
order: electronic oscillator networks; coupled mechanical resonators;
electrochemical oscillator networks. Select at most one concrete apparatus
per family using documented access and ordinary terrestrial availability,
not waveform resemblance. The old Rössler/DPLL, quartz/MEMS and electrode
leads have no automatic admission to JO/FO; prior exclusions keep their
original law and measurement scope.

The complete audit is limited to **six primary documents and six targeted
search queries**. Count an article, a separate methods/supplement document
and a repository's acquisition documentation separately; different views
of the same document are one item. Record a selected document's identity,
URL, family and access-based selection reason before detailed assessment.
Record access date and version/DOI/revision/commit when available, and the
section or page supporting each extracted fact. Unknown metadata remain
unknown rather than being inferred from another source.
An unavailable selected item remains in the six-item ledger; do not replace
it silently or expand the budget. Query results may locate those items but
are not evidence of an apparatus's capabilities. Do not inspect more source
documents merely because a lead appears promising.

Read primary papers, methods and authoritative acquisition documentation.
Do not download raw signal archives, digitize plots, extract responses,
calculate model outputs, fit parameters, simulate, install a controller or
build apparatus. Published descriptions and historical inventory entries
are prior exposure, not reserved validation data. No direct identification
of EPI, phase or capacity with a physical variable is admitted by this audit.

### Required evidence and decision

For each selected apparatus, record source-backed facts separately from
missing information and proposed interpretation. The evidence table must
cover the following linked requirements:

1. Actual support and accessible nodes/ports; complete published state and
   dynamics, environmental inputs, memory, filters and companion channels.
   A reported oscillator or graph is not the native `g,H` law.
2. Independent preparation/reset, relative form and phase information,
   capacity-profile access and mismatch control. An extracted phase does
   not automatically establish primitive phase or its initialization.
3. The reported mechanism for any capacity-like rate and its evolution or
   control, without inferring capacity from a measured frequency by name.
   Record whether a held reference is independently characterized and
   whether selective control changes other rows, support or hidden state.
4. Actuator/observer reactions and resources actually described; simultaneous
   readout, gain/offset calibration, elapsed clock and uncertainty evidence.
   Zero net capacity in CA is not an apparatus energy or resource balance.
5. The unresolved exact-law or controlled-reduction obligations. Do not use
   another apparatus's methods, an ideal compensator or a programmed equation
   emulator as independent evidence for the candidate's natural dynamics.

Use three outcome categories: **documented incompatibility with a specified
required access condition**; **insufficient, inaccessible or not yet
comparable evidence**; or **documented candidate warranting a separate
bridge review**. State the exact premise behind each verdict. A mismatch
for the actual apparatus or frozen route is not a theorem excluding its
whole platform class. Absence of documentation is not physical impossibility.
The positive category does not admit a physical identification or experiment.

Keep the one-bridge prepared consumer fixed: no transfer of two-bridge
selection, particles, fields, universal constants or formation claims.
FO's `2^-166` is a sufficient native error budget, not a necessary instrument
resolution. No comparison of published precision to that number is valid
without independently justified units, gain and clock; this audit does not
supply that conversion by assignment.

Stop new searches and document selections when either source/query limit
is reached; finish classifying only the material already admitted, without
extra retrievals or queries. Close the audit when those cases are classified,
even if no candidate survives. Retain the source
ledger and a single result in the [physical assessment](PHYSICAL_REGIME_CORRESPONDENCES.md);
this program remains the only task queue. Recommend at most one subsequent
bridge review or concrete unresolved dependency from the evidence.
Any new consumer, budget expansion, mapping derivation, fit, simulation or
empirical evaluation needs a new exact-task admission and, where applicable,
prospective F1-F4 scope, final-source review, source freeze and resource budget.
Changing this program or a pillar review invalidates the present readiness
binding; the completed audit cannot authorize its own follow-up implicitly.
