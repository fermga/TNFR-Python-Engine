## Findings

### 1. Wagensberg attribution is appropriate

The chapter uses the book to ask what a form contributes under specified
conditions. This accurately reflects printed pages 20–25 and 126–127. It does
not equate Wagensberg's boundary-based form with signed EPI, import his
philosophical function as an occurrence law, or assert that a polygon is
privileged by TNFR. The speculative persistence/probability argument on printed
127 and the broad natural examples are not adopted as TNFR premises.

The fractal passage explicitly concerns repeated resemblance over a finite
number of scales (printed 250) and raises functional questions (printed 253).
The draft appropriately distinguishes this motivation from an exact recursive
dynamics, an asymptotic scaling law or proof of a TNFR fractal hierarchy.
The common pre-contact/post-contact/control observation and the absence,
maintenance and response obligations are repository methodological judgments;
they are not attributed as theorems proved by Wagensberg.

This matches `EMERGENT_ONTOLOGY.md#generative-bound-organization`, especially
its distinction between a supplied identity, acquisition, persistence, recovery
and autonomous occurrence, and its requirement that unavailable observation
not be counted as initial absence. The integration changes no ontology.

### 2. Dimensional analysis and similarity retain their actual premises

Scaling printed 22–24 requires a complete list of governing parameters and
independent dimensions before reducing dimensional dependence to dimensionless
groups. Printed 91 explicitly includes initial and boundary conditions. The
drafts correctly say that this restricts possible dependence without supplying
the function or choosing a complete TNFR law.

Printed 84 requires a finite nonzero limit when selected dimensionless
parameters tend to zero/infinity while the remaining parameters are held as
declared. It also requires convergence useful at finite values before dropping
parameters in practice. Printed 85–86 distinguishes a special generalized
homogeneity of the dimensionless response: the governing parameter can remain
essential in rescaled power combinations, with exponents not determined by
ordinary dimensional analysis. The final insertion preserves these differences.

Printed 87 restricts the third outcome to absence of the stated similarity
reduction, not every conceivable reduction. Note 2 makes the power-type versus
logarithmic-factor distinction explicit. Root's corrected insertion and chapter
now preserve this limitation. No TNFR quotient is assigned to one of these
classes, and no limit, exponent, renormalization fixed point or physical scale
is asserted to have been derived.

### 3. Finite error and small-parameter claims are now sound

The final drafts require a domain, source family, boundary, clock, observable,
horizon and error when a fine model is approximated. That is a methodological
condition, consistent with the existing TNFR owners. A limiting argument alone
does not supply a usable error bound at the current parameter/time values.
An omitted initial component can remain relevant through an initial term or
memory. The drafts do not claim that Barenblatt proves a TNFR remainder estimate.

The original insertion implied that every term omission or approximation needs
a small-parameter argument. I requested correction: exact cancellation or a
different controlled error estimate may justify it. The final statement instead
requires its own exact justification or controlled bound. This is adequate and
does not prescribe a perturbation expansion where none is necessary.

The distinction is compatible with the scale owner's exact intertwining
criterion and its separate approximate/error statements. The organized-pattern
inheritance owner retains an exact invariant family and a separate actual-source
error domain. Reusing those methods remains conditional on matching all consumed
premises; no source or coefficient is transferred by this review.

### 4. Exact-isotropy wording is correct and limited

The draft requires a smooth equivariant flow, uniqueness, a local inverse and
finite times within the stated domain. Under those premises, preservation of a
state's isotropy is the source proposition's conclusion. The draft separately
allows a more symmetric limiting equilibrium and treats a support event as a
change of field. It does not claim global existence, invertibility of a
numerical step, or preservation through arbitrary hybrid jumps.

Thus the warning against using exact attainment of a new symmetry as a generic
finite-time formation criterion is justified. A finite membership domain,
controlled neighborhood or later predictive response is a separate claim; the
draft explicitly says that formation is not excluded. The source result does
not establish a new TNFR acquisition theorem or refute all organization.

This also preserves the ontology's separation between removal of interchangeable
labels, invariant restriction and loss of continuous state, and the source-
versus-reference distinction already maintained by the two-port capture owner.
