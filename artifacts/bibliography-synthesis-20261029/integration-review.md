## Substantive findings

1. **The formation question remains source-dependent.** The integration keeps
   compatibility, acquired membership, maintenance and predictive response
   separate. It correctly links SINE_TWO_PORT_COMPATIBILITY and
   SINE_TWO_PORT_CAPTURE as different results. The latter's midpoint-aligned
   preparation is not silently substituted for the actually formed endpoints.
   SINE_FORMED_CLASS_CONTACT#sine-formed-joint-cycle-eligibility obstructs the
   selected independent-cycle carrier on a single bridge; it does not forbid
   interaction, feedback or every collective identity. The new chapter
   preserves that restricted negative result and the existing positive results.

2. **Symmetry is applied to the correct object.** The law/reference/source/
   readout table is useful because these hypotheses differ in the actual
   capture and reduced-port owners. The finite-time isotropy statement is
   consistent with Golubitsky and Stewart, proposition 1.18, printed page 12
   (PDF 34); uniqueness, a local inverse and the unchanged smooth field are
   explicitly retained. An asymptotic gain of symmetry or a support event
   is not incorrectly ruled out. An invariant restriction does not become
   all-state observation closure. This agrees with
   DERIVED_FORM_PHASE#collective-interaction-closure-and-relational-state.
   The witness symmetry screen supplies only an obstruction test, not a
   positive response. No graph-spectrum-only stability claim is introduced.

3. **Memory and initialization are preserved without adding a statistical
   assumption.** The synthesis distinguishes coordinate elimination,
   symmetry quotient and measure-conditioned projection. In particular,
   deterministic uncertainty boxes are not treated as probability measures.
   The coordinate-memory contract in src/tnfr/mathematics/linear_observation.py
   retains hidden initialization and does not itself assert decay, stability
   or stochastic noise. SINE_CLASS_MEDIATED_MEMORY's reached-state obstruction
   is used as a scoped example; its three-C9 coefficients and source verdict
   are not transferred to the active two-C9 system. SINE_REDUCED_CLASS_PORTS
   remains a controlled model, not an exact full nonlinear quotient. These
   distinctions remove a potential duplication of existing shared methods.

4. **The pulse and phase passages retain current results and their limits.**
   Conservative organization, damped response, the exact phase-advance branch
   and its finite nonlinear timing retention are all retained. A complex
   eigenpair is not promoted to a Hopf bifurcation or an attracting pulse.
   RELATIONAL_PHASE_MEMORY's asymptotic origin is constant along recovering
   motion; it is not confused with the advancing asymptotic phase of an
   attracting periodic orbit. The semidefinite-loss passage correctly leaves
   the law-specific invariant-set argument at RESONANCE_FOUNDATIONS. The
   missing quantitative domain/metric/rate in the native source-entry audit
   remains missing. No general Lyapunov citation is used to close that gap.

5. **Scale transfer is now properly qualified.** I checked the corrected
   chapter, insertion and root note. The final insertion permits either an
   exact justification or a controlled error bound for omitted terms; it no
   longer imposes a small parameter on every valid simplification. The
   complete/incomplete/absent similarity alternatives are explicitly scoped
   to declared limits and power-type combinations. Their absence does not
   exclude logarithmic or other reductions. Barenblatt is used to clarify
   methodological premises, not to supply a TNFR exponent or renormalization
   result. DERIVED_EPI_MEMORY section 9.2 already has a genuine first quotient
   followed by a memory-producing merge; ORGANIZED_PATTERN_CONSTITUTIVE_

   promoted to an indefinitely iterable hierarchy. The conductance/length
   distinction and actual source/clock/error premises remain intact.

6. **The useful optimization is routing, not a replacement theory or queue.**

   source/event/identity declaration. It retains full-state reconstruction,
