## Form, persistence and prediction

Wagensberg distinguishes structure, composition, boundary form, size and
classification, and asks what a form contributes to persistence in its
environment. This is a fruitful *question-generating* perspective for TNFR:
specify an organization, its surroundings and an observation, then ask what
its relational arrangement changes. It supplies no mathematical theorem for
TNFR and no justification of a preferred triangle, hexagon or nested image.

## Dimensional reduction and asymptotic similarity

Barenblatt's dimensional analysis requires the governing parameter list,
including initial and boundary data, and the rank of independent dimensions.
Dimensionless groups restrict possible functional dependence; they do not
determine the function or select a unique complete TNFR law. An already
dimensionless structural model gains no additional state compression merely
by renaming its coefficients.

Section 4.1 distinguishes a finite nonzero limiting dimensionless response
(complete similarity), a singular dependence that survives in rescaled
combinations (incomplete similarity), and absence of the specified power-type
similarity. Printed page 87, note 2 limits this classification: it is not an
exclusion of every reduction, including logarithmic factors. For a finite
approximation, existence of a limit alone is not enough:
the approach must be useful at the actual parameter values. In incomplete
similarity, dimensional considerations do not in general determine the
extra exponents. Sections 4.2-4.3 stress that initial dimensions can remain
essential and that governing-parameter selection matters. Neither a fitted
power nor a small parameter proves the required asymptotics.

TNFR application is a proposed methodology, not a transfer of the book's PDE
examples. TNFR_SCALE_GEOMETRY_AND_BRIDGE.md already distinguishes exact closure,
larger effective families and memory. ORGANIZED_PATTERN_CONSTITUTIVE_INHERITANCE
already transforms form, clock and storage on registered organizations; its
output is not automatically another admissible input of that same map.
DERIVED_EPI_MEMORY already exhibits exact followed by memory-producing
reduction. These are stronger concrete results than visual self-similarity.

What is worth adding to the scale owner is a concise distinction among units,
exact quotient, finite-scale geometric resemblance and asymptotic similarity,
with an explicit retained-parameter/domain/error condition. This is an
editorial contract clarification, not an assertion that TNFR possesses a
renormalization fixed point, universal exponent or emergent physical scale.
