## One closing recommendation

**Treat memory, imperfect balance and scale composition as three obligations
of one interface-specific prediction contract, not three independent general
method-development campaigns.** Select a concrete organization, its permitted
interaction/continuation and its useful output first. Retain the internal state
or a uniform error budget needed for that output, then check the next admitted
operation. The mathematical theorems remain distinct; this unifies selection,
not their premises or coefficients.

Eliminate geometry or pattern classification as an independent prerequisite
to that decision. Symmetry is useful for finding invariants, excluding a
witness or simplifying a calculation; another symmetry label is not itself
a new causal capability. The active-pulse and acquired-state-switch proposals
are possible consumers of the contract. Switching retains the additional
obligation to prove an actual change of identity and destination, which an
ordinary finite response alone cannot establish.

## Two terminology and inference corrections

**Material correction: a zero tangent is not a complete negative response.**
The current active-object paragraph says that a zero projection would reject
the action/readout. Its preceding test is a first derivative of a crossing-time
functional. A zero first-order projection closes that *linear discriminator*;
it does not exclude a higher-order finite response. A higher-order study would
need its own justification and admission, rather than being automatically
launched. Unavailability of the selected crossing rejects that particular
observation on its declared domain, not every timing observation.

**Precision: finite response agreement is not automatically a response class.**
Pairwise closeness at a fixed tolerance is not generally transitive. The phrase
“finite response class” should therefore mean either a demonstrated exact
observational equivalence under the declared continuations, or a specified
family compared with one common reference and a uniform error budget. It must
not silently acquire equivalence-class or universality semantics. Any later
composition must propagate its inherited error; it cannot reset the tolerance
at each grouping and claim unchanged final accuracy.

Likewise, finite robust output prediction should not be called **structural
stability** without the relevant dynamical stability theorem. Exact symmetry
can disappear while a finite output remains close. Conversely, an orbit or
phase-portrait equivalence does not itself bound a marked readout in its
original units and clock. Keep the explicitly consumed perturbation class,
domain, output and horizon instead of promoting a broad stability adjective.

## Compact research-selection criteria

Before choosing one future consumer, require five concrete answers:

1. **Consequence:** which specified output, action or identity transition would
   become predictable, and what existing claim does that improve?
2. **Same object and interface:** which actual source, retained internal state,
   port marks, complete law and next operation are shared by the comparison?
3. **Useful resolution:** which source/model/numerical uncertainties and horizon
   could mask the outcome, and what bound or exact witness would decide it?
4. **Shortest missing link:** is the first obstruction sufficiency, event image,
   output availability, local response or source-to-capture, before any transit?
5. **Stopping result:** what scoped negative would close the proposed route,
   rather than trigger an indefinite scan or ever-tighter passive bounds?

Concrete stops include: the second operation is outside the first output's
domain; equal retained states have separated allowed outputs; a selected
first-order functional vanishes; an action range is excluded from a certified
capture domain; or the useful error budget cannot be supplied with the retained
information. Failure to supply a bound is an unresolved certificate, not proof
that every realization fails. A merely new drawing, harmonic label or symmetry
class with no changed prediction is insufficient benefit to activate a study.

These criteria close the bibliography review. They reuse F1–F4 and the existing
ontology rather than append another generic ontology, code layer, candidate
catalogue or active plan. The program still chooses one question; no physical
readiness, empirical identification or source-specific theorem is changed.
