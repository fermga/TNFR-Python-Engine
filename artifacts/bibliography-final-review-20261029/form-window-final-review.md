## What is useful, and what is excluded

Wagensberg asks what an organizational feature contributes under an environment
and distinguishes the intentional selection of a researcher/designer from a
feature retained by a process. That question is useful in TNFR as a demand for
a declared functional response, comparison and retained identity. It does not
mean that TNFR dynamics optimize a chosen score or select the contact supplied
by a study. An object's advantageous response, its acquisition, the occurrence
of its event and its relative frequency are four separate matters. A source
set or uncertainty box supplies no probability distribution. Nothing here
imports a universal progression toward complexity or biological adaptation.

This is already consistent with EMERGENT_ONTOLOGY.md sections 0 and 1 and its
generative-bound-organization anchor; AGENTS.md sections 3 and 5; and the
program's distinction between compatibility, capture, actual source and event.
No new definition of EPI, function, selection or NFR is needed. The practical
optimization is to stop proposing a geometry merely because it looks natural:
name the observable advantage or missing causal relation first. This does not
exclude useful exact classifications that settle compatibility or obstruction.

Barenblatt's intermediate asymptotics separates a scale on which preparation
details matter from another on which previously negligible conditions matter.
An effective description can be useful between those scales without being an
all-time limit or the final equilibrium. His example also emphasizes that
negligibility must actually hold; dimensionless smallness alone is insufficient.
The historical footnote on printed 63 even describes a case with no intervening
window. This motivates checking the existence of a useful TNFR interval before
extending dwell time or adding a larger hierarchy. It is not a requirement that
every exact reduction possess two separated scales.

For a proposed approximation the relevant lower requirement may be decay of a
discarded source contribution, entry to a domain or a minimum time for a
distinguishing response. The upper requirement may come from a reconstruction
bound, cumulative forcing, phase shear, domain exit or a new admitted event.
These must be put in the same declared clock and observation. A nonempty
intersection with an adequate response margin supports the approximation's
usefulness; an empty intersection rejects that certificate at that resolution,
not all possible descriptions of the object. Neither limit may be fitted after
inspecting the desired response. Exact source-aware descriptions can remain
useful without waiting for hidden state to decay.

## Final consolidation judgment

The previous five opportunities need not be five projects. Reusable internal
state, imperfect balance and composable scale descriptions are state/interface/
error obligations of one component problem. A marked-pulse response is a
possible consumer of such a description. Durable acquired reorganization is a
separate source-to-capture question that cannot be inferred from compactness or
response alone. For each selected case, retain the smallest sufficient existing
description; add a coordinate, memory or controlled approximation only when a
specific admitted continuation exposes missing predictive information.

Stop the bibliography expansion here. The scientifically useful next outcome
is the program's one source/event/identity admission or a precise obstruction.
Later task selection can use the consolidated criteria. A universal projection
container, generic polygon search, stochastic closure without an ensemble,
new fundamental pulse law or extrapolated scaling fit adds no justified
capability at this stage. This judgment prioritizes investigation; it is not a
measured computational improvement or proof of uniqueness of TNFR dynamics.
