## Scientific and attribution judgment

1. **The corrected negative is appropriately narrow.** A zero first derivative
   now closes only its linear discriminator. Higher-order response needs a
   separately justified question, while a missing crossing rejects the selected
   observation on its domain. No stronger null is inferred from a tangent test.
2. **Approximate agreement is no longer promoted to exact equivalence.** The
   common-reference/uniform-error wording, nontransitivity warning and inherited
   error propagation preserve the distinction from an exact quotient and from
   structural stability of a complete flow. The domain remains declared; no
   universal all-model response-class theorem is asserted.
3. **The repository already has the general tools credited here.** The directed
   pure-EPI imperfect-interface result supplies an ambiguity lower bound and
   useful upper budgets under its own law. The nonlinear star remains a
   different prospective consumer. The draft does not transfer its coefficients,
   stochastic-contraction bound or frozen response into that star.
4. **The additional memory/composition credit is accurate.** DERIVED_EPI_MEMORY
   §9.5 proves finite fixed-support diffusion/Euler and uniform REMESH-word
   closure with matched history. The cited nonlinear sine interface carries
   hidden initialization through a finite impulse family. The causal-composition
   owner supplies two-channel output substitution under feedback and a separate
   lifted-residual contract. Their source, uniform input-domain, feedback
   existence, work and identity premises remain at their owners. This is not
   a claim that a single-impulse tolerance proves arbitrary feedback accuracy.
5. **The unification introduces no extra generic prerequisite.** Full state is
   explicitly an acceptable reference. Compression needs a concrete benefit;
   exact closure does not require a new scale gap. Finite response does not
   require all-time attraction, recurrence, a basin classification or autonomous
   event selection. Capture remains necessary for claims actually consuming it.
6. **Generative priority is preserved rather than displaced by a framework.**
   The formed-source/two-port question is already selected by the program's
   comparison. The final text preserves it and closes the bibliography cycle.
   Pulse response and durable switching remain later possibilities, with
   different obligations. There is no second queue or automatic transit.
7. **The bibliography remains a method source.** The new page table agrees with
   the final notes, including Strogatz's overlapping boundary page and partial
   adjacent sections. The symmetry claims match my direct selected reading.
   Other new passages are assessed through their identified reviewers' notes;
   this review does not claim independent full reading of those books. No
   imported statistical measure, PDE, forcing law or physical identification
   becomes a TNFR premise merely by citation.
