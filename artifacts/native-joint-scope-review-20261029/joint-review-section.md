### Joint P1-P4 assessment

**P1:** the event-free predictions consume exactly the form, phase and
capacity rows. Their initialization, support and coefficients are supplied
premises. The registered thirteen actions are not all claimed as continuous
realizations; the finite Coupling comparison retains its obstruction.
Additional event laws are required only for claims that consume such events.

associations. Quotienting neutral origins is justified only with their
reconstruction and the declared mean leaf. A scalar receiver signal is an
observation, not a proven autonomous state. A smaller report, common phase,
pressure reconstruction or regional total cannot silently replace hidden
phase, capacity, preparation or memory.

**P3:** prepared persistence, winding-zero acquisition and selection among
prepared destinations now have separate positive accounts. Their proofs do
not establish every geometry, law, perturbation or scale. The RB radius
certifies an explicit mathematical neighborhood; its conservatism does not
prove either experimental controllability or a physically narrow basin.
Changes of mean, law, coefficients and support remain outside that radius.

**P4:** the complete native law is sufficient for these conditional
capabilities without becoming unique. Held capacity, alternative conserved
measures, passive completions and other admitted model families retain their
own domains. Agreement of storage loss, equilibria or tangents does not
identify a nonlinear law. A limited comparison must admit or distinguish
the alternatives affecting its observation; it need not classify every
conceivable TNFR law.

The four prerequisite resolutions retain exactly their
[prepared single-bridge scope](#native-foundation-scope-decision).
The acquisition, selection and robustness results remain separate supported
extensions, not an implicit broadening of those approvals. The joint review
is a mathematical scope decision, not the final physical-readiness review.

### Observation boundary and selected consumer

The [physical assessment](../PHYSICAL_REGIME_CORRESPONDENCES.md)
still lacks an independently admitted bridge from a concrete system's
preparation, readout, intervention, clock and uncertainty to this complete
law, or a controlled approximation establishing that bridge. No existing
dataset has passed that admission. A structural observation contract can
specify what such a bridge would have to preserve without assigning physical
units, fitting data or identifying a particle.

One existing discriminating consumer is the JO receiver-form observation
`y=x_5-(x_6+x_7+x_8+x_9)/4`. With the exact shared preparation (JO6), (JO12)
gives
\[
y_{\rm dynamic}(T)-y_{\rm held}(T)
 = c_3 T^3+O(T^4),\qquad
c_3=\frac{\kappa\eta a(\eta-b)K}{6}<0.
\]
Here `a>0`, `0<eta<b`, `K>0`, and the perturbation is sufficiently small
for the stated JO recovery claim. This is a transient deformation with
common-origin shifts removed. It is neither a permanent displacement nor
the two-bridge geometry-selection observable. The receiver accelerations
agree, so that second-order response alone does not detect redistribution.
At exact lock the form and phase rows vanish for every capacity profile;
their equilibrium tangent does not acquire a direct capacity-to-output
channel merely by releasing capacity.

The first mathematical obligation for this consumer is a **finite
observation separation with declared uncertainty**. A nonzero cubic jet
gives a qualitative short-time distinction, but JO supplies no explicit
regular horizon, remainder bound or preparation/readout/clock error budget.
Those quantities must come from the actual full law and independently
declared preparation, not from the very signal being judged. An error budget
can overwhelm the cubic signal as time or amplitudes tend to zero. Separate
shared preparation perturbations from mismatch between trials, and common
clock transformations from timing errors; mismatch in the common quadratic
response can itself dominate the cubic contrast.

The existing [prepared coefficient identification and sampling contract](RELATIONAL_RESPONSE_IDENTIFICATION.md#prepared-coefficient-identification)
already demonstrates why excitation, hidden phase, clock and finite-sample
errors matter. Its held-capacity consensus preparation, coefficient ratio
and derivative bounds do not transfer automatically to JO. Reuse its
observation discipline, not its numerical or model-specific verdicts.

This consumer is selected because it uses the already approved prepared
scope, a signed-form output and an exact matched-law discriminator. It
isolates a missing observation premise without requiring another geometry,
a larger selection radius or a general executor. Its feasibility rests on
the existing smooth regular native field and exact jets, not on a promise
that any desired noise level can be admitted. A positive contract must
exhibit a nonempty justified separation domain; a negative result must
identify the concrete obstruction or unavailable premise. Neither outcome
alone would supply the independent physical bridge.
